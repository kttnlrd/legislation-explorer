# 135  Regulations

The Governor-General may make regulations, not inconsistent with this Act, prescribing all matters:

<a id="s135-a"></a>
**(a)** required or permitted by this Act to be prescribed; or

<a id="s135-b"></a>
**(b)** necessary or convenient to be prescribed for carrying out or giving effect to this Act; and, in particular, may make regulations prescribing penalties not exceeding a fine of 5 penalty units for offences against the regulations.
