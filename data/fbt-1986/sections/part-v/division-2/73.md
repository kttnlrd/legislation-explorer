# 73  Default assessments

Where:

> <a id="s73-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an employer has not furnished a return in respect of a year of tax; and

> <a id="s73-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the Commissioner is of the opinion that the employer is liable to pay tax in respect of that year; the Commissioner may, whether during that year or after the end of that year, make an assessment of:

> <a id="s73-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the fringe benefits taxable amount of the employer of the year of tax; and

> <a id="s73-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the amount of tax payable on that fringe benefits taxable amount.
