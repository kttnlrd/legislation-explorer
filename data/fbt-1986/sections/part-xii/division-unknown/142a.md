# 142A  Benefits relating to transport


<a id="s142A-1"></a>
**(1)**  For the purposes of this Act, recipients expenditure that is in respect of, or a recipients benefit that consists of:

> <a id="s142A-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  accident insurance, airport or departure tax, passenger movement charge, a passport, a visa or a vaccination; or

> <a id="s142A-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  any similar matter or thing; in connection with transport shall be taken to be in respect of the provision of, or to consist of, transport.

<a id="s142A-2"></a>
**(2)**  For the purposes of this Act, where:

> <a id="s142A-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  transport is between a particular place and another place;

> <a id="s142A-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the transport is provided in consecutive stages; and

> <a id="s142A-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  apart from this subsection, a particular matter or thing would be in respect of only one, or only some, of those stages; the matter or thing shall be taken to be in respect of the provision of that transport.
