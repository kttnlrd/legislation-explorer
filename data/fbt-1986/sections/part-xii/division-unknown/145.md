# 145  Consideration not in cash


<a id="s145-1"></a>
**(1)**  For the purposes of this Act, where, upon any transaction, any consideration is given by way of the provision of property (other than money), the money value of that consideration shall be deemed to have been paid or given.

<a id="s145-2"></a>
**(2)**  Subsection (1) does not apply for the purpose of determining whether an act or thing constitutes the provision of a benefit to which a particular provision of this Act applies.
