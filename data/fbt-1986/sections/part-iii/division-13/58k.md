# 58K  Exempt benefits—in-house health care facilities

Where:

> <a id="s58K-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a benefit consisting of the provision of health care is provided in respect of the employment of an employee of an employer; and

> <a id="s58K-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the health care is provided:

> > <a id="s58K-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  in an in-house health care facility of the employer; or

> > <a id="s58K-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  by a member of the staff of an in-house health care facility of the employer in the performance of his or her duties as such a member; the benefit is an exempt benefit.
