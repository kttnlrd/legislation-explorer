# 58W  Exempt benefits—deposits under the Small Superannuation Accounts Act 1995

Accounts Act 1995 When section applies

<a id="s58W-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies if:

> <a id="s58W-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a benefit is provided in respect of the employment of an employee; and

> <a id="s58W-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the benefit consists of the making of a deposit, or purported deposit, under the Small Superannuation Accounts Act 1995. Exempt benefit

<a id="s58W-2"></a>
**(2)**  The benefit is an exempt benefit. Definition

<a id="s58W-3"></a>
**(3)**  In this section: deposit has the same meaning as in the Small Superannuation Accounts Act 1995.
