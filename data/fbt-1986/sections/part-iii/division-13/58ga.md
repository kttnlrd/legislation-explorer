# 58GA  Exempt benefits—small business car parking

Exemption

<a id="s58GA-1"></a>
**(1)** A car parking benefit provided in an FBT year in respect of the employment of an employee is an exempt benefit if:

<a id="s58GA-1-a"></a>
**(a)** the car is not parked at a commercial parking station; and

<a id="s58GA-1-b"></a>
**(b)** the employer of the employee is not a public company (see subsection (3)), or a subsidiary of a public company (see subsection (3)), in relation to the day on which the benefit is provided; and

<a id="s58GA-1-c"></a>
**(c)** the employer is not a government body; and

<a id="s58GA-1-d"></a>
**(d)** either:

<a id="s58GA-1-d-i"></a>
**(i)** the sum of the employer's ordinary income and statutory income for the year of income ending most recently before the start of the FBT year is less than $10 million; or

<a id="s58GA-1-d-ii"></a>
**(ii)** the employer is a small business entity, or is an employer covered by subsection (1A), for the year of income ending most recently before the start of the FBT year.

<a id="s58GA-1A"></a>
**(1A)**  An employer is covered by this subsection for a year of income if:

<a id="s58GA-1A-a"></a>
**(a)** the employer is not a small business entity for the year of income; and

<a id="s58GA-1A-b"></a>
**(b)** the employer would be a small business entity for the year of income if:

<a id="s58GA-1A-b-i"></a>
**(i)** each reference in Subdivision 328-C (about what is a small business entity) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 to $10 million were instead a reference to $50 million; and

<a id="s58GA-1A-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** the reference in paragraph 328-110(5)(b) of that Act to a small business entity were instead a reference to an employer covered by this subsection. New employers

<a id="s58GA-2"></a>
**(2)** However, if an employer to which subparagraph (1)(d)(i) applies:

<a id="s58GA-2-a"></a>
**(a)** in the case of a tax-exempt employer (see subsection (3))— did not start to carry out operations or activities; or

<a id="s58GA-2-b"></a>
**(b)** in any other case—did not start to carry out business operations; until after the start of the year of income mentioned in subparagraph (1)(d)(i), then:

<a id="s58GA-2-c"></a>
**(c)** that subparagraph does not apply; and

<a id="s58GA-2-d"></a>
**(d)** the employer must make a reasonable estimate of the amount that would be the sum of the employer's ordinary income and statutory income for the year of income (the business start-up year) in which the employer did start those operations or activities, or those business operations; and

<a id="s58GA-2-e"></a>
**(e)** that estimate is to be made on the assumption that the employer had started the operations or activities, or the business operations, at the start of the business start-up year; and

<a id="s58GA-2-f"></a>
**(f)** the benefit is an exempt benefit only if that estimate is less than $10 million. Definitions

<a id="s58GA-3"></a>
**(3)** In this section: ordinary income has the same meaning as in the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997. public company means a company covered by paragraph 103A(2)(a) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936, but reading the reference in that paragraph to the last day of the year of income as a reference to the day on which the benefit is provided. statutory income has the same meaning as in the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997. subsidiary of a public company means a subsidiary of a public company within the meaning of subsection 103A(4) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936, but reading:

<a id="s58GA-3-a"></a>
**(a)** a reference in section 103A of that Act to a year of income as a reference to the day on which the benefit is provided; and

<a id="s58GA-3-b"></a>
**(b)** a reference in that section to a public company as a reference to a public company within the meaning of this section. tax-exempt employer means an employer all of whose income is wholly exempt from income tax.
