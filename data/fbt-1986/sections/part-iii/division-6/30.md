# 30  Living-away-from-home allowance benefits

30 Living-away-from-home allowance benefits

<a id="s30-1"></a>
**(1)** Where:

<a id="s30-1-a"></a>
**(a)** at a particular time, in respect of the employment of an employee of an employer, the employer pays an allowance to the employee; and

<a id="s30-1-b"></a>
**(b)** it would be concluded that the whole or a part of the allowance is in the nature of compensation to the employee for:

<a id="s30-1-b-i"></a>
**(i)** additional expenses (not being deductible expenses) incurred by the employee during a period; or

<a id="s30-1-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** additional expenses (not being deductible expenses) incurred by the employee, and other additional disadvantages to which the employee is subject, during a period; by reason that the duties of that employment require the employee to live away from his or her normal residence; the payment of the whole, or of the part, as the case may be, of the allowance constitutes a benefit provided by the employer to the employee at that time.

<a id="s30-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

<a id="s30-2-a"></a>
**(a)** at a particular time after 10 October 1991, in respect of the employment of an employee of an employer, the employer pays an allowance to the employee; and

<a id="s30-2-b"></a>
**(b)** the employee's usual place of employment is on an oil rig, or other petroleum or gas installation, at sea; and

<a id="s30-2-c"></a>
**(c)** the employee is provided with residential accommodation at or near that usual place of employment; and

<a id="s30-2-d"></a>
**(d)** the allowance is expressed to be paid as a living-away-from-home allowance; and

<a id="s30-2-e"></a>
**(e)** no part of the allowance is covered by subsection (1); and

<a id="s30-2-f"></a>
**(f)** it would be concluded that the whole or a part of the allowance is in the nature of compensation to the employee for disadvantages to which the employee is subject, during a period, by reason that the duties of that employment require the employee to live away from his or her usual place of residence; the payment of the whole of the allowance constitutes a benefit provided by the employer to the employee at that time.
