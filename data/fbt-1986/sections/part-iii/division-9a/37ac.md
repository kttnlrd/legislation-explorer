# 37AC  Meal entertainment benefits

If, at a particular time:

> <a id="s37AC-a"></a>
> **(a)** an employer (the provider) to whom this Division applies provides meal entertainment to another person (the recipient); and

> <a id="s37AC-b"></a>
> **(b)** the meal entertainment is not provided under a salary packaging arrangement; the provision of the meal entertainment is a meal entertainment benefit provided by the provider to the recipient at that time.
