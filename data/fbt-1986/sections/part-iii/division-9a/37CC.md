# 37CC  Choosing the 12 week period for a register


<a id="s37CC-1"></a>
**(1)** The register must be kept for a continuous period of at least 12 weeks throughout which meal entertainment is provided by the employer.

<a id="s37CC-2"></a>
**(2)** The period for which the register is kept must be representative of the first FBT year for which it is valid.

<a id="s37CC-3"></a>
**(3)** If the register does not meet these conditions it is not valid.
