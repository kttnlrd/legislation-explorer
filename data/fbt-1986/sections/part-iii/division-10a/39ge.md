# 39GE  Choosing the 12 week period for a register


<a id="s39GE-1"></a>
**(1)**  The register must be kept for a continuous period of at least 12 weeks throughout which car parking benefits are provided to employees covered by the election.

<a id="s39GE-2"></a>
**(2)**  The period for which the register is kept must be representative of usage for the first FBT year for which it is valid.

<a id="s39GE-3"></a>
**(3)**  If subsection (1) or (2) is not satisfied, the register is not valid.
