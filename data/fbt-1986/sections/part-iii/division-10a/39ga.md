# 39GA  Employer may elect to use 12 week record keeping method


<a id="s39GA-1"></a>
**(1)** An employer may elect that this Subdivision applies to the employer's car parking fringe benefits for some or all of the employer's employees for that FBT year if the employer has a valid register for that FBT year covering those employees.

<a id="s39GA-2"></a>
**(2)** The employer must specify that the election covers:

> <a id="s39GA-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** all the employees; or

> <a id="s39GA-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** all employees of a particular class; or

> <a id="s39GA-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** particular employees.
