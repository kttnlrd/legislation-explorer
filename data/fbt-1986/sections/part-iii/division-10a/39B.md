# 39B  When commercial parking stations are located within a 1 km radius of business premises or associated premises

radius of business premises or associated premises For the purposes of this Division, a commercial parking station is taken to be located within a 1 km radius of particular business premises or particular associated premises if, and only if, a car entrance to the commercial parking station is situated less than 1 km, by the shortest practicable route, from a car entrance to those premises.
