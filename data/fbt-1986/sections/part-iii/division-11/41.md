# 41  Exempt property benefits


<a id="s41-1"></a>
**(1)** Where:

> <a id="s41-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** a property benefit is provided to a current employee of an employer in respect of his or her employment; and

> <a id="s41-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the property is provided to, and consumed by, the employee on a working day and on business premises of:

> > <a id="s41-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** the employer; or

> > <a id="s41-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** if the employer is a company, of the employer or of a company that is related to the employer; the benefit is an exempt benefit.

<a id="s41-2"></a>
**(2)** This section does not apply to food or drink provided to, and consumed by, an employee if the food or drink is provided under a salary packaging arrangement.
