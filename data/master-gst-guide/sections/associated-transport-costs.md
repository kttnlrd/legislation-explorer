# Associated transport costs ¶12-170

- repairs

- petrol and diesel fuel

- car rental

- public transport fares

- taxi services (¶12-130)

- vehicle insurance (¶10-110; ¶10-120).

Motor vehicle registration fees and the issue and renewal of driving licences are not subject to GST, though driving tests are taxable. Parking fees are taxable, but not parking fines or penalties for driving infringements (¶4-080). Extended vehicle warranties are subject to GST.

If a dealer registers and pays compulsory third party insurance on behalf of the purchaser of a car, and recoups that amount from the purchaser, a tax invoice for the insurance should be issued by the registration authority (as agent for the insurer), not the dealer. "Wholesale holdback" and "retail holdback" payments made by a car manufacturer or importer of new cars to a dealer that are made under an arrangement that does not form part of a dealership agreement are not consideration for a supply. GST therefore does not apply (GST Determination GSTD 2005/4).

For the GST treatment of vehicle modification permits, see the example at ¶4-080.
