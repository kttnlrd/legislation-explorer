# GST thresholds for gambling suppliers ¶16-030

Under the normal rules, various GST obligations or concessions may depend on the taxpayer meeting or not exceeding a turnover threshold, eg monthly tax periods are compulsory for businesses with a GST turnover of $20m or more (¶7-100). For these purposes, a supplier's turnover from gambling is calculated on the basis of the gambling margin, rather than the gross amount wagered (s 188-32). The gambling margin is the difference between the amount wagered and cash prizes paid out.

*Refs: s 188-32)*
