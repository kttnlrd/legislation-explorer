# Cancelling a branch registration ¶17-310

If the parent stops carrying on any enterprise through a particular registered branch, the parent must apply for cancellation of the registration. This must be done within 21 days (s 54-70).

The ATO must cancel a branch registration on application, unless the branch had been registered for less than 12 months. The ATO must also cancel a GST branch registration if it is satisfied that the parent is unlikely to carry on any enterprise through the branch for at least 12 months (s 54-75).

If the parent's registration is cancelled, this automatically cancels the registration of any of its branches (s 54-90).
