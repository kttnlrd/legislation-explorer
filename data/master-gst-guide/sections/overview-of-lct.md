# Overview of LCT ¶23-000

Where a "luxury" car is supplied or imported, luxury car tax (LCT) may be imposed in addition to GST. The rate of LCT is normally 33%, though a lower rate may apply to certain primary producers and tour operators (¶23-210).

The substantive rules governing LCT are set out in the A New Tax System (Luxury Car Tax) Act 1999 (LCT Act). Supporting regulations are in the A New Tax System (Luxury Car Tax) Regulations 2019 (LCT Regulations).

- A New Tax System (Luxury Car Tax Imposition — Customs) Act 1999

- A New Tax System (Luxury Car Tax Imposition — Excise) Act 1999, and

- A New Tax System (Luxury Car Tax Imposition — General) Act 1999.
