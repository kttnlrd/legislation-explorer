# Preparing for an audit ¶18-185

Early identification of relevant issues and common errors (¶18-170; ¶18-180) should help ensure that any subsequent compliance activity by the ATO causes as little disruption as possible.

The major steps that need to be taken in implementing a GST compliance program include:

- ensure that you "know" your business and how GST applies to it

- ensure that internal accounting and information systems reliably and verifiably capture all the

  necessary data to calculate and report the GST liability

- educate all relevant staff, including those at the point of supply

- have an ongoing process of reviewing contracts and provisions such as indemnity clauses.

GST positions taken in relation to complex transactions should be documented. You should also be aware of the extent to which you can rely on ATO rulings, and how this may affect your liability to penalties.

You should also consider providing the auditors with a GST Briefing Document, explaining your business operations and GST systems.
