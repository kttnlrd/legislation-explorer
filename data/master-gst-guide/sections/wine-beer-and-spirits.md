# Wine, beer and spirits ¶13-190

Alcoholic beverages such as wine, beer and spirits are taxable.

Individual products that are sold as home brew ingredients would also be taxable. However, if those ingredients are sold as food, they may be GST-free under the normal rules. For example, malt sold as food would be GST-free, but brewers' yeast would be subject to GST (Food Industry Register Issue 24). Jars filled with fruit in alcohol were held to be GST-free (Interpretative Decision ID 2004/289). De-alcoholised wine is taxable (JMB Beverages Pty Ltd v FC of T [2010] FCAFC 68).

A wide range of wine-related products may also attract a 29% wine equalisation tax (WET), in addition to GST.

The GST on the wine is imposed on the WET-inclusive price. For full details of WET, see ¶22-000.
