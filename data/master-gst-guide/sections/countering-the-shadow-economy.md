# Countering the "shadow economy" ¶18-182

On ongoing focus of ATO compliance activities is non-compliance with taxation obligations by operators in the "shadow" (or "black") economy. Estimates of the size of the shadow economy put it at in excess of 5% of GDP.

- demanding or paying for work cash-in-hand to avoid obligations (the "cash" economy: ¶21-045)

- not reporting or under-reporting income

- underpayment of wages

- visa fraud and bypassing visa restrictions

- identity fraud

- ABN and GST fraud

- dealing in illegal drugs and tobacco

- sham contracting – presenting an employment relationship as a contracting arrangement

- illegal "phoenixing" – liquidating and re-forming a business to avoid tax obligations (¶20-000)

- excise evasion

- money laundering

- unregulated gambling

- dealing in counterfeit goods.

The government has established a Shadow Economy Program to coordinate action against these operators.
