# Overview ¶13-000

This chapter explains two of the most common GST-free supplies — food and health care. For a full list of GST-free supplies, see ¶1-160.

Being GST-free means that no GST is payable on the supply. However, the supplier can claim an input tax credit for any GST it paid on the things it acquired to make the GST-free supply. To take a simple example, assume that a hospital buys a computer to use in providing health care to patients. The health care is GST- free, so no GST applies to it. The hospital can, however, claim a credit for any GST it paid when it acquired the computer. For further details about claiming input tax credits, see ¶5-020.

It is important to note that it is the supply which is GST-free, not the supplier. For example, all of a hospital's supplies are not automatically GST-free, only those that are covered by exemptions.
