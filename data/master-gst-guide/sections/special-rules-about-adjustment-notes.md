# Special rules about adjustment notes ¶6-140

## Groups.

The representative member of a GST group is responsible for adjustments relating to a group member (¶17-020), but that group member must issue the relevant adjustment note.

## Branches.

If you make a taxable supply through a GST branch, you must show the branch registration number on any related adjustment note (s 54-50).

## Agents and insurance brokers.

For the special rules that apply, see ¶5-190.

*Refs: s 54-50)*
