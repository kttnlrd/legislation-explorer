# Other rules affecting bad debts ¶6-220

For the treatment of bad debts arising from gambling, see ¶16-010. • If real estate is sold under the margin scheme, the amount of any GST bad debt adjustment for the th supplier is limited to 1/11 of the margin (¶11-130). • Special rules apply to bad debt adjustments where there is a change of accounting basis (¶7-400).
