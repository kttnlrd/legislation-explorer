# Calculating luxury car tax ¶23-200

The luxury car tax (LCT) is calculated by applying the LCT rate to the excess (excluding the GST component) of the car's value over the LCT threshold (LCT Act ss 5-15, 7-15). Mathematically represented by the formula: LCT rate × 10/11 × (LCT value − LCT threshold) The LCT rate, as prescribed in the relevant Imposition Acts (¶23-000), is 33%. However, primary producers and tourist operators are entitled to refunds for certain cars that may reduce their effective rates to 25% (¶23-210).

LCT on sales To calculate the amount of LCT payable on a sale of a luxury car: Step 1:         Calculate the amount by which the LCT value of the car exceeds the LCT threshold.

Step 2:         Exclude GST from this amount by multiplying it by 10/11.

Step 3:         Apply the relevant rate of LCT to the GST-exclusive amount calculated in Step 2. This is the amount of LCT payable.

Step 4:         Add the amount of LCT payable to the GST-inclusive price of the car. This is the LCT-inclusive price of the car.

Example: Calculating LCT — general threshold In January 2025, Diamond Motors supplied a car (not a fuel-efficient car) to Robert Enterprises for the GST-inclusive price of $90,000 (the LCT value). The LCT payable on the purchase is calculated as follows: Step 1:         The price of the car in excess of the 2024–25 threshold is $9,433 (ie $90,000 − $80,567).

Step 2:         Exclusive of GST, this amount is $8,575 (ie $9,433 × 10/11).

Step 3:         Multiply the amount calculated in Step 2 by 33% to determine the LCT payable (ie 8,575 × 33% = $2,829).

Step 4:         Add this amount to the GST-inclusive price of the car to determine the total cost (ie $90,000 + $2,829 = $92,829).

Example: Calculating LCT — fuel-efficient threshold In January 2025, Diamond Motors supplied a fuel-efficient car to Aisha Enterprises for the GST-inclusive price of $94,000 (the LCT value). The LCT payable on the purchase is calculated as follows: Step 1:         The price of the car in excess of the 2024–25 fuel-efficient threshold is $2,613 (ie $94,000 − $91,387).

Step 2:         Exclusive of GST, this amount is $2,375 (ie $2,613 × 10/11).

Step 3:         Multiply the amount calculated in Step 2 by 33% to determine the LCT payable (ie $2,375 × 33% = $783).

Step 4:         Add this amount to the GST-inclusive price of the car to determine the total cost (ie $94,000 + $783 = $94,783).

If the car is sold for an "all-up" price including GST and LCT, these amounts can be worked out as follows: Example: Calculating LCT — all-up price Continuing the general threshold calculation above, except that the $90,000 price is an all-up amount including GST and LCT (but not stamp duty, registration, etc (¶23-150)). The GST and LCT components are calculated as follows:

- Take out the GST and LCT components (10% + 33% = 43%) of the amount of the price above the LCT threshold. The

  balance is therefore: ($90,000 − $80,567) × 100/143 = $6,596.

- Multiply this balance by the LCT rate to get the LCT payable: 33% × $6,596 = $2,176.

- The LCT value is $87,824 ($90,000 − $2,176).

- The GST is $7,984 (1/11 × $87,824).

This means that the all-up price of $90,000 includes GST of $7,984 and LCT of $2,176.

The amount of LCT is reduced by any LCT that applied to a previous sale or importation of the car (s 5-15(2), (3)). This means that LCT on the later sale only applies in the relatively unlikely event that the car has increased in value. Relevant documentation substantiating that the earlier amount had been paid should be kept.

LCT on importations The formula used to calculate the amount of LCT payable on an importation is the same as for a taxable sale of a luxury car. The difference lies in the calculation of the LCT value of the car. As noted at ¶23-150, this is calculated as the customs value of the car together with customs duty payable on the car and relevant freight and insurance costs (s 7-15).

Example: Calculating LCT on imports In January 2025, Robert imported a car (not a fuel-efficient car). The car has a customs value of $70,000. Relevant freight and insurance costs for the car are $2,000. The LCT payable on the purchase is calculated as follows: Step 1:         Calculate the customs duty payable: 15% of the customs value = $10,500.
