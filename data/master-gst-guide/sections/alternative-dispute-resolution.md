# Alternative dispute resolution ¶18-750

Apart from the normal objection and appeal processes, a range of alternative dispute resolution (ADR) approaches, including mediation, may be used to assist in reaching settlement.

The ATO has an "independent review process" for eligible small businesses with an annual turnover of less than $10 million that disagree with the ATO's audit position on a particular matter. This means that an ATO technical officer, not associated with the audit, will review the facts and technical merits of the audit position.

This will be completed before any assessment, or amended assessment, is issued to the taxpayer in relation to the audit and is intended to provide an "early and fair" resolution of the dispute. Full details of the process are on the ATO Small business independent review webpage.

Previously issued guidelines for ATO officers on what procedures must be followed when attempting to resolve or limit disputes by means of ADR are in Practice Statement PS LA 2013/3.

Other alternatives include making a complaint to the Tax Ombudsman (previously the Inspector-General of Taxation), or referring the matter to the Australian Small Business and Family Enterprise Ombudsman.
