# 62 Notice about reporting obligations to be given to travellers to

**Scope**

**(1)** This section applies to a written notice:

> **(a)** that relates to reporting obligations under this Part; and

> **(b)** the form and contents of which are specified in the AML/CTF Rules. **Notice to be given to travellers**

**(2)** If an aircraft or ship leaves a place outside  to travel to a place in  without stopping at any other place outside , the person in charge of the aircraft or ship must:

> **(a)** give a copy of the notice, or communicate the information contained in the notice in the manner prescribed by the AML/CTF Rules, to all persons travelling on the aircraft or ship (including members of the crew); or

> **(b)** cause a copy of the notice to be given, or cause to be communicated the information contained in the notice in the manner prescribed by the AML/CTF Rules, to all persons travelling on the aircraft or ship (including members of the crew). **Offence**

**(3)** A person commits an offence if:

> **(a)** the person is subject to a requirement under subsection (2); and

> **(b)** the person engages in conduct; and

> **(c)** the person's conduct breaches the requirement. Penalty: Penalty: 50 penalty units.

**(4)** An offence against subsection (3) is an offence of strict liability.
>

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code.
