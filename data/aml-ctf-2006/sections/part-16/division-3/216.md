# 216 Leave of absence of the AUSTRAC CEO


**(1)** The AUSTRAC CEO has the recreation leave entitlements that are determined by the Remuneration Tribunal.

**(2)** The Minister may grant the AUSTRAC CEO leave of absence, other than recreation leave, on the terms and conditions as to remuneration or otherwise that the Minister determines.
