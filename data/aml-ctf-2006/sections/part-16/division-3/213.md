# 213 Policy principles


**(1)** The Minister may give written policy principles to the AUSTRAC CEO about the performance of the AUSTRAC CEO's functions.

**(2)** The Minister must cause a copy of the policy principles to be tabled in each House of the Parliament within 15 sitting days of that House after the day on which they were given to the AUSTRAC CEO.

**(3)** The AUSTRAC CEO must comply with the policy principles (if any) when performing the AUSTRAC CEO's functions.

**(4)** Policy principles are not legislative instruments.
