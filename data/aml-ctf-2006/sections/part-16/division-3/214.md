# 214 Appointment of the AUSTRAC CEO etc.


**(1)**  The AUSTRAC CEO is to be appointed by the Minister by written instrument.

**(2)**  The AUSTRAC CEO is to be appointed on a full-time basis.

**(3)**  The AUSTRAC CEO holds office for the period specified in the instrument of appointment. The period must not exceed 5 years.
>

> **Note:** The AUSTRAC CEO may be reappointed: see section 33AA of the Acts Interpretation Act 1901.
