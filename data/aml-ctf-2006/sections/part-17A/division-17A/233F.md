# 233F Review by the Administrative Review Tribunal

An application may be made to the Administrative Review Tribunal for review of:

> **(a)**  a decision of the AUSTRAC CEO under subsection 233E(2); or

> **(b)**  a reviewable decision made by the AUSTRAC CEO personally.
