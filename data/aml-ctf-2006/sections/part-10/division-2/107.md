# 107 Transaction records to be retained

**Scope**

**(1)** This section applies if:

**(a)** a reporting entity makes a record of information relating to the provision of a designated service to a customer; and

**(b)** the record is not declared by the AML/CTF Rules to be exempt from this section. **Retention**

**(2)** The reporting entity must retain:

**(a)** the record; or

**(b)** a copy of the record; or

**(c)** an extract from the record showing the prescribed information; for 7 years after the making of the record. **Civil penalty**

**(3)** Subsection (2) is a civil penalty provision.
**Designated business groups**

**(4)** If:

**(a)** a reporting entity is a member of a designated business group; and

**(b)** such other conditions (if any) as are specified in the AML/CTF Rules are satisfied; the obligation imposed on the reporting entity by subsection (2) may be discharged by any other member of the group.
