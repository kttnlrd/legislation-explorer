# 249 Specification by class

To avoid doubt, a reference in this Act to a class or kind of matter or thing does not, by implication, affect the application of:
(a)	subsection 13(3) of the Legislation Act 2003; or
(b)	subsection 33(3AB) of the Acts Interpretation Act 1901.
