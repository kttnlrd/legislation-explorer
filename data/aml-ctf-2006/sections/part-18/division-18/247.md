# 247 General exemptions


**(1)** This Act does not apply to a designated service that is of a kind specified in the AML/CTF Rules.

**(2)** The AML/CTF Rules may provide that a specified provision of this Act does not apply to a designated service that is of a kind specified in the AML/CTF Rules.

**(3)** This Act does not apply to a designated service that is provided in circumstances specified in the AML/CTF Rules.

**(4)** The AML/CTF Rules may provide that a specified provision of this Act does not apply to a designated service that is provided in circumstances specified in the AML/CTF Rules.
