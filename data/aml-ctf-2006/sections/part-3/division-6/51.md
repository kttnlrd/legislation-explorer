# 51 Division 400 and Chapter 5 of the Criminal Code

If a person, or an officer, employee or agent of a person, communicates or gives information under section 41, 43, 45, 49, 49B or 49C, the person, officer, employee or agent is taken, for the purposes of Division 400 and Chapter 5 of the Criminal Code, not to have been in possession of that information at any time.
