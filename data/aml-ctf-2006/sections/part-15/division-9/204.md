# 204 Breaching a notice requirement

A person commits an offence if:

**(a)** the person is subject to a requirement under subsection 202(4); and

**(b)** the person engages in conduct; and

**(c)** the person's conduct breaches the requirement. Penalty: Penalty: Imprisonment for 6 months or 30 penalty units, or both.
