# 84-5  Offshore supplies that are taxable supplies under this Subdivision
<a id="s84-5-1"></a>
**(1)**  A supply is a taxable supply (except to the extent that it is [*GST-free*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-free) or [*input taxed*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-input-taxed)) if:
> <a id="s84-5-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the supply is for *consideration; and
> <a id="s84-5-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the *recipient of the supply is [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered), or [*required to be registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-required-to-be-registered); and
> <a id="s84-5-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the supply is covered by the third column of this table. > **(b)**  the recipient does not acquire the thing supplied solely for a *creditable purpose. (1B) However, items 3 and 5 of the table in subsection (1) only cover a supply to the extent that it is *connected with the indirect tax zone solely because of:
> <a id="s84-5-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  for item 3—paragraph 9-25(5)(d); or
> <a id="s84-5-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  for item 5—Subdivision 84-C.
<a id="s84-5-2"></a>
**(2)**  For the purposes of this section, in determining whether the *recipient is [*required to be registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-required-to-be-registered), what would be the *value of such supplies (if they were *taxable supplies) is to be counted towards the recipient’s [*GST*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst) turnover.
<a id="s84-5-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section has effect despite section 9-5 (which is about what is a taxable supply).
