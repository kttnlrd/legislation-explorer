# 84-95  What this Subdivision is about

A supplier is treated in some situations to be making a supply to an entity that is not a consumer, or not an Australian consumer.

> **Note:** Whether the recipient of a supply of an intangible is an Australian consumer is one of the tests for whether the supply is connected with the indirect tax zone: see subsection 9-25(5).

> **Note:** Whether the recipient of an offshore supply of low value goods is a consumer is one of the tests for whether the supply is connected with the indirect tax zone: see subsection 84-75(1).

> **Note:** Supplies affected by this Subdivision may be supplies that are taxable supplies, and reverse charged, under Subdivision 84-A.
