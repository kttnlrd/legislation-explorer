# 84-100  When entities are treated as not being Australian consumers
<a id="s84-100-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [*GST law*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-law) applies in relation to you as if another entity was not an [*Australian consumer*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-australian-consumer) of a supply if:
> <a id="s84-100-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you take reasonable steps to obtain information about whether or not the other entity is an Australian consumer of the supply; and
> <a id="s84-100-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  after taking those steps, you reasonably believe that the other entity is not an Australian consumer of the supply.
<a id="s84-100-2"></a>
**(2)**  Without limiting subsection (1), the [*GST law*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-law) applies in relation to you as if another entity was not an [*Australian consumer*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-australian-consumer) of a supply if:
> <a id="s84-100-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  your usual business systems and processes provide you with a reasonable basis for forming a reasonable belief about whether the other entity is an Australian consumer of the supply; and
> <a id="s84-100-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you reasonably believe that the other entity is not an Australian consumer of the supply.
<a id="s84-100-3"></a>
**(3)**  For the purposes of subsections (1) and (2), to the extent that your belief that the other entity is not an [*Australian consumer*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-australian-consumer) of the supply is based on the other entity being [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered), your belief is reasonable only if:
> <a id="s84-100-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the other entity’s [*ABN*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-abn), or the other identifying information prescribed under subsection (4) relating to the other entity, has been disclosed to you; and
> <a id="s84-100-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the other entity has provided to you a declaration or information that indicates that the other entity is registered.
<a id="s84-100-4"></a>
**(4)**  The Commissioner may, by legislative instrument, prescribe identifying information for the purposes of paragraph (3)(a).
