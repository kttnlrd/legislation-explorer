# 84-85  Exception—when there is also a taxable importation
<a id="s84-85-1"></a>
**(1)**  Subsection (2) applies to an [*offshore supply of low value goods*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-offshore-supply-of-low-value-goods) you made to the extent that:
> <a id="s84-85-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the supply is, apart from this section, a *supplier-taxed offshore supply of low value goods; and
> <a id="s84-85-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an importation of the goods was a [*taxable importation*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-importation).
> **Note:** This section applies if section 42-15 has not applied to treat the importation as a non-taxable importation.
<a id="s84-85-2"></a>
**(2)**  The supply is treated as if it were not a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) if:
> <a id="s84-85-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  to the extent (if any) that you have *passed on the GST on the supply to another entity—you reimburse the other entity for the passed on GST; and
> <a id="s84-85-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an entity provides to you a declaration or information that indicates that GST has been paid on the [*taxable importation*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-importation).
<a id="s84-85-3"></a>
**(3)**  A supply of goods is a supplier-taxed offshore supply of low value goods if the supply is:
> <a id="s84-85-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an [*offshore supply of low value goods*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-offshore-supply-of-low-value-goods); and
> <a id="s84-85-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a taxable supply solely under section 9-5; and
> <a id="s84-85-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  *connected with the indirect tax zone solely because of this Subdivision.
