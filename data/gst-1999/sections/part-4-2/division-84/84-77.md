# 84-77  Meaning of offshore supply of low value goods
Supplies of low value goods delivered etc. into the indirect tax zone by suppliers
<a id="s84-77-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [*supply of low value goods*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-supply-of-low-value-goods) is an offshore supply of low value goods if:
> <a id="s84-77-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the supply involves the goods being brought to the indirect tax zone; and
> <a id="s84-77-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the supplier delivers the goods into the indirect tax zone, or procures, arranges or facilitates the delivery of the goods into the indirect tax zone. Supplies of low value goods made through an electronic distribution platform
<a id="s84-77-2"></a>
**(2)**  Without limiting subsection (1), a [*supply of low value goods*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-supply-of-low-value-goods) is an offshore supply of low value goods if:
> <a id="s84-77-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the supply involves the goods being brought to the indirect tax zone; and
> <a id="s84-77-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the supply is made through an [*electronic distribution platform*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-electronic-distribution-platform); and
> <a id="s84-77-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the operator of the platform delivers the goods into the indirect tax zone, or procures, arranges or facilitates the delivery of the goods into the indirect tax zone. Supplies of low value goods delivered etc. into the indirect tax zone by redeliverers
<a id="s84-77-3"></a>
**(3)**  A [*supply of low value goods*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-supply-of-low-value-goods) is an offshore supply of low value goods if:
> <a id="s84-77-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the supply involves the goods being delivered to a place outside the indirect tax zone; and
> <a id="s84-77-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a *redeliverer delivers the goods into the indirect tax zone, or procures, arranges or facilitates the delivery of the goods into the indirect tax zone.
<a id="s84-77-4"></a>
**(4)**  An entity is a redeliverer in relation to a [*supply of low value goods*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-supply-of-low-value-goods) if, as a result of an arrangement with the *recipient of the supply (or another entity acting on the recipient’s behalf), the entity, in the course of *carrying on an enterprise:
> <a id="s84-77-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  delivers the goods into the indirect tax zone, or procures, arranges or facilitates the delivery of the goods into the indirect tax zone; and
> <a id="s84-77-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  does one or more of the following:
> > <a id="s84-77-4-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  provides use of an address outside the indirect tax zone to which the goods are delivered;
> > <a id="s84-77-4-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  procures, arranges or facilitates use of an address outside the indirect tax zone to which the goods are delivered;
> > <a id="s84-77-4-b-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  purchases the goods;
> > <a id="s84-77-4-b-iv"></a>
> > **(iv)**  procures, arranges or facilitates purchase of the goods.
<a id="s84-77-5"></a>
**(5)**  Disregard section 84-81 in applying this section.
