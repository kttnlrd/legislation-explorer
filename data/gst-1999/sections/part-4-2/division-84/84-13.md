# 84-13  The amount of input tax credits relating to offshore supplies


<a id="s84-13-1"></a>
**(1)** The amount of the input tax credit for a *creditable acquisition that relates to a supply that is a *taxable supply because of section 84-5 is as follows:

**Full input ´ Extent of creditable ´ Extent of**

| tax credit | purpose | consideration |
| --- | --- | --- |
 where: extent of consideration is:

<a id="s84-13-1-a"></a>
**(a)** if the *recipient is the supplier's *associate and the supply is without *consideration—100%; or

<a id="s84-13-1-b"></a>
**(b)** in any other case—the extent to which you provide, or are liable to provide, the consideration for the acquisition, expressed as a percentage of the total consideration for the acquisition. extent of creditable purpose is the extent to which the *creditable acquisition is for a *creditable purpose, expressed as a percentage of the total purpose of the acquisition. full input tax credit is 11/10 of what would have been the amount of the input tax credit for the acquisition if:

<a id="s84-13-1-a-2"></a>
**(a)** the supply had been or is a *taxable supply otherwise than because of section 84-5; and

<a id="s84-13-1-b-2"></a>
**(b)** the acquisition had been made solely for a creditable purpose; and

<a id="s84-13-1-c"></a>
**(c)** you had provided, or had been liable to provide, all of the consideration for the acquisition. (1A) However, if:

<a id="s84-13-1-a-3"></a>
**(a)** an *annual apportionment election that you have made has effect at the end of the tax period to which the input tax credit is attributable; and

<a id="s84-13-1-b-3"></a>
**(b)** the acquisition is not an acquisition of a kind specified in the regulations made for the purposes of paragraph 131-40(1)(b); the amount of the input tax credit on the acquisition is worked out under section 131-40 as if full input tax credit had the same meaning in subsection 131-40(2) as it has in subsection (1) of this section.

<a id="s84-13-2"></a>
**(2)** This section has effect despite:

<a id="s84-13-2-a"></a>
**(a)** sections 11-25 and 11-30 (which are about the amount of input tax credits for creditable acquisitions); and

<a id="s84-13-2-b"></a>
**(b)** section 72-45 (which is about the amount of input tax credits on an acquisition from an associate without consideration).
