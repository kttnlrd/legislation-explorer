# 105-15  GST returns


<a id="s105-15-1"></a>
**(1)** If, during a month:

<a id="s105-15-1-a"></a>
**(a)** you make any *taxable supplies under section 105-5; or

<a id="s105-15-1-b"></a>
**(b)** you have any *increasing adjustments that arise in relation to any such supplies (whether made in that month or a previous month); and you are not *registered or *required to be registered during that month, you must give to the Commissioner a *GST return, within 21 days after the end of the month, relating to those supplies you made in that month and those adjustments.

<a id="s105-15-3"></a>
**(3)** This section has effect despite sections 31-5 and 31-10 (which are about giving GST returns).
