# 105-10  Net amounts
<a id="s105-10-1"></a>
**(1)**  If you are not [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered) or [*required to be registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-required-to-be-registered), you do not have a *net amount under Part 2-4 merely because you make a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) under section 105-5.
<a id="s105-10-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section does not prevent an [*adjustment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-adjustment) arising that relates to such a supply, but you cannot have a [*decreasing adjustment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-decreasing-adjustment) unless you are [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered) or [*required to be registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-required-to-be-registered).
<a id="s105-10-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section has effect despite Division 17 (which is about net amounts and adjustments).
