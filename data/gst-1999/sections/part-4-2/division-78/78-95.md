# 78-95  GST on premiums etc. under statutory compensation schemes is exclusive of stamp duty
<a id="s78-95-1"></a>
**(1)**  The *value of a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) of membership of, or participation in, a [*statutory compensation scheme*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-statutory-compensation-scheme) is worked out as if the *price of the supply were reduced by the amount of any stamp duty payable under a [*State law*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-state-law) or [*Territory law*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-territory-law) in respect of the supply.
<a id="s78-95-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite section 9-75 (which is about the value of taxable supplies).
