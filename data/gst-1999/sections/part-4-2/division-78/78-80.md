# 78-80  Net amounts
<a id="s78-80-1"></a>
**(1)**  If an entity insured under an *insurance policy is not [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered) or [*required to be registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-required-to-be-registered), it does not have a *net amount under Part 2-4 merely because it makes a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) under section 78-50.
<a id="s78-80-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section does not prevent an [*adjustment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-adjustment) arising that relates to such a supply, but the entity cannot have a [*decreasing adjustment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-decreasing-adjustment) unless it is [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered) or [*required to be registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-required-to-be-registered).
<a id="s78-80-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section has effect despite Division 17 (which is about net amounts and adjustments).
