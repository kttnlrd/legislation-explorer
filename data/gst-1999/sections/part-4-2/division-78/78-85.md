# 78-85  GST returns


<a id="s78-85-1"></a>
**(1)**  If, during a month:

> <a id="s78-85-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an entity makes any *taxable supplies under section 78-50; or

> <a id="s78-85-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an entity has any *increasing adjustments that arise in relation to any such supplies (whether made in that month or a previous month); and the entity is not *registered or *required to be registered during that month, it must give to the Commissioner a *GST return, within 21 days after the end of the month, relating to those supplies it made in that month and those adjustments.

<a id="s78-85-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section has effect despite sections 31-5 and 31-10 (which are about giving GST returns).
