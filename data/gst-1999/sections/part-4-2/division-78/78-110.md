# 78-110  Effect of judgments and court orders
If:
> <a id="s78-110-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an entity makes one or more of the following:
> > <a id="s78-110-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  a payment of [*money*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-money);
> > <a id="s78-110-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a payment of [*digital currency*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-digital-currency);
> > <a id="s78-110-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  a supply; in compliance with a judgment or order of a court relating to:
> > <a id="s78-110-iv"></a>
> > **(iv)**  a claim under an *insurance policy; or
> > <a id="s78-110-v"></a>
> > **(v)**  a claim by an insurer in exercising rights of subrogation in respect of an insurance policy; or
> > <a id="s78-110-vi"></a>
> > **(vi)**  a claim for compensation under a [*statutory compensation scheme*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-statutory-compensation-scheme); and
> <a id="s78-110-b"></a>
> **(b)**  had the payment or supply been made in the absence of such a judgment or order, it would have been a payment or supply made in settlement of the claim; the payment or supply is treated as having been made in settlement of the claim.
