# 86-5  “Reverse charge” on supplies of goods consisting of valuable metal
<a id="s86-5-1"></a>
**(1)**  The GST on a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) of goods is payable by the *recipient of the supply, and is not payable by the supplier, if:
> <a id="s86-5-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the goods consist wholly or partly of [*valuable metal*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-valuable-metal); and
> <a id="s86-5-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the recipient is [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered) or [*required to be registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-required-to-be-registered); and
> <a id="s86-5-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  either:
> > <a id="s86-5-1-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  at the time of the supply, the market value of the goods does not exceed the [*valuable metal threshold*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-valuable-metal-threshold); or
> > <a id="s86-5-1-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the supplier and the recipient agree, in writing, that the GST on the supply be payable by the recipient.
<a id="s86-5-2"></a>
**(2)**  Subsection (1) does not apply to a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) of goods if the supply is in a class of supplies determined under subsection (3). Determination
<a id="s86-5-3"></a>
**(3)**  For the purposes of subsection (2), the Commissioner may, by legislative instrument, determine that subsection (1) does not apply to a specified class of supplies.
<a id="s86-5-4"></a>
**(4)**  In making a determination under subsection (3), the Commissioner may have regard to the following:
> <a id="s86-5-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the likelihood that *recipients and suppliers of that class of supply will otherwise comply with their obligations under the [*GST law*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-law), and the risk of GST not being paid on *taxable supplies in that class if recipients do not pay the GST;
> <a id="s86-5-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the costs for recipients and suppliers of that class of supplies to comply with subsection (1);
> <a id="s86-5-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  any other relevant matters. Effect of this section on other sections
<a id="s86-5-5"></a>
**(5)**  This section has effect despite sections 9-40 (which is about liability for the GST), 48-40, 51-30 and 83-5 (which are about who is liable for GST).
