# 86-15  Recipients who are members of GST groups
<a id="s86-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  If section 86-5 applies to a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) but the *recipient of the supply is a *member of a [*GST group*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-group), the GST on the supply:
> <a id="s86-15-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  is payable by the [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) member; and
> <a id="s86-15-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  is not payable by the member (unless the member is the representative member).
<a id="s86-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite sections 48-40, 51-30 and 86-5 (which are about who is liable for GST).
