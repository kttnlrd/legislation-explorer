# 80-35  Adjustment events relating to managing operator’s payment or supply
> <a id="s80-35-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [*contributing operator’s payment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-contributing-operators-payment) were *consideration for a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) made by the managing operator; and
> <a id="s80-35-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the adjustment were the GST payable on the taxable supply; and
> <a id="s80-35-c"></a>
> **(c)**  any changes made to those payments were a change in the consideration for the supply.
