# 80-80  Meaning of hybrid settlement sharing arrangement etc.
Meaning of hybrid settlement sharing arrangement
<a id="s80-80-1"></a>
**(1)**  A hybrid settlement sharing arrangement is an arrangement:
> <a id="s80-80-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  that relates to an accident or other incident or 2 or more related accidents or other incidents; and
> <a id="s80-80-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  to which the parties are:
> > <a id="s80-80-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  an entity that is the [*managing operator*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-managing-operator) of a *nominal defendant settlement sharing arrangement, or entities that are managing operators of nominal defendant settlement sharing arrangements, that relate to the accidents or incidents; and
> > <a id="s80-80-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  an *operator or operators of a *compulsory third party scheme or schemes who have issued *insurance policies to persons involved in the accidents or incidents; and
> <a id="s80-80-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  under which:
> > <a id="s80-80-1-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  one party (the managing operator) is to make one or more payments or supplies in settlement of a claim, under the compulsory third party scheme or one of the compulsory third party schemes involved, relating to the accidents or incidents; and
> > <a id="s80-80-1-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  each other party (a contributing operator) is to make a payment to the [*managing operator*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-managing-operator) in respect of that operator settling the claim. Meaning of managing operator’s payment or supply
<a id="s80-80-2"></a>
**(2)**  If a payment or supply mentioned in subparagraph (1)(c)(i) is not a [*CTP ancillary payment or supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-ctp-ancillary-payment-or-supply), it is a managing operator’s payment or supply. Meaning of contributing operator’s payment
<a id="s80-80-3"></a>
**(3)**  A payment mentioned in subparagraph (1)(c)(ii), to the extent that it is not a fee to the [*managing operator*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-managing-operator) for managing the process of making settlements under the arrangement, is a contributing operator’s payment.
