# 80-45  Nominal defendant settlement sharing arrangements to which this Subdivision applies
This Subdivision applies to a *nominal defendant settlement sharing arrangement if its [*managing operator*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-managing-operator) is not a party to a [*hybrid settlement sharing arrangement*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-hybrid-settlement-sharing-arrangement) relating to the same accidents or incidents.
