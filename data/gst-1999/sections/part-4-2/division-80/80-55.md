# 80-55  Effect of contributing operator’s payment
<a id="s80-55-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [*contributing operator’s payment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-contributing-operators-payment) is not treated as *consideration for a supply by the [*managing operator*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-managing-operator), or for an acquisition by the [*contributing operator*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-contributing-operator).
<a id="s80-55-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite section 9-15 (which is about consideration) and section 11-5 (which is about what is a creditable acquisition).
