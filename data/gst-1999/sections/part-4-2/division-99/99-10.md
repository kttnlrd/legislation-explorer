# 99-10  Attributing the GST relating to deposits that are forfeited etc.
<a id="s99-10-1"></a>
**(1)**  The GST payable by you on a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) for which the *consideration is a deposit that was held as security for the performance of an obligation is attributable to the tax period during which the deposit:
> <a id="s99-10-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  is forfeited because of a failure to perform the obligation; or
> <a id="s99-10-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  is applied as all or part of the consideration for a supply.
<a id="s99-10-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite section 29-5 (which is about attributing GST for taxable supplies).
