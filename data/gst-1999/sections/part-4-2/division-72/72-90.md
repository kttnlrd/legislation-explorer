# 72-90  GST branches

This Division applies to a *GST branch of an entity as if the GST branch were an *associate of:

> <a id="s72-90-a"></a>
> **(a)** that entity; and

> <a id="s72-90-b"></a>
> **(b)** every other GST branch of that entity; and

> <a id="s72-90-c"></a>
> **(c)** any other associate of that entity.
