# 72-15  Attributing the GST to tax periods
<a id="s72-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  The tax period to which the GST on a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) to your [*associate*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-associate) without *consideration is attributable is the tax period in which the supply first becomes a supply that is *connected with the indirect tax zone.
<a id="s72-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite section 29-5 (which is about attributing GST on taxable supplies).
