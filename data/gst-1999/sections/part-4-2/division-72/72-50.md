# 72-50  Attributing the input tax credit to tax periods
<a id="s72-50-1"></a>
**(1)**  The tax period to which the input tax credit for a [*creditable acquisition*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-acquisition) from your [*associate*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-associate) without *consideration is attributable is the tax period in which the supply to which the acquisition relates first becomes a supply that is *connected with the indirect tax zone.
<a id="s72-50-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite section 29-10 (which is about attributing input tax credits for creditable acquisitions).
