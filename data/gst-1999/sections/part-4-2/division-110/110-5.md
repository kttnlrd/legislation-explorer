# 110-5  Transfers of tax losses and net capital losses
<a id="s110-5-1"></a>
**(1)**  A supply is not a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) if the supply is:
> <a id="s110-5-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the transfer of a [*tax loss*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-tax-loss) in accordance with Subdivision 170-A of the [*ITAA 1997*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-itaa-1997); or
> <a id="s110-5-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the transfer of a [*net capital loss*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-net-capital-loss) in accordance with Subdivision 170-B of the ITAA 1997.
<a id="s110-5-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite section 9-5 (which is about what are taxable supplies).
