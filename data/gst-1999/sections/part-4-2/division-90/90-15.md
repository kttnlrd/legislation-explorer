# 90-15  Acquisitions not creditable—amalgamated company registered or required to be registered
<a id="s90-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  An acquisition made by an *amalgamated company from an *amalgamating company in the course of [*amalgamation*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-amalgamation) is not a [*creditable acquisition*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-acquisition) if, immediately after the amalgamation, the amalgamated company is [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered) or [*required to be registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-required-to-be-registered).
<a id="s90-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite section 11-5 (which is about what is a creditable acquisition).
