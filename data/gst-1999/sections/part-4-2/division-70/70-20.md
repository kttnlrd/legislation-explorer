# 70-20  Extent of creditable purpose


<a id="s70-20-1"></a>
**(1)**  If:

> <a id="s70-20-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a *reduced credit acquisition is a *creditable acquisition; and

> <a id="s70-20-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  it is not wholly for a *creditable purpose because of this Division; it is *partly creditable.
