# 81-15  Other fees and charges that do not constitute consideration
The regulations may provide that the payment of a prescribed [*Australian fee or charge*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-australian-fee-or-charge), or of an Australian fee or charge of a prescribed kind, or the discharging of a liability to make such a payment, is not the provision of *consideration.
