# 111-30  Reimbursements etc. of former or future employees etc.

This Division applies in relation to:

> <a id="s111-30-a"></a>
> **(a)** reimbursements, of a kind referred to in paragraph 111-5(1)(ab) or (ac), of former employees and future employees, and of the *associates of former employees and future employees; and

> <a id="s111-30-b"></a>
> **(b)** payments, of a kind referred to in paragraph 111-25(b), that you make or are liable to make on behalf of former employees and future employees, and of the *associates of former employees and future employees; in the same way that this Division applies to such reimbursements of, and such payments that you make or are liable to make to, employees and their associates.
