# 111-15  Tax invoices relating to reimbursements
For the purposes of subsection 29-10(3), you are taken to hold a [*tax invoice*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-tax-invoice) for a [*creditable acquisition*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-acquisition) the *consideration for which is a reimbursement to which section 111-5 applies if you hold a tax invoice for the [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) referred to in subsection 111-5(3).
