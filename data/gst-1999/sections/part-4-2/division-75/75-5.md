# 75-5  Applying the margin scheme
<a id="s75-5-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [*margin scheme*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-margin-scheme) applies in working out the amount of GST on a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) of [*real property*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-real-property) that you make by:
> <a id="s75-5-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  selling a freehold interest in land; or
> <a id="s75-5-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  selling a [*stratum unit*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-stratum-unit); or
> <a id="s75-5-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  granting or selling a [*long-term lease*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-long-term-lease); if you and the *recipient of the supply have agreed in writing that the margin scheme is to apply. (1A) The agreement must be made:
> <a id="s75-5-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  on or before the making of the supply; or
> <a id="s75-5-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  within such further period as the Commissioner allows.
> **Note:** Refusing to allow, or allowing, a further period within which to make an agreement is a reviewable GST decision (see Subdivision 110-F in Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953). (1B) A supply that you make to your [*associate*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-associate) is taken for the purposes of subsection (1) to be a sale to your associate whether or not the supply is for *consideration.
<a id="s75-5-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, the [*margin scheme*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-margin-scheme) does not apply if you acquired the entire freehold interest, [*stratum unit*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-stratum-unit) or [*long-term lease*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-long-term-lease) through a supply that was [*ineligible for the margin scheme*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-ineligible-for-the-margin-scheme).
> **Note:** If you acquired part of the interest, unit or lease through a supply that was ineligible for the margin scheme, you may have an increasing adjustment: see section 75-22.
<a id="s75-5-3"></a>
**(3)**  A supply is ineligible for the margin scheme if:
> <a id="s75-5-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it is a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) on which the GST was worked out without applying the [*margin scheme*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-margin-scheme); or
> <a id="s75-5-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  it is a supply of a thing you acquired by *inheriting it from a deceased person, and the deceased person had acquired all of it through a supply that was ineligible for the margin scheme; or
> <a id="s75-5-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  it is a supply in relation to which all of the following apply:
> > <a id="s75-5-3-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  you were a *member of a [*GST group*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-group) at the time you acquired the interest, unit or lease in question;
> > <a id="s75-5-3-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the entity from whom you acquired it was a member of the GST group at that time;
> > <a id="s75-5-3-c-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  the last supply of the interest, unit or lease by an entity who was not (at the time of that supply) a member of the GST group to an entity who was (at that time) such a member was a supply that was ineligible for the margin scheme; or
> <a id="s75-5-3-d"></a>
> **(d)**  it is a supply in relation to which both of the following apply:
> > <a id="s75-5-3-d-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  you acquired the interest, unit or lease from the *joint venture operator of a [*GST joint venture*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-joint-venture) at a time when you were a *participant in the joint venture;
> > <a id="s75-5-3-d-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the joint venture operator had acquired the interest, unit or lease through a supply that was ineligible for the margin scheme; or
> <a id="s75-5-3-e"></a>
> **(e)**  it is a supply in relation to which all of the following apply:
> > <a id="s75-5-3-e-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  you acquired the interest, unit or lease from an entity as, or as part of, a [*supply of a going concern*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-supply-of-a-going-concern) to you that was [*GST-free*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-free) under Subdivision 38-J;
> > <a id="s75-5-3-e-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the entity was [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered) or [*required to be registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-required-to-be-registered), at the time of the acquisition;
> > <a id="s75-5-3-e-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  the entity had acquired the entire interest, unit or lease through a taxable supply on which the GST was worked out without applying the margin scheme; or
> <a id="s75-5-3-f"></a>
> **(f)**  it is a supply in relation to which all of the following apply:
> > <a id="s75-5-3-f-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  you acquired the interest, unit or lease from an entity as, or as part of, a supply to you that was GST-free under Subdivision 38-O;
> > <a id="s75-5-3-f-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the entity was registered or required to be registered, at the time of the acquisition;
> > <a id="s75-5-3-f-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  the entity had acquired the entire interest, unit or lease through a taxable supply on which the GST was worked out without applying the margin scheme; or
> <a id="s75-5-3-g"></a>
> **(g)**  it is a supply in relation to which all of the following apply:
> > <a id="s75-5-3-g-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  you acquired the interest, unit or lease from an entity who was your [*associate*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-associate), and who was registered or required to be registered, at the time of the acquisition;
> > <a id="s75-5-3-g-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the acquisition from your associate was without *consideration;
> > <a id="s75-5-3-g-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  the supply by your associate was not a taxable supply;
> > <a id="s75-5-3-g-iv"></a>
> > **(iv)**  your associate made the supply in the course or furtherance of an [*enterprise*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-enterprise) that your associate *carried on;
> > <a id="s75-5-3-g-v"></a>
> > **(v)**  your associate had acquired the entire interest, unit or lease through a taxable supply on which the GST was worked out without applying the margin scheme. (3A) Subparagraphs (3)(g)(iii) and (iv) do not apply if the acquisition from your [*associate*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-associate) was not by means of a supply by your associate.
<a id="s75-5-4"></a>
**(4)**  A reference in paragraph (3)(b), (c) or (d) to a supply that was ineligible for the margin scheme is a reference to a supply:
> <a id="s75-5-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  that was ineligible for the margin scheme because of one or more previous applications of subsection (3); or
> <a id="s75-5-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  that would have been ineligible for the margin scheme for that reason if subsection (3) had been in force at all relevant times.
