# 75-30  Tax invoices not required for supplies of real property under the margin scheme
<a id="s75-30-1"></a>
**(1)**  You are not required to issue a [*tax invoice*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-tax-invoice) for a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) that you make that is solely a supply of [*real property*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-real-property) under the [*margin scheme*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-margin-scheme).
<a id="s75-30-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite section 29-70 (which is about the requirement to issue tax invoices).
