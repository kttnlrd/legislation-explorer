# 75-25  Adjustments relating to bad debts
<a id="s75-25-1"></a>
**(1)**  If:
> <a id="s75-25-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you have an [*adjustment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-adjustment) under Division 21 relating to a supply that you made that is a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) of [*real property*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-real-property) under the [*margin scheme*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-margin-scheme); and
> <a id="s75-25-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the amount of the adjustment would (apart from this section) exceed 1/11 of the *margin for the supply; the amount of the adjustment is 1/11 of the margin for the supply.
<a id="s75-25-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite sections 21-5 and 21-10 (which are about adjustments for writing off and recovering suppliers’ bad debts).
