# 79-35  Meaning of CTP compensation or ancillary payment or supply etc.
Meaning of CTP compensation or ancillary payment or supply
<a id="s79-35-1"></a>
**(1)**  A payment or supply is a CTP compensation or ancillary payment or supply if it is a [*CTP compensation payment or supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-ctp-compensation-payment-or-supply) or a [*CTP ancillary payment or supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-ctp-ancillary-payment-or-supply). Meaning of CTP compensation payment or supply
<a id="s79-35-2"></a>
**(2)**  A payment or supply is a CTP compensation payment or supply if
> <a id="s79-35-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it is a payment or supply made under a *compulsory third party scheme; and
> <a id="s79-35-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  it is a payment or supply made in settlement of a claim for compensation under the scheme; and
> <a id="s79-35-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  it is not the case that the *operator making the payment or supply was required to do so by law because of the bankruptcy or insolvency of another operator who is an insurer; and
> <a id="s79-35-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  Division 78 does not apply in relation to the payment or supply; and
> <a id="s79-35-2-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the payment or supply is not a [*CTP dual premium or election payment or supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-ctp-dual-premium-or-election-payment-or-supply) or a [*CTP hybrid payment or supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-ctp-hybrid-payment-or-supply). Meaning of CTP ancillary payment or supply
<a id="s79-35-3"></a>
**(3)**  A payment or supply is a CTP ancillary payment or supply if:
> <a id="s79-35-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the payment or supply is made under a *compulsory third party scheme; and
> <a id="s79-35-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the payment or supply is of a kind specified in the regulations; and
> <a id="s79-35-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  it is not the case that the *operator making the payment or supply was required to do so by law because of the bankruptcy or insolvency of another operator who is an insurer; and
> <a id="s79-35-3-d"></a>
> **(d)**  Division 78 does not apply in relation to the payment or supply; and
> <a id="s79-35-3-e"></a>
> **(e)**  the payment or supply is not a[*CTP dual premium or election payment or supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-ctp-dual-premium-or-election-payment-or-supply) or a [*CTP hybrid payment or supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-ctp-hybrid-payment-or-supply); and
> <a id="s79-35-3-f"></a>
> **(f)**  the payment or supply is not made in settlement of a claim for compensation under the scheme; and
> <a id="s79-35-3-g"></a>
> **(g)**  the payment or supply is not *consideration for a [*creditable acquisition*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-acquisition).
