# 79-60  Effect of settlements and payments under compulsory third party schemes
<a id="s79-60-1"></a>
**(1)**  If an [*operator of a *compulsory third party scheme*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-operator-of-a-compulsory-third-party-scheme) makes a payment under the scheme, it is not treated as *consideration:
> <a id="s79-60-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  for an acquisition made by the operator; or
> <a id="s79-60-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  for a supply made to the operator by the entity to whom the payment was made; to the extent that the payment is a *CTP compensation or ancillary payment or supply.
<a id="s79-60-2"></a>
**(2)**  If an [*operator of a *compulsory third party scheme*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-operator-of-a-compulsory-third-party-scheme) makes a supply under the scheme:
> <a id="s79-60-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it is not a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply); and
> <a id="s79-60-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  it is not treated as *consideration for an acquisition made by the operator; and
> <a id="s79-60-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  it is not treated as *consideration for a supply made to the operator by the entity to whom the supply was made; to the extent that the supply is a *CTP compensation or ancillary payment or supply.
<a id="s79-60-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section has effect despite section 9-5 (which is about what are taxable supplies), section 9-15 (which is about consideration) and section 11-5 (which is about what is a creditable acquisition).
