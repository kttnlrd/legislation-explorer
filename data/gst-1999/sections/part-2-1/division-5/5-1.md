# 5-1  What this Chapter is about
This Chapter sets out the basic rules for the GST. In particular, these rules will tell you: •    where liability for GST arises; •    where entitlements to input tax credits arise; •    how the amounts of GST and input tax credits are combined to work out the amount payable by you or to you; •    when and how that amount is to be paid.
