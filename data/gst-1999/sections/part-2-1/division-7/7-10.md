# 7-10  Tax periods
Every entity that is [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered), or [*required to be registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-required-to-be-registered), has tax periods applying to it. For registration, see Part 2-5. For tax periods, see Part 2-6.
