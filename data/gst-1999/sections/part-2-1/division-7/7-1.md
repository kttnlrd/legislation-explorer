# 7-1  GST and input tax credits
<a id="s7-1-1"></a>
**(1)**  GST is payable on *taxable supplies and [*taxable importations*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-importation).
<a id="s7-1-2"></a>
**(2)**  Entitlements to input tax credits arise on [*creditable acquisitions*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-acquisition) and [*creditable importations*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-importation). For taxable supplies and creditable acquisitions, see Part 2-2. For taxable importations and creditable importations, see Part 2-3.
