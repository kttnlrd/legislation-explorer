# 7-1  GST and input tax credits


<a id="s7-1-1"></a>
**(1)** GST is payable on *taxable supplies and *taxable importations.

<a id="s7-1-2"></a>
**(2)** Entitlements to input tax credits arise on *creditable acquisitions and *creditable importations. For taxable supplies and creditable acquisitions, see Part 2-2. For taxable importations and creditable importations, see Part 2-3.
