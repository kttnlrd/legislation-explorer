# 158-5  Treat as not accounting on a cash basis


<a id="s158-5-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies if you *account on a cash basis.

<a id="s158-5-2"></a>
**(2)** This Act and the regulations apply in relation to:

<a id="s158-5-2-a"></a>
**(a)** an acquisition you make under a *hire purchase agreement; or

<a id="s158-5-2-b"></a>
**(b)** an input tax credit to which you are entitled, or an *adjustment you have, under subsection 58-10(1) for an acquisition made under a hire purchase agreement; as if you do not *account on a cash basis.
