# 156-20  Application of Division 129 to progressive or periodic acquisitions

An acquisition that is made:

<a id="s156-20-a"></a>
**(a)** for a period or on a progressive basis; and

<a id="s156-20-b"></a>
**(b)** for *consideration that is to be provided on a progressive or periodic basis; is treated, for the purposes of Division 129 (which is about changes in the extent of creditable purpose), as if each progressive or periodic component of the acquisition were a separate acquisition.
