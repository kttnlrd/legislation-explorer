# 151-25  Duration of an annual tax period election
General rule
<a id="s151-25-1"></a>
**(1)**  Your election ceases to have effect if:
> <a id="s151-25-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you revoke it by notifying the Commissioner in the [*approved form*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-approved-form); or
> <a id="s151-25-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the Commissioner disallows it under subsection (3); or
> <a id="s151-25-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  on 31 July in a [*financial year*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-financial-year), you are [*required to be registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-required-to-be-registered). Your election also ceases to have effect at the end of your tax period under subsection 27-39(1), at the end of your concluding tax period under section 27-40, or at the end of a tax period applying to you to which subsection 151-55(1) applies. Revocation
<a id="s151-25-2"></a>
**(2)**  A revocation of your election is taken to have had, or has, effect:
> <a id="s151-25-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if you notify the Commissioner on or before 28 October in a financial year—from the start of that [*financial year*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-financial-year); or
> <a id="s151-25-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if you notify the Commissioner after 28 October in a financial year—from the start of the next financial year. Disallowance
<a id="s151-25-3"></a>
**(3)**  The Commissioner may disallow your election if, and only if, the Commissioner is satisfied that you have failed to comply with one or more of your obligations under a [*taxation law*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxation-law).
> **Note:** Disallowing your election is a reviewable GST decision (see Subdivision 110-F in Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953).
<a id="s151-25-4"></a>
**(4)**  A disallowance of your election is taken to have had effect:
> <a id="s151-25-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if the Commissioner notifies you of the disallowance during the [*financial year*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-financial-year) in which your election first took effect— from the start of the tax period in which it first took effect; or
> <a id="s151-25-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if the Commissioner notifies you of the disallowance on or before 28 October during a later financial year—from the start of that later financial year; or
> <a id="s151-25-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  if the Commissioner notifies you of the disallowance after 28 October during a later financial year—from the start of the financial year immediately following that later financial year. Becoming subject to a requirement to register
<a id="s151-25-5"></a>
**(5)**  If paragraph (1)(c) applies, your election is taken to have ceased to have effect from the start of the [*financial year*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-financial-year) referred to in that paragraph.
