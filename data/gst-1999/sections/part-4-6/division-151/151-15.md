# 151-15  Annual tax period elections by representative members of GST groups
<a id="s151-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) member of a [*GST group*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-group) cannot make an [*annual tax period*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-annual-tax-period) election unless each *member of the GST group is eligible under section 151-5.
<a id="s151-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  If the [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) member makes such an election, the [*annual tax period*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-annual-tax-period) applying to the representative member also applies to each member.
