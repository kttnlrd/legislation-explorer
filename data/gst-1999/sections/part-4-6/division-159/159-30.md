# 159-30  Entities ceasing to exist or coming into existence
This Division does not apply in relation to an entity ceasing to [*account on a cash basis*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-account-on-a-cash-basis) as it ceases to exist, or in relation to an entity starting to account on a cash basis as it comes into existence.
