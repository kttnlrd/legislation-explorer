# 188-20  Projected GST turnover
General
<a id="s188-20-1"></a>
**(1)**  Your projected GST turnover at a time during a particular month is the sum of the *values of all the supplies that you have made, or are likely to make, during that month and the next 11 months, other than:
> <a id="s188-20-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  supplies that are [*input taxed*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-input-taxed); or
> <a id="s188-20-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  supplies that are not for *consideration (and are not *taxable supplies under section 72-5); or
> <a id="s188-20-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  supplies that are not made in connection with an [*enterprise*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-enterprise) that you *carry on. Members of GST groups
<a id="s188-20-2"></a>
**(2)**  If you are a *member of a [*GST group*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-group), your projected GST turnover at a time during a particular month is the sum of the *values of all the supplies that you or any other member of the group have made, or are likely to make, during that month and the next 11 months other than:
> <a id="s188-20-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  supplies made from one member of the group to another member of the group; or
> <a id="s188-20-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  supplies that are [*input taxed*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-input-taxed); or
> <a id="s188-20-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  supplies that are not for *consideration (and are not *taxable supplies under section 72-5); or
> <a id="s188-20-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  supplies that are not made in connection with an [*enterprise*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-enterprise) that you *carry on. Supplies that are disregarded
<a id="s188-20-3"></a>
**(3)**  In working out your projected GST turnover, disregard:
> <a id="s188-20-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  any supply that is not *connected with the indirect tax zone; and
> <a id="s188-20-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  any supply that is connected with the indirect tax zone because of paragraph 9-25(5)(c), unless:
> > <a id="s188-20-3-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the supply is made to an [*Australian consumer*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-australian-consumer); and
> > <a id="s188-20-3-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the supply is not [*GST-free*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-free); and
> > <a id="s188-20-3-b-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  the thing to be acquired under the right or option referred to in that paragraph is not goods or [*real property*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-real-property); and
> <a id="s188-20-3-d"></a>
> **(d)**  any GST-free supply made by a [*non-resident*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-non-resident) that does not * make the supply through an [*enterprise*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-enterprise) that the non-resident *carries on in the indirect tax zone.
