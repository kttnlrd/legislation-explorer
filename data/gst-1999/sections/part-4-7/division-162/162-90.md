# 162-90  The effect of incapacitation or cessation
<a id="s162-90-1"></a>
**(1)**  If a [*GST instalment payer*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-instalment-payer) becomes an [*incapacitated entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-incapacitated-entity), or for any reason ceases to exist, the GST instalment payer must give the [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return), for the [*instalment tax period*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-instalment-tax-period) that ends as a result, to the Commissioner:
> <a id="s162-90-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  on or before the 21st day of the month following the end of the instalment tax period; or
> <a id="s162-90-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  within such further period as the Commissioner allows.
<a id="s162-90-2"></a>
**(2)**  If the *assessed net amount for the [*instalment tax period*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-instalment-tax-period) is greater than zero, the [*GST instalment payer*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-instalment-payer) must pay the assessed net amount to the Commissioner on or before the 21st day of the month following the end of the instalment tax period.
<a id="s162-90-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section has effect despite sections 162-60 (which is about when GST instalment payers must give GST returns) and 162-110 (which is about when GST instalment payers must pay assessed net amounts).
