# 162-95  The effect of changing the membership of GST groups
<a id="s162-95-1"></a>
**(1)**  If you are:
> <a id="s162-95-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a [*GST instalment payer*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-instalment-payer); and
> <a id="s162-95-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a *member of a [*GST group*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-group) whose membership changes during an [*instalment tax period*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-instalment-tax-period) applying to you; the instalment tax period ends when the membership of the GST group changes.
<a id="s162-95-2"></a>
**(2)**  The [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) member of the [*GST group*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-group) must give the [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) for the [*instalment tax period*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-instalment-tax-period) to the Commissioner:
> <a id="s162-95-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  on or before the 21st day of the month following the end of the instalment tax period; or
> <a id="s162-95-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  within such further period as the Commissioner allows.
<a id="s162-95-3"></a>
**(3)**  If the *assessed net amount for the [*instalment tax period*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-instalment-tax-period) is greater than zero, the [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) member of the [*GST group*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-group) must pay the assessed net amount to the Commissioner on or before the 21st day of the month following the end of the instalment tax period.
<a id="s162-95-4"></a>
**(4)**  This section has effect despite sections 162-55 (which is about tax periods for GST instalment payers), 162-60 (which is about when GST instalment payers must give GST returns) and 162-110 (which is about when GST instalment payers must pay assessed net amounts).
