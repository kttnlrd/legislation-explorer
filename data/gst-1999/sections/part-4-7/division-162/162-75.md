# 162-75  Giving notices relating to GST instalments

If:

> <a id="s162-75-a"></a>
> **(a)** you are required to pay a *GST instalment; and

> <a id="s162-75-b"></a>
> **(b)** the Commissioner requires you to give a notice relating to the GST instalment; you must give the notice to the Commissioner, in the *approved form, on or before the day on which you are required to pay the GST instalment.
