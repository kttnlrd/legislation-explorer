# 162-80  Certain entities pay only 2 GST instalments for each year
<a id="s162-80-1"></a>
**(1)**  If:
> <a id="s162-80-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are a [*GST instalment payer*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-instalment-payer) for an [*instalment tax period*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-instalment-tax-period); and
> <a id="s162-80-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  subsection (2) applies to you; section 162-70 has effect as if you are only required to pay [*GST instalments*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-instalment) for the last 2 [*GST instalment quarters*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-instalment-quarter) for the instalment tax period.
<a id="s162-80-2"></a>
**(2)**  This subsection applies to you if:
> <a id="s162-80-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  both of the following conditions are satisfied:
> > <a id="s162-80-2-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  you are carrying on a [*primary production business*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-primary-production-business) in an [*income year*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-income-year) corresponding to, or ending during, the [*instalment tax period*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-instalment-tax-period);
> > <a id="s162-80-2-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the [*assessable income*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-assessable-income) that was *derived from, or resulted from, a primary production business that you carried on in the [*base year*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-base-year) exceeded the amount of so much of your deductions in that year that are reasonably related to that income; or
> <a id="s162-80-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  both of the following conditions are satisfied:
> > <a id="s162-80-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  you are a [*special professional*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-special-professional) in an income year corresponding to, or ending during, the instalment tax period;
> > <a id="s162-80-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  your [*assessable professional income*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-assessable-professional-income) in the base year exceeded the amount of so much of your deductions in that year that are reasonably related to that income.
