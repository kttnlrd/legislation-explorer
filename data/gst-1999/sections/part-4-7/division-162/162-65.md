# 162-65  The form and contents of GST returns for GST instalment payers
<a id="s162-65-1"></a>
**(1)**  If you are a [*GST instalment payer*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-instalment-payer) only for part of a [*financial year*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-financial-year), the [*approved form*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-approved-form) for your [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) for the [*instalment tax period*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-instalment-tax-period) consisting of that part of the financial year may require that the return relate to:
> <a id="s162-65-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the instalment tax period; and
> <a id="s162-65-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the one or more preceding tax periods applying to you that fall within the financial year; as if they are a single tax period consisting of the whole of the financial year.
<a id="s162-65-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect in addition to, and does not limit the scope of, section 31-15 (which is about the form and contents of GST returns).
