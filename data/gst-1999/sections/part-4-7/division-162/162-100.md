# 162-100  General interest charge on late payment
If you fail to pay some or all of a [*GST instalment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-instalment) by the time by which the GST instalment is due to be paid, you are liable to pay the [*general interest charge*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-general-interest-charge) on the unpaid amount for each day in the period that:
> <a id="s162-100-a"></a>
> **(a)**  started at the beginning of the day by which the GST instalment was due to be paid; and
> <a id="s162-100-b"></a>
> **(b)**  finishes at the end of the last day on which, at the end of the day, any of the following remains unpaid:
> > <a id="s162-100-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the GST instalment;
> > <a id="s162-100-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  general interest charge on any of the instalment.
