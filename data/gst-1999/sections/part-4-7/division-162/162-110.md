# 162-110  When payments of assessed net amounts must be made— GST instalment payers


<a id="s162-110-1"></a>
**(1)** If:

> <a id="s162-110-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** you are a *GST instalment payer; and

> <a id="s162-110-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the *assessed net amount for an *instalment tax period applying to you is greater than zero; you must pay the assessed net amount to the Commissioner on or before the day on which, under section 162-60, you are required to give to the Commissioner your *GST return for the instalment tax period.

<a id="s162-110-2"></a>
**(2)** This section has effect despite sections 33-3 and 33-5 (which are about when payments of assessed net amounts are made).
