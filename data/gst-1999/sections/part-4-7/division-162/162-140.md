# 162-140  Varied instalment amounts
<a id="s162-140-1"></a>
**(1)**  You may, by notifying the Commissioner in the [*approved form*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-approved-form), substitute another amount for:
> <a id="s162-140-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  your [*notified instalment amount*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-notified-instalment-amount) for a [*GST instalment quarter*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-instalment-quarter); or
> <a id="s162-140-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if paragraph 162-130(3)(b) applies to a GST instalment quarter—your [*GST instalment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-instalment) for the preceding GST instalment quarter. The amount substituted is your varied instalment amount for the GST instalment quarter.
<a id="s162-140-2"></a>
**(2)**  The amount substituted must not be less than zero.
<a id="s162-140-3"></a>
**(3)**  You must give the notice to the Commissioner on or before the day on which the [*GST instalment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-instalment) for the [*GST instalment quarter*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-instalment-quarter) is due.
<a id="s162-140-4"></a>
**(4)**  You must include in the notice an estimate of your *annual GST liability relating to the [*instalment tax period*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-instalment-tax-period) in question. This estimate is your estimated annual GST amount relating to the [*GST instalment quarter*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-instalment-quarter).
> **Note:** You may be liable to penalty under Subdivision 162-D if your variation of the notified instalment amount is too much of an underestimate of your total GST liability.
> **Note:** Your estimated annual GST amount is taken to be zero if it would otherwise be less than zero (see subsection (6)).
<a id="s162-140-5"></a>
**(5)**  However, if paragraph 162-130(3)(b) applies to a [*GST instalment quarter*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-instalment-quarter) but you do not, under subsection (1) of this section, substitute another amount by notifying the Commissioner in the [*approved form*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-approved-form):
> <a id="s162-140-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  your varied instalment amount for the GST instalment quarter is 25% of your [*estimated annual GST amount*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-estimated-annual-gst-amount) relating to the preceding GST instalment quarter; and
> <a id="s162-140-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  your estimated annual GST amount relating to the GST instalment quarter is your [*estimated annual GST amount*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-estimated-annual-gst-amount) relating to the preceding GST instalment quarter.
<a id="s162-140-6"></a>
**(6)**  Your estimated annual GST amount relating to the [*GST instalment quarter*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-instalment-quarter) is zero if, apart from this subsection, this estimate would be less than zero.
