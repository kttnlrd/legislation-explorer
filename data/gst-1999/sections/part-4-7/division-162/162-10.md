# 162-10  Your current GST lodgment record
<a id="s162-10-1"></a>
**(1)**  If you are not a *member of a [*GST group*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-group), your current GST lodgment record is the period, immediately preceding the current tax period applying to you, that is covered by tax periods applying to you for which you have given [*GST returns*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) to the Commissioner.
<a id="s162-10-2"></a>
**(2)**  If you are a *member of a [*GST group*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-group), your current GST lodgment record is the period, immediately preceding the current tax period applying to you, that is covered by tax periods applying to you:
> <a id="s162-10-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  for which you have given [*GST returns*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) to the Commissioner; and
> <a id="s162-10-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  during which the membership of the GST group has not changed.
<a id="s162-10-3"></a>
**(3)**  However, if you have been (but are not currently) the [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) member of a [*GST group*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-group), any tax periods applying to you during which you were such a representative member are not to be counted towards your current GST lodgment record.
