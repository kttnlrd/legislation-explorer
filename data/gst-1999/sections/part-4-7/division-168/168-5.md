# 168-5  Tourist refund scheme

Exporting goods as accompanied baggage

<a id="s168-5-1"></a>
**(1)**  If:

> <a id="s168-5-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you make an acquisition of goods the supply of which to you is a *taxable supply; and

> <a id="s168-5-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the acquisition is of a kind specified in the regulations; and

> <a id="s168-5-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  you leave the indirect tax zone, and export the goods from the indirect tax zone as accompanied baggage, in the circumstances specified in the regulations; the Commissioner must, on behalf of the Commonwealth, pay to you an amount equal to:

> <a id="s168-5-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the amount of the GST payable on the taxable supply; or

> <a id="s168-5-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  such proportion of that amount of GST as is specified in the regulations. Resident of external Territory sending goods home (1A) If:

> <a id="s168-5-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you make an acquisition of goods the supply of which to you is a *taxable supply; and

> <a id="s168-5-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the acquisition is of a kind specified in the regulations; and

> <a id="s168-5-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  at the time of the acquisition, you are an individual to whom one of the following subparagraphs applies:

> > <a id="s168-5-1-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  you reside in an external Territory;

> > <a id="s168-5-1-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  your domicile is in an external Territory;

> > <a id="s168-5-1-c-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  you have actually been in an external Territory, continuously or intermittently, during more than half of the last 12 months; and

> <a id="s168-5-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  at the time of the acquisition, you are not *registered or *required to be registered; and

> <a id="s168-5-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  you leave the indirect tax zone, and export the goods to the external Territory:

> > <a id="s168-5-1-e-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  in circumstances not covered by paragraph (1)(c); and

> > <a id="s168-5-1-e-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  in circumstances specified in the regulations; the Commissioner must, on behalf of the Commonwealth, pay to you an amount equal to:

> <a id="s168-5-1-f"></a>
> **(f)**  the amount of the GST payable on the taxable supply; or

> <a id="s168-5-1-g"></a>
> **(g)**  such proportion of that amount of GST as is specified in the regulations. Paying the refund

<a id="s168-5-2"></a>
**(2)**  An amount payable under this section is payable within the period and in the manner specified in the regulations. You may be found not to be a resident of an external Territory

<a id="s168-5-3"></a>
**(3)**  Subparagraph (1A)(c)(ii) does not apply to you if the Commissioner is satisfied that your permanent place of abode is outside that external Territory.

<a id="s168-5-4"></a>
**(4)**  Subparagraph (1A)(c)(iii) does not apply to you if the Commissioner is satisfied:

> <a id="s168-5-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  that your usual place of abode is outside that external Territory; and

> <a id="s168-5-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  that you do not intend to take up residence in that Territory.
