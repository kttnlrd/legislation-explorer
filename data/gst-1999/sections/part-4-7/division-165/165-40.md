# 165-40  Commissioner may make declaration for purpose of negating avoider’s GST benefits
<a id="s165-40-1"></a>
**(1)**  For the purpose of negating a [*GST benefit*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-benefit) the avoider mentioned in section 165-5 gets or got from the [*scheme*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-scheme), the Commissioner may make a declaration stating either or both of the following:
> <a id="s165-40-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the amount that is (and has been at all times) the avoider’s *net amount for a specified tax period that has ended;
> <a id="s165-40-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the amount that is (and has been at all times) the amount of GST on a specified [*taxable importation*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-importation) that was made (or is stated in the declaration to have been made) by the avoider.
<a id="s165-40-2"></a>
**(2)**  The Commissioner must take such action as he or she considers necessary to give effect to a declaration made under this section.
