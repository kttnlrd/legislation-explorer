# 38-480  Farm land supplied for farming
The supply of a freehold interest in, or the lease by an [*Australian government agency*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-australian-government-agency) of or the *long term lease of, land is GST-free if:
> <a id="s38-480-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the land is land on which a [*farming business*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-farming-business) has been *carried on for at least the period of 5 years preceding the supply; and
> <a id="s38-480-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the *recipient of the supply intends that a farming business be carried on, on the land.
