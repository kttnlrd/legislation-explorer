# 38-220  Religious services
A supply is GST-free if it is a supply of service that:
> <a id="s38-220-a"></a>
> **(a)**  is supplied by a [*ACNC-registered religious institution*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-acnc-registered-religious-institution); and
> <a id="s38-220-b"></a>
> **(b)**  is integral to the practice of that religion.
