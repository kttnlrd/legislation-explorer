# 38-40  Specialist disability services


<a id="s38-40-1"></a>
**(1)** A supply of services is GST-free if the supplier receives funding under the former Disability Services Act 1986 or under a complementary *State law or *Territory law in respect of the services.

<a id="s38-40-2"></a>
**(2)** A supply of services is GST-free if the supplier receives funding under the Disability Services and Inclusion Act 2023 or under a complementary *State law or *Territory law in respect of the services.
