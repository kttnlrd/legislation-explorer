<a id="s38-190-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, a supply covered by any of items 1 to 5 in the table in subsection (1) is not GST-free if it is the supply of a right or option to acquire something the supply of which would be *connected with the indirect tax zone and would not be [*GST-free*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-free). (2A) A supply covered by any of items 2 to 4 in the table in subsection (1) is not [*GST-free*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-free) if the acquisition of the supply relates (whether directly or indirectly, or wholly or partly) to the making of a supply of [*real property*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-real-property) situated in the indirect tax zone that would be, wholly or partly, [*input taxed*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-input-taxed) under Subdivision 40-B or 40-C.
> **Note:** Subdivision 40-B deals with the supply of premises (including a berth at a marina) by way of lease, hire or licence. Subdivision 40-C deals with the sale of residential premises and the supply of residential premises by way of long-term lease.
<a id="s38-190-3"></a>
**(3)**  Without limiting subsection (2) or (2A), a supply covered by item 2 in that table is not GST-free if:
> <a id="s38-190-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it is a supply under an agreement entered into, whether directly or indirectly, with a [*non-resident*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-non-resident); and
> <a id="s38-190-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the supply is provided, or the agreement requires it to be provided, to another entity in the indirect tax zone; and
> <a id="s38-190-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  for a supply other than an [*input taxed*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-input-taxed) supply—none of the following applies:
> > <a id="s38-190-3-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the other entity would be an [*Australian-based business recipient*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-australian-based-business-recipient) of the supply, if the supply had been made to it;
> > <a id="s38-190-3-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the other entity is an individual who is provided with the supply as an employee or [*officer*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-officer) of an entity that would be an Australian-based business recipient of the supply, if the supply had been made to it; or
> > <a id="s38-190-3-c-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  the other entity is an individual who is provided with the supply as an employee or officer of the *recipient, and the recipient’s acquisition of the thing is solely for a *creditable purpose and is not a [*non-deductible expense*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-non-deductible-expense).
<a id="s38-190-4"></a>
**(4)**  A supply is taken, for the purposes of item 3 in that table, to be a supply made to a *recipient who is not in the indirect tax zone if:
> <a id="s38-190-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it is a supply under an agreement entered into, whether directly or indirectly, with an [*Australian resident*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-australian-resident); and
> <a id="s38-190-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the supply is provided, or the agreement requires it to be provided, to another entity outside the indirect tax zone.
<a id="s38-190-5"></a>
**(5)**  Subsection (4) does not apply to any of the following supplies:
> <a id="s38-190-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a transport of goods within the indirect tax zone that is part of, or is connected with, the [*international transport*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-international-transport) of the goods;
> <a id="s38-190-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a loading or handling of goods within the indirect tax zone that is part of, or is connected with, the international transport of the goods;
> <a id="s38-190-5-c"></a>
> **(c)**  a service, done within the indirect tax zone, in relation to the goods that facilitates the international transport of the goods;
> **Example:** The services of a customs broker in processing the information necessary for the clearance of goods into home consumption.
> <a id="s38-190-5-d"></a>
> **(d)**  insuring transport covered by paragraph (a);
> <a id="s38-190-5-e"></a>
> **(e)**  arranging transport covered by paragraph (a), or insurance covered by paragraph (d).
> **Note:** The supply might still be GST-free under item 5, 5A, 6 or 7 in the table in subsection 38-355(1).
