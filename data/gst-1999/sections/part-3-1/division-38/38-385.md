# 38-385  Supplies of precious metals
A supply of *precious metal is GST-free if:
> <a id="s38-385-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it is the first supply of that precious metal after its refining by, or on behalf of, the supplier; and
> <a id="s38-385-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entity that refined the precious metal is a [*refiner of precious metal*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-refiner-of-precious-metal); and
> <a id="s38-385-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the *recipient of the supply is a [*dealer in precious metal*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-dealer-in-precious-metal).
> **Note:** Any other supply of precious metal is input taxed under section 40-100.
