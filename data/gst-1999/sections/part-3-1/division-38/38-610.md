# 38-610  Inbound intangible consumer supplies
<a id="s38-610-1"></a>
**(1)**  An [*inbound intangible consumer supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-inbound-intangible-consumer-supply) is GST-free if:
> <a id="s38-610-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it is made by a [*non-resident*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-non-resident); and
> <a id="s38-610-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  it is covered by a determination under subsection (2).
<a id="s38-610-2"></a>
**(2)**  The Minister may, by legislative instrument, determine that a specified class of *inbound intangible consumer supplies are GST-free.
<a id="s38-610-3"></a>
**(3)**  However, the Minister must not make the determination unless:
> <a id="s38-610-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the [*Foreign Minister*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-foreign-minister) has advised the Minister in writing that the treatment of the class of supplies under the [*GST law*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-law) would, apart from the determination, be inconsistent with Australia’s international obligations; and
> <a id="s38-610-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the Minister is satisfied that similar supplies made by [*Australian residents*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-australian-resident) would be GST-free.
