# 38-25  Residential care etc.


<a id="s38-25-1"></a>
**(1)** A supply of services is GST-free if:

<a id="s38-25-1-a"></a>
**(a)** it is a supply of services covered by a provision of the *aged care service list specified in regulations made for the purposes of this paragraph; and

<a id="s38-25-1-b"></a>
**(b)** it is provided at an *approved residential care home; and

<a id="s38-25-1-c"></a>
**(c)** the supplier is a registered provider (within the meaning of the Aged Care Act 2024).

<a id="s38-25-2"></a>
**(2)** A supply of services is GST-free if:

<a id="s38-25-2-a"></a>
**(a)** the services are provided to one or more aged or disabled people; and

<a id="s38-25-2-b"></a>
**(b)** the *Aged Care Minister has determined in writing that the services are of a kind covered by a provision of the *aged care service list specified in regulations made for the purposes of this paragraph; and

<a id="s38-25-2-c"></a>
**(c)** the supplier receives funding from the Commonwealth, a State or a Territory in connection with the supply.

<a id="s38-25-3"></a>
**(3)** A supply of services is GST-free if:

<a id="s38-25-3-a"></a>
**(a)** the services are provided to one or more aged or disabled people in a residential setting; and

<a id="s38-25-3-b"></a>
**(b)** the *Aged Care Minister has determined in writing that the services are of a kind covered by a provision of the *aged care service list specified in regulations made for the purposes of this paragraph; and

<a id="s38-25-3-c"></a>
**(c)** the services include, and are only provided to people who require, the services (care services) set out in a provision of the *aged care service list specified in regulations made for the purposes of this paragraph. (3A) Services provided to a resident of a *retirement village are taken, for the purposes of paragraph (3)(a), to be provided in a residential setting if, and only if:

<a id="s38-25-3-a-2"></a>
**(a)** he or she is a resident of a *serviced apartment in the retirement village; and

<a id="s38-25-3-b-2"></a>
**(b)** there is in force a written agreement under which the operator of the retirement village provides daily meals and heavy laundry services to all of the residents of the apartment. (3B) However, services provided to a resident of a *serviced apartment in a *retirement village are not taken, for the purposes of paragraph (3)(a), to be provided in a residential setting if:

<a id="s38-25-3-a-3"></a>
**(a)** the *Aged Care Minister has determined in writing:

<a id="s38-25-3-a-i"></a>
**(i)** the levels of care services that residents of serviced apartments in retirement villages must require in order for subsection (3) to apply; and

<a id="s38-25-3-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** the way in which the levels of care services required by residents are to be assessed; and

<a id="s38-25-3-b-3"></a>
**(b)** the *Aged Care System Governor has not, in accordance with the determination, assessed the person to whom the services are provided as requiring the levels of care services so determined. (3C) A determination made for the purposes of paragraph (3B)(a) may be restricted to a specified class of residents of *serviced apartments in *retirement villages.

<a id="s38-25-4"></a>
**(4)** A supply of accommodation is GST-free if it is made to a person in the course of making a supply to that person that is GST-free under subsection (1), (2) or (3). (4A) A supply is GST-free if:

<a id="s38-25-4-a"></a>
**(a)** it is made to a person who is a person of a kind referred to in paragraph (3)(c); and

<a id="s38-25-4-b"></a>
**(b)** it is:

<a id="s38-25-4-b-i"></a>
**(i)** a supply, by way of lease, hire or licence, of *residential premises consisting of a *serviced apartment in a *retirement village; or

<a id="s38-25-4-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** a sale of *real property that is residential premises consisting of a serviced apartment in a retirement village; or

<a id="s38-25-4-b-iii"></a>
**(iii)** a supply of an excluded security (within the meaning of the Corporations Act 2001) in respect of which the right to participate in a retirement village scheme (within the meaning of that Act) entitles the person to use or occupy a serviced apartment in a retirement village; and

<a id="s38-25-4-c"></a>
**(c)** in a case where:

<a id="s38-25-4-c-i"></a>
**(i)** a determination made for the purposes of paragraph (3B)(a) is in force; and

<a id="s38-25-4-c-ii"></a>
**(ii)** the determination is not restricted under subsection (3C) in such a way that the determination excludes the person; the *Aged Care System Governor has, in accordance with the determination, assessed the person as requiring the levels of care services determined in the determination; and

<a id="s38-25-4-d"></a>
**(d)** it is made in connection with one or more supplies, or proposed supplies, to the person that are or will be GST-free under subsection (3).

<a id="s38-25-5"></a>
**(5)** However, a supply of services that is covered by an extra service fee (within the meaning of the Aged Care Rules 2025) is only GST-free under this section to the extent that the services were:

<a id="s38-25-5-a"></a>
**(a)** supplied before 31 October 2026; and

<a id="s38-25-5-b"></a>
**(b)** covered by Schedule 1 to the Quality of Care Principles 2014 (as in force before the commencement of the Aged Care Act 2024) when the extra service agreement concerned was entered into.
