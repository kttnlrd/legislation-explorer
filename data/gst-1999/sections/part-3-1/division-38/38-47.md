# 38-47  Other GST-free health goods
<a id="s38-47-1"></a>
**(1)**  A supply is GST-free if it is a supply of goods of a kind that the [*Health Minister*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-health-minister), by determination in writing, declares to be goods the supply of which is GST-free.
<a id="s38-47-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, a supply is not GST-free under subsection (1) if the supplier and the *recipient have agreed that the supply, or supplies of a kind that include that supply, not be treated as GST-free supplies.
