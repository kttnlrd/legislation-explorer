# 149-15  GST law applies to registered government entities
For the purposes of the [*GST law*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-law), a [*government entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-government-entity) that is [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered) is treated, while its registration has effect, as if it were an entity carrying on an [*enterprise*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-enterprise).
