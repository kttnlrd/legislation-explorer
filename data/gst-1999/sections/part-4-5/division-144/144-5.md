# 144-5  Requirement to register
<a id="s144-5-1"></a>
**(1)**  You are required to be registered if, in *carrying on your enterprise, you supply [*taxi travel*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxi-travel).
<a id="s144-5-2"></a>
**(2)**  It does not matter whether:
> <a id="s144-5-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  your [*GST*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst) turnover meets the [*registration turnover threshold*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registration-turnover-threshold); or
> <a id="s144-5-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  in *carrying on your enterprise, you make other supplies besides supplies of [*taxi travel*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxi-travel).
<a id="s144-5-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section has effect despite section 23-5 (which is about who is required to be registered).
