# 131-20  Duration of an annual apportionment election

General rule

<a id="s131-20-1"></a>
**(1)** Your election ceases to have effect if:

> <a id="s131-20-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** you revoke it; or

> <a id="s131-20-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the Commissioner disallows it under subsection (3); or

> <a id="s131-20-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** in a case to which subparagraph 131-5(1)(a)(i) applied—you are not a *small business entity of the kind referred to in that subparagraph for an *income year; or

> <a id="s131-20-1-d"></a>
> **(d)** in a case to which subparagraph 131-5(1)(a)(ii) applied—on 31 July in a *financial year, you do not satisfy the requirements of that subparagraph. Revocation

<a id="s131-20-2"></a>
**(2)** A revocation of your election is taken to have had, or has, effect at the start of the earliest tax period for which, on the day of the revocation, your *GST return is not yet due. Disallowance

<a id="s131-20-3"></a>
**(3)** The Commissioner may disallow your election if, and only if, the Commissioner is satisfied that you have failed to comply with one or more of your obligations under a *taxation law.

> **Note:** Disallowing your election is a reviewable GST decision (see Subdivision 110-F in Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953).

<a id="s131-20-4"></a>
**(4)** A disallowance of your election is taken to have had effect from the start of the tax period in which the Commissioner notifies you of the disallowance. Not being a small business entity for an income year

<a id="s131-20-5"></a>
**(5)** If paragraph (1)(c) applies, your election is taken to have ceased to have effect from the start of the tax period in which the first day of the *income year referred to in that paragraph falls. Failing to satisfy the requirements of subparagraph 131-5(1)(a)(ii)

<a id="s131-20-6"></a>
**(6)** If paragraph (1)(d) applies, your election is taken to have ceased to have effect from the start of the tax period in which 31 July in the *financial year referred to in that paragraph falls.
