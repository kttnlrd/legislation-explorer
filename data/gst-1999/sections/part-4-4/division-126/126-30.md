# 126-30  Gambling supplies do not give rise to creditable acquisitions
<a id="s126-30-1"></a>
**(1)**  An acquisition of a thing is not a [*creditable acquisition*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-acquisition) if the supply of the thing acquired was a [*gambling supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gambling-supply).
<a id="s126-30-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite section 11-5 (which is about what is a creditable acquisition).
