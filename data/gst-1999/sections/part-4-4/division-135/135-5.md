# 135-5  Initial adjustments for supplies of going concerns
<a id="s135-5-1"></a>
**(1)**  You have an increasing adjustment if:
> <a id="s135-5-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are the *recipient of a [*supply of a going concern*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-supply-of-a-going-concern), or a supply that is [*GST-free*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-free) under section 38-480; and
> <a id="s135-5-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you intend that some or all of the supplies made through the [*enterprise*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-enterprise) to which the supply relates will be supplies that are neither *taxable supplies nor [*GST-free*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-free) supplies.
<a id="s135-5-2"></a>
**(2)**  The amount of the increasing adjustment is as follows: ´ Supply price ´ Proportion of 10                 non-creditable use where: proportion of non-creditable use is the proportion of all the supplies made through the [*enterprise*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-enterprise) that you intend will be supplies that are neither *taxable supplies nor [*GST-free*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-free) supplies, expressed as a percentage worked out on the basis of the *prices of those supplies. supply price means the *price of the supply in relation to which the increasing adjustment arises.
