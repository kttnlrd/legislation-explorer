# 129-5  Adjustments arising under this Division


<a id="s129-5-1"></a>
**(1)** An *adjustment can arise under this Division for:

> <a id="s129-5-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** an acquisition, even if it is not a *creditable acquisition; or

> <a id="s129-5-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** an importation, even if it is not a *creditable importation; in respect of any *adjustment period for the acquisition or importation.

<a id="s129-5-2"></a>
**(2)** However, in determining:

> <a id="s129-5-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** whether an adjustment under this Division arises; or

> <a id="s129-5-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the amount of such an *adjustment; disregard any change in the extent to which the thing acquired or imported is *applied in making *financial supplies, unless you *exceed the financial acquisitions threshold.
