# 129-90  Attributing your adjustments for changes in extent of creditable purpose
<a id="s129-90-1"></a>
**(1)**  An [*adjustment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-adjustment) that you have arising in respect of an [*adjustment period*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-adjustment-period) under this Division is attributable to the tax period that is that adjustment period.
<a id="s129-90-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite section 29-20 (which is about attributing adjustments).
