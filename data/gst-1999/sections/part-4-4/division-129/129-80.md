# 129-80  Effect of adjustment under certain Divisions
For the purpose of working out under this Subdivision the amount of an [*adjustment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-adjustment) for an acquisition, any adjustments under Division 19, 21, 133 or 134 that you have had for the acquisition are to be taken into account in working out the full input tax credit for the purpose of section 129-70 or 129-75.
