# 136-50  Meanings of taxable at less than 1/11 of the price and creditable at less than 1/11 of the consideration
<a id="s136-50-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) is taxable at less than 1/11 of the price if the amount of GST payable on the supply is an amount that is less than 1/11 of the *price of the supply.
<a id="s136-50-2"></a>
**(2)**  A [*creditable acquisition*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-acquisition) is creditable at less than 1/11 of the consideration if the [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) to which it relates is [*taxable at less than 1/11 of the price*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-at-less-than-111-of-the-price).
