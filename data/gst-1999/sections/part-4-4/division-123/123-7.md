# 123-7  Meaning of small enterprise entity
<a id="s123-7-1"></a>
**(1)**  An entity is a small enterprise entity at a particular time if:
> <a id="s123-7-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity is a [*small business entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-small-business-entity) (other than because of subsection 328-110(4) of the [*ITAA 1997*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-itaa-1997)) for the [*income year*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-income-year) in which the time occurs; or
> <a id="s123-7-1-aa"></a>
> **(aa)**  the entity is an entity covered by subsection (1A) for the income year in which the time occurs; or
> <a id="s123-7-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  at that time, the entity does not carry on a business and its [*GST*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst) turnover does not exceed the [*small enterprise turnover threshold*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-small-enterprise-turnover-threshold). (1A) An entity is covered by this subsection for an [*income year*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-income-year) if:
> <a id="s123-7-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity is not a [*small business entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-small-business-entity) (other than because of subsection 328-110(4) of the [*ITAA 1997*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-itaa-1997)) for the income year; and
> <a id="s123-7-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entity would be such a small business entity for the income year if:
> > <a id="s123-7-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  each reference in Subdivision 328-C (about what is a small business entity) of that Act to $10 million were instead a reference to $50 million; and
> > <a id="s123-7-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the reference in paragraph 328-110(5)(b) of that Act to a small business entity were instead a reference to an entity covered by this subsection.
<a id="s123-7-2"></a>
**(2)**  The small enterprise turnover threshold is $2 million.
