# 123-15  Net amounts


<a id="s123-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  If you are a *retailer or a *small enterprise entity who has chosen to apply a *simplified accounting method, the net amount for a tax period during which the choice has effect is worked out using the method provided for by the simplified accounting method. (1A) However, the *net amount worked out under subsection (1) for the tax period:

> <a id="s123-15-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  may be increased or decreased under Subdivision 21-A of the *Wine Tax Act; and

> <a id="s123-15-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  may be increased or decreased under Subdivision 13-A of the A New Tax System (Luxury Car Tax) Act 1999.

> **Note:** Under Subdivision 21-A of the Wine Tax Act, amounts of wine tax increase the net amount, and amounts of wine tax credits reduce the net amount.

> **Note:** Under Subdivision 13-A of the A New Tax System (Luxury Car Tax) Act 1999, amounts of luxury car tax increase the net amount, and luxury car tax adjustments alter the net amount.

<a id="s123-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite section 17-5 (which is about net amounts).
