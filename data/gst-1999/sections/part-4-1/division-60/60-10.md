# 60-10  Registration etc. not needed for input tax credits
<a id="s60-10-1"></a>
**(1)**  If you make a [*pre-establishment acquisition*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-pre-establishment-acquisition), the fact that you are not [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered) or [*required to be registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-required-to-be-registered) does not stop the acquisition being a [*creditable acquisition*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-acquisition).
<a id="s60-10-2"></a>
**(2)**  If you make a [*pre-establishment importation*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-pre-establishment-importation), the fact that you are not [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered) or [*required to be registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-required-to-be-registered) does not stop the acquisition being a [*creditable importation*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-importation).
<a id="s60-10-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section has effect despite sections 11-5 and 15-5 (which are about what are creditable acquisitions and creditable importations).
