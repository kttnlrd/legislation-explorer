# 58-50  Representatives to give GST returns for incapacitated entities
<a id="s58-50-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) of an [*incapacitated entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-incapacitated-entity) must give to the Commissioner a [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) for a tax period applying to the incapacitated entity if:
> <a id="s58-50-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the incapacitated entity has failed to give to the Commissioner a GST return for a tax period; and
> <a id="s58-50-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the Commissioner, in writing, directs the representative to give to the Commissioner a GST return.
> **Note:** Deciding to direct a representative of an incapacitated entity to give to the Commissioner a GST return is a reviewable GST decision (see Subdivision 110-F in Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953).
<a id="s58-50-2"></a>
**(2)**  The tax period may be any tax period applying to the [*incapacitated entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-incapacitated-entity), including:
> <a id="s58-50-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a tax period that ends before the [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) became a representative of the incapacitated entity; and
> <a id="s58-50-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a tax period that starts after the representative became a representative of the incapacitated entity.
<a id="s58-50-3"></a>
**(3)**  The [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) by the [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative):
> <a id="s58-50-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  must be in accordance with the requirements of Division 31 as they would apply in relation to the [*incapacitated entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-incapacitated-entity) except to the extent that the direction under paragraph (1)(b) modifies those requirements; and
> <a id="s58-50-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  must be given to the Commissioner within the period specified in the direction.
<a id="s58-50-4"></a>
**(4)**  Without limiting the matters that the Commissioner may take into account in deciding whether to give a direction under paragraph (1)(b), the Commissioner must take into account:
> <a id="s58-50-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the likelihood of a dividend to unsecured creditors of the [*incapacitated entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-incapacitated-entity) being declared, and the likely amounts of any such dividend; and
> <a id="s58-50-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the likelihood that, if the Commissioner were given the [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return), it would reveal a liability to pay an amount to the Commissioner under the [*GST law*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-law); and
> <a id="s58-50-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the availability of books and records that would make it possible to prepare the GST return; and
> <a id="s58-50-4-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the likelihood that the cost to the [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) of preparing the GST return would be covered by the incapacitated entity’s assets without resulting in an unreasonable impact on the other creditors of the incapacitated entity.
<a id="s58-50-5"></a>
**(5)**  The [*incapacitated entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-incapacitated-entity) is taken to have complied with Division 31 in relation to giving a [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) for a tax period if the [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) gives to the Commissioner a return for the tax period in accordance with this section.
<a id="s58-50-6"></a>
**(6)**  A direction under paragraph (1)(b) is not a legislative instrument.
<a id="s58-50-7"></a>
**(7)**  This section has effect despite section 31-5 (which is about who must give GST returns).
