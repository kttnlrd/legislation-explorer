# 58-45  GST returns for representatives of incapacitated entities
<a id="s58-45-1"></a>
**(1)**  If an individual is appointed as a [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) of 2 or more *incapacitated entities, the individual may give to the Commissioner one [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) for a tax period in respect of the entities if the entities are *members of the same [*GST group*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-group).
<a id="s58-45-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite section 31-5 (which is about who must give GST returns).
