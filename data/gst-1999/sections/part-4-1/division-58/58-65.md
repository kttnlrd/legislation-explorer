# 58-65  Money available to meet representative’s liabilities
A [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) of an [*incapacitated entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-incapacitated-entity) who is liable to pay an amount because of this Division is authorised and required to apply any money which the representative receives in his or her capacity as that representative in order to pay the liability.
