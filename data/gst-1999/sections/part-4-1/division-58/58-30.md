# 58-30  Notice of cessation of representation
A [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) who ceases to be a representative of an [*incapacitated entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-incapacitated-entity) must notify the Commissioner of that cessation, in the [*approved form*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-approved-form), within 21 days after so ceasing.
