# 58-60  Representative to notify Commissioner of certain liabilities etc.
<a id="s58-60-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) of an [*incapacitated entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-incapacitated-entity) must notify the Commissioner, in the [*approved form*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-approved-form), of an amount of GST for which the entity is liable, or an [*increasing adjustment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-increasing-adjustment) that the entity has, if:
> <a id="s58-60-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the representative becomes aware, or could reasonably be expected to have become aware, of the amount of GST, or the adjustment; and
> <a id="s58-60-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the amount of GST, or the adjustment, has not been taken into account in any [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) that has been given to the Commissioner; and
> <a id="s58-60-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the Commissioner has not been previously notified of the amount of GST, or the adjustment, under this section.
> **Note:** Section 286-75 in Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953 provides an administrative penalty for breach of this subsection.
<a id="s58-60-2"></a>
**(2)**  The notification must be given to the Commissioner before the day on which the [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) declares a dividend to unsecured creditors of the [*incapacitated entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-incapacitated-entity).
<a id="s58-60-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section does not apply if the [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) is a representative of a kind that does not have the capacity to declare dividends to unsecured creditors of the [*incapacitated entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-incapacitated-entity).
<a id="s58-60-4"></a>
**(4)**  This section does not apply in circumstances determined by the Commissioner under subsection (5).
<a id="s58-60-5"></a>
**(5)**  The Commissioner may, by legislative instrument, determine circumstances in which this section does not apply.
