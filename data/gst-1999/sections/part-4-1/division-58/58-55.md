# 58-55  Incapacitated entities not required to give GST returns in some cases
<a id="s58-55-1"></a>
**(1)**  An [*incapacitated entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-incapacitated-entity) is not required to give a [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) for a tax period if:
> <a id="s58-55-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity’s *net amount for the tax period is zero; and
> <a id="s58-55-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entity does not have an [*increasing adjustment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-increasing-adjustment) that is attributable to the tax period; and
> <a id="s58-55-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the entity is not liable for GST that is attributable to the tax period.
<a id="s58-55-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite section 31-5 (which is about who must give GST returns).
