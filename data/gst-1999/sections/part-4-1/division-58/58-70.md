# 58-70  Protection for actions of representative
A [*representative*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-representative) of an [*incapacitated entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-incapacitated-entity) is not liable to civil or criminal proceedings in relation to an act done, or omitted to be done, in good faith, in the performance or purported performance, or exercise or purported exercise, of the representative’s duties or powers under, or in relation to, the [*GST law*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-law).
