# 54-60  Payments of GST relating to GST branches
<a id="s54-60-1"></a>
**(1)**  If an entity has a [*GST branch*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-branch) and the *assessed net amount relating to the [*GST branch*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-branch) for a tax period is greater than zero:
> <a id="s54-60-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity must pay that assessed net amount to the Commissioner; and
> <a id="s54-60-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  Division 33 applies to payment of that amount as if it were a payment the entity was obliged to make under section 33-3 or 33-5 (as the case requires).
<a id="s54-60-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite Division 33 (which is about payments of GST).
