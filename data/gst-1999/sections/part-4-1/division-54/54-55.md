# 54-55  GST returns relating to GST branches
<a id="s54-55-1"></a>
**(1)**  An entity must, in relation to each [*GST branch*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-branch) of the entity, give to the Commissioner a [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) for each tax period applying to the entity.
<a id="s54-55-3"></a>
**(3)**  The entity must still give a [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) under section 31-5, unless all the *enterprises that it *carries on are carried on through its [*GST*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst) branches.
<a id="s54-55-4"></a>
**(4)**  This section has effect despite section 31-5 (which is about who must give GST returns).
