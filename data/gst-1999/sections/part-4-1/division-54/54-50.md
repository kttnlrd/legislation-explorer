# 54-50  Tax invoices and adjustment notes
<a id="s54-50-1"></a>
**(1)**  The [*GST branch*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-branch) registration number of a [*GST branch*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-branch) must be set out in:
> <a id="s54-50-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  any [*tax invoice*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-tax-invoice) relating to a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) made through that GST branch; and
> <a id="s54-50-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  any [*adjustment note*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-adjustment-note) for a [*decreasing adjustment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-decreasing-adjustment) that arose from the occurrence of an [*adjustment event*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-adjustment-event) relating to a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) made through that GST branch; and
> <a id="s54-50-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  any [*third party adjustment note*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-third-party-adjustment-note) for a decreasing adjustment under section 134-5 that relates to a taxable supply made through that GST branch.
<a id="s54-50-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite sections 29-70 and 29-75 (which are about tax invoices and adjustment notes), and section 134-20 (which is about third party adjustment notes).
