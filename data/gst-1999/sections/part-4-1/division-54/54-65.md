# 54-65  Refunds relating to GST branches
If an entity has a [*GST branch*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-branch) and the *assessed net amount relating to the [*GST branch*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-branch) for a tax period is less than zero, the Commissioner must, on behalf of the Commonwealth, pay that assessed net amount (expressed as a positive amount) to the entity.
> **Note:** See Division 3A of Part IIB of the Taxation Administration Act 1953 for the rules about how the Commissioner must pay the entity. Division 3 of Part IIB allows the Commissioner to apply the amount owing as a credit against tax debts that the entity owes to the Commonwealth.
> **Note:** Interest is payable under the Taxation (Interest on Overpayments and Early Payments) Act 1983 if the Commissioner is late in refunding the amount.
