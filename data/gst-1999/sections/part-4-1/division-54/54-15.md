# 54-15  GST branch registration number
If the Commissioner *registers a [*GST branch*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-branch), the Commissioner must notify the registered entity of the branch’s [*GST branch*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-branch) registration number.
