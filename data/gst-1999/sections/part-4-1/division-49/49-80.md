# 49-80  Notification by principal members

The principal member of a *GST religious group must notify the Commissioner of any circumstances under which the Commissioner must:

<a id="s49-80-a"></a>
**(a)** revoke the approval of one of the *members of the group under subsection 49-70(2); or

<a id="s49-80-b"></a>
**(b)** revoke the approval of the group under subsection 49-75(2). The notification may (in appropriate cases) be in the form of an application under subsection 49-70(1) or 49-75(1). The notification, or application, must be given to the Commissioner within 21 days after the circumstances occurred.
