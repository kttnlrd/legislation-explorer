# 63-20  Non-profit sub-entities may register
<a id="s63-20-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [*non-profit sub-entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-non-profit-sub-entity) may apply to be [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered) under section 23-10 even if it is not [*carrying on an *enterprise*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-carrying-on-an-enterprise) and is not intending to carry on an enterprise.
<a id="s63-20-2"></a>
**(2)**  The Commissioner must *register the [*non-profit sub-entity*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-non-profit-sub-entity) whether or not the Commissioner is satisfied that it is [*carrying on an *enterprise*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-carrying-on-an-enterprise) or intending to carry on an enterprise.
<a id="s63-20-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section has effect despite section 23-10 (which is about who may be registered) and section 25-5 (which is about when the Commissioner must register an entity).
