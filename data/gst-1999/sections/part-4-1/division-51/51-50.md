# 51-50  GST returns relating to GST joint ventures
<a id="s51-50-1"></a>
**(1)**  The *joint venture operator of a [*GST joint venture*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-joint-venture) must, in relation to each [*GST joint venture*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-joint-venture) of the joint venture operator, give to the Commissioner a [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) for each tax period applying to the joint venture operator.
<a id="s51-50-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, while an election made by the *joint venture operator under section 51-52 has effect, the joint venture operator must, in relation to all the [*GST joint ventures*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-joint-venture) for which the joint venture operator is the joint venture operator, give to the Commissioner a single [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) for each tax period applying to the joint venture operator.
<a id="s51-50-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section has effect despite section 31-5 (which is about who must give GST returns).
