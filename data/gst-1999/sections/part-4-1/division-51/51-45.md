# 51-45  Additional net amounts relating to GST joint ventures
<a id="s51-45-1"></a>
**(1)**  Division 17 applies to the *joint venture operator of a [*GST joint venture*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-joint-venture) as if the joint venture operator had an additional *net amount, relating to the joint venture, for each tax period.
<a id="s51-45-2"></a>
**(2)**  The additional *net amount relating to the joint venture is worked out as if the joint venture operator:
> <a id="s51-45-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  is only liable for the GST on *taxable supplies that the joint venture operator makes, on behalf of another entity that is a *participant in the joint venture, in the course of activities for which the joint venture was entered into; and
> <a id="s51-45-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  is only entitled to the input tax credits for [*creditable acquisitions*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-acquisition) or [*creditable importations*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-importation) that the joint venture operator makes on behalf of another entity that is a participant in the joint venture, in the course of activities for which the joint venture was entered into; and
> <a id="s51-45-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  only has adjustments relating to supplies, acquisitions or importations that the joint venture operator makes, on behalf of another entity that is a participant in the joint venture, in the course of activities for which the joint venture was entered into. (2A) However, while an election made by the *joint venture operator under section 51-52 has effect:
> <a id="s51-45-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  Division 17 applies to the joint venture operator as if the joint venture operator had an additional *net amount, relating to all the [*GST joint ventures*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-joint-venture) for which the joint venture operator is the joint venture operator, for each tax period; and
> <a id="s51-45-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  that additional net amount is worked out by aggregating what would be the additional *net amounts relating to each GST joint venture under subsection (2) if that subsection applied.
<a id="s51-45-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section has effect despite sections 17-5 and 17-10 (which are about net amounts and adjustments).
