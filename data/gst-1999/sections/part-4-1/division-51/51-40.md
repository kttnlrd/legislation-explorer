# 51-40  Adjustments
<a id="s51-40-1"></a>
**(1)**  Any [*adjustment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-adjustment) relating to any supply, acquisition or importation that the *joint venture operator of a [*GST joint venture*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-joint-venture) makes, on behalf of another entity that is a *participant in the joint venture, in the course of activities for which the joint venture was entered into is to be treated as if:
> <a id="s51-40-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the participant did not have the adjustment; and
> <a id="s51-40-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entity that is the joint venture operator at the time the adjustment arises had the adjustment.
<a id="s51-40-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section has effect despite section 17-10 (which is about the effect of adjustments on net amounts).
