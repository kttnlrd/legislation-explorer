# 48-51  Consequences of being a member of a GST group for part of a tax period
<a id="s48-51-1"></a>
**(1)**  If you are a *member of a [*GST group*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-group) only for one or more parts of a tax period:
> <a id="s48-51-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  section 48-40 does not apply to the GST payable on a [*taxable supply*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-supply) that you make, to the extent that the GST would be attributable to a period to which subsection (2) applies if it were a tax period applying to you; and
> <a id="s48-51-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  section 48-40 does not apply to the GST payable on a [*taxable importation*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-importation) that you make during a period to which subsection (2) applies; and
> <a id="s48-51-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  section 48-45 does not apply to the input tax credit for a [*creditable acquisition*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-acquisition) or [*creditable importation*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-importation) that you make, to the extent that the input tax credit would be attributable to a period to which subsection (2) applies if it were a tax period applying to you; and
> <a id="s48-51-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  section 48-50 does not apply to an [*adjustment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-adjustment) that you have that would be attributable to a period to which subsection (2) applies if it were a tax period applying to you.
<a id="s48-51-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section applies to any period, during the tax period, during which you were not a *member of that [*GST group*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-group) or any other GST group.
