# 57-30  Notice of cessation of agency
A [*resident agent*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-resident-agent) who ceases to act as agent for a [*non-resident*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-non-resident) must notify the Commissioner of that cessation, in the [*approved form*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-approved-form), within 14 days after so ceasing to act.
