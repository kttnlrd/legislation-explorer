# 57-20  Resident agents are required to be registered
<a id="s57-20-1"></a>
**(1)**  A [*resident agent*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-resident-agent) who is acting as agent for a [*non-resident*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-non-resident) is required to be registered if the non-resident is [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered) or [*required to be registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-required-to-be-registered).
<a id="s57-20-2"></a>
**(2)**  The section has effect despite section 23-5 (which is about who is required to be registered).
