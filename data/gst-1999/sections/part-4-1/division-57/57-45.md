# 57-45  Resident agents giving GST returns
If you are a [*resident agent*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-resident-agent) acting for a [*non-resident*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-non-resident), subsection 31-15(2) does not apply to you in relation to a tax period if, during the tax period:
> <a id="s57-45-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the non-resident made *taxable supplies, or supplies that would have been taxable supplies had they not been [*GST-free*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-free) or [*input taxed*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-input-taxed), through you as agent; or
> <a id="s57-45-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the non-resident made [*creditable acquisitions*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-acquisition) through you as agent.
