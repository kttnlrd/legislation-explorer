# 23-10  Who may be registered
<a id="s23-10-1"></a>
**(1)**  You may be [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered) under this Act if you are carrying on an [*enterprise*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-enterprise) (whether or not your [*GST*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst) turnover is at, above or below the [*registration turnover threshold*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registration-turnover-threshold)).
<a id="s23-10-2"></a>
**(2)**  You may be [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered) under this Act if you intend to carry on an [*enterprise*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-enterprise) from a particular date.
