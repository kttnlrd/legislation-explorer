# 23-5  Who is required to be registered
You are required to be registered under this Act if:
> <a id="s23-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are [*carrying on an *enterprise*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-carrying-on-an-enterprise); and
> <a id="s23-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  your [*GST*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst) turnover meets the [*registration turnover threshold*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registration-turnover-threshold).
> **Note:** It is the entity that carries on the enterprise that is required to be registered (and not the enterprise).
