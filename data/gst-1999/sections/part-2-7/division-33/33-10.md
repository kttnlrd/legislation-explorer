# 33-10  How payment of assessed net amounts are made
<a id="s33-10-1"></a>
**(1)**  You may pay by [*electronic payment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-electronic-payment) any *assessed net amounts payable by you. Any amounts of an assessed net amount that you do not pay by electronic payment must be paid in the manner determined in writing by the Commissioner.
<a id="s33-10-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, if your [*GST*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst) turnover meets the [*electronic lodgment turnover threshold*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-electronic-lodgment-turnover-threshold), you must pay by [*electronic payment*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-electronic-payment) any *assessed net amounts payable by you.
> **Note:** A penalty applies if you fail to pay electronically as required—see section 288-20 in Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953.
> **Note:** You must also pay other tax debts electronically—see section 8AAZMA in that Act.
