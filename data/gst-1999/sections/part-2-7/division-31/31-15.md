# 31-15  The form and contents of GST returns
<a id="s31-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  Your [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) for a tax period must be in the [*approved form*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-approved-form).
<a id="s31-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, if during the tax period:
> <a id="s31-15-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are not liable for the GST on any *taxable supplies, and you did not make any supplies that would have been taxable supplies had they not been [*GST-free*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-free) or [*input taxed*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-input-taxed); and
> <a id="s31-15-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you are not liable for the GST on any [*taxable importations*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-importation) the GST on which is payable at the time when GST on taxable supplies is normally payable; and
> <a id="s31-15-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  you are not entitled to the input tax credits on any [*creditable acquisitions*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-acquisition) or [*creditable importations*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-importation); you may give your [*GST return*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst-return) for the period to the Commissioner in the manner the Commissioner requires.
