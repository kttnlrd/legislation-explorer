# 9-26  Supplies by non-residents that are not connected with the indirect tax zone
<a id="s9-26-1"></a>
**(1)**  A supply is not connected with the indirect tax zone if:
> <a id="s9-26-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the supplier is a [*non-resident*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-non-resident); and
> <a id="s9-26-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the supplier does not make the supply through an [*enterprise*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-enterprise) that the supplier *carries on in the indirect tax zone; and

> <a id="s9-26-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the *recipient is an [*Australian-based business recipient*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-australian-based-business-recipient) of the supply. 2        Intangible           a supply of anything other than goods or [*real property*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-real-property) if: supply between       (a) the thing is done in the indirect tax zone; and non-residents

> **Note:** This subsection does not apply to supplies made by a non-resident through a resident agent if they have agreed it is not to apply: see section 57-7.
<a id="s9-26-2"></a>
**(2)**  An entity is an Australian-based business recipient of a supply made to the entity if:
> <a id="s9-26-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity is [*registered*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-registered); and
> <a id="s9-26-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an [*enterprise*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-enterprise) of the entity is *carried on in the indirect tax zone; and
> <a id="s9-26-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the entity’s acquisition of the thing supplied is not solely of a private or domestic nature.
> **Note:** If a supply is not connected with the indirect tax zone, the Australian-based business recipient may be subject to a reverse charge: see Subdivision 84-A.
<a id="s9-26-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section applies despite sections 9-25 (which is about when supplies are connected with the indirect tax zone) and 85-5 (which is about telecommunication supplies).
