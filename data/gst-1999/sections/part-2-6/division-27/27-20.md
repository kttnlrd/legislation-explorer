# 27-20  Withdrawing elections of one month tax periods
<a id="s27-20-1"></a>
**(1)**  You may, by notifying the Commissioner in the [*approved form*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-approved-form), withdraw an election under section 27-10, unless your [*GST*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-gst) turnover meets the [*tax period turnover threshold*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-tax-period-turnover-threshold).
<a id="s27-20-2"></a>
**(2)**  The withdrawal takes effect on the day specified in the notice. However, the day specified:
> <a id="s27-20-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  must be 1 January, 1 April, 1 July or 1 October, or any day occurring before the election takes effect; and
> <a id="s27-20-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  must not be a day occurring earlier than 12 months after the election took effect.
