# 177-11  Delegation by Aged Care System Governor

The *Aged Care System Governor may, in writing, delegate his or her powers under paragraph 38-25(3B)(b) to:

> <a id="s177-11-a"></a>
> **(a)** a person in relation to whom there is in force a delegation by the Aged Care System Governor of functions under Division 1 of Part 3 of Chapter 8 of the Aged Care Act 2024; or

> <a id="s177-11-b"></a>
> **(b)** a person:

> > <a id="s177-11-i"></a>
> > **(i)** who is a person of a kind specified in a determination that is in force and that is made by the *Aged Care Minister for the purposes of paragraph 38-25(3B)(a); and

> > <a id="s177-11-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** whom the Aged Care System Governor is satisfied is qualified and experienced to make assessments of the kind referred to in paragraph 38-25(3B)(b).
