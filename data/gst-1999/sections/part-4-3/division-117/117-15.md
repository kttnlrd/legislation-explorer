# 117-15  Refunds of assessed GST on certain reimportations of live animals
<a id="s117-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  If:
> <a id="s117-15-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you were liable to pay the *assessed GST on a [*taxable importation*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-taxable-importation) to which section 117-10 applied; and
> <a id="s117-15-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the importation was not a [*creditable importation*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-creditable-importation); and
> <a id="s117-15-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the circumstances specified in the regulations occur; the Commissioner must, on behalf of the Commonwealth, pay to you an amount equal to the amount of the assessed GST payable on the taxable importation.
<a id="s117-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  The amount is payable within the period and in the manner specified in the regulations.
