# 2-5  The basic rules (Chapter 2)
Chapter 2 has the basic rules for the GST, including:  when and how the GST arises, and who is liable to pay it;  when and how input tax credits arise, and who is entitled to them;  how to work out payments and refunds of GST;  when and how the payments and refunds are to be made.
