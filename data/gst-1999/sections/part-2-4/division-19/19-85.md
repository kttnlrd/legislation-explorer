# 19-85  Decreasing adjustments for acquisitions
If the *previously attributed input tax credit amount is less than the [*corrected input tax credit amount*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-corrected-input-tax-credit-amount), you have a decreasing adjustment equal to the difference between the corrected input tax credit amount and the previously attributed input tax credit amount.
