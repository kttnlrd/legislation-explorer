# 19-75  Previously attributed input tax credit amounts
The previously attributed input tax credit amount for an acquisition is:
> <a id="s19-75-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the amount of any input tax credit that was attributable to a tax period in respect of the acquisition; minus
> <a id="s19-75-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the sum of any [*increasing adjustments*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-increasing-adjustment), under this Subdivision or Division 21, 129, 131 or 134, that were previously attributable to a tax period in respect of the acquisition; plus
> <a id="s19-75-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the sum of any [*decreasing adjustments*](/gst-1999/s195-1#s195-1-decreasing-adjustment), under this Subdivision or Division 21, 129 or 133, that were previously attributable to a tax period in respect of the acquisition.
