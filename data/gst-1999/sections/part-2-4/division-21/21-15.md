# 21-15  Bad debts written off (creditable acquisitions)


<a id="s21-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  You have an increasing adjustment if:

> <a id="s21-15-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you made a *creditable acquisition for *consideration; and

> <a id="s21-15-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the whole or part of the consideration is *overdue, but you have not provided the consideration overdue; and

> <a id="s21-15-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the supplier of the thing you acquired writes off as bad the whole or a part of the debt, or the whole or a part of the debt has been overdue for 12 months or more. The amount of the increasing adjustment is 1/11 of the amount written off, or 1/11 of the amount that has been overdue for 12 months or more, as the case requires.

<a id="s21-15-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, you cannot have an *adjustment under this section if you *account on a cash basis.
