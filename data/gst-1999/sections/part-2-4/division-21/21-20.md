# 21-20  Recovering amounts previously written off (creditable acquisitions)

You have a decreasing adjustment if:

<a id="s21-20-a"></a>
**(a)** you made a *creditable acquisition in relation to which you had an *increasing adjustment under section 21-15 for a debt; and

<a id="s21-20-b"></a>
**(b)** you pay to the supplier of the thing you acquired the whole or a part of the amount written off, or the whole or a part of the amount that has been *overdue for 12 months or more, as the case requires. The amount of the decreasing adjustment is 1/11 of the amount recovered.
