# 21-10  Recovering amounts previously written off (taxable supplies)

You have an increasing adjustment if:

> <a id="s21-10-a"></a>
> **(a)** you made a *taxable supply in relation to which you had a *decreasing adjustment under section 21-5 for a debt; and

> <a id="s21-10-b"></a>
> **(b)** you recover the whole or a part of the amount written off, or the whole or a part of the amount that has been *overdue for 12 months or more, as the case requires. The amount of the increasing adjustment is 1/11 of the amount recovered.
