Preamble
The question of the eligibility of a computer data module for investment allowance purposes was considered. The module is an improvement on a disc pack.
Ruling
2. Careful consideration has been given to the
differences between a standard disc pack and a data module. It is
considered that the data module could not be regarded for investment
allowance purposes as other than a device similar to a disc pack. The
data module does not, therefore, qualify for the investment allowance
because of 82AF(2)(d) of the Income Tax Assessment Act which excludes
'discs or other similar devices' from the allowance.
Commissioner of Taxation
29 March 1977
