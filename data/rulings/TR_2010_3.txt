Notice of Withdrawal
Taxation Ruling TR 2010/3 is withdrawn with effect from 1 July 2022.
1. TR 2010/3 expresses the Commissioner's opinion on the circumstances in which a private company with a present entitlement to an amount from an associated trust estate makes a loan to that trust within the meaning of subsection 109D(3) of Division 7A of Part III of the
Income Tax Assessment Act 1936,
in circumstances where funds representing that present entitlement remain intermingled with funds of the trust.
2. TD 2022/D1
Income tax: Division 7A: when will an unpaid present entitlement or amount held on sub-trust become the provision of 'financial accommodation'?
was published on 23 February 2022. When finalised, this Determination will contain the ATO view (and compliance approach) on this issue that will apply to trust entitlements created on or after 1 July 2022.
3. An entity may continue to rely on this Taxation Ruling in respect of trust entitlements conferred on or before 30 June 2022. That is, the Commissioner will not devote compliance resources to arrangements conducted in accordance with TR 2010/3 in respect of trust entitlements arising on or before 30 June 2022.
