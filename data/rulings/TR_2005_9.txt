Notice of Withdrawal
Taxation Ruling TR 2005/9 is withdrawn with effect from today.
1. TR 2005/9 explains the principles associated with the retention of electronic records created from business transactions for the purposes of section 262A of the
Income Tax Assessment Act 1936
.
2. TR 2005/9 is replaced by Taxation Ruling TR 2018/2
Income tax: record keeping and access - electronic records
which issued on 14 February 2018.
Commissioner of Taxation
14 February 2018
