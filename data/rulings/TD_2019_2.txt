Notice of Withdrawal
Taxation Determination TD 2019/2 is withdrawn with effect from 21 November 2024.
1. TD 2019/2 provides the amounts that the Commissioner will accept as estimates of the value of goods taken from trading stock for private use during the 2018–19 income year by taxpayers in named industries.
2. TD 2019/2 is being withdrawn as its period of effect has passed. The Determination will continue to be legally binding on the Commissioner for the relevant period to which it relates.
