# LTC: Associated persons rule ¶19-235

An LTC and an owner are associated if the owner effectively holds 25% or greater interest in a right, obligation, property, status or thing of the LTC.

An LTC and an owner who has a look-through interest in the LTC are associated if the owner is a director or employee of the LTC.

The aggregation rule applies if ownership interests are held by an associated person. A modified rule applies for the land provisions (¶16-245).

The tripartite test has been modified for the LTC regime.
