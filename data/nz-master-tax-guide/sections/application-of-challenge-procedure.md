# Application of challenge procedure ¶4-215

The challenge procedure applies to:

## every notice of disputable decision issued by the Commissioner under a provision of the Inland

Revenue Acts on or after 1 October 1996, irrespective of the period to which the notice relates, and

## any assessment, reassessment or determination issued before 1 October 1996 that was elected

to be treated as if issued after that date, irrespective of the period to which the assessment or reassessment relates (s 125A(2) of the TAA).

The procedure does not apply to any assessment, reassessment or determination or decision to which Pt 8 of the TAA applies (objections).
