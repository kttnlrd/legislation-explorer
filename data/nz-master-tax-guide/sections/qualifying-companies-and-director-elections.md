# Qualifying companies and director elections ¶19-050

A company could only become a qualifying company (QC) if all the directors of that company elected that the company become a QC.

## Revocation

A director election can be revoked only by a resolution of the board of directors notified in writing to the Commissioner. A revocation will then take effect from the later of the beginning of the income year the board nominates or the income year in which the notice is received by the Commissioner. If a director resigns or dies, there is no deemed revocation of the director election. By electing that a company become a QC, directors, unlike shareholders in a shareholder election (see ¶19-055), do not become personally liable for the income tax of the company.

Law: IT07 ss HA 5, HA 30, HA 31
