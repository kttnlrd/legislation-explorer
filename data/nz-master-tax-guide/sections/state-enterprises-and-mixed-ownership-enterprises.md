# State enterprises and mixed-ownership enterprises ¶24-020

A State enterprise (formerly referred to as a State-owned enterprise) is treated as a separate business entity and is subject to income tax on any income it receives. They are excluded from the income tax exemption afforded to public authorities (¶5-605). A state enterprise is also a category of a special corporate entity (¶16-155). State enterprises are listed in Sch 36 pt A.

A mixed-ownership enterprise is excluded from the definition of "public authority" and therefore does not enjoy an income tax exemption either. A mixed-ownership enterprise can be included as part of a group of companies for the purposes of offsetting losses only if no other company in the group is a mixed-ownership enterprise. Mixed-ownership enterprises are listed in Sch 36 pt B.

Law: IT07 ss CW 38(5)(b), (5B), IC 3(2A), YA 1 ("public authority", "State enterprise"), Sch 36
