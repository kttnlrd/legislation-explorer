# Notice of proposed adjustment (NOPA) issued by Commissioner ¶4-130

Tax disputes are formally initiated by a NOPA. Such notice requires the relevant party to inform the respondent of any proposed adjustments to a person's tax position and to state the reasons for those adjustments.

Usually a NOPA is issued by the Commissioner after completing the audit of a taxpayer's affairs. Generally, the Commissioner will not issue an assessment until after the disputes procedure has been concluded.

However, in a limited number of situations, the Commissioner may issue an assessment without first issuing a NOPA. Those situations are outlined at ¶4-145.

Sometimes IR may discover an item that needs to be adjusted but is unable to issue a NOPA. This would occur when the proposed adjustment is already subject to a challenge or when the statutory time bar has expired (¶4-165).

The Commissioner may issue one or more NOPAs in respect of a tax return or an assessment. If an audit spans several years and the time bar is pending, it is possible for the Commissioner to issue one notice that covers more than one return period, issue and tax type to ensure that the assessment can still be issued within the time bar period.
