# Permitted disclosure: suspected offences under Companies Act 1993 ¶1-625

The Commissioner may pass information to the Registrar of Companies concerning offences under certain provisions of the Companies Act 1993.

The information may be communicated if the Commissioner or the Registrar reasonably suspects that:

## an offence under s 138A(1), 377, 382(4), 383(6), 385(9), 385AA(9) or 386A(2) of the Companies Act

1993 has been, is being, or will be committed, and

## the information is relevant for the purposes of preventing, detecting, investigating or providing

evidence of the offence.

The Commissioner must also be satisfied that the information is readily available, it is reasonable and practicable to communicate the information and that communication of the information is in the public interest.

The relevant offences, all of which carry a penalty of a fine of up to $200,000 or up to 5 years' imprisonment, are the following:

## serious breach of a director's duty to act in good faith and in best interests of company

## a person making or authorising false statements

## breach of statutory prohibition from managing or being a director, or promoter, of a company

## breach of a court order disqualifying a person from managing or being a director, or promoter, of a

company

## breach of notice from Registrar or Financial Markets Authority prohibiting a person's involvement as a

director, or promoter, of a company, or of managing a company, and

## director of a failed company being a director or promoter of, or managing, a phoenix company.

A "phoenix company", in relation to a failed company, is a company that, at any time before or within 5 years after the commencement of the liquidation of the failed company, is known by a pre-liquidation name of the failed company or a similar name: s 386B of the Companies Act 1993. "Pre-liquidation name" is any name, including a trading name, by which the failed company was known in the 12 months before commencement of its liquidation.

*Refs: s 138A(1), s 386B, Companies Act 1993*
