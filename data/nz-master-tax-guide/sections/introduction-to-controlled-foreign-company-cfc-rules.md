# Introduction to controlled foreign company (CFC) rules ¶26-038

The CFC rules may impute income in the hands of a New Zealand resident investor even though the person has not actually derived the income. This occurs if the person has an ownership interest above prescribed levels in a non-resident company earning passive income in the form of interest, certain dividends, royalties and rents.

The CFC rules are supported by the thin capitalisation rules, which may prevent interest deductibility in New Zealand if the funding assists with the generation of active income that is not tax under the CFC rules (¶26-620).

If the ownership interest in the non-resident company does not exceed the prescribed thresholds, the investment may be subject to taxation under the FIF rules. The foreign investments of a portfolio investment entity (PIE) are always subject to taxation under the FIF rules (¶26-110).
