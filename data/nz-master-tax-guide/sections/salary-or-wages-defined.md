# Salary or wages defined ¶3-016

"Salary or wages" is defined in s RD 5 as any salary, wages or allowances made to a person in connection with their employment, including:

## overtime pay, bonuses, gratuities, commissions or other pay of any kind (s RD 5(1)(b)(i))

## expenditure on account of an employee (s RD 5(2) and ¶3-018)

## periodic payments by way of pension, allowance or annuity made to a person or their spouse, civil

union partner, de facto partner, child or dependant in connection with the person's past employment (s RD 5(4))

## payment to working owners of look-through companies under s DC 3B and payments by a

partnership to a working partner under a contract of service (s RD 5(3) and (3B))

## a gratuitous payment for services within paragraph (a) of the definition of "pension" in s CF 1(2) (s RD

5(6)(a))

## any salary or allowance paid to a member of Parliament (s RD 5(5))

## any salary or principal allowance paid to a judicial officer (s RD 5(5))

## any salary or allowances paid to a member of the Employment Relations Authority (s RD 5(5))

## any salary paid to the Governor-General and any payment to the Governor-General on cessation of

holding office (s RD 5(5) and (5B) and ¶5-598)

## main benefits, veterans' pensions (other than certain pensions paid overseas) and New Zealand

superannuation (other than certain superannuation paid overseas) (s RD 5(6)(b)–(bc))

## all parental leave and preterm baby payments made under Pt 7A of the Parental Leave and

Employment Protection Act 1987 (s RD 5(7))

## basic grants and independent circumstances grants made under regulations made under s 645 of the

Education and Training Act 2020 (before 1 August 2020, s 193 of the Education Act 1964 or s 303 of the Education Act 1989) or an enactment substituted for that section (s RD 5(6)(c))

## an accident compensation earnings-related payment (s RD 5(1)(b)(iii))

## a payment of earnings compensation under the Compensation for Live Organ Donors Act 2016 (s RD

5(1)(b)(iiib))

## a retirement lump sum paid under Pt 5 subpart 7 of the Veterans' Support Act 2014 (s RD 5(6)(bd))

## weekly income compensation, weekly compensation or aggregated payments paid under certain

provisions of the Veterans' Support Act 2014 (s RD 5(6)(be)–(bg))

## the amount of an honorarium paid by Fire and Emergency New Zealand to a volunteer (s RD 5(6)

(bh))

## the provision of accommodation or an accommodation allowance under s CE 1(1)(bb) (s RD 5(8))

## an employer's superannuation cash contribution that an employee chooses to have treated as salary

or wages under s RD 68 (s RD 5(9))

## an unrepaid PAYE income overpayment that is treated as salary or wages under s RD 8B(2)(a) (s RD

5(10)).

A slightly different definition of "salary or wages" applies for KiwiSaver purposes (¶29-230).

Note that if a salary or wage earner receives an amount of income (not necessarily linked to employment) which has not had tax withheld on it, there may be provisional tax consequences (¶22-040).
