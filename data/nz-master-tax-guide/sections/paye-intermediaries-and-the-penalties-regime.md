# PAYE intermediaries and the penalties regime ¶14-024

Where an employer has transferred its pay as you earn (PAYE) obligations to a PAYE intermediary, the PAYE intermediary is subject to late filing penalties, late payment penalties and shortfall penalties as if the PAYE intermediary were the employer.

However, if the employer has failed:

## to pay the gross salary or wages to the intermediary, or

## to provide the information required by the intermediary within the agreed time

then the late filing penalty, late payment penalty and shortfall penalties will apply to the employer, not the intermediary (s 141JB of the TAA and ¶3-485).

A PAYE intermediary is also treated as an employer for the purposes of ss 22 and 23A of the KIW.
