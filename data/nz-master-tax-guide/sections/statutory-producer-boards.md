# Statutory producer boards ¶24-140

A "statutory producer board" is included in the definition of a company, and its activity treated as a business.

The term "statutory producer board" encompasses the following bodies:

## New Zealand Horticulture Export Authority

## New Zealand Meat Board

## New Zealand Pork Industry Board

## marketing authorities established by regulation under the Primary Products Marketing Act 1953, and

## primary producer boards and marketing boards established under any Act.

Levies received by a statutory producer board are, except for those charged for capital development, deemed to be income.

## Deduction for expenditure

The general permission in s DA 1 applies to expenditure incurred by the board in fulfilling its functions as if the expenditure were incurred in the derivation of assessable income or in the carrying on of a business by the board (s CV 5).

## Association rebates

A statutory producer board is also an "association" within the meaning given to that term in s YA 1 (¶24-090).

Consequently, it is permitted a deduction of the aggregate of the amount of rebates paid to members for their transactions with the board during the year. A statutory producer board may claim the deduction either for the income year in which the rebates are paid or for the income year in which the transactions giving rise to the rebates took place. Rebates that relate to business transactions between the member and the association are included as income in the hands of the member. If, however, the transactions or any part of them relate to non-business activities, rebates for such activities are not liable for tax. Where a rebate or any part of a rebate exceeds the profit attributable to the member's transactions with the association, the amount of the excess is treated as a dividend to the member and liable to tax in their hands (¶24-090).
