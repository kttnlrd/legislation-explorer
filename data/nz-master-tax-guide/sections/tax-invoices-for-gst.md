# Tax invoices for GST ¶32-150

From 1 April 2023, the provisions relating to tax invoices are repealed. For commentary on tax invoices before 1 April 2023, please refer to CCH, New Zealand Master Tax Guide 2023.

See ¶32-151 onwards for the new rules in relation to taxable supply information requirements.

Law: GST ss 2(1) ("invoice", "tax invoice")
