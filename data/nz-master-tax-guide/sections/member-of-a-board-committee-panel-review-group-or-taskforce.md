# Member of a board, committee, panel, review group or taskforce ¶3-280

A payment of fees to a member of a board, committee, panel, review group or task force is generally a schedular payment and therefore subject to withholding tax. The payment is either classified as:

## a payment of examiner's fees, or honoraria, or

## a payment for work or services performed by:

– a local government elected representative, or – an official of a community organisation, society, or club, or – a chair or member of a committee, board, or council, or – an official, chair, or member The withholding tax rate on these payments of fees to a member is 33¢ in the dollar, unless an exemption certificate is held or a special tax rate certificate is held, or the recipient has chosen their own rate (a minimum of 10¢ in the dollar).

See general article GA 25/01 "Tax on any fees paid to a member of a board, committee, panel, review group or task force" for more details on the withholding obligations of these types of payments.
