# Exempt income — disability support payments ¶5-655

Where a person receives a direct funding disability support payment — this being a payment made by Health New Zealand, the M#ori Health Authority, the Ministry for Disabled People, or the Ministry of Health to a disabled person, a person on their behalf, or their carer, to purchase disability support services — the payment will be treated as exempt income. "Disability support services" is defined in s 4 of the Pae Ora (Health Futures) Act 2022.
