# Migration of beneficiaries ¶25-440

A New Zealand resident beneficiary is taxed on all beneficiary income and taxable distributions whether comprised of amounts derived from New Zealand or from offshore.

A non-resident beneficiary is only taxed on beneficiary income and taxable distributions comprising amounts derived from New Zealand (¶25-230). See further at IS 25/16, "Tax residence" at 114 and 119.

Where a beneficiary moves to another jurisdiction, consideration must also be given as to how that beneficiary might be taxed with respect to a New Zealand trust, which will be a non-resident trust in another jurisdiction. With respect to Australia, see Practical Compliance Guideline PCG 2024/3 s 99B of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 — ATO compliance approach, which considers how property of a non-resident trust (or trust property accumulated while the trust was a non-resident) is paid to or applied for the benefit of a resident beneficiary.

Note that there is an anti-avoidance provision under s HC 23 to prevent beneficiaries from changing residence within a short time period to defeat the trust rules by changing residence (¶25-215).
