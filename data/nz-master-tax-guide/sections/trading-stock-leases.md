# Trading stock — leases ¶9-009

A lease that is not a finance lease is an excepted financial arrangement under the financial arrangements rules. Excepted financial arrangements that are trading stock or revenue account property must be valued at cost unless a nil value can be justified (¶9-060).

A lease that is an excepted financial arrangement is, in effect, an agreement under which a lessor transfers to a lessee the right to possess a personal property lease asset in consideration for a personal property lease payment, including a licence to use intangible property, a lease that is 2 or more consecutive or successive leases that are treated as one lease, a hire or bailment and a sublease.

A hire purchase agreement and an assignment of a hire purchase agreement are excluded from the definition of a lease under s YA 1.

*Refs: s YA 1*
