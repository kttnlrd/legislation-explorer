# Motor vehicle kilometre rates ¶50-084

The allowable deduction for motor vehicle expenditure may be determined using the kilometre rate method, which can be used regardless of the kilometres travelled (¶10-710).

## OS 19/04 (KM 2025)

In May 2025, the Commissioner released the following table of rates for the 2024–25 income year in OS 19/04 (KM 2025):

Vehicle Type Tier One Rate Tier Two rate Petrol $1.17 37 cents Diesel $1.26 35 cents Petrol Hybrid $0.86 21 cents Electric $1.08 19 cents

The Tier One Rate applies for the business portion of the first 14,000 kms travelled by the vehicle in a year.

The Tier One Rate increase reflects that increase in fuel costs for the 2025 income year. The Tier Two Rate is for running costs only. Taxpayers should use the Tier Two Rate for the business portion of any travel over 14,000 kms in a year. All other aspects of the calculation remain as directed in OS 19/04 discussed at ¶10-710.

## Historical motor vehicle kilometre rates

## OS 19/04 (2019)

In August 2019, IR released Operational Statements OS 19/04a, "Commissioner's statement on using a kilometre rate for business running of a motor vehicle – deductions" (¶10-710) and OS 19/04b, "Commissioner's statement on using a kilometre rate for employee reimbursement of a motor vehicle" (¶3-445). OS 19/04a explains how the kilometre rates are to be applied when dealing with deductions for the business use of a motor vehicle and OS 19/04b deals with the treatment of reimbursement payments to employees.

The rates that applied for the 2018–19 income year were as follows:

Vehicle Type Tier One Rate Tier Two Rate Petrol or Diesel 79 cents 30 cents Petrol Hybrid 79 cents 19 cents Electric 79 cents 9 cents

## OS 19/04 (KM 2020)

For the 2019–20 income year, the Commissioner originally advised that taxpayers could use the 2019 kilometre rates: Tax Information Bulletin ¶325-108 Vol 32, No 5, June 2020 at 23. This was a result of COVID-19 meaning the Commissioner had not received third party information to determine the 2020 kilometre rates. Taxpayers could use the 2019 rates and adjust their 2020 income tax returns under s 113 or 113A of the Tax Administration Act 1994 if there was a material difference from the final 2020 rates.

In December 2020, the Commissioner released the following table of rates for the 2020 income year in OS 19/04 (KM 2020) (Tax Information Bulletin ¶331-113 Vol 33, No 1, February 2021 at 57):

Vehicle Type Tier One Rate Tier Two rate Petrol or Diesel 82 cents 28 cents Petrol Hybrid 82 cents 17 cents Electric 82 cents 9 cents

## OS 19/04 (KM 2021)

In May 2021, the Commissioner released the following table of rates for the 2020–21 income year in OS 19/04 (KM 2021) (Tax Information Bulletin ¶336-109 Vol 33, No 6, July 2021 at 175):

Vehicle Type Tier One Rate Tier Two rate Petrol or Diesel 79 cents 27 cents Petrol Hybrid 79 cents 16 cents Electric 79 cents 9 cents

The Tier One Rate applies for the business portion of the first 14,000 kms travelled by the vehicle in a year.

The Tier Two Rate applies for the business portion of any travel in excess of 14,000 kms.

Where a logbook is maintained, the Tier One Rate can be applied for the business portion of the first 14,000 kms travelled, with the Tier Two Rate applying after that. However, where no logbook is maintained, the use of the Tier One Rate is limited to the first 3,500 kms travelled for business purposes. The Tier Two Rate must then be used for kilometres travelled over 3,500 kms.

Note that OS 19/04b provided that for the 2019–20 income year, employers could reimburse employees using the 2018–19 rates from the date of the statement, 16 August 2019.

Prescribed square metre rate for dual use of premises

With effect from 1 April 2017, for the 2017–18 and later income years, IR gives a taxpayer the option to use a single rate ("square metre rate") to the total area of any separately identifiable parts of a building that are used primarily for business purposes. The rate is based on average costs of utilities and insurance, but excludes rental, rates or mortgage costs. IR has set the following prescribed square metre rate for premises with dual use:

Income year Square metre rate 2024-25 $55.60 2023-24 $53.10 2022-23 $51.05 2021-22 $47.85 2020-21 $44.75 2019-20 $42.75 2018-19 $41.70 2017-18 $41.10

See ¶10-530 for commentary on this method.
