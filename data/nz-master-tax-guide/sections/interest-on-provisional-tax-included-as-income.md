# Interest on provisional tax included as income ¶22-356

Interest payable by the Commissioner on overpaid provisional tax is income to the recipient (¶5-281). The Commissioner may deduct resident withholding tax from gross interest paid on overpaid tax in accordance with that regime (¶15-040). The Commissioner may also apply interest against any unpaid tax not paid by the relevant due date (s 120F of the TAA and ¶22-354).
