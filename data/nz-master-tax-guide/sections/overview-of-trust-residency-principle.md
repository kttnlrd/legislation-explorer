# Overview of trust residency principle ¶25-400

Trusts are not treated as separate entities for tax purposes. Consequently, there is no universal test or residence for trusts (other than unit trusts that are treated as companies for tax purposes). However, the residence of the persons connected with the trust (the settlor, trustee and beneficiary) determines the tax treatment of trust income.
