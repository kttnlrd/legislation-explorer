# Child support may constitute a charge ¶12-039

Unpaid child support may constitute a charge on the person's property under subpart 5 of Pt 3 of the Land Transfer Act 2017 and the Personal Property Securities Act 1999. This charge operates and takes priority according to the relevant registration Act.

Registration of a charge over property for unpaid child support after the registration of a mortgage but before an advance of money secured by the mortgage has priority over the mortgage.

The Commissioner must release the charge registered over the property when the unpaid child support is paid. A fee is not required to be paid by the Commissioner to either register or release a charge.

Law: CHS s 169(4)–(7)
