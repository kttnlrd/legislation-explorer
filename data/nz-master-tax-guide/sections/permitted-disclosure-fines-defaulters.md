# Permitted disclosure: fines defaulters ¶1-629

Only where either an information matching agreement has been entered into under Pt 10 of the Privacy Act 1993 and continued under the Privacy Act 2020, or an approved information sharing agreement has been entered into under subpart 1 of Pt 7 of the Privacy Act 2020, may information be exchanged between the Commissioner and an officer, employee or agent of the Ministry of Justice for the purpose of establishing an information matching programme to enable the Ministry of Justice to locate persons with unpaid fines.

If the Commissioner holds information relating to a fines defaulter, the Commissioner may supply any of the following information about that person to the Ministry of Justice:

## the person's last known address and telephone number, and

## the name, address and telephone number of the person's last known employer.

A fines defaulter is a person in default in payment of:

## an amount required to be paid under sentence or by court order, including reparation, expenses,

costs of enforcement and an offender levy but excluding an amount ordered to be paid in civil proceedings

## a fine imposed by the High Court

## a fine relating to gains from drug dealing

## an amount payable for the shortfall on the sale of a confiscated motor vehicle.
