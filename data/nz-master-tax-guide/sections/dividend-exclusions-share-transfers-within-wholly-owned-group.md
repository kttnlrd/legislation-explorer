# Dividend exclusions — share transfers within wholly-owned group ¶16-730

A transfer of an excepted financial arrangement held on revenue account to another member of a wholly- owned group of companies is not a dividend. Nor does the transfer generate a realised loss that can be taken into account for tax purposes.

Law: IT07 ss CD 28, ED 2(4)
