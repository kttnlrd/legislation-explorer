# Responsibilities and status under PAYE rules and ESCT rules when using PAYE intermediaries ¶3-479

## Employer

An employer who uses a PAYE intermediary will generally not be subject to the PAYE rules or, if taken on by the PAYE intermediary, the ESCT rules, provided all the following conditions are met:

## If the employer has authorised the PAYE intermediary to direct fund transfers from the employer's

bank account in satisfaction of the obligations under the PAYE arrangement, the employer ensures that, at a time specified by the PAYE intermediary, there are sufficient funds in the employer's bank account to satisfy the obligations of the PAYE intermediary for the pay period (s RP 9).

## If the employer has not authorised the PAYE intermediary to direct fund transfers from the employer's

bank account, the employer pays into the trust account of the PAYE intermediary:

## the employees' gross salary or wages for the pay period after deducting any amount that is owed by

an employee to the employer and may be lawfully withheld by the employer from the salary or wages (s RP 10(2))

## if the employer has made a payment of salary or wages (either by cash or cheque) to the employee

(see below under the heading "Cash payments made by employer"), the deductions made as required under the PAYE rules and ESCT rules relating to that cash payment (s RP 10(1)), and

## if the PAYE intermediary has taken on the employer's ESCT obligations, the employer's

superannuation cash contributions made in the pay period by the employer (s RP 11).

## The employer keeps the required records relating to payments made to employees set out in table 1

of Sch 3 of the TAA (¶3-131): s 22AA of the TAA.

## The employer provides the information requested by the PAYE intermediary within the time agreed by

the employer and the intermediary (s RP 8).

## Cash payments made by employer

An employer who has engaged a PAYE intermediary may, in accordance with s RP 12, make a payment of salary or wages directly to the employee if all of the following conditions are met:

## the payment is made on a day in a pay period that is not the usual day for a payment of salary or

wages for the pay period (ie the payment is made on a day that is not a normal pay day)

## the payment is an advance of the employee's salary or wages, salary or wages owed to the

employee for an earlier pay period, or a payment on the termination of the employee's employment

## the employer makes the deductions required under the PAYE rules and ESCT rules from the salary

or wages paid as if the employer did not have a PAYE intermediary, and

## the PAYE and ESCT deducted by the employer are made available to the PAYE intermediary as

required — see above under the heading "Employer".

However, the fact that a PAYE intermediary is used does not mean that the employer ceases to be liable for the payment of an employee's salary or wages for the pay period or for making an employer's superannuation cash contribution on behalf of the employee.

## PAYE intermediary

In accordance with s RP 14 a PAYE intermediary who acts on behalf of an employer generally assumes the PAYE obligations and, if taken on, the ESCT obligations of that employer for its employees for the pay period, including the obligations to:

## calculate and make tax deductions and pay those deductions to the Commissioner by electronic

means and in the prescribed electronic format (¶3-020)

## transfer the amount of any payroll donation to the relevant recipient within the specified time period

(¶11-063)

## deliver the relevant employment income information required under subpart 3C of the TAA to the

Commissioner by electronic means and in the prescribed electronic format (¶3-023)

## keep records in accordance with s 22AA of the TAA (¶3-131).

If a PAYE intermediary has been authorised to direct fund transfers from the employer's bank account (see above under the heading "Employer"), the PAYE intermediary must direct that the amount of the deductions required to be made from the gross salary or wages of the employee under the PAYE rules and the ESCT rules be transferred to either the Commissioner or the PAYE intermediary's trust account as appropriate. This direction must be made at or before the time of the transfer of the salary or wages owing to the employee (ss RP 15 and RP 16).

However, a PAYE intermediary does not become liable as an employer for the payment to the employee of the salary or wages for the pay period (s RP 13(2)).

A PAYE intermediary must maintain and operate administration and information technology systems that protect the privacy of the personal information and payment details acquired in the performance of its functions.

A PAYE intermediary may make an amended return relating to employees of the employer for a different pay period. The intermediary will be responsible for the accuracy of these amendments.

## Refund by Commissioner of Inland Revenue

Under ss RA 19 and RM 7 the Commissioner must refund to a PAYE intermediary any payment of deductions that is made by the PAYE intermediary:

## in reliance on a payment to the trust account of the PAYE intermediary that was made by the

employer and later dishonoured by the employer, or made by a person because of a mistake and later recovered from the PAYE intermediary by the person, or

## because of a mistake by the PAYE intermediary or another person and from funds that were not

provided to the PAYE intermediary by the employer for the payment to which the deductions relate (ie the salary or wages or employer's superannuation cash contribution) or for the payment of the deductions.

## KiwiSaver

A PAYE intermediary is treated as the employer for the purposes of the information required to be supplied to the Commissioner in relation to new employees under ss 22 and 23 of the KiwiSaver Act 2006.

Part 3 of the KiwiSaver Act deals with KiwiSaver contributions. The role of employers under that Part also applies to PAYE intermediaries.
