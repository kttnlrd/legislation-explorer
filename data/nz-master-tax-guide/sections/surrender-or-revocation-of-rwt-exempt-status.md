# Surrender or revocation of RWT-exempt status ¶15-140

## Surrender

When a person becomes aware that they no longer meet the requirements for RWT-exempt status, they must notify the Commissioner within 5 days after the day they become aware. If requested to do so, the person must provide the Commissioner, within 5 days, with the full name and last known address of all persons to whom they have advised their RWT-exempt status.

## Revocation of RWT-exempt status

RWT-exempt status may be revoked by the Commissioner at any time if:

## the Commissioner reasonably believes the person no longer satisfies the requirements upon which

the exemption was based

## the person did not meet the requirements, having obtained the exemption on the basis of misleading

information

## the person should not have been provided with RWT-exempt status

## contrary to the person's estimate, evidence shows that the person did not derive annual gross income

of more than $2 million or the evidence was unsatisfactory or was materially incorrect or misleading, if this was the basis of the exemption (¶15-130), or

## the person has not paid any income tax by the due date.

If the Commissioner considers the person qualifies for another category of exemption, the Commissioner must not revoke the RWT-exempt status.

## Notification by Commissioner

The Commissioner must notify a person whose RWT-exempt status has been revoked within 30 working days of the revocation. The Commissioner may also request the person provide the Commissioner, within 5 days, with the full name and last known address of all persons to whom they have advised their RWT- exempt status.

The person must notify, within 5 days, all those persons to whom they have advised their RWT-exempt status and from whom they expect to receive further payments of resident passive income that their status has been revoked.
