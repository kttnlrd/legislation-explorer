# Pay in lieu of notice ¶3-301

When an employee is dismissed and given pay in lieu of notice, that pay should be treated as an extra pay for tax deduction purposes, not as ordinary salary or wages (Tax Information Bulletin ¶613-123 Vol 6, No 13, May 1995 at 20).
