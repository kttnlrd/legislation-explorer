# Use of money interest is income ¶5-281

Use of money interest (UOMI) payable by the Commissioner to a person for overpaid tax is income of the person.

Section EF 4(1) provides that UOMI payable by the Commissioner is allocated to the income year in which the Commissioner pays the interest.

Under s CC 8(3), UOMI is disregarded for the purposes of the financial arrangements rules.

*Refs: Section EF 4(1), s CC 8(3)*
