# Interest liability on a student loan ¶8-035

Interest is only imposed on loan balances of overseas-based borrowers.

Interest is applied to the student loan from the first day on which the borrower is overseas-based and is calculated daily on the loan balance at the current interest rate. Interest only compounds on 31 March of each year. The Commissioner is required to notify the borrower in writing of the amount of interest charged after interest is added to the borrower's loan balance.

For the 2025–26 income year the interest rate is 4.9% (3.3% pa for the 2024–2025 tax year).

Example: Rose is an overseas-based borrower and not on a repayment holiday. Rose's loan balance at 1 April 2024 is $15,000. Rose will be required to pay $1,000 in 2 instalments to meet her overseas-based repayment obligation. She pays the first amount on its due date (30 September), but pays the second amount early on 1 March (instead of its due date of 31 March). She also makes one voluntary repayment on 30 September. In the following year she makes the 2 instalments, and no voluntary payments.

Date paid Repayment Amount 30 September 2024 Repayment obligation payment $500 30 September 2024 Voluntary repayment $400 1 March 2025 Repayment obligation payment $500 30 September 2025 Repayment obligation payment $500 1 March 2026 Repayment obligation payment $500

Each time Rose makes a repayment a new daily interest calculation starts.

Rose's interest calculation from 1 April 2024 to 30 September 2024: ($15,000 × 3.3%) × (183/365) = $248.18.

## Note: For the purpose of calculating interest for the rest of the income year, the interest of $248.18 to 30 September is not added

to Rose's loan balance yet. If Rose checks her balance she will see this interest on her loan.

Rose's interest calculation from 1 October 2024 to 1 March 2025: (($15,000 # $900) × 3.3%) × (152/365) = $193.77.

## Note: Rose's loan balance for this daily interest calculation is now $14,100 ($15,000 # $900).

Rose's interest calculation from 2 March 2025 to 31 March 2025: (($14,100 # $500) × 3.3%) × (30/365) = $36.89.

## Note: Rose's loan balance for this daily interest calculation is now $13,600 ($14,100 # $500).

Rose's loan balance at the end of the tax year, including her daily interest, is $14,078.84: ($13,600 + $248.18 + $193.77 + $36.89).

Rose's interest calculation from 1 April 2025 to 30 September 2025: ($14,078.84 × 4.9%) × (183/365) = $345.88.

## Note: For the purpose of calculating interest for the rest of the income year, the interest of $345.88 to 30 September is not added

to Rose's loan balance yet. If Rose checks her balance she will see this interest on her loan.

Rose's interest calculation from 1 October 2025 to 1 March 2026: (($14,078.84 # $500) × 4.9%) × (153/365) = $278.91.

## Note: Rose's loan balance for this daily interest calculation is now $13,578.84 ($14,078.84 # $500).

Rose's interest calculation from 2 March 2026 to 31 March 2026:

(($13,578.84 # $500) × 4.9%) × (30/365) = $54.69.

## Note: Rose's loan balance for this daily interest calculation is now $13,078.84 ($13,578.84 # $500).

Rose's loan balance at the end of the tax year, including her daily interest, is $13,758.32: ($13,078.84 + $345.88 + $278.91 + $54.69).

Compound interest Rose's daily interest for the year is added to her loan balance annually. This means that the balance IR charges interest on for the next tax year includes Rose's previous year's daily interest.

In the example above, Rose's new loan balance for calculating her daily interest, as at 1 April 2026, increases from $13,078.84 to $13,758.32.

If Rose was a New Zealand-based borrower, her daily interest totalling $679.48 would be written off and her loan balance would not be increased by this amount.
