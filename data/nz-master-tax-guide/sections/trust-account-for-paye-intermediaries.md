# Trust account for PAYE intermediaries ¶3-476

There are certain restrictions in place for a PAYE intermediary's bank account. A PAYE intermediary must establish a trust account with a registered bank. The account must be named a trust account.

The only deposits that may be made to the account are:

## a payment of gross salary or wages by an employer

## an amount of employer's superannuation cash contribution paid by an employer

## an amount of tax for a payment of salary or wages required under the PAYE rules and employer's

superannuation contribution tax rules or made under s RP 12

## an amount of a refund made by the Commissioner (¶3-479), and

## interest on the amount of the funds in the trust account.

The only withdrawals that may be made from the account are:

## payments of net salary or wages to employees

## an amount of an employer's superannuation cash contributions paid by employers on behalf of

employees

## an amount of tax for a payment of salary or wages withheld under s RP 12 (¶3-479)

## deductions from the salary or wages of an employee, or an employer's superannuation cash

contribution made on behalf of an employee, that the employer would otherwise make, and

## interest earned by the funds in the account.

A payment relating to an employee that is credited to the trust account is held by the PAYE intermediary on trust for the benefit of the employee and the Commissioner according to their respective rights and obligations.

Interest earned in the trust account by a payment that relates to an employee is held beneficially by the PAYE intermediary.

*Refs: s RP 12*
