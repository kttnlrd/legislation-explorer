# Trustee tax rate ¶25-080

The Taxation (Annual Rates for 2024–25, Emergency Response, and Remedial Measures) Act 2025 introduced the following trustee tax rate remedials:

## clarification that income subject to the minor or corporate beneficiary rules is subject to a 39% tax

rate, regardless of whether the trust is eligible for an exclusion from the 39% trustee tax rate

## amendments to ensure beneficiary income derived from a disabled beneficiary trust by a minor is not

subject to the minor beneficiary rule

## clarification that when a company derives beneficiary income subject to the corporate beneficiary

rule, the capital gain amount included in the company's available capital distribution amount is the after- tax amount

## amendments to ensure trusts that no longer hold shares in electricity distribution companies but

continue to have the same class of beneficiaries for which the trust was established also qualify as energy consumer trusts.

## Trustee tax rate

From 1 April 2024, trustee income is taxed at the flat rate of 39% unless one of the following exceptions applies in which case the trustee tax rate remains at 33%:

## a de minimis trust (see below)

## a deceased estate for the income year the estate was created and the following 3 income years

## a disabled beneficiary trust, or

## an energy consumer trust (or a lines trust).

A legacy superannuation fund is also excluded from the 39% tax rate and subject to tax at 28% (¶29-410).

Before 1 April 2024, the trustee tax rate was 33%.

## De minimis trust

A trust is a "de minimis trust" for an income year if the net income (ie trustee income less deductible expenses) of the trustee is $10,000 or less (s HC 40). For the purposes of the $10,000 threshold, the net trustee income excludes any income treated as trustee income under the minor beneficiary rules (s HC 35 and ¶25-115) or the corporate beneficiary rules (s HC 38 and ¶25-120).

The eligibility criteria are to be assessed on a per-trust and per-income year basis. While closely related trusts can each separately qualify as a de minimis trust, a taxpayer who settles multiple trusts or fragments an existing trust to take advantage of the 33% tax rate may raise tax avoidance concerns for the Commissioner (Tax Information Bulletin, ¶364-102 Vol 36, No 2, May 2024 at 48).

## Deceased estate

The trustee income of a deceased estate is subject to a tax rate of 33% for the income year of the relevant person's death and the following 3 income years (s HC 8B). If the deceased estate is still in existence after this time period, the higher tax rate of 39% applies to the trustee income unless the estate qualifies as a de minimis trust.

Disabled beneficiary trust

The trustee income derived by a disabled beneficiary trust is subject to the 33% tax rate (s HC 39). A "disabled beneficiary trust" is defined as a trust with one or more disabled beneficiaries and no other beneficiaries may receive distributions except on the dissolution of the trust if no disabled beneficiaries are alive. A disabled beneficiary for an income year is defined as a person who receives one or more of the following payments (including carers who receive payments on behalf of a disabled beneficiary) under the Social Security Act 2018 for at least part of the income year:

## a supported living payment on the ground of restricted work capacity

## a child disability allowance

## jobseeker support on the ground of health condition, injury, or disability, or

## a disability allowance.

A person who reaches the age of 65 and receives a support living payment, the jobseeker support payment or a disability allowance in the income year or the prior income year also qualifies as a disabled beneficiary.
