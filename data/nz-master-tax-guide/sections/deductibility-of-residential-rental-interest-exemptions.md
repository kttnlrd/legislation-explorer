# Deductibility of residential rental interest — exemptions ¶10-815

As discussed in ¶10-814, generally deductions for interest connected to residential rental properties is disallowed. There are a number of residential properties that are excluded from the general rule.

## Which residential properties are not caught by the rules?

"Excepted residential land" is not subject to interest limitation rules. Interest can be claimed in respect of these properties if the usual principles of deductibility are met. Excepted residential land is listed in Sch 15, being land to the extent to which it is:

## the person's main home — ¶10-816

## business premises (excluding business premises that are used or available for use in a business of

supplying accommodation, unless it is also the person's main home) — ¶10-817

## farmland (including farmhouses and working quarters) — ¶10-817

## a hospital, convalescent home, nursing home, or hospice — ¶10-817

## a boarding establishment (as defined) — ¶10-817

## a hotel, motel, inn, hostel, or camping ground — ¶10-817

## a rest home or retirement village — ¶10-817

## student accommodation — ¶10-817

## employee accommodation — ¶10-817

## M#ori excepted land — ¶10-817

## build-to-rent land — ¶10-819.

## Which residential properties are not caught by the rules

Further exemptions to the interest limitation rules are specifically listed in s DH 4:

## new build land — ¶10-818

## land acquired for the purposes of business under s CB 7 — ¶10-817

## land acquired for development, division or building to create new build land — ¶10-817

## social, emergency, transitional, and support housing — ¶10-817

## council housing — ¶10-817

## Kainga Ora-Homes and Communities — ¶10-817.

Lastly, land outside New Zealand is not caught by the interest limitation rules.
