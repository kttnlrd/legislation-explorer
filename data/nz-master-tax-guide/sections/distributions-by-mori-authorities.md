# Distributions by M#ori authorities ¶24-311

Both cash and non-cash benefits provided to members are treated as distributions for the purpose of the M#ori authority rules, including amounts paid, credited or applied to members, advances that are not bona fide investments but are virtually a distribution of income and taxable bonus issues made by M#ori authorities that are companies. Transfers of property between M#ori authorities and their members may also be treated as distributions to the extent that the consideration (if any) paid for the property does not reflect the market value of the property. Where amounts would otherwise be dividends to members, the M#ori authority tax rules prevail and the amounts are treated as distributions made by the M#ori authority to the member, except for the purpose of s CW 10 (exemption for dividends between wholly-owned companies).

M#ori authorities may make taxable distributions and non-taxable distributions. A "taxable M#ori authority distribution" is a distribution made from income of the M#ori authority derived in the 2004–05 or subsequent income year that is not exempt from tax in the hands of the M#ori authority. A taxable bonus issue made by a M#ori authority to a member is also a taxable M#ori authority distribution. Taxable M#ori authority distributions are included in the income of members. All other distributions (eg distributions of exempt income or income earned under the rules applying to M#ori authorities before the 2004–05 income year) are not taxable to members.
