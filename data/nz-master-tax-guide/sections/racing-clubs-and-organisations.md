# Racing clubs and organisations ¶24-136

Amounts derived by most racing clubs are exempt income provided that members of the club are not able to use the club's funds for their own private profit-making purposes. The income of the following organisations is also exempt: New Zealand Thoroughbred Racing, TAB NZ (before 1 August 2020, the Racing Industry Transition Agency), Harness Racing New Zealand, the New Zealand Greyhound Racing Association and the Racing Integrity Board and Racing New Zealand.
