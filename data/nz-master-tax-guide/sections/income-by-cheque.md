# Income by cheque ¶5-065

Cheques have been phased out of use in New Zealand. Inland Revenue (IR) has not been accepting cheques since 1 March 2020, and most banks stopped accepting cheques in 2021. For commentary on income by cheque, see CCH, 2023 New Zealand Master Tax Guide.
