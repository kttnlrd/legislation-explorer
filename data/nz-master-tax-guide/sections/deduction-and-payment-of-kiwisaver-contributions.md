# Deduction and payment of KiwiSaver contributions ¶29-240

Deduction of employee KiwiSaver contributions and payment of compulsory employer contributions begin from the time a new employee commences work or the employer receives a deduction notice. If an employee later opts out of the scheme, any contributions already deducted or paid will have to be refunded (¶29-225).

All employee and employer KiwiSaver contributions must be paid to IR at the time the usual PAYE deductions are paid (¶3-020). The details of employer and employee KiwiSaver contributions form part of the employee income information requirements (¶3-023).

In order to improve IR's ability to ensure the correct contribution amounts are being received and that they are taxed at the correct rate, employers must provide in their employment income information the amount of salary and wages a KiwiSaver deduction is made from for a new employee if there is a difference between amounts an employer must treat as gross earnings for calculating PAYE and amounts they must treat as salary and wages for calculating KiwiSaver employee and employer contributions.

IR forwards employee and employer contributions to an employee's scheme provider as soon as practicable after receiving the employee income information that an employee or employer contribution has been deducted (even where the amount has not yet been received by IR), meaning the contributions can be forwarded to scheme providers sooner than they otherwise would be.

It has been clarified that if a change occurs to the minimum employee KiwiSaver contribution rate that affects the contribution to be deducted from a payment of salary or wages, the rate to be applied to determine the amount of the contribution is the rate applying on the day on which the salary or wages are paid. In addition, the compulsory employer contribution rate to be applied if there is a change in that rate is the rate applying on the day on which the salary or wages are paid.

The penalty provisions of the TAA apply to late payments of KiwiSaver deductions to IR (¶14-047).

IR will hold all contributions received for 62 days after a member joins KiwiSaver before passing these on to the KiwiSaver provider. IR may hold further contributions if agreement has been reached with the provider on a minimum level of contributions that can be passed on. All contributions held by IR will earn interest, which will be paid net of tax.

## Employer contributions for employees in 2 or more funds

Section 101FC of the KIW may exempt an employer from making a top-up contribution if an employee is a member of a KiwiSaver scheme and an existing employer superannuation fund. Provided that the amount of the contributions to the existing employer superannuation fund is calculated using a rate equal to or greater than the compulsory employer contribution rate, there is no additional contribution payable to the employee's KiwiSaver fund.

## Grace period

An employer is given a grace period for non-payment of compulsory employer contributions in certain circumstances (s 101FB).
