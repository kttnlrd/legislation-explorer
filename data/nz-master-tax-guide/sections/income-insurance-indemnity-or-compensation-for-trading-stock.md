# Income — insurance, indemnity or compensation for trading stock ¶5-346

Part of an amount of insurance, indemnity or compensation received for the loss or destruction of, or damage to:

## trading stock, or

## anything acquired, manufactured or produced for a purpose that is ancillary to a business of

manufacturing or producing goods for sale or exchange is income if the person is allowed a deduction (not being a deduction for depreciation loss) in an income year for the cost of the asset. The income amount is the part of the receipt that is attributable to the asset, and it is allocated to the income year in which it is received.

Law: IT07 s CG 6
