# Partnerships and deductions ¶23-125

For the purposes of the partnership's tax return, net income is computed in the same way as for an individual, ie annual total deductions are offset against annual gross income, giving rise to either a net income or a tax loss figure (¶1-090). However, unlike an individual who may carry a resulting loss balance forward to be used against future net income, the partnership may not do so (¶23-080). Certain expenditure incurred by a partnership is closely scrutinised by IR, as some payments may be seen as simple profit- sharing arrangements. The general deduction provisions of subpart DA are applicable (¶23-128–¶23-136, ¶23-142 and ¶23-144).
