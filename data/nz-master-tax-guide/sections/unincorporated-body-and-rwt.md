# Unincorporated body and RWT ¶15-160

When an "unincorporated body" (eg a partnership, a joint venture or trustees of a trust) has RWT-exempt status in relation to the carrying on of a taxable activity, the following rules under s RE 30 apply:

## the members cannot themselves have RWT-exempt status in relation to that taxable activity

## payments made in the course of that activity are deemed to be made by the body and not by any

member

## payments made to any member acting as a member of that body are deemed to have been made to

the body and not to the member

## the RWT-exempt status is in the name of the body

## changes in membership have no effect so far as RWT is concerned except that (1) members are

jointly and severally liable while they are members, and their estates are severally liable upon their deaths (where the member(s) are individuals), and (2) membership does not cease until a change of membership is notified to the Commissioner.

Note the above rules do not apply to a limited partnership (s RE 30(1B)).

Retrospective amendments have been made and under new s RE 31 of the IT07, limited partnerships may apply for RWT-exempt status in the name of the limited partnership and, once granted, payments made to or by a limited partner are treated as payments made to or by the limited partnership. A general partner has joint and several liability for outstanding RWT liabilities if the general partner was a partner in the limited partnership at the time the RWT liability was incurred. For the purposes of determining joint and several liability, a general partner continues as a partner until the Commissioner is notified that the person is no longer a partner (s RE 31(5)).

Amendments to s 32IB of the TAA clarifies that the $2 million income amount, deduction, assessable income and credit amounts referred to in the RWT-exempt status eligibility criteria (ss 32E(2) and 32I(1) of the TAA) are determined on an aggregate basis meaning the partnership is treated as a non-transparent entity for the purposes of this test.

Responsibilities in relation to RWT are the joint and several responsibilities of the following persons where a body is not a partnership, joint venture or trustees of a trust:

## every member holding office as president, chairperson, treasurer, secretary or similar, or

## in default of any such member, every committee member.

*Refs: s RE 30, s RE 30(1B), s RE 31, s RE 31(5), s 32I, ss 32E(2)*
