# Dividend exclusions — non-taxable bonus issues ¶16-685

A non-taxable bonus issue is not a dividend.

A non-taxable bonus issue is a bonus issue that is not a taxable bonus issue (¶16-570). Essentially this means that there is neither a cash alternative nor an election by the company for taxable bonus issue treatment.

Law: IT07 ss CD 29, YA 1 ("non-taxable bonus issue")
