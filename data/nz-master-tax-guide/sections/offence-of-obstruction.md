# Offence of obstruction ¶14-280

An offence is committed by any person who obstructs the Commissioner or an IR officer acting in the lawful discharge of the duties or in the exercise of the Commissioner's or officer's powers under a tax law.

The penalty for this offence is a maximum fine of $25,000 for the first offence and $50,000 for subsequent offences. See Highfield v C of IR (2000) 19 NZTC 15,609.

For discussion on shortfall penalties that may be increased due to obstruction by the taxpayer, see ¶14-150.
