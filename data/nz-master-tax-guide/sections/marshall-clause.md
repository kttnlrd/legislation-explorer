# Marshall Clause ¶90-200

1. The debt shall be payable by the Borrower to the Lender on demand. 2. The Lender may at any time demand that the Borrower repay the debt in whole or in part. 3. For so long as any part of the debt remains unpaid, the Borrower shall pay to the Lender interest on the debt from time to time outstanding during the preceding 12 months, calculated on a daily basis at the rate of ×% pa (or such other rate as the parties may from time to time agree). The interest will be payable on 31 March in each year, unless cl 5 applies. 4. Any demand for interest ("the Notice"):

## must be made in writing by facsimile transmission, e-mail or letter and signed by the Lender

## must be sent to any one of the Borrowers at the physical or e-mail address or facsimile number of

that Borrower

## shall not be effective until received by the Borrower,or

## [a further clause may be added here setting out when a demand is deemed to be received].

5. The Notice must be made no later than 1 March in each year of the Notice. If demand is not made by this date, the Borrower shall not be liable to pay the Lender any interest in respect of the 12 months ending on 31 March in that year.
