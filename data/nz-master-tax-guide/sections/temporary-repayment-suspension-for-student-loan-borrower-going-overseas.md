# Temporary repayment suspension for student loan borrower going overseas ¶8-050

A borrower who intends going overseas can apply for a temporary repayment suspension of up to 365 days.

A temporary repayment suspension means repayment obligations are reduced to zero but interest will still accrue on the loan.

A borrower who intends to be based overseas may apply to the Commissioner for a temporary repayment suspension. The application can be made from New Zealand or from overseas, but no application can be made by an overseas-based borrower (s 107). Therefore, the application should be made within 183 days of leaving New Zealand (¶8-025).

At the time of making the application the borrower must nominate a contact person who resides in New Zealand. The individual must be prepared to act as the borrower's contact person and the following information about the individual must be provided to the Commissioner:

## name and a New Zealand postal address

## an email address and a New Zealand phone number (if applicable)

## any further information specified in the regulations.

The Commissioner may grant a temporary repayment suspension to a borrower who has completed an acceptable application, provided a contact person (and the required details above) and not reached the borrower's limit.

Under s 107B a borrower who is granted a temporary repayment suspension becomes entitled to that repayment suspension on the day on which the borrower becomes overseas-based.

For overseas-based borrowers who choose to not have a temporary repayment suspension, the usual repayment rules for overseas-based borrowers will apply (¶8-045).

*Refs: s 107, s 107B*
