# Rewards from customer loyalty programmes not income ¶5-683

IR has issued a number of binding product rulings for various customer loyalty programmes. In general, the Commissioner's view is that the accumulation and redemption of points for rewards under these programmes is not income for tax purposes. For example, BR Prd 09/09 in relation to the redemption of Air New Zealand air points dollars and BR Prd 02/10, issued in respect of the Mico Wakefield Ltd customer trade loyalty programme.
