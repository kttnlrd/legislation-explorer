# Election to be a provisional taxpayer ¶22-048

A taxpayer who is not otherwise required to pay provisional tax may elect to become a provisional taxpayer.

Taxpayers are eligible to make such an election if they pay provisional tax of more than $5,000 on or before the date of instalment F (Sch 3), or, if the taxpayer is in a transitional year, on or before the final instalment date, and had (on the day that the first payment is made) a reasonable expectation of being a provisional taxpayer for that year. The election is made in the annual tax return for the tax year to which the election relates.

This provision does not apply to taxpayers described in s RC 3(2) (discussed in ¶22-040).
