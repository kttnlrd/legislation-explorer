# Reverse takeovers and mergers of limited attribution companies ¶16-217

A specific extension of the continuity rules covers the situation where a smaller limited attribution company takes over or amalgamates with a larger one.

In that situation, if the shareholders of an initial parent company become the shareholders in a new parent company in exactly the same proportions as their shareholding in the initial parent company and the continuity rules are met, then any wholly owned subsidiaries will be deemed to have transferred from one parent to the other without breaching the continuity rules.

Law: IT07 ss YA 1 ("ownership interest"), YC 18
