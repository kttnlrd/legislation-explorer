# Approval and revocation of PAYE intermediaries ¶3-475

An employer can enter into an arrangement with a person to transfer the employer's PAYE and employer's superannuation contribution tax (ESCT) obligations to a PAYE intermediary. The PAYE intermediary will be responsible for making the required payments and tax deductions and filing the relevant returns under the PAYE and ESCT rules on behalf of the employer.

## Approval

A person who meets the requirements set out in s 124K of the TAA may apply to the Commissioner for approval to become a PAYE intermediary. An application to become a PAYE intermediary must be made to the Commissioner by giving notice that:

## a trust account has been established and will be operated as required under the PAYE intermediary

provisions (see below)

## the applicant will comply with the PAYE and ESCT rules (if the employer's obligations under these

rules are taken over by the applicant)

## the required administration and information technology systems to protect the privacy of the personal

information and payment details of employees have been established and will be maintained (¶3-479)

## the applicant, or each member of the applicant or each of the persons acting as a director, secretary

or statutory officer of the applicant, and any principal, is not a discharged or undischarged bankrupt and has not been convicted of an offence involving fraud and is eligible to be a company director, and

## before acting as a PAYE intermediary, the applicant will inform the employer that the Commissioner

does not guarantee payments by the applicant to employees of the employer and will provide the employer with a copy of the applicant's notice to the Commissioner.

An application may be made on form IR910.

The Commissioner may:

## approve an applicant as a PAYE intermediary if he is satisfied that the applicant will comply with

the PAYE and ESCT rules and the applicant's information technology systems will allow it to make payments and file returns in the prescribed format

## specify a period for which a person is approved as a PAYE intermediary.

A person must have entered agreements that have been approved by the Commissioner (¶3-477) with at least 10 employers to be a PAYE intermediary.

## Revocation

The Commissioner may revoke the approval if any of the following occurs:

## the person fails to comply with the PAYE or the ESCT rules (where they have assumed the

employers' obligations for those rules)

## the person or a member of the person or a person acting as a director, secretary or statutory officer

of the person or a principal no longer meets the "fitness" requirement under s 124K of the TAA, ie has been made bankrupt, has been convicted of an offence involving fraud or is not eligible to be a company director

## the person has been put into liquidation or receivership

## the person, if a company, ceases to be registered in New Zealand.

If approval is revoked, the Commissioner must send a notice of revocation to the PAYE intermediary and any affected employer (being an employer who has notified the Commissioner that the PAYE intermediary

is a PAYE intermediary for the employer). This notice must be given at least 14 days before the date the revocation takes effect.

Any decision made by the Commissioner on a person's status as a PAYE intermediary cannot be challenged.

Law: IT07 ss RP 2, RP 6, YA 1 ("PAYE intermediary"); TAA ss 124H(1), 124I–124K
