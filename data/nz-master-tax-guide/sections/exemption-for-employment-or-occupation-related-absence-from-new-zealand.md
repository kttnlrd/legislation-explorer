# Exemption for employment or occupation-related absence from New Zealand ¶8-145

A borrower making application for an exemption because of an absence from New Zealand for reasons of employment or occupation must (Sch 1 cl 5):

## have been a New Zealand resident during the time in question

## have a permanent place of abode only in New Zealand

## provide evidence that they receive either

## a PAYE income payment derived from New Zealand, or

## income from a business that has a permanent place of business in New Zealand, and

## provide evidence that the majority of their personal absences from New Zealand are because of their

employment or occupation.

Law: SLSA 2011 s 25(1)(e), Sch 1
