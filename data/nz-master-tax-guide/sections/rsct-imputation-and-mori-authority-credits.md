# RSCT — imputation and M#ori authority credits ¶29-315

To the extent that a retirement scheme savings contribution is a distribution of profits to a shareholder, unitholder or member, imputation credits or M#ori authority credits (as appropriate) may be attached to the contribution, and used to meet the contributor's retirement scheme contribution tax (RSCT) liability in respect of the contribution.

If the amount of imputation or M#ori authority credits attached to the contribution exceeds the RSCT liability, the excess is treated as a credit to the individual saver, and can be used to reduce the individual saver's overall tax liability or be carried forward. If an excess occurs, the contributor (or the scheme, if acting as the contributor's agent) must give notice to the individual saver within 30 days showing the amount of the excess credit.

The contributing company must provide a shareholder dividend statement to its shareholder when it attaches imputation credits to a retirement scheme contribution (¶17-090). Similarly, a M#ori authority must give notice to a member of any taxable M#ori authority distribution paid to a retirement savings scheme as a retirement scheme contribution (¶24-335).
