# provisions ¶16-215

For the credit account continuity provisions (ICA debits for loss of shareholder continuity), excluded fixed rate securities are to be taken into account when measuring voting and market value interests.

Law: IT07 ss OB 41, YA 1 ("credit account continuity provisions"), YC 20

*Refs: ss OB 41*
