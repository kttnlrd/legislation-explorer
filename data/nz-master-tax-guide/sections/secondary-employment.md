# Secondary employment ¶3-341

An employee who already has a regular job and who takes on a second job should be taxed at the secondary earner rate on earnings from their secondary employment.

See the notes to form IR330 for tax codes and thresholds relating to secondary employment earnings.

Law: IT07 Sch 2 pt A cls 4, 5, 6, 9
