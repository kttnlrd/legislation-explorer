# Structure of the legislation ¶23-035

Subpart HG to the IT07 contains the tax rules relating to joint venturers, partners and partnerships. In addition to the rules in subpart HG, the following key provisions are also relevant:

## s CB 35 — Amounts of income for partners

## s CW 55B — Amounts of exempt income for partners

## s CX 62 — Amounts of excluded income for partners

## s DV 20 — Partners (deductions)

## s GB 50 — Arrangements involving partners and owners

## s YB 12 — Partnership and partner (associated persons)

## s YB 14 — Tripartite relationship (associated persons), and

## s YB 16B — Limited partnerships treated as companies.

*Refs: s CB 35, s CW 55B, s CX 62, s DV 20, s GB 50, s YB 12, s YB 14, s YB 16B*

Law: IT07 subpart HG
