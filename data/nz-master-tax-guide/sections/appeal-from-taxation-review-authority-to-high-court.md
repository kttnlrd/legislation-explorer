# Appeal from Taxation Review Authority to High Court ¶4-260

An appeal against a TRA decision determining a challenge is made under s 26A of the Taxation Review Authorities Act. This section applies to challenges brought under Pt 8A of the TAA.

Generally, an appeal to the High Court in respect of a challenge may be brought as of right if:

## a question of law, or

## the amount of tax involved in the appeal is $2,000 or more, or

## the amount of net loss is $4,000 or more.

There is an exception: there is no appeal if the TRA has made a determination under a tax law that provides for the Authority's determination to be final.

The procedure to be followed when filing an appeal against the determination of a challenge is set out in the High Court Rules. In C of IR v Dick (2000) 19 NZTC 15,849 the High Court held that Pt X of the High Court Rules (now Pt 20) require:

## the filing of the notice of appeal with the High Court registry and the service of a copy of the notice on

the Taxation Review Authority Registrar

## the service on the other party to the appeal within one month of the date of the TRA decision, and

## the completeness of the contents of the notice of appeal.

*Refs: s 26A*

## Time for lodging appeal

A party who wishes to appeal the determination of a challenge, and who is entitled to do so, must lodge a notice of appeal with the High Court and file a copy with the TRA. Under r 20.4(2) of the High Court Rules, the notice of appeal must be lodged within 20 working days of the Authority's written decision. The 20- working-day time limit must be observed unless special leave to extend the time to appeal is obtained under r 20.4(3).

There is no right of appeal against an interlocutory ruling of a TRA until the ruling forms part of the Authority's final determination of the objection or challenge: Case U41 (2000) 19 NZTC 9,380.

Law: HC rr 20.3–20.8; TRY s 26A
