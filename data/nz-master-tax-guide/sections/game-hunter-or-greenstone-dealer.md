# Game hunter or greenstone dealer ¶3-236

Included as schedular payments are payments made by a purchaser (and this includes any auctioneer or dealer) to a seller of greenstone, eels, whitebait or sphagnum moss ("schedular natural products"). Not included are payments made to a natural products dealer (being a registered dealer or person who holds a natural product dealer certificate), payments made on a purchase after the goods were sold by a natural product dealer, payments made to an auctioneer or dealer acting as agent for the seller, or payments made in respect of goods purchased in a retail shop.

Payments made to a game seller are also treated as schedular payments. "Game" is defined as all or part of wild deer, wild pigs or wild goats, whether dead or alive.

The standard rate of tax to be deducted for such schedular payments is 25¢ for each dollar of payment made to the seller (if they have not elected another rate and are not subject to a special or prescribed rate (¶3-044)).
