# Other income under subpart CB ¶5-230

The following amounts are also income from business or trade-like activities:

## certain amounts from the disposal of land — ss CB 6A to CB 23B and Chapter 7

## amounts from the sale of timber — ss CB 24, CB 25 and ¶28-014

## amounts from the disposal of certain shares by portfolio investment entities — s CB 26 and ¶29-130

## income equalisation scheme income — s CB 27 and ¶27-075, ¶27-085 and ¶27-120

## refunds and transfers from an environmental restoration account — s CB 28 and ¶10-412

## amounts from the disposal of minerals — s CB 29

## income from a look-through company — ss CB 32B, CB 32C and ¶19-155

## amounts derived by mutual associations and their members — ss CB 33, CB 34 and ¶24-090

## income for partners from partnership — s CB 35 and ¶23-040.

*Refs: ss CB 6A, ss CB 24, s CB 26, s CB 27, s CB 28, s CB 29, ss CB 32B, ss CB 33, s CB 35*
