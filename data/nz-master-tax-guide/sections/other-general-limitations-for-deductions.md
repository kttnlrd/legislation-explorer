# Other general limitations for deductions ¶10-085

## Withholding tax limitation

A non-resident cannot deduct expenses such as financing costs that have been incurred in deriving dividends, interest or royalties subject to non-resident withholding tax as a final tax (¶26-460).

## Non-residents foreign-sourced income limitation

A non-resident cannot deduct expenses incurred in deriving foreign-sourced income. Since that income is not assessable income, the limitation probably merely confirms that any such costs are not allowed as a deduction in any event.

## Other limitations

A deduction may not be allowed for a payment to a person outside New Zealand if the taxpayer fails to satisfy an information request by the Commissioner about the payment (¶10-730).
