# Trustee income — close company beneficiary income ¶25-120

Generally, beneficiary income distributed to a corporate beneficiary is taxed at the company tax rate of 28%.

From 1 April 2024, s HC 38 provides beneficiary income derived by certain close companies will be treated as trustee income and taxed at the higher rate of 39%.

This new corporate beneficiary rule applies for beneficiary income derived by a close company from a trust (Trust A) and if any voting interest or market value interest in the company is held directly, or indirectly by at least one of the following:

## a settlor of Trust A

## the trustees of Trust A

## a person for whom a settlor of Trust A has natural love and affection, or

## the trustees of another trust (Trust B), if a settlor of Trust A has natural love and affection for a settlor

or a beneficiary of Trust B.

Beneficiary income subject to the corporate beneficiary rule is excluded income for the close company (s CX 58B).

Section HC 38 was amended by the Taxation (Annual Rates for 2024–25, Emergency Response, and Remedial Measures) Act 2025 with effect from 1 April 2024 to ensure that the corporate beneficiary rule does not apply to foreign-sourced amounts of income derived by non-resident companies that do not have a New Zealand resident shareholder.

*Refs: s HC 38, s CX 58B, Section HC 38*

## Imputation credits

For beneficiary income distributed to a close company in the form of dividend and subject to the corporate beneficiary rule, s LE 4B provides that the trustees are able to utilise any imputation credits attached to the dividend (¶17-110). No imputation credits arise for the close company for any tax paid by the trustees on an amount of beneficiary income subject to the corporate beneficiary rule.

## Available capital distribution amount

For the purposes of determining the available capital distribution amount for a share on liquidation of the close company, s CD 44(7)(dc) provides any income subject to corporate beneficiary rule is treated as part of capital gains amount in the formula under s CD 44 (¶16-720). This means any beneficiary income subject to s HC 38 may be distributed tax free to shareholders on liquidation of the close company.

## Exclusions

A close company that is a M#ori authority, a tax charity or a securitisation trust beneficiary is excluded from the corporate beneficiary rule (s HC 38(2)). Section HC 38(3)(b) provides that the dividend exemption for New Zealand wholly-owned groups in s CW 10 overrides the corporate beneficiary rule (¶16-800).
