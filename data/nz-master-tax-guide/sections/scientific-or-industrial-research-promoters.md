# Scientific or industrial research promoters ¶24-135

Amounts derived by a society or an association established mainly to promote or encourage scientific or industrial research are treated as exempt income. The promoter must be approved by the Royal Society of New Zealand and none of the amounts derived must be used or be available for use for the private pecuniary profit of a member, proprietor, shareholder or any associates. Crown Research Institutes (CRIs) are excluded from this exemption.
