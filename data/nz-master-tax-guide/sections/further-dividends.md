# Further dividends ¶16-590

A dividend also includes:

## an amount derived in substitution for a dividend under a dividend stripping arrangement (¶33-175)

## excessive remuneration for services rendered by a person who is a relative of a director or

shareholder of a company, other than a close company (s GB 23(7)), and

## excessive remuneration, including lump sum retirement payments, made to a shareholder or director

(or a relative of a shareholder or director) of a close company to the extent of the excessive component (¶16-070 and s GB 25).

*Refs: s GB 23(7), s GB 25*

Law: IT07 ss CD 11, GB 1(3), GB 23(7), GB 25(2)
