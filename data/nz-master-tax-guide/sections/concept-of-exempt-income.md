# Concept of exempt income ¶1-165

Certain types of income are expressly treated as "exempt income" by specific provisions of the IT07. For example, income received by the TAB NZ (before 1 August 2020, Racing Industry Transition Agency) is exempt income (s CW 47(1)(a)). When a person derives both exempt income and assessable income, that person's taxable income for the year is calculated with reference to assessable income only (¶5-598).

*Refs: s CW 47(1)(a)*
