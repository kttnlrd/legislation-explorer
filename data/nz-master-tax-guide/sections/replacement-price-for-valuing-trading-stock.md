# Replacement price for valuing trading stock ¶9-050

## High-turnover trader

Replacement price may be used to value closing stock if it is used in the person's financial statements. The replacement price of a trading stock item is the market value (exclusive of GST) for the acquisition of the item by the person on the last day of the income year. When no replacement price is available for the last day of the year, the last price paid (exclusive of GST) for that item during the income year is taken as the relevant price (s EB 10).

## Low-turnover trader

A low-turnover trader may apply replacement price to closing stock if (s EB 20):

## they do not prepare financial statements, or

## they prepare financial statements and determine the value of their closing stock at its replacement

price.

The replacement price is:

## the market value of the trading stock on the last day of the income year, or

## the last price that the low-turnover trader paid during the income year to acquire equivalent trading

stock.
