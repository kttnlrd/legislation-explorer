# Background to savings investment, KiwiSaver and superannuation ¶29-010

## Portfolio investment entity regime

The portfolio investment entity (PIE) regime brings the tax treatment of individual investors into closer alignment with the tax treatment of investors in superannuation funds or KiwiSaver schemes. Entry to the regime by investment vehicles is optional providing certain requirements are met.

## KiwiSaver

KiwiSaver, New Zealand's work-based savings scheme, is voluntary and operates by employers deducting a set portion of an employee's earnings, in much the same way as PAYE, and passing it on to an approved scheme provider for investment on the employee's behalf. From 1 April 2008, matching employer contributions became compulsory.

## Retirement savings schemes

The retirement savings scheme operates alongside KiwiSaver and aligns the tax treatment of certain other organisations that make retirement savings contributions for members with the tax treatment of KiwiSaver schemes. Under the retirement savings scheme regime, certain eligible contributing entities are required to deduct retirement scheme contribution tax (RSCT) from contributions made to approved retirement schemes for their shareholders or members and pay the RSCT to Inland Revenue (IR). Where RSCT is deducted, the contribution is excluded income of the saver.

## Superannuation

Superannuation schemes are generally treated for tax purposes like any other form of investment savings entity. The aim of the superannuation scheme provisions is to tax all superannuation schemes constituted as trusts under the ordinary trust taxation provisions.
