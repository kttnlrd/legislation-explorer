# Employer defined for FBT purposes ¶21-040

An "employer" is any person who pays or is liable to pay (whether in the past, present or future) a PAYE income payment apart from those PAYE income payments that are specifically excluded (¶21-030). The definition of "employer" also includes each partner in the case of a partnership, a look-through company (unless the owner is a working owner), the manager (or principal officer) in the case of an unincorporated body of persons, a trustee or company liquidator (ie when property is controlled or vested in a fiduciary capacity), and the Crown when it provides taxable benefits to any employee.

Law: IT07 s YA 1 ("employer")
