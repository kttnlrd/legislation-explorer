# Sale of timber ¶27-013

The general principle is that a farmer who harvests a woodlot situated on the farmer's farm returns as income the amounts realised from the timber sales, with a corresponding deduction being available for the cost of the timber (¶28-014 and ¶28-024).

Law: IT07 ss CB 24, DP 11
