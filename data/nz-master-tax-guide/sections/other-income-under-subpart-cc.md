# Other income under subpart CC ¶5-285

The following amounts are also income from holding property (excluding equity):

## receipts from the exploitation of a film, or the rights to a film — s CC 10

## certain amounts from the disposal of a finance lease asset — ss CC 11, CC 12 and ¶6-700

## certain amounts paid after a hire purchase agreement ends — s CC 13 and ¶6-740

## amounts of income for an IFRS lease — s CC 14 and ¶10-675.

*Refs: s CC 10, ss CC 11, s CC 13, s CC 14*
