# Filing and notification requirements for M#ori authorities ¶24-335

M#ori authorities must file returns of income (IR8), unless they have an exemption. The authorities are also required to provide members with information about distributions they receive or retirement scheme contributions made for their benefit so that those members can comply with their tax obligations. M#ori authorities are also required to provide IR with a M#ori authority credit account return (IR8J), and, if appropriate, an annual reconciliation statement for retirement scheme contribution tax. No credit of tax is allowed or refund paid to members unless the Commissioner has received a distribution statement relating to the credit.

A M#ori authority that is a member of a consolidated imputation group is exempt from filing a credit account return if the account balance is nil at all times during the relevant tax year (s 69B(3)).

The record-keeping requirements in s 22(2) of the TAA are extended to include records relating to M#ori authority credit accounts and retirement scheme contributions.

Before 1 April 2020, M#ori authorities had to provide IR with a distribution statement containing details of all distributions made for the year. From 1 April 2020, this information is required to be provided under the new investment income information provisions. The required information is set out in s 25I and Sch 6 and must be provided by the 20th of the month following the month in which the distribution was paid to the member. All investment income payers must file their information electronically, unless they receive an exemption from the Commissioner (¶15-215).
