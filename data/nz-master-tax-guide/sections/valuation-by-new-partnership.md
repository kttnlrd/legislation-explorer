# Valuation by new partnership ¶23-187

Taxpayers in partnerships cannot change livestock valuation schemes, ie national standard costs, herd valuation, cost price, market value or replacement value, high-priced livestock, and bloodstock valuation, by forming another partnership of principally the same partners, thus circumventing the 2 years' notice requirement of s EC 11 (¶27-295).

Where a new partnership is formed in any income year and:

## the new partnership owns a type of specified livestock, eg cattle, and

## more than 50% of the property of the new partnership at the end of the income year is owned by

partners who owned, either in that income year or in the previous income year, all the property of the old partnership, and

## the old partnership owned the same type of specified livestock

the new partnership must value that type of livestock under the same valuation scheme as the old partnership.

A special rule also applies where livestock that is valued under the herd scheme by a taxpayer is transferred to an associated person (¶27-264).
