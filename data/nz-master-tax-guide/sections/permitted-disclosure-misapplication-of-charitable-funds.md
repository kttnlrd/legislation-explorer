# Permitted disclosure: misapplication of charitable funds ¶1-640

The Commissioner may disclose to the Minister of Revenue instances of the application of funds by a gift- exempt body for a purpose that is not of a charitable, benevolent, philanthropic or cultural nature.

Grounds for such a disclosure exist when the Commissioner has reason to believe funds might be or are being applied for a purpose other than one referred to above. The name of any person who has made a donation to the gift-exempt body must not be revealed to the Minister.

The nature of a charitable purpose is discussed at ¶24-195.
