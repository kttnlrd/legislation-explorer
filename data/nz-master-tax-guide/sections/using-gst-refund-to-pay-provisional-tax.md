# Using GST refund to pay provisional tax ¶22-145

If a provisional taxpayer has a GST refund taxable period, they may choose to use the refund to pay some or all of an instalment of provisional tax due on the same instalment date. The GST return allows the taxpayer to transfer all or part of a GST refund to offset a provisional tax payment.

Note that if a taxpayer elects to use some or all of their GST refund to pay their provisional tax, and the Commissioner makes an amended assessment that reduces the amount of the refund, then the reduced amount will be the amount of the payment of provisional tax.
