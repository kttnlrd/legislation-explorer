# GST and meal expenses ¶32-670

Interpretation statement IS 21/06, "Income tax and GST – Treatment of meal expenses" deals with the income tax and GST treatment of meal expenses incurred by self-employed persons, discusses the treatment of meal allowances paid to employees to illustrate the differences with the treatment of self- employed persons and covers the treatment of entertainment expenditure for the same reason. In summary, the GST treatment follows the income tax treatment. Goods and services used for private and domestic purposes are not used to make taxable supplies and input tax on the expenses will not be deductible for GST purposes.

For GST, the principal issue is whether input tax incurred on meals by a self-employed person is deductible, in whole or in part. To determine the deductibility of input tax for GST purposes, 3 criteria must be met:

## there must be an amount of "input tax" as defined in s 3A (¶32-021)

## the person must be registered for GST, and

## the person must be able to satisfy the test in s 20(3C) (¶32-021)

GST input tax may be deducted to the extent to which the goods or services are used for, or are available for use in, making taxable supplies. Consumption of private meals is not considered using goods or services in making taxable supplies.

The interpretation statement IS 21/06 has a number of examples that cover various different scenarios.

*Refs: s 3A, s 20(3C)*
