# Dividend exclusions — flat-owning companies ¶16-745

A dividend does not arise from the provision of residential property by a flat-owning company. A flat- owning company is a company whose constitution provides an entitlement to shareholders to use specific residential property in New Zealand and whose only significant assets are such residential properties and accompanying funds.
