# GST and refund of excess tax paid ¶32-240

The Commissioner is required to refund excess tax if an assessment results in a registered person having overpaid tax or being entitled to a refund. The Commissioner must refund GST overpaid if they are satisfied that the amount of the refund represents an excess over the amount properly payable. The Commissioner must also refund GST overpaid as a result of a reassessment and GST that should have been refunded at an earlier time.

In general, a 4-year time limit applies. However, an 8-year period applies for GST overpaid as a result of a clear mistake or simple oversight (s 45(4)).

Disagreement as to the merits of the refund claimed can trigger the disputes and/or penalties regime.

Interest will be payable by the party who ultimately has to refund or pay GST for the relevant taxable period.

The Commissioner may apply refunds of GST against tax, including income tax, owing under any of the Inland Revenue Acts.

Under s 174AA of the TAA, the Commissioner need not refund any amount of $20 or less.

*Refs: s 45(4), s 174A*
