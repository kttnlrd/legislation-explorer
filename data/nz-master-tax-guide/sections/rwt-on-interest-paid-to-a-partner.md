# RWT on interest paid to a partner ¶23-138

Interest paid to a partner for a loan or advance is subject to RWT if the partner is a non-exempt recipient. A partnership carrying on an activity in New Zealand will generally be liable to deduct RWT from a payment of interest. A partnership is not liable to deduct RWT from interest paid when, although it does not have RWT- exempt status at the time of payment:

## it has made payments of resident passive income, being interest, totalling $5,000 or less (before 1

April 2020, less than $5,000) in the year immediately preceding the year in which the time of payment falls, and

## the payment, when aggregated with earlier payments of resident passive income, being interest, paid

by the partnership in the year in which the time of payment falls, does not exceed $5,000.

This means that when a partnership pays interest to a partner, it must consider whether it paid more than $5,000 interest in the previous year, and whether the total amount of interest paid in the current year (including the payment being made) will exceed $5,000. If so, it must deduct RWT from the payment being made (¶15-080).

See ¶15-160 for commentary on the RWT-exemption status for limited partnerships.
