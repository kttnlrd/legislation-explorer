# Duty of the Commissioner to report on the application of penalties ¶14-025

At the end of each financial year, the Commissioner has a duty to notify the Minister of Finance by providing a report about the manner in which the following shortfall penalties have been applied:

## not taking reasonable care

## unacceptable tax position

## gross carelessness

## abusive tax position

## evasion or similar act, and

## promoter penalty.

The Minister must lay a copy of the report before the House of Representatives as soon as is practicable.

Law: TAA s 141L
