# Government grant or subsidy ¶3-241

If the government pays a wage subsidy to an employer for employing someone who has been unemployed for a specified number of months, the employee is taxed on the wages they receive without any allowance for any subsidy the employer may receive. The wages expense claimed by the employer as a deduction from income for tax purposes must, however, be reduced by the amount of the subsidy received. Additionally, government grants and subsidies received by a registered person in the course or furtherance of a taxable activity are subject to GST (¶32-023).
