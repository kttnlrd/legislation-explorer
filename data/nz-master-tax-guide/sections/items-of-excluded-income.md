# Items of excluded income ¶5-755

The following amounts are excluded income:

## goods and services tax (GST) — being output tax on goods and services supplied or GST refunds

received — s CX 1

## fringe benefits — s CX 3 and ¶21-010

## certain public transport fares subsidised by an employer — s CX 19C and ¶21-655

## certain self-powered and low powered vehicles, and vehicle-share services provided by an employer

— s CX 19D and ¶21-660

## amounts derived by a superannuation fund from an investment in a life insurance policy offered or

entered into in New Zealand — s CX 40 and ¶29-410

## certain income derived by New Zealand resident insurance underwriters from carrying on the

business outside New Zealand — s CX 41

## consideration derived from disposing of shares or trust interests in a controlled petroleum mining

entity — s CX 42 and ¶28-125

## farm-in expenditure of a petroleum miner or a mineral miner who is the farm-out party under a farm-

out arrangement — s CX 43, ¶28-060 and ¶28-125

## certain grants or subsidies or grant-related suspensory loans from a local authority or a public

authority — s CX 47 and ¶5-760

## certain payments by a public authority for funding of film and television productions — s CX 48C

## superannuation contributions made by employers — s CX 49 and ¶29-410

## payment by the Commissioner of an amount of a tax credit under subpart MK (KiwiSaver scheme and

complying superannuation fund tax credits) — s CX 50 and ¶29-250

## retirement scheme contributions made by an eligible contributor — s CX 50B and ¶29-320

## refunds of excess deposits to the main income equalisation account or the thinning operations

income equalisation account — s CX 51

## certain amounts derived from the disposal of a pre-1990 forest land emissions unit other than by

surrender — s CX 51B and ¶5-228

## certain amounts derived from the disposal of fishing quota emission units other than by surrender —

s CX 51C

## refunds from a person's environmental restoration account — s CX 52 and ¶10-412

## credits for inflation-indexed instruments that are a recovery of an earlier decrease that was previously

debited to the lender — s CX 53 and ¶5-055

## share-lending collateral derived under a share-lending arrangement — s CX 54 and ¶5-288

## certain amounts derived on the transfer of an emissions unit under an excepted financial

arrangement — s CX 54B and ¶5-228

## income derived by a portfolio investment entity (PIE), the Crown as owner of the New Zealand

Superannuation Fund or the VCF, a Fund investment vehicle (as referred to in s 59A of the New Zealand Superannuation and Retirement Income Act 2001) or a VCF investment vehicle (as referred to in s 25 of the Venture Capital Fund Act 2019) that is treated as being wholly owned by the Crown under s HR 4B, a company that is treated as being wholly owned by the Crown under s HR 4B, or a life insurer from the sale of shares in a New Zealand company or an Australian listed company where the share is not a non-participating redeemable preference share — s CX 55 and ¶29-130

## certain amounts derived by a foreign PIE equivalent from the disposal of a share or financial

arrangement — s CX 55B

## certain amounts of attributed PIE income derived by an investor (who is not a zero-rated investor)

from an interest in a multi-rate PIE — s CX 56 and ¶29-145

## distributions or dividends derived by an investor from a multi-rate PIE — s CX 56B and ¶29-145

## distributions or dividends derived by a resident investor (who is a natural person or trustee who has

not included the amount as income in a return for the income year) from a listed PIE — s CX 56C and ¶29-145

*Refs: s CX 1, s CX 3, s CX 19C, s CX 19D, s CX 40, s CX 41, s CX 42, s CX 43, s CX 47, s CX 48C, s CX 49, s CX 50, s CX 50B, s CX 51, s CX 51B, s CX 51C, s CX 52, s CX 53, s CX 54, s CX 54B*

## credits for fees attributed to investors by a multi-rate PIE if the payment is included as taxable income

of the multi-rate PIE — s CX 57 and ¶29-145

## amounts other than FIF income derived from an attributing interest in an FIF during a period in which

a calculation method referred to in s EX 59(1) was used — s CX 57B

## minor beneficiary income — s CX 58 and ¶25-115

## beneficiary income subject to the corporate beneficiary rules — s CX 58B and ¶25-120

## taxable distributions derived from non-complying trusts — s CX 59 and ¶25-360

## certain income derived by a consolidated group company from a transaction with another company in

the same group — s CX 60 and ¶20-040

## amounts treated as excluded income under the avoidance arrangement provisions of ss GA 1 and

GB 23 — s CX 61

## amounts treated as excluded income of partners resulting from the application of subpart HG — s CX

62 and ¶23-050

## certain dividends derived from a company after it has ceased to be a look-through company — s CX

63 and ¶19-230

## amounts treated as excluded income under subpart FH (Hybrid and branch mismatches of

deductions and income from multi-jurisdictional arrangements) — s CX 64 and ¶26-650.

## amounts received under the clean car discount scheme — s CX 47 and ¶5-770.

*Refs: s CX 57, s EX 59(1), s CX 57B, s CX 58, s CX 58B, s CX 59, s CX 60, ss GA 1, s CX 61, s CX 64, s CX 47*
