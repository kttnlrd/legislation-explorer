# Unclassified benefits: records required for FBT purposes ¶21-500

Records should be kept for gifts and prizes, subsidised or discounted goods below cost, subsidised or discounted services, and subsidised or discounted transport. In respect of each benefit, records may show:

## date of transaction

## name of employee receiving the benefit

## description of the benefit provided

## cost to employee

## cost to employer where goods are provided

## normal market price where services are provided, and

## highest price charged to the general public where subsidised transport is provided.

Law: TAA s 22(2)(i)
