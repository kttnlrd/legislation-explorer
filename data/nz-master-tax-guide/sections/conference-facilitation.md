# Conference facilitation ¶4-177

Conference facilitation is offered to all taxpayers by IR as part of the conference stage of the disputes resolution process. The offer is made in writing within one month from the date of issue of the taxpayer's NOR, or the taxpayer's rejection of the Commissioner's NOR (whichever is applicable).

An independent internal facilitator is a person who promotes and encourages structured discussion between IR officers and the taxpayer on an informed basis and with a bona fide intention of resolving the dispute. The facilitator is a senior IR officer, with sufficient technical knowledge, who has not been involved in the dispute or given advice on the dispute before the conference stage.

The conference facilitator is not responsible for making any decision in respect of the dispute (or settlement), but rather facilitates the discussion and resolution of difference, and ultimately determines when the conference phase has come to an end. Facilitation is optional and a conference can be held without a facilitator.

See SPS 23/01, "Disputes process".
