# Payment to working partners ¶3-296

Payments to a working partner may be treated as salary or wages subject to PAYE tax deductions, provided certain requirements are met. The requirements are that the partner is employed under a written contract of service signed by all the partners and the contract specifies the amount payable and the terms and conditions of the services to be performed by the partner. In such cases, PAYE and ACC levies must be deducted from such salaries and accounted for on form IR348 (¶23-132).
