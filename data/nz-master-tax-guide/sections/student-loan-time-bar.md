# Student loan time bar ¶8-103

The Commissioner is time barred from amending an assessment relating to a student loan repayment obligation that includes an amount of salary or wage deductions after 4 years. Once the 4-year period has passed, the Commissioner may not increase the amount assessed or decrease the amount of a net loss.

The time bar will not apply if fraudulent or wilfully misleading information has been provided or where not amending an assessment would result in a significant adverse effect on the borrower. The time bar is also subject to s 64 of the SLSA.
