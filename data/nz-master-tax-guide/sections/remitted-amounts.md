# Remitted amounts ¶10-820

An amount allowed as a deduction may subsequently be remitted to create an item of assessable income (¶5-340). However, if subsequently the amount in question is actually paid despite the remission, a deduction becomes available for the amount paid. The deduction is allowed in the year of payment.

The provisions allowing the deduction do not extend to amounts subject to the financial arrangements rules.

The outcome may be that the provisions apply, for example, to a debt for the purchase of trading stock.
