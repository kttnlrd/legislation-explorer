# Official Information Act releases ¶1-642

All information held by IR is official information subject to the Official Information Act 1982. A person may make a request under that Act if they are:

## a New Zealand citizen or permanent resident

## a person in New Zealand

## a corporate entity (a company or incorporated society) which:

## is incorporated in New Zealand, or

## has a place of business here.

Requests may be made via the IR website ird.govt.nz/contactus/information-requests. Otherwise, a person may:

## email oia@ird.govt.nz for official information requests

## email privacy.officer@ird.govt.nz for personal information requests

## call on 0800 775 247

## write to:

Information Requests Inland Revenue PO Box 2198 Wellington 6140 The IR website advises: "When you make your request, make sure you know how they work, and which types of requests you can make."
