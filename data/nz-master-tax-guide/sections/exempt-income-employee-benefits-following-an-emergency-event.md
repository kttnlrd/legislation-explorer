# Exempt income — employee benefits following an emergency event ¶5-715

The Taxation (Annual Rates for 2024–25, Emergency Response, and Remedial Measures) Act 2025 introduced a generic response to emergency events to improve IR's ability to provide timely tax relief such events. New subpart FP set out the various taxation reliefs available following the declaration of an emergency event. An "emergency event" is defined under s 3(1) of the TAA as an emergency as defined in s 4 of the Civil Defence Emergency Management Act 2002 that is declared an emergency under that Act, or subject to a power exercised under s 121 or 122 of the Biosecurity Act 1993.

Following an emergency event, employers may make ex-gratia welfare payments or benefits to their employees. From 1 April 2025, new s CW 19B provides that the following benefits or payments to an employee to alleviate the adverse effects of an emergency event are exempt income:

- accommodation benefits, or

- the first $5,000 monetary remuneration that is not accommodation benefit.

For the exemption to apply the payment must meet the following requirements (s FP 20):

## it is provided for the purpose of relief of employees from the adverse effects of an emergency event,

and

## it would otherwise be assessable income if not for this relief provision, and

## it is derived in the 8-week period beginning on the first day of the relevant emergency event, and

## it is not paid in substitution for wages or salary, and

## it does not depend on the rank, position or seniority of the employee, and

## if the employee is associated with the employer, it is also available to an unrelated full-time

employee, and

## the employer treats the income as being exempt income of the employee.

For a project of limited duration where the project is to restore the damaged caused by an emergency event and the distant workplace is in an emergency event are, new s FP 22 extends the 3-year exemption for accommodation and related expenses to up to 5 years (¶3-425).

See ¶21-665 for discussion on the FBT exemption for benefits provided after an emergency event.

Before 1 April 2025, a similar exemption applied for payments of up to $5,000 made to employees following the North Island flooding events in 2023 (s CZ 23B).
