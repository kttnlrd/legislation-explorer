# Partnership elections of livestock valuation ¶23-189

Where specified livestock is owned by 2 or more persons jointly:

## for an election to be valid in respect of that livestock, the election must be made by those persons

jointly, and

## if no election is made, that livestock must be valued under the national standard cost scheme (for

bailed or leased specified livestock, see below).

## Bailed or leased specified livestock or livestock subject to a

profit-sharing arrangement Specified livestock that is owned by 2 or more persons and that has been bailed or leased to another person must be valued at market value, unless the joint owners make a joint election to use a different valuation method. This rule also applies to specified livestock that is subject to a "profit-sharing arrangement".

Briefly, a "profit-sharing arrangement" is an arrangement with the following features:

## the specified livestock is made available to another person without a specified fee

## any return or compensation received for making the specified livestock available depends on the

profits of the business carried on by the other person

## the person making the livestock available participates in the profits and losses of the business carried

on by the other person, and

## if a partnership arises between the 2 parties, the person making the livestock available is bound by

the requirements of the Partnership Law Act 2019.

There are additional implications for specified livestock that is made available to another person under a profit-sharing arrangement and that is valued under the national standard cost scheme or the cost price method. In that event, there is a notional single owner of the livestock comprising the actual owner, any other bailor and the person who has use of the livestock.

A livestock valuation election made in respect of a person's livestock interest in a partnership is treated separately from any other livestock interest that the person has.

Law: IT07 ss EC 12, YA 1 ("profit-sharing arrangement")
