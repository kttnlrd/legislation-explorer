# GST record-keeping for supplies of imported services ¶32-460

The recipient of the imported services is also required to keep sufficient records to enable the following details to be ascertained:

## the name and the address of the supplier

## the date on which, or the period during which, the supply was received

## a description of the services that were supplied

## the consideration for the supply

## the time by which payment for the supply was required, and

## for intra-group services, the amount of the salary or wages and interest incurred that have been

excluded from the consideration for the supply, in accordance with s 10(15C)(a), (b).

*Refs: s 10(15C)(a)*

Law: GST ss 19E(2)(d), 19F, 19G
