# Two-monthly taxable period for GST ¶32-100

A registered person has a 2-monthly taxable period unless application for another taxable period is made or the value of taxable supplies exceeds (or is likely to exceed) $24 million. A person can elect to have a 2- month period ending in either odd or even months providing this aligns with their income tax balance date.

A person may apply for a change in taxable period in a way acceptable to the Commissioner.

Law: GST ss 15(1)(b), 15B(1), 15C
