# Deed of acknowledgment of debt ¶90-150

## DEED dated                      20XX

## PARTIES

## [Name] (The Lender)

## [Trustees' names] (The Borrowers)

## INTRODUCTION

A. The Borrowers are the present trustees of the trust known as [Trust name] (the Trust) which was created by deed dated [date of settlement] (The Trust Deed).

B. The Lender has advanced the sum of $ [amount of advance] to the Borrowers.

C. The parties wish to record the terms and conditions of the advance.

OPERATIVE PROVISIONS 1. The Borrowers acknowledge that they are indebted to the Lender in the sum of $[amount of advance] (the Advance). 2. The Advance shall remain outstanding as a debt, ie payable by the Borrowers to the Lender upon demand. 3. The Lender may demand that the Advance be repaid by notice in writing signed by the Lender, served upon the Borrowers either personally or by posting the same addressed to them at last known place of abode in New Zealand. 4. Any demand made under this deed may be made only by the Lender or the Lender's personal representatives upon the Lender's death. The ability to make demand shall not be transferable to any other person. 5. [A Marshall clause may be inserted here, see ¶90-200]. 6. The Advance shall include any further advances that may be made by the Lender to the Borrowers after the date of this deed. The words "further advances" mean such further sum or sums advanced or credited or other accommodation provided by the Lender to, or at the request of, the Borrowers. 7. The Borrowers have entered into this deed as trustees of the Trust so as to bind the trustees from time to time of the Trust. The liability of the Borrowers to the Lender under this deed shall be limited to the capital and assets and income of the Trust coming into the hands of the trustees in the proper course of the administration of the Trust and the Borrowers shall not be personally liable. Those funds shall not include any capital or income of the Trust that has been transferred or paid to, or appropriated or applied for the benefit of, any person in accordance with the Trust Deed.
