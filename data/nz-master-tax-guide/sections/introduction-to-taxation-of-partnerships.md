# Introduction to taxation of partnerships ¶23-030

Specific rules for partnerships were enacted in an attempt to codify and clarify the tax treatment of both general and limited partnerships.

Generally, both limited and general partnerships are taxed in the same manner under the IT07. However, some additional tax rules apply to limited partnerships:

## a loss limitation rule applies to restrict losses claimed by limited partners to the value of their

economic investment in the partnership (¶23-080), and

## a general partner will always be associated with the partnership under the associated persons test in

s YB 12. However, for limited partners, a 25% interest threshold must be met before the limited partner and the partnership will be associated (s YB 12(2)). Note the test of association in s YB 12(2) applies if s YB 16B does not apply (¶16-245).

The definition of "partnership" also includes joint ventures and co-owners of property if all the joint venturers or co-owners elect to be treated as a partnership for the purposes of the IT07 and the Tax Administration Act 1994 (TAA). Therefore, the tax rules for partnerships may also apply to joint ventures and co-owners of property.

*Refs: s YB 12, s YB 12(2), s YB 16B*
