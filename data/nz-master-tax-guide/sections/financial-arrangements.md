# financial arrangements ¶5-366

The following items held by a person at the end of an income year are income:

## the closing value of trading stock

## the closing value of livestock, and

## the closing value of excepted financial arrangements or share-lending rights that are revenue account

property.

At year-end, trading stock, livestock and excepted financial arrangements that are revenue account property are valued in accordance with subparts EB, EC and ED of the IT07 respectively.

Law: IT07 s CH 1
