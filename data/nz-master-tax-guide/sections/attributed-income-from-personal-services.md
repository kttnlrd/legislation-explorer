# Attributed income from personal services ¶5-293

Specific anti-avoidance rules in ss GB 27–GB 29 aim to prevent higher income earners from diverting personal services income to other associated entities such as companies and trusts.

Under the attribution rules, the amount of income that is derived by the associated entity of a taxpayer who provides personal services is attributed (income) to that taxpayer after the deduction of allowable expenditure. The income is allocated to the income year in which it is attributed.

Following the Supreme Court's decision in Penny and Hooper v C of IR (2011) 25 NZTC ¶20-073 IR issued Revenue Alert RA 11/02, which dealt with the issue of diverting personal services income by structuring revenue earning activities through an associated entity such as a trading trust or company and the circumstances when IR will consider this arrangement is tax avoidance. It replaced Revenue Alerts RA 08/01 issued in March 2008 and RA 10/01 issued in June 2010.

RA 11/02 was itself replaced when it was re-issued as Revenue Alert RA 21/01 on 29 March 2021 ahead of the increase in the top marginal tax rate on 1 April 2021 (¶33-325).
