# Notice of loss offset election ¶90-410

## Background

For the year/period ended 20XX [name of loss company] (the loss company) and [name of profit company] (the profit company) are a group of companies/wholly-owned group of companies as defined in s IC 3 of the Income Tax Act 2007.

The loss company has losses carried forward/incurred a loss in the year/period ended 20XX of $[amount of loss].

The profit company and the loss company elect to offset $[amount of loss offset] between them for the year/period ended 20XX.

Notice In accordance with s IC 9 of the Income Tax Act 2007, the loss company (IRD number [number]) elects to offset $[amount of loss] against the net income of the profit company (IRD number [number]) for the year/period ended 20XX.
