# Valuation of nursery plants ¶27-500

Generally, nursery plants that are trading stock must be valued at either market selling value or cost (ie the normal trading stock rules apply). See ¶9-010 onwards. As to whether plants are covered by the current definition of trading stock (¶9-010).

IR released an administrative interpretation on the valuation of nursery plants under the discounted selling price method (Tax Information Bulletin ¶1311-117 Vol 13, No 11, November 2001 at 68).
