# Exempt income derived by public purpose Crown-controlled companies ¶5-606

With effect from 18 March 2019, certain Crown-controlled companies have been given their own income tax exemption. The qualifying "public purpose Crown-controlled companies" are listed in Sch 35 to the IT07. Also included are wholly-owned subsidiaries of the companies listed in Sch 35 if the subsidiary's primary purpose is the carrying out of a public policy objective of the government. An Order in Council mechanism facilitates amendments to this schedule. The changes effectively reinstate the tax outcome the companies had before a Crown Law interpretation reduced who qualified as a "public authority". Schedule 35 includes the following companies:

## Crown Infrastructure Partners Limited

## Crown Regional Holdings Limited

## Education Payroll Limited

## New Zealand Green Investment Finance Limited

## Ng#puhi Investment Fund Limited

## Rau Paenga Limited

## Research and Education Advanced Network New Zealand Limited

## Southern Response Earthquake Services Limited

## T#maki Redevelopment Company Limited

## T#maki Regeneration Limited

## The Network for Learning Limited.
