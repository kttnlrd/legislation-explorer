# Late filing penalties and non-electronic filing penalties ¶2-090

Late filing penalties are imposed on a person who:

## fails to provide certain returns (see below), reconciliation statements (where relevant) or employment

income information on time, and

## is notified by the Commissioner that the penalty is payable.

Late filing penalties are chargeable on the following returns:

## annual tax returns (which includes returns by persons who receive family assistance, who have

tax credits for charitable gifts, partnership returns, returns by owners of interests in look-through companies, returns by an undischarged bankrupt, and returns by executors or administrators)

## a multinational top-up tax return (from 1 January 2025)

## annual ICA returns

## returns required by multi-rate PIEs or proxies for investors in the PIEs

## statements for payment of residential land withholding tax (RLWT), and

## employment income information.

See further at ¶14-040.

A late filing penalty may also be imposed on GST returns filed after their due dates (¶32-223).

## Non-electronic filing penalty

There is also a penalty, known as a non-electronic filing penalty, for those employers, PAYE intermediaries, portfolio investment entities and investment income payers that are required to file returns electronically but fail to do so (¶14-045).

Both these penalties will themselves be subject to the late payment penalty and interest if they are not paid by the due date.
