# Taxation of inflation-indexed bonds ¶5-055

Sections EI 2 and CX 53 govern the taxation of the inflation-linked component of financial instruments in respect of money lent.

The inflation-linked component of financial instruments is treated as having been credited in account and capitalised by the borrower for the benefit of the lender on the day after the day on which the level of the relevant indicator index becomes public knowledge. The policy intent is to tax holders of this type of instrument who are cash-basis persons on an annual basis instead of on a payments basis in respect of the inflation-linked amounts. Non-cash-basis persons are taxable under the financial arrangements rules (¶6-010). The deemed crediting in account of the inflation amount means the sum has been paid for resident withholding tax purposes. See Tax Information Bulletin ¶79-114 Vol 7, No 9, February 1996 at 12.

An amount credited to the lender of an inflation-indexed bond that is a recovery of a decrease in the amount payable over a previous period, that amount having been previously debited in account, is excluded income of the lender. Therefore, the credit paid in this case is not income derived by the lender.
