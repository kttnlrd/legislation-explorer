# KiwiSaver — time bar and information sharing ¶29-265

A 4-year time bar was introduced on increased and decreased assessments of KiwiSaver employer and employee deductions. This treats KiwiSaver employer and employee deductions the same as other PAYE deductions for time bar purposes.

The time bar means that, once 4 years have passed, the Commissioner may not amend an assessment to increase the amount assessed or decrease the amount of a net loss. A notable exception to this rule is that it does not apply in cases of fraud or willfully misleading the Commissioner. There is also a 4-year time bar on taxpayers' ability to claim a refund for overpayments of tax.

From 1 April 2024, the Commissioner of IR is able to electronically communicate information about deceased KiwiSaver members' estates to KiwiSaver providers. This will assist providers in contacting the executors or administrators of the deceased member's estate.
