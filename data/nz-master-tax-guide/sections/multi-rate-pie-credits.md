# Multi-rate PIE credits ¶11-092

A multi-rate PIE may be able to claim an income tax credit for an investor (who is not a zero-rated investor) where credits are allocated to the investor in accordance with s HM 51 or HM 53 or for a loss calculated under s HM 55 (¶29-140 and ¶29-145).

A multi-rate PIE cannot claim a tax credit for notified foreign investors in foreign investment PIEs in relation to imputation credits attached to dividends derived by the PIE.

In certain circumstances, where the income of an investor is not excluded income, the investor can claim a tax credit for tax paid by a multi-rate PIE on that income. Similarly, if tax is paid by a multi-rate PIE in respect of the income allocated to a zero-rated investor or after the end of an exit period, the affected investor can claim a tax credit.

*Refs: s HM 51, s HM 55*
