# Permitted disclosures to agencies for certain purposes ¶1-622

#Note: The Taxation (Annual Rates for 2025–26, Compliance Simplification, and Remedial Measures) Bill is proposing a number of amendments that would permit the Commissioner of IR to share certain information for set purposes, including:

## the Commissioner would be able to disclose certain information to another government agency for a

defined purpose, pursuant to a Ministerial agreement signed by the Minister of Finance and the minister responsible for the government agency to which the information is being disclosed

## to support the collection and enforcement of monetary penalties, the Commissioner would be able to

share the last known contact details, including email addresses, of fine defaulters with the Ministry of Justice

## assist with activities under the Criminal Proceeds (Recovery) Act 2009, where IR had previously

disclosed a person's information to the Police under existing rules, IR would be able to provide the Police with updated information to ensure the information remains accurate and up to date.

Section 18H of the TAA removes the restriction on disclosure of sensitive revenue information by the Commissioner when disclosure is made to an agency referred to, and for a purpose specified in, Sch 7 pt C of the TAA. A disclosure made in these circumstances is a permitted disclosure (s 18C).

Part C has 2 subparts. Subpart 1 (cls 19–39D deals with disclosures to certain agencies and subpart 2 (cls 41–46B) refers to disclosures under an exchange of information.

Permitted disclosures under an exchange of information allowed under subpart 2 are discussed in the following commentary:

## The Accident Compensation Corporation — earnings-related payments and levies (¶1-627)

## The Ministry of Justice — fines defaulter information (¶1-629)

## The Ministry of Justice or the New Zealand Police — child support exemption (¶1-631)

## Work and Income — foreign social security agreement (¶1-633)

## Work and Income — parental leave payments (¶1-634).

Permitted disclosures to certain agencies allowed under subpart 1 are summarised below.

*Refs: Section 18H, s 18C*

## Statistics New Zealand — Sch 7 cl 20

The Commissioner may communicate sensitive revenue information to the Government Statistician, for official statistics purposes, or research, which the Commissioner considers is not undesirable to disclose, and the disclosure of which is made for the purposes of the Data and Statistics Act 2022.

## The Treasury — Sch 7 cl 21

The Commissioner may communicate to an employee of the Treasury any information:

## the employee is authorised to receive, and

## the Commissioner considers is not undesirable to disclose and is essential to the preparation of

taxation revenue forecasts.

## Intelligence and security agencies — Sch 7 cl 22

The Commissioner may allow the Director-General of the New Zealand Security Intelligence Service or the Government Communications Security Bureau (or an authorised employee of those agencies) access to

information specified in a permission given under s 137 or 138 of the Intelligence and Security Act 2017.

Broadly, access may be granted if certain criteria based on New Zealand's national security, international relations or economic wellbeing are satisfied.

## Government agencies — AML/CFT purposes — Sch 7 cl 23

The Commissioner may disclose to a government agency, or an AML/CFT supervisor, information supplied or obtained under the IT07, the GST Act or the TAA if disclosure is necessary or desirable to ensure compliance with the AML/CFT legislation. An AML/CFT supervisor is defined in s 5 of the Anti-Money Laundering and Countering Financing of Terrorism Act 2009. These persons are the Reserve Bank of New Zealand, the Financial Markets Authority and the Department of Internal Affairs.

## Government agencies — Russia sanctions response purposes

## — Sch 7 cl 23A

The Commissioner may disclose to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Trade (or to another government agency) information supplied or obtained under an Act referred to in s 31 of the Russia Sanctions Act 2022 that is necessary or desirable for the purposes of that section about a person or entity for the purpose of enabling the government agency to enforce a sanction.

## New Zealand Customs Service — value of imported goods —

## Sch 7 cl 24

The Commissioner may disclose to the New Zealand Customs Service information for the purpose of assessing the suitability of an arrangement relating to the use of provisional values under the Customs and Excise Act 2018.

## Business, Innovation and Employment — New Zealand

## business number — Sch 7 cl 25

The Commissioner may communicate primary business data to authorised officers of the Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment for inclusion in the New Zealand Business Number Register. A New Zealand business number is a unique identifier for use by a business in all its dealings with the government.

Primary business data includes the business's trading name, address, type of entity, status and other information, including any Australian business number. Information necessary to correct the business register may also be communicated.

## Agencies for workplace safety — Sch 7 cl 26

The Commissioner may communicate to an officer or employee of the Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment, WorkSafe New Zealand, or a designated agency under the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015 (eg a government department or a Crown entity with specialised knowledge) information:

## the person is authorised to receive, and

## the Commissioner considers is not undesirable to disclose and is reasonably necessary to enable the

performance of the person's duties relating to workplace legislation.

## Agencies for charities regulation — Sch 7 cl 27

The Commissioner may communicate to a member, employee or agent of the Charities Registration Board, or the chief executive, any information:

## the person is authorised to receive, and

## the Commissioner considers is not undesirable to disclose and is reasonably necessary to enable the

performance of the person's duties relating to the exercise of the powers of the Board.

## Agencies for foreign trusts regulation — Sch 7 cl 28

The Commissioner may communicate to an officer, employee or agent of the Department of Internal Affairs or the New Zealand Police any information relating to the registration (or absence of registration) for a foreign trust that the person is authorised to receive.

Government agencies — voice recognition analysis — Sch 7 cl

The Commissioner may disclose information to an authorised officer or employee of a public sector agency if the information relates to the voice identification of a person that is used to identify that person, it is held and communicated for identity verification purposes and the Minister of Revenue is notified that the information will be communicated to the public sector agency.

## Financial Markets Authority — KiwiSaver information — Sch 7

cl 30 The Commissioner may communicate certain information to the Financial Markets Authority (FMA) relating to KiwiSaver schemes or complying superannuation funds and their members, provided the Commissioner considers it not undesirable to disclose the information and the information is reasonably necessary to enable the FMA to perform its duties or functions or exercise its powers.

## Land Information New Zealand — Sch 7 cl 31

The Commissioner may communicate information to the chief executive or an authorised employee of Land Information New Zealand relating to the tax statements required under the Land Transfer Act (s 86 of the Land Transfer Act 2017).

## Registrars of courts — Sch 7 cl 32

Upon request by a Registrar of a court, the Commissioner must supply the name, address and telephone number of the employer of a person who is the subject of an attachment order (s 104A of the Summary Proceedings Act 1957).

## Credit reporting agencies — Sch 7 cl 33

The Commissioner may disclose tax debt information for serious cases of non-compliance to credit reporting agencies (¶1-624).

## Agencies for gambling levy — Sch 7 cl 34

The Commissioner may communicate to any employee of the Department of Internal Affairs or the Ministry of Health information that:

## the employee is authorised to receive, and

## the Commissioner considers is not undesirable to disclose and is essential to enable the employee

to carry out any duty lawfully conferred on the person relating to the determination of the problem gambling levy rate.

## Department of Internal Affairs: offshore betting charges — Sch

## 7 cl 34A

The Commissioner may communicate information to a person who is an employee of the Department of Internal Affairs that:

## the person is authorised by the department to receive, and

## the Commissioner considers is not undesirable to disclose and is essential to enable the person to

carry out any duty lawfully conferred on the person relating to offshore betting charges payable under the Racing Industry Act 2020 and liable for GST.

## NZ Film Commission — government screen production

## payments — Sch 7 cl 35

The Commissioner may disclose information to an officer, employee or agent of the New Zealand Film Commission for the purposes of enabling the entitlement of a company to a government screen production payment (¶10-446).

Registrar of Companies — offences under Companies Act 1993 — Sch 7 cl 36 The Commissioner may disclose to the Registrar of Companies information concerning possible offences under certain provisions of the Companies Act 1993 (¶1-625).

## KiwiSaver providers — KiwiSaver details — Sch 7 cl 37

The Commissioner and a KiwiSaver scheme provider may communicate to each other a person's name, date of birth, tax file number, contact information and information relating to a person's transfer from one scheme to another for the purposes of administering the KiwiSaver Act 2006 or a KiwiSaver scheme ( s 220B of the KiwiSaver Act 2006.

## Agencies for research and development — Sch 7 cl 38

The Commissioner may communicate to officers, employees or agents of the Treasury, Callaghan Innovation, the Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment (MBIE) or a state sector entity responsible for any function related to research and development advice or incentives, information reasonably necessary for the person to perform their work in relation to evaluating, administering, statistical reporting on, and policy formation for, tax credits provided in subparts LY and MX of the IT07. Information may also be communicated to officers, employees or agents of Callaghan Innovation or MBIE if it is reasonably necessary for the person to perform their work in relation to:

## offering research and development advice and incentives, including tax incentives, grants, loans, and

any related measures aimed at incentivising R&D, or

## the grant and administration of a loan made under the research and development loan scheme

established by Ministerial direction to Callaghan Innovation under s 112 of the Crown Entities Act 2004, notice number 2020-go2811.

## Agencies for Australian wine producer rebate — Sch 7 cl 39

The exchange of information is permitted between the Commissioner, the New Zealand Customs Service and the Australian Taxation Office in relation to the approval or verification of the entitlement of a New Zealand wine producer to the Australian wine producer rebate.

## Regulator under the Overseas Investment Act 2005 — Sch 7 cl

## 39B

The Commissioner may disclose sensitive revenue information to any authorised officer or employee of the regulator under the Overseas Investment Act 2005 (OIA) if the regulator has reasonable grounds to believe disclosure of the information is necessary for determining if an investor meets the investor test under the OIA, for managing national security and public order risks associated with transactions by overseas persons, or for any purpose set out in s 41(1A) of the OIA. The Commissioner may disclose the information only where it is reasonable and practical to do so, the information is readily available, and it is not undesirable to disclose it.

## Chief Executive responsible for the administration of the

## Residential Tenancies Act 1986 — Sch 7 cl 39C

The Commissioner may disclose sensitive revenue information to the chief executive responsible for the administration of the Residential Tenancies Act 1986, and to those with delegated authority, if the disclosure of information is necessary for the purpose of the chief executive forming or changing an opinion that land meets, or does not meet, the definition of build-to-rent land.
