# Multi-rate PIE income attributed to investors ¶5-378

A person's income includes any amount of attributed PIE income. Attributed PIE income is that income attributed to an investor in a multi-rate PIE. The income is derived by the person in the income year of that person that includes the PIE's income year end for that attributed income. For investors who satisfy the requirements of s CX 56, the PIE attributed income will be excluded income (¶29-145).
