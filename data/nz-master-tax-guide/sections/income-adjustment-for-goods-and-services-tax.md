# Income — adjustment for goods and services tax ¶5-369

A GST input tax deduction made on a return-by-return basis for a change in use of goods and services from a non-GST purpose to a GST-purpose (¶32-088) is income. The income is allocated to the income year in which the input tax deduction is claimed. However, this does not apply to one-off GST adjustments arising when an asset used mostly for private or exempt purposes is applied permanently to 100% taxable purposes. In this case, the GST adjustment reduces the depreciation base price of the asset.
