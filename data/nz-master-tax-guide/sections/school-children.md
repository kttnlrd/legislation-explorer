# School children ¶3-326

School children and students earning income are liable to income tax the same as other taxpayers. However, school children may qualify for an exemption if:

## the income is none of the following:

## a PAYE income payment from which the person's employer is required to withhold tax under the

PAYE rules

## resident passive income

## non-resident passive income

## beneficiary income

## excluded income

## exempt income, and

## the total income derived in the tax year is less than $2,340.

Law: IT07 s CW 55BB
