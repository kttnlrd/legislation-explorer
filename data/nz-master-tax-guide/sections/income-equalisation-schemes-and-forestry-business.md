# Income equalisation schemes and forestry business ¶28-023

The income equalisation schemes are dealt with in subpart EH (¶27-065).

Individuals carrying on a forestry business on their own land in New Zealand are entitled to use the main farm income equalisation provisions. The maximum amount that can be paid into the main income equalisation account is the net income for the tax year, calculated by reference to income from the sale of any standing, or cut, or fallen timber (in its natural state and not further processed), or the proceeds from the right to cut or remove that timber (¶27-070).

In addition to the main income equalisation scheme for forestry companies there is a limited income equalisation scheme for proceeds from thinning operations. Proceeds from the sale of cutting rights are not eligible (¶27-085).

The definition of "income from forestry" in s EH 34(1) includes permanent forest sink initiative (PFSI) forestry income and, from 23 June 2020, income from a permanent forestry scheme, so that PFSI foresters have been entitled to use income equalisation accounts from the beginning of the 2008–09 income year.
