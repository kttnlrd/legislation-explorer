# Challenge to a determination ¶6-620

A person affected by a determination may challenge the determination in accordance with the disputes resolution procedures. A determination of this nature is treated as a disputable decision similar to an ordinary assessment or amended assessment (¶4-215–¶4-220).

Once made and applied, a determination forms the basis of an assessment of the person's financial arrangement income and expenditure. Limited exceptions exist for a person detrimentally affected by subsequent legislation, or if the application for a determination contained a material misrepresentation or omission.
