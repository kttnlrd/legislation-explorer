# Land affected by emergency events ¶7-451

The Taxation (Annual Rates for 2024–25, Emergency Response, and Remedial Measures) Act 2025 introduced the below tax reliefs for damaged land and buildings following an emergency event.

## Rollover relief for land and buildings held on revenue account

For land or buildings held as revenue account property that has been destroyed by an emergency event, the taxpayer can elect to treat the amount of compensation received for the property that exceeds the cost as not assessable upon receipt but as an item of suspended recovery income to be reduced by the cost of replacement assets (see ss CC 2B and FP 5–FP 7). IR must be notified for each income year in which suspended recovery income treatment is applied. See Tax Information Bulletin ¶375-102 Vol 37, No 5, June 2025 at 14–16 for detailed commentary and example on this rollover relief.

## Turning off bright-line and other timing land disposal tests

New s FP 27 provides that the following land provisions will not apply when a damaged property is subject to a Crown or local-authority buy-out following an emergency event:

## the new 2-year (s CB 6A) bright-line test

## other land disposed of by a dealer in land within 10 years of acquisition (s CB 9)

## other land disposed of by a land developer within 10 years of acquisition (s CB 10), and

## other land disposed of by a builder within 10 years of completing improvements (s CB 11).

Note a similar exemption applied for properties damaged by the North Island flooding events in 2023 (s CZ 26B).
