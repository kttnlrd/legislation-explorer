# Co-operative companies as M#ori authorities ¶24-324

A M#ori authority that is a co-operative company may make a notional distribution to a member under s OB 79 as if a M#ori authority credit were an imputation credit. Notional distributions made by M#ori authorities that are co-operative companies are expressly included in the income of the members to whom the distributions are made. M#ori authority co-operative companies may attach M#ori authority credits to notional distributions in the same way that non-M#ori authority co-operative companies may attach imputation credits to such distributions. Cash distributions made in respect of notional distributions that have already been taxed to members (ie for which an election has been made) are not taxable M#ori authority distributions and are not included in the income of members. A M#ori authority may not be a co-operative company if a shareholder is not a M#ori authority.

*Refs: s OB 79*
