# Paying child support deductions ¶12-050

Employers (or PAYE intermediaries) pay any child support deductions when they pay their PAYE and other deductions — ie on the 20th of each month or, for large employers, on the 5th and 20th of each month (¶3-020). The details of the child support deductions made are included in the employment income information an employer must provide (¶3-023). Failure by an employer to pay child support deductions may result in IR imposing a penalty on the employer (¶12-060).

## Records

An employer must keep all relevant records for at least 7 years.
