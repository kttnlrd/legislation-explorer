# Tax administration for LTCs ¶19-240

An LTC must file an annual tax return using an IR7 form. An LTC income tax return must specify the amount of income derived by the company, and the amount of income and expenditure allocated to each owner.

There is no assessment of the company. Each owner makes a separate return of income taking into account the allocation of income and deductions from the LTC. Each owner is assessed separately.

An LTC is treated in a similar manner to a QC for the purposes of the disputes resolution regime. Due to the transparent nature of an LTC, owners are treated as taking the LTC's tax position for the purposes of imposing shortfall penalties.

Law: TAA s 42B
