# Background to limited partnerships ¶23-020

The Limited Partnerships Act 2008 came into force on 2 May 2008, creating a new legal entity in New Zealand called the limited partnership. The Act was accompanied by codified tax rules for both limited and general partnerships that came into effect on 1 April 2008.

The limited partnership rules to some extent replaced the older special partnerships rules that were repealed with effect from 2 May 2008.
