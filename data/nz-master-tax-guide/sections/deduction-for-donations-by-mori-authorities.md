# Deduction for donations by M#ori authorities ¶24-340

Section DV 12 provides that a M#ori authority may deduct:

## a donation made to a M#ori association as defined in the M#ori Community Development Act 1962,

for the purposes of that Act, or

## a charitable or other public benefit gift made to a donee organisation (¶11-055).

All qualifying donations and gifts of money are deductible, to the extent that the donations do not exceed the M#ori authority's net income for that tax year.

However, a deduction is denied to the extent to which the amount is a donation or gift by a deregistered charity to another charity within 12 months of deregistration.
