# Permitted disclosure: parental leave payments ¶1-634

Information may be exchanged between the Commissioner and Work and Income in order to verify entitlements to a parental leave payment or a preterm baby payment. The Commissioner may refuse an application for payment of a parental leave payment or a preterm baby payment when it appears a parental tax credit has been paid. The Commissioner may also refuse or recover a parental tax credit if a parental leave payment or preterm baby payment is to be, or has been, paid.

Law: TAA s 18H, Sch 7 pt C cl 46
