# Exemption for unexpected delay overseas ¶8-135

To be eligible for the exemption for unexpected delays overseas, the borrower must:

## have been tax resident during the time in question

## provide proof of their intended return to New Zealand, and

## provide proof that, if the borrower had returned to New Zealand as intended, the borrower would have

been New Zealand-based.

In addition, the borrower will need to (Sch 1 cl 3):

## provide evidence of the unexpected delay that resulted in them not being able to return to New

Zealand as intended, and

## provide evidence that the unexpected delay was due to an event or circumstances beyond the

reasonable control of the borrower (for example, an airline strike, personal illness, a fire, an explosion or act of war).

Example 4 in IS 25/20 illustrates circumstances in which an application for a delay may or may not be applicable.

Law: SLSA 2011 s 25(1)(c), Sch 1
