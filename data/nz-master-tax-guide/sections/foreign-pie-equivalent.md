# Foreign PIE equivalent ¶29-113

A foreign PIE equivalent is, broadly, a non-resident entity that would be eligible to be a PIE if it were resident in New Zealand. Foreign PIE equivalents are able to hold 100% of a New Zealand resident PIE, and vice versa (s HM 3). The definition of a foreign equivalent PIE includes an Australian managed investment trust (MIT) provided that the MIT is not a tax resident in New Zealand. Several exceptions to the investment and investor requirements apply for foreign PIE equivalents (s HM 23).

A foreign PIE equivalent loses that status immediately if it becomes resident in New Zealand and, alternatively, may lose that status if it fails to meet any of the other eligibility requirements to be a foreign PIE equivalent at the end of 2 consecutive quarters, with such loss taking place from the first day of the next (being the third) quarter.
