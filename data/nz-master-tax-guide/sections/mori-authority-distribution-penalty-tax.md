# M#ori authority distribution penalty tax ¶14-090

Section 140CB of the TAA imposes a special tax known as M#ori authority distribution penalty tax on a M#ori authority liable for further income tax under ss OK 21 and OK 22 of the IT07.

Under ss OK 21 and OK 22, a M#ori authority is liable to pay further income tax as a result of having a debit balance in its M#ori authority credit account at either the end of the tax year (ie 31 March) or immediately before it stops being a M#ori authority (¶24-327).

If a M#ori authority is liable for further income tax, it is also liable to pay penalty tax under s 140CB. The penalty tax is 10% of the debit balance in the M#ori authority credit account on 31 March.

*Refs: Section 140C, ss OK 21, s 140C*
