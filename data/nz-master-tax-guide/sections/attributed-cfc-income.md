# Attributed CFC income ¶5-382

A person's income includes all attributed CFC income. CFC refers to a controlled foreign company.

Attributed CFC income is generally income attributed from a foreign company in accordance with ss EX 1–EX 27 of the IT07. Special rules set out when attributed CFC income arises and how it is calculated (¶26-042).
