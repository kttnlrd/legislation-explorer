# Valuation of fishing catch ¶27-375

The trading stock rules apply to the valuation of a fishing catch (¶9-010 onwards). A market value basis might be used in some cases and it can be determined by reference to the actual sale price contracted with the customer or the auction price on the day that the catch is sold. More commonly, a cost basis is used.
