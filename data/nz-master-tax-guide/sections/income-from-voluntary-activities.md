# Income from voluntary activities ¶5-374

Generally, amounts derived by a person from voluntary activities are taxable income to that person, unless the amounts received are reimbursement payments for expenditure actually incurred by the volunteer (¶3-455).

Law: IT07 ss CO 1, CW 62B
