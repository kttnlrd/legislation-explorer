# Tax rates for attributed fringe benefits ¶50-081

## For the 2025–26 and later income years

The FBT thresholds and tax rates for attributed fringe benefits for the 2025–26 and later income years are as follows:

Income bracket Tax rate $0–$13,962 0.1173 $13,963–$45,230 0.2121 $45,231–$62,450 0.4286 $62,451–$130,723 0.4925 $130,724 and higher 0.6393

Alternatively, FBT on attributed fringe benefits may be calculated on 63.93% of the taxable value of those benefits.

## For the 2021–22 to 2024–25 income years

The FBT thresholds and tax rates for attributed fringe benefits for the 2021–22 to 2024–25 income year are as follows:

Income bracket Tax rate $0–$12,530 0.1173 $12,531–$40,580 0.2121 $40,581–$55,980 0.4286 $55,981–$129,680 0.4925 $129,681 and higher 0.6393

Alternatively, FBT on attributed fringe benefits may be calculated on 63.93% of the taxable value of those benefits.

## For the 2011–12 to 2020–21 income years

The FBT thresholds and tax rates for attributed fringe benefits for the 2011–12 to 2020–21 income years are as follows:

Income bracket Tax rate $0–$12,530 0.1173 $12,531–$40,580 0.2121 $40,581–$55,980 0.4286 $55,981 and higher 0.4925

Alternatively, FBT on attributed fringe benefits may be calculated on 49.25% of the taxable value of those benefits.
