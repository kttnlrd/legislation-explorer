# Qualifying companies and loss balances carried forward ¶19-110

A qualifying company (QC) can carry forward its loss balances in the same way as a non-QC. However, on becoming a QC, any loss balances that arose in income years relating to when the company was a non- QC were forfeited. Similarly, the loss balance of a non-QC that ceases to exist on amalgamation with a QC cannot be carried forward to the amalgamated QC.

Law: IT07 ss HA 21, HA 23
