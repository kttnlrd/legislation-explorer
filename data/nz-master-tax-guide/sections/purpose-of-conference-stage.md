# Purpose of conference stage ¶4-175

If matters remain in dispute after the filing of the NOPA and the NOR, parties are generally required to attend a conference (with their legal representatives) to discuss, clarify and try to resolve the differences in their understanding of the various facts and issues. The conference phase is an administrative practice and is not mandatory. However, it is encouraged as a means for resolving disputes, where possible, without the need to proceed to adjudication (SPS 23/01, "Disputes process", at [154]–[195]).
