# Election to be M#ori authority ¶24-301

An eligible person must make an election to be a M#ori authority by giving notice to the Commissioner on form IR483. The Commissioner must give the person notice that the election is accepted. The election takes effect on the first day of the income year in which notice of the election is given to the Commissioner, unless the notice of election nominates the first day of the succeeding income year, in which case the election will take effect from that later date.

An election ceases to have effect if an entity ceases to be a M#ori authority or if a M#ori authority gives notice to the Commissioner cancelling the election. An eligible person who chooses not to make an election is taxed under the general tax rules.

A deregistered charity that is eligible to be a M#ori authority may make a retrospective election to become a M#ori authority from the date on which the charity fails to comply with its rules. This ensures that such a deregistered charity is not taxed at a higher rate for its retrospective tax obligations than for its prospective obligations.
