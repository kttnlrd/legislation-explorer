# Avoidance involving partners ¶23-070

A specific anti-avoidance rule applies for partnerships where a partner of a partnership enters into a transaction for consideration that is greater than or less than market value and that has a purpose or effect of defeating the intent and application of the partnership provisions. Where this section applies, the transaction will be deemed to have taken place at market value.

Law: IT07 s GB 50
