# Action on receipt of notice of response ¶4-170

## Commissioner-initiated procedure

After considering the merits of a NOR, the Commissioner will do one of the following:

## accept the NOR in full and make any appropriate adjustments

## accept the NOR in part and advise the taxpayer of those issues that have been accepted, and the

remainder of the issues will progress to the conference phase

## reject the NOR in full and advise the taxpayer or agent of the rejection, and the issue will progress to

the conference stage.

## Taxpayer-initiated procedure

If the NOR was issued by the Commissioner (ie the taxpayer initiated the dispute procedure) and the taxpayer disagrees with the NOR, the taxpayer must notify the Commissioner within the response period (ie within 2 months of the date of the Commissioner's NOR), rejecting all or part of the Commissioner's notice.

The matter will then progress to the conference stage. Failure to notify the Commissioner of the rejection will result in deemed acceptance of the matters in the Commissioner's notice (s 89H(3) of the TAA).

Law: TAA s 89H(3)
