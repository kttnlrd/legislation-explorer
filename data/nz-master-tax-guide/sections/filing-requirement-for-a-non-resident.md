# Filing requirement for a non-resident ¶2-035

An individual who is regarded as non-resident for New Zealand income tax purposes must file a return of income (IR3NR) showing all income derived from New Zealand. The exception is if non-resident withholding tax (NRWT) was imposed on that income and that tax is treated as a final tax (not all forms of NRWT tax are a final tax). The tax position relating to a non-resident is more fully described beginning at ¶26-400.

If an individual is a New Zealand resident for income tax purposes for part of the tax year, the individual is required to file only an IR3 (resident return) for the year.

A non-resident employer may have employment-relating filing obligations in respect of cross-border employees that perform work in New Zealand (¶3-367 and ¶26-400).
