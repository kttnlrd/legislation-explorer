# Adjustment for change in accounting basis ¶5-097

Under general law, a tax advantage may be obtained by changing from a cash to an accrual (or earnings) accounting method, and a corresponding tax disadvantage suffered on a change from an accrual to a cash method. To overcome these effects, ss CH 4, DB 52 and EG 2 generally provide that, in the income year in which a person changes from a cash accounting method to an accrual accounting method for tax purposes:

## an amount owed to the person on the last day of the income year before the year of change is

income in the year of change, and

## an amount owed by the person on the last day of the income year before the year of change is an

allowable deduction in the year of change.

Similarly, if a person changes from an accrual accounting method to a cash accounting method for tax purposes:

## the total of all amounts owing to the person in the year of change that have been treated as income in

prior years is an allowable deduction in the year of change, and

## the total of all amounts owing by the person in the year of change that have been allowed as a

deduction in previous income years is income in the year of change.
