# FIF income ¶5-384

## Note: The Taxation (Annual Rates for 2025–26, Compliance Simplification, and Remedial Measures) Bill

proposes an amendment to clarify that short selling foreign shares would be treated in a similar way to short selling New Zealand shares. That is the gain/loss from the short selling arrangement would generally be fully taxable/deductible under ordinary revenue account property rules rather than being subject to the FIF rules.

A person's income includes all FIF income. FIF refers to a foreign investment fund. The FIF rules apply to non-resident investment vehicles where New Zealand residents hold interests that do not qualify as CFCs.

The FIF rules are set out in ss EX 28–EX 72 of the IT07. Special rules set out when FIF income arises and how it is calculated (¶26-110).
