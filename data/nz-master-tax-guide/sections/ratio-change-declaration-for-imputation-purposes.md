# Ratio change declaration for imputation purposes ¶17-070

The purpose of the allocation rules is to prevent the streaming of credits. Therefore, the imputation ratio of a dividend paid after the benchmark dividend in a tax year may only differ from that applied to the benchmark dividend if:

## the company completes a declaration to the effect that a subsequent dividend is not being credited at

a different ratio to the benchmark dividend as part of an arrangement to obtain a tax advantage, and

## a ratio change declaration (form IR407) is filed with IR before the date of payment of the subsequent

dividend, although there is a discretion to accept late lodgements.

Law: IT07 s OB 61
