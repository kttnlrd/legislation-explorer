# Non-charging of interest on provisional tax ¶22-360

Taxpayers are not liable to pay late payment penalties, use of money interest or both in respect of unpaid tax of $100 or less (s 183F(1) of the TAA). Conversely, the Commissioner is not liable to pay interest on overpaid tax of $100 or less.
