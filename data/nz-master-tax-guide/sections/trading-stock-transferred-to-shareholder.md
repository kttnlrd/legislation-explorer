# Trading stock transferred to shareholder ¶9-071

Where a company transfers trading stock to a shareholder or an associated person of a shareholder, the transfer occurs at market value for both the transferor and the transferee.

Although see discussion at ¶9-087 for exclusions from the market value deemed disposal rule in certain cases.

Law: IT07 ss CD 6, FC 1, FC 2
