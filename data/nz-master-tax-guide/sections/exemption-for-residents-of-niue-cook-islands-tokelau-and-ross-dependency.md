# Exemption for residents of Niue, Cook Islands, Tokelau, and Ross Dependency ¶8-165

A borrower making application for an exemption because they are located in Niue, Cook Islands, Tokelau, and Ross Dependency must:

- pay their repayment obligation in full when, or before, it falls due, and

- provide the Commissioner with evidence that they are physically in one or more of New Zealand,

Niue, the Cook Islands, Tokelau, or the Ross Dependency for the period to which the application applies.

Failure to meet these 2 requirements will result in the borrower being treated as an overseas borrower (¶6-045).

Practically, the borrower should register for a myIR account to check student loan balances and receive correspondence from IR. An IR1231 (September 2024) form can also be uploaded on myIR or posted to IR.
