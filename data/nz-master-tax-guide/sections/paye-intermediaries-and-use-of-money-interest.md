# PAYE intermediaries and use of money interest ¶3-482

Generally, a PAYE intermediary is subject to use of money interest on any over- or underpayment of PAYE for employers for whom the PAYE intermediary acts. The PAYE intermediary is liable for interest only if the following criteria are satisfied:

## the employer has met the obligations imposed under ss RP 9–RP 11 (see the first 2 bullet points in

¶3-479 under the heading "Employer"), and

## the employer has provided any information requested by the PAYE intermediary by the agreed date

(s RP 8).

Note that the Commissioner cannot apply interest payable on PAYE overpaid by the PAYE intermediary towards the payment of the PAYE intermediary's unpaid tax.

Interest receivable by a PAYE intermediary on overpaid PAYE may not be:

## treated as tax overpaid by the PAYE intermediary in any other capacity, or

## transferred to the PAYE intermediary in another capacity or to another taxpayer.

The provisions relating to the cancellation, remission, relief and refund of use of money interest also apply to a PAYE intermediary (s 185B of the TAA and ¶22-200 and ¶22-355).

*Refs: ss RP 9, s RP 8, s 185B*
