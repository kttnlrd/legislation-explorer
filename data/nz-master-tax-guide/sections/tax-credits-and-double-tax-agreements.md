# Tax credits and double tax agreements ¶1-410

A credit may be allowable for tax paid overseas under a DTA entered into between the New Zealand Government and the government of the overseas country in which the taxpayer has paid tax (¶26-280). In most instances, any credit will be subject to New Zealand domestic law and must not exceed the amount of New Zealand tax that would be payable on the income (¶26-255).
