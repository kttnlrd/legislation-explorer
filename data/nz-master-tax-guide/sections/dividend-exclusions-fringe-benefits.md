# Dividend exclusions — fringe benefits ¶16-695

A dividend does not arise for the provision of a fringe benefit that attracts FBT.

A dividend also does not arise if a motor vehicle benefit is provided to a shareholder–employee of a close company and that benefit would be a fringe benefit subject to FBT except that an election has been made by the close company under s CX 17(4B) to apply subpart DE instead of the FBT rules.

Another exclusion from dividend classification applies to accommodation or an accommodation allowance that is employment income in the hands of the employee.
