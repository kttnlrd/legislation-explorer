# Tax implications of M#ori Land Act ¶24-280

The M#ori Land Act 1993 (Te Ture Whenua M#ori Act 1993) came into force on 1 July 1993. The Act's main purpose is to assist in retaining M#ori land (comprising M#ori customary land and M#ori freehold land) in M#ori ownership. IR sets out the tax implications of the Act in Tax Information Bulletin ¶57-103 Vol 5, No 7, December 1993 at 4.
