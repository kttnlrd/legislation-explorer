# Excluded income — clean car discount scheme ¶5-770

A grant, subsidy or grant-related suspensory loan received from a local authority or a public authority is excluded income of the recipient of the grant under s CX 47.

*Refs: s CX 47*

## Clean car discount scheme

From 1 July 2021 until 31 December 2023, under the clean car discount scheme, a rebate will be paid, on application, to the first registered person of an eligible vehicle (or to a lessor where the first registered person of the vehicle is a lessee). IR has commented that the amount of a rebate under the scheme will either be a capital receipt or excluded income. An adjustment may be required to the cost of the vehicle for depreciation purposes (¶13-520).

Law: ITA 2007, s CX 47
