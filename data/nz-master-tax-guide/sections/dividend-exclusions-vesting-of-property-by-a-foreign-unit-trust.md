# Dividend exclusions — vesting of property by a foreign unit trust ¶16-690

The vesting of a beneficial interest in the assets of a foreign unit trust that is a dividend (¶16-573) does not lead to a further dividend when the legal interest in the property vests in the unitholder.

Law: IT07 s CD 30
