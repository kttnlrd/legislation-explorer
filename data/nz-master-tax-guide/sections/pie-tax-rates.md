# PIE tax rates ¶50-090

## PIE rates from 1 April 2025

From 1 April 2025, prescribed investor rates (portfolio investment entity (PIE) tax rates) for a natural person who is an investor in a multi-rate PIE are:

Taxable income Combined taxable income and PIE PIE tax rate income of investor $0–$15,600 $0–$53,500 10.5% $0–$15,600 $53,501–$78,100 17.5% $15,601–$53,500 $15,601–$78,100 17.5% $53,501–$78,100 $53,501–$78,100 28% $0 and higher $78,101 and higher 28%

Note that investors must satisfy the criteria in both the first 2 columns in a particular row to use the PIE rate listed in the third column of that row.

PIE rates for the period from 1 October 2010 to 31 March 2025 For the period from 1 October 2010 to 31 March 2025, prescribed investor rates (portfolio investment entity (PIE) tax rates) for a natural person who is an investor in a multi-rate PIE are:

Taxable income Combined taxable income and PIE PIE tax rate income of investor $0–$14,000 $0–$48,000 10.5% $0–$14,000 $48,001–$70,000 17.5% $14,001–$48,000 $14,001–$70,000 17.5% $48,001–$70,000 $48,001–$70,000 28% $0 and higher $70,001 and higher 28%

Note that investors must satisfy the criteria in both the first 2 columns in a particular row to use the PIE rate listed in the third column of that row.

For all other prescribed investor rates, see ¶29-115.

See Sch 6 of the Income Tax Act 2007.
