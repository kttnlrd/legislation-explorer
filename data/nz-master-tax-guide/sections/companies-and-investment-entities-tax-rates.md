# Companies and investment entities tax rates ¶50-050

Tax rates for 2011–12 and subsequent income years

## Resident companies

A flat rate of 28 cents for every dollar of taxable income.

## Non-resident companies

A flat rate of 28 cents for every dollar of taxable income derived from New Zealand.

## Investment entities

Group investment funds (not widely-held): trustees of the fund are taxed at the rate of 28 cents for every dollar of Category A income and at the rate of 39 cents for every dollar of Category B income.

Life insurers: life insurers are taxed at the rate of 28 cents for every dollar of schedular policyholder base income.

Portfolio investment entities (PIEs): the highest prescribed investor rate for taxable income earned by a portfolio investor in a PIE is the rate of 28 cents for every dollar of taxable income.

Superannuation funds (not widely-held): trustees of the fund are taxed at the rate of 39 cents for every dollar of taxable income.

Unit trusts: trustees of the unit trust are taxed at the rate of 28 cents for every dollar of taxable income.

Widely-held group investment funds: trustees of the fund are taxed at the rate of 28 cents for every dollar of Category A income and Category B income.

Widely-held superannuation funds: trustees of the fund are taxed at the rate of 28 cents for every dollar of taxable income.
