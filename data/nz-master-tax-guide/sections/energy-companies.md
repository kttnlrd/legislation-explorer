# Energy companies ¶24-032

Energy companies are taxed like any other company under the general provisions of the IT07.

## Available subscribed capital — calculation for energy

companies When the energy companies were corporatised, there was some doubt about whether the shares issued on the corporatisation gave rise to any available subscribed capital. However, Inland Revenue (IR) confirmed that available subscribed capital arose from the shares issued on corporatisation. See interpretation statement IS3507, "Available subscribed capital — energy companies".
