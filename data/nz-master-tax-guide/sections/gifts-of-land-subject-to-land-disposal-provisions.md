# Gifts of land subject to land disposal provisions ¶7-420

A gift may be made of land that is subject to the land disposal provisions. In that event, the donor is treated as having disposed of the land for its market value on the date on which the gift is effective. The recipient is treated as having an acquisition cost that is the same as that value.

Under the general law, a gift of land committed to a development scheme by a donor may be regarded as a gift of a capital asset that does not lead to assessable income upon the subsequent sale of the land by the donee. See Gutwenger v FC of T 95 ATC 4008.

Law: IT07 ss FC 1(1)(e), FC 2(1)
