# Dividend — shares issued under profit distribution plans ¶16-571

A share issued under a PDP is a dividend. A PDP is a scheme that involves a company notifying some or all of its shareholders that shares are to be issued on a specified date, and gives those shareholders the option of selling some or all of the shares issued to them back to the company. The dividend is quantified as the amount the company offers for repurchase of the share. As a result, the amount actually received by the shareholder following the exercise of the option is not a dividend.

A bonus issue in the form of a share issued under a PDP is a taxable bonus issue.

Shares issued under an employee share scheme are excluded. In addition, a PDP share does not trigger the non-dividend provisions in s CD 22 (off-market share cancellations).

IR views a PDP share issue as similar to a bonus issue in lieu, with the shareholder in either case being able to take the share or a cash amount. The taxation of PDP shares is viewed as part of the overall policy of preventing imputation credit streaming.
