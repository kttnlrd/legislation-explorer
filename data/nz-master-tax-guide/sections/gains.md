# gains ¶7-280

Under s CB 22 there is an exclusion from land disposal provisions creating income from the rezoning provisions (¶7-135) if:

## the person (person A) acquired the land and they, their spouse or partner or both of them used, or

intended to use, the land mainly for the purposes of a farming or agricultural business carried on by them, their spouse or partner or both of them, and

## person A disposed of the land to another person (person B) mainly for the purposes of the continuing

use of the land in a farming or agricultural business.

Person B's (ie the purchaser's) purposes in acquiring the land are ascertained from the circumstances of the disposal arising after person A (ie the vendor) acquired the land (with the exception of the rezoning factors creating this category of income). Activities that may constitute a farming or agricultural business are referred to at ¶7-270. In these situations, vendors should ascertain the intentions of purchasers to ensure that the desired exclusions apply.
