# Sliding value clause ¶90-500

This [deed/agreement] is not intended to effect or include any gift from the Vendor to the Purchaser. If the value of the Property as at the date of this [deed/agreement] is determined by the Commissioner of Inland Revenue (the Commissioner) to be a sum different from the Price then:

## the value so determined shall be substituted for the Price if the Vendor and Purchaser agree, or

## either the Vendor or Purchaser may cancel this agreement by giving written notice to the other within

14 days of the Commissioner's determination; in which case neither the Vendor nor the Purchaser shall have any further obligation to the other under this [deed/agreement].
