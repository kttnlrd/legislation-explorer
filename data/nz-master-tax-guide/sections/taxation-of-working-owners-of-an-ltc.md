# Taxation of working owners of an LTC ¶19-205

The owners of an LTC are allowed a deduction for payments made to a working owner. The usual PAYE rules apply to the payments made to the working owner. For an owner to be considered a "working owner" that person must meet the definition set out in s YA 1, which requires that their share of salary be paid under a written employment contract and that they personally and actively perform their duties under that contract.

This rule applies as long as the company is not wholly or mainly engaged in the business of investing money or in holding or dealing in shares, securities, investments or land.

For FBT purposes, a working owner is not an employee (¶21-030). Instead, the benefit provided by the LTC to the shareholder is considered as a distribution of profits to that shareholder.

*Refs: s YA 1*
