# Lottery duty ¶30-365

Lottery duty of 5.5% is payable by the organiser of any lottery on a "New Zealand lottery" as defined in s 4(1) of the GA. The definition includes a lottery, prize competition, or an instant game conducted by the Lotteries Commission (or a combination of these). The lottery organiser must furnish a return to the Commissioner and pay the lottery duty owing within 14 days after the date of making the draw. The lottery duty payable is a debt due and payable to the Crown. With effect from 27 April 2021, lottery duty may be refunded by direct credit.

*Refs: s 4(1)*
