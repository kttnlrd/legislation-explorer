# Employer's superannuation contribution tax ¶50-078

## ESCT rates from 1 April 2025

From 1 April 2025, the following rates of ESCT apply to an employer's superannuation cash contribution.

Threshold ESCT rate: Salary or wages plus superannuation contributions up to $18,720: 10.5% on all contributions Salary or wages plus superannuation contributions from $18,721 to $64,200: 17.5% on all contributions Salary or wages plus superannuation contributions from $64,201 to $93,720: 30% on all contributions Salary or wages plus superannuation contributions from $93,721 to $216,000: 33% on all contributions Salary or wages plus superannuation contributions over $216,001 39% on all contributions

ESCT rates for the period from 1 October 2010 to 31 March

For the period from 1 October 2010 to 31 March 2025, the following rates of ESCT apply to an employer's superannuation cash contribution.

Threshold ESCT rate: Salary or wages plus superannuation contributions up to $16,800: 10.5% on all contributions Salary or wages plus superannuation contributions from $16,801 to $57,600: 17.5% on all contributions Salary or wages plus superannuation contributions from $57,601 to $84,000: 30% on all contributions Before 1 April 2021, salary or wages plus superannuation contributions over 33% on all contributions $84,000: From 1 April 2021, salary or wages plus superannuation contributions over 33% on all contributions $84,001 to $216,000 From 1 April 2021, salary or wages plus superannuation contributions over 39% on all contributions $216,001

An employer may elect a 39% rate (33% before 1 April 2021) for a contribution to a defined benefit fund.

Contributions to past employees are subject to a 39% rate (33% before 1 April 2021).
