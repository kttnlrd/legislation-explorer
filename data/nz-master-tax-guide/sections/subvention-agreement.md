# Subvention agreement ¶90-400

## DEED dated                      20XX

## PARTIES

[Profit company name], a duly incorporated company registered in [place of incorporation] (the Paying Company) [Loss company name], a duly incorporated company registered in [place of incorporation] (the Payee Company) INTRODUCTION A. For the 20XX income year (the year) the Paying Company and the Payee Company have been members of the same group of the companies as defined in s IC 6 of the Income Tax Act 2007 (the Act).

B. The Payee Company has [incurred a loss for income tax purposes during the year/has prior year losses incurred at times when the Paying Company and the Payee Company are included in the same group of companies] (the Losses).

C. The Paying Company and the Payee Company have agreed to share in the Losses.

OPERATIVE PROVISIONS 1. The Paying Company has agreed to pay to the Payee Company the sum of $[amount of subvention payment] to share in the Losses of the Payee. 2. The payment referred to in clause 1 shall be paid by the Paying Company to the Payee Company by 31 March [the year following the income year to which the subvention payment relates]. 3. The Paying Company and the Payee Company agree to meet any requirements imposed on either of them by s IC 5 of the Act.
