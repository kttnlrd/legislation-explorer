# Dividends — producer boards and co-operative companies ¶16-575

A dividend includes a notional distribution with imputation credits attached effected by:

## a producer board

## a co-operative company.

Any payment that the board or company makes which is based on the notional distribution is not a dividend but a return of capital (¶16-750).
