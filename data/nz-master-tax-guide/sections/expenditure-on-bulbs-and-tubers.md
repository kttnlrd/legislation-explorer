# Expenditure on bulbs and tubers ¶27-420

Expenditure on bulbs and tubers for a flower farming business, including expenditure on acquiring a basic stock of bulbs with which to establish the business, has been held to be a deductible expense. In Case S38 (1995) 17 NZTC 7,255, all expenditure on bulbs and tubers was found to be expenditure for the purpose of gaining or producing assessable income and part of the flower farming process or activity rather than part of the structure on which the growing activity was based.

On the valuation of nursery plants, see ¶27-500.
