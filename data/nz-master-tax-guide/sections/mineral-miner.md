# Mineral miner ¶28-055

The rules apply to all "mineral miners". The definition of a "mineral miner" is no longer restricted only to mining companies. The rules therefore apply to all persons:

## whose only or main source of income is the business of mining listed industrial minerals (specified

minerals under the former rules) in New Zealand, or

## whose only or main activity, or proposed activity, is exploring, searching or mining for a listed

industrial mineral in New Zealand or performing development work for those purposes.

Work done on a service for reward basis does not make a person a mineral miner unless the reward is dependent on the production of the mineral or the participation of profits from the mineral's production.

Law: IT07 ss CU 6, CU 8
