# In specie distribution of land ¶7-410

Land subject to the land disposal provisions that is distributed by a company to a shareholder may result in dividends (¶16-550). In that event, the land is treated as having been disposed of by the company for its market value and acquired by the recipient for market value on the date of the distribution.

The shareholder derives dividends to the extent that that results in a transfer of company value to the shareholder.

Law: IT07 ss FC 1(1)(d), FC 2(1)
