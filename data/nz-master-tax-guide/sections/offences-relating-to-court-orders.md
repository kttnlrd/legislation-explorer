# Offences relating to court orders ¶14-270

A person who fails to comply with the terms of a court order made under s 17H commits an offence. Anyone who is convicted of such an offence is liable for up to 3 months imprisonment or a fine of up to $1,000 for each offence.

*Refs: s 17H*
