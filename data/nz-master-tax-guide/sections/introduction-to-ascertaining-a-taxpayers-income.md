# Introduction to ascertaining a taxpayer's income ¶5-160

One of the first steps in determining a person's income tax liability for the income year is to ascertain the person's income. Whether an amount derived by a person is income is determined from case law (¶5-021 and ¶5-031) and Pt C of the IT07 (¶5-162). Income derived is then further categorised into assessable income, exempt income, excluded income and non-residents' foreign-sourced income (¶1-055, ¶1-163–¶1-170).
