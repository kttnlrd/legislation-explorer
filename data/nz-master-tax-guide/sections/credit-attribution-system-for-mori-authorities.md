# Credit attribution system for M#ori authorities ¶24-314

Most M#ori authorities are required to establish and maintain a M#ori authority credit account (MACA). Tax paid by a M#ori authority that is required to maintain a MACA gives rise to M#ori authority credits that are recorded in its MACA. M#ori authority credits may be attached to "taxable M#ori authority distributions" made to members. The MACA is debited by the amount of credits attached to a distribution (¶24-327).

RWT at the rate of 17.5% must be deducted from a taxable M#ori authority distribution that is not fully credited. The rate of RWT increases to 39% (before 1 April 2021, 33%) if the distribution is more than $200 and the M#ori authority does not have on record the IRD number of the member to whom the distribution is made. For a taxable M#ori authority distribution, RWT paid is treated as a M#ori authority credit attached to the distribution (¶15-020 and ¶15-065).

## Members' income

The amount of the distribution and the credits are included in the members' income. The M#ori authority credits (which include any RWT paid in respect of the distribution) are available to offset income tax payable by members. Excess credits are refundable to members (s LA 6). Charities and other exempt entities that receive taxable distributions from M#ori authorities are required to file a return to claim a refund of M#ori authority credits.

M#ori authority credits attached to distributions received by a company give rise to credits in the company's ICA.

A person receiving a taxable M#ori authority distribution has a tax credit for the amount of the M#ori authority credit attached to the distribution. There are qualifications regarding the amount of credits that may be recognised. For example, credits attached in excess of the maximum permitted are not recognised to the extent of the excess, and credits attached to distributions paid under tax advantage arrangements are disregarded entirely. A credit of tax or a refund may be disallowed if the Commissioner considers that the amount claimed is excessive.

Taxable M#ori authority distributions are treated in the same manner as dividends for the purposes of tax- return filing requirements.

Non-taxable distributions cannot have M#ori authority credits attached and are not subject to RWT. The ability to pass out exempt income and other amounts tax-free means that M#ori authorities must track their income sources.

## Retirement scheme contributions

Where a M#ori authority makes a contribution to an approved retirement savings scheme for a member, retirement scheme contribution tax (RSCT) will apply. The tax is deducted by the M#ori authority before the contribution is paid to the retirement savings scheme. The M#ori authority must pay any RSCT deducted to IR. To meet the RSCT liability the M#ori authority can attach M#ori authority tax credits to the contribution.

Any excess credits attached to a contribution are passed through to the member (¶29-315).
