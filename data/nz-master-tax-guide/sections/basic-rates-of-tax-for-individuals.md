# Basic rates of tax for individuals ¶50-040

## Tax rates from the 2025–26 income year

The tax rates applying from the 2025–26 income year are shown in the table below:

Income bracket Tax rate $0–$15,600 10.5% $15,601–$53,500 17.5% $53,501–$78,100 30% $78,101–$180,000 33% $180,001 and higher 39%
