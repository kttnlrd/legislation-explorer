# Exempt income — amounts exempt from tax ¶5-598

#Note: The Taxation (Annual Rates for 2025–26, Emergency Response, and Remedial Measures) Bill proposes a new s CW 61B to provide that the gross income derived by a natural person from the supply of excess electricity generated at a dwelling is exempt income.

The following are wholly or partially exempt from tax under subpart CW:

## Accident Compensation Corporation personal service rehabilitation payments paid to a claimant and

used for rehabilitation — ¶5-653

## Accommodation — ¶3-425

## Allowance for additional transport costs — ¶3-445

## Allowances and benefits — ¶5-630

## Amateur games and sports bodies — ¶24-130

## Amounts from Australian complying superannuation schemes reinvested in KiwiSaver schemes —

¶29-270

## Annuities from Crown bank accounts — an annuity granted by the Executive Council of New Zealand

and paid from the Crown Bank Account is exempt income, provided it is not designated as being subject to tax — s CW 30

## Annuities under life insurance policies — an annuity paid under a life insurance policy offered or

entered into in New Zealand by a life insurer or paid under a life insurance policy offered or entered into offshore by a New Zealand resident life insurer is exempt income. This exemption does not apply to an annuity that is excluded income of a superannuation fund — s CW 4

## Avoidance arrangements — amounts treated as exempt income under ss GA 1 and GB 23 — s CW

*Refs: s CW 61B, s CW 30, s CW 4, ss GA 1*

## Certain employee benefits after an emergency event — s CZ 23B and ¶5-715

## Certain subdivisions of land — s CW 3C and ¶7-300

## Charitable bequests — income derived by a deceased's administrator or executor from property left

to a charity. The exemption will only apply if the income in question, if derived by a charitable trust, society or institution, would have been exempt from income tax under the other exemptions for a charity — s CW 43

## Charities: business income — ¶24-200, ¶24-210

## Charities: non-business income — ¶24-240

## Community housing entities — ¶24-132

## Community trusts — ¶24-133

## Employee reimbursement and expenditure on account — ¶3-435

## Employee share schemes — ¶3-471

## Exemption under other Acts — ¶5-685 and ¶5-690

## Forestry companies established by Crown, M#ori owners and holding companies — ¶28-050

## Forestry rights — Treaty of Waitangi claim settlements — ¶28-021

## Forestry encouragement agreements — income advanced under a forestry encouragement

agreement is exempt income. Also, in some cases, interest under the agreement that a person is relieved from paying is exempt income — s CW 2

## Friendly societies — ¶24-131

## Funeral trusts — ¶24-134

## Gaming-machine income derived by a gaming-machine licensed operator who complies with the

Gambling Act 2003 — s CW 48

## Governor-General's income — the allowance, benefits and privileges of the Governor-General is

exempt income — s CW 16 and ¶3-016

## Herd improvement bodies — ¶24-139

## Interest: farm mortgages — 50% of the interest derived from a mortgage securing a loan made by

a seller of a farm is exempt income if the Rural Banking and Finance Corporation of New Zealand approves the mortgage and the Corporation gives the Commissioner notice of the approval and each variation. This exemption does not apply to an absentee, a company, a M#ori authority, a public authority, a trustee liable for income tax under subpart HC and s HZ 2, or to an unincorporated body.

A person deriving exempt interest cannot also claim a tax credit for the interest under the home vendor mortgages tax credit provisions — s CW 6

## Interest: foreign-sourced — ¶5-613

## Interest: paid by the Commissioner under s 84 of the KiwiSaver Act 2006 — s CW 62

## Interest: post-war credits — wholly exempt from income tax — s CW 5

## Jurors' and witnesses fees (but not expert witnesses) — wholly exempt from income tax — s CW 26

## Life reinsurance outside New Zealand — a life reinsurance claim derived by a life insurer to the

extent to which deductions for premiums are denied — s CW 59C

## Loans to New Zealand Government — ¶5-628

## Local authorities — ¶5-610, ¶16-800, ¶24-035 and ¶24-040

## Local and regional promotion bodies — ¶24-035 and ¶24-137

## M#ori authority distributions — ¶24-311

## Military and police service in operational areas — ¶5-658

## Mixed-use assets — ¶5-635

## Niue International Trust Fund — income derived or distributed by the trustees — s CW 59B
