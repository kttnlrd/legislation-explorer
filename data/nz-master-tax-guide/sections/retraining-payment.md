# Retraining payment ¶3-317

The Commissioner's policy regarding the tax treatment of retraining payments made in lieu of a redundancy payment is set out in Tax Information Bulletin ¶73-107 Vol 7, No 3, September 1995 at 6. The Commissioner considers that retraining payments received by an employee upon termination of employment are more in the nature of a redundancy payment than a bursary payment eligible for exemption under s CW 36 (¶5-633).

Retraining payments are therefore considered to fall within the definition of employment income and are taxable in the hands of the former employee under s CE 1. Consequently, an employer must deduct PAYE from the amount of any such payment.
