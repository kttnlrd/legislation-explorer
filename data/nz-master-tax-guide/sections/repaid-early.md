# repaid early ¶8-090

If the Commissioner notifies a borrower of their consolidated loan balance and the loan is repaid within 30 days of the date of such notification, the Commissioner must cancel the interest accruing between the date of notification and the date on which payment is received.

Law: SLSA 2011 s 196
