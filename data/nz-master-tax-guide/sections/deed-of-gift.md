# Deed of gift ¶90-100

DEED dated 20XX PARTIES [Name] (the Donor) [Trustees' names] (the Donees) INTRODUCTION A. The Donees are the trustees of a trust known as [Trust name] (the Trust) which was created by deed dated [date of settlement].

B. The Donees owe the Donor the sum of $[amount of debt] (the Debt), under a Deed of Acknowledgement of Debt dated [date of deed] on account of an advance made to the Donees by the Donor.

C. The Donor wishes to release the Donees from their liability to repay the sum of $[amount of gift] and reduce the Debt.

OPERATIVE PROVISIONS 1. In consideration of the Donor's natural love and affection for the primary beneficiaries of the Trust, the Donor releases the Donees from all liability to repay the sum of $[amount of gift] being part of the Debt. 2. The intent of the Donor is that the amount owing by the Donees to the Donor under the Deed is now the sum of $[amount of debt after gift]. 3. The Donees accept this partial forgiveness of the Debt.
