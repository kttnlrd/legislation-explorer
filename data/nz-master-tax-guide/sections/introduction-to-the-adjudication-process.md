# Introduction to the adjudication process ¶4-195

Disputes that are not resolved during the pre-assessment phase are subject to review and adjudication by the Tax Counsel Office (TCO). The adjudicator is an impartial IR officer who is independent of the audit function.

Not every case will be sent to adjudication. Sometimes the Commissioner may issue an assessment before the adjudication process can be utilised. There is no specific legislation governing the function of the TCO.
