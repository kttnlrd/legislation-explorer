# Approval of employer arrangement with PAYE intermediary ¶3-477

An employer who wishes to engage a PAYE intermediary must notify the Commissioner. The notice must include the following details:

## the name of the PAYE intermediary

## the period for which the PAYE intermediary will act for the employer

## the bank account number of the PAYE intermediary's trust account, and

## whether the proposed arrangement requires the PAYE intermediary to make employer's

superannuation contribution tax deductions.

Notification of these details may also be made by the PAYE intermediary.

The Commissioner must send a notice of approval to the employer. The approval will apply to pay periods that begin on or after the date that is 14 days after the date of the notice. For example, a notice of approval dated 1 May 2022 will apply to pay periods that begin on or after 15 May 2022.

An employer or a PAYE intermediary may end an arrangement by notifying the other party and the Commissioner. The notice must state the date that is after the notification for the end of the arrangement.

Law: TAA s 124O(1), (2)
