# Criminal offences under the Student Loan Scheme Act ¶8-110

The SLSA 2011 includes the criminal offences that apply for income tax purposes under the TAA, such as strict liability offences and knowledge and evasion offences. The same maximum penalty amounts that apply for income tax offences also apply to student loan offences.
