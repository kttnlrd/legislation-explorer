# Penalties for PAYE intermediaries ¶3-485

A PAYE intermediary is generally subject to late filing penalties, late payment penalties and shortfall penalties on any unpaid PAYE as if that person were the employer unless the employer has failed to:

## pay the gross salary or wages to the intermediary in accordance with ss RP 9 to RP 11 (see the first

2 bullet points in ¶3-479 under the heading "Employer")

## provide any information requested by the PAYE intermediary by the agreed date.

If the employer does not satisfy the above conditions, the late filing, late payment and shortfall penalties on any unpaid PAYE will continue to be the liability of the employer.

The non-electronic filing penalty also applies to a PAYE intermediary (¶14-045).

The provisions relating to the recovery, remission, and refund of penalties also apply to a PAYE intermediary.
