# Superannuation schemes and family assistance ¶29-435

For the purposes of calculating family scheme income (ie determining assistance for low-income households), the Commissioner is empowered to include in income any distributions received from a superannuation scheme. Such a distribution may be apportioned when the distribution is received by an employee in the current or preceding 2 income years, the employee continues to be employed at least one month after the distribution and the employer has made a contribution in the year of distribution. However, s MB 5 does not apply to a person receiving a distribution from a superannuation scheme on or after their retirement if the employer contributed to the superannuation scheme or if it is a KiwiSaver scheme or complying superannuation fund. As a result, an employee cannot reduce their income to a level whereby the employee would qualify for a family scheme tax credit.
