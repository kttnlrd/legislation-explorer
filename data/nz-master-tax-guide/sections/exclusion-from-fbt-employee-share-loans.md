# Exclusion from FBT — employee share loans ¶21-568

An employee share loan is excluded from the definition of fringe benefit.

An employee share loan meets the following criteria:

## the purpose of the loan must be to enable the employee (and not an associate) to acquire shares in

the employer (or a company associated with the employer)

## the loan must be used for that purpose only

## the shares must be beneficially owned by the employee at all times during the currency of the loan

## the loan must include a condition that the employee must immediately repay the loan in full if the

employee stops being the beneficial owner of the shares

## the company issuing the shares maintains a dividend paying policy

## the company issuing the shares is not a qualifying company throughout the term of the loan

## the employer and employee are not associated persons, and

## the employee share loan is not made under an exempt employee share scheme (ESS).

An employee share loan also applies to a loan for the purchase of rights or options in the company.

## Employee share loans exclusion and associates of the

employee QWBA QB 24/03: "Fringe benefit tax — employee share loans and associates" considered whether a fringe benefit arises where a trustee of a family trust that is associated with an employee is provided a loan to acquire shares under an ESS. The Commissioner's view is that the employee share loans exclusion can apply provided a fringe benefit would not arise if the employee were provided the loan to acquire the shares under the ESS in the same circumstances.
