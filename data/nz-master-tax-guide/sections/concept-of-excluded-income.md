# Concept of excluded income ¶1-170

Certain types of income are expressly treated as "excluded income" by specific provisions of the IT07. For example, a fringe benefit received by an employee is excluded income of the employee (s CX 3). Excluded income is omitted from a person's calculation of income tax for an income year.

*Refs: s CX 3*

Law: IT07 subpart CX, ss BD 1(3), CA 2(3)
