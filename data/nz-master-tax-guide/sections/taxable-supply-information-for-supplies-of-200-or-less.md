# Taxable supply information for supplies of $200 or less ¶32-180

For commentary on the requirements for tax invoices for supplies $50 or less before 1 April 2023, please refer to CCH, New Zealand Master Tax Guide 2023.

The taxable supply information that must be retained by both the supplier and the recipient for a supply that has a value not exceeding $200 is the supply information for the supply. In most cases, a record of the sale will contain the relevant taxable supply information will be generated by the point-of-sale system. A GST- registered recipient is entitled to request a manual receipt if no till receipt is available.

Law: GST ss 19E(2)(c), 19F, 75
