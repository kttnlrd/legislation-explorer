# Overview of M#ori authorities ¶24-288

A specific regime applies for the taxation of M#ori authorities and individuals who derive benefits from those organisations. All eligible persons who elect to be M#ori authorities are taxed under the regime. The regime includes a credit attribution system similar to the company imputation regime. The main provisions of the regime are contained in subparts HF and OK of the IT07.

The key features of the regime are:

## a M#ori authority is taxed on its income at the rate of 17.5%

## tax paid by the M#ori authority gives rise to M#ori authority credits that are credited to a "M#ori

authority credit account"

## M#ori authority credits may be attached to distributions of tax-paid income made to members to

reflect the tax paid by the M#ori authority

## the amount of the distribution and the value of the M#ori authority credits are included in a member's

income

## M#ori authority credits may be attached to contributions made to approved retirement savings

schemes for members, to satisfy the contributor's retirement scheme contribution tax liability (¶29-300 onwards)

## a member can use M#ori authority tax credits to offset their tax liabilities, and

## M#ori authority credits that cannot be offset against a member's tax liabilities are refundable to the

member.

Tax provisions outside of the M#ori authority rules apply to M#ori authorities, subject to the M#ori authority rules.

See IR booklet, "Becoming a M#ori authority", IR487 (November 2023).
