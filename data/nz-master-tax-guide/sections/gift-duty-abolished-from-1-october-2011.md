# Gift duty abolished from 1 October 2011 ¶30-125

Gift duty was abolished for dispositions of property made on or after 1 October 2011. The Estate and Gift Duties Act 1968 remained effective for dispositions of property before that date.

For commentary on gift duty for dispositions before 1 October 2011, please refer to CCH, New Zealand Master Tax Guide 2012.
