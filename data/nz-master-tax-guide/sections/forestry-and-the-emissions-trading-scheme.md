# Forestry and the Emissions Trading Scheme ¶28-055

Interpretation Statement IS 25/13: "Income Tax and GST — forestry activities registered in the Emissions Trading Scheme" sets out the Commissioner's view on the income tax and GST consequences of forestry activities registered in the Emissions Trading Scheme (ETS). The ETS uses 1 January 1990 as a starting point for measuring changes in forestry growth. The item covers a wide range of topics including:

## the types of New Zealand Units (NZUs) earned from forestry activities

## the general income tax treatment of NZUs, like sale and surrender

## what deductions are available

## specific events or transactions relating to forestry rights, changes to forestry activities, and exit from

the ETS

## purchase price allocation rules for the sale of forests.

## Income tax

The income tax treatment depends on the activity undertaken by the forester. When pre-1990 forest land NZUs were allocated to foresters, at the time of receipt those NZUs were tax exempt as long as the land was held on capital account (s CW 3B).

Generally, receipt of post-1989 forest land NZUs for removal activities are taxable, but the tax liability is delayed until the NZUs are sold (s ED 1(7B)). Gains made on the sale of NZUs are taxable (s CB 36). There are some exceptions to this general rule for certain situations involving pre-1990 forest land NZUs (s CX 51B), or for transfers of these units under a lending arrangement.

Surrender of NZUs is a deemed disposal and the amount to be treated as income under s CB 36. IS 25/13 details the tax implications of the types of surrender of different types of units at [56] onwards.

## GST

For GST purposes NZUs are services and not goods. Supplies of NZUs are subject to GST. Most supplies are zero-rated under s 11A(1)(s). If the registered forester deregisters from GST, the person is treated as making a zero-rated supply. Therefore, there are no GST consequences for the person who continues to hold NZUs after deregistration (s 5(3C)).

Law: IT07 ss CB 36, CX 51B, ED 1(7B); GST ss 5(3C), 11A(1)(s)
