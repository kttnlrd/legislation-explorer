# Other income under subpart CG ¶5-358

The following recovered amounts are also income under subpart CG:

## previously allowed deductions in relation to high-priced bloodstock on the sale or export of the

bloodstock from New Zealand without first being raced or used for breeding in New Zealand — ss CG 8B and CG 8C and ¶27-305

## previously allowed deductions for aircraft engine maintenance on the disposal of the aircraft engine or

aircraft — s CG 9 and ¶10-127.

*Refs: s CG 9*
