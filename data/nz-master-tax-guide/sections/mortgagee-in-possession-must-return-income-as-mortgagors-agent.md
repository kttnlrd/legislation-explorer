# Mortgagee in possession must return income as mortgagor's agent ¶5-252

When a mortgagee exercises its rights to enter into possession of the land or other property that constitutes their security, pending a later sale, the mortgagee is deemed to be the agent of the mortgagor in respect of any income derived by the mortgagee from the security on behalf of the mortgagor. As the mortgagor's agent, the mortgagee must make a return of the income derived from the property and pay any tax owing.

Law: IT07 s HD 10
