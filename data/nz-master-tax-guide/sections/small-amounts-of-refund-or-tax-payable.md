# Small amounts of refund or tax payable ¶2-155

The Commissioner has a discretion to write off tax, refrain from making an assessment, or refrain from collecting tax if the balance of the tax payable is not more than $20 or the tax paid, withheld or deducted is not more than $5.

Refund of tax subsequently exempted by Order in Council

The Commissioner may refund tax previously paid that is subsequently exempted by any Order in Council arising out of a double taxation agreement if application for the refund is made within 4 years after the date on which the Order in Council that gives effect to the double taxation agreement comes into force. The time limits for the payment of tax refunds (¶2-145) do not apply in this case.
