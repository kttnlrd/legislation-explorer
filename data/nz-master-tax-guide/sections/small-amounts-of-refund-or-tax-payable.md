# Small amounts of refund or tax payable ¶2-155

The Commissioner has a discretion to write off tax, refrain from making an assessment, or refrain from collecting tax if the balance of the tax payable is not more than $20 or the tax paid, withheld or deducted is not more than $5.

Law: TAA s 174AA
