# Value of supply under business-to-business regime for GST ¶32-340

A special value of supply rule may apply to the supply of a financial service that is zero rated or if the provider of the financial service claims an input tax credit for a financial service supplied to another financial services provider (¶32-330). In either of these cases, the value of the supply is its open market value if:

## the supply is made for more than the open market value, and

## the supplier and the recipient are associated persons.

This is an anti-avoidance measure to stop any over-valuation of supplies of financial services made between associated persons.

Law: GST s 10(3B)
