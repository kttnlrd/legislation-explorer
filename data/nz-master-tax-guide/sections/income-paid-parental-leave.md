# Income — paid parental leave ¶5-311

A person's income includes all payments of parental leave made under Pt 7A of the Parental Leave and Employment Protection Act 1987.

Paid parental leave is administered through IR. Form IR880 must be completed by the employee and the income and work details must be confirmed by the employer, the employee's chartered accountant or tax advisor, or a Justice of the Peace, and the form must then be filed with IR before the end of parental leave or before resignation.
