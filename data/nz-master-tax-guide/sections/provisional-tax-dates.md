# Provisional tax dates ¶50-020

A taxpayer's provisional tax dates depend on whether and how the taxpayer pays GST and whether they have adopted the GST ratio method of calculating provisional tax or the accounting income method (AIM).

Provisional tax is due on the 28th day of the month, except for the amounts that would normally be due on 28 December and 28 April, which are due on 15 January and 7 May, respectively, to take into account the Christmas and Easter holiday periods.

The tables below set out the provisional tax dates for the different types of provisional taxpayers, as specified.

For a discussion of the instalment dates for provisional taxpayers, see ¶22-012 and ¶22-115.
