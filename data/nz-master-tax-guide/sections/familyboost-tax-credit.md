# FamilyBoost tax credit ¶11-200

The FamilyBoost tax credit regime provides financial assistance to certain families who incur early childhood education (ECE) costs, like fees paid to a kindergarten. It is a childcare tax rebate. Eligible families are reimbursed up to 40% of their ECE fees. This subsidy is paid after any eligibility for 20 hours ECE and childcare subsidies from the Ministry of Social Development are taken into account. A single claim is permitted, irrespective of how many children the caregiver has under their care.

## Amount of tax credit

From 1 July 2025, reimbursement payments are paid quarterly and capped at $1,560 per quarter. The payments reduce for households with annual income of $140,000 and cease completely for households earning $229,144 or more a year.

## Eligibility

The ECE provider must be registered under s 10 of the licensed under the Education and Training Act 2020.

Only natural persons are entitled to receive a FamilyBoost tax credit. The person must be the caregiver of one or more children enrolled with a licensed ECE provider, incurs the costs of ECE and must be a New Zealand resident.

A refund may be applied for if both the caregiver and their spouse, civil union partner, or de fact partner have filed their tax returns in time. Only one FamilyBoost tax credit arises for a couple.

If couples separate during a quarter, and the caregiver does not have a new partner at the end of the quarter, then the rule does not apply on a couple basis. Each parent may make a claim for the tax credit provided that they meet a portion of the costs. Each parent is treated as having a separate household.

Grandparents may also be eligible to claim the credit provided that the child lives in the grandparent's household and the ECE invoices are in the grandparent's name.

If an eligible person dies during a quarter, the trustee of the deceased estate can apply for a refund.

## Refund application

Caregivers or parents must upload and submit their ECE invoices via IR's online myIR system, similar to the current donations tax credit model. Weekly invoices must be collected and submitted every 3 months.

Payments will be deposited directly into the applicant's bank account.
