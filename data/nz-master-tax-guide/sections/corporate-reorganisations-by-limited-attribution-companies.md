# Corporate reorganisations by limited attribution companies ¶16-219

Special provision is made in s YC 18B for corporate reorganisations by limited attribution companies when there is no change to the economic ownership within the group. Shareholder continuity is maintained for the initial parent company, the new parent and any interests deemed to be held by the initial parent before the restructuring. The section is aimed at certain reorganisations by Australian-owned banks (Tax Information Bulletin ¶218-143 Vol 21, No 8, October/November 2009, Pt II at 123).
