# How long can you get a student loan for? ¶8-022

Each new course requires a new loan and loan accounts run for 52 weeks.

There is a lifetime limit of 7 EFTS for student loans.

Full-time students usually have study loads of between 0.8 EFTS and 1.2 EFTS per a year. For a full-time student, 7 EFTS is equal to about 7 or 8 years of study. Part-time students use less EFTS each year.

Once any part of the student loan is used for living costs, fees or course-related costs, the EFTS for that loan will count towards the 7-EFTS limit.

A student can use more than 7 EFTS (eg post-graduate study); however, most students will not be able to receive more than 10 EFTS of student loan entitlements.
