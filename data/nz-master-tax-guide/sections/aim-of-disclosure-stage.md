# Aim of disclosure stage ¶4-180

If the dispute remains unresolved after the conference (and the parties have not opted out of the disputes process), the parties proceed to disclosure. This process aims to allow the taxpayer and Commissioner to lay all their cards on the table and delineate the nature and scope of the dispute. To encourage this aim, any future litigation is generally limited to the evidence and legal arguments that are disclosed in the SOPs (¶4-190).
