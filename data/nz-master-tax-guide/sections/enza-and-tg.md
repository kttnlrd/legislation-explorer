# ENZA and T&G ¶27-460

Enzafruit merged with Turners and Growers (T&G) to export New Zealand pipfruit to Europe. T&G deducts certain statutory levies, such as horticultural levies that are forwarded to the relevant body. Any levy paid through T&G is an allowable deduction when there is no provision for repayment of the levy.

T&G charges growers a freight and storage charge when fruit is stored at T&G-owned storage locations. This is an operational charge and is deductible by the grower.
