# Imputation credit account: debit or credit incorrectly recorded ¶17-050

Should the Commissioner consider that there has been an incorrect recording of debits and credits arising in the company's ICA, the Commissioner may issue a determination under s 104B of the TAA adjusting the amount of a credit or debit or a credit/debit date. Any such determination under this section may be challenged in the same way as an assessment.

*Refs: s 104B*

Law: IT07 s OA 2(5); TAA s 104B
