# Withholding tax obligations of a consolidated group ¶20-068

Joint and several liability for a consolidated group's income tax extends to RWT, non-resident withholding tax (NRWT), FBT, ESCT and tax deductions including PAYE. However, member companies remain individually responsible for complying with their respective obligations for these taxes, such as filing individual returns.
