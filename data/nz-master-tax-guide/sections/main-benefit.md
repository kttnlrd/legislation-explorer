# Main benefit ¶3-356

Payment of a main benefit (previously an income-tested benefit) is treated as a payment of salary or wages.

Normal PAYE tax deductions are made by the department responsible for the Social Security Act 2018.

A main benefit is any of the following benefits paid under the Social Security Act 2018:

## sole parent support

## emergency benefit

## youth payment

## supported living payment

## jobseeker support, and

## young parent payment.

The amount of PAYE deductions from a main benefit is deducted by the Ministry of Social Development and the tax rate is to be determined by the Commissioner in consultation with the chief executive of the Ministry of Social Development. The personal income tax thresholds was amended from 31 July 2024 and to facilitate the smooth administration of the taxation of main benefits, the Commissioner has issued DET 24/03, "Determination under section RD 11(3) of the Income Tax Act 2007 of the amount of tax for a payment of a main benefit", which sets out the rates of PAYE to be deducted from the main benefits:

## For main benefit payments made up to and including Thursday, 1 August 2024, the rate of tax is the

"M" tax rate set out in the Commissioner's weekly PAYE tables that apply from 1 April 2024 to 30 July 2024.

## For main benefit payments made on or after Friday, 2 August 2024, the rate of tax is the "M" tax rate

set out in the Commissioner's weekly PAYE tables that apply from 31 July 2024.

The tax deducted under s RD 11(3) is not a final tax for the recipient of the main benefit and an end of year square up is required to determine the final tax liability.

*Refs: section RD 11(3), s RD 11(3), Income Tax Act 2007*
