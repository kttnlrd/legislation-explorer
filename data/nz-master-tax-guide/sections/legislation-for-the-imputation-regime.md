# Legislation for the imputation regime ¶17-015

The imputation and related memorandum account provisions are located in Pt O of the Income Tax Act 2007 (IT07), consisting of the following subparts:

## Subpart OB — Imputation Credit Accounts (ICAs) (which deals with income tax paid by a New

Zealand company that may be credited to shareholders).

## Subpart OF — Available Subscribed Capital Accounts (ASCAs) (which deals with available

subscribed capital lost on redemption of units by unit holders in a group investment fund deriving category A income or in a qualifying unit trust) (¶24-070).

## Subpart OK — M#ori Authority Credit Accounts (MACAs) (which deals with income tax paid by a

M#ori authority that may be credited to a member of the authority) (¶24-314).

## Subpart OP — which deals with the various memorandum accounts of consolidated groups (¶17-250

and ¶20-070).

This chapter focuses principally on imputation.
