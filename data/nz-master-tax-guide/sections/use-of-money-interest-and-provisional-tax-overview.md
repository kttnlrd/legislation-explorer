# Use of money interest and provisional tax overview ¶22-350

Use of money interest applies to any underpayment or overpayment of provisional tax on each of the applicable instalment dates, and in addition to any late payment penalty that may apply.

Use of money interest generally runs from the first instalment date, except in the case of safe-harbour taxpayers and interest concession provisional taxpayers. Use of money interest generally runs from the last instalment date for interest concession provisional taxpayers.

Use of money interest does not apply to AIM provisional taxpayers.

Interest rates are set by Order in Council, amending the Taxation (Use of Money Interest Rates) Regulations 1998. See rates listed at ¶50-083.

From 16 January 2026 the interest rate charged by IR on unpaid tax is 8.97%. The rate for overpayments of tax is 2.25%.
