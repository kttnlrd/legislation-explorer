# Permitted disclosure: child support exemption ¶1-631

Information may be exchanged between the Commissioner and an officer, employee or agent of the New Zealand Police or the Ministry of Justice to determine the possible application of the child support exemption for a liable parent who conceives a child as a result of a sex offence.

A sex offence is any offence under ss 127–144C of the Crimes Act 1961.

The information that may be provided is:

## whether a particular person has been convicted of a sex offence or been proved to have committed a

sex offence before the Youth Court and details of that offence

## whether a particular person is the victim of that offence

## whether a conviction for that offence has been quashed, or a finding of the Youth Court that a sex

offence has been committed has been reversed or set aside

## whether any court has ordered a new trial, and

## any other particulars the Commissioner considers relevant.

*Refs: ss 127*

Permitted disclosure: foreign social security agreement

Only where either an information matching agreement has been entered into under Pt 10 of the Privacy Act 1993 and continued under the Privacy Act 2020, or an approved information sharing agreement has been entered into under subpart 1 of Pt 7 of the Privacy Act 2020, may information be exchanged between the Commissioner and Work and Income in order to provide assistance to another country with the implementation of a social security agreement made between New Zealand and that country (Sch 7 pt C cl 45 of the TAA).

Personal information received by the Commissioner in accordance with a social security agreement may be used only to make an assessment of tax or to detect tax fraud or evasion. Personal information is information that identifies an individual, and includes that person's tax file number.

The range of information that may be supplied by the Commissioner in relation to a person encompasses:

## the person's street address

## the name and street address of the person's last known employer

## details of income when a comparison indicates that the person is receiving, or has received, income

## the names and dates of birth of the person's dependent children if known, and

## any other information held by the Commissioner that is of a type specified in the social security

agreement.
