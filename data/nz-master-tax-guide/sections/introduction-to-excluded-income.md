# Introduction to excluded income ¶5-750

Some types of receipts or benefits are referred to as excluded income. To be excluded income, the amount in question must be listed in subpart CX (Excluded income) or CZ (Terminating provisions) of the IT07 and cannot be the person's non-resident foreign-sourced income. Excluded income is not taken into account when calculating a person's income tax liability (ie it is not included in the person's assessable income).

Any expenditure incurred in earning excluded income can be deducted under s DA 1 providing it is not disallowed by one of the limitations included in s DA 2.

*Refs: s DA 1, s DA 2*
