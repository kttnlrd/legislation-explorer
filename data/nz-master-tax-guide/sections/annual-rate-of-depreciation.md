# Annual rate of depreciation ¶13-124

The annual rate of depreciation for an item of depreciable property is:

## the economic rate (¶13-126), special rate, or provisional rate for property not described below, or

## for items acquired on or before 20 May 2010, the economic rate, special rate, or provisional rate for

the property multiplied by an uplift factor of 1.2 (commonly referred to as "depreciation loading") where the property has not previously been depreciable property in New Zealand and the property is not a building, a used imported car or an international aircraft, or

## 0% for:

## a building for the 2011–12 to 2019–20 income years

## a residential building for the 2020–21 to 2023–24 income year

## a building from the 2024–25 income year that has an estimated useful life of 50 years or more

(¶13-410), or

## a DV rate of 15% or a SL rate of 10% for an international aircraft (¶13-128).
