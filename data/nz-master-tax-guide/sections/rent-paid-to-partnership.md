# Rent paid to partnership ¶23-134

An anti-avoidance provision applies to ensure that the amount of rent paid by a relative of a partner or a related company to a partnership is "adequate rent", and for such a transaction the partnership treated as opaque (s GC 5(2)(d)). An adequate rent is an amount that the Commissioner determines to be adequate for the relevant property (generally, a market rent). That amount is deemed to have been payable for the period specified in any relevant lease agreement or, if no rent is payable under the lease, for each day of the lease term on a pro rata basis.

The amount of deemed rental is income derived by the partnership. The Commissioner has issued a public binding ruling, BR Pub 06/02, "Section GD 10 [of the Income Tax Act 2004] — rent deemed to be payable by the lessee", which concludes that a corresponding deduction will be allowed to the lessee in terms of the general permission. The ruling applies for an indefinite period to leases entered into on or following 1 February 2006.

*Refs: s GC 5(2)(d), Section GD 10, Income Tax Act 2004*
