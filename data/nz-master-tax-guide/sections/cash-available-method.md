# Cash available method ¶1-586

The cash available method isolates the cash available to the taxpayer for a year and compares that amount with any non-business cash payments made. The cash available method is used when it is suspected that a taxpayer who owns a cash business, eg a video shop, is dipping into the till and removing cash takings without keeping a record of amounts taken.
