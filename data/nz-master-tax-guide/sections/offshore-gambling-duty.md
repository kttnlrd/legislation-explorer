# Offshore gambling duty ¶30-366

The offshore gambling duty applies to offshore gambling profits arising on or after 1 July 2024.

The offshore gambling duty applies to offshore gambling operators who are defined as GST-registered persons located outside New Zealand who conduct any offshore gambling. "Offshore gambling" is defined as any gambling or prize competition supplied through remote services that are physically performed outside New Zealand that a person who is resident in New Zealand pays an amount of money to participate in. "Remote services" are defined in s 12S of the GD using the same definition as how remote services are defined in the GST Act (¶32-680).

The offshore gambling duty also applies to offshore websites owned by New Zealand casinos because this gambling is conducted by entities that are located outside New Zealand. Note the offshore gambling duty does not apply to existing online products offered by the Lotteries Commission (operating as Lotto NZ) and TAB NZ.

*Refs: s 12S*

## Calculating offshore gambling profits

The offshore gambling duty is calculated as 12% of the offshore gambling profits. Offshore gambling profits are calculated using the following formula (s 12T of the GD): amounts received from residents # prizes paid to residents # offshore betting amounts Note if the offshore operator has a negative amount of offshore gambling profits for a return period it will carry forward this negative amount to offset future offshore gambling profits in its next return periods.

See Tax Information Bulletin Vol 36, No 4, May 2024 at 72 for detailed calculation examples.

## Offshore gambling duty return periods and due dates

The offshore gambling duty returns are to be filed on a quarterly basis with the first period commencing on 1 July 2024 and ending on 30 September 2024. Section 12V of the GD requires that returns for offshore gambling duty must be filed on or before:

## 28 October for the return period and ending on 30 September

## 28 January for the return period ending 31 December

## 7 May for the return period ending 31 March, and

## 28 July for the return period ending 30 June.

The offshore gambling duty must be paid to IR no later than the relevant return due date for the corresponding return periods listed above (s 12W of the GD).

## Assessments, challenges, disputes process, penalties and

interest The rules for the Commissioner making an assessment of offshore gambling duty and for recovering any amount of unpaid gaming duty as a debt to the Crown, are identical to the existing rules that apply for casino duty.

The disputes process, penalties and interest rules that are prescribed by the TAA will apply to the offshore gambling duty.
