# Methods of calculating provisional tax payable ¶22-070

There are 4 methods to calculate a taxpayer's provisional tax liability:

## the standard (uplift) method (ss RC 5(2), (3), RC 6 and ¶22-075)

## an estimate of RIT on or before the due date of an instalment (ss RC 5(5), RC 7 and ¶22-082)

## the AIM (ss RC 5(5B), (5C), RC 7B and ¶22-090)

## the use of the taxpayer's GST ratio (ss RC 5(6), RC 8 and ¶22-105).

The standard method is the default method unless the taxpayer chooses an option. Additionally, the Commissioner may also determine the amount of provisional tax that a taxpayer should pay under the circumstances set out in accordance with a determination issued by the Commissioner under s 119 of the TAA (s RC 5(7) and ¶22-110).
