# Fringe benefits — Commissioner determining cost of vehicle ¶21-265

In certain circumstances, the Commissioner is authorised to determine the vehicle's cost price. The Commissioner may do this in circumstances where the vehicle was acquired by the employer at no cost, the vehicle was acquired at less than its current market value because of an arrangement between the employer and an associated person to defeat the intent and application of the fringe benefit tax provisions, or the cost price cannot be established by the employer to the satisfaction of the Commissioner. In any of these situations, the Commissioner may determine that the cost price of the vehicle is the vehicle's current market value on the date of acquisition by the employer. The Commissioner's power is subject to the rules relating to sale and buy back agreements (¶21-275).
