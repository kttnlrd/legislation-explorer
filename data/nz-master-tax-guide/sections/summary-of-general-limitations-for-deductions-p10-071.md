# Summary of general limitations for deductions ¶10-071

If applicable, or to the extent applicable, any one of the general limitations operates to prevent deduction of an expenditure or loss, including a depreciation loss, under the general permission. An exception applies if there is a supplement to the general permission that operates to restore deductibility. The interaction between the general permission, the general limitations and supplements is outlined at ¶10-016.

## General limitations

The general limitations comprise the following:

## capital limitation: a deduction is denied for an expenditure or loss to the extent to which it is of a

capital nature (¶10-075)

## private limitation: a deduction is denied for an expenditure or loss to the extent to which it is of a

private or domestic nature (¶10-078)

## exempt income limitation: a deduction is denied for an expenditure or loss to the extent to which it is

incurred in deriving exempt income (¶10-080)

## employment limitation: a deduction is denied for an expenditure or loss to the extent to which it is

incurred in deriving income from employment (¶10-082)

## withholding tax limitation: a deduction for an expenditure or loss is denied to the extent to which it is

incurred in deriving non-resident passive income subject to final withholding (¶10-085), and

## non-residents' foreign-sourced income limitation: a deduction is denied for an expenditure or loss to

the extent to which it is incurred in deriving non-residents' foreign-sourced income (¶10-085).
