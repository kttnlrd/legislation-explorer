# Eligibility to be M#ori authority ¶24-295

Only persons listed in s HF 2 of the IT07 may elect to become a M#ori authority. Eligibility is limited to entities that manage M#ori assets in community ownership and are subject to significant constraints and restrictions on their activities; eg restrictions imposed under the Te Ture Whenua M#ori Act 1993 on the ability to alienate or trade land interests. The list of eligible persons includes:

## a company or a trust that is established by an order made under Te Ture Whenua M#ori Act

## a company or a trust that owns land that is subject to Te Ture Whenua M#ori Act

## the M#ori Trustee in its capacity as an agent for an owner of land that is subject to Te Ture Whenua

M#ori Act

## a M#ori Trust Board as defined in s 2 of the M#ori Trust Boards Act 1955

## the Crown Forestry Rental Trust

## Te Ohu Kai Moana Trustee Ltd

## Aotearoa Fisheries Ltd

## a company that is:

## established by a mandated iwi organisation to be an asset-holding company, as contemplated by s

12(1)(d) of the M#ori Fisheries Act

## recognised by Te Ohu Kai Moana Trustee Ltd as a mandated iwi organisation under s 13(1) of the

M#ori Fisheries Act

## the trustees of a trust that is recognised by Te Ohu Kai Moana Trustee Ltd as a mandated iwi

organisation under s 13(1) of the M#ori Fisheries Act

## the trustees of trusts that are established by Te Ohu Kai Moana Trustee Ltd as a mandated iwi

organisation in accordance with ss 79 and 92 of the M#ori Fisheries Act, and

## a company or a trust that receives and manages assets on behalf of M#ori claimants transferred by

the Crown as part of a settlement of a claim under the Treaty of Waitangi.

Entities not listed in s HF 2 may apply to the government to be added to the list (Tax Information Bulletin ¶155-115 Vol 15, No 5, May 2003 at 35).

*Refs: s HF 2, s 2, s 12(1)(d), s 13(1), ss 79*
