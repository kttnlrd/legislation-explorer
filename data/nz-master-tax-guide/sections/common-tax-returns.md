# Common tax returns ¶1-360

The Commissioner prescribes the form in which tax returns are to be made. A number of different forms are prescribed for different taxpayers (¶2-015). The most common are:

## form IR3 for individuals

## form IR4 for companies

## form IR6 for trustees, and

## form IR7 for partnerships and look-through companies.

IR also accepts computer-produced tax returns.

From 21 February 2017, the Commissioner may allow a person to provide the information required in a prescribed form in a manner other than writing if the Commissioner is satisfied that it is appropriate in the circumstances. This change confirms that an IR staff member can telephone a taxpayer regarding incorrectly completed forms and correct errors in those forms on the basis of their telephone discussion with them.
