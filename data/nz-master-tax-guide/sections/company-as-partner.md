# Company as partner ¶23-184

When a limited liability company is a partner in a partnership, any imputation credit accruing to the partnership and allocated to the corporate partner using the formula in s LE 6 is credited by the company to its imputation credit account in the imputation year in which the dividend is paid.

*Refs: s LE 6*
