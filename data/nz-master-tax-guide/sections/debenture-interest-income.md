# Debenture interest income ¶5-274

Income from a debenture is taxed according to the type of debenture held. Interest payable under a debenture is treated as a dividend if it relates to profit-related (floating rate) debentures. Otherwise, an amount derived from a debenture is taxed in the hands of the recipient as interest under s CC 4.
