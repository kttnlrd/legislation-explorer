# FBT and late payment penalties ¶21-090

Any amount of FBT not paid by the due date attracts an initial penalty of 5%. This penalty is charged incrementally. A penalty of 1% is applied the day after the due date for unpaid tax and 4% is applied 7 days after the due date. An incremental monthly penalty of 1% applies on the amount of FBT outstanding, plus the initial late payment penalties, for each successive month until payment. Such late payment penalties are not deductible for income tax purposes (s DB 1(1)).

Taxpayers are notified the first time they make a late payment of tax. The late payment penalty will then only be imposed if the payment is not made within one month after the date of the notice. However, if the taxpayer makes other late payments within 2 years of the due date of the first late payment, the late payment penalty will be imposed as usual from the day after the due date.

The late filing penalty imposed by s 139A of the Tax Administration Act 1994 (TAA) does not apply to FBT returns.

*Refs: s DB 1(1), s 139A, Tax Administration Act 1994*
