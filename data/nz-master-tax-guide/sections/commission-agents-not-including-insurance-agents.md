# Commission agents not including insurance agents ¶3-391

In deciding on the employer/independent contractor relationship of commission agents, it is necessary to ascertain whether the commission being paid is salary or wages (subject to PAYE) or a schedular payment (subject to withholding from the gross commission). The question of whether remuneration is salary or wages or a schedular payment depends on the nature of the relationship in the contract of employment (¶3-201 and ¶3-376).
