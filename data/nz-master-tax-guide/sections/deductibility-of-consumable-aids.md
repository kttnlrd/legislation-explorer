# Deductibility of consumable aids ¶10-232

A consumable aid is an item used in a production process, for example the purchase by a farmer of hay for consumption by livestock. The cost of consumable aids can be expected to be allowed as a deduction under the general permission. The cost would have the character of a working expense (¶9-011).
