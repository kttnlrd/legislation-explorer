# Deduction for Maori authority donation ¶10-700

A M#ori authority is allowed a deduction for donations made to a M#ori association or gifts of money made to a donee organisation, ie an organisation that has charitable status. The deduction for the total amount of donations in an income year is limited to the amount of the M#ori authority's net income for the corresponding tax year, calculated before the deduction is taken into account.

A deduction is denied to the extent to which the amount is, for the M#ori authority, an asset disposed of by a deregistered charity to another charity within 12 months of deregistration.

Law: IT07 s DV 12
