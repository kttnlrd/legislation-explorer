# Income from sale of patent rights or patent applications ¶5-222

An amount derived from the sale of patent rights or the sale of a patent application with a complete specification is income.

Special rules apply if the patent rights or patent applications are transferred under a relationship agreement (s FB 8).

For the rules on deducting expenditure incurred in relation to a patent, see ¶10-750.

The income derived from the sale of patent applications and patent rights is able to be spread evenly over the income year of sale and the following 2 years. This rule is intended to alleviate cashflow difficulties for vendors who sell patent rights for non-cash consideration (s EI 4).

*Refs: s FB 8, s EI 4*
