# Redundancy payments and retiring allowances ¶3-313

Redundancy payments and retiring allowances are fully taxable to the recipient. The full amount of the payment is treated as an extra pay (¶3-221) and is subject to 10.5%, 17.5%, 30%, 33% or 39% tax at the time of payment. The withholding rate that applies is determined based on the combined total of the extra pay and the annualised value of the PAYE income payments paid to an employee in the previous 4 weeks.

Employers need not deduct the ACC earners' account levy from redundancy payments or retiring allowances.

Law: IT07 s RD 10(2), Sch 2 pt B
