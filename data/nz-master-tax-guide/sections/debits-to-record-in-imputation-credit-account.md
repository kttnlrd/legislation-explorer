# Debits to record in imputation credit account ¶17-045

Debits to the ICA result in a reduction in the tax credits available for distribution to shareholders.

## Timing of debits

For ease of reference the transactions that give rise to imputation debits in an ICA are discussed below and numbered in accordance with the numbering used in Table O2 of the IT07. The following are debits to an ICA during a tax year:
