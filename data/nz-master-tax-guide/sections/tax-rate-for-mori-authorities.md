# Tax rate for M#ori authorities ¶24-307

The income of a M#ori authority is taxed at the rate of 17.5%. This rate is considered to reflect the marginal tax rate of the majority of members receiving benefits from M#ori authorities. A "member" of a M#ori authority means a shareholder of a M#ori authority if it is a company and a beneficiary if the M#ori authority is a trust.

As with other taxpayers (with the exception of companies), M#ori authorities can make certain deduction rate elections for the deduction of RWT on resident passive income (¶15-020).

Law: IT07 s YA 1 ("M#ori authority", "member"), Sch 1 pt A cl 7
