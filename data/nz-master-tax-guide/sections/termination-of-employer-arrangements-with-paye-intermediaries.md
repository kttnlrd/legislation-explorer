# Termination of employer arrangements with PAYE intermediaries ¶3-480

An arrangement between an employer and a PAYE intermediary may be ended by either party giving notice to the other and to the Commissioner. Notice may be given to the Commissioner on form IR913.

The notice of termination must state the date on which the arrangement will end. This date must be one that occurs after the date on which the notice is given.

A person who stops being a PAYE intermediary (or, before 1 April 2020, listed intermediary) for an employer has the rights and obligations under the PAYE rules and ESCT rules of a PAYE intermediary (or listed intermediary) for funds that:

## the employer pays to the person as intermediary, and

## the person holds at the time they stop being an intermediary.

As a result of the repeal of the payroll subsidy from 1 April 2020, listed PAYE intermediaries were also repealed from 1 April 2020.

Law: IT07 s RP 2(3); TAA s 124O
