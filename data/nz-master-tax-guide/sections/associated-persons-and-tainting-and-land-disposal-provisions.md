# Associated persons and "tainting" and land disposal provisions ¶7-430

The associated persons concept applies in connection with those who are in the business of dealing in land, developing land or erecting buildings. Where the person who disposes of the land, or someone associated with that person, carried on one of those kinds of business at the time of acquisition of the land (or at the time improvements were commenced for builders), the disposal gives rise to income under the provisions relating to:

## land acquired for the purposes of dealing, developing or building businesses (¶7-055), and

## the other land of a dealer (¶7-065), developer (¶7-085) or builder (¶7-095) where the land is sold

within 10 years of acquisition (or within 10 years of completing improvements for builders).

A definition of "associated persons" for the purposes of the land transaction provisions applies to land acquired, or land on which improvements were commenced, on or after 6 October 2009.

For the tests of association which apply to land, see ¶7-433.
