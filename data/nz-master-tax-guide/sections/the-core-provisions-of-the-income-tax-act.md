# The core provisions of the Income Tax Act ¶1-050

The core provisions set out the key principles on which the rest of the income tax legislation is based. The provisions, found in Pt B of the IT07, are intended to:

## impose tax liability

## specify the steps that a taxpayer must follow to arrive at the taxpayer's tax liability

## provide a basis for applying the other Parts of the Income Tax Act, and

## set up the scheme of the Income Tax Act and the main links between its Parts.

## Terminology

The Act follows a global or gross approach to the calculation of taxable income. Some of the key terms used in the core provisions include income, exempt income, excluded income, assessable income, deduction and allocation (or attribution). These components make up the taxpayer's annual gross income and annual total deduction for the tax year.
