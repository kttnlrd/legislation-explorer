# Trustees tax rate ¶50-060

Tax rates in relation to trusts depend on the tax status of the person deriving the income and the nature of the trust from which the distribution is made. Tax rates applicable to trustee income, beneficiary income and non-complying trusts are set out below.

## Income category                                                  Tax rate

Trustee income and beneficiary income 39% (for the 2024–25 and later tax years)* subject to minor beneficiary or corporate beneficiary rules Trustee income and minor beneficiary income 33% (for the 2011–12 to 2023–24 tax years) Beneficiary income (excluding beneficiary Taxpayer's marginal rate income subject to minor beneficiary or (39% maximum (33% before 1 April 2021)) corporate beneficiary rules) Taxable distributions from a non-complying 45% trust Income of M#ori authorities 17.5%

* some exceptions apply (de minis trusts (s HC 40), disabled beneficiary trusts (s HC 39), deceased estate referred to in s HC 8B and lines trusts (s YA 1) continue to be taxed at 33%)
