# Summary of Commissioner- and taxpayer-initiated disputes processes ¶4-125

The disputes resolution process can be initiated by either the Commissioner (¶4-130) or the taxpayer (¶4-135) issuing a NOPA. Such notice requires the relevant party to inform the respondent of any proposed adjustments to a person's tax position and to state the reasons for those adjustments.

IR has issued a standard practice statement on the disputes resolution process that applies from 24 February 2023, SPS 23/01, "Disputes process", replacing SPS 16/05, "Disputes resolution process commenced by the Commissioner of Inland Revenue" and SPS 16/06, "Disputes resolution process commenced by a taxpayer".

## Struck-off companies

A company that is no longer registered on the Register of Companies cannot be a party to any disputes resolution procedure unless and until that company is reinstated to the register. This is because the company no longer exists once it is struck off the register.

A company that is restored to the register may be deemed to have continued in existence as if it had not been removed from the register. However, it does not follow that the company continues to have some form of status after its removal. See Case W13 (2003) 21 NZTC 11,134 and Case X3 (2005) 22 NZTC 12,063.
