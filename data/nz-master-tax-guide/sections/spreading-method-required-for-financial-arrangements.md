# Spreading method required for financial arrangements ¶6-102

A party to a financial arrangement must use a spreading method to calculate income and expenditure under the financial arrangement for each income year over the term of the financial arrangement. This is unless the person is not required to apply a spreading method.

A spreading method is not required to be applied:

## by a cash basis person, unless an election to the contrary is made

## by a trustee of a trust fund for personal injury compensation, if the trustee meets the criteria for

classification as a cash basis person

## in the income year in which a base price adjustment calculation is required upon disposal or maturity

of the financial arrangement.

Law: IT07 ss EW 12, EW 13
