# Trading stock — containers, packages, labels ¶9-012

In contrast to consumable aids, containers, packages, labels and similar materials are incorporated in the final product and are regarded as stock in trade (Case 115 (1951) 1 TBRD (NS) 485).

This would appear to be confirmed by s EB 2(2)(b). This provision refers to material held by the person for use in producing trading stock.
