# FBT — Resolution to restrict vehicle use ¶90-305

## Resolution of directors of The Blue Dress Company

## [Date]

The directors of The Blue Dress Company hereby resolve as follows: That the Mazda 6 motor vehicle, registration number CJK889 owned by The Blue Dress Company is to be used for company business only and is strictly not available for the private use of any employee of this company.
