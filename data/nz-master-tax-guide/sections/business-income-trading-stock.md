# Business income — trading stock ¶1-180

In the case of business income, there are specific provisions dealing with trading stock.

If any person owns or carries on business, the value of the person's trading stock at the beginning and the end of every income year is taken into account in calculating the person's net income or net loss for that year (s EB 3).

The value of a person's trading stock at the end of an income year is income for the income year and the value of opening stock is allowed as a deduction for that year (s EA 1(2), (3) and ¶9-010). This is in accordance with accounting practice, but other provisions dealing with trading stock have effects that do not necessarily follow from the rules of accounting.

*Refs: s EB 3, s EA 1(2)*

Example: If TP disposes of trading stock during the year for less than its market price or true value, it is not the actual price received by the taxpayer that is brought in as income in that year but the market price or true value. See s GC 1(1) and ¶9-087.

*Refs: s GC 1(1)*
