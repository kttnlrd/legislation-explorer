# Interest on borrowings to acquire shares in amalgamating company that ceases to exist ¶20-117

Section DB 8 permits interest to be deducted if it is payable by one company included in a group of companies in respect of money borrowed to acquire shares in another company included in that group.

Section DB 8(3) confirms that a deduction for interest on money borrowed to acquire shares in an amalgamating company is preserved notwithstanding the company ceasing to exist on a resident's restricted amalgamation. A deduction continues to be available provided the acquirer and the target were members of the same group of companies immediately before the resident's restricted amalgamation.

*Refs: Section DB 8, Section DB 8(3)*
