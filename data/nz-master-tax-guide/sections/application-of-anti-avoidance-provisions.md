# Application of anti-avoidance provisions ¶23-175

It is necessary to consider which provisions of the IT07 affect an assignment of a share in a partnership, including ss BG 1 and GA 1 — the general anti-avoidance provisions. Features of an arrangement that will indicate to IR that an assignment amounts to tax avoidance include the following:

## the transfer of income or property is for a short term

## the arrangement has little real effect on business income or earning activity

## the transaction cannot be described as an ordinary family or commercial transaction

## a high degree of control over the earning of income or the disposition of the property is a part of the

arrangement, and

## there is a close family or business relationship between parties to the transaction.

Interpretive guidelines have been developed by the courts to determine when alienation of income and short- term transfer of income-earning property constitute tax avoidance. See Elmiger v C of IR [1966] NZLR 683, Mangin v C of IR 70 ATC 6001; [1971] NZLR 591 and Case J61 (1987) 9 NZTC 1,366.
