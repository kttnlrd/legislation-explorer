# Forestry or bush workers ¶3-223

Payments for forestry or bush work are schedular payments. The standard rate of tax to be deducted from such payments is 15¢ in the dollar (if the contractor has not elected another rate and is not subject to a special or prescribed rate (¶3-044)). Such work, under Sch 4 pt C, includes bush felling, road and tramway work, removal of timber and undergrowth cutting, burning or clearing.
