# Due date for payment of provisional and terminal tax ¶22-012

The due date for a provisional tax payment depends on the method adopted by the taxpayer. In addition, the number of provisional tax instalments depends on which provisional tax method is used and, if the taxpayer is registered for GST, the GST filing frequency (¶22-115).

## Provisional tax from 2018–19 income year

The due dates for the payment of provisional and terminal tax instalments are set out in the table below (from Sch 3), which includes instalment dates for taxpayers who adopt the AIM (¶22-090). Provisional tax is due on the 28th day of the month specified, except for instalments due on 28 December and 28 April, which are due on 15 January and 7 May, respectively, to take into account the Christmas and Easter holiday periods.

## Schedule 3 table

With the exception of the final instalment (column F or F1) or, in the case of an AIM taxpayer with a one- month taxable period, the final 2 instalments (columns F and F1), all instalments of provisional tax are due prior to the entity's balance date (although note that the exception for 2 instalments also applies to an AIM taxpayer with an April balance date whose penultimate instalment is due on 7 May). The final instalment, or 2 instalments, as relevant, of provisional tax are due in the months after balance date.

Terminal tax is due in the 11th month following balance date for balance dates between October and March.

For balance dates between April and September, terminal tax is due in the month of February following the balance date — refer to column G in the table below.

If taxpayers are linked to a tax agent who have an extension of time arrangement, the due date for terminal tax is extended by 2 months (refer to column H).
