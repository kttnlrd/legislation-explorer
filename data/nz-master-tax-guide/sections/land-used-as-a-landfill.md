# Land used as a landfill ¶7-165

An owner of land that has previously been used as a landfill may elect that the land be held on revenue account property. Where the following conditions are satisfied the election will be valid:

## the person uses the land as a landfill prior to disposal

## at the time of disposal, the land is not being used as a landfill

## the purchaser is not an associated person

## the person has notified the Commissioner within 12 months of acquisition that the land will be subject

to the landfill provisions, and

## all land used by the person or an associated person is subject to similar election notice requirements.

The effect of a valid election is that the landfill property is held on revenue account. Therefore, any gain or loss on disposal will be income or a deductible loss. Section CB 8 is an exception to the rule that no deduction is allowed for a loss in value of land. It recognises that no amount of restoration expenditure by a landfill owner can prevent a loss in land value (Tax Information Bulletin ¶177-107 Vol 17, No 7, September 2005 at p 27). The prospect of being able to deduct a loss on disposal is likely to be influential in determining whether any election should be made.
