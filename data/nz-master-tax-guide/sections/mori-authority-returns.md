# M#ori authority returns ¶2-037

A M#ori authority is required to furnish an income tax return, and the investment income information for the payment of a distribution. A M#ori authority credit account return must also be filed unless the M#ori authority is a member of a consolidated imputation group and the imputation credit account of the member has a nil balance at all times during the relevant tax year (¶24-335).

Law: TAA ss 25I, 57, 69B, 70B, Sch 6
