# Student loan – what can you get? ¶8-015

The student loan scheme provides for the payment of:

## compulsory course fees — paid on enrolment (excluding late enrolment fees or administration

charges for instalment payments)

## course-related costs — a maximum of $1,000 a year for full-time full-year courses for course-related

costs such as equipment, textbooks, etc, and

## living costs after tax — a maximum of $323.43 per week of study (less any student allowances).

A new loan is required for each new course and loan accounts run for 52 weeks. Each new loan account has an establishment fee of $60 added to the loan plus an administration fee of $40 per year (where no establishment fee has been paid in the same year in respect of that loan).
