# Deductibility of ACC rehabilitation payments ¶10-112

When an ACC claimant receives a personal service rehabilitation payment (including backdated payments) from the accident compensation corporation and the amount is assessable income, the person is allowed a deduction for the amount paid by them to a rehabilitation service provider to the extent of the personal service rehabilitation payment received. This provision supplements the general permission and overrides the capital limitation and the private limitation. The other general limitations still apply.

See further at ¶3-180 and ¶5-303.
