# Test case procedures ¶4-250

When the Commissioner considers that a challenge may be determinative of all or a substantial number of issues involved in other challenges, that challenge may be designated as a test case. Test cases are to be heard in the High Court.

When a test case has been designated, the Commissioner may stay proceedings on other challenges by notifying the taxpayers concerned. The Commissioner may only stay proceedings where:

## the test case is considered to be determinative of all or a substantial number of the issues in the

challenge proposed to be stayed, and

## the challenge proposed to be stayed has not yet been determined by the hearing authority.

The taxpayer may contest the stay at any time by issuing a notice requiring the challenge to be heard. The Commissioner may, within 14 days of receiving the taxpayer's notice, apply to the High Court for an order that the challenge be stayed. In Bage Investments Ltd v C of IR (1999) 19 NZTC 15,531 the High Court set out principles relevant when considering an application for a stay. Justice Chambers noted that the list was neither exhaustive nor definitive. Also see C of IR v Erris Promotions; Wilson and Black Associates Ltd v C of IR; C of IR v West Coast Developments Ltd (2002) 20 NZTC 17,977 (CA), C of IR v A Taxpayer (2003) 21 NZTC 18,001 and B v C of IR; C of IR v Multiple Taxpayers (2004) 21 NZTC 18,404.

## Suspension of the disputes resolution process

A disputant and the Commissioner may agree in writing to suspend the proceedings in a dispute if there is significant similarity between the facts and questions of law in the dispute and facts and questions of law in a test case (the test case being a separate challenge). A suspension of the dispute resolution process begins on the date of the agreement and ends on the earliest of the following:

## the date of the court's decision in the test case

## the date on which the test case is otherwise resolved, or

## the date on which the dispute is otherwise resolved.

The Commissioner can make an assessment or perform an action in relation to a suspended dispute that is consistent with the resolution of the test case.

## Time limit for the Commissioner to amend assessment

If a dispute resolution process is suspended pending the decision in a test case, the Commissioner is required to make an amended assessment or perform an action in relation to the suspended dispute by the later of (Tax Information Bulletin ¶185-120 Vol 18, No 5, June 2006 at 137):

## the day that is 60 days after the last day of the suspension, or

## the last day of the period that:

## begins on the day following the day by which the Commissioner, in the absence of the suspension,

would be required under the Inland Revenue Acts (listed in Sch 1 to the TAA) to make the amended assessment or perform the action, and

## contains the same number of days as the suspension period.

Law: TAA ss 89O, 137, 138Q, 138R
