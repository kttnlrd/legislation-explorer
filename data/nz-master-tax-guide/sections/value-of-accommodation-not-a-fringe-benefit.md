# Value of accommodation not a fringe benefit ¶21-618

The value of any accommodation an employer provides to an employee in connection with their employment or services is not a fringe benefit. "Accommodation" is defined as including the use of a house or living premises, or the use of part of a house or living premises, whether permanent or temporary.

Law: IT07 ss CE 1(2), CX 28, YA 1 ("accommodation")
