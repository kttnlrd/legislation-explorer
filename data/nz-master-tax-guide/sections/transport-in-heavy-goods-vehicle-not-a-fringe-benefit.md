# Transport in heavy goods vehicle not a fringe benefit ¶21-620

Transport of an employee in a heavy goods vehicle is not a fringe benefit (for example, an employee of a removal company picked up from home on the way to the first removal task of the day). This rule applies to transport provided to an employee in a vehicle that is not a motor vehicle (¶21-170) and is not designed principally for the carriage of passengers.

Law: IT07 ss CX 19B, YA 1 ("motor vehicle")
