# Trusts established under M#ori Land Act ¶24-283

Five types of land-holding trusts are established under the Te Ture Whenua M#ori Act 1993. These trusts are generally designed to limit the further fragmentation of M#ori land titles by limiting rights of succession in certain cases.

## Charitable status

Under s 245 of the Te Ture Whenua M#ori Act, the trustees of any trust established under the Act may apply to the court for an order that they hold any part of the trust's income on trust for such charitable purposes as are specified in the court order. However, s 245 does not deem any income derived by an approved trust to be held for charitable purposes for the purposes of the revenue Acts, regardless of whether the trust's purposes are, in fact, charitable at general law. A trust must meet the following requirements to gain charitable status for IR's purposes:

## it must have a charitable purpose

## it must not be carried on for the private pecuniary profit of any individual

## if it is wound up, it must have provision in its rules for its assets to be given to another charity with

terms at least as strict as the donor charity

## it must have no power to amend its rules to detract from the above charitable requirements, and

## it must be registered with the Charities Registration Board.

## Charitable income of M#ori Trust Boards exempted

Public binding ruling BR Pub 08/02 indicates that income derived by a M#ori Trust Board from assets declared to be held on a charitable trust can be tax-exempt even when the twin legal requirements of public benefit and charitable purpose are not satisfied. The ruling applies only when all the purposes of the trust include purposes specified in s 24 or 24A of the M#ori Trust Boards Act 1955, all other elements necessary for a charitable trust are present, and the declaration of trust (under s 24B of that Act) has been submitted to and approved by the Commissioner.

No declaration of trust shall have any force or effect unless it has been approved by the Commissioner as required under s 24B(3) of the M#ori Trust Boards Act. Section 13(2)(b) of the Charities Act 2005 will treat such trusts as complying with the s 13(1)(a) requirement of that Act, which requires that an entity qualifies for registration as a charitable trust if the trustees derive income in trust for charitable purposes. Provided the trust can satisfy other registration criteria, it will be registered with the Charities Registration Board.

The Commissioner's interpretation effectively recognises Parliament's intention to give trusts established by M#ori Trust Boards charitable status in particular circumstances.

The ruling applies to income derived by a M#ori Trust Board for an indefinite period beginning on the first day of the 2008–09 income year.

Law: CHA s 13(2)(b); ML s 245
