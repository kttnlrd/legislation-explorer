# Voluntary payments of provisional tax ¶22-130

A person may at any time make voluntary payments of an amount of provisional tax that:

## relates to their income tax liability for a tax year in which they are not liable for provisional tax, or

## is more than the provisional tax payable by them for the tax year, or

## is more than the income tax payable by them for the tax year.

A taxpayer may make a voluntary payment to reduce exposure to use of money interest charges when there is a known tax shortfall. In addition, individuals whose residual income tax is under $60,000 may make a voluntary payment to reduce their terminal tax liability without filing a formal estimation (with IR) that would bring them within the use of money interest provisions (¶22-352).
