# Tax year ¶1-080

Income tax is imposed on the taxable income of a taxpayer for each tax year. A tax year runs from 1 April to the following 31 March. The tax years are numbered according to the years in which that 31 March falls.

For example, the 2025–26 tax year is the tax year ending on 31 March 2026. Although the tax year is from 1 April to 31 March, some taxpayers who have an accounting balance date other than 31 March may treat the year ending on their balance date as their tax year for the purpose of determining their business income.

This period is referred to as an income year (¶2-055).

Law: IT07 s YA 1 ("income year", "tax year")
