# Exclusion from FBT — assessable income ¶21-604

Any benefit provided in connection with an employee's employment which is otherwise included in the assessable income of the employee concerned is not a benefit for FBT purposes. For example, an accommodation allowance included as employment income (¶5-051 and ¶5-290) is excluded as a fringe benefit.
