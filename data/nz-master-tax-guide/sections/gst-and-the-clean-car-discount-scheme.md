# GST and the clean car discount scheme ¶32-620

A payment under the clean car discount scheme for a vehicle used in a taxable activity is treated as consideration for a deemed supply under the rules relating to government grants (¶32-023), unless the registered person is a public authority. Therefore, the GST input tax credit must be claimed on the purchase price of the vehicle and the output tax on the rebate received must be returned for GST purposes.

The clean car discount scheme applied from 1 July 2021 until 31 December 2023.

Law: GST s 5(6D) and 5(6E)
