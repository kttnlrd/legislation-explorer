# Recovery of tax from agent ¶2-175

The Commissioner has full power to recover the overdue tax of a principal from the actual or constructive agent of that principal. However, any person who pays tax on behalf of another is entitled to recover the amount of the tax from that other person. They may recover that money as a debt or by retaining that sum from any moneys due to that other person.
