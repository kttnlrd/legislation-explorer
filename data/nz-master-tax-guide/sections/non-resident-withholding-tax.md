# Non-resident withholding tax ¶50-070

NRWT rates under domestic law for the different classes of non-resident passive income are the following:

Dividends To the extent that a fully imputed dividend is paid to a shareholder holding:

- a direct voting interest of 10% or more in the New Zealand company

- a direct voting interest of less than 10% in the New Zealand company where a

  DTA would otherwise reduce the NRWT rate to less than 15%. 0%

To the extent that the dividend is:

- fully imputed, or

- a supplementary dividend, or

- an investment society dividend. 15%

  In all other cases 30%

Interest Where 2% approved issuer levy used 0% In all other cases 15% Royalties 15%

A DTA may reduce these rates or, in some cases, create an exemption from NRWT entirely. A usual requirement for DTA modification is that the non-resident passive income is not associated with a permanent establishment in New Zealand.

The following exceptions should be noted in respect of the above rates of tax:

## The double tax agreements (DTAs) to which New Zealand is a party generally provide that the

maximum withholding tax rate New Zealand may impose on gross dividend income is limited to 15%.

## Most of the DTAs to which New Zealand is a party provide a maximum withholding tax rate of 10% on

interest and royalties.
