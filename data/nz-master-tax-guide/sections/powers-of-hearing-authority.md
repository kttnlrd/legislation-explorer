# Powers of hearing authority ¶4-233

When hearing a challenge, the TRA or the High Court may (s 138P(1) of the TAA):

## confirm, cancel or vary an assessment

## reduce the amount of an assessment

## increase the amount of the assessment to the extent to which the Commissioner was able to make

the increased assessment at the time the challenged assessment was made

## make an assessment that the Commissioner was able to make at the time the Commissioner made

the challenged assessment, or

## direct the Commissioner to make such an assessment.

If it is proven, on the balance of probabilities, that the amount of an assessment is excessive by a specific amount, the Authority or High Court must reduce the assessment by that amount (s 138P(1B)).

The assessments made or changes to the assessments made in accordance with the Authority or High Court decision are not subject to the 4-year time bar (s 138P(5)).

If the challenge relates to a disputable decision that is not an assessment, the Authority or High Court must not make or alter the disputable decision and may direct the Commissioner to alter the decision to conform and give effect to the Authority's or High Court's decision (s 138P(2)).

Unless the Authority or High Court's decision is under appeal, the Commissioner must make or amend an assessment or other disputable decision as determined by the Authority or High Court (s 138P(3) and (4)).

The Authority exercised its right under s 138P in Case X9 (2005) 22 NZTC 12,123 to make an assessment that the Commissioner was entitled to make but did not (Case X13 (2005) 22 NZTC 12,193).
