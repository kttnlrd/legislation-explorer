# Assessment of RWT by the Commissioner ¶15-250

The Commissioner has the power to make an assessment of RWT. An assessment may be amended, although the right to do this is generally limited to within 4 years of the income year in which the tax return is provided. An assessment is deemed to be correct except upon challenge proceedings.

Law: TAA ss 99, 111
