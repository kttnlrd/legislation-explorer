# Fresh determination ¶6-630

Once a determination has been made, the Commissioner may vary or rescind it, in whole or in part, by issuing a fresh determination. The later determination must also be published within 30 days after it has been made. Clearly, a fresh determination could have a disruptive effect on a person who has treated a financial arrangement in accordance with previous arrangements. There is a period of grace for any financial arrangement acquired or issued before the fresh determination was notified or published. This class of arrangement is not subject to the later determination until 4 years after the determination is published or the person is notified of it. A person has the same rights of challenge to a fresh determination as arise on the making of the original determination.
