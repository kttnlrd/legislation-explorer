# Suppression of sales ¶32-290

Revenue Alert RA 22/01, "Consequences of acquiring, possessing or using electronic sales suppression tools" was issued as IR has become aware of electronic sales suppression tools (ESS tools) being used in New Zealand which systematically alter point-of-sale data. Use of ESS tools allow a GST-registered person to understate or completely conceal revenue and enables that person to evade both income tax and GST on the suppressed sales. IR issued the alert to educate taxpayers as to the consequences of using ESS tools.

The ESS tools can either be software programs, devices or other tools which have functionality that enables any of the following:

## permanently delete and re-sequence transactions

## change transactions to reduce the amount of a sale

## misrepresent records (eg recategorising a product to avoid GST)

## produce false records.

In response to the use of ESS tools and the threat to the integrity of the tax system, a civil penalty and 2 new criminal offences provisions have been introduced in relation to involvement with ESS tools:

## Section 141EE establishes the ESS penalty of $5,000 for the acquisition or possession of a

suppression tool.

## Section 143BB establishes an offence of manufacturing or supplying a suppression tool. A person

convicted of such an offence is liable to a fine of up to $250,000.

## Section 143BC establishes an offence of acquiring or possessing a suppression tool. A person

convicted of such an offence is liable to a fine of up to $50,000.

The Commissioner views the acquiring, possessing or using of ESS tools as serious, therefore the Commissioner will require payment of the evaded tax, plus penalties and interest. If payment is not made, the Commissioner may apply for bankruptcy or liquidation of the registered person.

*Refs: Section 141E, Section 143B*
