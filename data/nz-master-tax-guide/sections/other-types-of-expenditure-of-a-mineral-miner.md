# Other types of expenditure of a mineral miner ¶28-100

Other types of expenditure not covered by the rules are covered by the appropriate general rules set out in the Act. This includes the application of the capital/revenue distinction in determining whether expenditure is immediately deductible, deductible over time or not deductible.
