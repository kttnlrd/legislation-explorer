# Payment of tax ¶1-430

Once the income tax on a taxpayer's taxable income for the tax year has been determined and any credits to which that taxpayer is entitled to for the year have been deducted from the tax figure, the actual tax liability of the taxpayer for the year has been arrived at. This is a person's terminal tax liability for the tax year and must be paid to the Commissioner by prescribed due dates (¶22-235). All taxpayers pay tax in advance under either the PAYE system, the RWT system or the provisional tax system, with a balancing adjustment made after the end of the year when actual tax on the year's income is worked out.

Law: IT07 ss BC 8(3), RA 3, YA 1 ("terminal tax")
