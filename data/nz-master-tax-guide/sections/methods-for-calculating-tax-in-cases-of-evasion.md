# Methods for calculating tax in cases of evasion ¶1-572

When a case of evasion is detected, there are a number of methods that can be employed to calculate the amount of tax that should have been paid:

## the assets accretion method (¶1-574)

## the add-back method: adding back into the taxpayer's assessable income items of income that IR

discovers the taxpayer should have returned (¶1-582)

## the comparative method: comparing the returns of taxpayers whose affairs are very similar (¶1-584),

and

## the cash available method (¶1-586).
