# Offences of employees and officers ¶14-295

Under s 147 of the TAA, an employee, agent or officer of a body corporate commits an offence if:

## the body corporate commits an offence (the principal offence), and

## the principal offence:

## was caused by an act or omission of the employee, agent or officer, or through knowledge

attributable to them, or

## is evasion committed by the employee, agent or officer.

An employee or officer of a body corporate includes anyone who is responsible for undertaking an action on the body corporate's behalf.

An employee, agent or officer who does or carries out an act or omission, or has the knowledge or intent (as described above), is liable on conviction for the same maximum fine and/or term of imprisonment that applies for committing the principal offence.
