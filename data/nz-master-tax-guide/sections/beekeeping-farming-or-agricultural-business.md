# Beekeeping farming or agricultural business ¶27-505

IR accepts that beekeeping comes within the definition of an activity carried on for farming or agricultural purposes. Beekeepers, therefore, are given the same concessions that other farmers have, such as allowable deductions under s DO 1 (¶27-175) and access to income equalisation schemes.
