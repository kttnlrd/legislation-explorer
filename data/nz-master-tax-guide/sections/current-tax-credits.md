# Current tax credits ¶11-015

A number of provisions under the Income Tax Act 2007 (IT07) provide for tax credits. They relate to the following:

## ss EH 30–EH 33: main income equalisation scheme (¶27-075)

## s EH 77: thinning operations income equalisation scheme (¶27-085)

## s HC 24(2): entitlement of trustees (¶25-100)

## s LB 1: PAYE income payments (¶1-400)

## s LB 2: provisional tax payments (¶22-240)

## s LB 3: resident withholding tax (¶15-190)

## s LB 4: abating Working for Families tax credits, minimum family tax credit and Best Start tax credit

under subparts MD, ME and MG (¶12-170)

## s LB 4B: R&D tax losses (¶11-100)

## s LB 5: non-resident withholding tax (¶26-440)

## s LB 6: retirement scheme contribution tax (¶29-320)

## s LB 6B: residential land withholding tax (¶7-020)

## ss LB 7 and LB 8: personal service rehabilitation payments (¶5-303)

## s LC 13: independent earner (¶11-080)

## subpart LD: charitable or other public benefit gifts (¶11-055) and payroll donations (¶11-063)

## subpart LE: imputation credits (¶17-010)

## subpart LJ: foreign income tax (¶26-250)

## subpart LK: attributed controlled foreign company income (¶26-085)

## subpart LO: M#ori authority credits (¶24-314)

## subpart LP: supplementary dividends (¶26-500)

## subpart LS: multi-rate PIEs and investors (¶11-092, ¶29-140 and ¶29-145)

## subpart LT: petroleum mining (¶28-125)

## subpart LU: mineral miners (¶28-105)

## subpart LY: research and development tax credit (¶11-150)

## s LZ 6: home vendor mortgage (if approved before 5 August 1982)

## subpart MK: KiwiSaver schemes and complying superannuation funds (¶11-094 and ¶29-250)

## subpart MX: R&D tax losses (¶11-100)

## subpart MH: FamilyBoost tax credits (¶11-200).
