# Permitted disclosure: Accident Compensation Corporation ¶1-627

## Earnings-related compensation

Information may be exchanged between IR and the Accident Compensation Corporation (ACC) for compensation or levy purposes (Sch 7 pt C cl 41 of the TAA).

The range of information that may be exchanged between the government agencies encompasses information to verify:

## the entitlement or eligibility of a person to earnings-related compensation

## the amount of any earnings-related compensation to which a person is entitled, or

## whether any premium or levy is payable by a person or the amount of the premium or levy.

If information matching reveals that a person in receipt of earnings-related compensation was at the same time receiving income from employment (including self-employment) the Commissioner may supply ACC with the following information:

## the date or dates on which that employment commenced

## where applicable, the date or dates on which that employment ceased

## the name and business address of each employer so employing that person.

If the person received other income while receiving earnings-related compensation, the Commissioner may also disclose to the ACC the amount of that other income for the relevant period, provided the other income affected entitlement to the compensation.

## ACC levies

Where either an information matching agreement has been entered into under Pt 10 of the Privacy Act 1993 and continued under the Privacy Act 2020, or an approved information sharing agreement has been entered into under subpart 1 of Pt 7 of the Privacy Act 2020, information may be exchanged between IR and the ACC to facilitate the assessment of levies by the ACC (Sch 7 pt C cl 42 of the TAA).

The range of information the Commissioner may supply to the ACC encompasses:

## the names, addresses and ACC file numbers of employers, self-employed persons, private domestic

workers and shareholder–employees

## the time of the commencement or cessation of a person's status as an employer or a private

domestic worker

## the time at which a self-employed person commenced or ceased business

## the total amount paid in any year by an employer or a private domestic worker as employee earnings

## the earnings of a self-employed person

## the earnings of a shareholder–employee

## whether an employer, self-employed person, private domestic worker or shareholder–employee has a

tax agent and, if so, the tax agent's name and contact details, and

## in the case of an employer, self-employed person, private domestic worker or shareholder–employee

who is an individual, whether the individual is deceased and, if so, the individual's date of death and the name and contact details of the administrator or executor of the individual's estate.
