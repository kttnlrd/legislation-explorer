# Amendments to child support scheme ¶12-012

The Child Support (Pass On) Acts Amendment Act 2023 was enacted on 12 June 2023. In summary, the main changes from 1 July 2023 are:

## IR will pass child support payments on to receiving carers on a solo parent rate of the benefit

## receiving carers on a solo parent rate of benefit will no longer be required to have child support

through IR

## Ministry of Social Development (MSD) will apply new rules that treat child support as income for

benefit purposes and IR will share information about child support payments with MSD for this purpose

## parents paying formula assessed child support will be able to include their monthly child support

liability as a cost when they apply for Temporary Additional Support from MSD.
