# Resident withholding tax on taxable M#ori authority distributions ¶15-065

RWT is required to be deducted from certain distributions made by M#ori authorities known as "taxable M#ori authority distributions". RWT on taxable M#ori authority distributions is implemented in such a way as to integrate with the M#ori authority credit attribution system that was introduced to modify the way in which M#ori authorities are taxed (¶24-288–¶24-335). Briefly, the credit attribution system operates in a similar way to the company imputation system and allows M#ori authority credits to be attached to taxable M#ori authority distributions. RWT applies to taxable M#ori authority distributions to the extent that M#ori authority credits are not attached to the distribution.

The definition of "resident passive income" includes taxable M#ori authority distributions (other than retirement scheme contributions). A "taxable M#ori authority distribution" is an amount distributed by a M#ori authority from income derived in the 2004–05 or later tax year that is not exempt from tax (¶24-311).

## Calculation of tax deduction

The formulae set out in s RE 16 are used for calculating the amount of RWT which applies to cash and non- cash taxable M#ori authority distributions.

## Taxable M#ori authority distributions in cash form

Generally, RWT on taxable M#ori authority distributions in the form of cash or a credit of an amount of money is calculated on the amount of the distribution paid before the deduction of RWT plus M#ori authority credits attached to the distribution. Credit is then given for any M#ori authority credits attached to the distribution.

The formula for calculating RWT to be deducted from taxable M#ori authority distributions in the form of cash or a credit of an amount of money is (s RE 16(2)): (tax rate × (distribution amount + credit attached))  credit attached where: tax rate is the appropriate rate of RWT expressed as a percentage, specified in Sch 1, pt D, cl 6 of the IT07 (¶15-020) distribution amount is the amount of the distribution (before the deduction of RWT), and credit attached is the amount of the M#ori authority credit attached to the distribution.

## Non-cash taxable M#ori authority distributions

A M#ori authority must not make a deduction from a taxable M#ori authority distribution which is not paid in cash. Instead, the authority must pay the Commissioner an amount equivalent to the RWT that would have been deducted if the distribution were a cash distribution. This amount is treated as if it were a deduction of RWT.

The formula for calculating the amount of RWT in relation to a non-cash taxable M#ori authority distribution is: (tax rate × distribution amount ÷ (1 # tax rate))  credit attached where: tax rate is the appropriate rate of RWT expressed as a percentage, specified in Sch 1, pt D, cl 6 (¶15-020)

## Resident withholding tax deductions treated as M#ori authority

credits RWT deducted from a taxable M#ori authority distribution is treated as a M#ori authority credit and is dealt with under the M#ori authority credit regime (¶24-314). Details of the deduction must be provided to the Commissioner in the annual M#ori authority credit account return and recorded in the distribution statement prepared by the M#ori authority.
