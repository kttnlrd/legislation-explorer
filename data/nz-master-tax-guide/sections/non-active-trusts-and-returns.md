# Non-active trusts and returns ¶2-027

Non-active trusts and deceased estates may elect not to file a return. In the case of a trust, the trust must be a non-active complying trust. If the trust or estate has an IRD number, a trustee or administrator/executor must have declared that the trust or estate is non-active and that it will notify the Commissioner if it ceases to be so.

A trust or an estate is non-active if, throughout the tax year, the trustee, executor or administrator:

## has not derived any assessable income

## has no deductions, and

## has not been a party to, or perpetuated or continued with, any transactions with assets of the trust

with a person associated to the trust that, during the tax year, give rise to income in that person's hands or fringe benefits to that person as an employee or former employee.

Certain minimal amounts of income or deductions can be ignored in determining whether a trust or estate is non-active. This includes:

## reasonable fees paid to professional trustees to administer the trust

## up to $1,500 of bank charges or other minimal administration costs in the tax year

## up to $1,000 of income derived during the tax year that would be reportable income, if the trust or

estate were an individual in the tax year, or

## insurance, rates, interest and other expenditure incidental to the occupation of a dwelling owned by

the trust and incurred by the beneficiaries of the trust.

Where a trust or estate ceases to be non-active, the Commissioner must be notified. The Commissioner retains the power to require a non-active trust or estate to furnish a return despite its non-active status.

## Testamentary trusts

A testamentary trust that does not meet the criteria in s 43B(1) as set out above may nevertheless be excused from filing an income tax return if all of the following criteria are met:

## the testamentary trust is a complying trust (¶25-245)

## if the trust has an IRD number, a declaration is provided to IR in an approved form (IR633) stating

that the trust meets the requirements to be excused from filing a return and if these requirements cease to be met, IR will be notified

## the trust has not derived any assessable income, or any reportable income (if the trust was an

individual) that exceeds $5,000, and

## if the trust derives non-reportable income, the amount of non-reportable income does not exceed

$1,000, and net non-reportable income does not exceed $200 taking into account any available deductions.
