# suppression tool use ¶14-133

While no "good behaviour" reduction in shortfall penalty is available for taxpayers once an electronic sales suppression penalty is imposed, there is a reduction available to those taxpayers who make full voluntary disclosure of the use of these tools to IR.

If a voluntary disclosure is made pre-notification (before the taxpayer is notified of a pending audit or investigation), the penalty is reduced by 100%. If the voluntary disclosure is made post-notification (after the taxpayer is notified of the audit or investigation), the reduction is 40%.

Section 141GB operates in a similar way to s 141G (reduction in penalty for voluntary disclosure of tax shortfall (¶14-130)), except that the section requires disclosure of the details of the suppression tool, rather than a tax shortfall.
