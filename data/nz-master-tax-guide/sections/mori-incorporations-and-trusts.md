# M#ori incorporations and trusts ¶24-285

M#ori trusts and M#ori incorporations that are M#ori authorities (generally, all M#ori trusts and M#ori incorporations established or operating under the Te Ture Whenua M#ori Act 1993) are taxed according to the terms of subpart HF of the IT07 (¶24-288–¶24-335).

## Charitable status

M#ori incorporations are established by order of the M#ori Land Court. Under s 258 of the Te Ture Whenua M#ori Act, a M#ori incorporation may, by special resolution, declare that it shall possess any part of its property, or the income derived from part of its property, on trust for charitable purposes. However, the fact that a M#ori incorporation has declared a charitable trust under s 258 does not deem the trust to have a charitable purpose for the purposes of the revenue Acts. IR will apply its usual criteria when determining whether a trust established under s 258 should have a charitable status for tax purposes.
