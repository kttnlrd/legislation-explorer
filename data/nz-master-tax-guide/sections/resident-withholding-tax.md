# Resident withholding tax ¶50-075

RWT on payments of interest and dividends received during the 2011–12 and later tax years is as follows:

Interest See below Dividends (to extent not fully imputed) 33% Taxable M#ori authority distributions (to extent not fully credited) 17.5%

## RWT on interest

The RWT rate in respect of interest differs depending upon the type of taxpayer:

## Non-corporate taxpayers

For non-corporate taxpayers, provided the taxpayer's IRD number is supplied to the payer of interest, an election may be made to have RWT deducted at 10.5% (if there is a reasonable expectation that income will be $15,600 or less, or $14,000 or less before 31 July 2024), 17.5%, 30% or 33%.

With effect from 1 October 2021, a non-corporate taxpayer is able to elect the RWT rate of 39% if their taxable income is expected to exceed $180,000.

If an IRD number is supplied but no election is made, the default rate is 33%.

If no IRD number is supplied, the default rate is 45%.

## Corporate taxpayers

For corporate taxpayers (other than a company that is a trustee or a M#ori authority) the default rate is 28% if an IRD number has been supplied but no rate has been chosen.

Companies can also choose a rate of 33% or, from 1 October 2021, 39%.

If no IRD number is supplied, the default rate is 45%.

Taxable M#ori authority distributions have a rate of 17.5% unless no IRD number has been supplied by the member to whom the distribution is made, in which case, the rate is 39% (33% before 1 April 2021).
