# Recovery of excess tax credits ¶2-185

Section 165A of the TAA makes it clear that the Commissioner has the power to recover excess tax credits that have been allowed to a taxpayer. The power of recovery under s 165A is expressly subject to:

## the rules for recovering FamilyBoost tax credits (see below)

## the use of excess tax credits to settle income tax liability and treatment of remaining credits (ss LA

3–LA 5 and ¶11-010)

## refund or repayment of foreign tax (s LJ 7)

## tax credits that are available for use in the next tax year (s LK 4).

The Commissioner's powers of recovery are the same as those, with necessary modifications, for income tax payable by a person.

In the case of FamilyBoost tax credits, only significant over payments or credits arising out of false or misleading claims may be recovered (s 41C(5)–(7)).
