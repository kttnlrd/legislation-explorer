# The Commissioner's challenge notice ¶4-192

Where a taxpayer has initiated the dispute, the Commissioner must issue a challenge notice within 4 years of the taxpayer issuing its NOPA. Generally, the challenge notice can only be issued after the Commissioner has issued a SOP. The challenge notice forms the basis for the subsequent challenge in the courts. It marks the conclusion of the taxpayer-initiated dispute.

A challenge notice must state that:

## the Commissioner will not be issuing an amended assessment that includes or takes into account the

adjustment proposed by the taxpayer, and

## a challenge may proceed.

A challenge notice is not required where the Commissioner has accepted a taxpayer's NOPA if the Commissioner considers the taxpayer was fraudulent or wilfully misled the Commissioner in relation to the adjustment.

The Commissioner is also not required to issue a challenge notice to the extent that the dispute has ended, or when:

## an amended assessment has been issued reflecting some (but not all) of the disputant's proposed

adjustments, and

## the disputant has a right of challenge under s 138B(2).

The latter provisions concerning an amended assessment first applied on 30 March 2022.

If the Commissioner has failed to issue a challenge notice in time and exceptional circumstances exist, the Commissioner may make an application to the High Court to issue a late notice (¶4-165).
