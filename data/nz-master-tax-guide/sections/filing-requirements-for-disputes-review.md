# Filing requirements for disputes review ¶4-200

On completion of the pre-adjudication disputes resolution process, the following documents must be filed with IR:

## notice of proposed adjustment (NOPA)

## notice of response (NOR)

## notice rejecting the NOR (if applicable)

## conference notes

## the taxpayer's SOP

## the Commissioner's SOP, and

## any relevant documentary evidence (eg expert opinions, specialist advice obtained) and a schedule

of all evidence held.

The IR officer will attach a cover sheet to the documents that will be sent to the Tax Counsel Office (TCO).

A copy of the cover sheet will be sent to the taxpayer or agent together with a letter asking for the taxpayer's agreement on the material to be sent to the TCO. Taxpayers will be given 10 working days to respond, after which the file will be given to the TCO. The TCO will endeavour to determine the outcome of the case within 10 weeks from the date of receipt of the file, subject to the facts and complexity of the case.

See standard practice statement SPS 23/01, "Disputes process".

IR's performance standards require the TCO to complete low complexity disputes in an average of 8 weeks, medium complexity disputes in an average of 14 weeks and high complexity disputes in an average of 20 weeks.

Standard practice statement SPS 23/01 states at [261]: "Generally, the adjudicator will make such a decision within 10 weeks after the case is referred to the TCO. The length of time taken to complete the adjudication depends on the number of disputes before the TCO at any one time, any allocation delays, and the technical, legal and factual complexity of those disputes."
