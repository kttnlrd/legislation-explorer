# Other income under subpart CH ¶5-372

The following amounts are also income from adjustments:

## unexpired amounts of expenditure — s CH 2 and ¶10-052

## unpaid amounts of expenditure on employment income — s CH 3 and ¶10-292

## adjustments for changes to accounting practice — s CH 4 and ¶5-097

## adjustments for leases that become finance leases and certain operating leases — s CH 6 and

¶6-700

## adjustments for certain avoidance arrangements — s CH 7

## adjustments for non-market transactions — s CH 8

## adjustments for interest apportionment on thin capitalisation — ss CH 9, CH 10, CH 10B and ¶26-620

## adjustments for Te Pou Tupua expenditure — s CH 11

## income from a hybrid mismatch arrangement under subpart FH — s CH 12 and ¶26-650.

## income from feasibility expenditure clawback – s CH 13 and ¶10-440

*Refs: s CH 2, s CH 3, s CH 4, s CH 6, s CH 7, s CH 8, ss CH 9, s CH 11, s CH 12, s CH 13*
