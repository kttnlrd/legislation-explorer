# Election to apply zero-rating under business-to-business regime for GST ¶32-310

Section 20F states that a person may choose to zero-rate the business-to-business supply of financial services (eg dealing with money, certain securities or the provision of credits and loans). For taxable periods starting on or after 1 April 2025, the requirement to notify IR regarding this election has been removed.

Registered person who have previously notified the Commissioner that they are applying s 20F are able to continue to apply s 20F because their election will remain valid.
