# Non-profit organisations ¶24-120

Non-profit organisations are subject to income tax, unless there is a specific exemption available. However, some non-profit bodies are entitled to a deduction for the lesser of $1,000 or the amount that would be the net income of the body but for that concession.

This deduction is allowed to:

## an organisation that is not carried on for the purposes of profit to any proprietor, member or

shareholder, and

## an organisation that has a constitution prohibiting any distribution of money or property to any

proprietor, member or shareholder.

The deduction is available to both incorporated and unincorporated organisations, such as:

## associations established for community purposes not falling within the exemption for charitable

organisations

## trade associations

## progressive associations

## political parties

## social clubs, and

## sporting clubs that do not come within the exemption for amateur sporting bodies.

The exemption for sporting clubs is considered further at ¶24-130.
