# No taxable dividend when amalgamating company ceases to exist ¶20-101

An amount derived by an amalgamated company on a resident's restricted amalgamation from an amalgamating company that ceases to exist is not a dividend if it arises from:

## the amalgamated company acquiring property of the amalgamating company, or

## the amalgamated company being relieved of an obligation owed to the amalgamating company.

Law: IT07 s CD 35
