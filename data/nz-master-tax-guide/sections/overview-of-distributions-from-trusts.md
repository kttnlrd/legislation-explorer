# Overview of distributions from trusts ¶25-350

For tax purposes, at the time any distribution is made, a trust is classified as either a complying trust, a foreign trust or a non-complying trust. This classification is fundamental for determining the tax treatment of the distribution and the applicable tax rate. For a summary table on the tax treatment of the various distributions and the applicable tax rate, see ¶25-500.

## Distribution from complying trusts

A distribution from a comply trust is only taxable for a beneficiary if it is beneficiary income. All other distributions from a complying trust to a beneficiary are exempt income. The concept of taxable distribution is not relevant for distributions from complying trusts.

## Distribution from foreign trusts and non-complying trust

A taxable distribution is a distribution from a foreign trust or non-complying trust to a beneficiary that is included in the beneficiary income (¶25-360). A taxable distribution from a foreign trust is income of the beneficiary recipient and taxed at the beneficiary's marginal tax rate (s HC 18). A taxable distribution from a non-complying trust is excluded income for the beneficiary recipient but a tax rate of 45% is applicable (s HC 19).

The ordering rules in s HC 16 apply when distributions are made to beneficiaries by foreign trusts or non- complying trusts (¶25-370).

## Distribution includes monetary and non-monetary transfers

A trustee makes a distribution when the trustee transfers value to a person because the person is a beneficiary of the trust (s HC 14). A "transfer of value", which has a corresponding meaning to "transfers value", means a transfer between persons, being (s YA 1):

## the provision of money or money's worth, whether or not convertible into money

## the provision of money or money's worth, whether or not convertible into money, for more than

market value, and includes:

## the release of an obligation to pay money, either by agreement or operation of law, and

## a disposition of property at less than market value.

Example: Making trust property (eg the family home) available rent free to beneficiaries in their capacity as beneficiaries is a distribution. See ¶25-330.

From 23 March 2020, an amendment has clarified that where a trustee pays a beneficiary interest on an amount owed to the beneficiary, the payment is not a distribution except to the extent the interest exceeds the greater of market rate and the prescribed rate of interest.

Law: IT07 ss HC 14, HC 15, HC 17, HC 18, HC 19, YA 1 ("transfer of value")
