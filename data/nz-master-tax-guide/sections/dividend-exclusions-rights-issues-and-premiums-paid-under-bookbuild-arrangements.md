# Dividend exclusions — rights issues and premiums paid under bookbuild arrangements ¶16-715

Companies can offer their shareholders rights to buy new shares, generally at a discount. The discounted amount is not a taxable dividend for those shareholders who exercise the right and that the right itself is also not a taxable dividend.

It has been clarified that, in general, premiums paid under bookbuilds are not dividends for tax purposes (Tax Information Bulletin ¶259-103 Vol 25, No 9, October 2013 at 63). A bookbuild can take place following a rights issue. A bookbuild involves the rights of non-participating shareholders (who chose not to participate or were not entitled to participate) being offered to other investors who pay a premium for them. The original shareholder is paid all or part of this premium for giving up their rights. The premium in that case will not be a dividend. Note, however, that in some cases the premium may be paid to the company and this may give rise to ASC. In those circumstances, the exclusion will not apply.
