# Double tax agreements negotiated ¶26-305

New Zealand continues to enter into and renegotiate DTAs. It is obliged to do this as a member of the OECD.

All New Zealand's DTAs are reproduced in CCH, New Zealand Income Tax Legislation and CCH, New Zealand Double Tax Agreements Law and Practice.

New Zealand has concluded DTAs with:

Australia Indonesia Singapore Austria Ireland Slovak Republic Belgium Italy South Africa Canada Japan Spain Chile Korea Sweden China Malaysia Switzerland Czech Republic Mexico Taiwan Denmark Netherlands Thailand Fiji Norway Turkey Finland Papua New Guinea United Arab Emirates France Philippines United Kingdom Germany Poland United States of America Hong Kong Russian Federation Viet Nam India Samoa

Progress on the negotiation of DTAs is monitored by the Commissioner at www.taxpolicy.ird.govt.nz. From time to time New Zealand negotiates amendments to one of its DTAs by executing a protocol; see, for example, the protocol to the DTA with the United Kingdom made on 4 November 2003.
