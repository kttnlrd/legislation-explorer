# Timing of deductibility of employment income ¶3-015

For an amount incurred by an employer in respect of an employee (employment income) to be deductible, it must be paid within a statutory time period. For employees other than shareholder–employees, the employment income must be paid in the income year the deduction is claimed or, if the employer chooses, within 63 days after the end of the income year in which the deduction is claimed. For shareholder– employees, the employment income must be paid in the income year or, if the employer chooses, by the last day the person could file a return of income for the income year.

If the employment expenditure is not paid by the relevant date, the unpaid amount is income of the person in the income year under s CH 3.

If an amount is treated as income under s CH 3, the amount should qualify for a deduction in the following income year under s DB 51.

For the rules governing the deductibility of wage-related provisions (eg a provision for holiday pay) on the sale of a business, see ¶10-052 and ¶10-292.
