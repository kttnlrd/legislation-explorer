# 7-275 - Catch-up concessional superannuation contributions

Worked example: Catch-up concessional superannuation contributions Issue Roger is aged 55. In the 2023–24 income year, Roger's employer made concessional superannuation guarantee contributions of $12,500 on his behalf to his superannuation fund. Roger did not make any deductible personal contributions to his fund. Because the concessional contributions cap is $27,500 for 2023–24, Roger's unused concessional contributions cap amount is $15,000.

In the 2024–25 income year, the concessional contributions cap is $30,000. Roger's employer makes concessional contributions of $20,000 for Roger, and Roger does not make any deductible contributions. His unused concessional contributions cap amount for the 2024–25 financial year is therefore $25,000 (being $15,000 brought forward from 2023–24 and $10,000 from 2024–25).

In 2025–26, Roger wishes to use his unused concessional contributions cap amounts to make concessional contributions of $25,000, on top of his employer's concessional contribution of $30,000 for Roger.

At the end of 30 June 2025, Roger had a total superannuation balance of $506,000.

Can Roger make an additional concessional contribution of $25,000 in 2025–26, as he wishes?

Solution Individuals with a total superannuation balance of less than $500,000 just before the start of a financial year may be able to increase their concessional contributions cap in the financial year by applying previously unused concessional contributions cap amounts from one or more of the previous 5 financial years.

An individual's concessional contributions cap for a financial year may be increased by catch-up contributions if: (a) their concessional contributions for the year would otherwise exceed their concessional contributions cap for the year (b) their total superannuation balance just before the start of the financial year is less than $500,000, and (c) they have previously unapplied "unused concessional contributions cap" for one or more of the previous 5 financial years (ITAA97 s 291-20(3)).

An individual's "unused concessional contributions cap" for a financial year is the amount by which their concessional contributions for the year falls short of their concessional contributions cap for the year (ITAA97 s 291-20(6)).

As Roger's superannuation balance at 30 June 2025, just before the start of the financial year in which he wishes to make the extra contribution, is $506,000, Roger cannot increase his concessional contributions cap by using his unused cap amounts from the 2 previous years.

s 291-20(6))*
