# 10-300 - Calculation of tax liability of resident private company

Worked example: Calculation of tax liability of resident private company Issue Serendipity Pty Ltd (Serendipity) is an Australian resident, private company, with an income tax rate of 25% for the 2024–25 income year. Its income and expenses for that year were: Income:                                                                                                                        $                         $ Operating revenue                                                                                                            3,600,000 Distribution franked to 60% from a resident public company with a 2024–25                                                       270,000 corporate tax rate for imputation purposes of 30% Unfranked distribution from resident public company                                                                                90,000                3,960,000 Less expenses: Operating expenses                                                                                                              750,000 Fines paid for environmental breaches                                                                                              35,000 Interest paid on loan for purchasing equipment                                                                                     65,000                 850,000 NET INCOME                                                                                                                                               3,110,000 Calculate Serendipity's taxable income and income tax payable for the 2024–25 income year. Explain assumptions and give brief explanations as appropriate.

Solution Serendipity's taxable income and income tax payable for the 2024–25 income year is calculated as follows.

Serendipity Pty Ltd Statement of taxable income for the tax year ended 30 June 2025 $                                       $ Operating revenue                                                                           3,600,000 Distribution from resident public company                                                       270,000 12                                 69,429 Franking credit on distribution franked to 60% Unfranked distribution from resident public                                                      90,000                                                  4,029,429 company Less: Allowable deductions Operating expenses                                                                              750,000 Interest paid on loan for purchasing equipment                                                   65,000                                                  (815,000) TAXABLE INCOME                                                                                                                                           3,214,429 Gross tax payable: $3,214,429 @ 25%                                                                                                803,607.25 4                                                                                          (69,429) Less: franking tax offset (ITAA97 s 207-20(2)) NET TAX PAYABLE                                                                                                                                      734,178.25 Assumptions and explanations 1. Both the franked and unfranked distribution are included in Serendipity's assessable income (ITAA36 s 44). 2. The franked distribution is grossed-up for the franking credit ($270,000 × 30 / 70 × 60% = $69,429) and the franking credit is included in assessable income (ITAA97 s 207-20(1)). 3. The $35,000 fines paid for environmental breaches are not deductible (ITAA97 s 26-5). 4. Because Serendipity meets the residency requirement for the income year, it is entitled to a franking tax offset for the franking credit on the distribution (ITAA97 s 207-20(2)).

*Refs: ITAA97 s 207-20(2)), ITAA97 s 207-20(1)), ITAA97 s 26-5)*
