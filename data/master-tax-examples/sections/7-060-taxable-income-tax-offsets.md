# 7-060 - Taxable income tax offsets

Worked example: Taxable income; tax offsets Issue Ian Jacobs, aged 67 years, is married with children. Ian derived $56,000 in salary, $3,000 in bonuses, $800 in bank interest and $5,400 in fully franked dividends during the 2024–25 income tax year. His deductions, which all relate to earning his salary, totalled $2,342.

Ian's wife Mandy does not work and is in receipt of a disability support pension. She had an adjusted taxable income of $730 (including the pension) for the 2024–25 tax year and was not entitled to any family tax benefits.

Ian had adequate private hospital health insurance cover for the year and the tax withheld from Ian's salary totalled $8,167 during the 2024–25 tax year.

Based on the above information, calculate Ian Jacobs's taxable income and tax payable/refund including Medicare levy for the tax year ended 30 June 2025.
