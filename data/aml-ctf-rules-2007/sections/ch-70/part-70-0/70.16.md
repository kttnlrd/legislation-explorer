# 70.16 The application for review must:


**(1)** be in writing;

**(2)** set out the reasons why the decision should be reviewed;

**(3)** be made to the AUSTRAC CEO within:

> **(a)** 30 days of the date of the decision of the AUSTRAC CEO made under paragraphs 70.9(2) and 70.18; or

> **(b)** such longer period as the AUSTRAC CEO (whether before or after the end of the 30 day period) allows.
