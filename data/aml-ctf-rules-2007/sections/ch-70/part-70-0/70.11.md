# 70.11 The AUSTRAC CEO must, as soon as practicable after making a decision under paragraph 70.9, give written notice to the person of:


**(1)** the decision to renew the person's registration in accordance with paragraph 70.9(1); or

**(2)** the decision not to renew the person's registration in accordance with paragraph 70.9(2).
