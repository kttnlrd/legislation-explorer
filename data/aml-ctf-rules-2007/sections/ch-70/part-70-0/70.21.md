# 70.21 If an application for a renewal of registration is not made within the appropriate application period specified in paragraph 70.2:

(1)	the registration of the person will cease at the end of the current registration period; and
(2)	the AUSTRAC CEO will remove the person from the Remittance Sector Register in accordance with subsections 75K(3) – (6) of the AML/CTF Act, as applicable.
Note:	Under paragraphs 75F(1)(c) and 75J(3)(a) of the AML/CTF Act, the current default registration period is 3 years from the day the registration takes effect. There are currently no AML/CTF Rules which specify other registration periods.
