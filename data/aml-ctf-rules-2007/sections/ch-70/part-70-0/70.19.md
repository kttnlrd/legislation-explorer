# 70.19 The person must, as soon as practicable after making a decision under paragraph 70.18, give written notice to the applicant of:

70.19	The person must, as soon as practicable after making a decision under paragraph 70.18, give written notice to the applicant of:
(1)	the decision;
(2)	if the decision is to refuse a renewal of the person’s registration – the date the decision takes effect; and
(3)	the reasons for the decision.
