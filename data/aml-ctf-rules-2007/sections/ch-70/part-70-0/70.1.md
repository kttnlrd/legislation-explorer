# 70.1 These Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Rules (Rules) are made under section 229 of the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 (AML/CTF Act) for the purposes of section 75J of that Act. Sections 136 and 137 of the AML/CTF Act apply to each paragraph of this Chapter.
