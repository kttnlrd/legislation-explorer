# 75.4 An application for exemption must:

75.4	An application for exemption must:
be made in writing by a requesting officer;
provide sufficient information to enable the AUSTRAC CEO to decide whether or not to approve the application; and
contain the requesting officer’s declaration that the information contained in the application is true, accurate and complete.
