# 76.3 In determining whether it is appropriate to register a person the AUSTRAC CEO must have regard to:

whether the applicant has complied with the application requirements under paragraph 76.2; and
the information contained in the application, including whether there are reasonable grounds to suspect that any information or document provided by the applicant is false or misleading (whether by inclusion or omission) in a material particular.
