# 76.18 If a person registered on the Digital Currency Exchange Register becomes aware:

of a change that materially affects any information previously provided under this Chapter; or that any information or document provided under this Chapter is incorrect, incomplete, or misleading in a material respect, the person must advise the AUSTRAC CEO and provide updated information in accordance with subsection 76P(2) of the AML/CTF Act.

> **Note:** Subsection 76P(2) of the AML/CTF Act provides that a person registered on the Digital Currency Exchange Register who is required to advise the AUSTRAC CEO of a change in circumstances or a matter must do so in accordance with the approved form, and within 14 days of the change in circumstances or the matter arising.
