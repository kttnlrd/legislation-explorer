# 76.12 In deciding whether it is appropriate to suspend a person’s registration, and without limiting the matters that may be considered, the AUSTRAC CEO may have regard to:

76.12	In deciding whether it is appropriate to suspend a person’s registration, and without limiting the matters that may be considered, the AUSTRAC CEO may have regard to:
whether the person or any of its key personnel have been:
charged, prosecuted, or convicted in relation to money laundering, terrorism financing, terrorism, people smuggling, fraud, a serious offence, or an offence under the AML/CTF Act or FTR Act;
the subject of a civil penalty order made under the AML/CTF Act; or
the subject of any adversely determined civil or criminal proceedings or enforcement action in relation to the management of an entity, or their commercial or professional activities; and
whether there are reasonable grounds to believe that:
any information or document provided under this Chapter was false or misleading (whether by inclusion or omission) in a material particular;
the continued registration of the person involves, or may involve, significant money laundering, financing of terrorism, or other serious crime risk; or
the person has contravened a condition of registration imposed under section 76G of the AML/CTF Act.
