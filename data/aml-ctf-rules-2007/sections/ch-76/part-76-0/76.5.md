# 76.5 An application for renewal must be made using the approved form and must contain all relevant information prescribed by that form, including a declaration that:

the person has complied with the requirements of section 76P of the AML/CTF Act, where applicable; and
the person’s enrolment and registration details as they appear on both the Reporting Entities Roll and the Digital Currency Exchange Register are current and correct.
