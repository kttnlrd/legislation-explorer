# 17.4 A report under subsection 45(2) of the AML/CTF Act must contain the following details about the person completing the report:

17.4	A report under subsection 45(2) of the AML/CTF Act must contain the following details about the person completing the report:
(1)	Full name;
(2)	Job title or position;
(3)	Telephone number; and
(4)	Email address.
