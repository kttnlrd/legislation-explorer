# Rates of tax payable by trustees ¶6-250

A summary of the rates of tax payable by trustees for the 2023–24 income year is set out at ¶42-030. Where the trustee is assessed under s 98 at individual rates, those rates reflect any pro-rated threshold that may apply to the beneficiary on the ground of non-residency during part of the year (¶2-130).

Medicare levy and Medicare levy surcharge A summary of the Medicare levy and Medicare levy surcharge rates payable by trustees for the 2023–24 income year is set out at ¶42-033.
