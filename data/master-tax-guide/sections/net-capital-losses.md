# Net capital losses ¶11-040

A net capital loss is worked out by subtracting capital gains for the income year from capital losses for the income year. If the resulting amount is more than nil, it is the taxpayer's net capital loss for the income year (s 102-10).

A net capital loss is not deductible from a taxpayer's assessable income. However, it can generally be oﬀset against capital gains made by the taxpayer in later income years (¶11-030).

To the extent that a net capital loss cannot be used to oﬀset capital gains in an income year, it can be carried forward to a later income year.

*Refs: s 102-10)*
