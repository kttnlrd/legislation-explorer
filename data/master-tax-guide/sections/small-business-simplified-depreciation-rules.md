# Small business simpliﬁed depreciation rules ¶17-325

A simpliﬁed regime for calculating capital allowances on depreciating assets is contained in ITAA97 Subdiv 328-D. Small business entities that choose to use this regime for an income year are not subject to the provisions of ITAA97 Div 40 and are instead subject to Subdiv 328-D for all eligible depreciating assets (ITAA97 s 328-175). Entities cannot choose to apply the regime on an asset-by-asset basis or only apply individual elements of the regime (eg instant asset write-oﬀ). For full details see ¶7-250.

*Refs: ITAA97 s 328-175)*
