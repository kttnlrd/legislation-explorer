# Members and membership interests ¶4-480

The meaning of a "member" of an entity is set out in ITAA97 s 960-130. The members of various types of entities are shown in the table in ¶8-000.

Where interests or rights that give rise to membership of an entity are held jointly, each holder of the joint interest is a member of the entity.

Membership interest A "membership interest" in an entity means each interest or set of interests in the entity, or each right or set of rights in relation to the entity, by virtue of which a person is a member of the entity (ITAA97 s 960-135).

*Refs: ITAA97 s 960-130, ITAA97 s 960-135)*
