# CGT: Exemption for granny ﬂat arrangements ¶11-915

A CGT event does not happen where a granny ﬂat arrangement is entered into, varied or terminated that meets certain requirements on or after 1 July 2021 (ITAA97 Subdiv 137-A).

A CGT event will not arise on the creation or variation of a "granny ﬂat interest" in a dwelling if: 1. the individual who holds the interest is eligible for a granny ﬂat interest at the time 2. another individual holds an ownership interest, or agrees to acquire an ownership interest, in the relevant dwelling 3. both individuals are a party to the arrangement 4. the arrangement is in writing and indicates an intention for both parties to be legally bound by it, and 5. the arrangement is not of a commercial nature (ss 137-15, 137-20).

A CGT event will not happen on the termination of such an arrangement for which a CGT event did not arise (s 137-25).

For these purposes, an individual will hold a "granny ﬂat interest" in a dwelling under an arrangement if the individual has a right to occupy the dwelling for life that had been conferred by the arrangement (s 137-10(1)). The dwelling can include adjacent land and structures in the same way as the main residence exemption (¶11-730). The CGT exemption is only available for an individual who has reached pension age or has a disability that means they require assistance for most day-to-day activities for at least 12 months (s 137-10(2)).
