# Investigations by Tax Practitioners Board ¶32-060

A significant function of the TPB is to investigate applications for registration or any conduct that may breach the Tax Agent Services Act 2009 (TASA s 60-95).

In conducting an investigation, the TPB has the power to request information or documents to be produced and to require a witness to attend to give evidence (TASA ss 60-100; 60-105).

An investigation is taken to have commenced on the date of the notification that is given to the investigated entity that an investigation is to be carried out and the TPB must make a decision as a result of an investigation within a stipulated period after that date or a longer period determined by the TPB. The stipulated period was originally 6 months, but this was extended to 24 months for investigations that commence on or after 1 July 2024, subject to transitional provisions. The TPB may only determine a longer period if it is satisfied that, for reasons beyond the control of the TPB, a decision in relation to the investigation cannot be made within the stipulated period (TASA s 60-125). If a decision is not made within the stipulated period (or longer period determined) the TPB is taken to have decided to take no further action in relation to the matter that was the subject of investigation. A decision of the TPB to determine a longer period for the carrying out of an investigation is reviewable by the ART.

If the TPB conducts an investigation and finds that the conduct breaches the TASA, the TPB must either make a decision that no further action will be taken or do one or more of certain listed actions. These actions now include deciding that the entity that engaged in the conduct and the information in respect of the entity prescribed by the regulations be entered on the register for the period prescribed by the regulations (TASA s 60-125).
