# Farm management deposits scheme ¶18-290

The farm management deposits (FMD) scheme is designed to allow primary producers, in effect, to shift income from good to bad years in order to deal with adverse economic events and seasonal fluctuations (ITAA97 Div 393). The FMD scheme allows primary producers (with a limited amount of non-primary production income) to claim deductions for FMDs made in the year of deposit (and to reduce their PAYG instalment income accordingly). When an FMD is withdrawn, the amount of the deduction previously allowed is included in both their PAYG instalment income and their assessable income in the repayment year.
