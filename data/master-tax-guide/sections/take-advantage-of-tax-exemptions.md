# Take advantage of tax exemptions ¶31-140

For details of tax exemptions, see ¶10-600 and the checklist at ¶10-005.

Note particularly the exemption for superannuation benefits paid to taxpayers aged 60 or over (¶14-220) and the rules affecting commencement and cessation of superannuation income streams (¶14-120).
