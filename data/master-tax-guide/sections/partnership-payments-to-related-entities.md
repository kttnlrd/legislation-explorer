# Partnership payments to related entities ¶5-100

Where a partnership makes a payment or incurs a liability (eg salary or interest) to a person who is associated with a partner (ie a related entity), and that payment or liability that otherwise qualifies as a deduction to the partnership is unreasonable in amount, the Commissioner may reduce the deduction to the partnership (ITAA97 s 26-35). The amount disallowed is not assessable to the payee unless the partner with whom the payee is associated is a private company, in which case the disallowed amount is treated as a dividend paid by the company to that person (s 65).

Whether a payment is reasonable in amount is a question of fact to be determined in the light of the particular circumstances of each case but, generally, commercial standards should be adopted.

The effect of the disallowance of such a payment will, of course, be to increase the net partnership income or reduce the net partnership loss.
