# First $1,000 of in-house beneﬁts ¶35-660

The ﬁrst $1,000 of the aggregate of the taxable values of certain "in-house" beneﬁts given to an employee in a year is exempt from FBT (s 62). The exemption includes beneﬁts provided to an associate of the employee (MT 2044).

The beneﬁts to which this applies are: in-house property fringe beneﬁts (¶35-520); in-house residual fringe beneﬁts (¶35-590); and in-house expense payment beneﬁts (¶35-350). However, the $1,000 reduction is removed where the beneﬁts are provided under a salary packaging arrangement (¶35-520).
