It is specifically provided that, for the purposes of ITAA36 and ITAA97, a dividend (as defined: ¶4-110) paid out of an amount other than profits is taken to be a dividend paid out of profits (ITAA36 s 44(1A)). However, the Commissioner takes the view that, for the purposes of the Corporations Act and company accounting, dividends can only be paid from profits and not from "amounts other than profits" and that Corporations Act s 254T imposes 3 specified additional prohibitions on the circumstances in which a dividend can be paid (TR 2012/5).
