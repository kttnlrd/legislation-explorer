# Other exempt government payments ¶10-197

In addition to the social security and veterans aﬀairs payments listed at ¶10-195 and ¶10-200, the federal government also makes a range of other types of payments that are exempt from tax (albeit special conditions may apply). These include the following:

- acute support packages (ITAA97 s 52-185)

- carer adjustment payment (ITAA97 s 53-10, item 1)

- Commonwealth education or training supplementary payment (ITAA97 s 52-140; ¶10-700)

- Continence Aids Payment Scheme payments (ITAA97 s 52-175)

- disability services payment (ITAA97 s 53-10, item 2)

- disaster recovery payments to special category visa (subclass 444) holders (ITAA97 s 51-30)

- economic security strategy payment (ITAA97 s 52-160)

- family assistance payments (including child care subsidy, additional child care subsidy, family tax

  beneﬁt, stillborn baby payment, economic security strategy payment, back to school bonus, single

  income family bonus, clean energy advance, single income family supplement, ETR payment and

  economic support payments) (ITAA97 ss 52-150; 52-162)

- household stimulus payments (ITAA97 s 52-165)

- National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) payment (ITAA97 s 52-180)

- outer regional and remote payments under the Better Start for Children with Disability initiative

  (ITAA97 s 52-172)

- outer regional and remote payments under the Helping Children with Autism package (ITAA97 s

  52-170)

- Support for Australia's Thalidomide Survivors program or the Thalidomide Australian Fixed Trust

  payments (ITAA97 s 51-30)

- Territories Stolen Generations Redress Scheme payments (ITAA97 s 53-30)

- tobacco industry exit grant (ITAA97 s 53-10, item 4C)

- wounds and disability pension (ITAA97 s 53-10, item 5).
