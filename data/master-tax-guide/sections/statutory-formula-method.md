# Statutory formula method ¶35-180

The eﬀect of the statutory formula is to value a car fringe beneﬁt by applying a statutory fraction to a specially determined value of the car. The formula reduces the beneﬁt value to take account of any days during the year when the car was not applied to private use or taken to be available for private use. The value is also reduced by the amount of any payments for the beneﬁt by a recipient.
