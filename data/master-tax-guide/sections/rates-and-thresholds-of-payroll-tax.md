(2)     Victoria payroll tax rate is 4.85% for employers other than regional employers and 1.2125% for regional employers. (3)     In Victoria, a mental health and wellbeing payroll tax surcharge applies from 1 January 2022 to employers or group of employers with total taxable wages and interstate wages of $10 million or more in a ﬁnancial year. The surcharge rate is 0.5% of taxable wages where employers or group of employers have annual total taxable wages and interstate wages of more than $10 million but not more than $100 million. Employers or group of employers with annual total taxable wages and interstate wages of more than $100 million pay an additional 0.5% surcharge on their taxable wages. A COVID-19 debt temporary payroll tax surcharge calculated on the same basis as the mental health and wellbeing surcharge also applies for 10 years from 1 July 2023. (4)     Victoria annual threshold will increase to $1,000,000 from 1 July 2025. (5)     Victoria monthly threshold will increase to $83,333 from 1 July 2025. (6)     Queensland payroll tax rate is 4.95% for employers paying more than $6,500,000 in Australian taxable wages and 4.75% for other employers. For the 2019–20 to 2029–30 ﬁnancial years a 1% rate discount applies to regional employers paying at least 85% of taxable wages to regional employees. From 1 July 2024 an employer will need to pay annual taxable wages of $350 million or less to be eligible for the regional rate discount. (7)     From 1 January 2023, a mental health levy is imposed on Queensland employers or group of employers with annual Australian taxable wages over $10 million. The levy is 0.25% of the employer's or group's taxable wages to the extent that the annual Australian taxable wages for a ﬁnancial year exceed $10 million, plus an additional 0.5% of the employer's or group's taxable wages to the extent that the annual Australian taxable wages for a ﬁnancial year exceed $100 million. (8)     In South Australia, a payroll tax variable rate of 0% to 4.95% applies for annual taxable wages between $1,500,000 and $1,700,000 and 4.95% for annual wages above $1,700,000. (9)     Tasmania payroll tax rate is 4% for taxable wages between $1,250,000 and $2,000,000 and 6.1% for taxable wages over $2,000,000. (10)    Tasmania monthly threshold is based on 28, 30 or 31 days in the month. (11)    In the ACT, a payroll tax surcharge applies from 1 July 2024 at an additional 0.25% on ACT wages above the payroll tax threshold for businesses with Australia-wide wages above $50 million and 0.5% on ACT wages above the payroll tax threshold for businesses with Australia-wide wages above $100 million. The surcharge does not apply to prescribed eligible universities with an ACT campus. (12)    Northern Territory annual threshold will increase to $2,500,000 from 1 July 2025 (Payroll Tax Amendment Bill 2024 (NT)). (13)    Northern Territory monthly threshold will increase to $208,333.33 from 1 July 2025 (Payroll Tax Amendment Bill 2024 (NT)).
