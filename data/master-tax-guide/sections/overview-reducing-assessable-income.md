# Overview: reducing assessable income ¶31-095

There are a number of ways in which the assessable income of a taxpayer may be reduced. The CGT provisions must also be considered to ensure that assets are acquired by the most appropriate taxpayer in the most appropriate circumstances. Further, the provisions that tax certain non-cash business benefits even though they are not convertible into money must be considered (¶10-030).
