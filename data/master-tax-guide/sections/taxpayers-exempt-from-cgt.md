# Taxpayers exempt from CGT ¶11-680

If the income of a taxpayer for an income year is exempt, any capital gains are also exempt. A capital loss made by an entity is disregarded if it was an exempt entity at the time that it made the loss despite not being an exempt entity at all times during the year (s 118-70). Exempt entities are detailed in ¶10-604, which include entities exempt from taxation pursuant to Div 50 such as charitable, educational, scientific or religious institutions.

All income of constitutionally protected funds is exempt (¶13-300).

*Refs: s 118-70)*
