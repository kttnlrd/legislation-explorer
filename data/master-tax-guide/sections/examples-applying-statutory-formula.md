# Examples applying statutory formula ¶35-200

Example 1: Car held for whole year This example shows the application of the formula to a car that was originally owned by the employer before the commencement of the year, is held by the employer for the whole year and is available for private use on every day during the year.

FBT year to 31 March 2025 Date car purchased ..................................................................1 October 2021 Time car held at commencement of year ..................................................................3 years 6 months Cost price of car..................................................................$30,000 Number of days in year..................................................................365 Number of days when car beneﬁt arises..................................................................365 Recipient's payments..................................................................$0 Taxable value of beneﬁt:

0.2 × $30,000 × 365 − 0 = $6,000 FBT is payable on the grossed-up taxable value (being a type 1 beneﬁt: ¶35-025), assuming GST input tax credits are available, ie $6,000 × 2.0802                                                             = $12,481 Tax at 47%                                                                  = $5,866.07 Example 2: Car held for part of year This example shows the application of the formula to a car that is held by the employer for part of the year and is available for private use on every day during that part of the year.

FBT year to 31 March 2025 Date car purchased..................................................................1 October 2024 Time car held at commencement of year..................................................................0 years Cost price of car..................................................................$30,000 Number of days in year..................................................................365 Number of days when car beneﬁt arises..................................................................182 Recipient's payments..................................................................$0 Taxable value of beneﬁt:

0.2 × $30,000 × 182 −     0   = $2,991.78 FBT is payable on the grossed-up taxable value (being a type 1 beneﬁt: ¶35-025), assuming GST input tax credits are available, ie: $2,991.78 × 2.0802                                                                                    = $6,223.50 Tax at 47%                                                                                            = $2,925.05
