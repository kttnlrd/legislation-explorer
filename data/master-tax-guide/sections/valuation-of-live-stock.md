# Valuation of live stock ¶9-250

The bases of measuring the closing value of trading stock (¶9-180) apply to determining the closing value for live stock, including horse breeding stock (ITAA97 s 70-45, subject to closing value options in ss 70-60 and 70-65).

Although, the basis of live stock valuation can be changed from year-to-year and can be varied on an item- by-item basis, where the cost basis is used for valuing live stock, the valuation is typically based on average cost. For horse breeding stock per unit per year valuation may be more appropriate.
