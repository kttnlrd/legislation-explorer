# Examples of deductible legal expenses ¶16-842

In addition to the expenses noted at ¶16-840, the following are examples of legal expenses that have been held to be deductible under ITAA97 s 8-1:

- costs of defending proceedings for the unauthorised use of a trademark (Pech 2001 ATC 2210;

  [2001] AATA 573)

- costs of patent infringement action ((1963) 13 TBRD Case N78)

- costs of defending an employee against corruption charges ((1953) 3 TBRD Case C85), assault

  charges (Case 9/97 97 ATC 157; [1997] AATA 35)

- costs of recovering a payment in lieu of a termination notice period of 12 months (Romanin 2008

  ATC ¶20-055; [2008] FCA 1532; ID 2010/131)

- costs incurred by an employee to prevent defamatory statements being made by a colleague (ID

  2001/549)

- costs of defending misrepresentation suit in connection with sale of goods (Reo Motors (1931) 1

  ATD 163)

- costs and damages of an employer in an employee's personal injuries claim against the employer

  (5 TBRD Case E14)

- legal expenses of pursuing an assessable workers compensation claim (ID 2010/209) or income

  entitlements under an employment contract (TR 2012/8)

- costs of a landlord in ejectment proceedings against a rent-defaulting tenant (15 TBRD Case

Q49), but not costs of evicting tenant to obtain virtually indeﬁnite occupancy of whole of premises for business purposes (5 TBRD Case E6)

- costs of opinion as to whether goods could be sold in another state ((1950) 1 TBRD Case 50)

- damages and costs of a newspaper in defending a libel action (Herald and Weekly Times (1932)

  48 CLR 113)

- costs incurred by a company in supporting an application by an ex-director for leave to resume his

  activities as a director notwithstanding a prior conviction (Magna Alloys & Research 80 ATC 4542;

[1980] FCA 150)

- costs of defending an action for wrongful dismissal brought by a former director (Case P16 82

*Refs: ITAA97 s 8-1*

- costs incurred by a director in defending a defamation action brought against the board of

  directors by a dismissed executive (Case V116 88 ATC 737; [1988] AATA 233)

- cost of arbitration to settle a dispute between a solicitor and his partners as to his share of the

  proﬁts and his subsequent purported expulsion from the partnership (Creer 94 ATC 4454; [1994]

FCA 1228)

- costs of recovering a misappropriated bank deposit but only to the extent that the costs related to

  recovery of interest accrued on the deposit from the date of the misappropriation (Case V123 88

ATC 767; [1988] AATA 589)

- costs of opposing a neighbourhood development that would cause excessive dust and damage to

  the taxpayer's plant nursery (Case W30 89 ATC 300; [1989] AATA 79)

- costs of an accountant/trustee in defending a charge of conspiring with the debtor of an insolvent

  estate to defraud the Commonwealth (Putnin 91 ATC 4097; [1991] FCA 14)

- cost of maintaining the amount of trust income to which the taxpayer was entitled under a will (ID

  2004/214)

- costs incurred by a trustee company in defence of a writ served on a director of the trustee

  company (ID 2003/145)

- legal expenses incurred by an employee in recovering wages paid by a dishonoured cheque (TD

93/29) and travel and incidental expenses relating to a legal action to recover unpaid wages (ID 2004/659).
