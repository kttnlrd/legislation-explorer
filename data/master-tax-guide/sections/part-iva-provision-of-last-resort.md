# Part IVA provision of last resort ¶31-030

In addition to considering any specific anti-avoidance provisions that may be relevant to a particular transaction, the tax planner must pay constant regard to the general anti-avoidance provisions of ITAA36 Pt IVA which generally apply to schemes entered into with the sole or dominant purpose of obtaining a tax benefit (¶30-120). If a transaction is a sham, it will be inherently ineffective and the Commissioner would not need to rely on Pt IVA to strike it down (¶30-110).
