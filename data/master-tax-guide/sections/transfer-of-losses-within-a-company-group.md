# Transfer of losses within a company group ¶3-090

As part of the introduction of the consolidation regime (¶8-000), the group loss transfer rules generally ceased to apply for income years that commence after 30 June 2003. However, group loss transfers continue to be available within company groups provided either the loss company or the income company is an Australian branch of a foreign bank or non-bank foreign financial entity.
