# roads ¶40-300

For eligible business uses other than heavy vehicles on public roads, the credit rate for taxable liquid fuels since 1 February 2016 is:

- 1/2/17 to 31/7/17: 40.1 cents per litre

- 1/8/17 to 4/2/18: 40.3 cents per litre

- 5/2/18 to 31/7/18: 40.9 cents per litre

- 1/8/18 to 3/2/19: 41.2 cents per litre

- 4/2/19 to 4/8/19: 41.6 cents per litre

- 5/8/19 to 2/2/20: 41.8 cents per litre

- 3/2/20 to 30/6/20: 42.3 cents per litre

- 1/7/20 to 31/1/21: 42.3 cents per litre

- 1/2/21 to 1/8/21: 42.7 cents per litre

- 2/8/21 to 31/1/22: 43.3 cents per litre

- 1/2/22 to 29/3/22: 44.2 cents per litre

- 30/3/22 to 31/7/22: 22.1 cents per litre

- 1/8/22 to 28/9/22: 23.0 cents per litre

- 29/9/22 to 31/1/23: 46.0 cents per litre

- 1/2/23 to 31/7/23: 47.7 cents per litre

- 1/8/23 to 4/2/24: 48.8 cents per litre

- 5/2/24 to 4/8/24: 49.6 cents per litre

- 5/8/24 to 2/2/25: 50.6 cents per litre.

  The above rate is indexed twice a year generally on 1 February and 1 August.
