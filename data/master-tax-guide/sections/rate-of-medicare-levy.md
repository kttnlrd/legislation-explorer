# Rate of Medicare levy ¶2-320

The basic rate of the Medicare levy is 2% of the taxpayer's taxable income for 2024–25. There is no ceiling on the amount of Medicare levy payable.

Example Buddy's taxable income is $40,000. The Medicare levy is calculated as 2% of $40,000 = $800. This is additional to Buddy's ordinary tax.

Note that those on higher incomes without adequate private patient hospital insurance may be liable to an additional 1% to 1.5% Medicare levy surcharge (¶2-335).
