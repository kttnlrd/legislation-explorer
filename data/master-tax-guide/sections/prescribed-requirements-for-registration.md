# Prescribed requirements for registration ¶32-030

To satisfy the prescribed requirements for registration as an individual tax agent, the Tax Agent Services Regulations 2022 provide that the individual must satisfy one of a number of academic and relevant experience combinations (reg 21 and Pt 2 sch 2). The relevant information is set out on the TPB's website.

TPB's mix and match practice The TPB has issued an information sheet which sets out the views of the TPB in relation to the practice adopted by the TPB of identifying a number of subjects or units which have been successfully completed by an applicant for registration which, taken together, will satisfy the requirements of a TPB-approved course (TPB(I) 06/2011). This practice is called the "mix and match" approach and enables applicants for registration to satisfy the requirements of courses which are referred to in sch 2 of the Tax Agent Services Regulations 2022.
