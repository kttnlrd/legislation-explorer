# Paid parental leave superannuation contributions ¶39-070

Recipients of parental leave pay (PLP) under the Commonwealth-funded Paid Parental Leave Scheme (the Scheme) in the Paid Parental Leave Act 2010 (PPLA 2010) will receive Paid Parental Leave Superannuation Contribution (PPL superannuation contribution) in respect of children born on or after 1 July 2025 (or regarded as having been born on or after this date for the purposes of PPLA 2010).

PLP recipients are not required to make a separate claim for PPL superannuation contribution which will be made annually to the superannuation funds of the PLP recipients from 1 July 2026.

The ATO will calculate and disburse the PPL superannuation contribution amount in accordance with PPLA 2010 based on information it receives from the government agency Services Australia (servicesaustralia.gov.au).

The Commissioner of Taxation has the general administration of Ch 3A, ie Ch 3A is "taxation law" within the meaning of ITAA97 and TAA. One consequence of this is that people who acquire information under Ch 3 are subject to the conﬁdentiality regime in TAA sch 1 Div 355.

- A person is eligible for a PPL superannuation contribution for an income year if the person

  receives one or more PPL funding amounts or instalments under the Scheme during the income

  year.

- The Commissioner will work out the PPL superannuation contribution amount payable to a

  person, based on the sum of all PPL funding amounts and instalments paid for the person during

  the income year multiplied by the SG charge percentage (PPLA 2010 s 115D). An interest

  component is added to account for the PPL superannuation contribution being paid at a later time

  than the PPL funding amounts or instalments.

- If the Commissioner makes a determination of the PPL superannuation contribution amount for a

  person for an income year, the Commissioner must determine whether that amount is to be paid:

– to the trustee of a complying superannuation fund or RSA providers for crediting to an account of the person within that fund or RSA. The Commissioner must also determine which particular account the PPL superannuation contribution is to be paid into – to the person's legal personal representative, or – into an account of the person in the Superannuation Holding Accounts Special Account (SHASA: ¶39-650) (PPLA 2010 s 115F).

- The Commissioner must pay a PPL superannuation contribution payable for a person for an

  income year on or before the PPL superannuation contribution payment date worked out in

  accordance with the PPL rules. The payment date for the PPL superannuation contribution may be

  a day in a later income year.

- If the Commissioner pays a PPL superannuation contribution for a person for an income year, the

Commissioner must give written notice, containing the information speciﬁed in PPLA 2010, to the person or the legal personal representative of a deceased person.

The person will also be given details of the contribution: (a) by the superannuation provider under the Corporations Regulations 2001 if the contribution is paid into a complying superannuation fund or an RSA account; and (b) by the Commissioner under the Small Superannuation Accounts Act 1995 if the contribution is paid into an account in the SHASA.

- If the PPL superannuation contribution for a person for an income year is paid to a

  superannuation fund or RSA, the Commissioner must give the trustee or provider the information

  prescribed by the PPL rules in relation to the contribution at the time the contribution is paid.

- A superannuation fund or RSA provider must return a PPL superannuation contribution or

  underpaid amount that cannot be credited to an account of the person.

- The Commissioner may recover any overpayment of PPL superannuation contribution. GIC is

  payable in some circumstances in relation to overpaid amounts.

- Superannuation providers (ie complying superannuation funds, RSAs and constitutionally

  protected funds) are required to keep and retain records in relation to transactions and acts

  engaged in, or required to be engaged in, under Ch 3A.

- Infringement notices can be given to superannuation providers for failing to give statements

  relating to returned PPL superannuation contributions or underpaid amounts.

- A person aﬀected by a decision (the original decision) made by the Commissioner under PPLA

2010 Pts 1, 2 or 3 (including a decision not to make a determination under s 115D or 115F (see above) or s 115K (about underpayments) of that Act) may apply to the Commissioner for review of the decision.

Components of a PPL superannuation contribution

- a core component, which is calculated by multiplying the total amount of PLP paid for the person

  in an income year by the SG rate (the SG charge percentage: ¶39-100) for that income year, and

- a nominal interest component, to address the foregone returns resulting from the annual payment

  of the PPL superannuation contribution.

The applicable SG rate is the rate as at 1 July of the income year that the PLP is paid. For example, the PPL superannuation contribution amount for a person for the ﬁnancial year 2025–26 will be the total amount of PLP paid for the person in that ﬁnancial year multiplied by 12% (the SG charge percentage on 1 July 2025).

and payments A PPL superannuation contribution is treated as a concessional contribution when it is received by a person's superannuation fund, in line with the treatment of employer SG contributions. This means that the contribution is subject to 15% concessional contributions tax in the fund and counts towards a person's concessional contributions cap in the year it is received by the fund.

A PPL superannuation contribution payment to the legal personal representative of a deceased person is a superannuation beneﬁt (ITAA97 s 307-5: ¶14-100). The superannuation beneﬁt will consist entirely of a taxable component, ie the tax free component is nil (ITAA97 s 307-133: ¶14-130).

In TAA s 8AAB(4) (index of laws dealing with liability to pay the GIC), table item 27A reﬂects that GIC is payable under s 115Q of PPLA 2010, and in TAA sch 1 s 250-10(2) (index of tax-related liabilities under other Acts), table items 39C to 39F reﬂect the tax-related liabilities arising under Ch 3A.

Part 12B of the Small Superannuation Accounts Act 1995 (¶39-650) empowers the Commissioner to deposit a PPL superannuation contribution payable for an individual into the individual's account in the SHASA, and provides for administration of these deposits and their eventual withdrawal.
