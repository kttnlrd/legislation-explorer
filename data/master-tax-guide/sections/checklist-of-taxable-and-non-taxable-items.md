# Checklist of taxable and non-taxable items ¶10-005

- the taxation of social security payments (¶10-195)

- the taxation of veterans' pensions and similar beneﬁts (¶10-200), and

- the taxation of superannuation beneﬁts and termination of employment payments (¶42-250 and

  following).
