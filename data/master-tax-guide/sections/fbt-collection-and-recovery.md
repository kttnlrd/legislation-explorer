# FBT collection and recovery ¶35-710

The mechanical provisions for the collection and recovery of FBT generally correspond to those applying to income tax (TAA sch 1 Pt 4-15: ¶25-510).

GIC (s 93; ¶29-510) applies to any late payment of tax and/or instalment of tax. If an underpayment occurs because of an amendment to an assessment, other than an amendment to correct a false or misleading statement or an amendment due to a tax avoidance arrangement, the per annum rate is the lower rate which applies to overpayments of tax (¶28-170). For the Commissioner's power to remit late payment penalties, see ¶29-530.
