# Franking surplus or deﬁcit ¶4-730

An entity's franking account is in surplus at a particular time to the extent that the sum of the franking credits in the account exceeds the sum of the franking debits in the account at that time.

An entity's franking account is in deﬁcit at a particular time to the extent that the sum of the franking debits in the account exceeds the sum of the franking credits in the account at that time (ITAA97 s 205-40).
