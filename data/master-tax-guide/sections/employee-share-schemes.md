# Employee share schemes ¶10-080

Special rules apply to the taxation of benefits in the form of discounts on shares and rights acquired under employee share schemes (ITAA97 Div 83A). There are also employee share scheme withholding tax provisions (TAA sch 1 Subdiv 14-C) and employee share scheme reporting requirements (TAA sch 1 Div 392) (¶10-090).

For guidance about the ATO's view on the criteria to be met by an employee share trust in order for the trust, its beneficiaries and the employer company to qualify for certain CGT and FBT concessions, see TD 2019/13.
