# Record-keeping by attributable taxpayers ¶21-230

A taxpayer whose assessable income includes an amount of attributable income from a CFC is required to maintain for 5 years records that show how the taxpayer came to be an attributable taxpayer, the basis on which the attributable income included in the taxpayer's assessable income was calculated, the aggregate of the taxpayer's indirect attribution interests in the CFC and the calculation of the taxpayer's attribution percentage in the CFC (ITAA36 ss 462 to 464A).
