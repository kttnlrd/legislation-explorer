# Non-assessability of windfall gains ¶10-842

Windfall gains such as gambling and lottery wins and competition prizes that are not won as part of the taxpayer's income-producing activities or business are generally not income and are therefore non- assessable (¶10-440). They are also ignored for CGT purposes under ITAA97 s 118-37(1)(c) (¶11-660).
