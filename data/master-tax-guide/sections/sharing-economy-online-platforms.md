# Sharing economy online platforms ¶33-210

The taxable payments reporting system (¶33-200) has been extended to require electronic platform operators to provide information to the ATO on transactions made through the platform where the supply is connected to Australia. However, there will generally be no requirement to report the transaction where:

- it relates to the supply of goods where the ownership permanently changes

- title to real property changes

- it is a financial supply

- it occurs within the same consolidated or MEC group, or

- it is subject to another reporting or withholding obligation and is reported to the ATO.

The reporting applies from 1 July 2023 for transactions in relation to the supply of taxi travel (as defined in the GST Act, which includes ride-sourcing) and short-term accommodation, and from 1 July 2024 for all other transactions.

The ATO currently uses its data matching programs to obtain information about the sharing economy.

Reporting exemptions In addition to the reporting exemptions discussed above, from 1 July 2024 the following exemptions apply (Legislative Instrument F2024L00622): Exemption for certain operators of electronic distribution platforms (EDPs): The operator of an EDP (the first platform) is not required to prepare and give a report about a reportable transaction if:

- the supply was made through the first platform operator and at least one other EDP interposed

  between the first platform and the supplier, and

- the first platform operator did not itself provide any consideration for the supply directly to the

  supplier, and

- the operator of another EDP provided all or part of the consideration for the supply to the supplier

  and has a reporting obligation in relation to the reportable transaction, and

- the first platform operator notified the Commissioner in writing that they will be applying the

  reporting exemption on or before the day the report would otherwise be due.

Exemption for transactions involving certain types of suppliers: The operator of an EDP is not required to prepare and give a report about a reportable transaction if:

- the supplier was a listed entity, or wholly-owned subsidiary of a listed entity; or a government

  department, agency, or authority (including an entity wholly-owned by a government), or

- the supplier made supplies in relation to a reporting period that are facilitated by the EDP of at

  least $1 million (including GST).

Exemption for transactions involving certain types of supplies: The operator of an EDP is not required to prepare and give a report about the following reportable transactions:

- Supplies of substantial properties: The transaction involved the provision of consideration relating

  to a substantial property. A substantial property is a property where, for a reporting period, at least

2,000 transactions were facilitated by the EDP in relation to that property in the 12 months ending on the last day of that reporting period.

- Supplies of certain services outside Australia: The transaction is a supply outside Australia;

  and the supplier has provided the operator with one or more addresses all of which are outside

Australia; and the consideration provided to the supplier in relation to the transaction was not paid to an account held with a financial institution in Australia; and there is no other information available to the operator that indicates that the supplier is a resident of Australia. economy online platforms

- Mere reservations or bookings: The EDP facilitates a mere booking or reservation but does not

  otherwise facilitate the supply. Transactions are exempt where the consideration for the supply

  was not specified at the time the booking or reservation was made; and the consideration for the

  supply will not be provided via the platform; and the operator of the platform will not have visibility of

  whether the supply is made in practice, or whether consideration is provided for it.

- Scheduled passenger travel services: The transaction involved the supply of a scheduled

  passenger travel service other than a charter service or taxi travel, where the supplier had made at

  least 10 places for that service available for booking on the platform.

- Scheduled events: The transaction involved the supply of a right to attend or participate in a

  scheduled event, where the supplier had made at least 200 places for that event available for

  booking on the platform.

- Permanent attractions and experiences: The transaction involved the supply of a right to attend or

  participate in a permanent attraction or experience, where the supplier had made at least 50 places

  for that attraction or experience available for booking on the platform each day it was open during

  the reporting period.

- Supplies of certain assets: The transaction involved the supply of an asset by way of rent or

  lease, other than real property, where the transaction was not for the supply of a specific asset;

  and the supplier had at least 50 assets available for booking on the platform each day during the

  reporting period.
