# Payments on termination of employment ¶14-600

The concessional taxation of payments made in consequence of the termination of employment is governed by ITAA97 Pt 2-40:

- Div 82 for "employment termination payments", as deﬁned in ITAA97 s 82-130, and

- Div 83 for unused annual leave payments and unused long service leave payments, genuine

  redundancy payments and early retirement scheme payments, and foreign termination payments

(¶14-700 – ¶14-740).

The phrase "in consequence of" as used in the provisions governing the above payments is discussed in ¶14-610.

For the purposes of Pt 2-40, termination of employment includes retirement from employment and the cessation of employment because of death. If a person holds or has held an oﬃce, Pt 2-40 applies to the person in the same way as it would apply if the person were or had been employed (ITAA97 ss 80-5 and 80-10).

A termination of employment payment covered by Pt 2-40 (except unused annual leave payments or unused long service leave payments) can be or include a transfer of property, in which case the amount of the payment is or includes the market value of the property (ITAA97 s 80-15).

Examples of payments in consequence of the termination of employment include a gratuity or "golden handshake", payments made for unused sick leave or unused rostered days oﬀ, payments in lieu of notice, compensation for loss of job or wrongful dismissal, invalidity payments, and genuine redundancy payments and early retirement scheme payments in ¶14-700 – ¶14-710.

The concessional taxation rules for employment termination payments are discussed in ¶14-610 – ¶14-680, and for genuine redundancy payments or early retirement scheme payments and unused annual leave or long service leave payments in ¶14-700 – ¶14-730.

Constructive receipt rule For the purposes of determining whether Div 82 or 83 applies to a payment and whether a payment mentioned in those Divisions is made to or received by a person, a payment is treated as being made to or received by the person if it is made for the person's beneﬁt or to another person or entity at the person's request or direction (ITAA97 s 80-20).

One eﬀect of the rule is that these payments cannot be rolled over to the superannuation system so as to defer its liability to tax at the time of payment, for example, where an employee requests that the payment be made directly to a superannuation fund.

A similar constructive receipt rule applies for the purposes of determining whether a payment is a superannuation beneﬁt and whether the beneﬁt is made to or received by the person (ITAA97 s 307-15).
