# Native title payments ¶10-187

An amount or benefit that an indigenous person or indigenous holding entity receives directly from entering into an agreement or as compensation under the Native Title Act 1993 (ie a native title benefit) is non- assessable non-exempt income (ITAA97 s 59-50). An indigenous holding entity includes distributing bodies, trusts and registered charities (ITAA97 s 59-50(6)).

*Refs: ITAA97 s 59-50), ITAA97 s 59-50(6))*
