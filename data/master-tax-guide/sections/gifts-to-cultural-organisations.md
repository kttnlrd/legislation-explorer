# Gifts to cultural organisations ¶16-957

Gifts of $2 or more made to the public gift fund of a cultural organisation that is a DGR will be tax-deductible if the organisation is a DGR when the gift is made (ss 30-15; 30-100(1)) or it is one of the specified organisations listed in s 30-100(2) (note that it is proposed to remove the $2 minimum requirement: ¶16-942). Prior to 1 January 2024, cultural organisations and their public fund had to be registered on the Register of Cultural Organisations, which was administered by the Department of Infrastructure, Transport, Regional Development, Communications and the Arts, to have their public fund endorsed as a DGR. From 1 January 2024, the ATO administers the DGR category for cultural organisations and assesses eligibility for endorsement (ATO website). Transitional provisions ensure that organisations included on the Register of Cultural Organisations continue to be endorsed if they continue to meet the eligibility criteria.

*Refs: s 30-15, s 30-100(2)*
