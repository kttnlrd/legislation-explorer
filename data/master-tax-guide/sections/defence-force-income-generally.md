# Defence Force income generally ¶10-750

Generally, persons serving with the Defence Forces and Reserves include the same items in income as do civilians. Hence amounts based on periods of service or special duty and comparable allowances are fully included in assessable income in the absence of specific statutory exclusions.

Similarly, the same deductions and rebates are available to service personnel as to civilians. The interaction between the rebate and exemption provisions for Defence Force personnel is discussed in TR 97/2. The estates of deceased service personnel are released from so much of any unpaid tax liability as is applicable to Defence Force pay and allowances (ITAA36 s 265A).
