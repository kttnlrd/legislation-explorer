# PAYG: certain labour mobility programs ¶26-275

An amount must be withheld from salary, wages, commission, bonuses or allowances paid to an individual as an employee of an approved employer under a labour mobility program covered by the legislation (see below) when the employee is a foreign resident who holds:

- a Temporary Work (International Relations) Visa (subclass 403)

- a Temporary Activity Visa (subclass 408) having previously held a Temporary Work (International

- a Special Program Visa (subclass 416).

- the Pacific Australia Labour Mobility scheme (from 1 July 2022), and

- each program prescribed by regulation (there are currently no such regulations).

The rate of tax payable under the Income Tax (Labour Mobility Program Withholding Tax) Act 2012 is 15% and so the amount to be withheld is 15% of the payment (TAR s 52). The employee is entitled to a credit for the amount withheld (TAA sch 1 s 18-33).
