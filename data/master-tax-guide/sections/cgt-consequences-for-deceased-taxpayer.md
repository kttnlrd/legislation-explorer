# CGT consequences for deceased taxpayer ¶12-570

The general rule is that, when a taxpayer dies, a capital gain or loss from a CGT event relating to a CGT asset the taxpayer owned just before death is disregarded (s 128-10). However, a capital gain or loss can still arise from CGT event K3 if the asset passes to a beneficiary that is a non-resident, an exempt entity or the trustee of a complying superannuation fund, complying ADF or PST (¶11-350).

A capital loss of a deceased taxpayer may be offset against any capital gain of the taxpayer in the final individual tax return. Any unrecouped net capital losses lapse altogether on death (TD 95/47).

For a checklist of the tax consequences of death, see ¶44-170.
