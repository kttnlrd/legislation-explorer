# Anti-avoidance provisions relevant to minors ¶2-190

Anti-avoidance provisions are designed to ensure that the income exclusions noted at ¶2-180 are not exploited.

First, where parties were not dealing with each other at arm's length in relation to the derivation of excluded income, the excluded income is limited to the amount that would have been derived if they had been at arm's length (ITAA36 s 102AE(6)).

Second, income is not treated as excluded income if it arises from an arrangement designed at least in part to secure that the income would qualify as excluded income (s 102AE(7), (8)).
