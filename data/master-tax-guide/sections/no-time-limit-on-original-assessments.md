# No time limit on original assessments ¶25-120

There is no time limit on making an original assessment. However, an individual taxpayer who has not received a notice of assessment within 12 months of ﬁling a return may request in writing that the Commissioner make an assessment (ITAA36 s 171). If an assessment notice is not received within 3 months of the Commissioner's receipt of that request, the taxpayer is regarded as having been served, on the last day of the 3-month period, with a notional original assessment notice payable on that day. Theoretically, any subsequent assessment is deemed to be an amended assessment, the validity of which will depend on the usual time limits for amended assessments (¶25-300).

Section 171 is not relevant to returns lodged by full self-assessment taxpayers (¶25-100) because such returns are themselves deemed to be notices of assessment.
