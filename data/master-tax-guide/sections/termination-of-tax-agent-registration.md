# Termination of tax agent registration ¶32-050

In addition to the power to terminate registration of a tax agent or BAS agent for a failure to comply with the Code of Professional Conduct (¶32-045), the TPB has power to terminate registration where an event listed at ¶32-025 occurs in relation to a registered individual, in relation to an individual partner of a registered partnership or in relation to a director of a registered company or of a company that is a partner in a registered partnership. Where such an event occurs in relation to a partner or a director, the TPB has the power to direct that the partner or director be removed.

The TPB may also terminate registration if the registered agent ceases to meet one of the "tax practitioner registration requirements" or breaches a condition of registration (TASA s 40-5). The "tax practitioner registration requirements" are the requirements as to which the TPB must be satisﬁed in order to grant registration.

Where the TPB terminates an agent's registration it may also determine a period (of not more than 5 years) during which registration may not be reapplied for (TASA s 40-25).

Where a registered agent surrenders his registration in writing the TPB does not have to cancel the registration if either: (a) the TPB considers that, due to a current investigation or the outcome of an investigation, it would be inappropriate to terminate the registration; or (b) the TPB, within 30 days after receiving the surrender notice, decides to investigate the agent and considers that it would be inappropriate to terminate the agent's registration (TASA ss 40-5(3); 40-10(2A); 40-15(2A)).

A decision of the TPB to terminate registration or to determine a period during which registration may not be reapplied for is reviewable by the ART.

There is an increasing number of cases where a tax agent seeking to challenge a termination of registration decision of the TPB has sought to have the TPB's termination decision stayed by the AAT (now the ART) until the hearing of the substantive application for the review of the TPB's termination decision (eg see Gao v Tax Practitioners Board [2019] AATA 3651; Le'Sam Accounting Pty Ltd and Tax Practitioners Board [2020] AATA 890 and Norman and Tax Practitioners Board [2020] AATA 640).

*Refs: s 40-5), s 40-25), s 40-5(3)*
