# Valuation of live stock natural increase ¶9-260

Livestock acquired by natural increase may be valued at cost, market selling value or replacement value (¶9-180). If live stock is valued at cost, the taxpayer can use actual cost or the cost prescribed by the regulations, regardless of which is lower (ITAA97 s 70-55). Actual cost is the full cost of bringing the live stock into its current state and location, including expenses such as veterinary fees, feed, fencing. A horse acquired by natural increase that is on hand at the end of an income year cannot be valued a less than the service fees incurred in breeding the horse (s 70-55(2)).
