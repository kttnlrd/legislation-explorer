# GST and charities and non-proﬁt bodies ¶34-175

Apart from their GST-free concessions (¶34-160), charities and non-proﬁt bodies will not have to register unless their GST turnover is $150,000 or more, and have the option of splitting their operations into separate independent branches for GST purposes.

Charities are also entitled to use the cash basis of accounting irrespective of GST turnover, and can take advantage of simpliﬁed accounting methods in relation to supplies of food (¶34-165), second-hand goods and other sales for nominal value. They may also opt to have speciﬁed fundraising events treated as input taxed (s 40-160). Non-proﬁt bodies that are voluntarily registered for GST have the option of reporting and paying their GST on an annual basis (Div 151).

*Refs: s 40-160)*
