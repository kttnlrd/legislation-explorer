# Obligation to keep records under PAYG ¶26-720

A payment summary given to an individual or any other entity must be retained for 5 years after the end of that year (TAA sch 1 s 18-100).

Each party must keep a copy of a voluntary agreement to withhold from when it is made until 5 years after the making of the last payment covered by the agreement (TAA sch 1 s 12-55).

A record-keeping checklist can be found at ¶44-100.
