# Digital games tax offset ¶20-400

The digital games tax offset (ITAA97 Div 378) provides a refundable income tax offset for companies that develop digital games in Australia. The offset applies to eligible expenditure incurred on or after 1 July 2022.

- have an ABN and be either an Australian tax resident or foreign tax resident with a permanent

  establishment in Australia

- incur a minimum of $500,000 qualifying Australian development expenditure (QADE) on

  developing an eligible digital game or games (¶20-455)

- have one or more certificates issued to the company by the Arts Minister following the completion

  of a new digital game, the porting of a digital game to a new platform, and/or for ongoing

  development of one or more existing digital games during an income year (¶20-445)

- claim the offset in its income tax return in respect of the income year, and

- lodge any required notice with the Commissioner (¶20-415).

The Income Tax Assessment (Digital Games Tax Offset) Rules 2023 (Legislative Instrument F2023L00992) provide for the way in which the Digital Games Tax Offset Advisory Board will issue provisional certificates and specify how applications for certificates are to be made. The Office for the Arts' Digital Games Tax Offset (DGTO) Guidelines provide guidance on preparing applications for certification.
