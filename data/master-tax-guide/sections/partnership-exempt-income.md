# Partnership exempt income ¶5-120

The exempt income of a partnership is calculated as if the partnership were a resident taxpayer (s 90). This means that exempt income from sources in and out of Australia is taken into account. If there is exempt income of a partnership and there is a partnership loss (¶5-110) for the year, the loss is not offset against the partnership exempt income. In the case of resident partners, the full amount of the partnership loss and the full amount of the partnership exempt income are distributed to the partners according to their individual interests in the loss and the exempt income. The adjustment offsetting a share in the partnership loss against a share in the partnership exempt income is made in the individual returns of the partners. For the position where a partner is a non-resident for the whole or a part of the year, see ¶5-150.

The non-assessable non-exempt income of a partnership (¶10-890) is also calculated as if the partnership were a resident taxpayer.
