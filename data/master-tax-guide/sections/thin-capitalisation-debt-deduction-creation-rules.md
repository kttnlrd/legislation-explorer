# Thin capitalisation: debt deduction creation rules ¶22-710

The debt deduction creation rules (DDCRs) in Subdiv 820-EAA address the risk of a taxpayer having excessive debt deductions for debts created in connection with an acquisition from an associate entity or distributions or payments to an associate entity for income years starting on or after 1 July 2024.

The DDCRs apply to "general class investors", "outward investing ﬁnancial entities (non-ADI)" and "inward investing ﬁnancial entities (non-ADI)" that are subject to Div 820 (¶22-700). The DDCRs do not apply to ADIs, Australian plantation forestry entities (¶22-700), "securitisation vehicles" as deﬁned in s 820-942(2) nor entities that have chosen to apply the "third party debt test" (¶22-700) for a relevant income year (s 820-423A).

820-423*

- an acquisition of CGT assets, or legal or equitable obligations, from "associate pairs" (see below)

  of the acquirer — if the conditions in s 820-423A(2) are satisﬁed, and

- a ﬁnancial arrangement that is entered into by an entity to fund, etc, certain payments or

  distributions to one or more associate pairs of the entity — if the conditions in s 820-423A(5) are

  satisﬁed.

Broadly, an entity is an "associate pair" of another entity if either the entity is an associate of the other entity, or the other entity is an associate of the entity (ITAA97 s 995-1(1) and s 820-423D).

An anti-avoidance rule in s 820-423D ensures Subdiv 820-EAA cannot be readily avoided.

Subdivision 820-EAA does not limit the application of other provisions in Div 820 to reduce (or further reduce) an entity's debt deductions (s 820-423C). A debt deduction that is disallowed under the DDCRs is disregarded for the purposes of applying the rules in Subdiv 820-AA for general class investors; Subdiv 820- B for outward investing ﬁnancial entities (non-ADI) and Subdiv 820-C for inward investing ﬁnancial entities (non-ADI) (¶22-700) (s 820-31).

*Refs: s 820-423, ITAA97 s 995-1(1), s 820-31)*
