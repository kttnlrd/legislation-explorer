# Improvements by lessee ¶10-505

The value of improvements effected on leasehold land by a lessee that, on termination of the lease, by effluxion of time or otherwise, eventually benefit the lessor, are generally capital and not assessable to the lessor as ordinary income. The relevant CGT provisions are considered at ¶12-680.
