# Effective life — Table A (industry items) ¶43-005

Table A is an industry table which contains assets under industry headings generally derived from the Australian and New Zealand Standard Industry Classification (ANZSIC) subject categories. This table may only be used by members of the specified industry. If an item is listed in both Table A and Table B, industry members must use the Table A rate and non-industry members must use the Table B rate (¶43-100). If an asset does not appear in either table, taxpayers must make their own determination of the effective life of the asset (¶17-270).

New and reviewed items have been marked with an asterisk (*) in the third column of the table. Where the Commissioner has determined effective lives for assets in excess of the statutory caps for those assets (¶43-105), they have been marked with a hash (#). Table A lists the effective life determinations made to date, with effect from 1 July 2022.
