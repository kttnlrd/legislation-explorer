# Liability of directors, etc ¶3-040

Although there must be a public officer, a notice or process may if the Commissioner thinks fit be given to, or served on, a company by giving the notice to, or serving the process on, a director, the secretary, another officer or an attorney or agent of the company (ITAA36 s 253). Note that there are special provisions that enable the Commissioner to recover from the directors of companies unpaid amounts under, for example, the PAYG withholding system (¶25-560). See also ¶29-710.
