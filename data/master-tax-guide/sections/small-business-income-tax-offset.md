# Small business income tax oﬀset ¶42-027

For 2024–25, individuals who receive business income from a small business entity (¶7-050), other than via a company, are entitled to a discount of 16% of the income tax payable on that business income up to a maximum of $1,000 a year. This applies for entities with an annual aggregated turnover of less than $5 million (¶7-210).
