# PAYG: First Home Super Saver released amounts ¶26-335

The Commissioner must withhold an amount from the First Home Super Saver (FHSS) released amounts paid in respect of a person (s 12-460). For details of the FHSS Scheme, see ¶13-790.

The amount to be withheld is: (a) the amount of tax that the Commissioner estimates will be payable by the individual in relation to the individual's assessable FHSS released amount for an income year; or (b) if the Commissioner is unable to make such an estimate — 17% of the individual's assessable FHSS released amount for an income year (TAR s 53A).

*Refs: s 12-460)*
