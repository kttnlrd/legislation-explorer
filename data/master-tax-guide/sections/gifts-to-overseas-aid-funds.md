# Gifts to overseas aid funds ¶16-955

Gifts of $2 or more to eligible overseas aid funds that are DGRs to provide relief for persons in developing countries qualify for deduction (ss 30-15; 30-80(1); 30-85; 30-86), as well as those specified in s 30-80(2) (note that it is proposed to remove the $2 minimum requirement: ¶16-942). Prior to 1 January 2024, an overseas aid organisation had to be admitted as an approved organisation under the Overseas Aid Gift Deduction Scheme administered by the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade and establish an overseas aid fund in order to have their public fund endorsed as a DGR. From 1 January 2024, the ATO administers the DGR category for overseas aid funds that are DGRs and assesses eligibility for a developing country relief organisation, or an organisation operating a developing country relief fund, to be endorsed as a DGR (ATO website). Transitional provisions ensure that organisations included under the Overseas Aid Gift Deduction Scheme continue to be endorsed if they continue to meet the eligibility criteria.

*Refs: s 30-15, s 30-80(2)*
