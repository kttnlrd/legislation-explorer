# Payroll tax: introduction ¶36-000

Payroll tax is a state and territory (state) impost controlled by 8 separate legislatures. Accordingly, the requirements, liabilities and rates of tax vary from state to state so that, for example, a particular allowance or beneﬁt that is subject to payroll tax in Victoria may well be exempt in Queensland (and vice versa).

The states have enacted legislation to have common provisions and deﬁnitions of a range of payroll tax arrangements while retaining individual control over rates and thresholds. The legislation in each state broadly provides for payroll tax to be levied on "wages", in cash or in kind, provided by employers to their employees. However, factors determining an employer's liability, which may diﬀer from state to state, include: the tax-exempt wage level threshold; any allowable deduction applying to the payroll tax threshold; and the applicable rate of tax.
