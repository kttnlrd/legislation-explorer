# Public officer of trust ¶6-050

A trust that does not have a resident trustee and that has a business in Australia or Australian source property income (except dividends, interest or royalties subject to withholding tax) is required to appoint a public officer to ensure that the trustee's taxation responsibilities are met (s 252A). The qualifications for the public officer are the same as for public officers generally (¶3-030). The Commissioner has power to exempt (conditionally or unconditionally) particular trusts from the requirement to appoint a public officer.

The appointment of a public officer must be made within 90 days after the earlier of: (a) the date on which any business was commenced to be carried on by the trust in Australia; or (b) the date on which the trust commences to derive relevant property income from Australian sources. Failure to appoint a public officer is a strict liability offence that may result in a fine not exceeding 1 penalty unit (¶29-000) for each day that an appointment is not duly made.
