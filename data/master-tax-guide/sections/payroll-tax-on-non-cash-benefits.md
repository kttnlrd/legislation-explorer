# Payroll tax on non-cash beneﬁts ¶36-080

The legislation of all of the states expressly provides for payroll tax to be levied on non-cash "beneﬁts" provided to employees by employers. However, certain beneﬁts have been prescribed to be not a fringe beneﬁt for payroll tax purposes.

The value of beneﬁts are to be calculated in accordance with the FBTAA.

Generally, the value of fringe beneﬁts for payroll tax purposes is determined by using the "fringe beneﬁts taxable amount" under FBTAA s 5B, ie the "grossed-up" component of taxable fringe beneﬁts is included in the payroll tax base.
