# Payroll tax on non-cash benefits ¶36-080

The legislation of all of the states expressly provides for payroll tax to be levied on non-cash "benefits" provided to employees by employers. However, certain benefits have been prescribed to be not a fringe benefit for payroll tax purposes.

The value of benefits are to be calculated in accordance with the FBTAA.

Generally, the value of fringe benefits for payroll tax purposes is determined by using the "fringe benefits taxable amount" under FBTAA s 5B, ie the "grossed-up" component of taxable fringe benefits is included in the payroll tax base.
