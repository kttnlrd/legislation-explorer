Clarifying rules about when an entity pays a PAYG instalment or pays income tax or diverted proﬁts tax are contained in ITAA97 s 205-20. Note that where a debit has not been made to an entity's franking account because a refund of income tax is attributable to the refund of a tax oﬀset which the entity is entitled to under ITAA97 Div 355 (research and development), a franking credit will not arise in respect of the payment of a PAYG instalment or income tax until these deferred franking debits are recovered (ITAA97 s 205-15).

Table item 4A was inserted to deal with the situation where a corporate tax entity's tax oﬀset refund (under the temporary loss carry back rules (¶3-080)) is subsequently reduced as a result of a change to the loss carry back choice. The entity's franking account balance is restored to the amount it would have been if the entity had received the correct amount of tax oﬀset refund in the ﬁrst instance.

Franking credits where tax liabilities partly paid PS LA 2001/13, which provided guidance as to how partial payments were allocated on running balance accounts, was withdrawn with eﬀect from 17 June 2021. For more information regarding the allocation of franking credits, the withdrawal notice refers readers to PS LA 2011/20 Payment and credit allocation.
