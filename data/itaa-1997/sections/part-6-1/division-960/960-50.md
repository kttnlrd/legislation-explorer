# 960-50  Translation of amounts into Australian currency

<a id="s960-50-1"></a>
**(1)** For the purposes of this Act, an amount in a *foreign currency is to be translated into Australian currency. Examples of an amount

<a id="s960-50-2"></a>
**(2)** The following are examples of an amount:

> <a id="s960-50-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** an amount of *ordinary income;

> <a id="s960-50-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** an amount of an expense;

> <a id="s960-50-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** an amount of an obligation;

> <a id="s960-50-2-d"></a>
> **(d)** an amount of a liability;

> <a id="s960-50-2-e"></a>
> **(e)** an amount of a receipt;

> <a id="s960-50-2-f"></a>
> **(f)** an amount of a payment;

> <a id="s960-50-2-g"></a>
> **(g)** an amount of consideration;

> <a id="s960-50-2-h"></a>
> **(h)** a value.

<a id="s960-50-3"></a>
**(3)** The amounts set out in paragraphs (2)(b) to (h) may be amounts on revenue account, capital account or otherwise. Amounts that are elements in the calculation of other amounts

<a id="s960-50-4"></a>
**(4)** In applying this section:

> <a id="s960-50-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** first, translate any amounts that are elements in the calculation of other amounts (except *special accrual amounts); and

> <a id="s960-50-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** then, calculate the other amounts. Special accrual amounts

<a id="s960-50-5"></a>
**(5)** In applying this section:

> <a id="s960-50-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** calculate a *special accrual amount without translation; and

> <a id="s960-50-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** then, translate the special accrual amount.

<a id="s960-50-7"></a>
**(7)** Subsection (6) has effect subject to any modifications made by the regulations.

<a id="s960-50-7A"></a>
**(7A)** Despite subsections (6) and (7), an amount that is relevant for the purposes of quantifying, for the purposes of section 960-565, the *annual global income of an entity as shown in *global financial statements for the entity is to be translated into Australian currency at the average exchange rate applicable for the period for which the statements are prepared.

<a id="s960-50-7B"></a>
**(7B)** For the purposes of subsection (7A):

> <a id="s960-50-7B-a"></a>
> **(a)** the entity must obtain:

> <a id="s960-50-7B-i"></a>
> **(i)** all of the exchange rates that it will use to work out the average exchange rate; or

> <a id="s960-50-7B-ii"></a>
> **(ii)** an average exchange rate that has been worked out for the period referred to in that subsection; from one or more sources that are not *associates of the entity, and not the entity itself, or from one or more sources specified by the Commissioner in a notice to the entity; and

> <a id="s960-50-7B-b"></a>
> **(b)** the entity must use the average exchange rate so worked out in translating into Australian currency any amount referred to in that subsection that is relevant to that period.

<a id="s960-50-7C"></a>
**(7C)** A notice under paragraph (7B)(a) is not a legislative instrument. Regulations about translation

<a id="s960-50-8"></a>
**(8)** An entity must comply with the regulations (if any) in translating an amount into Australian currency.

> **Note:** For example, the regulations could require the use of a particular translation method and require consistency in the use of the translation method.

<a id="s960-50-9"></a>
**(9)** Regulations made for the purposes of subsection (8) may make provision in relation to a matter by applying, adopting or incorporating (with or without modifications) matter contained in any of the *accounting standards:

> <a id="s960-50-9-a"></a>
> **(a)** as in force or existing at a particular time; or

(b) as in force or existing from time to time.

<a id="s960-50-9A"></a>
**(9A)** Regulations made for the purposes of subsection (8) do not apply to translating an amount into Australian currency under subsection (7A), unless they provide otherwise. Operation of certain provisions unaffected

<a id="s960-50-10"></a>
**(10)** This section does not affect the operation of the following provisions:

> <a id="s960-50-10-aa"></a>
> **(aa)** section 220-110 (*maximum franking credit);

> <a id="s960-50-10-a"></a>
> **(a)** section 775-210 (notional loans under *facility agreements);

> <a id="s960-50-10-b"></a>
> **(b)** Subdivision 960-D (functional currency);

> <a id="s960-50-10-c"></a>
> **(c)** subsection 974-35(6) (valuation of financial benefits for the purposes of the debt/equity provisions).
