# 960-285  Indexation—superannuation and employment termination

<a id="s960-285-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies in relation to the amounts listed at items 8 to 12 in section 960-265. Indexing amounts

<a id="s960-285-2"></a>
**(2)** You index the amount by:

> <a id="s960-285-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** first, multiplying its base amount mentioned in subsection (3) by its *indexation factor mentioned in subsection (5); and

> <a id="s960-285-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** next, rounding the result in paragraph (a) down to the nearest multiple of its *rounding amount.

> **Example:** An amount of $140,000 is to be indexed, with a rounding amount of $5,000. If the indexation factor increases this to an indexed amount of $143,000, the indexed amount is rounded back down to $140,000.

> **Example:** An amount of $140,000 is to be indexed, with a rounding amount of $5,000. If the indexation factor increases this to an indexed amount of $146,000, the indexed amount is rounded down to $145,000.

<a id="s960-285-3"></a>
**(3)** The amount (the base amount) for an amount to which this section applies is:

> <a id="s960-285-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** unless paragraph (b) or (c) applies—the amount for the 2007-2008 income year or *financial year; or

> <a id="s960-285-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** if the amount is mentioned in item 9 or 10A in section 960-265—the amount for the 2017-2018 financial year; or

> <a id="s960-285-3-c"></a>
> **(c)** if the amount is mentioned in item 10B or 10C in section 960-265—the amount for the 2026-27 income year.

<a id="s960-285-4"></a>
**(4)** You do not index the amount if the *indexation factor is 1 or less. Indexation factor

<a id="s960-285-5"></a>
**(5)** For indexing an amount, its indexation factor is: *Index number mentioned in subsection (7) for the *quarter ending on 31 December just before the start of the relevant income year or *financial year *Index number mentioned in subsection (7) for the base quarter where: base quarter means:

> <a id="s960-285-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** unless paragraph (b) or (c) applies—the quarter ending on 31 December 2006; or

> <a id="s960-285-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** if the amount is mentioned in item 9 or 10A in section 960-265—the quarter ending on 31 December 2016; or

> <a id="s960-285-5-c"></a>
> **(c)** if the amount is mentioned in item 10B or 10C in section 960-265—the quarter ending on 31 December 2025.

<a id="s960-285-6"></a>
**(6)** You work out the *indexation factor mentioned in subsection (5) to 3 decimal places (rounding up if the fourth decimal place is 5 or more). Index number and rounding amount

<a id="s960-285-7"></a>
**(7)** For indexing an amount to which this section applies:

> <a id="s960-285-7-a"></a>
> **(a)** the index number for a *quarter is set out in column 2 of the relevant item in the following table; and

> <a id="s960-285-7-b"></a>
> **(b)** the rounding amount is set out in column 3 of that item.
