# 960-290  Indexation—levy threshold for the major bank levy

<a id="s960-290-1"></a>
**(1)** You index, on a *quarterly basis, the amount mentioned in the provision listed at item 14 in section 960-265 by:

<a id="s960-290-1-a"></a>
**(a)** first, multiplying the amount by its *indexation factor mentioned in subsection (3); and

<a id="s960-290-1-b"></a>
**(b)** next, rounding the result in paragraph (a) down to the nearest multiple of $1,000,000.

<a id="s960-290-2"></a>
**(2)** You do not index the amount if the *indexation factor is 1 or less.

<a id="s960-290-3"></a>
**(3)** For indexation of the amount, the indexation factor is: GDP number for the preceding quarter GDP number for the base quarter where: GDP number for the base quarter is the estimate that is, at the end of the *quarter to which the indexation is to be applied, the estimate of the Gross Domestic Product: Current Prices-Seasonally General Division 960 Section 960-400 Adjusted most recently published by the Australian Statistician for the *quarter ending on 30 June 2017. GDP number for the preceding quarter is the estimate of the Gross Domestic Product: Current Prices-Seasonally Adjusted first published by the Australian Statistician for the *quarter preceding the quarter to which the indexation is to be applied.

<a id="s960-290-4"></a>
**(4)** You work out the *indexation factor mentioned in subsection (3) to 3 decimal places (rounding up if the fourth decimal place is 5 or more).
