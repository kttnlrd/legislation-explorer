# 974-160  Financial benefit

<a id="s974-160-1"></a>
**(1)** In this Act: financial benefit:

> <a id="s974-160-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** means anything of economic value; and

> <a id="s974-160-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** includes property and services; and

> <a id="s974-160-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** includes anything that regulations made for the purposes of subsection (3) provide is a financial benefit; even if the transaction that confers the benefit on an entity also imposes an obligation on the entity.

<a id="s974-160-2"></a>
**(2)** In applying subsection (1), benefits and obligations are to be looked at separately and not set off against each other.

<a id="s974-160-3"></a>
**(3)** The regulations may provide that a thing specified in the regulations is a financial benefit for the purposes of this Act.
