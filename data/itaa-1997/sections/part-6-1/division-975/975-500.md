# 975-500  Wholly-owned groups

Two companies are members of the same wholly-owned group if:

> <a id="s975-500-a"></a>
> **(a)** one of the companies is a *100% subsidiary of the other company; or

> <a id="s975-500-b"></a>
> **(b)** each of the companies is a *100% subsidiary of the same third company. Concepts about companies Division 975 Section 975-505
