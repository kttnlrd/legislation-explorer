# 975-160  When an entity has an associate-inclusive control interest

<a id="s975-160-1"></a>
**(1)** An entity has an associate-inclusive control interest in a company in the circumstances set out in Subdivision A of Division 3 of Part X of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936.

<a id="s975-160-2"></a>
**(2)** However, in working out whether an entity has an associate-inclusive control interest of a particular percentage for the purposes of section 975-155, there are these modifications to the way Part X of that Act operates:

<a id="s975-160-2-a"></a>
**(a)** that Part is applied to any company, including one acting as a trustee; and

<a id="s975-160-2-b"></a>
**(b)** subsection 349(4) applies in all cases in working out which entity holds a direct control interest or a control tracing interest equal to 100%; and

<a id="s975-160-2-c"></a>
**(c)** subsections 350(6) and (7) and 355(1) are ignored; and

<a id="s975-160-2-d"></a>
**(d)** despite subsection 352(2), an interposed entity may be taken into account in calculating an indirect control interest if the interposed entity is:

<a id="s975-160-2-d-i"></a>
**(i)** a company of which the first entity or an *associate is a controller; or

<a id="s975-160-2-d-ii"></a>
**(ii)** a partnership or a trust; and Concepts about companies Division 975 Section 975-300

<a id="s975-160-2-e"></a>
**(e)** section 354 applies as if it referred to partnerships rather than CFP's; and

<a id="s975-160-2-f"></a>
**(f)** section 355 applies as if it referred to trusts rather than CFT's.

**Note:** Part X of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 defines company to exclude a company in the capacity of a trustee.

**Note:** The terms direct control interest and control tracing interest are relevant to working out associate-inclusive control interests in a company: see sections 350, 351, 353, 354 and 355 of that Act.

**Note:** Under subsection 349(4) of that Act, if 2 or more entities would have a direct control interest or a control tracing interest in a company or trust equal to 100%, only one of them holds the interest.

**Note:** Subsections 350(6) and (7) of that Act deal with direct control interests in a company. They deal with interests held by Australian entities. Under subsection 355(1), certain entities are taken to hold a control tracing interest in a trust equal to 100%.

**Note:** Paragraphs (2)(d), (e) and (f) of this section are necessary because Part X of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 applies only to CFE's (which comprise CFC's, CFP's and CFT's).
