# 976-1  Franked part of a distribution

The franked part of a *distribution is an amount worked out using the formula: *Franking credit on the distribution ´ Applicable gross-up rate where: applicable gross-up rate means the *corporate tax gross-up rate of the entity making the distribution for the income year in which the distribution is made.
