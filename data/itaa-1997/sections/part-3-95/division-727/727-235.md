# 727-235 Services provided by gaining entity to losing entity for no more than a commercially realistic price

<a id="s727-235-1"></a>
**(1)** An *indirect value shift does not have consequences under this Division if:

<a id="s727-235-1-a"></a>
**(a)** there are *lesser benefits and, to the extent of at least 95% of their total *market value, the lesser benefits consist entirely of: arising from non-arm's length dealings Section 727-235

<a id="s727-235-1-a-i"></a>
**(i)** a right to have services that are covered by section 727-240 provided directly by the gaining entity to the losing entity; or

<a id="s727-235-1-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** services that are covered by section 727-240 and have been, are being, or are to be, so provided; or both; and

<a id="s727-235-1-b"></a>
**(b)** as at the *IVS time, the total market value of the greater benefits is not more than the total of:

<a id="s727-235-1-b-i"></a>
**(i)** the present value of the direct cost to the gaining entity of providing the services; and

<a id="s727-235-1-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** the present value of a reasonable allocation of the total direct cost to the gaining entity of providing services that include the first-mentioned services (so far as it is not already covered by subparagraph (i)); and

<a id="s727-235-1-b-iii"></a>
**(iii)** the present value of a reasonable allocation of the indirect cost to the gaining entity of providing the first-mentioned services; and

<a id="s727-235-1-b-iv"></a>
**(iv)** the mark-up worked out under subsection (2) or (3) of this section. To work out the costs and present values referred to in paragraph (1)(b), see section 727-245.

<a id="s727-235-2"></a>
**(2)** If it is reasonable to estimate that an entity providing the same quantity of services of the same kind in the same market would charge for them on the basis of a particular percentage mark-up, or on the basis of a percentage mark-up within a particular range, the mark-up for the purposes of subparagraph (1)(b)(iv) is: • the total of the respective present values of the costs mentioned in subparagraphs (1)(b)(i), (ii) and (iii); multiplied by: • that percentage mark-up, or the highest percentage in that range.

<a id="s727-235-3"></a>
**(3)** Otherwise, the mark-up for the purposes of subparagraph (1)(b)(iv) is 10% of the total of the respective present values of the costs mentioned in subparagraphs (1)(b)(i), (ii) and (iii).
