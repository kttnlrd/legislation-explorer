# 727-110 Common-ownership nexus test (if both losing and gaining entities are closely held)


<a id="s727-110-1"></a>
**(1)** Or, it must be the case that:

> <a id="s727-110-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** at some time during the *IVS period, neither the *losing entity nor the *gaining entity has 300 or more members (in the case of a company) or 300 or more beneficiaries (in the case of a trust); and

> <a id="s727-110-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the losing entity and the gaining entity have a *common-ownership nexus within the IVS period. For the concept of IVS period, see section 727-150. For the concept of common-ownership nexus, see section 727-400.

<a id="s727-110-2"></a>
**(2)** Section 124-810 (under which certain companies and trusts are not regarded as having 300 or more members or beneficiaries) also applies for the purposes of this Division. arising from non-arm’s length dealings Section 727-125

<a id="s727-110-3"></a>
**(3)** In addition, this Division applies to a *non-fixed trust as if it did not have 300 or more beneficiaries.
