# 727-450  What this Subdivision is about

This Subdivision tells you: •      which method to use to work out the consequences of an indirect value shift for equity or loan interests, and indirect equity or loan interests, in the losing entity and in the gaining entity; and •      which interests, and which owners, are affected.
