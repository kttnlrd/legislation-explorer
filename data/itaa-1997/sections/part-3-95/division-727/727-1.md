# 727-1 What this Division is about

If there is a net shift of value between 2 related entities because of a non-arm's length dealing, this Division:

> <a id="s727-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** prevents losses from arising, because of the value shift, on realisation of direct or indirect equity or loan interests in the losing entity; and

> <a id="s727-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** within limits, prevents gains from arising, because of the value shift, on realisation of direct or indirect equity or loan interests in the gaining entity. arising from non-arm's length dealings Section 727-5 However, it does so only for interests that are owned by entities involved in the value shift.
