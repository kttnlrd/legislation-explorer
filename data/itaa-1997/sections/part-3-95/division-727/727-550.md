# 727-550 Choosing the adjustable value method

<a id="s727-550-1"></a>
**(1)** This section sets out rules for:

> <a id="s727-550-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** choosing to use the *adjustable value method to work out the consequences of an *indirect value shift; or

> <a id="s727-550-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** choosing (when using the adjustable value method) not to work out on a *loss-focussed basis the reductions in the *adjustable values of *affected interests. Who makes the choice

<a id="s727-550-2"></a>
**(2)** The choice must be made in accordance with the table.

> <a id="s727-550-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** who is the sole *ultimate controller because of whom the conditions in section 727-105 (ultimate controller test) are satisfied; or

> <a id="s727-550-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** who would be that sole ultimate controller if sections 727-355 to 727-375 were applied ignoring that entity's *associates 3 Neither of items 1 and 2 applies jointly by the 2 or more *ultimate controllers because of whom the conditions in section 727-105 (ultimate controller test) are satisfied When choice must be made

<a id="s727-550-3"></a>
**(3)** The choice must be made within 2 years after the first *realisation event that happens to an *affected interest at or after the IVS time. Choice binds all affected owners

<a id="s727-550-4"></a>
**(4)** The choice binds all *affected owners for the *indirect value shift. arising from non-arm's length dealings Section 727-555
