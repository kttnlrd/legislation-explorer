# 112-185  Method for apportioning capital gains and losses between realisation events and earlier deemed CGT events

<a id="s112-185-1"></a>
**(1)** The Minister may, by legislative instrument, determine a method for apportioning *capital gains and *capital losses between:

> <a id="s112-185-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** *realisation events happening on or after 1 July 2027 in relation to *CGT assets; and

> <a id="s112-185-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** earlier *CGT events relating to those CGT assets that are taken to have happened under subsection 112-155(2), 112-165(2) or 112-175(2).

<a id="s112-185-2"></a>
**(2)** For such a *CGT asset, such a method must take into account:

> <a id="s112-185-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** the *acquisition of the *CGT asset that is taken to have happened under paragraph 112-155(2)(b), 112-165(2)(b) or 112-175(2)(b); and

> <a id="s112-185-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** any expenditure (including indexation) in an element of the *cost base or *reduced cost base of the CGT asset on or after 1 July 2027; and

> <a id="s112-185-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** any other matter the Minister considers relevant.

<a id="s112-185-3"></a>
**(3)** For such a *CGT asset, such a method must enable the following to be worked out:

> <a id="s112-185-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** the *capital proceeds for the sale of the CGT asset that is taken to have happened under paragraph 112-155(2)(a), 112-165(2)(a) or 112-175(2)(a);

> <a id="s112-185-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** the *cost base and *reduced cost base of the CGT asset when it is taken to have been *acquired under paragraph 112-155(2)(b), 112-165(2)(b) or 112-175(2)(b). The method may also enable other things to be worked out.
