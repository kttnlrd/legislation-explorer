# 112-140  What is a same-asset roll-over?

A same-asset roll-over allows one entity (the transferor) to disregard a capital gain or loss it makes from disposing of a CGT asset to, or creating a CGT asset in, another entity (the transferee). Any gain or loss is deferred until another CGT event happens in relation to the asset (in the hands of the transferee). All same-asset roll-overs are set out in the table in section 112-150.
