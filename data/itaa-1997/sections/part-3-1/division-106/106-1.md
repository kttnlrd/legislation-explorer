# 106-1  What this Division is about

This Division sets out the cases where a capital gain or loss is made by someone other than the entity to which a CGT event happens. The entities affected are: •      partnerships (Subdivision 106-A); •      bankruptcy trustees and company liquidators (Subdivision 106-B); •      trustees where there is an absolutely entitled beneficiary (Subdivision 106-C); •      security holders (Subdivision 106-D).
