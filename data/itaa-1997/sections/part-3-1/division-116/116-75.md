# 116-75  Special rule for CGT event happening to a lease

The *capital proceeds from the expiry, surrender or forfeiture of a lease include any payment (because of the lease ending) by the lessor to the lessee for expenditure of a capital nature incurred by the lessee in making improvements to the leased property. The payment or expenditure can include giving property: see section 103-5.
