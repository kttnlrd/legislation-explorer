# 116-50  Repaid rule: modification 4


<a id="s116-50-1"></a>
**(1)** The *capital proceeds from a *CGT event are reduced by:

> <a id="s116-50-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** any part of them that you repay; or

> <a id="s116-50-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** any compensation you pay that can reasonably be regarded as a repayment of part of them. However, the capital proceeds are not reduced by any part of the payment that you can deduct.

> **Example:** You sell a block of land for $50,000 (the capital proceeds). The purchaser later finds out that you misrepresented a term in the contract. The purchaser sues you and the court orders you to pay $10,000 in damages to the purchaser. The capital proceeds are reduced by $10,000.

<a id="s116-50-2"></a>
**(2)** The payment can include giving property: see section 103-5.
