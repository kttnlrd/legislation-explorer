# 116-105  Conservation covenants

If *CGT event D4 happens because you enter into a *conservation covenant over land you own and you can deduct an amount under proceeds from the event are the amount you can deduct.

> **Note:** To get a deduction under Division 31, you must not receive money, property or other material benefit for entering into the covenant.
