# 116-20  General rules about capital proceeds

<a id="s116-20-1"></a>
**(1)** The capital proceeds from a *CGT event are the total of:

> <a id="s116-20-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the money you have received, or are entitled to receive, in respect of the event happening; and

> <a id="s116-20-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the *market value of any other property you have received, or are entitled to receive, in respect of the event happening (worked out as at the time of the event).

> **Note:** The timing rules for each event are in Division 104.

> **Note:** In some situations you are treated as having received money or other property, or being entitled to receive it: see section 103-10.

> **Note:** If you dispose of shares in a buy-back, the capital proceeds are worked out under Division 16K of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936.

<a id="s116-20-2"></a>
**(2)** This table sets out what the capital proceeds from *CGT events F1, F2, H2 and K9 are:

| number | event: | The capital proceeds are: |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | Granting, renewing or extending a lease | Any premium paid or payable to you for the grant, renewal or extension |
| F2 | Granting, renewing or extending a long-term lease | The greatest of: (a) the *market value of the estate in fee simple or head lease (worked out when you grant, renew or extend the lease); and |

> <a id="s116-20-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** what would have been that market value if you had not granted, renewed or extended the lease; and

| H2 | Receipt for event | The money or other consideration you received, |
| --- | --- | --- |
| K9 | Entitlement to receive payment of a *carried interest | The amount of the payment, to the extent that it is a payment of the *carried interest |

<a id="s116-20-3"></a>
**(3)** In working out the *market value of the property the subject of the grant, renewal or extension of a long-term lease:

> <a id="s116-20-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** include the market value of any building, part of a building, structure or improvement that is treated as a separate *CGT asset from the property; and

> <a id="s116-20-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** disregard any *depreciating assets for whose decline in value the lessor has deducted or can deduct an amount under this Act.

> **Note:** Subdivision 108-D sets out when a building, structure or improvement is treated as a separate CGT asset.

<a id="s116-20-4"></a>
**(4)** In working out the amount of any premium paid or payable to the lessor for the grant, renewal or extension of a long-term lease, disregard any part of it that is attributable to a *depreciating asset of that kind. The payment of any premium can include giving property: see section 103-5.

<a id="s116-20-5"></a>
**(5)** In working out the proceeds of a *CGT event that is a *supply, disregard the amount of your *net GST (if any) on the supply. Modifications to general rules
