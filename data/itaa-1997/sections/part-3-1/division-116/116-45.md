# 116-45  Non-receipt rule: modification 3


<a id="s116-45-1"></a>
**(1)**  The *capital proceeds from a *CGT event are reduced if:

> <a id="s116-45-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you are not likely to receive some or all (the unpaid amount) of those proceeds; and

> <a id="s116-45-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  this is not because of anything you (or your *associate) have done or omitted to do; and

> <a id="s116-45-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  you took all reasonable steps to get the unpaid amount paid. The capital proceeds are reduced by the unpaid amount.

> **Note:** This rule exists because the general rules treat you as having received an amount when you are entitled to receive it. Example    You sell a painting to another entity for $5,000 (the capital proceeds). You agree to accept monthly instalments of $100. You receive $2,000, but then the other entity stops making payments. It becomes clear that you are not likely to receive the remaining $3,000. The capital proceeds are reduced to $2,000.

<a id="s116-45-2"></a>
**(2)**  There is a further consequence if:

> <a id="s116-45-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  those proceeds are reduced by the unpaid amount; but

> <a id="s116-45-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you later receive a part of that amount. Those proceeds are increased by that part.

<a id="s116-45-3"></a>
**(3)**  This Part and Part 3-3 apply to the debt owed to you (the unpaid amount) as if it were not a *CGT asset.
