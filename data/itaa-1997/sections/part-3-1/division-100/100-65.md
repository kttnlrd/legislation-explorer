# 100-65  What records?

Keeping full records will make it easier for you to comply. For example, keep records of: • receipts of purchase or transfer; • interest on money you borrowed; • costs of agents, accountants, legal, advertising etc.; • insurance costs and land rates or taxes; • any market valuations; • costs of maintenance, repairs or modifications; • brokerage on shares; • legal costs.
