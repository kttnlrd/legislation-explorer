# 121-25  How long you must retain the records


<a id="s121-25-1"></a>
**(1)** You must retain records that section 121-20 requires you to keep.

<a id="s121-25-2"></a>
**(2)** You must retain them until the end of 5 years after it becomes certain that no *CGT event (or no further *CGT event) can happen such that the records could reasonably be expected to be relevant to working out whether you have made a *capital gain or *capital loss from the event. (2A) An offence under this section is an offence of strict liability.

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code.

<a id="s121-25-3"></a>
**(3)** This section has effect despite subsection 262A(4) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 (which requires records to be retained for a different period).

<a id="s121-25-4"></a>
**(4)** However, it is not necessary to retain records:

> <a id="s121-25-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** if the Commissioner notifies you that you do not need to retain them; or

> <a id="s121-25-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** for a company that has finally ceased to exist.

> **Note:** There are special record keeping rules where there has been a roll-over for a merger between superannuation funds under former section 160ZZPI of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936: see section 121-25 of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997. Penalty: 30 penalty units.

> **Note:** See section 4AA of the Crimes Act 1914 for the current value of a penalty unit.
