# 114-25  Residency requirements for individuals for indexation to be included in a cost base under subsection 110-36(1A)

<a id="s114-25-1"></a>
**(1)** This section sets out requirements for indexation to be included under subsection 110-36(1A) in the *cost base of a *CGT asset for the purposes of working out your *capital gain from a *CGT event happening in relation to the *CGT asset if:

> <a id="s114-25-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** you are an individual; and

> <a id="s114-25-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the CGT event happened while you were holding the CGT asset (as a result of earlier *acquiring it).

> **Note:** This section applies for working out a capital gain you make from directly holding the asset. A similar result arises for any capital gain you make indirectly as a beneficiary of a trust (see Subdivision 115-C, in particular subsections 115-225(4) and (5)).

<a id="s114-25-2"></a>
**(2)** You must be neither a foreign resident nor a *temporary resident at any time during the period (the testing period):

> <a id="s114-25-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** starting on the later of 1 July 2027 and the day of *acquiring the *CGT asset; and

> <a id="s114-25-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** ending on the day the *CGT event happens.

<a id="s114-25-3"></a>
**(3)** For the purposes of subsection (2), if:

> <a id="s114-25-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** another individual owned the *CGT asset on a particular day before the testing period ends; and

> <a id="s114-25-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** on that day, that individual was one of the following (that individual's residency status):

> > <a id="s114-25-3-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** an Australian resident (but not a *temporary resident);

> > <a id="s114-25-3-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** a temporary resident;

> > <a id="s114-25-3-b-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)** a foreign resident; and

> <a id="s114-25-3-c"></a>
> **(c)** section 115-30 treats you as having *acquired the CGT asset when that individual, or an earlier owner of the CGT asset, acquired it; you are treated as having the same residency status on that day as that individual had on that day.

<a id="s114-25-4"></a>
**(4)** Section 115-30 applies to subsections (2) and (3) of this section in a corresponding way to the way that section applies in relation to section 115-105.

> **Note:** Section 115-30 has special rules about when assets are acquired.
