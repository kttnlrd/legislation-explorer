# 118-240  What the following provisions are about

You can ignore a capital gain or capital loss you make from a compulsory acquisition (or similar arrangement) that happens only to land that is adjacent to:

> <a id="s118-240-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a dwelling that is your main residence; or

> <a id="s118-240-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a dwelling that passed to you as a beneficiary, or trustee, of a deceased estate; to the extent that the land was used primarily for private or domestic purposes in association with the dwelling. There is a limit on the maximum area of land covered by the exemption.

> **Note:** The exemption may not apply in full if the dwelling:

> <a id="s118-240-a"></a>
> **(a)**  was not always a main residence; or

> <a id="s118-240-b"></a>
> **(b)**  was used for the purpose of producing assessable income.

> **Note:** The exemption may not apply at all if you are a foreign resident.
