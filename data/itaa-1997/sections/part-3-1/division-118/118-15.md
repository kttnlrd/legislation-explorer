# 118-15  Registered emissions units


<a id="s118-15-1"></a>
**(1)**  A *capital gain or *capital loss you make from a *registered emissions unit is disregarded.

<a id="s118-15-3"></a>
**(3)**  A *capital gain or *capital loss you make from a right to receive an *Australian carbon credit unit is disregarded. Anti-overlap provisions
