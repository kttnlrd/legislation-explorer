# 118-42  Transfer of stratum units

If:

> <a id="s118-42-a"></a>
> **(a)**  you own land on which there is a building; and

> <a id="s118-42-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you subdivide the building into *stratum units; and

> <a id="s118-42-c"></a>
> **(c)**  you transfer each unit to the entity who had the right to occupy it just before the subdivision; a capital gain or *capital loss you make from transferring the unit * is disregarded.
