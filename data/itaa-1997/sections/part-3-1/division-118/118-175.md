# 118-175  Dependent child having different main residence

If, at a particular time, a *dwelling is your main residence and another *dwelling is the main residence of a *child of yours who is under 18 and is dependent on you for economic support, you must choose one of them as the main residence of both of you. Roll-overs under Subdivision 126-A
