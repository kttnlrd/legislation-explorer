# 115-235  Giving information to beneficiaries

<a id="s115-235-1"></a>
**(1)** If a trust is covered by a determination under subsection (4), the trustee must:

> <a id="s115-235-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** prepare a statement for each beneficiary of the trust who, because of subsection 115-215(3), makes one or more *capital gains for an income year in relation to capital gains of the trust estate for the income year; and

> <a id="s115-235-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** ensure the statement complies with subsection (2); and

> <a id="s115-235-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** give the statement to the beneficiary within the period specified in, or worked out under, the determination.

> **Note:** Section 286-75 in Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953 provides an administrative penalty for breach of this subsection.

<a id="s115-235-2"></a>
**(2)** The statement complies with this subsection if it:

> <a id="s115-235-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** is in the *approved form; and

> <a id="s115-235-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** states the amount of each of those *capital gains that the beneficiary makes; and

> <a id="s115-235-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** sets out which of those capital gains are *non-residential capital gains, *residential capital gains, *deferred non-residential capital gains or *deferred residential capital gains; and

> <a id="s115-235-2-d"></a>
> **(d)** sets out the effect of subsections 115-215(4), and 115-225(5) if applicable, in relation to each of those capital gains.

> **Note:** Subsection 115-215(4) deals with the status of capital gains as discount capital gains, and the effect of Subdivision 152-C having been applied to the trust gain. Subsection 115-225(5) makes adjustments to remove the effect of indexation on the cost bases of the relevant CGT assets.

<a id="s115-235-3"></a>
**(3)** A trustee of a trust is not required to prepare and give a statement under subsection (1) for an income year if a report is given, or required to be given, under section 393-10 in Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953 in respect of the trust estate for the *financial year that corresponds to the income year.

<a id="s115-235-4"></a>
**(4)** The Commissioner may, by legislative instrument, determine that trustees of all or specified kinds of trusts are required:

> <a id="s115-235-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** to prepare and give statements under subsection (1) to beneficiaries of the trusts; and

> <a id="s115-235-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** to do so before the end of:

> > <a id="s115-235-4-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** a specified period; or

> > <a id="s115-235-4-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** a period worked out using a specified method.
