# 115-102  New residential dwellings

<a id="s115-102-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies to a *discount capital gain if:

> <a id="s115-102-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** you are an individual; and

> <a id="s115-102-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** neither section 115-105 nor 115-110 (about foreign or temporary residents) applies to the discount capital gain; and

> <a id="s115-102-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** either:

> > <a id="s115-102-1-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)** you make the discount capital gain from a *CGT event happening on or after 1 July 2027 in relation to a *CGT asset covered by subsection (2); or

> > <a id="s115-102-1-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** because of section 115-215, Division 102 applies to you as if you had made the discount capital gain for a *capital gain of a trust covered by subsection (4); and

> <a id="s115-102-1-d"></a>
> **(d)** where subparagraph (c)(ii) applies—the trust's capital gain was made directly, or indirectly through one or more entities that are all covered by subsection (4), from a CGT event happening on or after 1 July 2027 in relation to a CGT asset covered by subsection (2).

> **Note:** The discount percentage for the discount capital gain is 50% (see paragraph 115-100(a)). CGT assets to which this section relates

<a id="s115-102-2"></a>
**(2)** This subsection covers a *new residential dwelling. Relevant trusts and interposed entities

<a id="s115-102-4"></a>
**(4)** This subsection covers the following:

> <a id="s115-102-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** a trust, other than a *superannuation fund or a public unit trust (within the meaning of section 102P of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936);

> <a id="s115-102-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** a partnership.

> **Note:** For paragraph (a), a trust includes a managed investment trust. Indexation may be able to be chosen as an alternative

<a id="s115-102-5"></a>
**(5)** Despite subsection (1), the following person may choose for this section not to apply to the *discount capital gain:

> <a id="s115-102-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** if you make the discount capital gain in the way described in subparagraph (1)(c)(i)—you;

> <a id="s115-102-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** if you make the discount capital gain in the way described in subparagraph (1)(c)(ii)—the trustee of the trust. Such a choice by the trustee applies to you in a similar way to a choice made by you for a gain covered by paragraph (a).

> **Note:** Instead, the cost base of the CGT asset may be able to be indexed (see subsection 110-36(1A)).
