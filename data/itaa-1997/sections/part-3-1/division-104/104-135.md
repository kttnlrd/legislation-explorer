# 104-135 Capital payment for shares: CGT event G1

<a id="s104-135-1"></a>
**(1)** CGT event G1 happens if:

> <a id="s104-135-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** a company makes a payment to you in respect of a *share you own in the company (except for *CGT event A1 or C2 happening in relation to the share); and

> <a id="s104-135-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** some or all of the payment (the non-assessable part) is not a *dividend, or an amount that is taken to be a dividend under section 47 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936; and

> <a id="s104-135-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** the payment is not included in your assessable income. The payment can include giving property: see section 103-5. (1A) In working out the non-assessable part, disregard any part of the payment that is:

> <a id="s104-135-1-aa"></a>
> **(aa)** *non-assessable non-exempt income; or

> <a id="s104-135-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** repaid by you; or

> <a id="s104-135-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** compensation you paid that can reasonably be regarded as a repayment of all or part of the payment; or

> <a id="s104-135-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** an amount referred to in section 152-125 (which exempts a payment of a small business 15-year exemption amount) as an exempt amount. The payment can include giving property: see section 103-5. (1B) However, the non-assessable part is not reduced by any part of the payment that you can deduct.

<a id="s104-135-2"></a>
**(2)** The time of the event is when the company makes the payment.

<a id="s104-135-3"></a>
**(3)** You make a capital gain if the amount of the non-assessable part is more than the *share's *cost base. If you make a *capital gain, the share's *cost base and *reduced cost base are reduced to nil.

> **Note:** You cannot make a capital loss.

> **Note:** A capital gain under former section 160ZL of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 is also taken into account for the purposes of this subsection: see section 104-135 of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997.

<a id="s104-135-4"></a>
**(4)** However, if the amount of the non-assessable part is not more than the *share's *cost base, that cost base and its *reduced cost base are reduced by the amount of the non-assessable part.

> **Note:** Cost base adjustments are made only under Subdivision 125-B if there is a roll-over under that Subdivision for CGT event G1 happening as a result of a demerger. Exceptions

<a id="s104-135-5"></a>
**(5)** A *capital gain you make is disregarded if you *acquired the *CGT asset that is the *share before 20 September 1985.

<a id="s104-135-6"></a>
**(6)** You disregard a payment by a liquidator for the purposes of this section if the company ceases to exist within 18 months of the payment.

> **Note:** The payment will be part of your capital proceeds for CGT event C2 happening when the share ends.

<a id="s104-135-7"></a>
**(7)** You also disregard a payment that is *personal services income included in your assessable income, or another entity's assessable income, under section 86-15.
