# 104-220  CGT asset starts being trading stock: CGT event K4


<a id="s104-220-1"></a>
**(1)** CGT event K4 happens if:

<a id="s104-220-1-a"></a>
**(a)** you start holding as *trading stock a *CGT asset you already own but do not hold as trading stock; and

<a id="s104-220-1-b"></a>
**(b)** you elect under paragraph 70-30(1)(a) to be treated as having sold the asset for its *market value.

**Note:** Paragraph 70-30(1)(a) allows you to elect the cost of the asset, or its market value, just before it became trading stock.

**Note:** There is an exemption if you elect its cost: see section 118-25.

<a id="s104-220-2"></a>
**(2)** The time of the event is when you start.

<a id="s104-220-3"></a>
**(3)** You make a capital gain if the asset's *market value (just before it became *trading stock) is more than its *cost base. You make a capital loss if that market value is less than its *reduced cost base. Exception

<a id="s104-220-4"></a>
**(4)** A *capital gain or *capital loss you make is disregarded if you *acquired the asset before 20 September 1985.
