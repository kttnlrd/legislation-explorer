# 104-107D  AMIT cost base reduction amount

<a id="s104-107D-1"></a>
**(1)** The *CGT asset's AMIT cost base reduction amount for the income year is the total of:

> <a id="s104-107D-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** money, and the *market value of any property, if:

> > <a id="s104-107D-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** you start to have a right to receive the money or property from the trustee of the *AMIT in the income year; and

> > <a id="s104-107D-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** that right is indefeasible (disregarding section 276-55) or is reasonably likely not to be defeated; and

> <a id="s104-107D-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** all amounts of *tax offset that you have for the income year in respect of the AMIT because of the operation of section 276-80; to the extent that the total is reasonably attributable to the CGT asset.

<a id="s104-107D-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

> <a id="s104-107D-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** *CGT event A1, C2, E1, E2, E6 or E7 happens to the *CGT asset before the end of the income year; and

> <a id="s104-107D-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** as a result, the time of the reduction or increase mentioned in subsection 104-107B(4) is just before the time of that CGT event; do not include in the CGT asset's AMIT cost base reduction amount for the income year any *capital proceeds from that CGT event.
