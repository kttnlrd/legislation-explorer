# 52-15  Supplementary amounts of payments

You work out the supplementary amount of a social security payment using the following table:

**Supplementary amount of a social security payment**

| Item | For this category of social | the supplementary amount is the |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Age pension Carer payment Special benefit | (a) so much of the payment as is included by way of rent assistance; and |

> <a id="s52-15-b"></a>
> **(b)** so much of the payment as is Special needs age pension                     included by way of remote area Special needs disability support              allowance; and pension                                   (c) so much of the payment as is Special needs wife pension                    included by way of pharmaceutical allowance; and

> <a id="s52-15-d"></a>
> **(d)** so much of the payment as is included by way of tax-exempt pension supplement; and

> <a id="s52-15-e"></a>
> **(e)** so much of the payment as is included by way of energy supplement
