# 52-65 How much of a veterans' affairs payment is exempt?


<a id="s52-65-1"></a>
**(1)** The table in this section tells you about the income tax treatment of veterans' affairs payments, other than:

> <a id="s52-65-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** payments of pension bonus or pension bonus bereavement payment; or

> <a id="s52-65-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** clean energy payments; or

> <a id="s52-65-1-ba"></a>
> **(ba)** clean energy payments under the scheme prepared under Part VII (about educating veterans' children) of the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986; or

> <a id="s52-65-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** one-off energy assistance payments under the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986; or

> <a id="s52-65-1-d"></a>
> **(d)** first 2020 economic support payments under the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986; or

> <a id="s52-65-1-da"></a>
> **(da)** second 2020 economic support payments under the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986; or

> <a id="s52-65-1-db"></a>
> **(db)** payments of additional economic support payment 2020 under the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986; or

> <a id="s52-65-1-dc"></a>
> **(dc)** payments of additional economic support payment 2021 under the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986; or

> <a id="s52-65-1-e"></a>
> **(e)** a prisoner of war recognition supplement under Part VIB of the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986; or

> <a id="s52-65-1-f"></a>
> **(f)** a 2022 cost of living payment under the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986.

> **Note:** Section 52-75 sets out the provisions of the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986 under which the payments are made. (1A) Payments of pension bonus and pension bonus bereavement payment under Part IIIAB of the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986 are exempt from income tax. (1E) A lump sum payment under section 198N of the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986 is exempt from income tax. (1F) A prisoner of war recognition supplement under Part VIB of the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986 is exempt from income tax. (1G) The following are exempt from income tax:

> <a id="s52-65-1-a-2"></a>
> **(a)** clean energy payments under the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986;

> <a id="s52-65-1-b-2"></a>
> **(b)** clean energy payments under the scheme prepared under Part VII (about educating veterans' children) of that Act.

> **Note:** The supplementary amount of each other payment under the scheme mentioned in paragraph (b) is also exempt from income tax (see section 52-140). (1GA) One-off energy assistance payments under Part IIIF of the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986 are exempt from income tax. (1H) One-off energy assistance payments under Part IIIG of the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986 are exempt from income tax. (1J) The following payments are exempt from income tax:

> <a id="s52-65-1-a-3"></a>
> **(a)** first 2020 economic support payments under Division 1 of Part IIIH of the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986;

> <a id="s52-65-1-b-3"></a>
> **(b)** second 2020 economic support payments under Division 2 of Part IIIH of the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986. (1K) The following payments are exempt from income tax:

> <a id="s52-65-1-a-4"></a>
> **(a)** additional economic support payment 2020 under Division 1 of Part IIIJ of the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986;

> <a id="s52-65-1-b-4"></a>
> **(b)** additional economic support payment 2021 under Division 2 of Part IIIJ of the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986. (1L) 2022 cost of living payment under Division 1 of Part IIIK of the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986 is exempt from income tax.

<a id="s52-65-2"></a>
**(2)** Expressions (except "pension age") used in this Subdivision that are also used in the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986 have the same meaning as in that Act.

> **Note:** Pension age has the meaning given by subsection 23(1) of the Social Security Act 1991: see subsection 995-1(1).
