# 50-50  Special conditions for item 1.1

<a id="s50-50-1"></a>
**(1)** An entity covered by item 1.1 is not exempt from income tax unless the entity:

> <a id="s50-50-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** has a physical presence in Australia and, to that extent, incurs its expenditure and pursues its objectives principally in Australia; or

> <a id="s50-50-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** is an institution that meets the description and requirements in item 1 of the table in section 30-15; or

> <a id="s50-50-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** is a prescribed institution which is located outside Australia and is exempt from income tax in the country in which it is resident; or

> <a id="s50-50-1-d"></a>
> **(d)** is a prescribed institution that has a physical presence in Australia but which incurs its expenditure and pursues its objectives principally outside Australia; and the entity satisfies the conditions in subsection (2).

> **Note:** Certain distributions may be disregarded: see section 50-75.

> **Note:** The entity must also meet other conditions to be exempt from income tax: see section 50-52.

<a id="s50-50-2"></a>
**(2)** The entity must:

> <a id="s50-50-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** comply with all the substantive requirements in its governing rules; and

> <a id="s50-50-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** apply its income and assets solely for the purpose for which the entity is established.
