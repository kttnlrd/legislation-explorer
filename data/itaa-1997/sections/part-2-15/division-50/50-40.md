# 50-40 Primary and secondary resources, and tourism

Primary and secondary resources, and tourism Item Exempt entity Special conditions 8.1 a society or association not carried on for the profit or gain established for the purpose of of its individual members promoting the development of:

<a id="s50-40-a"></a>
**(a)** aviation; or

<a id="s50-40-b"></a>
**(b)** tourism  Primary and secondary resources, and tourism Item Exempt entity Special conditions 8.2 a society or association not carried on for the profit or gain established for the purpose of of its individual members promoting the development of any of the following Australian resources:

<a id="s50-40-a-2"></a>
**(a)** agricultural resources;

<a id="s50-40-b-2"></a>
**(b)** horticultural resources;

<a id="s50-40-c"></a>
**(c)** industrial resources;

<a id="s50-40-d"></a>
**(d)** manufacturing resources;

<a id="s50-40-e"></a>
**(e)** pastoral resources;

<a id="s50-40-f"></a>
**(f)** viticultural resources;

<a id="s50-40-g"></a>
**(g)** aquacultural resources;

<a id="s50-40-h"></a>
**(h)** fishing resources 8.3 a society or association not carried on for the profit or gain established for the purpose of of its individual members promoting the development of Australian information and communications technology resources
