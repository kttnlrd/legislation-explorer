# 50-110 Entitlement to endorsement

General rule

<a id="s50-110-1"></a>
**(1)** An entity is entitled to be endorsed as exempt from income tax if the entity meets all the relevant requirements of this section. Which entities are entitled to be endorsed?

<a id="s50-110-2"></a>
**(2)** To be entitled, the entity must be an entity covered by item 1.1 of the table in section 50-5. Requirement for ABN

<a id="s50-110-3"></a>
**(3)** To be entitled, the entity must have an *ABN. Requirement to meet special conditions

<a id="s50-110-5"></a>
**(5)** To be entitled:

> <a id="s50-110-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** the entity must meet the relevant conditions referred to in the column headed "Special conditions" of item 1.1 of the table in section 50-5; or

> <a id="s50-110-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** both of the following conditions must be met:

> > <a id="s50-110-5-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** the entity must not have carried on any activities as a charity;

> > <a id="s50-110-5-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** there must be reasonable grounds for believing that the entity will meet the relevant conditions referred to in the column headed "Special conditions" of item 1.1 of the table. The entity must also satisfy section 50-47, if the entity is an *ACNC type of entity.

<a id="s50-110-6"></a>
**(6)** To avoid doubt, the condition set out in section 50-52 (requiring the entity to be endorsed under this Subdivision) is not a relevant condition for the purposes of subsection (5).
