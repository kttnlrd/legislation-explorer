# 51-54 Gain or profit from disposal of eligible venture capital investments

Partners in VCLPs and ESVCLPs

<a id="s51-54-1"></a>
**(1)** An entity's share of any gain or profit made from the disposal or other realisation of an *eligible venture capital investment is exempt from income tax if:

<a id="s51-54-1-a"></a>
**(a)** it is made by a *VCLP, or an *ESVCLP, that is *unconditionally registered; and

<a id="s51-54-1-b"></a>
**(b)** were that disposal or other realisation to be a *disposal of a *CGT asset, the entity's share of any *capital gain or *capital loss would be disregarded under section 118-405 or 118-407. (1A) An entity's share of any gain or profit made:

<a id="s51-54-1-a-2"></a>
**(a)** by an *ESVCLP that is *unconditionally registered; and

<a id="s51-54-1-b-2"></a>
**(b)** from the disposal or other realisation of an *eligible venture capital investment; is exempt from income tax to the extent that, were that disposal or other realisation to be a *disposal of a *CGT asset, the equivalent *capital gain arising from the *CGT event would be disregarded because of a partial exemption from the CGT event under section 118-408. Partners in AFOFs

<a id="s51-54-2"></a>
**(2)** An entity's share of any gain or profit made from the disposal or other realisation of an *eligible venture capital investment is exempt from income tax if:

<a id="s51-54-2-a"></a>
**(a)** it is made by:

<a id="s51-54-2-a-i"></a>
**(i)** an *AFOF that is *unconditionally registered; or

<a id="s51-54-2-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** a *VCLP, or an *ESVCLP, that is unconditionally registered and in which an AFOF that is *unconditionally registered is a partner; and 
<a id="s51-54-2-b"></a>
**(b)** were that disposal or other realisation to be a *disposal of a *CGT asset, the entity's share of any *capital gain or *capital loss would be disregarded under section 118-410. Eligible venture capital investors

<a id="s51-54-3"></a>
**(3)** Any gain or profit made from the disposal or other realisation of an *eligible venture capital investment is exempt from income tax if:

<a id="s51-54-3-a"></a>
**(a)** you are an *eligible venture capital investor; and

<a id="s51-54-3-b"></a>
**(b)** were that disposal or other realisation to be a *disposal of a *CGT asset, any *capital gain or *capital loss would be disregarded under section 118-415.
