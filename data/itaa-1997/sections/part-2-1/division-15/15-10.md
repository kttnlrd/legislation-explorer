# 15-10  Bounties and subsidies

Your assessable income includes a bounty or subsidy that:

> <a id="s15-10-a"></a>
> **(a)** you receive in relation to carrying on a *business; and

> <a id="s15-10-b"></a>
> **(b)** is not assessable as *ordinary income under section 6-5.
