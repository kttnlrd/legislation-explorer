# 832-60  Valuation of trading stock affected by hybrid mismatch rules

If:

> <a id="s832-60-a"></a>
> **(a)** an amount of a deduction for an outgoing is disallowed under this Division; and

> <a id="s832-60-b"></a>
> **(b)** the outgoing was incurred in connection with acquiring an item of *trading stock; and

> <a id="s832-60-c"></a>
> **(c)** the item is on hand at the end of an income year; the amount disallowed is to be disregarded in working out the *cost, market selling value or replacement value of the item at the end of the income year under section 70-45.
