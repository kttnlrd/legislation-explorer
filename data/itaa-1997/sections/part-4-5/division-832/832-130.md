# 832-130 Meaning of subject to foreign income tax

<a id="s832-130-1"></a>
**(1)** An amount of income or profits is subject to foreign income tax in a foreign country in a *foreign tax period if *foreign income tax (except a tax covered by subsection (7)) is payable under a law of the foreign country in respect of the amount because the amount is included in the tax base of that law for the foreign tax period.

**Note:** Subdivision 832-C (Hybrid financial instrument mismatch) has effect as if certain amounts that are subject to a concessional rate of foreign income tax were not subject to foreign income tax: see section 832-235.

<a id="s832-130-2"></a>
**(2)** To avoid doubt, an amount of income or profits may be subject to foreign income tax in a foreign country in a *foreign tax period even if the relevant entity's tax base is nil, or a negative amount. Effect of credits etc. for underlying taxes

<a id="s832-130-3"></a>
**(3)** Despite subsection (1), if:

<a id="s832-130-3-a"></a>
**(a)** an amount (the pre-credit amount) of income or profits would, apart from this subsection, be *subject to foreign income tax in a foreign country; and

<a id="s832-130-3-b"></a>
**(b)** an entity is entitled under the law of the foreign country to a credit, rebate or other tax concession in respect of the amount for foreign tax (other than a withholding-type tax) payable under a tax law of a different country (including Australia); then only so much of the pre-credit amount as reasonably represents an amount not effectively sheltered from *foreign income tax (except a tax covered by subsection (7)) by the credit, rebate or tax concession is subject to foreign income tax.

**Note:** This subsection is disregarded in working out whether an amount of income or profits is dual inclusion income: see subsection 832-680(3). Effect of "dividend received deductions" in foreign countries

<a id="s832-130-4"></a>
**(4)** Despite subsection (1), if:

<a id="s832-130-4-a"></a>
**(a)** an amount (the pre-deduction amount) of income or profits would, apart from this subsection, be *subject to foreign income tax in a foreign country; and

<a id="s832-130-4-b"></a>
**(b)** the amount consists of a dividend received by an entity from a company; and

<a id="s832-130-4-c"></a>
**(c)** the entity is entitled to a *foreign income tax deduction in respect of all or part of the amount of the dividend; then only so much of the pre-deduction amount as reasonably represents an amount not effectively sheltered from *foreign  income tax (except a tax covered by subsection (7)) by the foreign income tax deduction is subject to foreign income tax. Effect of CFC regimes

<a id="s832-130-5"></a>
**(5)** An amount of income or profits of an entity is subject to foreign income tax if the amount is included in working out the tax base of another entity under a provision of a law of a foreign country that corresponds to section 456 or 457 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 (including a tax base that is nil, or a negative amount). Effect of foreign hybrid mismatch rules

<a id="s832-130-6"></a>
**(6)** In determining for the purposes of this section whether a payment is included in a tax base of a law of a foreign country as mentioned in subsection (1), disregard the effect of the following:

<a id="s832-130-6-a"></a>
**(a)** any provisions of *foreign hybrid mismatch rules of a foreign country;

<a id="s832-130-6-b"></a>
**(b)** any provisions of another law of a foreign country relating to *foreign income tax (except a tax covered by subsection (7)) that has substantially the same effect as foreign hybrid mismatch rules. Certain foreign taxes disregarded in this Division

<a id="s832-130-7"></a>
**(7)** This subsection covers each of the following:

<a id="s832-130-7-a"></a>
**(a)** *credit absorption tax;

<a id="s832-130-7-b"></a>
**(b)** *unitary tax;

<a id="s832-130-7-c"></a>
**(c)** withholding-type tax;

<a id="s832-130-7-d"></a>
**(d)** municipal tax;

<a id="s832-130-7-e"></a>
**(e)** in the case of a federal foreign country—a State tax;

<a id="s832-130-7-f"></a>
**(f)** *foreign GloBE tax or other foreign minimum tax.

**Note:** The definitions of credit absorption tax and unitary tax are in section 770-15.

<a id="s832-130-8"></a>
**(8)** Foreign minimum tax mentioned in paragraph (7)(f) includes a tax specified in regulations made for the purposes of paragraph 393(2)(c) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936.
