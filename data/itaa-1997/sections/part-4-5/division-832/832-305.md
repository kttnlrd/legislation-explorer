# 832-305 When a payment gives rise to a hybrid payer mismatch

<a id="s832-305-1"></a>
**(1)** A payment gives rise to a hybrid payer mismatch if:

<a id="s832-305-1-a"></a>
**(a)** the payment gives rise to a *hybrid mismatch under section 832-310; and International aspects of income tax Chapter 4 General Part 4-5 Hybrid mismatch rules Division 832 Section 832-310

<a id="s832-305-1-b"></a>
**(b)** subsection (3) or (4) applies.

<a id="s832-305-2"></a>
**(2)** The deduction component of the *hybrid payer mismatch is the *deduction component of the *deduction/non-inclusion mismatch mentioned in section 832-310. Control group

<a id="s832-305-3"></a>
**(3)** This subsection applies if the following entities are in the same *Division 832 control group:

<a id="s832-305-3-a"></a>
**(a)** the *hybrid payer;

<a id="s832-305-3-b"></a>
**(b)** each entity that is a *liable entity in respect of the income or profits of the hybrid payer.

**Note:** For the meaning of Division 832 control group, see section 832-205. Structured arrangement

<a id="s832-305-4"></a>
**(4)** This subsection applies if the payment is made under a *structured arrangement.

**Note:** For the meaning of structured arrangement, see section 832-210.
