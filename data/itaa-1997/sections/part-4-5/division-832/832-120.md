# 832-120 Meaning of foreign income tax deduction

<a id="s832-120-1"></a>
**(1)** An amount of a loss or outgoing is a foreign income tax deduction in a foreign country in a *foreign tax period to which an entity is entitled, if the entity is entitled to deduct the amount in working out its tax base for the foreign tax period under a law of the foreign country dealing with *foreign income tax (except a tax covered by subsection 832-130(7)).

<a id="s832-120-2"></a>
**(2)** To avoid doubt, an amount of a loss or outgoing may be a foreign income tax deduction in a foreign country in a *foreign tax period even if the relevant entity's tax base is nil, or a negative amount. Effect of foreign hybrid mismatch rules

<a id="s832-120-3"></a>
**(3)** In determining for the purposes of this section whether an entity is entitled to deduct an amount as mentioned in subsection (1), disregard the effect of the following:

> <a id="s832-120-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** any provisions of *foreign hybrid mismatch rules of a foreign country;

> <a id="s832-120-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** any provisions of another law of a foreign country relating to *foreign income tax (except a tax covered by International aspects of income tax Chapter 4 General Part 4-5 Hybrid mismatch rules Division 832 Section 832-125 subsection 832-130(7)) that has substantially the same effect as foreign hybrid mismatch rules.
