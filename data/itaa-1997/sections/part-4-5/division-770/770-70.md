# 770-70 Amount of foreign income tax offset

The amount of your *tax offset for the year is the sum of the *foreign income tax you paid that counts towards the offset for the year.

**Note:** The amount of foreign income tax you paid may be affected by Subdivision 770-C.

**Note:** The amount of the offset might be increased under section 770-230 of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997, if you have pre-commencement excess foreign income tax.
