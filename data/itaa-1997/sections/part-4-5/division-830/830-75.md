# 830-75 Extended meaning of subject to foreign tax

Where entity becomes a partner

<a id="s830-75-1"></a>
**(1)** If:

<a id="s830-75-1-a"></a>
**(a)** an entity becomes a partner (the first partner) in a *foreign hybrid in relation to an income year; and

<a id="s830-75-1-b"></a>
**(b)** a gain or profit of a capital nature accrues to another partner as a result of the disposal of the whole or part of that other partner's interest in an asset of the foreign hybrid that happens when the first partner becomes a partner; and

<a id="s830-75-1-c"></a>
**(c)** apart from this subsection, the gain or profit is not *subject to foreign tax in a *listed country in any *tax accounting period; and International aspects of income tax Chapter 4 General Part 4-5 Foreign hybrids Division 830 Section 830-75

<a id="s830-75-1-d"></a>
**(d)** if the foreign hybrid had disposed of the whole or an equivalent part of the asset at the time of the disposal of the whole or the part of the interest, any gain or profit of a capital nature that accrued to the foreign hybrid in respect of the disposal would have been subject to foreign tax in a listed country in a tax accounting period; then, for the purposes of Part X of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936, the gain or profit mentioned in paragraph (b) is taken to be subject to foreign tax in the listed country, and in the tax accounting period, mentioned in paragraph (d). Where partner increases its interest

<a id="s830-75-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

<a id="s830-75-2-a"></a>
**(a)** an entity is a partner (the first partner) that increases its interest in a *foreign hybrid in relation to an income year; and

<a id="s830-75-2-b"></a>
**(b)** a gain or profit of a capital nature accrues to another partner as a result of the disposal of the whole or part of that other partner's interest in an asset of the foreign hybrid that happens when the first partner increases its interest in the foreign hybrid; and

<a id="s830-75-2-c"></a>
**(c)** apart from this subsection, the gain or profit is not *subject to foreign tax in a *listed country in any *tax accounting period; and

<a id="s830-75-2-d"></a>
**(d)** if the foreign hybrid had disposed of the whole or an equivalent part of the asset at the time of the disposal of the whole or the part of the interest, any gain or profit of a capital nature that accrued to the foreign hybrid in respect of the disposal would have been subject to foreign tax in a listed country in a tax accounting period; then, for the purposes of Part X of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936, the gain or profit mentioned in paragraph (b) is taken to be subject to foreign tax in the listed country, and in the tax accounting period, mentioned in paragraph (d).

<a id="s830-75-3"></a>
**(3)** If:

<a id="s830-75-3-a"></a>
**(a)** an entity ceases to be a partner in a *foreign hybrid in relation to an income year; and

<a id="s830-75-3-b"></a>
**(b)** a gain or profit of a capital nature accrues to the entity as a result of the disposal of its interest in an asset of the foreign hybrid that happens when the entity ceases to be a partner; and

<a id="s830-75-3-c"></a>
**(c)** apart from this subsection, the gain or profit is not *subject to foreign tax in a *listed country in any *tax accounting period; and

<a id="s830-75-3-d"></a>
**(d)** any gain or profit of a capital nature that accrues to the entity as a result of the disposal of its interest in the foreign hybrid that happens when the entity ceases to be a partner is subject to foreign tax in a listed country in a tax accounting period; then, for the purposes of Part X of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936, the gain or profit mentioned in paragraph (b) is taken to be subject to foreign tax in the listed country, and in the tax accounting period, mentioned in paragraph (d). Where partner disposes of part of its interest

<a id="s830-75-4"></a>
**(4)** If:

<a id="s830-75-4-a"></a>
**(a)** an entity is a partner that disposes of part of its interest in a *foreign hybrid in relation to an income year; and

<a id="s830-75-4-b"></a>
**(b)** a gain or profit of a capital nature accrues to the entity as a result of the disposal of part of its interest in an asset of the foreign hybrid that happens when the entity disposes of the part of its interest in the foreign hybrid; and

<a id="s830-75-4-c"></a>
**(c)** apart from this subsection, the gain or profit is not *subject to foreign tax in a *listed country in any *tax accounting period; and

<a id="s830-75-4-d"></a>
**(d)** any gain or profit of a capital nature that accrues to the entity as a result of the disposal of the part of its interest in the foreign hybrid is subject to foreign tax in a listed country in a tax accounting period; International aspects of income tax Chapter 4 General Part 4-5 Foreign hybrids Division 830 Section 830-80 then, for the purposes of Part X of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936, the gain or profit mentioned in paragraph (b) is taken to be subject to foreign tax in the listed country, and in the tax accounting period, mentioned in paragraph (d).
