# 830-115 Tax losses cannot be transferred to a foreign hybrid

<a id="s830-115-1"></a>
**(1)** If an entity is a *foreign hybrid in relation to an income year, it cannot deduct in that income year a *tax loss for a *loss year in relation to which it was not a foreign hybrid. Former foreign hybrid can deduct tax losses for income years before it became a foreign hybrid

<a id="s830-115-2"></a>
**(2)** This section does not prevent an entity that:

<a id="s830-115-2-a"></a>
**(a)** is not a *foreign hybrid in relation to an income year (the post-hybrid year); and

<a id="s830-115-2-b"></a>
**(b)** was a foreign hybrid in relation to a previous income year; and

<a id="s830-115-2-c"></a>
**(c)** was not a foreign hybrid in relation to an income year (the pre-hybrid year) before the previous year; from deducting, in the post-hybrid year, a *tax loss for the pre-hybrid year.
