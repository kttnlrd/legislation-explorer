# 830-55 Meaning of foreign hybrid net capital loss amount

If:

<a id="s830-55-a"></a>
**(a)** the sum of a partner's *capital losses from *CGT events happening during an income year in relation to a *foreign hybrid or *CGT assets of a foreign hybrid; exceeds:

<a id="s830-55-b"></a>
**(b)** the sum of the partner's *capital gains from CGT events happening during the income year in relation to the foreign hybrid or CGT assets of the foreign hybrid; the partner has a foreign hybrid net capital loss amount in respect of the foreign hybrid for the income year equal to the excess.
