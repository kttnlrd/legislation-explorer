# 830-5  Foreign hybrid

The expression foreign hybrid means:

> <a id="s830-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** a *foreign hybrid limited partnership; or

> <a id="s830-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** a *foreign hybrid company.
