# 768-7 Foreign equity distributions entitled to a foreign income tax deduction

<a id="s768-7-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies to a *foreign equity distribution if:

<a id="s768-7-1-a"></a>
**(a)** all or part of the distribution gives rise to a *foreign income tax deduction; and

<a id="s768-7-1-b"></a>
**(b)** the exception in subsection (2) does not apply to the distribution. Exception for foreign corporate collective investment vehicles

<a id="s768-7-2"></a>
**(2)** This subsection applies to a *foreign equity distribution if:

<a id="s768-7-2-a"></a>
**(a)** the *foreign income tax deduction arises because the company that made the distribution is recognised under the law of the foreign country in which the deduction arises as being used for collective investment; and International aspects of income tax Chapter 4 General Part 4-5 Foreign non-assessable income and gains Division 768 Section 768-10

<a id="s768-7-2-b"></a>
**(b)** *foreign income tax or a withholding-type tax was payable in respect of the distribution.
