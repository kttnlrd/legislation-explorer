# 802-40  Effect of foreign income tax offset on conduit foreign income

The entity's conduit foreign income includes an amount if a tax offset arose for the entity under Division 770 for the income year immediately before the one in which the relevant time occurs. The amount is worked out using the formula: (1     * Corporate tax rate) Offset *Corporate tax rate
