# 802-10 Objects

The objects of this Subdivision are:

> <a id="s802-10-a"></a>
> **(a)** to encourage the establishment in Australia of regional holding companies for foreign groups; and

> <a id="s802-10-b"></a>
> **(b)** to improve Australia's attractiveness as a continuing base for its multinational companies; by providing relief from tax on *distributions by *Australian corporate tax entities to *members who are foreign residents or other Australian corporate tax entities if those distributions relate to *conduit foreign income.
