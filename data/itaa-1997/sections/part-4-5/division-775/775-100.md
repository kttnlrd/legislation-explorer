# 775-100  Net costs of assuming an obligation to receive foreign currency

<a id="s775-100-1"></a>
**(1)** For the purposes of this Division, the net costs of assuming an obligation, or a part of an obligation, to receive *foreign currency are the total of:

> <a id="s775-100-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the money you:

> > <a id="s775-100-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** are required to pay; or

> > <a id="s775-100-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** would be required to pay in the event of the exercise of an option; in respect of the fulfilment of the obligation or the part of the obligation; and

> <a id="s775-100-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the *market value of any *non-cash benefit you:

> > <a id="s775-100-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** are required to provide; or

> > <a id="s775-100-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** would be required to provide in the event of the exercise of an option; in respect of the fulfilment of the obligation or the part of the obligation; reduced by the amount worked out under subsection (2).

<a id="s775-100-2"></a>
**(2)** The amount worked out under this subsection is the total of:

> <a id="s775-100-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** the money you:

> > <a id="s775-100-2-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** received; or

> > <a id="s775-100-2-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** are entitled to receive; because you incurred the obligation or the part of the obligation; and

> <a id="s775-100-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the *market value of any *non-cash benefit you:

> > <a id="s775-100-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** received or obtained; or

> > <a id="s775-100-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** are entitled to receive or obtain; because you incurred the obligation or the part of the obligation; reduced by any amounts that are included in assessable income under a provision of this Act other than this Division.

<a id="s775-100-3"></a>
**(3)** To avoid doubt, paragraphs (2)(a) and (b) do not apply to money or a *non-cash benefit that you:

> <a id="s775-100-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** received or obtained; or

> <a id="s775-100-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** are entitled to receive or obtain; because of the fulfilment of the obligation or the part of the obligation.
