# 775-220 Material variation of a facility agreement—forex realisation event 7

Forex realisation event 7

<a id="s775-220-1"></a>
**(1)** Forex realisation event 7 happens if:

<a id="s775-220-1-a"></a>
**(a)** a material variation is made to the terms or conditions of a *facility agreement; or

<a id="s775-220-1-b"></a>
**(b)** a material variation is made to the effect of a facility agreement; or

<a id="s775-220-1-c"></a>
**(c)** a material variation is made to the type or types of security that can be issued under a facility agreement; so long as you have made a choice for roll-over relief for the facility agreement, and that choice is in effect.

**Note:** See also subsections (7) and (8). Time of the event

<a id="s775-220-2"></a>
**(2)** The time of the event is when the material variation happens. Forex realisation gain

<a id="s775-220-3"></a>
**(3)** You make a forex realisation gain if:

<a id="s775-220-3-a"></a>
**(a)** the total of the forex realisation gains that you would have made as a result of forex realisation event 6 if you had, at the time of forex realisation event 7:

<a id="s775-220-3-a-i"></a>
**(i)** discharged your liabilities under each of the notional loans to which the agreement relates; and

<a id="s775-220-3-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** not rolled-over any *eligible security; exceeds:

<a id="s775-220-3-b"></a>
**(b)** the total of the forex realisation losses that you would have made as a result of forex realisation event 6 if you had, at the time of forex realisation event 7:

<a id="s775-220-3-b-i"></a>
**(i)** discharged your liabilities under each of the notional loans to which the agreement relates; and

<a id="s775-220-3-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** not rolled-over any eligible security. The amount of the forex realisation gain is the amount of the excess.

**Note:** See also subsection (9). Forex realisation loss

<a id="s775-220-4"></a>
**(4)** You make a forex realisation loss if:

<a id="s775-220-4-a"></a>
**(a)** the total of the forex realisation losses that you would have made as a result of forex realisation event 6 if you had, at the time of forex realisation event 7:

<a id="s775-220-4-a-i"></a>
**(i)** discharged your liabilities under each of the notional loans to which the agreement relates; and

<a id="s775-220-4-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** not rolled-over any *eligible security; exceeds:

<a id="s775-220-4-b"></a>
**(b)** the total of the forex realisation gains that you would have made as a result of forex realisation event 6 if you had, at the time of forex realisation event 7:

<a id="s775-220-4-b-i"></a>
**(i)** discharged your liabilities under each of the notional loans to which the agreement relates; and

<a id="s775-220-4-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** not rolled-over any eligible security. The amount of the forex realisation loss is the amount of the excess.

**Note:** See also subsection (9). Termination of choice

<a id="s775-220-5"></a>
**(5)** If forex realisation event 7 happens in relation to a *facility agreement:

<a id="s775-220-5-a"></a>
**(a)** your choice for roll-over relief for the facility agreement ceases to have effect immediately after the event; and

<a id="s775-220-5-b"></a>
**(b)** you are not entitled to make a fresh choice for roll-over relief for the facility agreement. Modification of tax recognition time

<a id="s775-220-6"></a>
**(6)** If:

<a id="s775-220-6-a"></a>
**(a)** forex realisation event 7 happens in relation to a *facility agreement; and

<a id="s775-220-6-b"></a>
**(b)** an *eligible security issued by you under the facility agreement was in existence at the time of that event; and

<a id="s775-220-6-c"></a>
**(c)** at a later time, forex realisation event 4 happens because you cease to have an obligation, or a part of an obligation, to pay *foreign currency under the security; section 775-55 applies to you as if the tax recognition time for the obligation, or the part of the obligation, were the time of forex realisation event 7 (despite subsection 775-55(7)). Material variation

<a id="s775-220-7"></a>
**(7)** To avoid doubt, if a variation to:

<a id="s775-220-7-a"></a>
**(a)** the terms or conditions of a facility agreement; or

<a id="s775-220-7-b"></a>
**(b)** the effect of a facility agreement; results in the agreement ceasing to be a facility agreement, the variation is taken to be a material variation for the purposes of subsection (1).

<a id="s775-220-8"></a>
**(8)** The regulations may provide that a specified kind of variation is taken to be a material variation for the purposes of subsection (1). Total amount

<a id="s775-220-9"></a>
**(9)** To avoid doubt, the total amount referred to in paragraph (3)(b) or (4)(b) may be zero.
