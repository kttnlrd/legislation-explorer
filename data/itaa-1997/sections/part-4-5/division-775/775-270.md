# 775-270  You may choose retranslation for a qualifying forex account

<a id="s775-270-1"></a>
**(1)** You may choose retranslation for a *qualifying forex account held by you. (1A) A choice under subsection (1) does not apply to a *qualifying forex account held by you if a *foreign exchange retranslation election by you is in effect in relation to the account under Subdivision 230-D.

<a id="s775-270-2"></a>
**(2)** A choice must be in writing. (2A) If:

> <a id="s775-270-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** either:

> > <a id="s775-270-2-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** you make a choice within 30 days after the commencement of the New Business Tax System (Taxation of Financial Arrangements) Act (No. 1) 2003; or

> > <a id="s775-270-2-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** you make a choice within 90 days after the commencement of Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Tax Laws Amendment (Taxation of Financial Arrangements) Act 2009; and

> <a id="s775-270-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the choice is expressed to have come into effect on a specified day; and

> <a id="s775-270-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** the specified day is included in the period:

> > <a id="s775-270-2-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)** beginning on 1 July 2003; and

> > <a id="s775-270-2-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** ending on the day on which the choice is made; the choice is taken to have come into effect on the specified day.

<a id="s775-270-3"></a>
**(3)** A choice continues in effect until:

> <a id="s775-270-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** you cease to hold the account; or

> <a id="s775-270-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** the account ceases to be a *qualifying forex account; or

> <a id="s775-270-3-c"></a>
> **(c)** a withdrawal of the choice takes effect; whichever happens first.

> **Note:** For withdrawal of choice, see section 775-275.
