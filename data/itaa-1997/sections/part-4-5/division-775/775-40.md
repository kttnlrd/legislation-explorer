# 775-40  Disposal of foreign currency or right to receive foreign currency—forex realisation event 1

Forex realisation event 1

<a id="s775-40-1"></a>
**(1)** Forex realisation event 1 is *CGT event A1 that happens if you dispose of:

> <a id="s775-40-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** *foreign currency; or

> <a id="s775-40-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** a right, or a part of a right, to receive foreign currency.

> **Note:** For extended meaning of right to receive foreign currency, see section 775-135. Disposal

<a id="s775-40-2"></a>
**(2)** For the purposes of this section, use subsection 104-10(2) to work out whether you have disposed of:

> <a id="s775-40-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** *foreign currency; or

> <a id="s775-40-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** a right, or a part of a right, to receive foreign currency.

> **Note:** Under subsection 104-10(2), a disposal requires a change of ownership.

<a id="s775-40-3"></a>
**(3)** For the purposes of this section, subsection 104-10(3) is modified so that the time of the event is when:

> <a id="s775-40-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** the *foreign currency is disposed of; or

> <a id="s775-40-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** the right, or the part of the right, is disposed of. Forex realisation gain

<a id="s775-40-4"></a>
**(4)** You make a forex realisation gain if:

> <a id="s775-40-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** you make a *capital gain from the event; and

> <a id="s775-40-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** some or all of the capital gain is attributable to a *currency exchange rate effect. The amount of the forex realisation gain is so much of the capital gain as is attributable to a currency exchange rate effect.

> **Note:** For currency exchange rate effect, see section 775-105.

<a id="s775-40-5"></a>
**(5)** For the purposes of paragraph (4)(a), Part 3-1 is modified so that section 118-20 is disregarded in working out the *capital gain.

> **Note:** Section 118-20 deals with reducing capital gains if an amount is otherwise assessable. Forex realisation loss

<a id="s775-40-6"></a>
**(6)** You make a forex realisation loss if:

> <a id="s775-40-6-a"></a>
> **(a)** you make a *capital loss from the event; and

> <a id="s775-40-6-b"></a>
> **(b)** some or all of the capital loss is attributable to a *currency exchange rate effect. The amount of the forex realisation loss is so much of the capital loss as is attributable to a currency exchange rate effect.

> **Note:** For currency exchange rate effect, see section 775-105. No indexation of cost base

<a id="s775-40-7"></a>
**(7)** For the purposes of this section, disregard Division 114.

> **Note:** Division 114 deals with indexation of the cost base. Foreign currency hedging gains and losses

<a id="s775-40-8"></a>
**(8)** For the purposes of this section, disregard section 118-55.

> **Note:** Section 118-55 deals with foreign currency hedging gains and losses. Capital proceeds

<a id="s775-40-9"></a>
**(9)** For the purposes of this section, if the *capital proceeds from the event are more or less than the *market value of:

> <a id="s775-40-9-a"></a>
> **(a)** the *foreign currency; or

> <a id="s775-40-9-b"></a>
> **(b)** the right, or the part of the right; the capital proceeds from the event are taken to be the market value. (The market value is worked out as at the time of the event.)
