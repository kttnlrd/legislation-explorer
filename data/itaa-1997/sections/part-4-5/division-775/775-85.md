# 775-85  Forex cost base of a right to receive foreign currency

The forex cost base of a right, or a part of a right, to receive *foreign currency is the total of:

> <a id="s775-85-a"></a>
> **(a)** the money you:

> > <a id="s775-85-i"></a>
> > **(i)** paid; or

> > <a id="s775-85-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** are required to pay; or

> > <a id="s775-85-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)** would be required to pay in the event of the exercise of an option; in respect of acquiring the right or part of the right; and

> <a id="s775-85-b"></a>
> **(b)** the *market value of any *non-cash benefit you:

> > <a id="s775-85-i-2"></a>
> > **(i)** provided; or

> > <a id="s775-85-ii-2"></a>
> > **(ii)** are required to provide; or

> > <a id="s775-85-iii-2"></a>
> > **(iii)** would be required to provide in the event of the exercise of an option; in respect of acquiring the right or part of the right; reduced by any amounts that are deductible under a provision of this Act other than this Division.
