# 775-210 Notional loan

<a id="s775-210-1"></a>
**(1)** The rules in this section have effect only for the purposes of this Subdivision. Notional loan

<a id="s775-210-2"></a>
**(2)** If you issue an *eligible security under a *facility agreement otherwise than as a result of a roll-over, you are taken to have been given a loan (the notional loan):

<a id="s775-210-2-a"></a>
**(a)** of a *foreign currency principal amount equal to the foreign currency face value of the security; and 
<a id="s775-210-2-b"></a>
**(b)** for a period equal to the term of the security; and

<a id="s775-210-2-c"></a>
**(c)** that is taken to be attached to the security; and

<a id="s775-210-2-d"></a>
**(d)** the start time of which is the time when you issued the security.

**Note:** The period of the notional loan may be extended as the result of a later roll-over—see subsection (3).

**Note:** The notional loan may become attached to a later security as the result of a roll-over—see subsection (3).

**Note:** The foreign currency principal amount of the notional loan may remain the same, or may fall (but not rise), as a result of a later roll-over—see subsection (3).

**Note:** If, at a later time, the security is rolled-over, and the foreign currency face value of the new security exceeds the foreign currency face value of the rolled-over security, you are taken to have been given an additional notional loan of a foreign currency principal amount equal to the excess—see subsection (3). **(4)** If:

**(a)** you discharge your obligation under an *eligible security issued under a *facility agreement; and

<a id="s775-210-4-b"></a>
**(b)** the security is not rolled-over at the time of discharge; and

<a id="s775-210-4-c"></a>
**(c)** you have made a choice for roll-over relief for the facility agreement, and that choice is in effect; then, for each notional loan attached to the security, you are taken to have paid a *foreign currency amount equal to the foreign currency principal amount of the notional loan in order to discharge your obligation to pay the foreign currency principal amount of the notional loan. Foreign currency

<a id="s775-210-5"></a>
**(5)** For the purposes of the application of this section to a particular *facility agreement that provides for the issue of *eligible securities, foreign currency is the *foreign currency in which the securities are denominated.

**Note:** Section 960-50 (Australian currency translation rule) does not affect the operation of this section—see subsection 960-50(10). You translate to Australian currency when you apply section 775-215 (forex realisation event 6).
