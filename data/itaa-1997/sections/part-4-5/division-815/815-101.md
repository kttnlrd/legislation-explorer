# 815-101  What this Subdivision is about

This Subdivision applies if an entity would otherwise get a tax advantage in Australia from cross-border conditions that are inconsistent with the internationally accepted arm's length principle. The entity is treated for income tax and withholding tax purposes as if arm's length conditions had operated.
