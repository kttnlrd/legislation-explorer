# 820-690 Commissioner's power

If the Commissioner considers that, in relation to a calculation under this Division, an entity has:

> <a id="s820-690-a"></a>
> **(a)** overvalued its assets; or

> <a id="s820-690-b"></a>
> **(b)** undervalued its liabilities (including its *debt capital); the Commissioner may, having regard to the *accounting standards and this Subdivision, substitute a value that the Commissioner considers is appropriate.
