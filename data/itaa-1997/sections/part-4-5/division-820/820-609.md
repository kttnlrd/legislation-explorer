# 820-609 Effect on classification of head company or single company

<a id="s820-609-1"></a>
**(1)** The *head company or single company is an outward investing entity (ADI) for a period (the trial period) that is all or part of the grouping period if:

> <a id="s820-609-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** apart from this Subdivision, the head company or single company would be an *outward investing entity (ADI) for the trial period; or

> <a id="s820-609-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** apart from this Subdivision, the head company or single company:

> > <a id="s820-609-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** would be an *outward investing financial entity (non-ADI) for the trial period; and

> > <a id="s820-609-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** at least one of the *Australian permanent establishments is a *permanent establishment through which a *foreign bank carries on banking *business in Australia.

<a id="s820-609-2"></a>
**(2)** The *head company is also an outward investing entity (ADI) for the trial period if, apart from this Subdivision:

> <a id="s820-609-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** section 820-585 would prevent the disallowance of a *debt deduction for the income year including the trial period; or

> <a id="s820-609-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** section 820-587 would apply Subdivision 820-D to the head company as if it were an *outward investing entity (ADI) for the trial period.

<a id="s820-609-3"></a>
**(3)** The single company is also an outward investing entity (ADI) for the trial period if it is both a *foreign controlled Australian company and an *ADI for that period.

<a id="s820-609-4"></a>
**(4)** The *head company or single company is an inward investing entity (ADI) for the trial period if:

> <a id="s820-609-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** apart from this Subdivision, it would be an *inward investment vehicle (financial), and not an *outward investing financial entity (non-ADI), for the trial period; and

> <a id="s820-609-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** at least one of the *Australian permanent establishments is a *permanent establishment through which a *foreign bank carries on banking *business in Australia. International aspects of income tax Chapter 4 General Part 4-5 Thin capitalisation rules Division 820 Section 820-609

<a id="s820-609-5"></a>
**(5)** The *head company or single company is an outward investing financial entity (non-ADI) for the trial period if, apart from this Subdivision:

> <a id="s820-609-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** it would be an *outward investing financial entity (non-ADI) for that period; and

> <a id="s820-609-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** at least one of the *Australian permanent establishments is a *permanent establishment of a *foreign entity that is a *financial entity; and

> <a id="s820-609-5-c"></a>
> **(c)** none of the Australian permanent establishments is a permanent establishment through which a *foreign bank carries on banking *business in Australia.

<a id="s820-609-6"></a>
**(6)** The *head company or single company is an inward investing financial entity (non-ADI) and an inward investment vehicle (financial) for the trial period if, apart from this Subdivision:

> <a id="s820-609-6-a"></a>
> **(a)** it would be an *inward investing financial entity (non-ADI) and an *inward investment vehicle (financial) for that period; and

> <a id="s820-609-6-b"></a>
> **(b)** it would not be an *outward investing financial entity (non-ADI) for that period; and

> <a id="s820-609-6-c"></a>
> **(c)** at least one of the *Australian permanent establishments is a *permanent establishment of a *foreign entity that is a *financial entity; and

> <a id="s820-609-6-d"></a>
> **(d)** none of the Australian permanent establishments is a permanent establishment through which a *foreign bank carries on banking *business in Australia.

<a id="s820-609-7"></a>
**(7)** This section has effect despite any other provision of this Division, except Subdivision 820-EA and section 820-610.

> **Note:** If the head company or single company is an outward investing financial entity (non-ADI) or inward investment vehicle (financial) under this section and satisfies subsection 820-430(5), it may choose under Subdivision 820-EA to be treated as an outward investing entity (ADI). Section 820-603 affects whether the company satisfies that subsection, by treating as part of the company each relevant foreign financial entity's Australian permanent establishment.
