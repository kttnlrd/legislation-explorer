# 820-1  What this Division is about

This Division applies to foreign controlled Australian entities, Australian entities that operate internationally and foreign entities that operate in Australia. Financing expenses that an entity can otherwise deduct from its assessable income may be disallowed under this Division where the entity is "thinly capitalised".
