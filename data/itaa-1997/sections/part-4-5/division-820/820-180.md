# 820-180  What this Subdivision is about

This Subdivision sets out the thin capitalisation rules that apply to an entity that is an inward investing financial entity (non-ADI) for all of an income year (but not an outward investing financial entity (non-ADI) for all or any part of that year). These rules deal with the following matters: •      how to work out the entity's maximum allowable debt for an income year; •      how all or a part of the debt deductions claimed by the entity may be disallowed if the maximum allowable debt is exceeded; •      how to apply these rules to a period that is less than an income year.
