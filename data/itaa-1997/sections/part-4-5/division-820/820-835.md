# 820-835  Commissioner's power

For the purposes of this Subdivision, the Commissioner may decide:

> <a id="s820-835-a"></a>
> **(a)** which one of 2 things is to be taken into account for the purposes of subsection 820-820(4) or subsection 820-825(3); or

> <a id="s820-835-b"></a>
> **(b)** which one of 2 or more entities is to be chosen for the purposes of paragraph 820-830(2)(a). TC direct control interest, TC indirect control interest and TC control tracing interest
