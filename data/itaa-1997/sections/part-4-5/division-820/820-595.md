# 820-595  What this Subdivision is about

If:

> <a id="s820-595-a"></a>
> **(a)** the head company of a consolidated group or MEC group; or

> <a id="s820-595-b"></a>
> **(b)** an Australian company that cannot consolidate; is a member of the same wholly-owned group as a foreign bank or foreign financial entity, the company can choose to treat as part of itself the Australian branches of the foreign bank or foreign financial entity, affecting how the rest of this Division applies.
