# 820-300  Thin capitalisation rule for outward investing entities (ADI)

Thin capitalisation rule

<a id="s820-300-1"></a>
**(1)** This subsection disallows all or a part of each *debt deduction of an entity for an income year (to the extent that it is not attributable to an *overseas permanent establishment of the entity) if, for that year:

> <a id="s820-300-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the entity is an *outward investing entity (ADI) (see subsection (2)); and

> <a id="s820-300-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the entity's *adjusted average equity capital (see subsection (3)) is less than the entity's *minimum capital amount (see section 820-305).

> **Note:** This Subdivision does not apply if the total debt deductions of that entity and all its associate entities for that year are $2 million or less, see section 820-35.

> **Note:** To work out the amount to be disallowed, see section 820-325.

> **Note:** For the rules that apply to an entity that is an outward investing entity (ADI) for only part of an income year, see section 820-330 in conjunction with subsection (2) of this section.

> **Note:** A consolidated group or MEC group may be an outward investing entity (ADI) to which this Subdivision applies: see Subdivisions 820-FA and 820-FB. Outward investing entity (ADI)

<a id="s820-300-2"></a>
**(2)** The entity is an outward investing entity (ADI) for a period that is all or a part of an income year if, and only if, throughout that period, the entity is an *ADI to which at least one of the following paragraphs applies:

> <a id="s820-300-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** the entity is an *Australian controller of at least one *Australian controlled foreign entity (not necessarily the same Australian controlled foreign entity throughout that period);

> <a id="s820-300-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the entity is an *Australian entity that carries on a *business at or through at least one *overseas permanent establishment (not necessarily the same permanent establishment throughout that period);

> <a id="s820-300-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** the entity is:

> > <a id="s820-300-2-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)** an Australian entity; and

> > <a id="s820-300-2-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** an *associate entity of another entity (a related investor) to which subsection (2AA) applies for that period.

> **Note:** To determine whether an entity is an Australian controller of an Australian controlled foreign entity, see Subdivision 820-H. (2AA) This subsection applies to a related investor for a period if at all times during the period:

> <a id="s820-300-2-a-2"></a>
> **(a)** it is an *outward investing financial entity (non-ADI); or

> <a id="s820-300-2-b-2"></a>
> **(b)** it is an *outward investing entity (ADI); or

> <a id="s820-300-2-c-2"></a>
> **(c)** both:

> > <a id="s820-300-2-c-i-2"></a>
> > **(i)** it is a *general class investor; and

> > <a id="s820-300-2-c-ii-2"></a>
> > **(ii)** assuming that it were a *financial entity, it would be an outward investing financial entity (non-ADI). (2A) However, the entity is not an outward investing entity (ADI) for a period that is all or a part of an income year if it is a *general class investor for that year. (2B) Subsection (2A) does not apply for the purposes of subsection 820-46(2) (definition of general class investor). Adjusted average equity capital

<a id="s820-300-3"></a>
**(3)** The entity's adjusted average equity capital for an income year is:

> <a id="s820-300-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** the average value, for that year, of all the *ADI equity capital of the entity (other than ADI equity capital attributable to its *overseas permanent establishments); minus

> <a id="s820-300-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** the average value, for that year, of all the *controlled foreign entity equity of the entity (other than controlled foreign entity equity attributable to its overseas permanent establishments).

> **Note:** To calculate an average value for the purposes of this Division, see Subdivision 820-G.

<a id="s820-300-4"></a>
**(4)** For the purposes of paragraph (3)(a), treat treasury shares (within the meaning of *accounting standard AASB 132) in the entity as included in the *ADI equity capital of the entity, to the extent that those shares are part of the entity's eligible tier 1 capital (within the meaning of the *prudential standards).
