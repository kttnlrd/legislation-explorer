# 820-400  Minimum capital amount

The entity's minimum capital amount for an income year is the lesser of the following amounts:

> <a id="s820-400-a"></a>
> **(a)** the *safe harbour capital amount;

> <a id="s820-400-b"></a>
> **(b)** the *arm's length capital amount.
