# 842-200 What this Subdivision is about

- This Subdivision sets out rules about the taxation of some foreign residents (known as IMR entities) that invest into or through Australia. International aspects of income tax
- 842-205 Income and capital gains from IMR financial arrangements are not subject to Australian income tax. Deductions and capital losses from IMR financial arrangements are disregarded for the purposes of this Act.
