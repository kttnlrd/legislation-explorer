# 842-210 IMR concessions apply only to foreign residents etc.

<a id="s842-210-1"></a>
**(1)** This Subdivision applies only for the purposes of working out the assessable income of an entity (the foreign entity) that:

<a id="s842-210-1-a"></a>
**(a)** is a foreign resident; and

<a id="s842-210-1-b"></a>
**(b)** is not a trust or partnership.

<a id="s842-210-2"></a>
**(2)** Despite subsection (1), this Subdivision applies in relation to a partnership or trust, to the extent necessary to work out an amount included in the assessable income of the foreign entity.

**Note:** This Subdivision applies, for example, in working out the net income of a partnership or trust, to the extent necessary to work out the assessable income, attributable to that partnership or trust, of a partner or beneficiary who is a foreign resident.

**Note:** This Subdivision could operate in relation to an entity (if it is a partnership or trust) and/or one or more partnerships or trusts interposed between the entity and the foreign resident.
