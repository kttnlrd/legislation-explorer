# 245-150  Allocation of remaining total net forgiven amount in respect of expenditures

<a id="s245-150-1"></a>
**(1)** You may choose:

> <a id="s245-150-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the order in which your expenditures are reduced; and

> <a id="s245-150-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the amount applied in reduction of each of those expenditures; so long as that the *total net forgiven amount remaining is applied, to the maximum extent possible, in reduction of your expenditures.

<a id="s245-150-2"></a>
**(2)** If you do not make a choice for the purposes of subsection (1), the Commissioner may make the choice on your behalf in a reasonable way.
