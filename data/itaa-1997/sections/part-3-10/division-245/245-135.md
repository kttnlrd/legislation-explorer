# 245-135  Allocation of remaining total net forgiven amount in respect of net capital losses

<a id="s245-135-1"></a>
**(1)** You may choose:

> <a id="s245-135-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the order in which your *net capital losses are reduced; and

> <a id="s245-135-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the amount applied in reduction of each of those losses; so long as the *total net forgiven amount remaining is applied, to the maximum extent possible, in reduction of those losses.

<a id="s245-135-2"></a>
**(2)** If you do not make a choice for the purposes of subsection (1), the Commissioner may make the choice on your behalf in a reasonable way. Reduction of expenditure
