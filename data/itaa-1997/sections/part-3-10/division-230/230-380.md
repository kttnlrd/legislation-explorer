# 230-380 Commissioner may determine that requirement met

Commissioner may determine that requirement met

<a id="s230-380-1"></a>
- **(1)** If (apart from this section) the requirements of sections
- 230-355 to
- 230-365 are not met in relation to a *hedging financial arrangement that you have, treat those requirements as having been so met if the Commissioner makes a determination under subsection (1A) in relation to the arrangement. (1A) The Commissioner may make the determination if the Commissioner considers that this is appropriate, having regard to:

> <a id="s230-380-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the respects in which the arrangement does not meet those requirements; and

> <a id="s230-380-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the extent to which it does not meet those requirements; and

> <a id="s230-380-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** the reasons why it does not meet those requirements; and

> <a id="s230-380-1-d"></a>
> **(d)** if the Commissioner is considering whether to impose conditions under subsection (2)—the likelihood that you will comply with those conditions; and

> <a id="s230-380-1-e"></a>
> **(e)** the objects of this Subdivision. Commissioner may impose additional record keeping requirements

<a id="s230-380-2"></a>
**(2)** The Commissioner may make a determination under subsection (1A) conditional on your keeping records in addition to those required by section 230-355.

<a id="s230-380-3"></a>
**(3)** A determination under subsection (1A) ceases to have effect if you breach a condition imposed under subsection (2).

<a id="s230-380-4"></a>
**(4)** Subsection (3) ceases to apply to you if the Commissioner determines that that subsection ceases to apply to you. The determination takes effect from the date specified in the determination.

<a id="s230-380-5"></a>
**(5)** In deciding whether to make the determination under subsection (4), the Commissioner must have regard to:

> <a id="s230-380-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** your record keeping practices; and

> <a id="s230-380-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** your compliance history; and

> <a id="s230-380-5-c"></a>
> **(c)** any changes that have been made to:

> > <a id="s230-380-5-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)** your accounting systems and controls; and

> > <a id="s230-380-5-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** your internal governance processes; to ensure that breaches of the kind referred to in subsection (3) do not happen again; and

> <a id="s230-380-5-d"></a>
> **(d)** any other relevant matter. Commissioner may determine matter under section 230-360

<a id="s230-380-6"></a>
**(6)** If:

> <a id="s230-380-6-a"></a>
> **(a)** the Commissioner makes a determination under subsection (1A) in relation to a *hedging financial arrangement; and

> <a id="s230-380-6-b"></a>
> **(b)** either or both of the following applies:

> > <a id="s230-380-6-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** you fail to determine a matter in relation to the arrangement under section 230-360;

> > <a id="s230-380-6-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** you determine a matter in relation to the arrangement under section 230-360 but the determination does not satisfy the requirements of subsection 230-360(2); the Commissioner may determine that matter, in a way that satisfies the requirements of section 230-360. The Commissioner's determination has effect as if you had made the determination and recorded it under that section.
