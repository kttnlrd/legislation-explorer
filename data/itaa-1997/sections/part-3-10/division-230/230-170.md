# 230-170 Allocating gain or loss to income years

<a id="s230-170-1"></a>
**(1)** You are taken, for the purposes of section 230-15, to make, for an income year, a gain or loss equal to a part of a gain or loss if:

<a id="s230-170-1-a"></a>
**(a)** that part of the gain or loss is allocated to an interval under section 230-135; and

<a id="s230-170-1-b"></a>
**(b)** that interval falls wholly within that income year.

<a id="s230-170-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

<a id="s230-170-2-a"></a>
**(a)** a part of a gain or loss is allocated to an interval under section 230-135; and

<a id="s230-170-2-b"></a>
**(b)** that interval straddles 2 income years; you are taken, for purposes of section 230-15, to make a gain or loss equal to so much of that part of the gain or loss as is allocated between those income years on a reasonable basis. (2A) Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply to a part of a gain or loss if:

<a id="s230-170-2-a-2"></a>
**(a)** subsection 230-100(3A) or 230-130(4A) applies to the gain or loss; and

<a id="s230-170-2-b-2"></a>
**(b)** that part of the gain or loss is allocated to an interval under section 230-135; and

<a id="s230-170-2-c"></a>
**(c)** that interval ends before or during the income year during which the gain or loss becomes sufficiently certain (as mentioned in paragraph 230-100(3A)(b) or 230-130(4A)(f), whichever is applicable). Instead, you are taken, for the purposes of section 230-15, to make, for that income year, a gain or loss equal to that part of that gain or loss.

<a id="s230-170-3"></a>
**(3)** If:

<a id="s230-170-3-a"></a>
**(a)** a *head company of a *consolidated group or *MEC group has a *financial arrangement; and

<a id="s230-170-3-b"></a>
**(b)** a subsidiary member of the group ceases to be a member of the group at a particular time (the leaving time); and

<a id="s230-170-3-c"></a>
**(c)** immediately after the leaving time, the head company no longer has the arrangement because the subsidiary member ceased to be a member of the group; an income year of the group is taken, for the purposes of applying this section to the group and the arrangement, to end at the leaving time.
