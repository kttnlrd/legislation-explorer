# 230-25  Associated financial benefits to be taken into account only once under this Act

Application of section

<a id="s230-25-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies to a *financial benefit whose amount or value is taken into account in working out whether you make, or the amount of, a gain or loss to which paragraph 230-20(1)(a), (b) or

> <a id="s230-25-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** applies. Associated financial benefit to be taken into account only once

<a id="s230-25-2"></a>
**(2)** A *financial benefit to which this section applies is not to be (to any extent):

> <a id="s230-25-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** included in your assessable income; or

> <a id="s230-25-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** allowable as a deduction to you; under any provision of this Act outside this Division for the same or any other income year. Exception for certain bad debts

<a id="s230-25-3"></a>
**(3)** If:

> <a id="s230-25-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** a *financial benefit has been included in your assessable income under a provision of this Act outside this Division; and

> <a id="s230-25-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** a bad debt deduction would have been allowed under section 25-35 in relation to the financial benefit; subsection (2) does not prevent that bad debt deduction from being allowed under section 25-35 in relation to the financial benefit as if the debt were still outstanding. Section does not give rise to exempt income

<a id="s230-25-4"></a>
**(4)** A *financial benefit is not to be treated as *exempt income merely because it is not included in your assessable income under this section.
