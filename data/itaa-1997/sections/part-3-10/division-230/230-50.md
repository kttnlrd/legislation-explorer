# 230-50  Financial arrangement (equity interest or right or obligation in relation to equity interest)

<a id="s230-50-1"></a>
**(1)** You also have a financial arrangement if you have an *equity interest. The equity interest constitutes the financial arrangement.

<a id="s230-50-2"></a>
**(2)** You also have a financial arrangement if:

> <a id="s230-50-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** you have, under an *arrangement:

> > <a id="s230-50-2-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** a legal or equitable right to receive something that is a financial arrangement under this section; or

> > <a id="s230-50-2-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** a legal or equitable obligation to provide something that is a financial arrangement under this section; or

> > <a id="s230-50-2-a-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)** a combination of one or more such rights and/or obligations; and

> <a id="s230-50-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the right, obligation or combination does not constitute, or form part of, a financial arrangement under subsection 230-45(1). The right, obligation or combination referred to in paragraph (a) constitutes the financial arrangement.

> **Note:** Paragraph 230-40(4)(e) prevents the accruals method or the realisation method being applied to something that is a financial arrangement under this section.

> **Note:** Subsection 230-270(1) prevents the retranslation method being applied to something that is a financial arrangement under this section.

> **Note:** Subsection 230-330(1) prevents the hedging method being applied to something that is a financial arrangement under this section.
