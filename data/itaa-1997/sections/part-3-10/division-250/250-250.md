# 250-250 Allocating gain or loss to income years

<a id="s250-250-1"></a>
**(1)** You are taken, for the purposes of section 250-205, to make, for an income year, a gain or loss equal to a part of a gain or loss if:

<a id="s250-250-1-a"></a>
**(a)** that part of the gain or loss is allocated to an interval under section 250-245; and

<a id="s250-250-1-b"></a>
**(b)** that interval falls wholly within that income year.

<a id="s250-250-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

<a id="s250-250-2-a"></a>
**(a)** a part of a gain or loss is allocated to an interval under section 250-245; and

<a id="s250-250-2-b"></a>
**(b)** that interval straddles 2 income years; you are taken, for purposes of section 250-205, to make a gain or loss equal to so much of that part of the gain or loss as is allocated between those income years on a reasonable basis.

<a id="s250-250-3"></a>
**(3)** If:

<a id="s250-250-3-a"></a>
**(a)** a *consolidated group or *MEC group has a *financial arrangement; and

<a id="s250-250-3-b"></a>
**(b)** a subsidiary member of the group ceases to be a member of the group at a particular time (the exit time); and

<a id="s250-250-3-c"></a>
**(c)** immediately after the exit time, the subsidiary member has the financial arrangement; an income year of the group is taken, for the purposes of applying this section to the group and the financial arrangement, to end at the exit time.
