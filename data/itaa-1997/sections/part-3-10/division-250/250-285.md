# 250-285 Treatment of asset after Division ceases to apply to the asset

<a id="s250-285-1"></a>
**(1)** For the purposes of Division 40, if:

> <a id="s250-285-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** this Division applies to you and an asset; and

> <a id="s250-285-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the *arrangement period for the *tax preferred use of the asset ends at a particular time; and

> <a id="s250-285-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** the asset would have had an *adjustable value at that time, for the purposes of Division 40, if this Division had never applied to the asset; the adjustable value of the asset, immediately after the end of the arrangement period, is taken to be equal to the amount worked out using the following method statement: Method statement
- Step 1. Work out whether section
- 250-150 applies. Step 2. If section
- 250-150 does not apply, the amount is the *end value of the asset at the end of the arrangement period. Step 3. If section
- 250-150 does apply, the amount is worked out by:

> <a id="s250-285-1-a-2"></a>
> **(a)** multiplying the *end value of the asset at the end of the *arrangement period by the *disallowed capital percentage; and

> <a id="s250-285-1-b-2"></a>
> **(b)** then multiplying the adjustable value of the asset at the end of the arrangement period (worked out under section 40-85) by 100% minus the disallowed capital percentage); and

> <a id="s250-285-1-c-2"></a>
> **(c)** then adding the amount obtained under paragraph (a) and the amount obtained under paragraph (b).

<a id="s250-285-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

> <a id="s250-285-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** this Division applies to you and an asset; and

> <a id="s250-285-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the *arrangement period for the *tax preferred use of the asset ends; and

> <a id="s250-285-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** a net amount is included in your assessable income in relation to the *financial benefits that are *subject to the deemed loan treatment (taking into account the adjustments under Subdivision 250-E in relation to the financial benefits that are subject to the deemed loan treatment); the cost base, and the *reduced cost base, of the asset are each * taken to be reduced at the end of the arrangement period by an amount equal to the difference between:

> <a id="s250-285-2-d"></a>
> **(d)** the total amounts or values of the financial benefits that were subject to deemed loan treatment; and

> <a id="s250-285-2-e"></a>
> **(e)** the net amount referred to in paragraph (c).

> **Note:** See subsection (6) in relation to the application of paragraph (d).

<a id="s250-285-3"></a>
**(3)** If:

> <a id="s250-285-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** this Division applies to you and an asset; and

> <a id="s250-285-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** the *arrangement period for the *tax preferred use of the asset ends; and

> <a id="s250-285-3-c"></a>
> **(c)** a net amount is allowed to you as a deduction in relation to the *financial benefits that are *subject to the deemed loan treatment (taking into account the adjustments under Subdivision 250-E in relation to the financial benefits that are subject to the deemed loan treatment);

> <a id="s250-285-3-d"></a>
> **(d)** the total amounts or values of the financial benefits that were subject to deemed loan treatment; and

> <a id="s250-285-3-e"></a>
> **(e)** the net amount referred to in paragraph (c).

> **Note:** See subsection (6) in relation to the application of paragraph (d).

<a id="s250-285-4"></a>
**(4)** If:

> <a id="s250-285-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** this Division applies to you and an asset; and

> <a id="s250-285-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** the *arrangement period for the *tax preferred use of the asset ends; and

> <a id="s250-285-4-c"></a>
> **(c)** a net amount is included in your assessable income in relation to the *financial benefits that are *subject to the deemed loan treatment (taking into account the adjustments under Subdivision 250-E in relation to the financial benefits that are subject to the deemed loan treatment); then, in determining the profit or loss on the sale of the asset, a deduction equal to the difference between the following is taken to have been allowed for expenditure by you in connection with the asset:

> <a id="s250-285-4-d"></a>
> **(d)** the total amounts or values of the financial benefits that were subject to deemed loan treatment; and

> <a id="s250-285-4-e"></a>
> **(e)** the net amount referred to in paragraph (c).

> **Note:** See subsection (6) in relation to the application of paragraph (d).

<a id="s250-285-5"></a>
**(5)** If:

> <a id="s250-285-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** this Division applies to you and an asset; and

> <a id="s250-285-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** the *arrangement period for the *tax preferred use of the asset ends; and

> <a id="s250-285-5-c"></a>
> **(c)** a net amount is allowed to you as a deduction in relation to the *financial benefits that are *subject to the deemed loan treatment (taking into account the adjustments under Subdivision 250-E in relation to the financial benefits that are subject to the deemed loan treatment); then, in determining the profit or loss on the sale of the asset, a deduction equal to the sum of the following is taken to have been allowed for expenditure by you in connection with the asset:

> <a id="s250-285-5-d"></a>
> **(d)** the total amounts or values of the financial benefits that were subject to deemed loan treatment; and

> <a id="s250-285-5-e"></a>
> **(e)** the net amount referred to in paragraph (c).

> **Note:** See subsection (6) in relation to the application of paragraph (d).

<a id="s250-285-6"></a>
**(6)** In applying paragraphs (2)(d), (3)(d), (4)(d) and (5)(d), disregard subsection 250-160(2) (reasonable estimate of end value treated as financial benefit subject to deemed loan treatment).
