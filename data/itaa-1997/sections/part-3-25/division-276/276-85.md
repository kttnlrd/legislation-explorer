# 276-85 Member's assessable income or tax offsets for determined member components—specific rules


<a id="s276-85-1"></a>
**(1)** This section makes clarifications and modifications of the operation of section 276-80 in respect of a *member of an *AMIT in respect of an income year.

<a id="s276-85-2"></a>
**(2)** For the purposes of this Act, if an amount is included in the *member's assessable income because of the operation of this section, treat that amount as being so included because of the operation of subsection 276-80(2). Discount capital gains

<a id="s276-85-3"></a>
**(3)** Subsection (4) applies if the *member has, for the income year, a *determined member component of the character of:

> <a id="s276-85-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** a *discount capital gain from a *CGT asset that is *taxable Australian property; or

> <a id="s276-85-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** a discount capital gain from a CGT asset that is not taxable Australian property.

<a id="s276-85-4"></a>
**(4)** For the purposes of section 276-80 and this section, treat the amount of the component as being double what it would be apart from this subsection. Franking credit gross-up

<a id="s276-85-5"></a>
**(5)** Subsection (6) applies if the *member has, for the income year, a *determined member component (the franking credit gross-up Australian managed investment trusts: attribution managed investment trusts Division Section 276-90 component) of the character of assessable income under subsection 207-20(1) (franking credit gross-up).

<a id="s276-85-6"></a>
**(6)** For the purposes of subsection 207-20(1) (franking credit gross-up), treat the reference in that subsection to the amount of the *franking credit on the distribution as instead being a reference to the amount of the franking credit gross-up component. Limitation on circumstances in paragraph 276-80(2)(b)

<a id="s276-85-7"></a>
**(7)** The circumstances mentioned in paragraph 276-80(2)(b) or (5)(b) do not include the following:

> <a id="s276-85-7-a"></a>
> **(a)** the residence of the trustee of the *AMIT;

> <a id="s276-85-7-b"></a>
> **(b)** the place of the central management and control of the AMIT.
