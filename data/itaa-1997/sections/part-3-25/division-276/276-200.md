# 276-200 What this Subdivision is about

A member's member component of a particular character is so much of an AMIT's determined trust component of that character (see Subdivision 276-E) as is attributable to membership interests held by the member, worked out in accordance with certain requirements. trusts Section 276-205 A member's determined member component of a particular character is the amount stated to be the member's member component of that character in an AMMA statement (see Subdivision 276-H).
