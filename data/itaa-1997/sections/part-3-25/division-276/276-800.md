# 276-800  What this Subdivision is about

If a trust ceases to be an AMIT, and discovers an under or over from an income year when it was an AMIT, the under or over will have taxation consequences for the trust in the discovery year. 276-805    Application of Subdivision to former AMIT 276-810    Continue to work out trust components, unders, overs etc. 276-815    Effect of increase 276-820    Effect of decrease Operative provisions
