# 276-805  Application of Subdivision to former AMIT

This Subdivision applies if:

> <a id="s276-805-a"></a>
> **(a)** a trust was an *AMIT for an income year; and

> <a id="s276-805-b"></a>
> **(b)** the trust is not an AMIT for a later income year (the discovery year).
