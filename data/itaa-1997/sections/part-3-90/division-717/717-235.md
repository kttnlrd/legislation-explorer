# 717-235  What this Subdivision is about

Each attribution surplus and post FIF abolition surplus relating to a company that ceases to be a subsidiary member of a consolidated group is transferred to that company from the head company of the group. 717-240    Object of this Subdivision Transfers 717-245    Attribution surpluses 717-255    FIF surpluses 717-262    Deferred attribution credits Object
