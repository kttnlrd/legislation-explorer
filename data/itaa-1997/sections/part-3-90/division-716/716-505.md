# 716-505  History for entitlement to tax offset: joining entity

If:

> <a id="s716-505-a"></a>
> **(a)** a company becomes a *subsidiary member of a *consolidated group; and

> <a id="s716-505-b"></a>
> **(b)** apart from this section, things happening in relation to the company before it became a subsidiary member would, because of section 701-5 (the entry history rule), be taken into account as things happening in relation to the *head company for working out the head company's *aggregated turnover for the purposes of section 355-100 (tax offsets for R&D); the things happening are not to be taken into account as mentioned in paragraph (b).
