# 719-450  What this Subdivision is about

The head company of a MEC group is taken to meet the conditions in section 165-123 (about maintaining the same ownership in an ownership test period to be able to deduct a bad debt) if and only if the top company for the group at the start of the period meets those conditions for the period. Maintaining the same ownership to be able to deduct bad debt 719-455     Special test for deducting a bad debt because a company maintains the same owners 719-460     Assumptions about nothing happening to affect direct and indirect ownership of the test company 719-465     Assumptions about the test company failing to meet the conditions in section 165-123 Maintaining the same ownership to be able to deduct bad debt
