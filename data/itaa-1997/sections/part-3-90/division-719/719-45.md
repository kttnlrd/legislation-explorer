# 719-45  Application of sections 703-20 and 703-25

<a id="s719-45-1"></a>
**(1)** For the purposes of this Division, if an item in section 703-20 refers to an income year, an entity is covered by that item at a particular time if, and only if, that time is in that income year.

<a id="s719-45-2"></a>
**(2)** For the purposes of this Division, if a condition in item 1, 2 or 3 of the table in section 703-25 refers to an income year, an entity meets that condition at a particular time if, and only if, that time is in that income year. Choice to consolidate a potential MEC group
