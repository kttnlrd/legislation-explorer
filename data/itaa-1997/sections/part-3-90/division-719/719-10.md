# 719-10 What is a potential MEC group?

**(2)** For the purposes of column 3 of the table, if there are one or more entities interposed between an entity (the test entity) and an eligible tier-1 company, the test entity can be a wholly-owned subsidiary of the eligible tier-1 company only if each of the interposed entities:

<a id="s719-10-2-a"></a>
**(a)** meets the conditions in columns 1 and 2 of the table; or

<a id="s719-10-2-b"></a>
**(b)** holds membership interests only as a nominee of one or more entities each of which is:

<a id="s719-10-2-b-i"></a>
**(i)** an eligible tier-1 company of the top company; or

<a id="s719-10-2-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** a wholly-owned subsidiary of an eligible tier-1 company of the top company, being a subsidiary that meets the conditions in columns 1 and 2 of the table.

<a id="s719-10-3"></a>
**(3)** For the purposes of subparagraph (2)(b)(ii), in determining whether an entity is a wholly-owned subsidiary of an eligible *tier-1 company of the *top company, assume that all of the *membership interests that are beneficially owned by eligible tier-1 companies of the top company were owned by a single eligible tier-1 company of the top company. Only one eligible tier-1 company in a potential MEC group

<a id="s719-10-6"></a>
**(6)** To avoid doubt, if:

<a id="s719-10-6-a"></a>
**(a)** there is only one *eligible tier-1 company of a *top company; and

<a id="s719-10-6-b"></a>
**(b)** there are no entities which meet the requirements of the table in subsection (1); and

<a id="s719-10-6-c"></a>
**(c)** there are no entities for which the requirements mentioned in subparagraph (1)(b)(ii) are met; and

<a id="s719-10-6-d"></a>
**(d)** there are no entities for which the requirements mentioned in subparagraph (1)(b)(iii) are met; * the potential MEC group derived from the eligible tier-1 company consists of the eligible tier-1 company alone. When potential MEC group ceases to exist

<a id="s719-10-7"></a>
**(7)** If a *potential MEC group is derived from one or more *eligible tier-1 companies of a *top company, the potential MEC group ceases to exist when:

<a id="s719-10-7-a"></a>
**(a)** none of those companies are eligible tier-1 companies of the top company; or

<a id="s719-10-7-b"></a>
**(b)** there is a change in the identity of the top company, and the eligible tier-1 companies that were members of the group immediately before the change are not the same as the eligible tier-1 companies that are members of the group immediately after the change. Continuity of potential MEC group

<a id="s719-10-8"></a>
**(8)** If: 

<a id="s719-10-8-a"></a>
**(a)** a *potential MEC group is derived from one or more *eligible tier-1 companies of a *top company; and

<a id="s719-10-8-b"></a>
**(b)** there is a change in the identity of the top company in relation to the potential MEC group; and

<a id="s719-10-8-c"></a>
**(c)** the eligible tier-1 companies that were members of the group immediately before the change are the same as the eligible tier-1 companies that are members of the group immediately after the change; the change does not affect the continuity of:

<a id="s719-10-8-d"></a>
**(d)** the group; or

<a id="s719-10-8-e"></a>
**(e)** the status of any of those companies as eligible tier-1 companies of the top company.
