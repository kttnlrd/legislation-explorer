# 719-790 Exception for interests in entity leaving MEC group

Membership interests in leaving entity

<a id="s719-790-1"></a>
**(1)** If:

<a id="s719-790-1-a"></a>
**(a)** the realised interest is a *membership interest; and

<a id="s719-790-1-b"></a>
**(b)** during the ownership period the first entity ceased to be a *subsidiary member of a *MEC group; the first condition in section 719-775 cannot be satisfied, because of that MEC group, at a time when the first entity was a member of the group, unless the interest needed to be disregarded under section 719-30 (about employee shares) in order for the first entity to be a member of the group at that time. Liabilities owed by leaving entity

<a id="s719-790-2"></a>
**(2)** If the realised interest:

<a id="s719-790-2-a"></a>
**(a)** consists of a liability owed by the first entity to the owner; and

<a id="s719-790-2-b"></a>
**(b)** became an asset of the owner because subsection 701-1(1) (the single entity rule) ceased to apply to the first entity when it ceased to be a *subsidiary member of a *MEC group; the first condition in section 719-775 cannot be satisfied, because of that MEC group, at a time when the first entity was a member of the group.
