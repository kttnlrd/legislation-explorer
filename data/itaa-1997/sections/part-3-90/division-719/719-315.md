# 719-315  Further adjustment of available fractions for all bundles

<a id="s719-315-1"></a>
**(1)** If, because of the application event:

> <a id="s719-315-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** there is under section 719-305 an *available fraction for the *bundle of prior group losses; and

> <a id="s719-315-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** section 719-310 affects the available fraction for one or more other bundles of losses; this section affects the available fraction for every one of those bundles.

<a id="s719-315-2"></a>
**(2)** The available fraction (as affected by section 719-305 or 719-310) is reduced by multiplying it by this fraction: *Available fraction for the *bundle of prior group losses Sum of the *available fraction for every *bundle of losses whose available fraction is affected by this section

<a id="s719-315-3"></a>
**(3)** For the purposes of working out the fraction in subsection (2), use the value of an *available fraction for a *bundle of losses apart from:

> <a id="s719-315-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** this section; and

> <a id="s719-315-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** if item 5 of the table in subsection 707-320(2) would apply as a result of the calculation of the available fraction in accordance with section 719-305 or 719-310—that item.
