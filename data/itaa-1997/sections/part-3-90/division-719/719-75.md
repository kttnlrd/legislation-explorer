# 719-75  Head company

Group in existence throughout income year

<a id="s719-75-1"></a>
**(1)** If:

> <a id="s719-75-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** a company is the *provisional head company of a *MEC group at the end of the income year of the company; and

> <a id="s719-75-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the group was in existence throughout the income year; the company is the head company of the group at all times during the income year. Group comes into existence in income year

<a id="s719-75-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

> <a id="s719-75-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** a company is the *provisional head company of a *MEC group at the end of the income year of the company; and

> <a id="s719-75-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the group is in existence at the end of the income year; and

> <a id="s719-75-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** the group came into existence in the income year; that company is the head company of the group at all times during the period:

> <a id="s719-75-2-d"></a>
> **(d)** beginning when the group came into existence; and

> <a id="s719-75-2-e"></a>
> **(e)** ending at the end of the income year. Group ceases to exist in income year

<a id="s719-75-3"></a>
**(3)** If:

> <a id="s719-75-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** a *MEC group ceases to exist in an income year of a company; and

> <a id="s719-75-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** the company was the *provisional head company of the group immediately before the group ceased to exist; that company is the head company of the group at all times during the period:

> <a id="s719-75-3-c"></a>
> **(c)** beginning at whichever is the later of:

> > <a id="s719-75-3-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)** the start of the income year; and

> > <a id="s719-75-3-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** the time the group came into existence; and

> <a id="s719-75-3-d"></a>
> **(d)** ending at the time when the group ceased to exist. Notice of events affecting group
