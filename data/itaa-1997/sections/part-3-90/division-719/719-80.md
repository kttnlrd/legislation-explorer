# 719-80 Notice of events affecting MEC group

<a id="s719-80-1"></a>
**(1)** If an event (the notifiable event) described in column 2 of an item of the table happens in relation to a *MEC group, the entity described in column 3 of the item must give the Commissioner notice in the *approved form of the notifiable event.

<a id="s719-80-2"></a>
**(2)** The entity described in column 3 of the relevant item must give notice of the notifiable event:

<a id="s719-80-2-a"></a>
**(a)** if:

<a id="s719-80-2-a-i"></a>
**(i)** the group came into existence because of a choice under section 719-50; and

<a id="s719-80-2-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** the notifiable event happens before the relevant notice is given to the Commissioner under section 719-76 (notice of choice to consolidate); no later than the day mentioned in subsection (3); or

<a id="s719-80-2-b"></a>
**(b)** if:

<a id="s719-80-2-b-i"></a>
**(i)** the group results from a *special conversion event; and

<a id="s719-80-2-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** a choice under section 703-50 is made in relation to the *consolidated group mentioned in paragraph 719-40(1)(b); and

<a id="s719-80-2-b-iii"></a>
**(iii)** the notifiable event happens before the relevant notice is given to the Commissioner under section 703-58 (notice of choice to consolidate); no later than the day mentioned in subsection (3); or

<a id="s719-80-2-c"></a>
**(c)** in any other case—within 28 days after the notifiable event. 

<a id="s719-80-3"></a>
**(3)** The day is:

<a id="s719-80-3-a"></a>
**(a)** if the entity is required to give the Commissioner its *income tax return for the income year during which the notifiable event happens—the day on which the company gives the Commissioner that income tax return; or

<a id="s719-80-3-b"></a>
**(b)** otherwise—the last day in the period within which the entity would be required to give the Commissioner such a return if it were required to give the Commissioner such a return. Effects of change of head company
