# 701-56 Application of subsection 701-55(6)


<a id="s701-56-1"></a>
**(1)** Subsection (2) applies in relation to each asset that would be an asset of an entity at the time (the joining time) it becomes a *subsidiary member of a *consolidated group, assuming that subsection 701-1(1) (the single entity rule) did not apply. (1A) Subsection (2) applies only to the extent necessary for the purposes of subsection 701-55(6) to determine whether a provision of this Act is to apply in relation to each of those assets on and after the joining time. (1B) Subsection (2) applies despite section 701-5 (the entry history rule).

<a id="s701-56-2"></a>
**(2)** Treat the *head company as having acquired each of those assets at the joining time as part of acquiring the business of the joining entity as a going concern. Certain depreciating assets etc.

<a id="s701-56-3"></a>
**(3)** Subsection 701-55(6) does not apply in relation to an asset if any of the following provisions are to apply in relation to the asset:

<a id="s701-56-3-a"></a>
**(a)** Subdivision 40-F (Primary production depreciating assets);

<a id="s701-56-3-b"></a>
**(b)** Subdivision 40-G (Capital expenditure of primary producers and other landholders);

<a id="s701-56-3-c"></a>
**(c)** Subdivision 40-H (Capital expenditure that is immediately deductible);

<a id="s701-56-3-d"></a>
**(d)** Subdivision 40-I (Capital expenditure that is deductible over time);

<a id="s701-56-3-e"></a>
**(e)** Subdivision 40-J (Capital expenditure for the establishment of trees in carbon sink forests);

<a id="s701-56-3-f"></a>
**(f)** Division 41 (Additional deduction for certain new business investment);

<a id="s701-56-3-g"></a>
**(g)** Division 43 (Deductions for capital works).
