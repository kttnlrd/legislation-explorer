# 703-65 Application

- Sections
- 703-70 to
- 703-80 set out the effects if a company (the interposed company) chooses under subsection
- 615-30(2) that a *consolidated group is to continue in existence at and after the time referred to in that subsection as the completion time.

**Note:** The choice is one of the conditions for a compulsory roll-over under Division 615 on an exchange of shares in the head company of a consolidated group for shares in the interposed company.
