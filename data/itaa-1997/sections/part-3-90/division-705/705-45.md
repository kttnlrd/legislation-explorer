# 705-45  Reduction in tax cost setting amount for accelerated depreciation assets

<a id="s705-45-1"></a>
**(1)** If:

> <a id="s705-45-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** an asset of the joining entity is a *depreciating asset to which Division 40 applies; and

> <a id="s705-45-1-aa"></a>
> **(aa)** just before the entity became a subsidiary member, subsection 40-10(3) or 40-12(3) of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997 applied for the purposes of the joining entity working out the asset's decline in value under Division 40; and

> **Note:** The effect of those subsections was to preserve an entitlement to accelerated depreciation.

> <a id="s705-45-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the asset's *tax cost setting amount would be greater than the joining entity's *terminating value for the asset; and

> <a id="s705-45-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** the *head company chooses to apply this section to the asset; the asset's tax cost setting amount is reduced so that it equals the terminating value.

> **Note:** A consequence of the choice is that accelerated depreciation will apply to the asset: see section 701-80.

> **Note:** Unlike the position with a reduction in tax cost setting amount under section 705-40, the amount of the reduction is not re-allocated among other assets.

<a id="s705-45-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

> <a id="s705-45-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** an asset of the joining entity is a *depreciating asset to which Division 40 applies; and

> <a id="s705-45-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** any of the following has applied before the joining entity became a *subsidiary member for the purposes of working out the asset's decline in value under Division 40:

> > <a id="s705-45-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** section 40-82;

> > <a id="s705-45-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** Subdivision 40-BA of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997;

> > <a id="s705-45-2-b-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)** Subdivision 40-BB of that Act; and

> <a id="s705-45-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** the asset's *tax cost setting amount would be greater than the joining entity's *terminating value for the asset; members of consolidated groups Section 705-47 the asset's tax cost setting amount is reduced so that it equals the terminating value.

> **Note:** The provisions referred to in paragraph (b) provide for an accelerated decline in value of certain assets.

> **Note:** Unlike the position with a reduction in tax cost setting amount under section 705-40, the amount of the reduction is not re-allocated among other assets.
