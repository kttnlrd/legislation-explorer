# 705-230  Adjustments to restrict step 4 reduction of allocable cost amount to effective distributions to head company in respect of direct membership interests

Object

<a id="s705-230-1"></a>
- **(1)**  The object of this section is to ensure that, in working out the group's *allocable cost amount for the linked entities, the reduction under step 4 in the table in section
- 705-60 (about pre-formation time distributions out of certain profits) is made only for profits that have been effectively distributed to the *head company in respect of its direct *membership interests in the entities. This ensures consistency with the ordering rule in section
- 705-225. When section applies

<a id="s705-230-2"></a>
**(2)**  This section applies to a distribution to the extent that the following conditions are satisfied:

> <a id="s705-230-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the distribution is made by a linked entity;

> <a id="s705-230-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  in working out the group's *allocable cost amount for the linked entity there would, apart from this section, be a reduction under step 4 in the table in section 705-60 for the distribution. Step 4 reduction only if subject distribution is made to head company

<a id="s705-230-3"></a>
**(3)**  There is no reduction as mentioned in subsection (2) for the distribution unless it is made to the *head company of the group.
