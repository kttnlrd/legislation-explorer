# 705-195  Modified application of subsection 705-65(6)

Object

<a id="s705-195-1"></a>
**(1)** The object of this section is to ensure that certain *non-membership equity interests held by *members of the acquiring group that are part of the cost of acquiring the acquired group are taken into account in working out the acquiring group's *allocable cost amount for the acquired group. Non-membership equity interests

<a id="s705-195-2"></a>
**(2)** Subsection 705-65(6) has effect as if it also treated as a *membership interest in the *head company of the acquired group a *non-membership equity interest in a *subsidiary member of the acquired group, where that interest was held at the acquisition time by a *member of the acquiring group.
