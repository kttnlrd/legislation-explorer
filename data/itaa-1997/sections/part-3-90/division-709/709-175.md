# 709-175 Head company is former exempting entity

<a id="s709-175-1"></a>
**(1)** Subsection (2) operates if:

> <a id="s709-175-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the *head company of a *consolidated group is a *former exempting entity; and

> <a id="s709-175-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** a *corporate tax entity becomes a *subsidiary member of the group at a time (also the joining time); and

> <a id="s709-175-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** the entity is an *exempting entity at the joining time.

<a id="s709-175-2"></a>
**(2)** These rules apply to the *consolidated group. Rules applying to *consolidated group Item Rule 1 There is no change to the status of the *head company 2 If the subsidiary member's *franking account has a *franking surplus at the joining time:

> <a id="s709-175-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** a debit equal to that surplus arises in that account at the joining time; and

> <a id="s709-175-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** a credit equal to that surplus arises in the *exempting account of the *head company at the joining time 3 Subsection 709-60(2) (about franking surplus) does not apply to the *subsidiary member

> **Note:** If the subsidiary's franking account is in deficit, it will be liable for franking deficit tax: see subsection 709-60(3). 

> **Note:** The subsidiary's franking account does not operate while it is a member of the group: see section 709-65.

<a id="s709-175-3"></a>
**(3)** Subsection (4) operates if:

> <a id="s709-175-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** the *head company of a *consolidated group is a *former exempting entity; and

> <a id="s709-175-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** a *corporate tax entity becomes a *subsidiary member of the group at a time (also the joining time); and

> <a id="s709-175-3-c"></a>
> **(c)** the entity is a *former exempting entity at the joining time.

<a id="s709-175-4"></a>
**(4)** These rules apply to the *consolidated group. Rules applying to *consolidated group Item Rule 1 There is no change to the status of the *head company 2 If the *subsidiary member's *exempting account has an *exempting surplus at the joining time:

> <a id="s709-175-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** a debit equal to that surplus arises in that account at the joining time; and

> <a id="s709-175-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** a credit equal to that surplus arises in the exempting account of the *head company at the joining time 3 If the *subsidiary member's *exempting account has an *exempting deficit at the joining time:

> <a id="s709-175-4-a-2"></a>
> **(a)** a credit equal to that deficit arises in that account at the joining time; and

> <a id="s709-175-4-b-2"></a>
> **(b)** a debit equal to that deficit arises in the subsidiary's *franking account just before the joining time 4 The *subsidiary member's *exempting account does not operate during the period:

> <a id="s709-175-4-a-3"></a>
> **(a)** starting just after the joining time; and

> <a id="s709-175-4-b-3"></a>
> **(b)** ending when the entity ceases to be a subsidiary member of the group

> **Note:** If the subsidiary's franking account is in deficit, it will be liable for franking deficit tax: see subsection 709-60(3). This deficit may be increased by item 3 in the table in subsection (4).

> **Note:** The subsidiary's franking account does not operate while it is a member of the group: see section 709-65.

<a id="s709-175-5"></a>
**(5)** There is no change to the status of the *head company of a *consolidated group if:

> <a id="s709-175-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** the head company is a *former exempting entity; and

> <a id="s709-175-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** a *corporate tax entity becomes a *subsidiary member of the group; and

> <a id="s709-175-5-c"></a>
> **(c)** the entity is neither an *exempting entity nor a former exempting entity at the joining time.

> **Note:** If the subsidiary's franking account is in surplus, that surplus will be transferred to the head company's franking account: see subsection 709-60(2).

> **Note:** If the subsidiary's franking account is in deficit, it will be liable for franking deficit tax: see subsection 709-60(3).

> **Note:** The subsidiary's franking account does not operate while it is a member of the group: see section 709-65.
