# 709-170 Head company and subsidiary are exempting entities

There is no change to the status of the *head company of a *consolidated group if:

<a id="s709-170-a"></a>
**(a)** the head company is an *exempting entity; and

<a id="s709-170-b"></a>
**(b)** a *corporate tax entity becomes a *subsidiary member of the group at a time (also the joining time); and

<a id="s709-170-c"></a>
**(c)** the entity is an exempting entity at the joining time.

**Note:** If the subsidiary's franking account is in surplus, that surplus will be transferred to the head company's franking account: see subsection 709-60(2).

**Note:** If the subsidiary's franking account is in deficit, it will be liable for franking deficit tax: see subsection 709-60(3).

**Note:** The subsidiary's franking account does not operate while it is a member of the group: see section 709-65.
