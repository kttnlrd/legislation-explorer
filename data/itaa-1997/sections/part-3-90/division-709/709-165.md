# 709-165  Subsidiary member is former exempting entity

<a id="s709-165-1"></a>
**(1)** This section operates if:

> <a id="s709-165-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the *head company of a *consolidated group is neither an exempting entity nor a *former exempting entity; and

> <a id="s709-165-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** a *corporate tax entity becomes a *subsidiary member of the group at a time (also the joining time); and

> <a id="s709-165-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** the entity is a *former exempting entity at the joining time.

<a id="s709-165-2"></a>
**(2)** These rules apply to the *consolidated group. Rules applying to *consolidated group Item      Rule 1         The *head company becomes a *former exempting entity at the joining time 2         The *head company has both a *franking account and an *exempting account Rules applying to *consolidated group Item      Rule 3         If the *subsidiary member's *exempting account has an *exempting surplus at the joining time:

> <a id="s709-165-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** a debit equal to that surplus arises in that account at the joining time; and

> <a id="s709-165-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** a credit equal to that surplus arises in the exempting account of the *head company at the joining time 4         If the *subsidiary member's *exempting account has an *exempting deficit at the joining time:

> <a id="s709-165-2-a-2"></a>
> **(a)** a credit equal to that deficit arises in that account at the joining time; and

> <a id="s709-165-2-b-2"></a>
> **(b)** a debit equal to that deficit arises in the subsidiary's *franking account just before the joining time 5         The *subsidiary member's *exempting account does not operate during the period:

> <a id="s709-165-2-a-3"></a>
> **(a)** starting just after the joining time; and

> <a id="s709-165-2-b-3"></a>
> **(b)** ending when the entity ceases to be a subsidiary member of the group 6         Item 1 of the table in section 208-115 does not apply to the *head company 7         Item 1 of the table in section 208-120 does not apply to the *head company 8         Item 1 of the table in section 208-130 does not apply to the *head company 9         Item 1 of the table in section 208-145 does not apply to the *head company

> **Note:** Any surplus in the subsidiary's franking account will be transferred to the head company's franking account: see subsection 709-60(2).

> **Note:** If the subsidiary's franking account is in deficit, it will be liable for franking deficit tax: see subsection 709-60(3). This deficit may be increased by item 4 in the table in subsection (2).

> **Note:** The subsidiary's franking account does not operate while it is a member of the group: see section 709-65.
