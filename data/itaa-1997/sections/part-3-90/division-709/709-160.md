# 709-160 Subsidiary member is exempting entity

<a id="s709-160-1"></a>
**(1)** This section operates if:

> <a id="s709-160-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the *head company of a *consolidated group is neither an exempting entity nor a *former exempting entity; and

> <a id="s709-160-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** a *corporate tax entity becomes a *subsidiary member of the group at a time (the joining time); and

> <a id="s709-160-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** the entity is an *exempting entity at the joining time.

<a id="s709-160-2"></a>
**(2)** These rules apply to the *consolidated group. Rules applying to *consolidated group Item Rule 1 The *head company becomes a *former exempting entity at the joining time 2 The *head company has both a *franking account and an *exempting account 3 If the *subsidiary member's *franking account has a *franking surplus at the joining time:

> <a id="s709-160-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** a debit equal to that surplus arises in that account at the joining time; and

> <a id="s709-160-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** a credit equal to that surplus arises in the *exempting account of the *head company at the joining time

> **Note:** If the subsidiary's franking account is in deficit, it will be liable for franking deficit tax: see subsection 709-60(3).

> **Note:** The subsidiary's franking account does not operate while it is a member of the group: see section 709-65.
