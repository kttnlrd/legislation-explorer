# 713-210 Partnership cost setting interests

A partnership cost setting interest in a partnership is the asset that is comprised of:

<a id="s713-210-a"></a>
**(a)** an interest in an asset of the partnership; or

<a id="s713-210-b"></a>
**(b)** an interest in the partnership that is not covered by paragraph (a); but does not include an asset that is comprised of a *membership interest in the partnership. 

**Note:** A partner may have more than one partnership cost setting interest that relates to an asset of the partnership (see section 106-5).

**Note:** A partnership cost setting interest may relate to an asset of the partnership, but the asset of the partnership is not a partnership cost setting interest in the partnership.
