# 715-700  Choices leaving entity can make ignoring exit history rule

Application

<a id="s715-700-1"></a>
**(1)** This section has effect if:

> <a id="s715-700-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** an entity ceases to be a *subsidiary member of a *consolidated group at a time (the leaving time); and

> <a id="s715-700-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the question whether the *head company of the group had made a choice (however described) under a provision (the choice provision) listed in the table in subsection 715-660(1) was relevant to working out that company's liability (if any) for income tax, or the entity's loss (if any) of a particular *sort, calculated by reference to an income year starting before the leaving time.

> **Note:** Declarations, elections and selections made under the choice provision at the option of a company are all examples of choices under that provision (even though it does not call them choices) because the company has chosen to make them.

<a id="s715-700-2"></a>
**(2)** The main objects of this section are:

> <a id="s715-700-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** to override section 701-40 (Exit history rule) and let the entity make a choice (however described) under the choice provision with effect after the leaving time; and

> <a id="s715-700-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** to extend, in some cases, the time for the entity to make such a choice after the leaving time; and

> <a id="s715-700-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** to modify, in some cases, the rules about when such a choice by the entity starts to have effect. Overriding the exit history rule

<a id="s715-700-3"></a>
**(3)** For the entity core purposes set out in section 701-1 (Single entity rule) relating to income years ending after the leaving time, ignore a choice (however described) made by the *head company of the *consolidated group under the choice provision or the absence of such a choice. Fresh choice by the entity

<a id="s715-700-4"></a>
**(4)** The entity may make a choice (however described) under the provision if the question whether the entity has made such a choice is relevant to working out the entity's liability (if any) for income tax, or loss (if any) of a particular *sort, calculated by reference to an income year ending after the leaving time. Extension of time for fresh choice by the entity

<a id="s715-700-5"></a>
**(5)** If there is a time limit (apart from this subsection) on the entity making such a choice, the entity has until the later of these times to make the choice:

> <a id="s715-700-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** the last time it may make the choice under the provision (apart from this section);

> <a id="s715-700-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** the end of 90 days after the leaving time or, if the Commissioner allows a later time for the purposes of this paragraph, that later time. Start of effect of choice

<a id="s715-700-6"></a>
**(6)** If the entity makes a choice because of this section, the choice starts to have effect:

> <a id="s715-700-6-a"></a>
> **(a)** at the leaving time; or

> <a id="s715-700-6-b"></a>
> **(b)** if the choice relates (explicitly or implicitly) to one or more whole income years—for the income year in which the leaving time occurs. Relationship with other provisions

<a id="s715-700-7"></a>
**(7)** Section 701-40 (Exit history rule) and the choice provision have effect subject to this section. Choices leaving entity can make ignoring exit history rule to overcome inconsistencies
