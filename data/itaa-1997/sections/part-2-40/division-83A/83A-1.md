# 83A-1  What this Division is about

Your assessable income includes discounts on shares, rights and stapled securities you (or your associate) acquire under an employee share scheme. You may be entitled:

> <a id="s83A-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** to have the amount included in your assessable income reduced; or

> <a id="s83A-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** to have the income year in which it is included deferred.
