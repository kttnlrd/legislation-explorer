# 83-165  What this Subdivision is about

This Subdivision defines what are genuine redundancy payments and early retirement scheme payments. If you receive a genuine redundancy payment or an early retirement scheme payment, you do not have to pay income tax on the payment so far as it does not exceed a certain amount worked out under this Subdivision. A part of a genuine redundancy payment or an early retirement scheme payment that is not tax free under this Subdivision will normally be an employment termination payment. payments
