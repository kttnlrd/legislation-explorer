# 82-130 What is an employment termination payment?

<a id="s82-130-1"></a>
**(1)** A payment is an employment termination payment if:

> <a id="s82-130-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** it is received by you:

> > <a id="s82-130-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** in consequence of the termination of your employment; or

> > <a id="s82-130-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** after another person's death, in consequence of the termination of the other person's employment; and

> <a id="s82-130-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** it is received no later than 12 months after that termination (but see subsection (4)); and

> <a id="s82-130-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** it is not a payment mentioned in section 82-135.

> **Note:** If a payment would be an employment termination payment but for paragraph (b), see subsection (4) and section 83-295.

> **Note:** The holding of an office is treated as employment for this Part: see section 80-5. Also, the termination of employment is treated as including the termination of employment by retirement or by death: see section 80-10. Types of employment termination payment

<a id="s82-130-2"></a>
**(2)** A life benefit termination payment is an *employment termination payment to which subparagraph (1)(a)(i) applies.

<a id="s82-130-3"></a>
**(3)** A death benefit termination payment is an *employment termination payment to which subparagraph (1)(a)(ii) applies. Exemption from 12 month rule

<a id="s82-130-4"></a>
**(4)** Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply to you if:

> <a id="s82-130-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** you are covered by a determination under subsection (5) or (7); or

> <a id="s82-130-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** the payment is a *genuine redundancy payment or an *early retirement scheme payment. payments
