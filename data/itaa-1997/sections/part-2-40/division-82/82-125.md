# 82-125  What this Subdivision is about

This Subdivision defines an employment termination payment as a payment made in consequence of the termination of a person’s employment that is received no later than 12 months after the termination (though the 12 month restriction is relaxed in some circumstances). An employment termination payment can be a life benefit termination payment (received by the person whose employment is terminated) or a death benefit termination payment (received by another person after the death of a person whose employment is terminated). Certain types of payments are declared not to be employment termination payments. Various other terms used in describing the taxation treatment of employment termination payments are defined in the Subdivision.
