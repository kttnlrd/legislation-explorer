# 82-155  What is a pre-July 83 segment of an employment termination payment?

<a id="s82-155-1"></a>
**(1)** An *employment termination payment includes a pre-July 83 segment if any of the employment to which the payment relates occurred before 1 July 1983.

<a id="s82-155-2"></a>
**(2)** Work out the amount of the pre-July 83 segment as follows: Step 1.      Subtract the *invalidity segment (if any) from the *employment termination payment. Step 2.      Multiply the amount at step 1 by the fraction: Number of days of employment to which the payment relates that occurred before 1 July 1983 Total number of days of employment to which the payment relates
