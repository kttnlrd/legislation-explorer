# 292-325  Further amendment of an amended particular

If:

> <a id="s292-325-a"></a>
> **(a)** an *excess non-concessional contributions tax assessment has been amended (the earlier amendment) in any particular; and

> <a id="s292-325-b"></a>
> **(b)** the Commissioner is of the opinion that it would be just to further amend the assessment in that particular; the Commissioner may do so within a period of 4 years after the earlier amendment.
