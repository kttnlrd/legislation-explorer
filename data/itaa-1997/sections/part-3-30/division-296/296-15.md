# 296-15  Liability for tax

- Subject to sections
- 296-20 and
- 296-25, you are liable to pay *Division 296 tax for an income year if you have *taxable superannuation earnings for the year.

**Note:** The amount of the tax is set out in the Superannuation (Building a Stronger and Fairer Super System) Imposition Act 2026.
