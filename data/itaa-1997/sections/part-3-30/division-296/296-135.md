# 296-135  When tax is payable—amended assessments

<a id="s296-135-1"></a>
**(1)** If the Commissioner amends your assessment, any extra *assessed Division 296 tax resulting from the amendment is due and payable 84 days after the day the Commissioner gives you notice of the amended assessment. Exception for tax deferred to a Division 296 debt account

<a id="s296-135-2"></a>
**(2)** However, subsection (1) does not apply to an amount of extra *assessed Division 296 tax that is *deferred to a Division 296 debt account for a *superannuation interest.

**Note:** For deferred to a Division 296 debt account, see Division 134 in Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953.

**Note:** For release of money from a superannuation plan to pay these amounts, see Division 131 in that Schedule.
