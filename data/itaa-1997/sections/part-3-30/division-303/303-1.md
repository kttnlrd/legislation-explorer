# 303-1  What this Division is about

Under Subdivision 303-A, the tax treatment of superannuation income stream benefits that are defined benefit income can be less favourable to you if that income exceeds your defined benefit income cap. Subdivision 303-B sets out special circumstances in which superannuation benefits are neither assessable income nor exempt income.
