# 303-4  Meaning of defined benefit income cap

<a id="s303-4-1"></a>
**(1)** Your defined benefit income cap for a *financial year is the following amount (rounded up to the nearest dollar): The *general transfer balance cap for the *financial year

<a id="s303-4-2"></a>
**(2)** Despite subsection (1) of this section, if a particular day in a *financial year is the first day in relation to which section 301-10, 301-100, 302-65 or 302-85:

> <a id="s303-4-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** applies to you in respect of an amount of *defined benefit income; or

> <a id="s303-4-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** would apart from this Subdivision apply to you in respect of an amount of defined benefit income; your defined benefit income cap for the financial year is the following amount (rounded up to the nearest dollar): The *general transfer balance 1 + Number of days remaining in the cap for the *financial year        *financial year after that day ´ 16                Number of days in the *financial year

<a id="s303-4-3"></a>
**(3)** Despite subsections (1) and (2) of this section, if:

> <a id="s303-4-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** in a case where subsection (1) applies—during the *financial year, you receive any amounts of *defined benefit income to which none of sections 301-10, 301-100, 302-65 and 302-85 apply; or

> <a id="s303-4-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** in a case where subsection (2) applies—during the financial year, you receive after the day mentioned in that subsection any amounts of defined benefit income to which none of sections 301-10, 301-100, 302-65 and 302-85 apply; your defined benefit income cap for the financial year under subsection (1) or (2) (as the case requires) is reduced by the sum of those amounts.
