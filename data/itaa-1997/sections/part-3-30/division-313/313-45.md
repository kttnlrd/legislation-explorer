# 313-45  What this Subdivision is about

If an amount is released from your superannuation interests under the first home super saver scheme, and you do not enter into a contract within a particular period to purchase or construct a residential premises, you may make one or more non-concessional contributions. If you do not notify the Commissioner that you have made the contributions, you may be liable for tax under Subdivision 313-E. Operative provisions 313-50      Contributing amounts to superannuation
