# 302-60  All of superannuation lump sum is tax free

A *superannuation lump sum that you receive because of the death of a person of whom you are a *death benefits dependant is not assessable income and is not *exempt income. Superannuation income stream—either deceased died aged 60 or above or dependant aged 60 or above
