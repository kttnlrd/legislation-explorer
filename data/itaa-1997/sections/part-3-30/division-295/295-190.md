# 295-190 Personal contributions and roll-over amounts

<a id="s295-190-1"></a>
**(1)** The assessable income of an entity includes amounts as set out in this table.

**Note:** For an explanation of the acronyms used, see section 295-35. Personal contributions and roll-over amounts included in assessable income Item Assessable income of this entity: Includes: 1 CSF A contribution: *RSA provider (a) made to the CSF or *RSA; and

<a id="s295-190-1-b"></a>
**(b)** covered by a valid and acknowledged notice given to the *superannuation provider of the CSF or RSA under section 290-170 Personal contributions and roll-over amounts included in assessable income Item Assessable income of this entity: Includes: 2 CSF A *roll-over superannuation benefit CADF that an individual is taken to receive under section 307-15 to the extent that: N-CADF *RSA provider (a) it consists of an *element untaxed in the fund (other than an element untaxed in the fund under subsection 307-290(4)); and

<a id="s295-190-1-b-2"></a>
**(b)** is not an *excess untaxed roll-over amount for that individual 2A CSF A *roll-over superannuation benefit *RSA provider that an individual is taken to receive under section 307-15 to the extent that:

<a id="s295-190-1-a"></a>
**(a)** the CSF or *RSA is a *successor fund; and

<a id="s295-190-1-b-3"></a>
**(b)** the benefit relates to a contribution that, before it was transferred to the successor fund, was not covered by a valid and acknowledged notice given to any *superannuation provider under section 290-170; and

<a id="s295-190-1-c"></a>
**(c)** while the benefit is held in the successor fund, the contribution becomes covered by a valid and acknowledged notice given to the superannuation provider of the successor fund under that section 3 CSF The *taxable component of a directed CADF termination payment (within the *RSA provider meaning of section 82-10F of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997) (1A) Items 2 and 2A of the table in subsection (1) do not apply to a *roll-over superannuation benefit that is a *departing Australia

<a id="s295-190-2"></a>
**(2)** A contribution referred to in item 1 is included in the income year in which it is received if the notice is received by the *superannuation provider by the day the provider lodges its *income tax return for that income year.

<a id="s295-190-3"></a>
**(3)** Otherwise it is included in the income year in which the notice is received.

<a id="s295-190-4"></a>
**(4)** A payment referred to in item 2 or 3 is included in the income year in which it is received by the *superannuation provider.

<a id="s295-190-5"></a>
**(5)** A benefit referred to in item 2A is included in the income year in which it is received if the notice is received by the *superannuation provider by the day the provider lodges its *income tax return for that income year.

<a id="s295-190-6"></a>
**(6)** Otherwise it is included in the income year in which the notice is received.
