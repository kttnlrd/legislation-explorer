# 295-495  Amounts that cannot be deducted

These entities cannot deduct anything for these amounts:

> **Note:** For an explanation of the acronyms used, see section 295-35. Amounts that cannot be deducted Item      This entity           Cannot deduct anything for: 1         CSF                   *Superannuation benefits 2         N-CSF                 *Superannuation benefits (except amounts paid as mentioned in item 4 of the table in section 295-490) 3         *RSA provider         *Superannuation benefits paid from, or amounts withdrawn from, *RSAs 4         *RSA provider         Amounts credited to *RSAs 5         CSF                   A repayment of a grant of financial assistance under N-CSF                 Part 23 of the Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993 CADF N-CADF 6         CSF                   An amount payable to a person under an income stream N-CSF                 because of the person's temporary inability to engage in *gainful employment *RSA provider
