# 295-325 Previously complying funds

The amount of *ordinary income and *statutory income from previous years included in the assessable income of a fund in an income year under item 2 of the table in section 295-320 is: Sum of the part of the *crystallised undeducted contributions Sum of the *market values of that relates to the period after the fund's assets just before – 30 June 1983 and the *contributions the start of the income year segment for current members at that time so far as they have not been, and cannot be, deducted
