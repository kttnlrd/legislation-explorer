# 295-405  Other exempt income

The *ordinary income or *statutory income of an entity is exempt from income tax as set out in this table.

**Note:** For an explanation of the acronyms used, see section 295-35. Exempt income Item      For this entity:     This is exempt: 1         CSF                  A grant of financial assistance under Part 23 of the N-CSF                Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993 CADF N-CADF 2         *RSA provider        Amount credited to the *RSA where a *superannuation income stream covered by section 295-407 was paid from the RSA for all of the period in the income year that the RSA existed 3         *RSA provider        Part of an amount credited to the *RSA (worked out under section 295-410) where a *superannuation income stream covered by section 295-407 was paid from the RSA for part of the period in the income year that the RSA existed
