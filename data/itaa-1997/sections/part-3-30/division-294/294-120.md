# 294-120  What this Subdivision is about

Certain defined benefit lifetime pensions that are subject to commutation restrictions cannot result in excess transfer balance (instead, Subdivision 303-A applies to the superannuation income stream benefits). Certain commutation-restricted income streams started before 1 July 2017 are covered by the same modification.
