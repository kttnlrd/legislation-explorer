# 293-1 What this Division is about

This Division reduces the concessional tax treatment of certain superannuation contributions made for high income individuals. The high income threshold is $250,000. There are special rules for defined benefit interests, constitutionally protected State higher level office holders, certain Commonwealth justices and temporary residents who depart Australia.

**Note:** Part 3-20 in Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953 contains rules about the administration of the Division 293 tax.
