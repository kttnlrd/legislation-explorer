# 293-20 Your taxable contributions

<a id="s293-20-1"></a>
**(1)** If the sum of:

<a id="s293-20-1-a"></a>
**(a)** your *income for surcharge purposes for an income year (disregarding your *reportable superannuation contributions); and

<a id="s293-20-1-b"></a>
**(b)** your *low tax contributions for the corresponding *financial year; exceeds $250,000, you have taxable contributions for the income year equal to the lesser of the low tax contributions and the amount of the excess.

<a id="s293-20-2"></a>
**(2)** However, you do not have taxable contributions for an income year if the amount of your *low tax contributions is nil.
