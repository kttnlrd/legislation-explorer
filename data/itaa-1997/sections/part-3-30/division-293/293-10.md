# 293-10  What this Subdivision is about

This Subdivision reduces the superannuation tax concession for high income earners. An individual's income is added to certain superannuation contributions and compared to the high income threshold of $250,000. A tax is payable on the excess, or on the superannuation contributions (whichever is less).
