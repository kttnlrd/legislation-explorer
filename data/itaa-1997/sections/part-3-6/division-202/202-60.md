# 202-60  Amount of the franking credit on a distribution

<a id="s202-60-1"></a>
**(1)** The amount of the *franking credit on a *distribution is that stated in the *distribution statement for the distribution, unless that amount exceeds the *maximum franking credit for the distribution.

<a id="s202-60-2"></a>
**(2)** The maximum franking credit for a *distribution is worked out using the formula: 1 Amount of the *frankable distribution ´ Applicable gross-up rate where: applicable gross-up rate means the *corporate tax gross-up rate of the entity making the distribution for the income year in which the distribution is made.
