# 216-20  Cum dividend sale—statement by securities dealer

If:

> <a id="s216-20-a"></a>
> **(a)** section 216-5 applies in relation to a *franked distribution or a *distribution franked with an exempting credit (cum dividend sales); and

> <a id="s216-20-b"></a>
> **(b)** a *securities dealer has acted for a particular party to the contract concerned; the securities dealer must, as soon as practicable after the making of the distribution, give to the other party to the contract a statement in the *approved form setting out such information in relation to the distribution as is required by the approved form.
