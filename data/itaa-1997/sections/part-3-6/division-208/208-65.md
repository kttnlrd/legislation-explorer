# 208-65  What this Subdivision is about

The amount of the exempting credit on a distribution is that stated in the distribution statement, unless the amount stated exceeds the maximum franking credit for the distribution. In that case, it is nil. Operative provisions 208-70      Amount of the exempting credit on a distribution Operative provisions
