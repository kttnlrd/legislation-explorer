# 208-180  What is an entity's share of the exempting credit on a distribution?

- To work out an entity's share of the *exempting credit on a *distribution *franked with that credit, use sections
- 207-55 and
- 207-57 to work out what the entity's share of the credit would be it if were a *franking credit on a *franked distribution. The entity's share of the exempting credit is equal to that amount.
