# 204-15  Linked distributions

Franking debit arises where a distribution by one entity is substituted for a distribution by another

<a id="s204-15-1"></a>
**(1)** This section gives rise to a *franking debit if:

> <a id="s204-15-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the exercise of a choice or selection by a *member of an entity (the first entity); or

> <a id="s204-15-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the member's failure to exercise a choice or selection; has the effect of determining (to any extent) that another entity makes to one of its members a *distribution (the linked distribution) that is:

> <a id="s204-15-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** in substitution (in whole or in part) for a distribution by the first entity to that member or any other member of the first entity; and

> <a id="s204-15-1-d"></a>
> **(d)** unfranked, or *franked at a *franking percentage that differs from the first entity's *benchmark franking percentage for the *franking period in which the linked distribution is made.

> **Note:** Division 205 deals with a corporate tax entity's franking account and sets out when a debit, known as a franking debit, arises in that account. Franking account in which the debit arises

<a id="s204-15-2"></a>
**(2)** The debit arises in the *franking account of the entity with the higher *benchmark franking percentage for the *franking period in which the linked distribution is made. Amount of the debit

<a id="s204-15-3"></a>
**(3)** The debit is equal to the one that would arise in that *franking account if the entity had made a *franked distribution, equal to the linked distribution, with a *franking percentage equal to the *benchmark franking percentage for that entity. When does the debit arise

<a id="s204-15-4"></a>
**(4)** The debit arises on the day on which the linked distribution is made. Debit is in addition to any other franking debit arising because of the linked distribution

<a id="s204-15-5"></a>
**(5)** The debit is in addition to any other debit that arises in an entity's *franking account because of the linked distribution. Where an entity has no benchmark franking percentage

<a id="s204-15-6"></a>
**(6)** If an entity has no *benchmark franking percentage for the *franking period in which the linked distribution is made, this section applies as if:

> <a id="s204-15-6-a"></a>
> **(a)** in a case where the linked distribution has a *franking percentage of less than 50%—the entity had a benchmark franking percentage of 100% for that period; and

> <a id="s204-15-6-b"></a>
> **(b)** in a case where the linked distribution has a franking percentage equal to or greater than 50%—the entity had a benchmark franking percentage of 0% for that period.
