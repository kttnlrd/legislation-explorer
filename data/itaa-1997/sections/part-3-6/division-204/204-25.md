# 204-25  Substituting tax-exempt bonus shares for franked distributions

Franking debit arises if tax-exempt bonus shares are issued in substitution for a franked distribution

<a id="s204-25-1"></a>
**(1)** This section gives rise to a *franking debit in an entity's *franking account if:

> <a id="s204-25-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the exercise of a choice or selection by a *member of the entity; or

> <a id="s204-25-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the member's failure to exercise a choice or selection; has the effect of determining (to any extent) that the entity issues one or more *tax-exempt bonus shares, to that member or another member of the entity, in substitution (in whole or in part) for one or more *franked distributions by the entity to that member or another member. Amount of the debit

<a id="s204-25-2"></a>
**(2)** The debit is equal to the one that would arise in the entity's *franking account if the entity made a *distribution, equal to the *franked distributions referred to in subsection (1), franked at the entity's *benchmark franking percentage for the *franking period in which the shares are issued. When does the debit arise

<a id="s204-25-3"></a>
**(3)** The debit arises on the day when the shares are issued. Meaning of tax-exempt bonus share

<a id="s204-25-4"></a>
**(4)** For a company whose *shares have no par value, tax-exempt bonus share means a share issued by the company in the circumstances mentioned in subsection 6BA(6) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936.

<a id="s204-25-5"></a>
**(5)** For any other company, tax-exempt bonus share means a *share issued by the company to a *shareholder in the company where:

> <a id="s204-25-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** the amount or value of the share is debited against an amount standing to the credit of a share premium account of the company; and

> <a id="s204-25-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** no part of the paid-up value of the share is a dividend; and

> <a id="s204-25-5-c"></a>
> **(c)** the share is issued:

> > <a id="s204-25-5-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)** as a bonus share; or

> > <a id="s204-25-5-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** in the circumstances mentioned in subsection 6BA(1) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936, as in force immediately before 1 July 1998. Where a company has no benchmark franking percentage for the franking period

<a id="s204-25-6"></a>
**(6)** If a company has no *benchmark franking percentage for the *franking period in which the *tax-exempt bonus share is issued, this section applies as if the entity had a benchmark franking percentage of 100% for that period.
