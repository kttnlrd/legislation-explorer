# 205-35  Refund of income tax, diverted profits tax or Australian DMT tax

<a id="s205-35-1"></a>
**(1)** An entity receives a refund of income tax if and only if:

> <a id="s205-35-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** either:

> > <a id="s205-35-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** the entity receives an amount as a refund; or

> > <a id="s205-35-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** the Commissioner applies a credit, or an *RBA surplus, against a liability or liabilities of the entity; and

> <a id="s205-35-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the refund of the amount, or the application of the credit, represents in whole or in part:

> > <a id="s205-35-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** a return to the entity of an amount paid or applied to satisfy the entity's liability to pay income tax; or

> > <a id="s205-35-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** the amount remaining after applying a *loss carry back tax offset, or a *tax offset that is subject to the refundable tax offset rules because of section 67-30 (about R&D), against the entity's basic income tax liability. (1A) An entity receives a refund of diverted profits tax if and only if:

> <a id="s205-35-1-a-2"></a>
> **(a)** either:

> > <a id="s205-35-1-a-i-2"></a>
> > **(i)** the entity receives an amount as a refund; or

> > <a id="s205-35-1-a-ii-2"></a>
> > **(ii)** the Commissioner applies a credit, or an *RBA surplus, against a liability or liabilities of the entity; and

> <a id="s205-35-1-b-2"></a>
> **(b)** the refund of the amount, or the application of the credit, represents in whole or in part a return to the entity of an amount paid or applied to satisfy the entity's liability to pay *diverted profits tax. (1B) An entity receives a refund of Australian DMT tax if and only if:

> <a id="s205-35-1-a-3"></a>
> **(a)** either:

> > <a id="s205-35-1-a-i-3"></a>
> > **(i)** the entity receives an amount as a refund; or

> > <a id="s205-35-1-a-ii-3"></a>
> > **(ii)** the Commissioner applies a credit, or an *RBA surplus, against a liability or liabilities of the entity; and

> <a id="s205-35-1-b-3"></a>
> **(b)** the refund of the amount, or the application of the credit, represents in whole or in part a return to the entity of an amount paid or applied to satisfy the entity's liability to pay *Australian DMT tax.

<a id="s205-35-2"></a>
**(2)** The amount of the refund is so much of the amount refunded or applied as represents the return, or amount remaining, referred to in paragraph (1)(b), (1A)(b) or (1B)(b).
