# 205-45  Franking deficit tax

Object

<a id="s205-45-1"></a>
**(1)** While recognising that an entity may anticipate *franking credits when *franking *distributions, the object of this section is to prevent those credits from being anticipated indefinitely by requiring the entity to reconcile its *franking account at certain times and levying tax if the account is in *deficit. Franking deficit at end of income year

<a id="s205-45-2"></a>
**(2)** An entity is liable to pay franking deficit tax imposed by the New Business Tax System (Franking Deficit Tax) Act 2002 if its *franking account is in *deficit at the end of an income year. Corporate tax entity ceases to be a franking entity

<a id="s205-45-3"></a>
**(3)** An entity is liable to pay *franking deficit tax imposed by the New Business Tax System (Franking Deficit Tax) Act 2002 if:

> <a id="s205-45-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** it ceases to be a *franking entity; and

> <a id="s205-45-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** immediately before it ceases to be a franking entity, its *franking account is in *deficit.

> **Note:** The tax is imposed in the New Business Tax System (Franking Deficit Tax) Act 2002 and the amount of the tax is set out in that Act.
