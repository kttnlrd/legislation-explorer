# 207-25  What this Subdivision is about

This Subdivision deals with an entity that receives a benefit of a franked distribution where:

> <a id="s207-25-a"></a>
> **(a)** the distribution is made to a partnership or the trustee of a trust; and

> <a id="s207-25-b"></a>
> **(b)** the benefit is received either directly or through other interposed partnerships or trusts. The distribution is regarded as flowing indirectly to the entity under this Subdivision.
