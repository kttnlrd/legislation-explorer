# 207-90  Distribution that is made to an entity

Whole of distribution not assessable

<a id="s207-90-1"></a>
**(1)** If:

> <a id="s207-90-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** a *franked distribution is made to an entity; and

> <a id="s207-90-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the distribution does not *flow indirectly through the entity to another entity; and

> <a id="s207-90-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** the distribution is *exempt income or *non-assessable non-exempt income in the hands of the entity; then, for the purposes of this Act:

> <a id="s207-90-1-d"></a>
> **(d)** the amount of the *franking credit on the distribution is not included in the assessable income of the entity under section 207-20; and

> <a id="s207-90-1-e"></a>
> **(e)** the entity is not entitled to a *tax offset under this Division because of the distribution. Part of distribution not assessable

<a id="s207-90-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

> <a id="s207-90-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** a *franked distribution is made to an entity; and

> <a id="s207-90-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the distribution does not *flow indirectly through the entity to another entity; and

> <a id="s207-90-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** a part of the distribution (the relevant part) is *exempt income or *non-assessable non-exempt income in the hands of the entity; then, for the purposes of this Act:

> <a id="s207-90-2-d"></a>
> **(d)** the amount of the distribution is taken to have been reduced by the relevant part; and
