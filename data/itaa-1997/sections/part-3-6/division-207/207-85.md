# 207-85  Applying this Subdivision

This Subdivision applies subject to Subdivisions 207-E and 207-F.

**Note:** Subdivision 207-E sets out exceptions to the rules in this Subdivision.

**Note:** Where both this Subdivision and Subdivision 207-F apply to an entity, the application of this Subdivision is subject to the rules in Subdivision 207-F: see subsections 207-145(3) and 207-150(7) and (8).
