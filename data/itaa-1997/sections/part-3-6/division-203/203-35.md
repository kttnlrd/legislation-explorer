# 203-35  Franking percentage

<a id="s203-35-1"></a>
**(1)** Subject to subsection (2), the franking percentage for a *frankable distribution is worked out using the formula: *Franking credit allocated to the *frankable distribution × 100 *Maximum franking credit for the distribution

<a id="s203-35-2"></a>
**(2)** If the *franking percentage for a *frankable distribution would exceed 100% if it were worked out under subsection (1), it is taken to be 100%.
