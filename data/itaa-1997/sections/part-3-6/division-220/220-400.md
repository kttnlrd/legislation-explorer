# 220-400 Gross-up and tax offset for distribution from NZ franking company reduced by supplementary dividend


<a id="s220-400-1"></a>
**(1)** This section has effect if:

> <a id="s220-400-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** an *NZ franking company:

> > <a id="s220-400-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** makes a *franked distribution to an entity (the recipient) in an income year; and

> > <a id="s220-400-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** pays a supplementary dividend (as defined in section OB1 of the Income Tax Act 1994 of New Zealand) to the recipient in connection with the franked distribution; and

> <a id="s220-400-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** an amount is included in the recipient’s assessable income for the income year under section 207-20, and the recipient is entitled to a *tax offset for the income year under that section or section 207-110; and

> <a id="s220-400-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** the recipient is entitled to a tax offset under Division 770 because of the inclusion of the *distribution in the recipient’s assessable income for the income year. Reduced gross-up

<a id="s220-400-2"></a>
**(2)** The amount included in the recipient’s assessable income under section 207-20 is reduced by the amount of the supplementary dividend (but not below zero). Reduced tax offset

<a id="s220-400-3"></a>
**(3)** The amount of the *tax offset under section 207-20 is reduced by the amount of the supplementary dividend (but not below zero). What happens if certain provisions apply

<a id="s220-400-4"></a>
**(4)** Subsections (2) and (3) do not apply to the recipient in relation to the *franked distribution if one or more of the following provisions also apply to the recipient in relation to the distribution:

> <a id="s220-400-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** subsection 207-90(1);

> <a id="s220-400-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** subsection 207-90(2);

> <a id="s220-400-4-c"></a>
> **(c)** subsection 207-145(1);

> <a id="s220-400-4-d"></a>
> **(d)** subsection 207-145(2).

<a id="s220-400-5"></a>
**(5)** If subsection 207-90(2) or 207-145(2) would also apply to the recipient in relation to the *franked distribution, apply that subsection on the basis that:

> <a id="s220-400-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** the amount of the *franking credit on the distribution; had been reduced by:

> <a id="s220-400-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** so much of the supplementary dividend as does not exceed that amount of the franking credit. Relationship with sections 207-20, 207-90 and 207-145

<a id="s220-400-6"></a>
**(6)** Sections 207-20, 207-90 and 207-145 have effect subject to this section.
