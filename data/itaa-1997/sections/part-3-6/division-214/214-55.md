# 214-55  What this Subdivision is about

The Commissioner may make an assessment of a corporate tax entity's liability to pay franking tax, and the franking account balance and the venture capital sub-account balance on which that liability is based. An entity's first franking return for an income year is treated as an assessment by the Commissioner.
