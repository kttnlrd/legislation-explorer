# 214-40  Meaning of franking tax

Each of the following is a franking tax:

> <a id="s214-40-a"></a>
> **(a)** *franking deficit tax;

> <a id="s214-40-b"></a>
> **(b)** *over-franking tax;

> <a id="s214-40-c"></a>
> **(c)** *venture capital deficit tax.
