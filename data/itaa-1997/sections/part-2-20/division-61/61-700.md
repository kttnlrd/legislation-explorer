# 61-700  Object of this Subdivision

The object of this Subdivision is to stimulate opportunities for Australian seafarers to:

> <a id="s61-700-a"></a>
> **(a)** be employed or engaged on overseas voyages; and

> <a id="s61-700-b"></a>
> **(b)** acquire maritime skills.
