# 45-1  What this Division is about

This Division is designed to prevent tax being avoided through:

> <a id="s45-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the disposal of leased plant, or an interest in leased plant; or

> <a id="s45-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the disposal of a partnership interest in a partnership that leased plant; or

> <a id="s45-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** the disposal of shares in a 100% subsidiary that leased plant; where amounts have been deducted for the decline in value of the plant. It includes amounts in assessable income. Any benefit received, and any reduction in a liability, is taken into account in calculating the amounts included. Where the disposal of shares in a 100% subsidiary is involved, the companies in the former wholly-owned group may be made jointly and severally liable for tax that the former subsidiary does not pay.
