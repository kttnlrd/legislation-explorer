# 43-10 Deductions for capital works

<a id="s43-10-1"></a>
**(1)** You can deduct an amount for capital works for an income year.

<a id="s43-10-2"></a>
**(2)** You can only deduct the amount if:

<a id="s43-10-2-a"></a>
**(a)** the capital works have a *construction expenditure area; and

<a id="s43-10-2-b"></a>
**(b)** there is a *pool of construction expenditure for that area; and Chapter 2 Capital allowances: rules about deductibility of capital expenditure 
<a id="s43-10-2-c"></a>
**(c)** you use *your area in the income year in the way set out in Table 43-140 (Current year use).

**Note:** The deduction is limited to capital works to which this Division applies, see section 43-20.

**Note:** Amongst other things, the definition of your area ensures that only owners and certain lessees of capital works, and certain holders of quasi-ownership rights over land on which capital works are constructed, can deduct an amount under this Division.
