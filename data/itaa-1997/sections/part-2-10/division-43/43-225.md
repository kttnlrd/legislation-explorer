# 43-225  What this Subdivision is about

The undeducted construction expenditure for your area is the part of your construction expenditure you have left to write off. It is used to work out: •      the number of years in which you can deduct amounts for your construction expenditure; and •      the amount that you can deduct under section 43-40 if your area or a part is destroyed.
