# 43-152  Build to rent developments

Commencement

<a id="s43-152-1"></a>
**(1)** On and after the first day on which a building has 50 or more *dwellings:

> <a id="s43-152-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** that satisfy subsection 43-153(1); and

> <a id="s43-152-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** that the owner of the dwellings chooses to form a *build to rent development in accordance with subsection (6) of this section; those dwellings are a build to rent development, of the building, that commences to be an *active build to rent development on that day.

<a id="s43-152-2"></a>
**(2)** Also, on and after the first day (if any):

> <a id="s43-152-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** after the most recent instance of a *build to rent development of a building *commencing to be an *active build to rent development; and

> <a id="s43-152-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** on which the building has 50 or more *dwellings:

> > <a id="s43-152-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** that satisfy subsection 43-153(1); and

> > <a id="s43-152-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** that were not part of a build to rent development just before that day; and

> > <a id="s43-152-2-b-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)** that the owner of the dwellings chooses to form a build to rent development in accordance with subsection (6) of this section; those dwellings are a build to rent development, of the building, that commences to be an active build to rent development on that day unless an active build to rent development *expands under subsection (3) on that day to include the dwellings. Expansion

<a id="s43-152-3"></a>
**(3)** If a building has a *build to rent development (the existing development) that has *commenced to be an *active build to rent development, on the first day (if any) on which the building has *dwellings (the new dwellings):

> <a id="s43-152-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** that taken together with the dwellings of the existing development for which the *build to rent compliance period has not ended, satisfy subsection 43-153(1); and

> <a id="s43-152-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** that are not already a part of a build to rent development; and

> <a id="s43-152-3-c"></a>
> **(c)** that the owner of the dwellings chooses to form part of the existing development in accordance with subsection (6) of this section; the existing development expands to comprise:

> <a id="s43-152-3-d"></a>
> **(d)** the dwellings of the existing development; and

> <a id="s43-152-3-e"></a>
> **(e)** the new dwellings. Cessation

<a id="s43-152-4"></a>
**(4)** A *build to rent development ceases to be an *active build to rent development if the dwellings of the active build to rent development for which the *build to rent compliance period has not ended cease to satisfy subsection 43-153(1). Build to rent compliance period

<a id="s43-152-5"></a>
**(5)** The build to rent compliance period for a *dwelling of an *active build to rent development is the 15 years beginning on the day after the day on which:

> <a id="s43-152-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** unless paragraph (b) applies—the development *commences to be an active build to rent development; or

> <a id="s43-152-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** if:

> > <a id="s43-152-5-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** the dwelling is not part of the development when it commences to be an active build to rent development; but

> > <a id="s43-152-5-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** the development *expands to include the dwelling; the development expands to include the dwelling.

<a id="s43-152-6"></a>
**(6)** To make a choice for the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), subparagraph (2)(b)(iii) or paragraph (3)(c) in respect of *dwellings, the owner of the dwellings must:

> <a id="s43-152-6-a"></a>
> **(a)** make the choice in the *approved form; and

> <a id="s43-152-6-b"></a>
> **(b)** give it to the Commissioner.

<a id="s43-152-7"></a>
**(7)** The choice is taken to be made on the following day:

> <a id="s43-152-7-a"></a>
> **(a)** if:

> > <a id="s43-152-7-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** the owner nominates a day in the choice; and

> > <a id="s43-152-7-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** the Commissioner receives the choice before the nominated day; the nominated day; or

> <a id="s43-152-7-b"></a>
> **(b)** otherwise—the day the Commissioner receives the choice.
