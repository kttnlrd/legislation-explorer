# 43-245  What this Subdivision is about

You may deduct an amount for the undeducted construction expenditure for your area if your area or part of it is destroyed in the circumstances described in section 43-40. This Subdivision shows you how to work out that deduction. The calculations in this Subdivision are made separately for each part of the capital works that is identified as your area.
