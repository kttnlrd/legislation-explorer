# 40-1025  Non-arm's length transactions

If an entity incurred capital expenditure under an *arrangement and:

> <a id="s40-1025-a"></a>
> **(a)** there is at least one other party to the arrangement with whom the entity did not deal at *arm's length; and

> <a id="s40-1025-b"></a>
> **(b)** apart from this section, the amount of the expenditure would be more than the *market value of what it was for; the amount of expenditure taken into account under this Subdivision is that market value.
