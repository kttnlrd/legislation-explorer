# 40-70 Diminishing value method

<a id="s40-70-1"></a>
**(1)** You work out the decline in value of a *depreciating asset for an income year using the diminishing value method in this way: Days held 150% Base value   365 Asset's * effective life where: base value is:

<a id="s40-70-1-a"></a>
**(a)** for the income year in which the asset's *start time occurs— its *cost; or

<a id="s40-70-1-b"></a>
**(b)** for a later year—the sum of its *opening adjustable value for that year and any amount included in the second element of its cost for that year. days held is the number of days you *held the asset in the income year from its *start time, ignoring any days in that year when you did not use the asset, or have it *installed ready for use, for any purpose.

**Note:** If you recalculate the effective life of a depreciating asset, you use that recalculated life in working out your deduction. You can choose to recalculate effective life because of changed circumstances: see section 40-110. That section also requires you to recalculate effective life in some cases.

**Note:** The effective life of a vessel can change in some cases: see subsection 40-103(2). Exception: intangibles

<a id="s40-70-2"></a>
**(2)** You cannot use the *diminishing value method to work out the decline in value of:

<a id="s40-70-2-a"></a>
**(a)** *in-house software; or

<a id="s40-70-2-b"></a>
**(b)** an item of *intellectual property (except copyright in a *film); or

<a id="s40-70-2-c"></a>
**(c)** a *spectrum licence; or

<a id="s40-70-2-e"></a>
**(e)** a *telecommunications site access right. Limit on decline

<a id="s40-70-3"></a>
**(3)** The decline in value of a *depreciating asset under this section for an income year cannot be more than the amount that is the asset's *base value for that income year.
