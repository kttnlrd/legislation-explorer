# 40-1120  Cost base and reduced cost base of exploration benefits etc.

If:

<a id="s40-1120-a"></a>
**(a)** under a *farm-in farm-out arrangement, you receive an *exploration benefit; and

<a id="s40-1120-b"></a>
**(b)** the benefit involves one or more undertakings of the kinds referred to in subparagraphs 40-1100(2)(a)(ii) and (iv); the first element of the *cost base and the *reduced cost base of the benefit are reduced by the *market value of the undertakings.
