# 40-660 Non-arm's length transactions

If you incurred capital expenditure under an *arrangement and:

<a id="s40-660-a"></a>
**(a)** there is at least one other party to the arrangement with whom you did not deal at *arm's length; and

<a id="s40-660-b"></a>
**(b)** apart from this section, the amount of the expenditure would be more than the *market value of what it was for; the amount of expenditure you take into account under this Subdivision is that market value. Chapter 2 Capital allowances: rules about deductibility of capital expenditure
