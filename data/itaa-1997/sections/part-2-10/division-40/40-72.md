# 40-72  Diminishing value method for post-9 May 2006 assets

<a id="s40-72-1"></a>
**(1)** You work out the decline in value of a *depreciating asset for an income year using the diminishing value method in this way if you started to *hold the asset on or after 10 May 2006: Days held              200% *Base value 365          Asset's *effective life where: days held has the same meaning as in subsection 40-70(1).

> **Note:** If you recalculate the effective life of a depreciating asset, you use that recalculated life in working out your deduction. You can choose to recalculate effective life because of changed circumstances: see section 40-110. That section also requires you to recalculate effective life in some cases.

<a id="s40-72-2"></a>
**(2)** You cannot use the *diminishing value method to work out the decline in value of:

> <a id="s40-72-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** *in-house software; or

> <a id="s40-72-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** an item of *intellectual property (except copyright in a *film); or

> <a id="s40-72-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** a *spectrum licence; or

> <a id="s40-72-2-e"></a>
> **(e)** a *telecommunications site access right. Limit on decline

<a id="s40-72-3"></a>
**(3)** The decline in value of a *depreciating asset under this section for an income year cannot be more than the amount that is the asset's *base value for that income year.
