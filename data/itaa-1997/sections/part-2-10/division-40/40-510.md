# 40-510  What this Subdivision is about

You can deduct amounts for capital expenditure on depreciating assets that are water facilities, horticultural plants, fodder storage assets or fencing assets. The amount you can deduct is equal to the asset's decline in value during an income year (as measured under this Subdivision).
