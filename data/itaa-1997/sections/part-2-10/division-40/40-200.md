# 40-200 Exclusion from cost

The *cost of a *depreciating asset that is not *plant does not include any amount that was incurred:

<a id="s40-200-a"></a>
**(a)** before 1 July 2001; or

<a id="s40-200-b"></a>
**(b)** under a contract entered into before that day.
