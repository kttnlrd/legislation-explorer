# 320-130  What this Subdivision is about

This Subdivision explains how a life insurance company's income tax is worked out. For that purpose, this Subdivision enables a life insurance company to have taxable incomes and tax losses of the following classes: •       the complying superannuation class; •       the ordinary class.
