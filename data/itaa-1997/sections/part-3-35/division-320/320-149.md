# 320-149 Provisions that apply only in relation to the ordinary class

<a id="s320-149-1"></a>
**(1)** The provisions covered by subsection (2):

> <a id="s320-149-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** have effect as provided by section 320-135 in relation to a *life insurance company's taxable income, or *tax loss, of the *ordinary class; but

> <a id="s320-149-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** have no effect in relation to the company's taxable income, or tax loss, of the *complying superannuation class.

<a id="s320-149-2"></a>
**(2)** This subsection covers these provisions:

> <a id="s320-149-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** section 36-55;

> <a id="s320-149-2-aa"></a>
> **(aa)** Division 160 (Corporate loss carry back tax offset for 2020-21, 2021-22 or 2022-23 for businesses with turnover under $5 billion);

> <a id="s320-149-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** Division 165 (except Subdivision 165-CD).

> **Example:** A life insurance company that has an amount of excess franking offsets will need to recalculate its tax loss of the ordinary class under section 36-55. But its tax loss of the complying superannuation class is unaffected by that section.

> **Example:** A life insurance company that fails to meet the relevant tests of Division 165 will need to recalculate the ordinary class of its taxable income and tax loss under Subdivision 165-B. But the complying superannuation class of its taxable income and tax loss are unaffected by that Subdivision.
