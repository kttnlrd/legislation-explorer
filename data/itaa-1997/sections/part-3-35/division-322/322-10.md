# 322-10  HIH Trust exempt from tax

The total *ordinary income and *statutory income of:

> <a id="s322-10-a"></a>
> **(a)** the HIH Trust; and

> <a id="s322-10-b"></a>
> **(b)** an entity prescribed for the purposes of this Division; is exempt from income tax.
