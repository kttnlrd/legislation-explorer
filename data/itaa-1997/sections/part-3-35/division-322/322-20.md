# 322-20 What this Subdivision is about

This Act applies to a payment of an entitlement under Part VC (Financial claims scheme for policyholders with insolvent general insurers) of the Insurance Act 1973 as if the payment were made by the insurer under the insurance policy concerned. Disregard a capital gain or loss from:

<a id="s322-20-a"></a>
**(a)** the disposal to APRA under that Part of rights against the insurer under an insurance policy; or

<a id="s322-20-b"></a>
**(b)** the payment of an entitlement under that Part.
