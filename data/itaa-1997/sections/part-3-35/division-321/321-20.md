# 321-20 How the value of adjusted liability for incurred claims is worked out

Work out the value, at the end of an income year, of a *general insurance company's adjusted *liability for incurred claims under *general insurance policies in this way: Method statement
Step 1. Use the *applicable insurance contracts accounting standard to measure, at the end of the income year, the company's *liability for incurred claims under *general insurance policies, but when doing so disregard any claims handling costs that are neither attached to, nor directly attributable to, a particular claim. Step 2. Using that standard, reduce the result from step 1 by so much of that result as the company expects at the end of the income year to recover under a reinsurance contract:

<a id="s321-20-a"></a>
**(a)** within the meaning of that standard; but

<a id="s321-20-b"></a>
