# 30-85 Developing country relief funds

<a id="s30-85-1"></a>
**(1)** For the purposes of item 9.1.1 of the table in subsection 30-80(1), a country is covered by this section if:

> <a id="s30-85-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** it is included in the list of official development assistance recipients published from time to time by the Organisation Chapter 2 Rules about deductibility of particular kinds of amounts Part 2-5 Gifts or contributions Division 30 Section 30-86 for Economic Co-operation and Development's Development Assistance Committee; or

> <a id="s30-85-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** it is specified in a declaration under subsection (2) of this section.

<a id="s30-85-2"></a>
**(2)** For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), the *Foreign Affairs Minister may, by legislative instrument, make a declaration specifying a country as a developing country.
