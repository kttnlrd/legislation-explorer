# 30-226  What this Subdivision is about

An entity must ensure certain details must appear on a receipt it issues for a gift that:

> <a id="s30-226-a"></a>
> **(a)** is made to the entity or a fund, authority or institution it operates; and

> <a id="s30-226-b"></a>
> **(b)** is of a kind that the giver can deduct under Subdivision 30-A. If the entity has an ABN, the Australian Business Registrar must state in the Australian Business Register that the entity is a deductible gift recipient.
