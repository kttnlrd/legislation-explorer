# 30-220 Reducing the amount you can deduct

<a id="s30-220-1"></a>
**(1)** The amount you can deduct is reduced by a reasonable amount if:

<a id="s30-220-1-a"></a>
**(a)** the terms and conditions on which the gift is made are such that the recipient: Chapter 2 Rules about deductibility of particular kinds of amounts Part 2-5 Gifts or contributions Division 30 Section 30-225

<a id="s30-220-1-a-i"></a>
**(i)** does not receive immediate custody and control of the property; or

<a id="s30-220-1-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** does not have the unconditional right to retain custody and control of the property in perpetuity; or

<a id="s30-220-1-a-iii"></a>
**(iii)** does not obtain an immediate, indefeasible and unencumbered legal and equitable title to the property; or

<a id="s30-220-1-b"></a>
**(b)** the custody, control or use of the property by the recipient is affected by an *arrangement entered into in respect of the making of the gift.

<a id="s30-220-2"></a>
**(2)** In deciding what is a reasonable amount, have regard to the effect of those terms and conditions, or that *arrangement, on the *GST inclusive market value of the gift. Joint ownership of property
