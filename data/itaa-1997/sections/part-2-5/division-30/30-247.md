# 30-247 Gifts and covenants for which elections can be made

<a id="s30-247-1"></a>
**(1)** An election under this Subdivision may be made for a gift, made on or after 1 July 2003, that is:

<a id="s30-247-1-a"></a>
**(a)** a gift of:

<a id="s30-247-1-a-i"></a>
**(i)** money; or

<a id="s30-247-1-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** property valued by the Commissioner at more than $5,000; made to a fund, authority or institution covered by item 1 or 2 of the table in section 30-15; or Chapter 2 Rules about deductibility of particular kinds of amounts Part 2-5 Gifts or contributions Division 30 Section 30-248

<a id="s30-247-1-b"></a>
**(b)** a gift that is covered by item 4, 5 or 6 of the table in section 30-15.

<a id="s30-247-2"></a>
**(2)** An election under this Subdivision may also be made for entering into a *conservation covenant, under Division 31, on or after 1 July 2003.
