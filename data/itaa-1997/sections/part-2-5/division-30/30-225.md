# 30-225  Gift of property by joint owners

If:

> <a id="s30-225-a"></a>
> **(a)** you own property jointly with one or more other entities; and

> <a id="s30-225-b"></a>
> **(b)** you and the other entities make a gift of the property; and

> <a id="s30-225-c"></a>
> **(c)** you would have been able to deduct the gift under section 30-15 because of item 4, 5 or 6 of the table in that section if you had made a gift of the property as sole owner of it; you can deduct so much of the gift as is reasonable, having regard to your interest in the property.
