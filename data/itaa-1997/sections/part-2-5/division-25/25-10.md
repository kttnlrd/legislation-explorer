# 25-10  Repairs

<a id="s25-10-1"></a>
**(1)** You can deduct expenditure you incur for repairs to premises (or part of premises) or a *depreciating asset that you held or used solely for the *purpose of producing assessable income. Property held or used partly for that purpose

<a id="s25-10-2"></a>
**(2)** If you held or used the property only partly for that purpose, you can deduct so much of the expenditure as is reasonable in the circumstances. No deduction for capital expenditure

<a id="s25-10-3"></a>
**(3)** You cannot deduct capital expenditure under this section.
