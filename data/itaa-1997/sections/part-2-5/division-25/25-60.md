# 25-60  Parliament election expenses

<a id="s25-60-1"></a>
**(1)** You can deduct expenditure you incur in contesting an election for membership of:

> <a id="s25-60-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the Parliament of the Commonwealth; or

> <a id="s25-60-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the Parliament of a State; or

> <a id="s25-60-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** the Legislative Assembly for the Australian Capital Territory; or

> <a id="s25-60-1-d"></a>
> **(d)** the Legislative Assembly of the Northern Territory of Australia.

> **Note:** Entertainment expenses are excluded: see section 25-70.

> **Note:** If you receive an amount as recoupment of the expenditure, the amount may be included in your assessable income: see Subdivision 20-A.
