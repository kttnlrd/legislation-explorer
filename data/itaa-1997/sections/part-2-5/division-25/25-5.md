# 25-5 Tax-related expenses


<a id="s25-5-1"></a>
**(1)** You can deduct expenditure you incur to the extent that it is for:

<a id="s25-5-1-a"></a>
**(a)** managing your *tax affairs; or

<a id="s25-5-1-b"></a>
**(b)** complying with an obligation imposed on you by a *Commonwealth law, insofar as that obligation relates to the *tax affairs of an entity; or

<a id="s25-5-1-ca"></a>
**(ca)** a penalty under Subdivision 162-D of the *GST Act; or

<a id="s25-5-1-cb"></a>
**(cb)** levy under the Major Bank Levy Act 2017; or

<a id="s25-5-1-d"></a>
**(d)** obtaining a valuation in accordance with section 30-212 or 31-15; or

<a id="s25-5-1-e"></a>
**(e)** managing your *Australian GloBE tax affairs; or

<a id="s25-5-1-f"></a>
**(f)** complying with an obligation imposed on you by a *Commonwealth law, insofar as that obligation relates to the Australian GloBE tax affairs of an entity.

**Note:** To find out whether a trustee of a deceased estate can deduct expenditure under this section, see subsection 69(7) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936.

**Note:** If you receive an amount as recoupment of the expenditure, the amount may be included in your assessable income: see Subdivision 20-A. No deduction for certain expenditure

<a id="s25-5-2"></a>
**(2)** You cannot deduct under subsection (1):

<a id="s25-5-2-a"></a>
**(a)** *tax; or

<a id="s25-5-2-b"></a>
**(b)** an amount withheld or payable under Part 2-5 or Part 2-10 in Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953; or

<a id="s25-5-2-c"></a>
**(c)** expenditure for *borrowing money (including payments of interest) to pay an amount covered by paragraph (a) or (b); or

<a id="s25-5-2-d"></a>
**(d)** expenditure for a matter relating to the commission (or possible commission) of an offence against an *Australian law or a *foreign law; or

<a id="s25-5-2-e"></a>
**(e)** a fee or commission for advice about the operation of a *Commonwealth law relating to taxation, unless that advice is provided by a *recognised tax adviser. No deduction for expenditure excluded from general deductions

<a id="s25-5-3"></a>
**(3)** You cannot deduct expenditure under subsection (1) to the extent that a provision of this Act (except section 8-1) expressly prevents or limits your deducting it under section 8-1 (about general deductions). It does not matter whether the provision specifically refers to section 8-1. No deduction for capital expenditure

<a id="s25-5-4"></a>
**(4)** You cannot deduct capital expenditure under subsection (1). However, for this purpose, expenditure is not capital expenditure merely because the *tax affairs or *Australian GloBE tax affairs concerned relate to matters of a capital nature.

**Example:** Under this section, you can deduct expenditure you incur in applying for a private ruling on whether you can depreciate an item of property. Use of property taken to be for income producing purpose

<a id="s25-5-5"></a>
**(5)** Under some provisions of this Act it is important to decide whether you used property for the *purpose of producing assessable income. For provisions of that kind, your use of property is taken to be for that purpose insofar as you use the property for:

<a id="s25-5-5-a"></a>
**(a)** managing your *tax affairs; or

<a id="s25-5-5-b"></a>
**(b)** complying with an obligation imposed on you by a *Commonwealth law, insofar as that obligation relates to the *tax affairs of another entity.

**Example:** You buy a computer to prepare your tax returns. The expenditure you incur in buying the computer is capital expenditure and cannot be deducted under this section. However, to the extent that you use the computer in preparing your income tax return, you will be able to deduct the decline in value of your computer under Division 40. That is because, under this subsection, the computer is property that you are taken to use for the purpose of producing assessable income.

<a id="s25-5-6"></a>
**(6)** If another provision of this Act expressly provides that a particular use of property is not taken to be for the *purpose of producing assessable income, that provision overrides subsection (5). Expenditure by trustee of deceased estate

<a id="s25-5-8"></a>
**(8)** If:

<a id="s25-5-8-a"></a>
**(a)** after you die, the trustee of your deceased estate incurs expenditure; and

<a id="s25-5-8-b"></a>
**(b)** had you incurred the expenditure before you died, you could have deducted it under subsection (1); for the purposes of assessing the trustee for the income year in which you died, the expenditure is a deduction under that subsection.
