# 25-100  Travel between workplaces

When a deduction is allowed

<a id="s25-100-1"></a>
**(1)** If you are an individual, you can deduct a *transport expense to the extent that it is incurred in your *travel between workplaces. Transport expense (1A) A transport expense is a loss or outgoing to do with transport (including the decline in value of a *depreciating asset used in connection with transport) but does not include:

> <a id="s25-100-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** a loss or outgoing for accommodation or for food or drink; or

> <a id="s25-100-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** expenditure incidental to transport. Travel between workplaces

<a id="s25-100-2"></a>
**(2)** Your travel between workplaces is travel directly between 2 places, to the extent that:

> <a id="s25-100-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** while you were at the first place, you were:

> > <a id="s25-100-2-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** engaged in activities to gain or produce your assessable income; or

> > <a id="s25-100-2-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** engaged in activities in the course of carrying on a *business for the purpose of gaining or producing your assessable income; and

> <a id="s25-100-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the purpose of your travel to the second place was to:

> > <a id="s25-100-2-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** engage in activities to gain or produce your assessable income; or

> > <a id="s25-100-2-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** engage in activities in the course of carrying on a business for the purpose of gaining or producing your assessable income; and you engaged in those activities while you were at the second place.

<a id="s25-100-3"></a>
**(3)** Travel between 2 places is not travel between workplaces if one of the places you are travelling between is a place at which you reside.

<a id="s25-100-4"></a>
**(4)** Travel between 2 places is not travel between workplaces if, at the time of your travel to the second place:

> <a id="s25-100-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** the arrangement under which you gained or produced assessable income at the first place has ceased; or

> <a id="s25-100-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** the *business in respect of which you engaged in activities at the first place has ceased. No deduction for capital expenditure

<a id="s25-100-5"></a>
**(5)** You cannot deduct expenditure under subsection (1) to the extent that the expenditure is capital, or of a capital nature.
