# 32-10  Meaning of entertainment

<a id="s32-10-1"></a>
**(1)** Entertainment means:

> <a id="s32-10-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** entertainment by way of food, drink or *recreation; or

> <a id="s32-10-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** accommodation or travel to do with providing entertainment by way of food, drink or *recreation.

<a id="s32-10-2"></a>
**(2)** You are taken to provide entertainment even if business discussions or transactions occur.

> **Note:** These are some examples of what is entertainment: • business lunches • social functions. These are some examples of what is not entertainment: • meals on business travel overnight • theatre attendance by a critic • a restaurant meal of a food writer.
