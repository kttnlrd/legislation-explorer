# 32-25  The tables set out the other exceptions

Section 32-5 does not stop you deducting a loss or outgoing to the extent that you incur it in respect of providing *entertainment as described in column 2 of an item of a table in this Subdivision. However, if column 3 of that item applies, the exception in column 2 of that item does not.
