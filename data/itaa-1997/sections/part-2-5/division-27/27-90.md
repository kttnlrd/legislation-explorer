# 27-90 Cost or opening adjustable value of depreciating assets increased: increasing adjustments

<a id="s27-90-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies to an entity if:

<a id="s27-90-1-a"></a>
**(a)** the entity can deduct amounts for a *depreciating asset under Division 40 or 328; and

<a id="s27-90-1-b"></a>
**(b)** the entity has an *increasing adjustment in an income year that relates directly or indirectly to the asset. (1A) However, this section does not apply to an *increasing adjustment that arises under Division 129 or 132 of the *GST Act.

**Note:** See instead section 27-92.

<a id="s27-90-2"></a>
**(2)** The asset's *cost is increased by an amount equal to the *increasing adjustment if the adjustment arises in the income year in which the asset's *start time occurs.

<a id="s27-90-3"></a>
**(3)** The asset's *opening adjustable value for an income year and its *cost is increased by an amount equal to the *increasing adjustment if the adjustment arises in that year and that year is after the one in which the asset's *start time occurs. Exception: pooling

<a id="s27-90-4"></a>
**(4)** This section does not apply to:

<a id="s27-90-4-a"></a>
**(a)** a depreciating asset allocated to a low-value pool or a pool under Division 328 for or in the *current year; or

<a id="s27-90-4-b"></a>
**(b)** *in-house software if expenditure on the software is allocated to a software development pool for the current year; or

<a id="s27-90-4-c"></a>
**(c)** a project pool.
