# 28-13 Meaning of car expense

<a id="s28-13-1"></a>
**(1)** A car expense is a loss or outgoing to do with a *car.

<a id="s28-13-2"></a>
**(2)** In addition, any of the following is a car expense:

<a id="s28-13-2-a"></a>
**(a)** a loss or outgoing to do with operating a *car;

<a id="s28-13-2-b"></a>
**(b)** the decline in value of a car.

<a id="s28-13-3"></a>
**(3)** None of the following is a car expense:

<a id="s28-13-3-a"></a>
**(a)** a loss or outgoing incurred, or a payment made, in respect of travel outside Australia; Chapter 2 Rules about deductibility of particular kinds of amounts Part 2-5 Car expenses Division 28 Section 28-14

<a id="s28-13-3-b"></a>
**(b)** a taxi fare or similar loss or outgoing.
