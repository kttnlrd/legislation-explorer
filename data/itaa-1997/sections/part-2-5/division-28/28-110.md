# 28-110  Steps for keeping a log book

There are 3 steps you need to follow in keeping a log book: • identify an income year for which to keep a log book; • choose a period of at least 12 weeks for the log book to cover; • record journeys made in the car during the log book period in the course of producing your assessable income.
