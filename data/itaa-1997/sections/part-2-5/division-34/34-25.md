# 34-25 Application to register the design

<a id="s34-25-1"></a>
**(1)** The employer of an employee who has, or will have, a *non-compulsory *uniform can apply to the *Industry Secretary for the *design of the uniform to be registered.

**Note:** This Division also applies to entities that are not employers: see Subdivision 34-A. Meaning of design of a uniform

<a id="s34-25-2"></a>
**(2)** The design of a *uniform includes features such as its colouring, construction, durability, ornamentation, pattern and shape. Form of application

<a id="s34-25-3"></a>
**(3)** The application must be:

<a id="s34-25-3-a"></a>
**(a)** in writing; and

<a id="s34-25-3-b"></a>
**(b)** in a form approved in writing by the *Industry Secretary; and

<a id="s34-25-3-c"></a>
**(c)** accompanied by such information as the Industry Secretary requires.
