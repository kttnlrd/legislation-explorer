# 34-45  Keeping of the Register

<a id="s34-45-1"></a>
**(1)** The *Industry Secretary must keep the Register of Approved Occupational Clothing, listing the designs that are required to be entered on the Register because of this Division. Register to be open for inspection

<a id="s34-45-2"></a>
**(2)** The *Industry Secretary must arrange for the Register to be available for inspection at any reasonable time by any person on request.
