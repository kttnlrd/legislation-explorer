# 394-1  What this Division is about

This Division sets out rules about deductions for contributions to forestry managed investment schemes. It also sets out the tax treatment of proceeds from the sale of interests in such schemes, and of proceeds from harvesting trees under such schemes. scheme—initial participant 394-30     CGT event in relation to forestry interest in forestry managed investment scheme—subsequent participant 394-35     70% DFE rule 394-40     Payments under forestry managed investment scheme 394-45     Direct forestry expenditure
