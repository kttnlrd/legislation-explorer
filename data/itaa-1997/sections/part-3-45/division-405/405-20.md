# 405-20 What you count as assessable professional income

<a id="s405-20-1"></a>
**(1)** Work out your assessable professional income for an income year by adding up all your assessable income for the income year that you count under this Subdivision.

**Note:** Section 405-30 may stop you counting an amount.

**Note:** Subsection 405-35(1) stops you counting an amount more than once, even if it is described in more than one subsection of this section.

**Note:** Subsection 405-35(2) may affect the amount you count. Assessable income from professional services

<a id="s405-20-2"></a>
**(2)** You count any assessable income that you *derive as a reward for providing services relating to your activities as a *special professional. Assessable income from prizes

<a id="s405-20-3"></a>
**(3)** You also count any assessable income that you *derive as a prize for your activities as a *special professional. Assessable income from promotions and commentary

<a id="s405-20-4"></a>
**(4)** You also count any assessable income that you *derive, because you are or were a *special professional, for:

<a id="s405-20-4-a"></a>
**(a)** endorsing or promoting goods or services; or

<a id="s405-20-4-b"></a>
**(b)** appearing or participating in an advertisement; or performing artists, production associates and sportspersons Section 405-20

<a id="s405-20-4-c"></a>
**(c)** appearing or participating in an interview; or

<a id="s405-20-4-d"></a>
**(d)** providing services as a commentator; or

<a id="s405-20-4-e"></a>
**(e)** providing similar services. Assessable income from assigning copyright or granting a licence

<a id="s405-20-5"></a>
**(5)** You also count any assessable income that you *derive:

<a id="s405-20-5-a"></a>
**(a)** as consideration for:

<a id="s405-20-5-a-i"></a>
**(i)** assigning all or part of the copyright in a literary, dramatic, musical or artistic work of which you are the author; or

<a id="s405-20-5-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** granting an interest in the copyright in such a work by granting a licence; or

<a id="s405-20-5-b"></a>
**(b)** as an advance on account of royalties relating to such a copyright. Assessable income from assigning or granting patent rights

<a id="s405-20-6"></a>
**(6)** You also count any assessable income that you *derive:

<a id="s405-20-6-a"></a>
**(a)** as consideration for:

<a id="s405-20-6-a-i"></a>
**(i)** assigning all or part of the patent for an invention that you invented; or

<a id="s405-20-6-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** granting an interest in the patent for such an invention by granting a licence; or

<a id="s405-20-6-a-iii"></a>
**(iii)** assigning the right to apply for a patent for such an invention; or

<a id="s405-20-6-b"></a>
**(b)** as an advance on account of royalties relating to such a patent. Other assessable income from works or inventions

<a id="s405-20-7"></a>
**(7)** You also count any assessable income that you *derive (as *royalties or otherwise):

<a id="s405-20-7-a"></a>
**(a)** for a literary, dramatic, musical or artistic work of which you are the author; or

<a id="s405-20-7-b"></a>
**(b)** in relation to copyright in such a work; or

<a id="s405-20-7-c"></a>
**(c)** for an invention that you invented; or

<a id="s405-20-7-d"></a>
**(d)** in relation to a patent for such an invention.
