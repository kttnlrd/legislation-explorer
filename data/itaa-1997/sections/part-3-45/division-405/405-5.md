# 405-5  Special rate of income tax on your above-average special professional income

<a id="s405-5-1"></a>
**(1)** If you have above-average special professional income, the Income Tax Rates Act 1986 generally sets a special rate so that the amount of income tax you pay on the top 4/5 of your above-average special professional income is effectively 4 times what you would pay on the bottom 1/5 of that income at basic rates. Note : Your overall income tax will be less only if 2 marginal rates of income tax would apply to your above-average special professional income if it were treated as the top slice of your taxable income.
