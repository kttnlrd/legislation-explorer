# 405-50  Working out your average taxable professional income

It is generally a 4-year average

<a id="s405-50-1"></a>
**(1)** Work out your average taxable professional income for the *current year by:

> <a id="s405-50-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** adding up your *taxable professional income for each of the last 4 income years before the current year; and

> <a id="s405-50-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** dividing the total by 4.

Phasing-in arrangements for new professionals

<a id="s405-50-2"></a>
**(2)** However, if the *current year is less than 4 income years after *professional year 1, work out your average taxable professional income using the table in subsection (5).

<a id="s405-50-3"></a>
**(3)** Professional year 1 is the first income year:

> <a id="s405-50-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** during which you were an Australian resident (for all or part of the income year); and

> <a id="s405-50-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** for which your *taxable professional income was more than $2,500.

<a id="s405-50-4"></a>
**(4)** Professional year 2, professional year 3 and professional year 4 are respectively the next 3 income years after *professional year 1.

<a id="s405-50-5"></a>
**(5)** The table is as follows:

Average taxable professional income during phase-in period

| Item | Current year | Average taxable professional income if you were an Australian resident for all or part of the income year immediately before professional year 1 | Average taxable professional income if you were a foreign resident for any of the income year immediately before professional year 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Professional year 1 | Nil | Your *taxable professional income for *professional year 1 |
| 2 | Professional year 2 | 1/3 of your *taxable professional income for *professional year 1 | Your *taxable professional income for *professional year 1 |
| 3 | Professional year 3 | 1/4 of the sum of your *taxable professional income for each of *professional years 1 and 2 | 1/2 of the sum of your *taxable professional income for each of *professional years 1 and 2 |
| 4 | Professional year 4 | 1/4 of the sum of your *taxable professional income for each of *professional years 1, 2 and 3 | 1/3 of the sum of your *taxable professional income for each of *professional years 1, 2 and 3 |
