# 355-30 Supporting R&D activities

<a id="s355-30-1"></a>
**(1)** Supporting R&D activities are activities directly related to *core R&D activities.

<a id="s355-30-2"></a>
**(2)** However, if an activity:

<a id="s355-30-2-a"></a>
**(a)** is an activity referred to in subsection 355-25(2); or

<a id="s355-30-2-b"></a>
**(b)** produces goods or services; or

<a id="s355-30-2-c"></a>
**(c)** is directly related to producing goods or services; the activity is a supporting R&D activity only if it is undertaken for the dominant purpose of supporting *core R&D activities.
