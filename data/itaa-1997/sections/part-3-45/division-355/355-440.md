# 355-440  R&D recoupments

<a id="s355-440-1"></a>
**(1)** The *R&D entity has an amount under this section if:

> <a id="s355-440-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the entity, or another entity mentioned in subsection (5), receives or becomes entitled to receive a *recoupment from either of the following (otherwise than under the *CRC program):

> > <a id="s355-440-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** an *Australian government agency;

> > <a id="s355-440-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** an STB (within the meaning of Division 1AB of Part III of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936); and

> <a id="s355-440-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the recoupment is received, or the entitlement to receive the recoupment arises, during the present year; and

> <a id="s355-440-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** either:

> > <a id="s355-440-1-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)** the recoupment is of expenditure incurred on or in relation to certain activities; or

> > <a id="s355-440-1-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** the recoupment requires expenditure (the project expenditure) to have been incurred, or to be incurred, on certain activities.

> **Note:** Paragraph (c) includes expenditure incurred in purchasing a tangible depreciating asset to be used when conducting R&D activities.

<a id="s355-440-2"></a>
**(2)** The amount is equal to the sum of:

> <a id="s355-440-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** so much of the expenditure referred to in subsection (1) that is deducted under this Division; and

> <a id="s355-440-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** for each asset (if any) for which expenditure referred to in subsection (1) is included in the asset's *cost—each amount (if any) equal to the asset's decline in value that is deducted under this Division; that is taken into account in working out *tax offsets under section 355-100 obtained by the *R&D entity for one or more income years.

> **Note:** Paragraphs (a) and (b) of this subsection refer to amounts notionally deducted under this Division (see section 355-105). Amount is reduced by any repayments of the recoupment

<a id="s355-440-3"></a>
**(3)** For the purposes of subsection (2), reduce the expenditure referred to in subparagraph (1)(c)(i) by any repayments of the *recoupment during an income year. Cap on extra income tax if recoupment relates to a project

<a id="s355-440-4"></a>
**(4)** Despite subsection (2), if the *recoupment is covered by subparagraph (1)(c)(ii), the amount mentioned in subsection (2) for the present year cannot exceed the amount worked out using the following formula: R&D expenditure Net amount of the recoupment Project expenditure where: net amount of the recoupment means the total amount of the *recoupment, less any repayments of the recoupment during an income year. R&D expenditure means the amount mentioned in subsection (2), disregarding subsection (3). Related entities

<a id="s355-440-5"></a>
**(5)** The other entities for the purposes of paragraph (1)(a) are as follows:

> <a id="s355-440-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** an entity *connected with the *R&D entity;

> <a id="s355-440-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** an *affiliate of the R&D entity or an entity of which the R&D entity is an affiliate.
