# 355-705  Effect of findings by Industry Innovation and Science Australia

Findings about registration or core technology

<a id="s355-705-1"></a>
**(1)** If:

> <a id="s355-705-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** a certificate given to the Commissioner under the Industry Research and Development Act 1986 sets out:

> > <a id="s355-705-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** a finding under section 27B of that Act about an *R&D entity's application for registration under section 27A of that Act for an income year; or

> > <a id="s355-705-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** a finding under section 27J of that Act about an R&D entity's registration under section 27A of that Act for an income year; or

> > <a id="s355-705-1-a-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)** a finding under section 28E of that Act about an R&D entity and one or more *R&D activities conducted or to be conducted during one or more income years; and

> <a id="s355-705-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the finding was made within 4 years after the end of the income year or the last of the income years (as appropriate); the finding binds the Commissioner for the purposes of assessments of the R&D entity for the income year or years (as appropriate).

> **Note:** Section 28E of the Industry Research and Development Act 1986 deals with findings that technology is core technology for particular R&D activities. Expenditure incurred in acquiring such technology is not deductible under this Division (see subsection 355-225(2)). Advance findings about activities yet to be completed

<a id="s355-705-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

> <a id="s355-705-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** an activity is being conducted, or is yet to be conducted, in an income year; and

> <a id="s355-705-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** an *R&D entity applies in the income year for a finding under section 28A of the Industry Research and Development Act 1986 about the activity; and

> <a id="s355-705-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** Industry Innovation and Science Australia makes the finding and gives the Commissioner a certificate under that Act setting out the finding; the finding binds the Commissioner for the purposes of assessments of the R&D entity for the income year and the next 2 income years. Advance findings about completed activities

<a id="s355-705-3"></a>
**(3)** However, if:

> <a id="s355-705-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** an activity is completed during an income year; and

> <a id="s355-705-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** an *R&D entity applies in the income year for a finding under section 28A of the Industry Research and Development Act 1986 about the activity; and

> <a id="s355-705-3-c"></a>
> **(c)** Industry Innovation and Science Australia makes the finding and gives the Commissioner a certificate under that Act setting out the finding; the finding binds the Commissioner for the purposes of assessments of the R&D entity for the income year.
