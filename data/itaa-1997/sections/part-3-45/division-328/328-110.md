# 328-110 Meaning of small business entity

General rule: based on aggregated turnover worked out as at the beginning of the current income year

<a id="s328-110-1"></a>
**(1)** You are a small business entity for an income year (the current year) if:

> <a id="s328-110-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** you carry on a *business in the current year; and

> <a id="s328-110-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** one or both of the following applies:

> > <a id="s328-110-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** you carried on a business in the income year (the previous year) before the current year and your *aggregated turnover for the previous year was less than $10 million;

> > <a id="s328-110-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** your aggregated turnover for the current year is likely to be less than $10 million.

> **Note:** The $10 million thresholds in this subsection and in subsections (3) and (4) have been increased to $50 million for certain concessions (for example, see subsection 328-285(2)).

> **Note:** If you are or would (if the $10 million thresholds in this subsection and subsection (3) were increased to $50 million) be a small business entity for an income year, you may apply for permission:

> <a id="s328-110-1-a-2"></a>
> **(a)** under section 61C of the Excise Act 1901 to deliver goods for home consumption (without entering them for that purpose) in respect of a calendar month or a quarter; or

> <a id="s328-110-1-b-2"></a>
> **(b)** under section 69 of the Customs Act 1901 to deliver like customable goods or excise-equivalent goods into home consumption (without entering them for that purpose) in respect of a calendar month or, for excise-equivalent goods, a quarter.

<a id="s328-110-2"></a>
**(2)** You work out your *aggregated turnover for the current year for the purposes of subparagraph (1)(b)(ii):

> <a id="s328-110-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** as at the first day of the current year; or

> <a id="s328-110-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** if you start to carry on a *business during the current year— as at the day you start to carry on the business.

> **Note:** Subsection 328-120(5) provides for how to work out your annual turnover (which is relevant to working out your aggregated turnover) if you do not carry on a business for the whole of an income year. Exception: aggregated turnover for 2 previous income years was $10 million or more

<a id="s328-110-3"></a>
**(3)** However, you are not a small business entity for an income year (the current year) because of subparagraph (1)(b)(ii) if:

> <a id="s328-110-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** you carried on a *business in each of the 2 income years before the current year; and

> <a id="s328-110-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** your *aggregated turnover for each of those income years was $10 million or more.

> **Note:** Section 328-110 of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997 affects the operation of this subsection in relation to the 2007-08 and 2008-09 income years. Additional rule: based on aggregated turnover worked out as at the end of the current income year

<a id="s328-110-4"></a>
**(4)** You are also a small business entity for an income year (the current year) if:

> <a id="s328-110-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** you carry on a *business in the current year; and

> <a id="s328-110-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** your *aggregated turnover for the current year, worked out as at the end of that year, is less than $10 million.

> **Note:** If you are a small business entity only because of subsection (4), you cannot choose any of the following concessions:

> <a id="s328-110-4-a-2"></a>
> **(a)** paying PAYG instalments based on GDP-adjusted notional tax: see section 45-130 in Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953;

> <a id="s328-110-4-b-2"></a>
> **(b)** accounting for GST on a cash basis: see section 29-40 of the GST Act;

> <a id="s328-110-4-c"></a>
> **(c)** making an annual apportionment of input tax credits for acquisitions and importations that are partly creditable: see section 131-5 of the GST Act;

> <a id="s328-110-4-d"></a>
> **(d)** paying GST by quarterly instalments: see section 162-5 of the GST Act;

> <a id="s328-110-4-e"></a>
> **(e)** applying for permission under the Excise Act 1901 to deliver goods for home consumption (without entering them for that

> <a id="s328-110-4-f"></a>
> **(f)** applying for permission under the Customs Act 1901 to deliver like customable goods or excise-equivalent goods for home consumption (without entering them for that purpose) in respect of a calendar month or, for excise-equivalent goods, a quarter: see section 69 of that Act. Winding up a business previously carried on

<a id="s328-110-5"></a>
**(5)** This Subdivision applies to you as if you carried on a *business in an income year if:

> <a id="s328-110-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** in that year you were winding up a business you previously carried on; and

> <a id="s328-110-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** you were a *small business entity for the income year in which you stopped carrying on that business.

> **Note:** Subsection 328-120(5) provides for how to work out your annual turnover (which is relevant to working out your aggregated turnover) if you do not carry on a business for the whole of an income year.

> **Note:** A special rule applies if you were an STS taxpayer under this Division (as in force immediately before the commencement of this section) in the income year in which you stopped carrying on the business: see section 328-111 of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997. Partners in a partnership

<a id="s328-110-6"></a>
**(6)** A person who is a partner in a partnership in an income year is not, in his or her capacity as a partner, a small business entity for the income year.
