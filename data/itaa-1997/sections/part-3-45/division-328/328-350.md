# 328-350 What this Subdivision is about

You may be entitled to a tax offset if you are an individual:

<a id="s328-350-a"></a>
**(a)** who is a small business entity; or

<a id="s328-350-b"></a>
**(b)** whose assessable income includes a share of the net small business income of an unincorporated small business entity; or

<a id="s328-350-c"></a>
**(c)** whose assessable income includes an amount because you are a partner in a partnership, or a beneficiary in a trust, that is a small business entity. In working out whether you are or another entity is a small business entity, a special $5 million turnover threshold applies (see section 328-357).
