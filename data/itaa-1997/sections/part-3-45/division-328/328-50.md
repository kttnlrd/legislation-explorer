# 328-50  Objects of this Division

<a id="s328-50-1"></a>
**(1)** The main object of this Division is to offer eligible small businesses the choice of a new platform to deal with their tax. The platform is designed to benefit those businesses in one or more of these ways: • reducing their tax; • providing simpler rules for determining their income and deductions; • providing simpler capital allowances and trading stock requirements; • reducing their compliance costs.

<a id="s328-50-2"></a>
**(2)** This Division also provides rules that are intended to prevent other businesses from taking advantage of those benefits.
