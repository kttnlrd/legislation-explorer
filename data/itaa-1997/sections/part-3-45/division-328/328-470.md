# 328-470 Membership interests affected by transfers of assets

If:

<a id="s328-470-a"></a>
**(a)** section 328-455 applies in relation to the transfer of an asset under a transaction; and

<a id="s328-470-b"></a>
**(b)** an entity holds, either directly or indirectly:

<a id="s328-470-i"></a>
**(i)** a *membership interest in the transferor or a transferee; or

<a id="s328-470-ii"></a>
**(ii)** a membership interest that was issued as provided for by the transaction; disregard a *capital loss from a *CGT event that arises in relation to the membership interest after the transaction takes effect, except to the extent that the entity can demonstrate that the loss is attributable to a matter other than the transaction.
