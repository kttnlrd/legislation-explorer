# 328-235 Interaction with Divisions 85 and 86


<a id="s328-235-1"></a>
**(1)** Despite sections 85-10 and 86-60, if you are a *small business entity for an income year you can deduct amounts for *depreciating assets under this Subdivision.

<a id="s328-235-2"></a>
**(2)** However, you cannot deduct an amount for a *car under this Subdivision if, had you not been a *small business entity and chosen to use this Subdivision, sections 86-60 and 86-70 would have prevented you deducting an amount for it. Special rules about roll-overs
