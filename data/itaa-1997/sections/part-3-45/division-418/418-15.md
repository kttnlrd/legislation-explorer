# 418-15 Who is entitled to the tax offset—life insurance company

<a id="s418-15-1"></a>
**(1)** An entity is entitled to a *tax offset for an income year if:

<a id="s418-15-1-a"></a>
**(a)** the entity is a *life insurance company; and

<a id="s418-15-1-b"></a>
**(b)** an *exploration credit is issued to the entity under Subdivision 418-E for the income year; and

<a id="s418-15-1-c"></a>
**(c)** the entity is an Australian resident during the whole of that income year; and

<a id="s418-15-1-d"></a>
**(d)** were the exploration credit to be a *franked distribution made:

<a id="s418-15-1-d-i"></a>
**(i)** by the same entity that issued the credit; and

<a id="s418-15-1-d-ii"></a>
**(ii)** in the same circumstances in which the credit was issued; the exploration credit would give rise to a *tax offset for the entity that would be subject to the refundable tax offset rules because of paragraph 67-25(1C)(b) or (1D)(b).

<a id="s418-15-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

<a id="s418-15-2-a"></a>
**(a)** an *exploration credit is issued to a *life insurance company; and

<a id="s418-15-2-b"></a>
**(b)** paragraph (1)(d) applies in relation to only part of the exploration credit; this Division applies as if that part of the exploration credit, and the part of the exploration credit in relation to which that paragraph does not apply, were 2 separate exploration credits issued to the life insurance company.
