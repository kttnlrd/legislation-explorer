# 418-30 Reduced amount of the tax offset for certain trusts

<a id="s418-30-1"></a>
**(1)** If an entity is a trust in relation to which some, but not all, of the liability of the trustee to tax is provided under subsection 98(1) or

<a id="s418-30-2"></a>
**(2)** or 99(2) or (3) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936, the amount of the entity's *tax offset under this Subdivision for an income year is:

<a id="s418-30-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

> <a id="s418-30-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** an entity is a trust; and

> <a id="s418-30-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** one or more *members of the trust are taken under section 418-20 to have been issued with one or more *exploration credits for an income year; the amount of the entity's *tax offset, under section 418-25 or subsection (1) of this section, for the income year is reduced by the sum of amounts of the exploration credits taken to be issued to those members.
