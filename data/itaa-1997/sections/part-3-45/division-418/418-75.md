# 418-75 Meaning of greenfields minerals explorer

<a id="s418-75-1"></a>
**(1)** An entity is a greenfields minerals explorer in an income year if:

<a id="s418-75-1-a"></a>
**(a)** the entity has *greenfields minerals expenditure for the income year; and

<a id="s418-75-1-b"></a>
**(b)** during the income year, the entity is a disclosing entity (within the meaning of section 111AC of the Corporations Act 2001); and

<a id="s418-75-1-c"></a>
**(c)** during the income year, the entity is a *constitutional corporation; and

<a id="s418-75-1-d"></a>
**(d)** during the income year, and during the immediately preceding income year, neither:

<a id="s418-75-1-d-i"></a>
**(i)** the entity; nor

<a id="s418-75-1-d-ii"></a>
**(ii)** any other entity that is *connected with or is an *affiliate of the entity; carried on any mining operations on a mining property for extracting *minerals (except *petroleum) from their natural site, for the *purpose of producing assessable income.

<a id="s418-75-2"></a>
**(2)** However, an entity is not a greenfields minerals explorer in an income year in which either or both of the following happens, or in any subsequent income year:

<a id="s418-75-2-a"></a>
**(a)** the entity fails to comply with a request of the Commissioner under subsection 418-80(5);

<a id="s418-75-2-b"></a>
**(b)** a determination under section 418-185 has effect.

**Note:** Under subsection 418-80(5), the Commissioner may request a report on an area in relation to which an entity has greenfields minerals expenditure.

**Note:** Under section 418-185, the Commissioner may determine that an entity that is, or has been, liable to excess exploration credit tax is not to be treated as a greenfields minerals explorer.
