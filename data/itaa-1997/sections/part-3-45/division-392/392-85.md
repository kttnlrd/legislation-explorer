# 392-85  Work out your taxable non-primary production income

<a id="s392-85-1"></a>
**(1)** Work out your taxable non-primary production income for the *current year in this way: Step 1.      Compare your *assessable non-primary production income for the *current year with your *non-primary production deductions for the current year. Step 2.      If your assessable non-primary production income is larger than your non-primary production deductions, your taxable non-primary production income is the difference between them. Step 3.      If your non-primary production deductions are larger than (or equal to) your assessable non-primary production income, your taxable non-primary production income is nil. Assessable non-primary production income

<a id="s392-85-2"></a>
**(2)** Your assessable non-primary production income for the *current year is the difference between:

> <a id="s392-85-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** your *basic assessable income for the current year; and

> <a id="s392-85-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** your *assessable primary production income for the current year. Non-primary production deductions

<a id="s392-85-3"></a>
**(3)** Your non-primary production deductions for the *current year are the difference between:

> <a id="s392-85-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** the sum of your deductions for the current year; and

> <a id="s392-85-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** your *primary production deductions for the current year.
