# 378-60 Notice of refusal to issue certificate

If:

<a id="s378-60-a"></a>
**(a)** an application is made under subsection 378-55(1) for the issue of a certificate; and

<a id="s378-60-b"></a>
**(b)** the *Arts Minister decides under section 378-25 not to issue the certificate; the Arts Minister must give the applicant written notice of the decision (including reasons for the decision).
