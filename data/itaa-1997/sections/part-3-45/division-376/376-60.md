# 376-60 Amount of the producer offset

The amount of the producer offset is:

<a id="s376-60-a"></a>
**(a)** if the *film is a *feature film that was produced for commercial exhibition to the public in cinemas—40%; or

<a id="s376-60-b"></a>
**(b)** otherwise—30%; of the total of the company's *qualifying Australian production expenditure on the film (as determined by the *film authority under section 376-75).
