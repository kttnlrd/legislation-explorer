# 376-1  What this Division is about

Companies may be entitled to 1 of 3 refundable tax offsets in relation to Australian expenditure incurred in making films. The offsets are designed to support and develop the Australian screen industry by providing concessional tax treatment for Australian expenditure. films 376-5      Structure of this Division
