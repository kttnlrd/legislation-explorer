# 376-165 Qualifying Australian production expenditure—special rules for the location offset and the PDV offset

<a id="s376-165-1"></a>
**(1)** For the purposes of the location offset and the PDV offset, the following expenditure of a company is also qualifying Australian production expenditure of the company on a *film:

> <a id="s376-165-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** are not incurred in, or in relation to, the *making of the *film; and

> <a id="s376-165-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** are not reasonably attributable to:

> > <a id="s376-165-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** the use of equipment or other facilities for; or

> > <a id="s376-165-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** activities undertaken in; the making of the film; to the extent to which they:

> <a id="s376-165-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** are incurred for, or are reasonably attributable to:

> > <a id="s376-165-1-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)** goods and services provided in Australia; or

> > <a id="s376-165-1-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** the use of land located in Australia; or

> > <a id="s376-165-1-c-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)** the use of goods that are located in Australia at the time they are used in the making of the film; and

> <a id="s376-165-1-d"></a>
> **(d)** represent a reasonable apportionment of those overheads between the making of the film and the other activities undertaken by the company This item has effect subject to subsection (2). 2 Travel to Australia expenditure of the company in relation to an individual's travel to Australia to undertake activities in Australia in relation to the *making of the *film, if the remuneration paid to the individual for those activities is *qualifying Australian production expenditure of the company 3 Expenditure incurred in freighting goods to Australia expenditure incurred in freighting goods to Australia, to the extent that the goods will be used in the *making of the *film

<a id="s376-165-2"></a>
**(2)** General business overheads of the company are covered by item 1 of the table in subsection (1) only to the extent to which they do not exceed the lesser of:

> <a id="s376-165-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** 2% of the total of all the company's *production expenditure on the *film; and

> <a id="s376-165-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** $500,000.
