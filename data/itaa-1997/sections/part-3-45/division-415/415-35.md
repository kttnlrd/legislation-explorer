# 415-35 Tax losses of companies

Scope

<a id="s415-35-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies to a *tax loss of a company if the company is a *designated infrastructure project entity at a time (the status time) in the *loss year. Modifications of Divisions 165 and 166

<a id="s415-35-2"></a>
**(2)** Despite subsection 165-12(1), 166-5(2) or 166-20(1), the *ownership test period or *test period under that subsection starts at the earlier of:

<a id="s415-35-2-a"></a>
**(a)** the first time:

<a id="s415-35-2-a-i"></a>
**(i)** that occurs after the status time; and

<a id="s415-35-2-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** at which the company is not a *designated infrastructure project entity; and

<a id="s415-35-2-b"></a>
**(b)** the end of the income year referred to in that subsection as the income year.

<a id="s415-35-3"></a>
**(3)** In a case to which paragraph (2)(b) applies, the company is treated as meeting the conditions in section 165-12.

<a id="s415-35-4"></a>
**(4)** Despite subsection 165-13(2), 166-5(5), 165-15(2) or 166-20(4), the *business continuity test period under that subsection starts at the start of the *ownership test period or *test period (whichever is applicable) if, apart from this subsection, the business continuity test period would start earlier.

<a id="s415-35-5"></a>
**(5)** Despite subsection 165-13(2), 165-15(3), 166-5(6) or 166-20(4), the *test time under that subsection occurs just after the start of the *ownership test period or *test period (whichever is applicable) if, apart from this subsection, the test time would occur earlier.

<a id="s415-35-6"></a>
**(6)** A reference in subsection 165-15(1) to the *loss year is treated as being a reference to the period:

<a id="s415-35-6-a"></a>
**(a)** starting at the start of the *ownership test period; and

<a id="s415-35-6-b"></a>
**(b)** ending at the end of the income year in which the ownership test period starts.

<a id="s415-35-7"></a>
**(7)** For the purposes of working out, under paragraph 165-45(3)(a) or

<a id="s415-35-7-b"></a>
**(b)** or subsection 165-45(4), the end of the first period, disregard any part of the income year mentioned in section 165-45 during which the company is a *designated infrastructure project entity.

**Note:** A company does not calculate its taxable income and tax loss under Subdivision 165-B if the company was a designated infrastructure project entity during the whole of the income year: see paragraph 165-35(c). Exceptions

<a id="s415-35-8"></a>
**(8)** Disregard this section for the purposes of Subdivisions 165-CA and 165-CB (about net capital losses) and 175-A and 175-CA (about tax benefits).
