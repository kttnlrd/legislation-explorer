# 415-50 What this Subdivision is about

To receive the special treatment for tax losses and bad debts under Subdivision 415-B, an entity must only engage in activities for the purposes of carrying on an infrastructure project designated by the Infrastructure CEO under this Subdivision. Designation is dependent on:

<a id="s415-50-a"></a>
**(a)** criteria prescribed by the Minister; and

<a id="s415-50-b"></a>
**(b)** a cap on the total estimated private capital expenditure that would be incurred for all provisionally designated and designated infrastructure projects.
