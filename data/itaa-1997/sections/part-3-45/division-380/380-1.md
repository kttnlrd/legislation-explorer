# 380-1  What this Division is about

This Division provides a tax offset to certain entities as a result of certificates issued under the National Rental Affordability Scheme Act 2008. It also ensures that payments made, and non-cash benefits provided, by a State or Territory governmental body in relation to the National Rental Affordability Scheme are not assessable income and not exempt income.
