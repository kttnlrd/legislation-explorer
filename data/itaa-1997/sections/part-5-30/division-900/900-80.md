# 900-80  Getting written evidence

<a id="s900-80-1"></a>
**(1)** To deduct a *business travel expense:

<a id="s900-80-1-a"></a>
**(a)** it must qualify as a deduction under some provision of this Act outside this Division; and

<a id="s900-80-1-b"></a>
**(b)** you need to substantiate it by getting written evidence. Subdivision 900-E tells you about the evidence you need. To find out whether an expense qualifies as a deduction under this Act, see Division 8 (Deductions).

<a id="s900-80-2"></a>
**(2)** If your expense is for fuel or oil, you have a choice of either:

<a id="s900-80-2-a"></a>
**(a)** getting written evidence of it under Subdivision 900-E; or

<a id="s900-80-2-b"></a>
**(b)** keeping odometer records for the period when you owned or leased the *car in the income year. Subdivision 28-H tells you about odometer records.

**Note:** In certain circumstances (for example, under a hire purchase agreement) the notional buyer of property is taken to be its owner (see subsection 240-20(2)).
