# 900-240  Deducting in anticipation of receiving award transport payment

If:

> <a id="s900-240-a"></a>
> **(a)** you have incurred a *transport expense during an income year; and

> <a id="s900-240-b"></a>
> **(b)** when you lodge your *income tax return for the income year, you reasonably believe that you will later receive an *award transport payment to cover the expense; you may deduct the expense without getting written evidence or keeping travel records.
