# 900-170  Extending the retention period if an expense is disputed

The *retention period is automatically extended if one of the following types of dispute relating to the expense is unresolved when the 5 years end:

> <a id="s900-170-a"></a>
> **(a)** an objection;

> <a id="s900-170-b"></a>
> **(b)** a review or appeal arising from an objection; Administration Chapter 5 Record-keeping and other obligations Part 5-30 Substantiation rules Division 900 Section 900-175

> <a id="s900-170-c"></a>
> **(c)** a request for amendment of an assessment. The extension lasts until the dispute is resolved.
