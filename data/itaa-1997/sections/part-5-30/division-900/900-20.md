# 900-20  Keeping travel records

- You need to keep travel records if your expense is for travel that involves you being away from your ordinary residence for 6 or more nights in a row. The travel may be within or outside Australia. Subdivision 900-F tells you about travel records. Administration

**Note:** Members of international flight crews may be exempt from keeping travel records for losses or outgoings covered by travel allowances: see section 900-65.
