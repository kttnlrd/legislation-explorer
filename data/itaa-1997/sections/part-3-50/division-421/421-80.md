# 421-80  Information sharing

<a id="s421-80-1"></a>
**(1)**  Each of the following regulators:

> <a id="s421-80-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the Clean Energy Regulator;

> <a id="s421-80-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the Commissioner; may request the other regulator to provide them with information held by the other regulator that is reasonably necessary or convenient for the requesting regulator's administration of this Division.

<a id="s421-80-2"></a>
**(2)**  The other regulator must comply with the request.

> **Note:** The request could be an ad hoc or standing request, and the information requested could be general or specific.
