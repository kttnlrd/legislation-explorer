# 420-30 Non-arm's length transactions and transactions with associates

If:

> <a id="s420-30-a"></a>
> **(a)** an entity (the transferor) ceases to *hold a *registered emissions unit; and

> <a id="s420-30-b"></a>
> **(b)** the cessation is because of the transfer of the unit to:

> > <a id="s420-30-i"></a>
> > **(i)** a Registry account (within the meaning of the Australian National Registry of Emissions Units Act 2011); or

> > <a id="s420-30-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** a foreign account (within the meaning of that Act); kept by another entity (the transferee); and

> <a id="s420-30-c"></a>
> **(c)** either:

> > <a id="s420-30-i-2"></a>
> > **(i)** the transferor and the transferee did not deal with each other at *arm's length; or

> > <a id="s420-30-ii-2"></a>
> > **(ii)** the transferee is the transferor's *associate; and

> <a id="s420-30-d"></a>
> **(d)** the transferee did not pay or give consideration equal to the *market value of the unit for the transfer of the unit; the transferor is treated as if the transferor were entitled to receive an amount equal to that market value because the transferor ceased to be the holder of the unit.
