# 420-25  Assessable income on disposal of registered emissions units

<a id="s420-25-1"></a>
**(1)** Your assessable income includes an amount that you are entitled to receive because you cease to *hold a *registered emissions unit. Timing

<a id="s420-25-2"></a>
**(2)** The amount is included in your assessable income for the income year in which you cease to *hold the unit. Source

<a id="s420-25-3"></a>
**(3)** An amount included in your assessable income under subsection (1) is taken, for the purposes of the *income tax laws, to have a source in Australia.
