# 160-1  What this Division is about

A corporate tax entity can choose to "carry back" a tax loss it had for 2019-20, 2020-21, 2021-22 or 2022-23 against the income tax liability it had for 2018-19, 2019-20, 2020-21 or 2021-22. The entity gets a refundable tax offset for 2020-21, 2021-22 or 2022-23 that is a proxy for the tax the entity would save if it deducted the loss in the income year to which the loss is "carried back". The refundable tax offset:

> <a id="s160-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** is capped at the entity's franking account balance; and

> <a id="s160-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** is only available for losses for years for which the entity's turnover was less than $5 billion. businesses with turnover under $5 billion Section 160-5
