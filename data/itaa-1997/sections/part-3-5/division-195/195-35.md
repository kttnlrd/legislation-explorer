# 195-35  Net capital loss for year in which company becomes a PDF

<a id="s195-35-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies if a company becomes a *PDF during an income year and is still a PDF at the end of it.

<a id="s195-35-2"></a>
**(2)** Divide the income year into periods according to subsection 195-15(2) (about working out the company's tax loss for the income year).

<a id="s195-35-3"></a>
**(3)** For each period, work out whether the company has a *net capital gain or a *net capital loss (or both), treating each period as if it were an income year.

<a id="s195-35-4"></a>
**(4)** If the company has:

> <a id="s195-35-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** a *net capital gain for the non-PDF period; and

> <a id="s195-35-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** a *net capital loss for the PDF period; that loss is a net capital loss of the company for the income year.

> **Note:** The company can only apply the loss while it is a PDF: see section 195-25.

<a id="s195-35-5"></a>
**(5)** If the company has a *net capital loss for the non-PDF period:

> <a id="s195-35-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** section 195-25 does not prevent the company from applying its *net capital loss for the income year in working out its *net capital gain for a later income year; and

> <a id="s195-35-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** section 195-30 does not prevent the company from transferring an amount of its net capital loss for the income year under Subdivision 170-B (which is about the transfer of net capital losses within certain wholly-owned groups of companies); to the extent that its net capital loss for the income year does not exceed its net capital loss for the non-PDF period.

<a id="s195-35-6"></a>
**(6)** These rules apply in addition to the other rules about how *net capital losses are applied or transferred. The other rules start in Division 102 (about net capital gains and losses). Working out a PDF's loss carry back tax offset
