# 195-123 How to work out the income of the trust estate of a CCIV sub-fund trust for an income year

<a id="s195-123-1"></a>
**(1)** The income (the trust income) of the trust estate of a *CCIV sub-fund trust for an income year is worked out in accordance with this section.

> **Note:** This is relevant to working out the income tax position of the CCIV sub-fund trust and its beneficiaries under Division 6 of Part III of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936.

<a id="s195-123-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

> <a id="s195-123-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** the *CCIV is a *retail CCIV at the end of the income year; and

> <a id="s195-123-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the amount of the *sub-fund's profit for the income year, as required to be stated in the financial statements included in the financial report for the sub-fund for the income year that the CCIV is required to prepare because of paragraph 1232C(1)(a) of the Corporations Act 2001, is greater than nil; the trust income is that profit.

<a id="s195-123-3"></a>
**(3)** If:

> <a id="s195-123-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** the *CCIV is not a *retail CCIV at the end of the income year; and

> <a id="s195-123-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** the amount of the *sub-fund's profit for the income year that would, if the CCIV had been a retail CCIV at the end of the income year, be required to be stated as mentioned in paragraph (2)(b) is greater than nil; the trust income is that profit.

<a id="s195-123-4"></a>
**(4)** If neither of subsections (2) and (3) applies, the trust income is nil.
