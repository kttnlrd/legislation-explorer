# 195-37  PDF cannot carry back tax loss

A company that:

> <a id="s195-37-a"></a>
> **(a)** has a *tax loss for an income year; and

> <a id="s195-37-b"></a>
> **(b)** is a *PDF at the end of the income year; cannot *carry back the loss to an earlier income year for the purposes of working out the amount of the company's *loss carry back tax offset for an income year (the offset year) unless the company is a PDF throughout the earlier income year and the offset year.
