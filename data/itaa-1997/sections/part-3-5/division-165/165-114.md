# 165-114 How to work out the company's net capital loss

The company's net capital loss for the income year is worked out in this way: Working out the company's net capital loss Step 1. Add up the *notional net capital losses (if any) worked out under section 165-108. Step 2. If the Step 1 amount is more than zero, it is the company's net capital loss. 

**Note:** For exceptions and modifications to these rules: see section 102-30.
