# 165-132 When tax losses resulting from bad debts cannot be deducted

<a id="s165-132-1"></a>
**(1)** If:

<a id="s165-132-1-a"></a>
**(a)** a company can deduct a debt (or part of a debt) that it wrote off as bad in an income year; and

<a id="s165-132-1-b"></a>
**(b)** because the company failed to meet a condition in section 165-123 (about the company maintaining the same owners), it could not have deducted the debt (or part) apart from section 165-126 (about the company satisfying the business continuity test); and 

<a id="s165-132-1-c"></a>
**(c)** the company wrote off the debt after the *test time worked out under section 165-126; and

<a id="s165-132-1-d"></a>
**(d)** because of the deduction, the company has a *tax loss for that income year, or there was an increase in the amount of its *tax loss for that income year; and

<a id="s165-132-1-e"></a>
**(e)** the company carried on a *business during that income year for the purpose, or for purposes including the purpose, of securing a deduction for the debt (or part) by relying on section 165-126; the company cannot deduct the *tax loss for a later income year, or cannot deduct it to the extent of the increase, unless it also satisfies the *business continuity test for the later income year (the business continuity test period).

<a id="s165-132-2"></a>
**(2)** Apply the test to the *business that the company carried on immediately before the *test time worked out for section 165-126. For the business continuity test: see Subdivision 165-E.
