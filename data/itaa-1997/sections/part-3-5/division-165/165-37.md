# 165-37 Who has more than a 50% stake in the company during a period


<a id="s165-37-1"></a>
**(1)** If:

> <a id="s165-37-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** there are persons who had *more than 50% of the voting power in the company during the whole of a period (the ownership test period) consisting of the income year or a part of it; and

> <a id="s165-37-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** there are persons who had rights to *more than 50% of the company's dividends during the whole of the ownership test period; and

> <a id="s165-37-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** there are persons who had rights to *more than 50% of the company's capital distributions during the whole of the ownership test period; those persons had more than a 50% stake in the company during the ownership test period.

> **Note:** Division 167 has special rules for working out rights to voting power, dividends and capital distributions in a company whose shares do not all carry the same rights to those matters.

<a id="s165-37-2"></a>
**(2)** To work out whether a condition in subsection (1) was satisfied during the *ownership test period, apply the primary test for that condition unless subsection (3) requires the alternative test to be applied. For the primary tests: see subsections 165-150(1), 165-155(1) and 165-160(1).

<a id="s165-37-3"></a>
**(3)** Apply the alternative test for that condition if one or more other companies beneficially owned *shares, or interests in shares, in the company at any time during the *ownership test period. For the alternative tests: see subsections 165-150(2), 165-155(2) and 165-160(2). Conditions in subsection (1) may be treated as having been satisfied in certain circumstances

<a id="s165-37-4"></a>
**(4)** If any of the conditions in subsection (1) have not been satisfied, those conditions are taken to have been satisfied if:

> <a id="s165-37-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** they would have been satisfied except for the operation of section 165-165; and

> <a id="s165-37-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** the company has information from which it would be reasonable to conclude that less than 50% of the *notional loss for the *ownership test period has been reflected in deductions, capital losses, or reduced assessable income, that occurred, or could occur in future, because of the happening of any *CGT event in relation to any *direct equity interests or *indirect equity interests in the company during that period. (4A) If the company is:

> <a id="s165-37-4-a-2"></a>
> **(a)** a *non-profit company; or

> <a id="s165-37-4-b-2"></a>
> **(b)** a *mutual affiliate company; or

> <a id="s165-37-4-c"></a>
> **(c)** a *mutual insurance company; during the whole of the *ownership test period, the conditions in paragraphs (1)(b) and (c) are taken to have been satisfied by the company. Time of happening of CGT event

<a id="s165-37-5"></a>
**(5)** The happening of a *CGT event in relation to a *direct equity interest or *indirect equity interest in the company that results in the failure of the company to satisfy a condition in subsection (1) is taken, for the purposes of paragraph (4)(b), to have occurred during the *ownership test period.
