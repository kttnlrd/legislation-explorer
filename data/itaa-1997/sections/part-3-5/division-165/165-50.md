# 165-50 Next, calculate the notional loss or notional taxable income for each period


<a id="s165-50-1"></a>
**(1)** The company has a *notional loss for a period if the deductions attributed to the period under section 165-55 exceed the assessable income attributed to the period under section 165-60. The notional loss is the amount of the excess. For a period during which the company was in partnership, the notional loss is worked out under section 165-75.

<a id="s165-50-2"></a>
**(2)** On the other hand, if that assessable income exceeds those deductions, the company has a notional taxable income for the period, equal to the excess. For a period during which the company was in partnership, the notional taxable income is worked out under section 165-75.

<a id="s165-50-3"></a>
**(3)** If the company has a *notional loss for none of the periods in the income year, this Subdivision has no further application, and the company’s taxable income for the income year is calculated in the usual way. The usual way of working out taxable income is set out in section 4-15.
