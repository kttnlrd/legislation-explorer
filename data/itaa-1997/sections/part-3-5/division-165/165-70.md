# 165-70 How to calculate the company's tax loss for the income year

<a id="s165-70-1"></a>
**(1)** The company's tax loss for the income year is calculated as follows.

<a id="s165-70-2"></a>
**(2)** Total the *notional losses worked out under section 165-50 or 165-75.

<a id="s165-70-3"></a>
**(3)** Add to the total in subsection (2) the amount (if any) by which the company's *full year deductions of these kinds:

> <a id="s165-70-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** deductions for bad debts under section 8-1 (about general deductions) or section 25-35 (about bad debts);

> <a id="s165-70-3-c"></a>
> **(c)** deductions, so far as they are allowable under Division 8 (which is about deductions) because Subdivision H (Period of deductibility of certain advance expenditure) of Division 3 of Part III of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 applies to the company in relation to the income year; exceed the total of:

> <a id="s165-70-3-d"></a>
> **(d)** the *notional taxable incomes (if any); and To work out the notional taxable income: see section 165-50.

> <a id="s165-70-3-e"></a>
> **(e)** the *full year amounts referred to in section 165-60 (if any); and

> <a id="s165-70-3-f"></a>
> **(f)** any *net capital gain of the company for the income year.

<a id="s165-70-4"></a>
**(4)** If the company *derived exempt income, subtract its *net exempt income (worked out under section 36-20).

<a id="s165-70-5"></a>
**(5)** Any amount remaining is the company's tax loss for the income year, which is called a loss year.

> **Note:** The meanings of tax loss and loss year are modified by section 36-55 for a corporate tax entity that has an amount of excess franking offsets. To find out how much of the tax loss can be deducted in later income years: see Subdivision 165-A. To find out how to deduct it: see section 36-17.
