# 165-1  What this Division is about

A change in the ownership or control of a company can affect: •      whether it can deduct its tax losses of earlier income years; and •       how it calculates its taxable income and tax loss for the income year of the change; and •       whether it can deduct debts owed to it that are written off as bad.
