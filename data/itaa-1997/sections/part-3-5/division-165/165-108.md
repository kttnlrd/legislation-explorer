# 165-108  Next, calculate the notional net capital gain or notional net capital loss for each period

<a id="s165-108-1"></a>
**(1)** The company has a notional net capital gain for a period if the total of the *capital gains it made during the period exceeds the total of the *capital losses it made during the period. The notional net capital gain is the amount of the excess.

<a id="s165-108-2"></a>
**(2)** On the other hand, if the total of those losses exceeds the total of those gains, the company has a notional net capital loss for the period, equal to the excess.

<a id="s165-108-3"></a>
**(3)** If the company has a *notional net capital loss for none of the periods in the income year, this Subdivision has no further application, and the company's *net capital gain for the income year is calculated in the usual way. The usual way of working out the net capital gain is set out in section 102-5. Trust's capital gain attributed to company beneficiary

<a id="s165-108-4"></a>
**(4)** If some or all (the attributable amount) of an amount included in the company's assessable income for the income year under:

> <a id="s165-108-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** section 97 (Beneficiary of a trust estate who is not under a legal disability) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936; or

> <a id="s165-108-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** section 98A (Non-resident beneficiaries assessable in respect of certain income) of that Act; is attributable to a *capital gain that the trust made at a particular time during the period, this section applies to the attributable amount as if it were a *capital gain made by the company at that time.
