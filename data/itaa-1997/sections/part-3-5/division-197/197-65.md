# 197-65 Choosing to untaint—further franking debits may arise

When this section applies

<a id="s197-65-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies if:

<a id="s197-65-1-a"></a>
**(a)** a company chooses to untaint its *share capital account; and

<a id="s197-65-1-b"></a>
**(b)** the applicable franking percentage (within the meaning of subsection (3)) is higher than the percentage that was the *benchmark franking percentage in relation to the *franking period in which the transfer of an amount (the transferred amount) that is, or is part of, the *tainting amount occurred.

**Note:** If paragraph (b) is satisfied in relation to 2 or more amounts, this section is to be applied separately in relation to each of those amounts (so a separate franking debit will arise in relation to each of those amounts). Franking debit arises in relation to making the choice

<a id="s197-65-2"></a>
**(2)** A *franking debit arises in the company's *franking account in relation to the transferred amount. The debit arises immediately before the end of the *franking period in which the choice to untaint is made.

<a id="s197-65-3"></a>
**(3)** The amount of the *franking debit is the amount by which the amount calculated in accordance with the following formula exceeds the amount of the franking debit that arose under section 197-45 in relation to the transferred amount: Applicable franking percentage Transferred amount  Applicable gross-up rate where: applicable franking percentage means:

<a id="s197-65-3-a"></a>
**(a)** if, before the debit arises, the *benchmark franking percentage for the *franking period in which the choice to untaint is made has already been set by section 203-30—that percentage; or

<a id="s197-65-3-b"></a>
**(b)** otherwise—100%. applicable gross-up rate means the company's *corporate tax gross-up rate for the income year in which the franking debit arises.
