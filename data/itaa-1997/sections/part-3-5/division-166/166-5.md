# 166-5 How Subdivision 165-A applies to a widely held or eligible Division 166 company

<a id="s166-5-1"></a>
**(1)** This Subdivision modifies the way Subdivision 165-A applies to a company that is:

> <a id="s166-5-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** a *widely held company at all times during the income year; or

> <a id="s166-5-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** an *eligible Division 166 company at all times during the income year; or

> <a id="s166-5-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** a widely held company for a part of the income year and an eligible Division 166 company for the rest of the income year.

> **Note:** Subdivision 165-A is about the conditions a company must meet before it can deduct a tax loss for an earlier income year.

> **Note:** A company can choose that this Subdivision is not to apply to it: see section 166-15.

> **Note:** See section 165-255 for the rule about incomplete income years. Meaning of test period

<a id="s166-5-2"></a>
**(2)** The company's test period is the period consisting of the *loss year, the income year and any intervening period.

> **Note:** See section 165-255 for the rule about incomplete test periods. Substantial continuity of ownership

<a id="s166-5-3"></a>
**(3)** The company is taken to have met the conditions in section 165-12 (which is about the company maintaining the same owners) if there is *substantial continuity of ownership of the company as between the start of the *test period and:

> <a id="s166-5-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** the end of each income year in that period; and

> <a id="s166-5-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** the *end of each *corporate change in that period.

> **Note:** See sections 166-145 and 166-175 to work out whether there is substantial continuity of ownership and a corporate change. held or eligible Division 166 company Section 166-15 No substantial continuity of ownership

<a id="s166-5-4"></a>
**(4)** The company is taken to have failed to meet the conditions in section 165-12 if there is no *substantial continuity of ownership of the company as between the start of the *test period and:

> <a id="s166-5-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** the end of an income year in that period; or

> <a id="s166-5-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** the *end of a *corporate change in that period. Satisfies the business continuity test

<a id="s166-5-5"></a>
**(5)** However, if the company satisfies the *business continuity test for the income year (the business continuity test period), it is taken to have satisfied the condition in section 165-13.

> **Note:** For the business continuity test, see Subdivision 165-E.

> **Note:** See section 165-255 for the rule about incomplete test periods.

<a id="s166-5-6"></a>
**(6)** Apply the *business continuity test to the *business that the company carried on immediately before the earlier of the following times (the test time):

> <a id="s166-5-6-a"></a>
> **(a)** the end of the first income year;

> <a id="s166-5-6-b"></a>
> **(b)** the first time in the test period that a *corporate change in the company *ends; for which there is no *substantial continuity of ownership of the company as between the start of the *test period and that time.
