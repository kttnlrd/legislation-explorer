# 166-20 How Subdivisions 165-B and 165-CB apply to a widely held or eligible Division 166 company

<a id="s166-20-1"></a>
**(1)** This Subdivision modifies how Subdivisions 165-B and 165-CB apply to a company that is:

<a id="s166-20-1-a"></a>
**(a)** a *widely held company at all times during the income year (the test period); or

<a id="s166-20-1-b"></a>
**(b)** an *eligible Division 166 company at all times during the income year (the test period); or

<a id="s166-20-1-c"></a>
**(c)** a widely held company for a part of the income year and an eligible Division 166 company for the rest of the income year (the whole year being the test period).

**Note:** Subdivision 165-B is about when a company must calculate its taxable income and tax loss for the income year in a special way. Subdivision 165-CB is about when a company must calculate its net capital gain and net capital loss for the income year in a special way.

**Note:** A company can choose that this Subdivision is not to apply to it: see section 166-35.

**Note:** See section 165-255 for the rule about incomplete test periods. No corporate change etc.

<a id="s166-20-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

<a id="s166-20-2-a"></a>
**(a)** no *corporate change in the company *ends at any time in the *test period; or held or eligible Division 166 company Section 166-20

<a id="s166-20-2-b"></a>
**(b)** a corporate change in the company *ends during the test period, but there is *substantial continuity of ownership as between the start of the test period and immediately after the corporate change ends; the company is taken to have met the condition in paragraph 165-35(a) (which is about there being persons having *more than a 50% stake in it during the whole of the income year).

**Note:** See sections 166-145 and 166-175 to work out whether there is substantial continuity of ownership and a corporate change. Corporate change

<a id="s166-20-3"></a>
**(3)** If:

<a id="s166-20-3-a"></a>
**(a)** a *corporate change in the company *ends at any time in the *test period; and

<a id="s166-20-3-b"></a>
**(b)** there is no *substantial continuity of ownership as between the start of the test period and immediately after the corporate change ends; then the company is taken to have failed to meet the condition in paragraph 165-35(a). Satisfies the business continuity test

<a id="s166-20-4"></a>
**(4)** However, if the company satisfies the *business continuity test for the rest of the income year (the business continuity test period) after the first time (the test time) in the *test period that a *corporate change in the company *ended, the company is taken to have satisfied the condition in paragraph 165-35(b).

**Note:** For the business continuity test, see Subdivision 165-E.

**Note:** See section 165-255 for the rule about incomplete test periods.

<a id="s166-20-5"></a>
**(5)** Apply the *business continuity test to the *business that the company carried on immediately before the *test time.
