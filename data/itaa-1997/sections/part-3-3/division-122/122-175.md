# 122-175  Other consequences

The other consequences relate to the *shares the partners receive and depend on when they *acquired their interests in the assets of the *business.

**Note:** There are 3 possible cases: •   a partner acquired all the interests on or after 20 September 1985: see section 122-180; •   a partner acquired all the interests before that day: see section 122-185; •   a partner acquired some of the interests on or after that day: see section 122-190.

**Note:** There are other consequences for the partnership and the company if the partners dispose of their interests in trading stock of the partnership: see Division 70.
