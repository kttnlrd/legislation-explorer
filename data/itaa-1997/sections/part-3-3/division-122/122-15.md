# 122-15  Disposal or creation of assets—wholly-owned company

If you are an individual or a trustee, you can choose to obtain a roll-over if one of the *CGT events (the trigger event) specified in this table happens involving you and a company in the circumstances set out in sections 122-20 to 122-35. Relevant *CGT events Event No.       What you do A1              *Dispose of a CGT asset, or all the assets of a business, to the company D1              Create contractual or other rights in the company D2              Grant an option to the company D3              Grant the company a right to income from mining F1              Grant a lease to the company, or renew or extend a lease

> **Note:** The roll-over starts at section 122-40.

> **Note:** Section 103-25 tells you when you have to make the choice.

> **Note:** A roll-over may also be available under Subdivision 328-G (Restructures of small businesses).

> **Example:** Gavin runs a plumbing business. He wants to incorporate it so he disposes of all its assets to a company. He becomes the sole shareholder of the company.
