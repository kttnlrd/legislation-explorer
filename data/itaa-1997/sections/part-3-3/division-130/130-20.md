# 130-20 Issue of bonus shares or units

<a id="s130-20-1"></a>
**(1)** This section sets out what happens if:

<a id="s130-20-1-a"></a>
**(a)** you own *shares in a company or units in a unit trust (the original equities); and

<a id="s130-20-1-b"></a>
**(b)** the company issues other shares, or the trustee issues other units, (the bonus equities) to you in relation to the original equities.

<a id="s130-20-2"></a>
**(2)** The first element of your *cost base and *reduced cost base for the bonus equities includes:

<a id="s130-20-2-a"></a>
**(a)** for *shares—any part of the shares that are a *dividend (or taken to be a dividend under subsection 45(2) or 45C(1) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936); and

<a id="s130-20-2-b"></a>
**(b)** for units—any part of the other units that are or will be included in your assessable income. You are taken to have *acquired the bonus equities when they were issued.

**Note:** There are special indexation rules for cost base modifications: see Division 114.

**Note:** The amounts of calls you pay on partly-paid equities will also form part of the first element of their cost base and reduced cost base.

**Note:** There is a special rule for shares issued on or before 30 June 1987: see subsection 130-20(2) of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997.

**Note:** Certain capital distributions are taken to be dividends under subsections 45(2) and 45C(1) if a company has entered into a capital streaming or dividend substitution arrangement.
