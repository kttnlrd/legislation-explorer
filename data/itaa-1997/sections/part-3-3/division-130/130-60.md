# 130-60  Shares or units acquired by converting a convertible interest

<a id="s130-60-1"></a>
**(1)** This table sets out the modification to the rules about *cost base and *reduced cost base that happens if you *acquire *shares, or units in a unit trust, by converting a *convertible interest.

**Conversion of a convertible interest**

| Item | In this situation: | The modification is... |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | You exercise rights issued to you to *acquire the *shares, units or options, and you acquired the original | The first element of your *cost base for the shares, units or options is the sum of: (a) the *market value of the rights when they were exercised; and |
| 1 | You *acquire *shares or units in a unit trust by converting a *convertible interest that is a *traditional security. | The first element of the *cost base of the shares or units is the sum of: (a) the cost base of the convertible interest at the time of conversion; and (b) any amount paid to convert the convertible interest, except to the extent that the amount is represented in the paragraph (a) amount; and (c) all the amounts to be added under subsection (1A). The first element of their *reduced cost base is worked out similarly. |
| 2 | You *acquire *shares (except shares acquired under an *employee share scheme) by converting a *convertible | The first element of the *cost base of the shares is the sum of: (a) the cost base of the convertible interest at the time of conversion; and |
| 3 | You *acquire units in a unit trust by converting a *convertible interest (except one that is a *traditional security) that | The first element of the *cost base of the units is the sum of: (a) the cost base of the convertible interest at the time of conversion; and |

<a id="s130-60-2"></a>
**(2)** You are taken to have *acquired the shares or units when the conversion of the convertible interest happened.

<a id="s130-60-3"></a>
**(3)** A *capital gain or *capital loss you make from converting the convertible interest is disregarded.

> **Note:** The conversion of the convertible interest would be an example of CGT event C2 (about a CGT asset ending).

> **Note:** There are transitional rules for some convertible notes: see section 130-60 of the Income Tax (Transitional Provisions) Act 1997.
