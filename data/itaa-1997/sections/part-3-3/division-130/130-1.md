# 130-1  What this Division is about

This Division sets out the rules for these kinds of investments: •      bonus shares and units; and •      rights; and •      convertible interests; and •      shares acquired under an employee share scheme; and •      exchangeable interests; and •      exploration investments. Most are about modifying the cost base and reduced cost base of a CGT asset.
