# 126-255  No other cost base etc. adjustment for beneficiaries

- If a beneficiary of the trusts makes adjustments under section
- 126-245 or
- 126-250 to the *cost base and *reduced cost base of the beneficiary's *membership interests in relation to the *CGT event that is:

> <a id="s126-255-a"></a>
> **(a)** the creation of the receiving trust over the roll-over asset; or

> <a id="s126-255-b"></a>
> **(b)** the transfer of the roll-over asset to the receiving trust; no other adjustment is to be made under this Act to those cost bases and reduced cost bases because of something that happens in relation to that event.

> **Note:** This section prevents the general value shifting regime from applying in relation to the event because sections 126-245 and 126-250 deal with any value shift that might occur.
