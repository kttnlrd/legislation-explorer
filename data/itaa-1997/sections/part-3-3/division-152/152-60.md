# 152-60  Meaning of CGT concession stakeholder

An individual is a CGT concession stakeholder of a company or trust at a time if the individual is:

<a id="s152-60-a"></a>
**(a)** a *significant individual in the company or trust; or

<a id="s152-60-b"></a>
**(b)** a spouse of a significant individual in the company or trust, if the spouse has a *small business participation percentage in the company or trust at that time that is greater than zero. Small business participation percentage
