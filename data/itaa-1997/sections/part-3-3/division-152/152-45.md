# 152-45 Continuing time periods for involuntary disposals

Asset compulsorily acquired, lost or destroyed

<a id="s152-45-1"></a>
**(1)** If a *CGT asset is an asset (the new asset) you acquired to satisfy the requirement in subsection 124-70(2) or 124-75(2) for a roll-over under Subdivision 124-B, then the active asset test in section 152-35 applies as if:

> <a id="s152-45-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** you had acquired the new asset when you acquired the old asset; and

> <a id="s152-45-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the new asset had been your *active asset at all times when the original asset was your active asset; and

> <a id="s152-45-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** the new asset had not been your active asset at all times when the original asset was not your active asset.

> **Note:** Subdivision 124-B allows you to choose a roll-over if your CGT asset is compulsorily acquired, lost or destroyed.

> **Note:** If this subsection applies to a CGT asset, then section 152-115 (which is about continuing time periods) will apply for the 15-year exemption. Assets replaced during FSR transition (same owner roll-overs) (1A) If a *CGT asset is an asset (the new asset) you acquired in a situation covered by former section 124-880, 124-885 or 124-890, then the active asset test in section 152-35 applies as if:

> <a id="s152-45-1-a-2"></a>
> **(a)** you had acquired the new asset when you acquired the original asset; and

> <a id="s152-45-1-b-2"></a>
> **(b)** the new asset had been your *active asset at all times when the original asset was your active asset; and

> <a id="s152-45-1-c-2"></a>
> **(c)** the new asset had not been your active asset at all times when the original asset was not your active asset.

> **Note:** Former Subdivision 124-O provided a roll-over for certain CGT assets that came to an end as a result of an FSR transition.

> **Note:** If this subsection applies to a CGT asset, then section 152-115 (which is about continuing time periods) will apply for the 15-year exemption. Assets replaced during FSR transition (new owner roll-overs) (1B) If a *CGT asset is an asset (the new asset) acquired in a situation covered by former section 124-900, 124-905 or 124-910, then the active asset test in section 152-35 applies as if:

> <a id="s152-45-1-a-3"></a>
> **(a)** the new owner had acquired the new asset when the original owner acquired the original asset; and

> <a id="s152-45-1-b-3"></a>
> **(b)** the new asset had been the *active asset of the new owner at all times when the original asset was the original owner's active asset; and

> <a id="s152-45-1-c-3"></a>
> **(c)** the new asset had not been the active asset of the new owner at all times when the original asset was not the original owner's active asset.

> **Note:** Former Subdivision 124-O provided a roll-over for certain CGT assets that came to an end as a result of an FSR transition.

> **Note:** If this subsection applies to a CGT asset, then section 152-115 (which is about continuing time periods) will apply for the 15-year exemption. Marriage or relationship breakdowns

<a id="s152-45-2"></a>
**(2)** If you were the transferee of a *CGT asset for which there has been a roll-over under Subdivision 126-A, then you may choose that the active asset test in section 152-35 applies as if:

> <a id="s152-45-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** you had acquired the asset when the transferor acquired the asset; and

> <a id="s152-45-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the asset had been an *active asset of yours at all times when the asset was an active asset of the transferor; and

> <a id="s152-45-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** the asset had not been an active asset of yours at all times when the asset was not an active asset of the transferor.

> **Note:** Section 103-25 tells you when the choice must be made.

> **Note:** There is a roll-over under Subdivision 126-A if CGT assets are transferred because of a marriage or relationship breakdown.

> **Note:** If you don't make the choice, the time of acquisition is simply the time of the transfer.

> **Note:** Making the choice here has certain consequences for the 15-year exemption: see section 152-115. Treatment of passively held CGT assets
