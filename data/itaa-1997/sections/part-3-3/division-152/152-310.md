# 152-310  Consequences of choice

Consequences in all cases

<a id="s152-310-1"></a>
**(1)** If the individual, company or trust makes the choice mentioned in section 152-305 for any part of the *capital gain from the *CGT asset, that part of the capital gain equal to its *CGT exempt amount is disregarded. Additional consequences in relation to company or trust

<a id="s152-310-2"></a>
**(2)** Any payment or part of one the company or trust makes to comply with section 152-325:

> <a id="s152-310-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** is not assessable income, and is not *exempt income, of the *CGT concession stakeholder to whom it is made; and

> <a id="s152-310-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** cannot be deducted from the company's or trust's assessable income. Additional consequences in relation to interposed entities

<a id="s152-310-3"></a>
**(3)** If:

> <a id="s152-310-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** an entity (the paying entity) receives a payment (whether directly or indirectly through one or more interposed entities) that a company or trust makes to comply with section 152-325; and

> <a id="s152-310-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** the paying entity passes on the payment to the *CGT concession stakeholder or another interposed entity; then:

> <a id="s152-310-3-c"></a>
> **(c)** the payment cannot be deducted from the paying entity's assessable income; and

> <a id="s152-310-3-d"></a>
> **(d)** the payment received by the paying entity is not assessable income and is not *exempt income.
