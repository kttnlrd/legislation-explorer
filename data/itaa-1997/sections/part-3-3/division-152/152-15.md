# 152-15  Maximum net asset value test

You satisfy the maximum net asset value test if, just before the *CGT event, the sum of the following amounts does not exceed $6,000,000:

> <a id="s152-15-a"></a>
> **(a)** the *net value of the CGT assets of yours;

> <a id="s152-15-b"></a>
> **(b)** the net value of the CGT assets of any entities *connected with you;

> <a id="s152-15-c"></a>
> **(c)** the net value of the CGT assets of any *affiliates of yours or entities connected with your affiliates (not counting any assets already counted under paragraph (b)).

> **Note:** Some assets are not included in the definition of net value of the CGT assets: see subsections 152-20(2), (3) and (4).

> **Note:** The meaning of connected with is affected by section 152-78.
