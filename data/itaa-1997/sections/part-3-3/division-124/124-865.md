# 124-865  Entities both choose the roll-over

A roll-over is only available for the transferor and transferee if both the transferor and transferee choose to obtain it.

**Note:** If they do so, the consequences for the transferor and transferee are set out in section 124-875.

**Note:** An entity that owns a unit or interest in the transferor can also choose to obtain a roll-over: see section 124-870.
