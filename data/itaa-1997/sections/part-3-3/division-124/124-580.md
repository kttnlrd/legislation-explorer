# 124-580  Meaning of Crown lease

A Crown lease is:

> <a id="s124-580-a"></a>
> **(a)** a lease of land granted by the Crown under an *Australian law (other than the common law); or

> <a id="s124-580-b"></a>
> **(b)** a similar lease granted under a *foreign law.
