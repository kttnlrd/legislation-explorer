# 124-165  Roll-over consequences—some original licences were pre-CGT, others were post-CGT

<a id="s124-165-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies if:

> <a id="s124-165-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** there was more than one original licence; and

> <a id="s124-165-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** you *acquired one or more of the original licences before 20 September 1985; and

> <a id="s124-165-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** you acquired one or more of the original licences on or after that day.

<a id="s124-165-2"></a>
**(2)** Each new licence is taken to be 2 separate *CGT assets that are both *statutory licences:

> <a id="s124-165-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** one (which you are taken to have *acquired on or after 20 September 1985) representing the extent to which you acquired the original licences on or after that day; and

> <a id="s124-165-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** another (which you are taken to have acquired before that day) representing the extent to which you acquired the original licences before that day.

<a id="s124-165-3"></a>
**(3)** The first element of the *cost base and *reduced cost base of the *CGT asset mentioned in paragraph (2)(a) in relation to a new licence is worked out under the formula: Market value of new licence Total post-CGT cost base Market value of all new licences where: market value of all new licences is the total of the *market values of all of the new licences. market value of new licence is the *market value of the new licence to which the *CGT asset mentioned in paragraph (2)(a) relates. total post-CGT cost base is the total of the *cost bases of all the original licences that you *acquired on or after 20 September 1985.
