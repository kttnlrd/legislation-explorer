# 128-10 Capital gain or loss when you die is disregarded

When you die, a *capital gain or *capital loss from a *CGT event that results for a *CGT asset you owned just before dying is disregarded.

**Note:** Section 104-215 sets out an exception to this rule if the CGT asset passes to a beneficiary in your estate who is: • an exempt entity; or • the trustee of a complying superannuation entity; or • a foreign resident.

**Note:** There is a special indexation rule for deceased estates: see section 114-10.
