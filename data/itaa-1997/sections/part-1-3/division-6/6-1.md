# 6-1  Diagram showing relationships among concepts in this Division

Assessable income Ordinary income                                                    Statutory income Non-assessable non-exempt income Exempt income

<a id="s6-1-1"></a>
**(1)** Assessable income consists of ordinary income and statutory income.

<a id="s6-1-2"></a>
**(2)** Some ordinary income, and some statutory income, is exempt income.

<a id="s6-1-3"></a>
**(3)** Exempt income is not assessable income.

<a id="s6-1-4"></a>
**(4)** Some ordinary income, and some statutory income, is neither assessable income nor exempt income. For the effect of the GST in working out assessable income, see Division 17.

<a id="s6-1-5"></a>
**(5)** An amount of ordinary income or statutory income can have only one status (that is, assessable income, exempt income or non-assessable non-exempt income) in the hands of a particular entity. Operative provisions
