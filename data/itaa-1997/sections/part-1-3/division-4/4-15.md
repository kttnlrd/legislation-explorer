# 4-15 How to work out your taxable income

<a id="s4-15-1"></a>
**(1)** Work out your taxable income for the income year like this: Taxable income  Assessable income – Deductions Method statement
Step 1. Add up all your assessable income for the income year. To find out about your assessable income, see Division 6. Step 2. Add up your deductions for the income year. To find out what you can deduct, see Division 8.

**Note:** If the deductions exceed the assessable income, you may have a tax loss which you may be able to utilise in that or a later income year: see Division 36.
