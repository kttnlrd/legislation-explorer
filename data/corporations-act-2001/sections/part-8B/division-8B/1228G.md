# 1228G Corporate director and associates cannot vote if interested in resolution

**(1)** Section 253E applies in relation to a CCIV as if the associates of the corporate director of the CCIV were also the CCIV's associates.

**(2)** Section 253E applies in relation to a sub‑fund of a CCIV as if the associates of the corporate director of the CCIV, and the CCIV's associates, were also the sub‑fund's associates.
