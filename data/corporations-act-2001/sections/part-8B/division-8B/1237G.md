# 1237G Statutory demand—determining application to set aside where there is a dispute or offsetting claim

**(1)** Paragraph 459H(1)(b) applies in relation to a debt to which a demand relates only if the CCIV has an offsetting claim that is an asset of the sub‑fund of which the debt or a proportion of the debt (as the case requires) is a liability.

**(2)** Subsection 459H(2) has effect as if it required the Court to calculate a separate substantiated amount in relation to each sub‑fund specified in the statutory demand.

**(3)** For the purposes of the calculation:

**(a)** the amount of the debt is to be determined having regard only to a debt or a proportion of a debt (as the case requires) that is a liability of the sub‑fund; and

**(b)** the amount of an offsetting claim is to be determined having regard only to so much of an offsetting claim as is an asset of the sub‑fund.
