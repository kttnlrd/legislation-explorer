# 1237ZC Vesting of PPSA security interests if collateral not registered within time

**(a)** an order is made, or a resolution is passed, for the winding up of a sub‑fund of the CCIV; and

**(b)** the PPSA security interest is in collateral that is an asset of the sub‑fund of the CCIV.
