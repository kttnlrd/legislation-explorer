# 1237F Statutory demand—CCIV may apply to set aside demand

**(1)** A CCIV may apply to the Court under section 459G for an order setting aside a statutory demand served on the CCIV.

**(2)** Division 3 of Part 5.4 applies in relation to the application subject to the modifications set out in sections 1237G to 1237J.
