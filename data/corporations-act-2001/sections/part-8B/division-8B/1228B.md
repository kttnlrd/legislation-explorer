# 1228B Meetings of members of sub‑funds

**(1)** Subject to the modifications set out in this Subdivision, Part 2G.4 (about meetings of members of registered managed investment schemes) applies in relation to a sub‑fund of a CCIV and the members of the sub‑fund as if:

**(a)** the sub‑fund were a registered scheme; and

**(b)** the members of the sub‑fund were the members of that scheme; and

**(c)** the corporate director of the CCIV were the responsible entity of that scheme; and

**(d)** the CCIV's constitution were the scheme's constitution; and

**(e)** the CCIV's compliance plan were the compliance plan of that scheme.

**(2)** Any power to prescribe, for the purposes of a provision of Part 2G.4, a number of members of a particular registered scheme or class of scheme includes a power to prescribe, for the purposes of that provision as it applies under this section, a number of members of:

**(a)** a particular sub‑fund of a CCIV; or

**(b)** a particular class of sub‑funds of one or more CCIVs.

**Note:** Subsections 252B(1A), 252L(1A) and 252N(2A) provide for the prescribing of numbers of members.
