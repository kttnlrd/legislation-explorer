# 1232M Relodgement if financial reports, sustainability reports or directors' reports relating to sub‑fund amended after lodgement

Section 322 applies in relation to a financial report, sustainability report or directors' report relating to a sub‑fund of a retail CCIV as if references in that section to a member were instead references to a member of the sub‑fund.
