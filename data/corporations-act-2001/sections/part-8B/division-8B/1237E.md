# 1237E Statutory demand—creditor may serve demand on CCIV

**(1)** A person may serve a statutory demand on a CCIV under section 459E.

**(2)** However, subsection 459E(2) applies to a statutory demand served on a CCIV as if it also required the demand to specify, in relation to each debt to which the demand relates:

**(a)** the sub‑fund or sub‑funds of the CCIV of which the debt is a liability; and

**(b)** if the debt is a liability of 2 or more sub‑funds of the CCIV—the proportion of the debt allocated to each sub‑fund at the time the demand is served.

**(3)** Whether a CCIV has failed to comply with a statutory demand is determined under section 459F. If the CCIV has failed to comply with a statutory demand, the failure affects each sub‑fund specified in the demand.

**Note:** The sub‑funds specified may be affected by a variation made by the Court under subsection 1237H(3).
