# 1232T Extended liability of directors of the corporate director for contraventions of Chapter 2M

**(1)** Section 344 applies as if each of the following were a director of a CCIV:

> **(a)** the corporate director of the CCIV;

> **(b)** a director of the corporate director of the CCIV.

> **Note:** Section 344 creates civil and criminal liability for contraventions of certain provisions in Chapter 2M.

**(2)** Paragraph (1)(a) is included for the avoidance of doubt.
