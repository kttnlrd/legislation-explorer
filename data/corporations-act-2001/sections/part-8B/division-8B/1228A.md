# 1228A Meetings of members of CCIVs

**(1)** Parts 2G.2 (about meetings of members of companies) and 2G.3 (about minutes and members' access to minutes) do not apply to a CCIV or its members.

**(2)** Subject to the modifications set out in this Subdivision, Part 2G.4 (about meetings of members of registered managed investment schemes) applies in relation to a CCIV and its members as if:

**(a)** the CCIV were a registered scheme; and

**(b)** the members of the CCIV were the members of that scheme; and

**(c)** the corporate director of the CCIV were the responsible entity of that scheme; and

**(d)** the CCIV's constitution were the scheme's constitution; and

**(e)** the CCIV's compliance plan were the compliance plan of that scheme.

**(3)** Any power to prescribe, for the purposes of a provision of Part 2G.4, a number of members of a particular registered scheme or class of scheme includes a power to prescribe, for the purposes of that provision as it applies under this section, a number of members of:

**(a)** a particular CCIV; or

**(b)** a particular class of CCIV.

**Note:** Subsections 252B(1A), 252L(1A) and 252N(2A) provide for the prescribing of numbers of members.
