# 1239K Deregistration of a CCIV

**(1)** If, as the result of ASIC deregistering a sub‑fund of a CCIV, the CCIV has no registered sub‑funds, ASIC must deregister the CCIV.

> **Note:** Subject to this Subdivision, sections 601AD to 601AG apply in relation to the deregistration of the CCIV.

**(2)** ASIC must give the corporate director of the CCIV written notice that the CCIV has been deregistered and the date on which it has been deregistered.

**(3)** The notice must be given within 5 business days after the date the CCIV has been deregistered.

CCIV may not be deregistered under section 601AA, 601AB or 601AC

**(4)** ASIC may not deregister a CCIV under section 601AA, 601AB or 601AC.
