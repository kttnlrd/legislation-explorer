# 1242F Powers of Courts—irregularities

**(a)** a meeting of the members of a sub‑fund of the CCIV in the same way as it applies in relation to a meeting of a corporation; and

**(b)** a joint meeting of creditors and members of a sub‑fund of a CCIV in the same way as it applies in relation to a joint meeting of creditors and members of a corporation.
