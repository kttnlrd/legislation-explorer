# 1233B Sub‑funds to be operated as separate businesses

**(1)** A part of the business of a CCIV must not be operated if it is not registered as a sub‑fund.

**(2)** A part of the business of a CCIV that is registered as a sub‑fund must be operated as a separate business from the business of any other sub‑fund of the CCIV.

Requirement for corporate director to operate sub‑funds as separate businesses

**(3)** The corporate director of a CCIV contravenes this subsection if:

**(a)** the corporate director operates a part of the business of the CCIV; and

**(b)** the operation contravenes subsection (1) or (2).

Fault‑based offence

**(4)** A person commits an offence if the person contravenes subsection (3).

Strict liability offence

**(5)** A person commits an offence of strict liability if the person contravenes subsection (3).
