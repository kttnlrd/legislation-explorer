# 1241P Modifications of Part 7.8A

**(1)** Paragraph 994B(3)(d) (about an exception to making a target market determination for a financial product) does not apply to a fully paid ordinary share in a CCIV.

**(2)** If a contravention referred to in paragraph 994M(1)(a) is a contravention by a CCIV, subsection 994M(1) applies to authorise recovery by action against the CCIV's corporate director instead of against the CCIV.

**Note:** Subsection 994M(1) authorises a client to recover the amount of loss or damage suffered because of certain contraventions.
