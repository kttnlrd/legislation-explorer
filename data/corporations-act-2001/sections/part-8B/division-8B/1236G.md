# 1236G Powers of receiver

**(a)** a receiver is appointed in respect of property of a sub‑fund of a CCIV; and

**(b)** one or more persons, other than the CCIV, holds assets of the sub‑fund of the CCIV;

the powers conferred under section 420 on the receiver are taken to include the power to instruct each such person in relation to dealing with the assets of the sub‑fund.
