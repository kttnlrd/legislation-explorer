# 1239J Notices relating to reinstatement of a sub‑fund

If the registration of a sub‑fund of a CCIV is reinstated under section 601AH, ASIC must publish notice in the Gazette and give notice to the corporate director of the CCIV of the following:

**(a)** the reinstatement of the registration of the sub‑fund;

**(b)** if as a result of reinstating the sub‑fund the registration of the CCIV is also reinstated under section 1239H—the reinstatement of the registration of the CCIV.
