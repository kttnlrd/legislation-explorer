# 1239F Claims against insurers in relation to deregistered sub‑fund

**(a)** a sub‑fund of the CCIV is deregistered; and

**(b)** the CCIV has a liability to the person that was a liability of the sub‑fund; and

**(c)** the insurance contract covered that liability immediately before the sub‑fund was deregistered.
