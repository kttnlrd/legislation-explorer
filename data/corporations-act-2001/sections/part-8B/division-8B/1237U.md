# 1237U Mandatory examination

**(1)** Section 596A applies in relation to a CCIV as if the following were officers of the CCIV:

> **(a)** a natural person who is an officer of the CCIV;

> **(b)** a natural person who is a director of the corporate director of the CCIV.

**(2)** Paragraph (1)(a) is included for the avoidance of doubt.
