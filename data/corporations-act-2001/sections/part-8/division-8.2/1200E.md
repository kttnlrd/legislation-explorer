# 1200E Warning statement

The regulations may, in relation to offer documents used in this jurisdiction for recognised offers, prescribe either or both of the following:

**(a)** statements to be included with those documents that relate to the status of an offer as a recognised offer and the laws that regulate the offer;

**(b)** details to be given in statements to be included with those documents that relate to the status of an offer as a recognised offer and the laws that regulate the offer.
