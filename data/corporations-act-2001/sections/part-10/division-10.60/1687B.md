# 1687B Application—meetings

> **(a)** a meeting of the members of a company or a registered scheme (including a meeting of a class of members); or

> **(b)** a meeting of the directors of a company (including a meeting of a committee of directors);

if the meeting is held on or after the day that Schedule commences.
