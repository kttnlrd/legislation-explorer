# 1694G Application of amendments made by Division 2 of Part 1 of Schedule 4 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Modernising Business Communications and Other Measures) Act 2023

The amendments made by Division 2 of Part 1 of Schedule 4 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Modernising Business Communications and Other Measures) Act 2023 apply in relation to representations made to ASIC:

**(a)** under subsection 1317DAT(1) of this Act; and

**(b)** on or after the commencement of that Part;

whether the related infringement notice was given before, on or after that commencement.
