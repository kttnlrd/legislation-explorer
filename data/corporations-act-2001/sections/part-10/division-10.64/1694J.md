# 1694J Transitional—Reference Checking and Information Sharing Protocol

A protocol determined under subsection 912A(3A) of the Corporations Act 2001 in force immediately before the commencement of Division 18 of Part 1 of Schedule 4 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Modernising Business Communications and Other Measures) Act 2023 continues in force as if it had been determined under that subsection as substituted by that Schedule.
