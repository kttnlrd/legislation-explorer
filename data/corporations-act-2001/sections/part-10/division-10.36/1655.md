# 1655 Definitions

amending Act means the Treasury Laws Amendment (Strengthening Corporate and Financial Sector Penalties) Act 2019.

commencement day means the day on which Schedule 1 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Strengthening Corporate and Financial Sector Penalties) Act 2019 commences.
