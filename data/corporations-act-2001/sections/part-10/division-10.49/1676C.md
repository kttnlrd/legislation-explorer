# 1676C Automatic extension of licence conditions—variation applications pending just before commencement day

**(1)** This section applies if:

> **(a)** before the commencement day, a person lodges an application for ASIC to vary the conditions on the person's Australian financial services licence by authorising the person to deal in a superannuation product; and

> **(b)** on or after the commencement day, ASIC decides the application by varying the conditions on the person's licence to authorise the person to deal in a superannuation product; and

> **(c)** at the time the licence is varied, the person is an RSE licensee.

**(2)** The licensee's Australian financial services licence is taken from the time it is so varied to also be subject to a condition authorising the licensee to provide a superannuation trustee service.
