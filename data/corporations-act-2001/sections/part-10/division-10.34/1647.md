# 1647 Application—protection of employee entitlements

The amendments made by Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Corporations Amendment (Strengthening Protections for Employee Entitlements) Act 2019 apply in relation to a relevant agreement or a transaction that is entered into at or after the commencement of that Part.
