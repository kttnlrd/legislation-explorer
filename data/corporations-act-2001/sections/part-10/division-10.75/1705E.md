# 1705E Application of amendments—suspension or cancellation of licences

For the purposes of section 797B, 826B, 905H, or 908BI as amended by Part 2 of the amending Schedule, it does not matter whether:

> **(a)** a related act, omission or change happens; or

> **(b)** a period starts;

before, at or after the commencement time.
