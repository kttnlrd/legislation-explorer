# 1681 Application of amendments relating to the simplified liquidation process

The amendments made by Schedule 3 to the Corporations Amendment (Corporate Insolvency Reforms) Act 2020 apply in relation to the winding up of a company because of a triggering event that occurs on or after 1 January 2021.
