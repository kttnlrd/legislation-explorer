# 1662 Application of amendments made by Schedule 1 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Mutual Reforms) Act 2019

The amendments made by Schedule 1 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Mutual Reforms) Act 2019 apply on and after the commencement of that Schedule.
