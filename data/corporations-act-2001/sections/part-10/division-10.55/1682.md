# 1682 Application of amendment relating to portfolio holdings disclosure

The amendment of section 1017BB made by Schedule 3 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Your Future, Your Super) Act 2021 applies in relation to the reporting day that is 31 December 2021 and to later reporting days.
