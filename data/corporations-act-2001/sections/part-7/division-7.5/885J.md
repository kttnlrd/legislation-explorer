# 885J The losses to be covered—other matters to be taken into account

**(1)** In considering whether the arrangements are adequate, ASIC must also have regard to:

> **(a)** the services provided by the market and by the participants in the market; and

> **(b)** any risk assessment report in relation to the market given to ASIC under section 892K.

**(2)** ASIC may take into account such other matters as ASIC thinks appropriate.
