# 948C Obligation to give client a Cash Settlement Fact Sheet

**(1)** The providing entity must give the client a Cash Settlement Fact Sheet in accordance with this Division.

**(2)** A person contravenes this subsection if the person contravenes subsection (1).

> **Note:** This subsection is a civil penalty provision (see section 1317E).
