# 908BD Applying for a licence

**(1)** A body corporate may, by lodging an application with ASIC in a prescribed form, apply for a benchmark administrator licence for a particular financial benchmark.

**Note:** For fees for lodging applications, see Part 9.10.

**(2)** ASIC may, by written notice given to an applicant, require the applicant to lodge with ASIC in a prescribed form, within the period specified in the notice, further information in connection with the application.

**(3)** If the applicant fails to lodge the further information as required, ASIC may, by written notice given to the applicant:

**(a)** refuse to consider the application; or

**(b)** refuse to take any action, or any further action, in relation to the application.
