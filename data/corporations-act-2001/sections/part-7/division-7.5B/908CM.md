# 908CM Ministerial consent to rules required

**(1)** ASIC must not make a rule under this Division unless consent for it has been given under subsection (2).

**Note:** In some situations, consent is not required (see section 908CN).

**(2)** The Minister may, in writing, consent to the making of a rule under this Division.
