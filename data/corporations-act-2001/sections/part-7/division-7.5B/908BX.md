# 908BX Basis of licences

**(1)** A benchmark administrator licence is granted under this Part on the basis that:

**(a)** the licence may be varied, suspended or cancelled, or made subject to conditions (or additional conditions), by or under:

**(i)** this Division; or

**(ii)** later legislation; and

**(b)** no compensation is payable if the licence is varied, suspended or cancelled, or made subject to conditions (or additional conditions), as mentioned in paragraph (a).

**(2)** Subsection (1) does not, by implication, affect the interpretation of any other provision:

**(a)** of this Act; or

**(b)** of an instrument made under this Act.
