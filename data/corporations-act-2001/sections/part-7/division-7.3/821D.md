# 821D Obligation to give access to the facility

**(1)** A CS facility licensee must give a person authorised by ASIC such reasonable access to the facility as the person requests for any of the purposes of this Chapter.

> **Note:** Failure to comply with this subsection is an offence (see subsection 1311(1)).

**(2)** A CS facility licensee must give a person authorised by the Reserve Bank such reasonable access to the facility as the person requests for any of the purposes of this Part or Part 7.3B.

> **Note:** Failure to comply with this subsection is an offence: see subsection 1311(1).
