# 823B ASIC's power to require special report

**(1)** ASIC may give a CS facility licensee a written notice requiring the licensee to give ASIC a special report on specified matters.

**(2)** The licensee must give the special report to ASIC within the time required by the notice.

**Note:** Failure to comply with this subsection is an offence: see subsection 1311(1).
