# 848J Disclosure in circumstances determined by the Minister

**(1)** A person covered by subsection 848C(2) in relation to information may disclose the information if the disclosure is made in circumstances (if any) determined under subsection (2) of this section.

**(2)** The Minister may, by legislative instrument, determine circumstances for the purpose of subsection (1).
