# 837G Consultation

**(1)** Subject to subsection (2) of this section, the Reserve Bank must, before making a determination under subsection 837A(1) or (2) or 837B(1) or (2), consult with the ACCC.

**(2)** The Reserve Bank does not have to consult with the ACCC in relation to the making of a determination under subsection 837A(1) or (2) or 837B(1) or (2) if the ACCC has notified the Reserve Bank, in writing, that it does not wish to be consulted about:

> **(a)** the transfer concerned; or

> **(b)** a class of transfers that includes that transfer.
