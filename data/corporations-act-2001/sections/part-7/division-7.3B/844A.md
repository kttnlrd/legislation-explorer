# 844A Winding up body corporate

**(1)** While section 842A applies to a body corporate, it cannot be wound up voluntarily.

**(2)** The Court is to adjourn the hearing of an application for an order to wind up a body corporate while section 842A applies to the body corporate.

**(3)** The Court is not to appoint a provisional liquidator of a body corporate while section 842A applies to the body corporate.
