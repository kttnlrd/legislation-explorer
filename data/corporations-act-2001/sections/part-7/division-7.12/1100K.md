# 1100K Financial markets recognised under this Division

**(1)** For the purposes of this Division, the following financial markets are covered by this section:

**(a)** a financial market operated by a market licensee;

**(b)** a foreign financial market determined by ASIC under subsection (2).

**(2)** ASIC may, by legislative instrument, determine one or more foreign financial markets for the purposes of this section.
