# 1052D Approval of material changes to the AFCA scheme

**(1)** AFCA may request ASIC to approve a material change to the AFCA scheme.

> **Note 1:** A material change must not be made to the scheme without ASIC's approval (see paragraph 1051(5)(b)).

> **Note 2:** This right to make requests extends to any application of the AFCA scheme in relation to members of the scheme that are not providers of financial products or services.

**(2)** ASIC may, by written notice given to AFCA, approve the change.

**(3)** In considering whether to approve the change, ASIC must take into account the following in relation to the scheme as it is proposed to be changed:

> **(a)** the mandatory requirements for the scheme under section 1051;

> **(b)** the general considerations for the scheme under section 1051A;

> **(c)** any conditions imposed by the Minister on the authorisation of the scheme under paragraph 1050(5)(b);

> **(d)** any regulatory requirements issued under section 1052A.
