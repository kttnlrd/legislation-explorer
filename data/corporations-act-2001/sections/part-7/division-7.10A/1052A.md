# 1052A ASIC may issue regulatory requirements

> **(a)** compliance with the mandatory requirements for the AFCA scheme under section 1051; or

> **(b)** any of the general considerations for the AFCA scheme under section 1051A.

> **Note:** This power to issue regulatory requirements extends to any application of the AFCA scheme in relation to members of the scheme that are not providers of financial products or services.
