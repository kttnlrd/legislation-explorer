# 1055C Evidence of determination of superannuation complaint

**(1)** A document purporting to be a copy of AFCA's determination of a superannuation complaint, and purporting to be certified, by an AFCA staff member who is authorised to do so, to be a true copy of the determination, is, in any proceeding, prima facie evidence of the determination.

**(2)** This section does not affect any other method available by law for the proof of determinations of AFCA.
