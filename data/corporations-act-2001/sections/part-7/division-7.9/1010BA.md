# 1010BA Part does not apply to contribution plans and ESS contribution plans

Apart from section 1017F and Divisions 5A and 6 (and provisions of Division 7 that apply in relation to that section or provisions of those Divisions), nothing in this Part applies in relation to contribution plans or ESS contribution plans for offers of ESS interests that are eligible to be made under Division 1A of Part 7.12.
