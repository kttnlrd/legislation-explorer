# 287 Language requirements

**(1)** The financial and sustainability records may be kept in any language.

**(2)** An English translation of financial and sustainability records not kept in English must be made available within a reasonable time to a person who:

> **(a)** is entitled to inspect the records; and

> **(b)** asks for the English translation.

**(3)** An offence based on subsection (2) is an offence of strict liability.

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code.
