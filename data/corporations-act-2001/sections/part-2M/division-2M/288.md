# 288 Physical format

**(1)** If financial and sustainability records are kept in electronic form, they must be convertible into hard copy. Hard copy must be made available within a reasonable time to a person who is entitled to inspect the records.

**(2)** An offence based on subsection (1) is an offence of strict liability.

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code.
