# 9D Meaning of declared financial market

**(1)** A declared financial market is a financial market declared under subsection (2).

**(2)** ASIC may, by legislative instrument, declare a specified financial market for the purposes of subsection (1).

**(3)** ASIC must not make a declaration under subsection (2) unless the Minister has approved it in writing.
