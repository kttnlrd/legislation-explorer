# 588R Creditor may sue for compensation with liquidator's consent

**(1)** A creditor of a company that is being wound up may, with the written consent of the company's liquidator, begin proceedings under section 588M in relation to the incurring by the company of a debt that is owed to the creditor.

**(1A)** If section 588M applies in relation to a company because of subsection 588M(1A), a creditor described in that subsection may begin proceedings under that section with the written consent of the company's liquidator.

**(2)** Subsections (1) and (1A) have effect despite section 588T, but subject to section 588U.
