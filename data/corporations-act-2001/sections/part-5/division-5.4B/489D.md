# 489D Court's power to make orders under other provisions

**(1)** To avoid doubt, the Court may make an order under section 486A, 598 or 1323, or section 45‑1 or 90‑15 of Schedule 2 in relation to a person appearing before the Court under:

> **(a)** a section 486B warrant; or

> **(b)** section 489B.

**(2)** Subsection (1) does not limit section 486A, 598 or 1323, or section 45‑1 or 90‑15 of Schedule 2.
