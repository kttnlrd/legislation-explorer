# 457A Regulations may deal with information etc.

> **(a)** giving information, providing a report or producing a document to the restructuring practitioner for a company or for a restructuring plan; and

> **(b)** giving information, providing a report or producing a document to ASIC in relation to a company under restructuring or a company that has made a restructuring plan; and

> **(c)** giving information, providing a report or producing a document to any other person (including creditors) in relation to a company under restructuring or a company that has made a restructuring plan; and

> **(d)** publishing information, a report or a document in relation to a company under restructuring or that has made a restructuring plan.
