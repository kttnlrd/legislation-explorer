# 457B Notice in public documents of company

**(1)** A company under restructuring must set out, in every public document, and in every negotiable instrument, of the company, after the company's name where it first appears, the expression ("restructuring practitioner appointed").

**(2)** An offence based on subsection (1) is an offence of strict liability.
