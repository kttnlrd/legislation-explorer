# 456H No liability for consent etc.

A person who is or has been the restructuring practitioner for a company under restructuring is not liable to an action or other proceeding for damages in respect of:

**(a)** a decision to terminate, or not to terminate, the restructuring of a company under section 453J; or

**(b)** a decision to give, or refuse to give, an approval or consent under this Division.
