# 456L Lien to secure indemnity

**(1)** To secure a right of indemnity under section 456J, the restructuring practitioner has a lien on the company's property.

**(2)** A lien under subsection (1) has priority over another security interest only in so far as the right of indemnity under section 456J has priority over debts secured by the other security interest.
