# 455A Proposing a restructuring plan

**(1)** A company may propose a restructuring plan to its creditors.

**(2)** The company is taken to be insolvent if the company does so.

**(3)** The regulations may prescribe the time at which the company is taken to have done so, for the purpose of determining when the company became insolvent under subsection (2).
