# 738S Company may notify responsible intermediary that it wants CSF offer withdrawn

The company making a CSF offer may, at any time before the offer is complete, notify the responsible intermediary that the company wants the offer withdrawn.

> **Note:** The responsible intermediary must close the offer (see subsection 738N(4)).
