# 738K Other requirements for CSF offer document

The information in a CSF offer document must be worded and presented in a clear, concise and effective manner. The document must also comply with any other requirements prescribed by the regulations.

**Note 1:** If this section is contravened, ASIC may make a stop order under section 739.

**Note 2:** See also subsections 738W(8) and (9) about the effect of supplementary or replacement CSF offer documents.
