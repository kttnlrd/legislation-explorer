# 738D Meaning of retail client in relation to a CSF offer

If a person (not being the company making the CSF offer) is a retail client in relation to the provision of a crowd‑funding service that relates to a particular CSF offer, then, for the purposes of this Part, the person is a retail client in relation to that CSF offer.
