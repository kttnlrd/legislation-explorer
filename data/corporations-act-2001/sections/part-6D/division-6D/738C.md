# 738C Meaning of CSF intermediary

A CSF intermediary is a financial services licensee whose licence expressly authorises the licensee to provide a crowd funding service.

**Note:** Because a CSF intermediary is a financial services licensee, the intermediary must (in addition to complying with this Part) comply with the provisions of Chapter 7 that apply to financial services licensees.
