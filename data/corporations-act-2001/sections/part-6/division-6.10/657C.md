# 657C Applying for declarations and orders

**(1)** The Takeovers Panel may make a declaration under section 657A, or an order under section 657D or 657E, only on an application made under this section.

**(2)** An application for a declaration under section 657A or an order under section 657D or 657E may be made by:

> **(a)** the bidder; or

> **(b)** the target; or

> **(c)** ASIC; or

> **(d)** any other person whose interests are affected by the relevant circumstances.

> **Note:** The Administrative Review Tribunal cannot review ASIC's decision whether to apply to the Takeovers Panel (see paragraph 1317C(gc)).

**(3)** An application for a declaration under section 657A can be made only within:

> **(a)** 2 months after the circumstances have occurred; or

> **(b)** a longer period determined by the Takeovers Panel.
