# 658B Evidentiary value of findings of fact by Takeovers Panel

**(1)** A finding of fact recorded in an order by the Takeovers Panel, or a written statement of the reasons for an order of the Takeovers Panel, is proof of the fact in the absence of evidence to the contrary.

**(2)** A certificate signed by the President of the Takeovers Panel that states a finding of fact made in proceedings before the Takeovers Panel is proof of the fact in the absence of evidence to the contrary.
