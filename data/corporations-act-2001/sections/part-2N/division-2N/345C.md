# 345C When choice has effect

If ASIC notifies the company, responsible entity or operator of its approval under section 345B, the choice has effect:

**(a)** if the different date occurs before the next review date for the company, scheme or fund—at the time that ASIC notifies its approval; or

**(b)** otherwise—immediately after the next review date for the company, scheme or fund.
