# 250M When and how polls must be taken (replaceable rule—see section 135)

**(1)** A poll, other than a poll demanded on the election of a chair or the question of an adjournment, must be taken when and in the manner the chair directs.

**(2)** A poll demanded on the election of a chair or on the question of an adjournment must be taken immediately.
