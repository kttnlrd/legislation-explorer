# 18  Public offer superannuation fund

Definition

<a id="s18-1"></a>
**(1)**  A superannuation fund is a public offer superannuation fund if:

> <a id="s18-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  one of the following subparagraphs applies to the fund:

> > <a id="s18-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  it is a regulated superannuation fund that is not a standard employer-sponsored fund;

> > <a id="s18-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  it is a standard employer-sponsored fund that has at least one member: (A) who is not a standard employer-sponsored member; and (B) who is not a member of a prescribed class;

> > <a id="s18-1-a-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  it is a standard employer-sponsored fund in relation to which an election under subsection (2) has been made;

> > <a id="s18-1-a-iv"></a>
> > **(iv)**  a declaration under subsection (6) (which allows for funds to be declared to be public offer superannuation funds) is in force in relation to the fund; and

> <a id="s18-1-aa"></a>
> **(aa)**  the fund is not a self managed superannuation fund; and

> <a id="s18-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  no declaration under subsection (7) (which allows for funds to be declared not to be public offer superannuation funds) is in force in relation to the fund. Election to be a public offer superannuation fund

<a id="s18-2"></a>
**(2)**  The trustee of a standard employer-sponsored fund may elect that the fund is to be treated as a public offer superannuation fund. How an election is made

<a id="s18-3"></a>
**(3)**  An election must be made by giving APRA a written notice that is:

> <a id="s18-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  in the approved form; and

> <a id="s18-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  signed by the trustee. Trustee has power to make election despite anything in the governing rules

<a id="s18-4"></a>
**(4)**  The trustee has the power to make an election despite anything in the governing rules of the fund. Election is irrevocable

<a id="s18-5"></a>
**(5)**  An election is irrevocable. Declaration that fund is a public offer superannuation fund

<a id="s18-6"></a>
**(6)**  APRA may, in writing, declare a superannuation fund to be a public offer superannuation fund. Declaration that fund is not a public offer superannuation fund

<a id="s18-7"></a>
**(7)**  APRA may, in writing, declare a superannuation fund not to be a public offer superannuation fund.

<a id="s18-7A"></a>
**(7A)**  A declaration that a superannuation fund is not a public offer superannuation fund may be subject to conditions.

<a id="s18-7B"></a>
**(7B)**  If a condition has been breached the trustee must immediately notify APRA, in writing, of the breach. Penalty: 30 penalty units.

<a id="s18-7BA"></a>
**(7BA)**  Subsection (7B) is an offence of strict liability.

> **Note:** Chapter 2 of the Criminal Code sets out the general principles of criminal responsibility.

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code.

<a id="s18-7C"></a>
**(7C)**  If APRA is satisfied, whether because of a notification under subsection (7B) or otherwise, that a condition to which the declaration is subject has been breached:

> <a id="s18-7C-a"></a>
> **(a)**  APRA may revoke the declaration; and

> <a id="s18-7C-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the superannuation fund is taken, with effect from the revocation, to have become a public offer superannuation fund. Commencement of declaration

<a id="s18-8"></a>
**(8)**  A declaration comes into force when it is made, or, if a later time is specified in the declaration as the time when it comes into force, it comes into force at that later time. Cessation of declaration

<a id="s18-9"></a>
**(9)**  A declaration remains in force:

> <a id="s18-9-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if a time is specified in the declaration as the time when it stops being in force—until that time, or until the declaration is revoked, whichever occurs first; or

> <a id="s18-9-b"></a>
> **(b)**  otherwise—until the declaration is revoked. Revocation of declaration

<a id="s18-10"></a>
**(10)**  APRA may, in writing, revoke a declaration. APRA must have regard to guidelines when making or revoking a declaration

<a id="s18-11"></a>
**(11)**  When making or revoking a declaration, APRA must have regard to any written guidelines determined by APRA under this subsection. Copy of declaration or revocation to be given to trustee

<a id="s18-12"></a>
**(12)**  As soon as practicable after making or revoking a declaration, APRA must give the trustee of the superannuation fund concerned a copy of the instrument making or revoking the declaration.
