# 29HA  Application for approval to hold a controlling stake


<a id="s29HA-1"></a>
**(1)** A person may apply to APRA for approval to hold a controlling stake in an RSE licensee.

<a id="s29HA-2"></a>
**(2)** The application must:

> <a id="s29HA-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** be in the approved form; and

> <a id="s29HA-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** contain the information required by the approved form.

<a id="s29HA-3"></a>
**(3)** If:

> <a id="s29HA-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** a person applies for approval to hold a controlling stake in an RSE licensee; and

> <a id="s29HA-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** after the application is made, but before APRA decides the application, information contained in the application ceases to be correct; the person must give APRA the correct information, in writing, as soon as practicable after the information in the application ceases to be correct.

<a id="s29HA-4"></a>
**(4)** An application is taken not to comply with this section if subsection (3) is contravened.
