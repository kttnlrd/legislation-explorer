# 29DE  APRA to give notice of refusal of applications

29DE APRA to give notice of refusal of applications If APRA refuses an application by a body corporate or a group of individual trustees for an RSE licence, APRA must take all reasonable steps to ensure that the body or a member of the group is given a notice informing the body or group of:

> <a id="s29DE-a"></a>
> **(a)** APRA's refusal of the application; and

> <a id="s29DE-b"></a>
> **(b)** the reasons for that refusal; as soon as practicable after refusing the application.
