# 29DD  Licence period


<a id="s29DD-1"></a>
**(1)**  An RSE licence comes into force at the later of:

> <a id="s29DD-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the time when it is granted; or

> <a id="s29DD-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the time specified on the licence as the time when it comes into force.

<a id="s29DD-2"></a>
**(2)**  An RSE licence continues in force, subject to:

> <a id="s29DD-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  any imposition of licence conditions under Division 5; or

> <a id="s29DD-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  any variation or revocation of the licence conditions, or variation of the licence class, under Division 6; until the RSE licence is cancelled under Division 7.
