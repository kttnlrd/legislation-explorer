# 29B  Classes of RSE licences

29B Classes of RSE licences

<a id="s29B-1"></a>
**(1)** There are to be classes of RSE licences.

<a id="s29B-2"></a>
**(2)** One class of RSE licences is to be a class that enables a trustee that holds a licence of that class to be a trustee of:

> <a id="s29B-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** any public offer entity; and

> <a id="s29B-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** any other registrable superannuation entity included in a class of registrable superannuation entities specified in regulations made for the purposes of this subsection; subject to any condition imposed on that licence under subsection 29EA(3).

> **Note:** An RSE licence of this class is called a public offer entity licence: see subsection 10(1).

> **Note:** Only constitutional corporations may hold public offer entity licences: see paragraph 29D(1)(g).

<a id="s29B-3"></a>
**(3)** Another class of RSE licences is to be a class that enables a trustee that:

> <a id="s29B-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** holds a licence of that class; or

> <a id="s29B-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** is a member of a group of individual trustees that holds a licence of that class; to be a trustee of any registrable superannuation entity included in a class of registrable superannuation entities (other than a class of public offer entities) specified in regulations made for the purposes of this subsection, subject to any condition imposed on that licence under subsection 29EA(3).

<a id="s29B-4"></a>
**(4)** The regulations may provide for other classes of RSE licences. For each such class, the regulations must specify the classes of registrable superannuation entities of which a trustee that:

> <a id="s29B-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** holds a licence of that class; or

> <a id="s29B-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** is a member of a group of individual trustees that holds a licence of that class; is enabled to be a trustee, subject to any condition imposed on that licence under subsection 29EA(3).

<a id="s29B-5"></a>
**(5)** The classes of registrable superannuation entity that the regulations may specify in relation to a particular class of RSE licence may include one or more classes of registrable superannuation entity that the regulations specify in relation to another class of RSE licence.
