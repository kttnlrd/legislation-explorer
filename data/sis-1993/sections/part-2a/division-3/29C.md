# 29C  Applications for RSE licences

Who may apply for RSE licences

<a id="s29C-1"></a>
**(1)** A constitutional corporation may apply to APRA for an RSE licence of any class.

<a id="s29C-2"></a>
**(2)** A body corporate that is not a constitutional corporation may apply to APRA for an RSE licence of any class other than a class that would enable it to be a trustee of a public offer entity.

<a id="s29C-3"></a>
**(3)** A group of individual trustees may apply to APRA for an RSE licence of any class other than a class that would enable each of the members of the group to be a trustee of a public offer entity. Requirements for applications

<a id="s29C-4"></a>
**(4)** An application for an RSE licence must:

> <a id="s29C-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** be in the approved form; and

> <a id="s29C-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** contain the information required by the approved form; and

> <a id="s29C-4-c"></a>
> **(c)** be accompanied by the application fee (if any) prescribed by regulations made for the purposes of this paragraph.

<a id="s29C-5"></a>
**(5)** Regulations made for the purposes of paragraph (4)(c) may prescribe different application fees for applications for different classes of RSE licences. Notifying certain changes while applications are pending

<a id="s29C-6"></a>
**(6)** If:

> <a id="s29C-6-a"></a>
> **(a)** a body corporate applies for an RSE licence; and

> <a id="s29C-6-b"></a>
> **(b)** after the application is made, but before APRA decides the application, another director is added to, or removed from the board; the body corporate must notify APRA, in the approved form, about the change to the membership of the board as soon as practicable after that change occurs.

> **Note:** Part 9 has requirements about equal representation rules.

<a id="s29C-7"></a>
**(7)** If:

> <a id="s29C-7-a"></a>
> **(a)** a group of individual trustees applies for an RSE licence; and

> <a id="s29C-7-b"></a>
> **(b)** after the application is made, but before APRA decides the application, another trustee is added to, or removed from the group; a member of the group must notify APRA, in the approved form, about the change to the membership of the group as soon as practicable after that change occurs.

> **Note:** Part 9 has requirements about equal representation rules.

<a id="s29C-9"></a>
**(9)** An application is taken not to comply with this section if subsection (6) or (7) is contravened.

> **Note:** APRA cannot grant an RSE licence while the application does not comply with this section: see paragraph 29D(1)(c).
