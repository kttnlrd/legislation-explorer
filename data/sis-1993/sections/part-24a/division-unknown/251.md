# 251  Rights of beneficiary to rolled-over benefits


<a id="s251-1"></a>
**(1)** If a fund has paid in accordance with old Part 24 to an eligible transitional fund an amount equal to the benefits of a beneficiary in the first-mentioned fund:

> <a id="s251-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the beneficiary ceases to have any rights against the first-mentioned fund in respect of those benefits; but

> <a id="s251-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** has the corresponding rights against the eligible transitional fund in respect of those benefits. Transitional provisions relating to pre-1 July 1995 automatic rollovers of benefits between funds Part 24A Section 252

<a id="s251-2"></a>
**(2)** For the purposes of this section, a mere contingent right to a death or disability benefit is taken not to be a right in respect of the first-mentioned benefits.

<a id="s251-3"></a>
**(3)** Subsection (2) is enacted for the avoidance of doubt.
