# 250  Definitions

In this Part: eligible transitional fund means a fund that was, at any time before 1 July 1995, an eligible rollover fund within the meaning of old Part 24. fund means a regulated superannuation fund or an approved deposit fund. old Part 24 means Part 24 as in force at any time before 1 July 1995.
