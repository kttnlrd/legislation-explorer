# 242K  Additional obligations of a trustee in relation to an eligible rollover fund

rollover fund Each trustee of an eligible rollover fund must promote the financial interests of the beneficiaries of the fund, in particular returns to those beneficiaries (after the deduction of fees, costs and taxes).
