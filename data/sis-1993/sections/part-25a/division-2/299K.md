# 299K  

Obligation to record tax file number

<a id="s299K-2"></a>
**(2)** If the trustee, or the trustees, of the entity do not already have a record of the tax file number, as soon as is reasonably practicable after the quotation, the trustee to whom the quotation is made must make a record of the number. Obligation to retain and later destroy tax file number

<a id="s299K-3"></a>
**(3)** Each trustee of the entity must ensure that:

> <a id="s299K-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** the record is retained until the time (the last retention time) at which:

> > <a id="s299K-3-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** if the person becomes a beneficiary of the entity—the person ceases to be a beneficiary of the entity; or

> > <a id="s299K-3-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** if not—the person ceases to be an applicant; and

> <a id="s299K-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** the record is destroyed as soon as is reasonably practicable after the last retention time. Offences

<a id="s299K-6"></a>
**(6)** A trustee of the entity commits an offence if a requirement of subsection (2) or (3) is contravened by the trustee. Penalty: 100 penalty units.

<a id="s299K-7"></a>
**(7)** A trustee of the entity commits an offence if a requirement of subsection (2) or (3) is contravened by the trustee. This is an offence of strict liability. Penalty: 50 penalty units.

> **Note:** Chapter 2 of the Criminal Code sets out the general principles of criminal responsibility.

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code. 299L Use of tax file number for certain purposes—applicants to become beneficiaries of regulated exempt public sector superannuation schemes

<a id="s299K-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies if a person who is an applicant to become a beneficiary of a regulated exempt public sector superannuation scheme quotes his or her tax file number to a trustee of the scheme in connection with the operation or the possible future operation of this Act and the other Superannuation Acts. Trustee may record tax file number

<a id="s299K-2-2"></a>
**(2)** If the trustee, or the trustees, do not already have a record of the tax file number, a trustee of the scheme may make a record of it. Obligation to retain and later destroy tax file number

<a id="s299K-3-2"></a>
**(3)** Each trustee of the scheme must ensure that:

> <a id="s299K-3-a-2"></a>
> **(a)** the record is retained until the time (the last retention time) at which:

> > <a id="s299K-3-a-i-2"></a>
> > **(i)** if the person becomes a beneficiary of the scheme—the person ceases to be a beneficiary of the scheme; or

> > <a id="s299K-3-a-ii-2"></a>
> > **(ii)** if not—the person ceases to be an applicant; and

> <a id="s299K-3-b-2"></a>
> **(b)** the record is destroyed as soon as is reasonably practicable after the last retention time. Offences

<a id="s299K-6-2"></a>
**(6)** A trustee of the scheme commits an offence if a requirement of subsection (3) is contravened by the trustee. Penalty: 100 penalty units.

<a id="s299K-7-2"></a>
**(7)** A trustee of the scheme commits an offence if a requirement of subsection (3) is contravened by the trustee. This is an offence of strict liability. Penalty: 50 penalty units.

> **Note:** Chapter 2 of the Criminal Code sets out the general principles of criminal responsibility.

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code.
