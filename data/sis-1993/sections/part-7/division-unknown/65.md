# 65  Lending to members of regulated superannuation fund prohibited

65 Lending to members of regulated superannuation fund prohibited Prohibition

<a id="s65-1"></a>
**(1)** A trustee or an investment manager of a regulated superannuation fund must not:

> <a id="s65-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** lend money of the fund to:

> > <a id="s65-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** a member of the fund; or

> > <a id="s65-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** a relative of a member of the fund; or

> <a id="s65-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** give any other financial assistance using the resources of the fund to:

> > <a id="s65-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** a member of the fund; or

> > <a id="s65-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** a relative of a member of the fund.

> **Note:** Section 166 imposes an administrative penalty for a contravention of subsection (1) by a trustee in relation to a self managed superannuation fund. Exception—private sector funds

<a id="s65-2"></a>
**(2)** Subsection (1) does not prohibit the lending of money of a private sector fund established before 16 December 1985 to a member if the trustee of the fund, on or before that date:

> <a id="s65-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** had express power to lend money to members; or

> <a id="s65-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** lent money to members and that lending was not expressly prohibited by the governing rules of the fund. Exception—public sector funds

<a id="s65-3"></a>
**(3)** Subsection (1) does not prohibit the lending of money of a public sector fund established before 25 May 1988 to a member if the trustee of the fund, on or before that date:

> <a id="s65-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** had express power to lend money to members; or

> <a id="s65-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** lent money to members and that lending was not expressly prohibited by the governing rules of the fund. Variation of governing rules

<a id="s65-4"></a>
**(4)** If:

> <a id="s65-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** subsection (2) or (3) applies to a regulated superannuation fund; and

> <a id="s65-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** at the beginning of the fund's 1994-95 year of income, a provision included in the governing rules of the fund authorised the lending of the fund's money to members; a variation of that provision is void unless the variation:

> <a id="s65-4-c"></a>
> **(c)** limits the power to lend the fund's money to members; or

> <a id="s65-4-d"></a>
> **(d)** removes the power to lend the fund's money to members. Civil penalty provision

<a id="s65-5"></a>
**(5)** Subsection (1) is a civil penalty provision as defined by section 193, and Part 21 therefore provides for civil and criminal consequences of contravening, or of being involved in a contravention of, that subsection. Effect of Part 8

<a id="s65-7"></a>
**(7)** Nothing in Part 8 limits the operation of this section.
