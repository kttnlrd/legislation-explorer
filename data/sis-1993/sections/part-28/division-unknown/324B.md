# 324B  Instituting criminal proceedings


<a id="s324B-1"></a>
**(1)** Proceedings before the Federal Court of Australia for an offence against a provision of this Act must not be instituted except with the written consent of the Minister, or of a person authorised in writing by the Minister to give such consents.

<a id="s324B-2"></a>
**(2)** Subsection (1) does not apply to proceedings instituted by the Regulator or a person authorised in writing by the Regulator.

<a id="s324B-3"></a>
**(3)** Nothing in this section affects the operation of the Director of Public Prosecutions Act 1983.
