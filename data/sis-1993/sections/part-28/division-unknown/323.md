# 323  Relief from civil liability for contravention of certain provisions

Proceedings to which this section applies

<a id="s323-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies to:

> <a id="s323-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** eligible proceedings (within the meaning of section 221); and

> <a id="s323-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** proceedings under subsection 55(3). Defences

<a id="s323-2"></a>
**(2)** Subject to subsection (4), in proceedings against a person (the defendant) in respect of a contravention, it is a defence if the defendant establishes:

> <a id="s323-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** that the contravention was due to reasonable mistake; or

> <a id="s323-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** that the contravention was due to reasonable reliance on information supplied by another person; or

> <a id="s323-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** that:

> > <a id="s323-2-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)** the contravention was due to: (A) the act or default of another person; or (B) an accident; or (C) some other cause beyond the defendant's control; and

> > <a id="s323-2-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** the defendant took reasonable precautions and exercised due diligence to avoid the contravention. Meaning of another person

<a id="s323-3"></a>
**(3)** For the purposes of the application of subsection (2) to the defendant, a reference to another person does not include a person who was, at the time when the contravention occurred:

> <a id="s323-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** in any case—a servant or agent of the defendant; or

> <a id="s323-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** if the defendant is a body corporate—a director, servant or agent of the defendant. Notice to be given about reliance on defence

<a id="s323-4"></a>
**(4)** If a defence provided by subsection (2) involves an allegation that a contravention was due to:

> <a id="s323-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** reliance on information supplied by another person; or

> <a id="s323-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** the act or default of another person; the defendant is not entitled to rely on that defence unless:

> <a id="s323-4-c"></a>
> **(c)** the court grants leave; or

> <a id="s323-4-d"></a>
> **(d)** both:

> > <a id="s323-4-d-i"></a>
> > **(i)** the defendant has served on the person by whom the proceedings were instituted a written notice giving such information: (A) that would identify, or assist in the identification of, the other person; and (B) as was then in the defendant's possession; and

> > <a id="s323-4-d-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** that notice is served not later than 7 days before the day on which the hearing of the proceedings begins.
