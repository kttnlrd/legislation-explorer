# 223A  Provisions subject to an infringement notice


<a id="s223A-1"></a>
**(1)** An offence against one of the following provisions is subject to an infringement notice under this Part:

> <a id="s223A-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** subsection 18(7B);

> <a id="s223A-1-aa"></a>
> **(aa)** subsection 29JCB(1);

> <a id="s223A-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** subsection 29W(1);

> <a id="s223A-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** subsection 29WA(3);

> <a id="s223A-1-d"></a>
> **(d)** subsection 29WB(3);

> <a id="s223A-1-e"></a>
> **(e)** subsection 35A(7);

> <a id="s223A-1-f"></a>
> **(f)** subsection 107(4);

> <a id="s223A-1-g"></a>
> **(g)** subsection 108(4);

> <a id="s223A-1-h"></a>
> **(h)** subsection 140(3);

> > <a id="s223A-1-h-i"></a>
> > **(i)** subsection 242P(1);

> <a id="s223A-1-j"></a>
> **(j)** subsection 260(3);

> <a id="s223A-1-k"></a>
> **(k)** subsection 262(2).

<a id="s223A-2"></a>
**(2)** An offence against one of the following provisions is subject to an infringement notice under this Part, unless the superannuation entity to which the offence relates is a self managed superannuation fund:

> <a id="s223A-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** subsection 11C(2), (3) or (4);

> <a id="s223A-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** subsection 63(7) or (10);

> <a id="s223A-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** subsection 64(3A);

> <a id="s223A-2-d"></a>
> **(d)** subsection 71EA(5);

> <a id="s223A-2-e"></a>
> **(e)** subsection 103(3);

> <a id="s223A-2-f"></a>
> **(f)** subsection 104(2);

> <a id="s223A-2-g"></a>
> **(g)** subsection 105(2);

> <a id="s223A-2-h"></a>
> **(h)** subsection 122(2);

> > <a id="s223A-2-h-i"></a>
> > **(i)** subsection 124(2);

> <a id="s223A-2-j"></a>
> **(j)** subsection 141A(3) or (6);

> <a id="s223A-2-k"></a>
> **(k)** subsection 252A(3).

<a id="s223A-3"></a>
**(3)** The regulations may provide that:

> <a id="s223A-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** an offence against a provision of this Act not already specified in this section, or a civil penalty provision in this Act, is subject to an infringement notice under this Part; and

> <a id="s223A-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** an offence against the regulations, or a civil penalty provision in the regulations, is subject to an infringement notice under this Part.
