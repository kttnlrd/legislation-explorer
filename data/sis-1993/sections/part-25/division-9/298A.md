# 298A  Authorisation of members of staff


<a id="s298A-1"></a>
**(1)** The Regulator may authorise in writing a member of staff of the Regulator, or a member of staff of another person or body referred to in the definition of Regulator in subsection 10(1), for the purposes of a specified provision of this Act.

<a id="s298A-2"></a>
**(2)** The authorisation may be restricted to a particular function or power under the provision.
