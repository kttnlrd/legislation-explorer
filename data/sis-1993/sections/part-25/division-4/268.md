# 268  Inspector may enter premises for purposes of an investigation

268 Inspector may enter premises for purposes of an investigation If an inspector believes on reasonable grounds that it is necessary to enter premises for the purposes of an investigation of the whole or a part of the affairs of a superannuation entity or approved SMSF auditor, or the conduct of an audit of a self managed superannuation fund, the inspector may, at any reasonable time, enter the premises and:

> <a id="s268-a"></a>
> **(a)**  inspect any book found on the premises that relates to the affairs of the entity or auditor, or to the audit, or that he or she believes on reasonable grounds to relate to those affairs or that audit; and

> <a id="s268-b"></a>
> **(b)**  make copies of, or take extracts from, any such book.
