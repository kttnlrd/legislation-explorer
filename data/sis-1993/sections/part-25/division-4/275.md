# 275  Power to require person to identify property of superannuation entity

entity A person who has power under this Part to require another person to produce books relating to affairs of a superannuation entity may, whether or not that power is exercised, require the other person, so far as the other person can do so:

> <a id="s275-a"></a>
> **(a)**  to identify property of the entity; and

> <a id="s275-b"></a>
> **(b)**  to explain how a trustee or an investment manager of the entity has kept account of that property.
