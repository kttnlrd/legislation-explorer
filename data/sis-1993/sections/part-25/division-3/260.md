# 260  Deadline for receipt of report


<a id="s260-1"></a>
**(1)**  APRA’s notice under section 257 must specify a date as the deadline for the receipt of the report.

<a id="s260-2"></a>
**(2)**  A person appointed to investigate and make a report under subsection 257(1) (whether as an individual or as a member of a committee) commits an offence if the report is not given to APRA:

> <a id="s260-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  before the expiry of the deadline; or

> <a id="s260-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  within such further time as APRA, by written notice, allows. Penalty: 100 penalty units.

<a id="s260-3"></a>
**(3)**  A person appointed to investigate and make a report under subsection 257(1) (whether as an individual or as a member of a committee) commits an offence if the report is not given to APRA:

> <a id="s260-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  before the expiry of the deadline; or

> <a id="s260-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  within such further time as APRA, by written notice, allows. This is an offence of strict liability. or a committee, to investigate the financial position of the entity Penalty: 50 penalty units.

> **Note:** Chapter 2 of the Criminal Code sets out the general principles of criminal responsibility.

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code.
