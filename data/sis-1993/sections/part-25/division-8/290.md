# 290  Statements made at an examination: proceedings against examinee

examinee Admissibility of statements made at examination

<a id="s290-1"></a>
**(1)** Subject to this section, a statement that a person makes at an examination of the person is admissible in evidence against the person in a proceeding. Self-incrimination exception

<a id="s290-2"></a>
**(2)** The statement is not admissible if:

> <a id="s290-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** the proceeding is:

> > <a id="s290-2-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** a criminal proceeding; or

> > <a id="s290-2-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** a proceeding for the imposition of a penalty; other than a proceeding in respect of the falsity of the statement; and

> <a id="s290-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the person is an individual who, before making the statement, claimed that it might tend to incriminate him or her or make him or her liable to a penalty. Irrelevant statement exception

<a id="s290-3"></a>
**(3)** The statement is not admissible if it is not relevant to the proceeding and the person objects to the admission of evidence of the statement. Related statement exception

<a id="s290-4"></a>
**(4)** The statement (the subject statement) is not admissible if:

> <a id="s290-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** it is qualified or explained by some other statement made at the examination; and

> <a id="s290-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** evidence of the other statement is not tendered in the proceeding; and

> <a id="s290-4-c"></a>
> **(c)** the person objects to the admission of evidence of the subject statement. Legal professional privilege exception

<a id="s290-5"></a>
**(5)** The statement is not admissible if:

> <a id="s290-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** it discloses matter in respect of which the person could claim legal professional privilege in the proceeding if subsection (1) did not apply in relation to the statement; and

> <a id="s290-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** the person objects to the admission of evidence of the statement. Joint proceedings

<a id="s290-6"></a>
**(6)** Subsection (1) applies in relation to a proceeding against a person even if it is heard together with a proceeding against another person. Record is prima facie evidence

<a id="s290-7"></a>
**(7)** If a written record of an examination of a person is signed by the person under subsection 280(2) or authenticated in any other prescribed manner, the record is, in a proceeding, prima facie evidence of the statements it records. Admissibility of other evidence

<a id="s290-8"></a>
**(8)** This Part does not limit or affect the admissibility in the proceeding of other evidence to statements made at the examination.
