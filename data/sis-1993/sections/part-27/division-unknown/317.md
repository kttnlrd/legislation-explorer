# 317  Power of Court to punish for contempt of court

Nothing in a provision of this Act that provides:

> <a id="s317-a"></a>
> **(a)** that a person must not contravene an order of the Court; or

> <a id="s317-b"></a>
> **(b)** that a person who contravenes an order of the Court contravenes a provision of this Act or commits an offence; affects the powers of the Court in relation to the punishment of contempts of the Court.
