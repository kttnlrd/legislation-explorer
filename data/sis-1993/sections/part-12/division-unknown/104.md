# 104  Duty to keep records of changes of trustees


<a id="s104-1"></a>
**(1)** Each trustee of a superannuation entity must ensure that up-to-date records of:

> <a id="s104-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** all changes of trustees of the entity; and

> <a id="s104-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** all changes of directors of any corporate trustee of the entity; and

> <a id="s104-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** all consents given under section 118; are kept and retained for at least 10 years.

> **Note:** Section 166 imposes an administrative penalty for a contravention of subsection (1) in relation to a self managed superannuation fund.

<a id="s104-2"></a>
**(2)** A trustee commits an offence if the trustee contravenes subsection (1). This is an offence of strict liability. Penalty: 50 penalty units.

> **Note:** Chapter 2 of the Criminal Code sets out the general principles of criminal responsibility.

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code.
