# 165  Taxation objection

A person who is dissatisfied with:

> <a id="s165-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a decision of the Regulator to give a rectification direction or an education direction, or to vary one otherwise than in accordance with a request under section 164; or

> <a id="s165-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a decision of the Regulator under section 164 to refuse to vary a rectification direction or an education direction; may object against the decision in the manner set out in Part IVC of the Taxation Administration Act 1953. managed superannuation funds
