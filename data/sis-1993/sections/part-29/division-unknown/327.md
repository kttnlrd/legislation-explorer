# 327  Interpretation

In this Part: modifiable provision means any of the following:

> <a id="s327-a"></a>
> **(a)** a provision of Part 2A, 2B or 3;

> <a id="s327-c"></a>
> **(c)** section 54;

> <a id="s327-d"></a>
> **(d)** subsection 63(7B), (7C) or (7D);

> <a id="s327-e"></a>
> **(e)** a provision of Part 9;

> <a id="s327-g"></a>
> **(g)** a provision of Part 19 or 24;

> <a id="s327-h"></a>
> **(h)** a provision of any regulations made for the purposes of a provision referred to in paragraphs (a) to (g).

> **Note:** For the definition of Regulator, see subsection 10(1).
