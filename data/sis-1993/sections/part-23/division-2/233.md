# 233  Financial assistance to be subject to conditions

233 Financial assistance to be subject to conditions

<a id="s233-1"></a>
**(1)**  The payment to a trustee of a fund of a grant of financial assistance is subject to the following conditions:

> <a id="s233-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a condition that the amount of financial assistance granted will be deposited in the corpus of the fund;

> <a id="s233-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a condition that the amount will be applied, within a period determined by the Minister:

> > <a id="s233-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  in making payments to persons who were beneficiaries in the fund at the time the fund suffered the eligible loss; or

> > <a id="s233-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  for the benefit of those persons in such other manner as the Minister approves in writing;

> <a id="s233-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  a condition that a trustee of the fund will prepare and give to the Minister such reports on the application of the amount as are required by the Minister;

> <a id="s233-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  such other conditions (if any) as the Minister determines and notifies in writing to a trustee of the fund.

<a id="s233-2"></a>
**(2)**  Despite any law of the Commonwealth, a State or a Territory, whether written or unwritten, or any provision of a trust deed or other rules according to which a fund is administered:

> <a id="s233-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a trustee of a fund must comply with a condition mentioned in paragraph (1)(b); and

> <a id="s233-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the trustee does not contravene the law, trust deed or rules by complying with such a condition.
