# 230  Minister may request additional information


<a id="s230-1"></a>
**(1)** The Minister may request a trustee of the fund to give such additional information as the Minister considers necessary to enable the application to be determined.

<a id="s230-2"></a>
**(2)** The Minister may, by signed instrument, delegate the Minister's power under subsection (1) to an SES employee, or acting SES employee, in the Department.

<a id="s230-3"></a>
**(3)** In exercising a power delegated under subsection (2), a delegate must comply with any directions of the Minister.
