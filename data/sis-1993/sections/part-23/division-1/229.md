# 229  Application for assistance


<a id="s229-1"></a>
**(1)** If:

> <a id="s229-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** a fund suffers an eligible loss after the commencement of this Part; and

> <a id="s229-1-aa"></a>
> **(aa)** at the time it suffers the loss, the fund is:

> > <a id="s229-1-aa-i"></a>
> > **(i)** a regulated superannuation fund (other than a self managed superannuation fund); or

> > <a id="s229-1-aa-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** an approved deposit fund; and

> <a id="s229-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the loss has caused substantial diminution of the fund leading to difficulties in the payment of benefits; a trustee of the fund may apply to the Minister for a grant of financial assistance for the fund.

<a id="s229-2"></a>
**(2)** The application must be in writing and be accompanied by such information as the Minister determines.

<a id="s229-3"></a>
**(3)** To avoid doubt, an application may be made under this section by a trustee of a self managed superannuation fund as long as the fund met the requirements in subsection (1) at the time the fund suffered the loss to which the application relates.
