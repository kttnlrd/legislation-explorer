# 128N  ASIC may disclose information to the Commissioner of Taxation

Taxation ASIC may disclose information, given to it in or in connection with the performance of its functions or the exercise of its powers under this Part or Part 25, to the Commissioner of Taxation for the purpose of administering the provisions of this Act.

> **Note:** A disclosure of information permitted by this section is an authorised disclosure for the purposes of subsection 127(2) of the Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001.
