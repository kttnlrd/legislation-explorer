# 131CA  Disqualified firms and disqualified companies not to be an RSE auditor

RSE auditor

<a id="s131CA-1"></a>
**(1)**  A person commits an offence if:

> <a id="s131CA-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the person is a member of a firm; and

> <a id="s131CA-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the firm is, or acts as, an RSE auditor; and

> <a id="s131CA-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the firm is disqualified under section 130EA; and

> <a id="s131CA-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the person knows that the firm is so disqualified. Penalty: Imprisonment for 2 years.

<a id="s131CA-2"></a>
**(2)**  A person commits an offence if:

> <a id="s131CA-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the person is, or acts as, an RSE auditor; and

> <a id="s131CA-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the person is a company; and

> <a id="s131CA-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the company is disqualified under section 130EA; and

> <a id="s131CA-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the person knows that the company is so disqualified. Penalty: 600 penalty units.
