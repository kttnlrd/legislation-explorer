# 131CB  


> <a id="s131CB-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the firm is, or acts as, an RSE auditor; and

> <a id="s131CB-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the firm is disqualified under section 130EA. Penalty: 60 penalty units.

<a id="s131CB-4"></a>
**(4)**  A person commits an offence if:

> <a id="s131CB-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the person is, or acts as, an RSE auditor; and

> <a id="s131CB-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the person is a company; and

> <a id="s131CB-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the company is disqualified under section 130EA. Penalty: 300 penalty units.

<a id="s131CB-5"></a>
**(5)**  Subsections (3) and (4) are offences of strict liability. 131CB Members or employees of disqualified firms, and directors or employees of disqualified companies, not to be RSE auditors

<a id="s131CB-1"></a>
**(1)**  A person commits an offence if:

> <a id="s131CB-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the person is, or acts as, an RSE auditor; and

> <a id="s131CB-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the person is a member or employee of a firm; and

> <a id="s131CB-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the firm is disqualified under section 130EA; and

> <a id="s131CB-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the person knows that the firm is so disqualified. Penalty: Imprisonment for 2 years.

<a id="s131CB-2"></a>
**(2)**  A person commits an offence if:

> <a id="s131CB-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the person is, or acts as, an RSE auditor; and

> <a id="s131CB-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the person is a director or employee of a company; and

> <a id="s131CB-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the company is disqualified under section 130EA; and

> <a id="s131CB-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the person knows that the company is so disqualified. Penalty: Imprisonment for 2 years.

<a id="s131CB-3"></a>
**(3)**  A person commits an offence if:

> <a id="s131CB-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the person is, or acts as, an RSE auditor; and

> <a id="s131CB-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the person is a member or employee of a firm; and

> <a id="s131CB-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the firm is disqualified under section 130EA. Penalty: 60 penalty units.

<a id="s131CB-4"></a>
**(4)**  A person commits an offence if:

> <a id="s131CB-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the person is, or acts as, an RSE auditor; and

> <a id="s131CB-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the person is a director or employee of a company; and

> <a id="s131CB-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the company is disqualified under section 130EA. Penalty: 60 penalty units.

<a id="s131CB-5"></a>
**(5)**  Subsections (3) and (4) are offences of strict liability.
