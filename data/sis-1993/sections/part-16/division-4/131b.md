# 131B  Offence of holding oneself out as an actuary or auditor


<a id="s131B-1"></a>
**(1)**  A person commits an offence if:

> <a id="s131B-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the person holds themself out as a superannuation actuary; and

> <a id="s131B-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the person is not a superannuation actuary. Penalty: 50 penalty units.

<a id="s131B-2"></a>
**(2)**  A person commits an offence if:

> <a id="s131B-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the person holds themself out as an approved SMSF auditor; and

> <a id="s131B-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the person is not an approved SMSF auditor. Penalty: 50 penalty units.

<a id="s131B-2A"></a>
**(2A)**  A person commits an offence if:

> <a id="s131B-2A-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the person holds themself out as an RSE auditor; and

> <a id="s131B-2A-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the person is not an RSE auditor. Penalty: 50 penalty units.

<a id="s131B-3"></a>
**(3)**  Subsections (1), (2) and (2A) are offences of strict liability.

> **Note:** Chapter 2 of the Criminal Code sets out the general principles of criminal responsibility.

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code.

> **Note:** See also sections 131CC and 131CD.
