# 205  Effect during criminal proceedings


<a id="s205-1"></a>
**(1)** An application may be made for a civil penalty order against the person in relation to the same contravention.

<a id="s205-2"></a>
**(2)** However, such an application is stayed, because of this subsection, until:

> <a id="s205-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** the criminal proceedings; and

> <a id="s205-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** all appeals and applications for review (including appeals and applications for review under this Division) arising out of the criminal proceedings; have been finally determined or otherwise disposed of.
