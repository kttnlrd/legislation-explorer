# 201A  Criminal jurisdiction of the Federal Court of Australia in relation to certain indictable offences

relation to certain indictable offences The Federal Court of Australia has jurisdiction to hear and determine prosecutions for indictable offences against provisions of this Act that are administered by ASIC.
