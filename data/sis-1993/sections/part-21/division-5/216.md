# 216  Criminal court may order compensation


<a id="s216-1"></a>
**(1)** If:

> <a id="s216-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** a court finds a person guilty of an offence constituted by a contravention of a civil penalty provision in relation to a superannuation entity; and

> <a id="s216-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the court is satisfied that the superannuation entity has suffered loss or damage as a result of the act or omission constituting the contravention; the court may (whether or not it imposes a penalty) order the person to pay to a trustee of the entity or, if the person is a trustee of the entity, to pay to the entity compensation of such amount as the order specifies.

> **Note:** Section 195 defines when a court is taken to find a person guilty of an offence.

<a id="s216-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

> <a id="s216-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** a court declares under Division 4 that a person has, by an act or omission, contravened a civil penalty provision in relation to a superannuation entity; and

> <a id="s216-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the court is satisfied that the superannuation entity has suffered loss or damage as a result of that act or omission; the court may (whether or not it makes an order under subsection 196(3)) order the person to pay to a trustee of the entity or, if the person is a trustee of the entity, to pay to the entity compensation of such amount as the order specifies.
