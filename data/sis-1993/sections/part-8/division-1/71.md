# 71  Meaning of in-house asset

Basic meaning

<a id="s71-1"></a>
**(1)** For the purposes of this Part, an in-house asset of a superannuation fund is an asset of the fund that is a loan to, or an investment in, a related party of the fund, an investment in a related trust of the fund, or an asset of the fund subject to a lease or lease arrangement between a trustee of the fund and a related party of the fund, but does not include:

> <a id="s71-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** a life policy issued by a life insurance company; or

> <a id="s71-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** a deposit with an ADI; or

> <a id="s71-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** an investment in a pooled superannuation trust, where a trustee of the fund and the trustee of the pooled superannuation trust acted at arm's length in relation to the making of that investment; or

> <a id="s71-1-d"></a>
> **(d)** an asset of a public sector fund, where the asset consists of an investment in securities issued under the authority of:

> > <a id="s71-1-d-i"></a>
> > **(i)** the Commonwealth or a government of a State or a Territory; or

> > <a id="s71-1-d-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** a public authority constituted by or under a law of the Commonwealth, a State or a Territory, where the public authority is neither a standard employer-sponsor, nor an associate of a standard employer-sponsor, of the fund; or

> <a id="s71-1-e"></a>
> **(e)** an asset which the Regulator, by written notice given to a trustee of the fund, determines is not an in-house asset of the fund; or

> <a id="s71-1-f"></a>
> **(f)** an asset which the Regulator, by legislative instrument, determines is not an in-house asset of:

> > <a id="s71-1-f-i"></a>
> > **(i)** any fund; or

> > <a id="s71-1-f-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** a class of funds in which the fund is included; or

> <a id="s71-1-g"></a>
> **(g)** if the superannuation fund has no more than 6 members— real property subject to a lease, or to a lease arrangement enforceable by legal proceedings, between a trustee of the fund and a related party of the fund, if, throughout the term of the lease or lease arrangement, the property is business real property (within the meaning of subsection 66(5)) of the fund; or

> <a id="s71-1-h"></a>
> **(h)** an investment in a widely held unit trust; or

> > <a id="s71-1-h-i"></a>
> > **(i)** property owned by the superannuation fund and a related party as tenants in common, other than property subject to a lease or lease arrangement between a trustee of the fund and a related party; or

> <a id="s71-1-j"></a>
> **(j)** an asset included in a class of assets specified in the regulations:

> > <a id="s71-1-j-i"></a>
> > **(i)** not to be in-house assets of any fund; or

> > <a id="s71-1-j-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** not to be in-house assets of a class of funds to which the fund belongs. For this purpose, a class of assets may consist of, but is not limited to, assets that are investments in entities that undertake, or do not undertake, specified activities. Widely held trust

<a id="s71-1A"></a>
**(1A)**  For the purposes of paragraph (1)(h), a trust is a widely held unit trust if:

> <a id="s71-1A-a"></a>
> **(a)** it is a unit trust in which entities have fixed entitlements to all of the income and capital of the trust; and

> <a id="s71-1A-b"></a>
> **(b)** it is not a trust in which fewer than 20 entities between them have:

> > <a id="s71-1A-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** fixed entitlements to 75% or more of the income of the trust; or

> > <a id="s71-1A-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** fixed entitlements to 75% or more of the capital of the trust. For this purpose, an entity and the Part 8 associates of the entity are taken to be a single entity. Agreements

<a id="s71-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

> <a id="s71-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** apart from this subsection, an asset of a fund consists of a loan, an investment or an asset that is subject to a lease or lease arrangement, other than an in-house asset; and

> <a id="s71-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the loan, investment, lease or lease arrangement was made as a result of entering into or carrying out an agreement; and

> <a id="s71-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** any of the persons who entered into or carried out the agreement was aware that the result of carrying out the agreement would be that:

> > <a id="s71-2-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)** a loan would be made to, an investment would be made in, or an asset would be subject to a lease or lease arrangement with, a related party of the fund; or

> > <a id="s71-2-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** an investment would be made in a related trust of the fund; then the asset is taken, for the purposes of this Part, to be a loan to, an investment in, or an asset subject to a lease or lease arrangement with, that related party or related trust, as the case requires. Definition

<a id="s71-2A"></a>
**(2A)**  In subsection (2): agreement includes any arrangement, understanding, promise or undertaking whether express or implied, and whether or not enforceable, or intended to be enforceable, by legal proceedings. Exceptions

<a id="s71-2B"></a>
**(2B)**  Subsection (2) does not apply to an investment referred to in paragraph 71(1)(a), (b), (c) or (h). 2 or more persons

<a id="s71-3"></a>
**(3)** Subsection (2) does not stop the same asset from being treated as if it were a loan to, an investment in, or an asset subject to a lease or lease arrangement with, 2 or more persons. The Regulator's determination

<a id="s71-4"></a>
**(4)** If:

> <a id="s71-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** apart from this subsection, an asset of a fund consists of a loan, an investment, or an asset subject to a lease or lease arrangement, other than an in-house asset; and

> <a id="s71-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** the Regulator, by written notice given to a trustee of the fund, determines that the asset is to be treated, with effect from the day on which the notice is given, as if the asset were a loan to, an investment in, or an asset subject to a lease or lease arrangement with, a specified related party or related trust of the fund, including a person taken to be a standard employer-sponsor of the fund under section 70A; then, despite paragraphs (1)(a) to (j), the asset is taken, for the purposes of this Part, to be a loan to or an investment in the related party or related trust, or an asset subject to a lease or lease agreement between a trustee of the fund and the related party. Paragraph (1)(e) determinations or paragraph (1)(j) regulations may be retrospective

<a id="s71-5"></a>
**(5)** A determination under paragraph (1)(e) or regulations under paragraph (1)(j) may be expressed to have taken effect at a time earlier than the time when the determination or regulations were made. Public sector superannuation funds

<a id="s71-7"></a>
**(7)** For the purposes of applying this section to determine what is an in-house asset of a public sector superannuation fund, a reference to a Part 8 associate of an employer-sponsor of the fund is a reference to a body corporate in respect of which either of the following conditions is satisfied:

> <a id="s71-7-a"></a>
> **(a)** the body corporate is sufficiently influenced by, or a majority voting interest in the body corporate is held by, the employer-sponsor;

> <a id="s71-7-b"></a>
> **(b)** the employer-sponsor is sufficiently influenced by, or a majority voting interest in the employer-sponsor is held by, the body corporate. Limit on when investments in related trusts are in-house assets

<a id="s71-8"></a>
**(8)** If, at a time:

> <a id="s71-8-a"></a>
> **(a)** an asset (the investment asset) of a superannuation fund is an investment in a related trust of the fund; and

> <a id="s71-8-b"></a>
> **(b)** the related trust is one described in paragraph 67A(1)(b) in connection with a borrowing, by the trustee of the fund, that is covered by subsection 67A(1); and

> <a id="s71-8-c"></a>
> **(c)** the only property of the related trust is the acquirable asset mentioned in that paragraph; the investment asset is an in-house asset of the fund at the time only if the acquirable asset mentioned in that paragraph would be an in-house asset of the fund if it were an asset of the fund at the time.

<a id="s71-9"></a>
**(9)** Subsections (1), (2) and (4) have effect subject to subsection (8).
