# 252A  APRA or Commissioner of Taxation may request certain information

information Notice may be given

<a id="s252A-1"></a>
**(1)** APRA or the Commissioner of Taxation may give a written notice under this section to a trustee of a regulated superannuation fund if APRA or the Commissioner of Taxation, as the case requires, considers that the fund has no more than 6 members. Information that may be requested

<a id="s252A-2"></a>
**(2)** The notice may require each trustee of the fund, within a specified period (which must not be shorter than 21 days), to ensure that APRA or the Commissioner of Taxation is informed:

> <a id="s252A-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** whether or not the fund was a self managed superannuation fund as at the date (the response date) on which APRA or the Commissioner of Taxation was so informed; or

> <a id="s252A-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** if the fund was not a self managed superannuation fund as at the response date—whether the trustee, or the trustees, of the fund consider that the fund is likely to become a self managed superannuation fund within the period specified in the notice; and

> <a id="s252A-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** if the fund was a self managed superannuation fund as at the response date—whether the trustee, or the trustees, of the fund consider that the fund is likely to cease to be a self Provisions relating to the administration by APRA and the Commissioner of Taxation of superannuation funds with no more than 6 members Part 24B Monitoring of superannuation funds with no more than 6 members Division 1 Section 252A managed superannuation fund within the period specified in the notice. Offence

<a id="s252A-3"></a>
**(3)** A person who contravenes subsection (2) commits an offence punishable on conviction by a fine not exceeding 50 penalty units. Strict liability

<a id="s252A-4"></a>
**(4)** An offence under subsection (3) is an offence of strict liability. Taxation of superannuation funds with no more than 6 members
