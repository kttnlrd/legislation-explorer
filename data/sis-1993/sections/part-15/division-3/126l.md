# 126L  Privilege against exposure to penalty—disqualification under section 126A, 126H, 130D or 130EA

section 126A, 126H, 130D or 130EA Proceedings

<a id="s126L-1"></a>
**(1)** In the case of any proceeding under, or arising out of, this Act, a person is not entitled to refuse or fail to comply with a requirement:

> <a id="s126L-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** to answer a question or give information; or

> <a id="s126L-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** to produce books; or

> <a id="s126L-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** to do any other act; on the ground that the answer or information, production of the books, or doing that other act, as the case may be, might tend to make the person liable to a penalty by way of a disqualification under section 126A, 126H or 130D or tend to make a firm or company liable to disqualification under section 130EA.

<a id="s126L-2"></a>
**(2)** Subsection (1) applies whether or not the person is a defendant in, or a party to, the proceeding or any other proceeding. Statutory requirements

<a id="s126L-3"></a>
**(3)** A person is not entitled to refuse or fail to comply with a requirement under this Act:

> <a id="s126L-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** to answer a question or give information; or

> <a id="s126L-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** to produce books; or

> <a id="s126L-3-c"></a>
> **(c)** to do any other act; on the ground that the answer or information, production of the books, or doing that other act, as the case may be, might tend to make the person liable to a penalty by way of a disqualification under section 126A, 126H or 130D or tend to make a firm or company liable to disqualification under section 130EA.  Admissibility

<a id="s126L-4"></a>
**(4)** Subsections 130B(2), 287(3), 290(2) and 336F(2) do not apply to a proceeding for the imposition of a penalty by way of a disqualification under section 126A, 126H or 130D or a proceeding under section 130EA. Other provisions

<a id="s126L-5"></a>
**(5)** Subsections (1) and (3) of this section have effect despite anything in:

> <a id="s126L-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** section 199; or

> <a id="s126L-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** any other provision of this Act; or

> <a id="s126L-5-c"></a>
> **(c)** the Administrative Review Tribunal Act 2024. Definition

<a id="s126L-6"></a>
**(6)** In this section: penalty includes forfeiture. entities
