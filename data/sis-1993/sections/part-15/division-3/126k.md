# 126K  Disqualified persons not to be trustees, investment managers or custodians of superannuation entities

or custodians of superannuation entities

<a id="s126K-1"></a>
**(1)** A person commits an offence if:

> <a id="s126K-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the person is a disqualified person; and

> <a id="s126K-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the person knows he or she is a disqualified person; and

> <a id="s126K-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** the person is or acts as a trustee, investment manager or custodian of a superannuation entity; and

> <a id="s126K-1-d"></a>
> **(d)** for a person who is an individual and who is a disqualified person only because he or she was disqualified under section 126H—the person is disqualified from being or acting as a trustee of that superannuation entity. Penalty: Imprisonment for 2 years.

<a id="s126K-2"></a>
**(2)** A person commits an offence if:

> <a id="s126K-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** the person is a disqualified person; and

> <a id="s126K-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the person knows he or she is a disqualified person; and

> <a id="s126K-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** the person is or acts as a trustee, investment manager or custodian of a superannuation entity; and

> <a id="s126K-2-d"></a>
> **(d)** for a person who is an individual and who is a disqualified person only because he or she was disqualified under section 126H—the person is disqualified from being or acting as a trustee of that superannuation entity. Penalty: 60 penalty units.

<a id="s126K-3"></a>
**(3)** Subsection (2) is an offence of strict liability. Standards for trustees, custodians and investment managers of superannuation entities Part 15 Disqualified persons Division 3 Section 126K

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code.

<a id="s126K-4"></a>
**(4)** A person commits an offence if:

> <a id="s126K-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** the person is a disqualified person; and

> <a id="s126K-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** the person knows he or she is a disqualified person; and

> <a id="s126K-4-c"></a>
> **(c)** the person is or acts as a responsible officer of a body corporate that is a trustee, investment manager or custodian of a superannuation entity; and

> <a id="s126K-4-d"></a>
> **(d)** for a person who is an individual and who is a disqualified person only because he or she was disqualified under section 126H—the person is disqualified from being or acting as that responsible officer. Penalty: Imprisonment for 2 years.

<a id="s126K-5"></a>
**(5)** A person commits an offence if:

> <a id="s126K-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** the person is a disqualified person; and

> <a id="s126K-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** the person knows he or she is a disqualified person; and

> <a id="s126K-5-c"></a>
> **(c)** the person is or acts as a responsible officer of a body corporate that is a trustee, investment manager or custodian of a superannuation entity; and

> <a id="s126K-5-d"></a>
> **(d)** for a person who is an individual and who is a disqualified person only because he or she was disqualified under section 126H—the person is disqualified from being or acting as that responsible officer. Penalty: 60 penalty units.

<a id="s126K-6"></a>
**(6)** Subsection (5) is an offence of strict liability.

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code.

<a id="s126K-7"></a>
**(7)** A person commits an offence if:

> <a id="s126K-7-a"></a>
> **(a)** the person is a trustee of a superannuation entity; and

> <a id="s126K-7-b"></a>
> **(b)** the person is or becomes a disqualified person; and

> <a id="s126K-7-c"></a>
> **(c)** the person does not tell the Regulator in writing immediately. Penalty: 50 penalty units. entities

<a id="s126K-8"></a>
**(8)** Subsection (7) is an offence of strict liability.

> **Note:** Chapter 2 of the Criminal Code sets out the general principles of criminal responsibility.

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code.
