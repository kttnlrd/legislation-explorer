# 124  Investment managers must be appointed in writing


<a id="s124-1"></a>
**(1)** A trustee of a superannuation entity must not make a non-written appointment of an investment manager of the entity.

> **Note:** Section 166 imposes an administrative penalty for a contravention of subsection (1) in relation to a self managed superannuation fund. Standards for trustees, custodians and investment managers of superannuation entities Part 15 Requirements for custodians and investment managers Division 2 Section 125

<a id="s124-2"></a>
**(2)** A trustee commits an offence if the trustee contravenes subsection (1). This is an offence of strict liability. Penalty: 50 penalty units.

> **Note:** Chapter 2 of the Criminal Code sets out the general principles of criminal responsibility.

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code.
