# 155-1  What this Division is about

This Division contains rules relating to assessments. The rules in this Division deal with the following:

<a id="s155-1-a"></a>
**(a)** how assessments are made or amended and their effect;

<a id="s155-1-b"></a>
**(b)** review of assessments.
