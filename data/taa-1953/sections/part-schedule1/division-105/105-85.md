# 105-85  Amending Acts cannot impose penalties or general interest charge earlier than 28 days after Royal Assent


<a id="s105-85-1"></a>
**(1)** An Act that amends an *indirect tax law does not have the effect of making you liable to:

<a id="s105-85-1-a"></a>
**(a)** a penalty for an offence against an indirect tax law; or

<a id="s105-85-1-b"></a>
**(b)** *general interest charge under section 105-80; Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of other taxes Chapter 3 Indirect taxes Part 3-10 Section 105-120 for any act or omission that happens before the 28th day (the postponed day) after the day on which the amending Act receives the Royal Assent.

<a id="s105-85-2"></a>
**(2)** If the amending Act would (apart from this section) have the effect of making you liable to such a penalty or charge because you contravened a requirement to do something:

<a id="s105-85-2-a"></a>
**(a)** within a specified period ending before the postponed day; or

<a id="s105-85-2-b"></a>
**(b)** before a specified time happening before the postponed day; the requirement has effect instead by reference to a period ending at the start of the postponed day, or by reference to the start of the postponed day, as the case requires.

<a id="s105-85-3"></a>
**(3)** This section does not relieve you from liability to such a penalty or charge to the extent to which the liability would have existed if the amending Act had not been enacted.
