# 260-55  Joint liability of 2 or more liquidators

If there are 2 or more persons who become liquidators of the company, the obligations and liabilities under this Subdivision:

<a id="s260-55-a"></a>
**(a)** apply to all the liquidators; but

<a id="s260-55-b"></a>
**(b)** may be discharged by any of them. Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Generic assessment, collection and recovery rules Chapter 4 Collection and recovery of tax-related liabilities and other amounts Part 4-15 Section 260-60
