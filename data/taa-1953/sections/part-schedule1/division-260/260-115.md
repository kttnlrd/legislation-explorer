# 260-115  Joint liability of 2 or more agents

If 2 or more agents are jointly instructed by the principal to wind up the business, the obligations and liabilities under this Subdivision: Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Generic assessment, collection and recovery rules Chapter 4 Collection and recovery of tax-related liabilities and other amounts Part 4-15 Section 260-120

<a id="s260-115-a"></a>
**(a)** apply to all the agents; but

<a id="s260-115-b"></a>
**(b)** may be discharged by any of them.
