# 358-15  When a public ruling ceases to apply


<a id="s358-15-1"></a>
**(1)** A *public ruling may specify the time at which it ceases to apply.

<a id="s358-15-2"></a>
**(2)** If a *public ruling does not do this, it applies until it is withdrawn. Withdrawing public rulings
