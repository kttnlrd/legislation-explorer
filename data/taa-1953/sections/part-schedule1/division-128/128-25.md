# 128-25  Unincorporated GloBE Joint Ventures, etc. and certain unincorporated Group Entities

Obligations

<a id="s128-25-1"></a>
**(1)**  Subject to subsection (2), if an obligation is imposed under the *Minimum Tax law on a *GloBE Entity of a kind specified in an item in the table in subsection (5), the obligation is imposed on each liable entity specified in the item, but may be discharged by any such entity. Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of other taxes Chapter 3 Australian IIR/UTPR tax and Australian DMT tax Part 3-18 Section 128-25 Liabilities to pay an amount

<a id="s128-25-2"></a>
**(2)**  If an amount is payable under the *Minimum Tax law by a *GloBE Entity of a kind specified in an item in the table in subsection (5), the liable entities specified in the item are jointly and severally liable to pay the amount. Offences

<a id="s128-25-3"></a>
**(3)**  Any offence against the *Minimum Tax law that is committed by a *GloBE Entity of a kind specified in an item in the table in subsection (5) is taken to have been committed by each liable entity specified in the item.

<a id="s128-25-4"></a>
**(4)**  In a prosecution of an entity for an offence that the entity is taken to have committed because of subsection (3), it is a defence if the entity proves that the entity:

> <a id="s128-25-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  did not aid, abet, counsel or procure the relevant act or omission; and

> <a id="s128-25-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  was not in any way knowingly concerned in, or party to, the relevant act or omission (whether directly or indirectly and whether by any act or omission of the entity).

> **Note:** The defence in subsection (4) does not apply in relation to offences under Part 2.4 of the Criminal Code.

> **Note:** A defendant bears a legal burden in relation to the matters in subsection (4): see section 13.4 of the Criminal Code. Liable entities
