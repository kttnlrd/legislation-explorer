# 128-35  Joint and several liability does not extend to GloBE

Securitisation Entities

<a id="s128-35-1"></a>
**(1)**  For the purposes of paragraph 128-5(1)(b), treat a *Group Entity that is a *GloBE Securitisation Entity as being a Group Entity excluded by subsection 128-5(2).

<a id="s128-35-2"></a>
**(2)**  For the purposes of subsection 128-10(2), treat a *Group Entity that is a *GloBE Securitisation Entity as being an Entity excluded by subsection 128-10(3). Part 3-20—Superannuation
