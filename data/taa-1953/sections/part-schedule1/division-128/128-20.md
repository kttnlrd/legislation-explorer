# 128-20  Partnerships

Application

<a id="s128-20-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies in relation to a *GloBE partnership that is not:

> <a id="s128-20-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a *GloBE Joint Venture of an *Applicable MNE Group; or

> <a id="s128-20-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a *GloBE JV Subsidiary of a GloBE Joint Venture of an Applicable MNE Group. Obligations

<a id="s128-20-2"></a>
**(2)**  Subject to subsection (3), if an obligation is imposed under the *Minimum Tax law on the *GloBE partnership, the obligation is imposed on each partner of the GloBE partnership, but may be discharged by any such partner. Liabilities to pay an amount

<a id="s128-20-3"></a>
**(3)**  If an amount is payable under the *Minimum Tax law by the *GloBE partnership, the partners of the GloBE partnership are jointly and severally liable to pay the amount. Offences

<a id="s128-20-4"></a>
**(4)**  Any offence against the *Minimum Tax law that is committed by the *GloBE partnership is taken to have been committed by each partner of the GloBE partnership.

<a id="s128-20-5"></a>
**(5)**  In a prosecution of an entity for an offence that the entity is taken to have committed because of subsection (4), it is a defence if the entity proves that the entity:

> <a id="s128-20-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  did not aid, abet, counsel or procure the relevant act or omission; and

> <a id="s128-20-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  was not in any way knowingly concerned in, or party to, the relevant act or omission (whether directly or indirectly and whether by any act or omission of the entity).

> **Note:** The defence in subsection (5) does not apply in relation to offences under Part 2.4 of the Criminal Code.

> **Note:** A defendant bears a legal burden in relation to the matters in subsection (5): see section 13.4 of the Criminal Code. Meaning of GloBE partnership

<a id="s128-20-6"></a>
**(6)**  A GloBE partnership is a partnership (within the meaning of paragraph 13(1)(b) of the *Minimum Tax Act).

<a id="s128-20-7"></a>
**(7)**  To avoid doubt, section 94K of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 (about corporate limited partnerships) does not apply in relation to this section.
