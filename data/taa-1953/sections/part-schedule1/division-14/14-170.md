# 14-170  Application of rules in Division 18

These provisions:

> <a id="s14-170-a"></a>
> **(a)** subsection 18-15(1) (about credits for amounts withheld from withholding payments); and

> <a id="s14-170-b"></a>
> **(b)** sections 18-65 and 18-70 (about refunds of amounts withheld in error); apply as if any of the *TFN withholding tax (ESS) that has been paid were an amount withheld under section 12-35 from a *withholding payment made to the individual and covered by that section.
