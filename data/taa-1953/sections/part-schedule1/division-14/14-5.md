# 14-5  Provider of non-cash benefit must pay amount to the

Commissioner if payment would be subject to withholding

<a id="s14-5-1"></a>
**(1)**  An entity (the payer) must pay an amount to the Commissioner before providing a *non-cash benefit to another entity (the Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of income tax Chapter 2 Pay as you go (PAYG) withholding Part 2-5 Section 14-10 recipient) if Division 12 would require the payer to withhold an amount (the notionally withheld amount) if, instead of providing the benefit to the recipient, the payer made a payment to the recipient in money equal to the *market value of the benefit when the benefit is provided.

<a id="s14-5-2"></a>
**(2)**  The amount to be paid to the Commissioner is equal to the notionally withheld amount.

> **Example:** Nick is a building contractor who has entered into a voluntary agreement with Mike for the purposes of section 12-55. Nick proposes to give Mike his old utility van (whose market value is $1,000) as payment for work Mike has done for him over a fortnight. If Nick were instead to pay Mike $1,000, Nick would have had to withhold $203 under Division 12 (in accordance with withholding rates current at the time). This section requires Nick to pay $203 to the Commissioner before giving the van to Mike.

<a id="s14-5-3"></a>
**(3)**  This section does not apply to providing:

> <a id="s14-5-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a *fringe benefit; or

> <a id="s14-5-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a benefit that is an exempt benefit under the Fringe Benefits Tax Assessment Act 1986; or

> <a id="s14-5-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  a benefit that would be an exempt benefit under that Act if paragraphs (d) and (e) of the definition of employer in subsection 136(1) of that Act were omitted; or

> <a id="s14-5-3-d"></a>
> **(d)**  a benefit constituted by the acquisition of an *ESS interest *under an employee share scheme to which Subdivision 83A-B or 83A-C of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 applies.
