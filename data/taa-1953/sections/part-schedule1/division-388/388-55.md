# 388-55  Commissioner may defer time for lodgment


<a id="s388-55-1"></a>
**(1)** The Commissioner may defer the time within which an *approved form is required to be given to the Commissioner or to another entity. Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Administration Chapter 5 Record-keeping and other obligations relating to taxpayers Part 5-25 Section 388-60

<a id="s388-55-2"></a>
**(2)** A deferral under subsection (1) does not defer the time for payment of any amount to the Commissioner.

**Note:** Section 255-10 allows the Commissioner to defer the time for payment of an amount of a tax-related liability.
