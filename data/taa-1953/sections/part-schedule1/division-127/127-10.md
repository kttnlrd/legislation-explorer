# 127-10  GloBE Information Returns given by Designated Local

Entities

<a id="s127-10-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies if a *Designated Local Entity in relation to a *GloBE Information Return in respect of an *Applicable MNE Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of other taxes Chapter 3 Australian IIR/UTPR tax and Australian DMT tax Part 3-18 Section 127-15 Group for a *Fiscal Year gives the GloBE Information Return to the Commissioner in accordance with subsection 127-5(2).

<a id="s127-10-2"></a>
**(2)**  Each other *Group Entity of the *Applicable MNE Group that is *GloBE located in Australia for the *Fiscal year is taken to give the *GloBE Information Return to the Commissioner at the time the *Designated Local Entity gives the GloBE Information Return to the Commissioner.
