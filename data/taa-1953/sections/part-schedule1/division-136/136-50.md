# 136-50  What this Subdivision is about

The Commissioner must issue a commutation authority to a superannuation income stream provider, unless you have notified the Commissioner that you have already reduced your excess transfer balance by the crystallised reduction amount. A superannuation income stream provider will usually be required to commute the superannuation income stream stated in the authority.
