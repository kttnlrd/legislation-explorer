# 136-55  Issuing of commutation authorities

Commutation authority must be issued if there is a commutable amount

<a id="s136-55-1"></a>
**(1)** The Commissioner must issue a commutation authority under this section to one or more *superannuation income stream providers if:

<a id="s136-55-1-a"></a>
**(a)** an *excess transfer balance determination has been issued to you; and

<a id="s136-55-1-b"></a>
**(b)** the excess transfer balance determination has not been revoked; and

<a id="s136-55-1-c"></a>
**(c)** the period mentioned in subsection 136-20(4) has ended; and

<a id="s136-55-1-d"></a>
**(d)** an amount (the commutable amount) greater than nil remains after reducing the *crystallised reduction amount by the sum of any *transfer balance debits notified to the Commissioner under section 136-25. Issuing in response to a valid election

<a id="s136-55-2"></a>
**(2)** If you have made a valid election under section 136-20, the Commissioner must issue a commutation authority under this section to each *superannuation income stream provider identified in your election.

<a id="s136-55-3"></a>
**(3)** If the total of the amounts stated in your election under section 136-20 falls short of the commutable amount, the Commissioner must also issue a commutation authority to one or more *superannuation income stream providers specified in the *default commutation notice. Issuing if you do not make a valid election

<a id="s136-55-4"></a>
**(4)** If you have not made a valid election under section 136-20, the Commissioner must issue a commutation authority to each *superannuation income stream provider specified in the *default commutation notice. Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of other taxes Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-20 Section 136-60 Requirements for commutation authority

<a id="s136-55-5"></a>
**(5)** Each commutation authority must:

<a id="s136-55-5-a"></a>
**(a)** specify the *superannuation income stream that the *superannuation income stream provider is to commute, in full or in part; and

<a id="s136-55-5-b"></a>
**(b)** state the amount (the reduction amount) by which the superannuation income stream is to be reduced; and

<a id="s136-55-5-c"></a>
**(c)** be dated; and

<a id="s136-55-5-d"></a>
**(d)** contain any other information that the Commissioner considers relevant.

<a id="s136-55-6"></a>
**(6)** The total of all reduction amounts stated in commutation authorities issued under this section relating to an *excess transfer balance determination must not exceed the commutable amount.
