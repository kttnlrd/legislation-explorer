# 136-25  Notifying Commissioner of transfer balance debits


<a id="s136-25-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies to you if you have received an *excess transfer balance determination.

<a id="s136-25-2"></a>
**(2)** You may notify the Commissioner in the *approved form of the amount of a *transfer balance debit that arises in your *transfer balance account if the debit arises in the period:

<a id="s136-25-2-a"></a>
**(a)** beginning when the determination is made; and

<a id="s136-25-2-b"></a>
**(b)** ending at the earlier of: Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of other taxes Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-20 Section 136-50

<a id="s136-25-2-b-i"></a>
**(i)** the time you made an election under section 136-20; and

<a id="s136-25-2-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** the end of the period within which an election under section 136-20 may be made. Subdivision 136-B—Commutation authorities Guide to Subdivision 136-B
