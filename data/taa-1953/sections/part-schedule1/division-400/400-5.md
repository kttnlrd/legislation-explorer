# 400-5  The payment, ABN and identification verification system


<a id="s400-5-1"></a>
**(1)** There are 4 components in the payment, ABN and identification verification system:

<a id="s400-5-1-a"></a>
**(a)** transaction reporting by purchasers (Division 405);

<a id="s400-5-1-b"></a>
**(b)** transaction reporting by suppliers (Division 410);

<a id="s400-5-1-c"></a>
**(c)** verification of suppliers' *ABNs by purchasers (Division 415);

<a id="s400-5-1-d"></a>
**(d)** verification of suppliers' identities by purchasers (Division 417); One or more of the components may apply to a particular payment. The regulations will specify which components apply.

<a id="s400-5-2"></a>
**(2)** Where a component of the system applies to a payment, the requirements of that component must be complied with. Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Administration Chapter 5 Payment, ABN and identification verification system Part 5-30 Section 405-5
