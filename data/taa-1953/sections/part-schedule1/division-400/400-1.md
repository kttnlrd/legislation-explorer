# 400-1  What Part 5-30 is about

To improve compliance with the tax laws that relate to payments for certain supplies, this Part imposes additional requirements on purchasers and suppliers. The additional requirements relate to verification of ABNs and to reporting information about payments. Regulations will specify the supplies that are covered and the additional requirements that apply to payments for those supplies.
