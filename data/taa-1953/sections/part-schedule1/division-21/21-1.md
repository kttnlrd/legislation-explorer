# 21-1  What this Division is about

This Part applies in relation to a payment by APRA under:

<a id="s21-1-a"></a>
**(a)** Division 2AA of Part II of the Banking Act 1959 applying in relation to an account with an ADI; or

<a id="s21-1-b"></a>
**(b)** Part VC of the Insurance Act 1973 applying in relation to a general insurance policy issued by a general insurance company; in a way corresponding to the way this Part would have applied if the payment had been made by the ADI or company in connection with the account or policy.
