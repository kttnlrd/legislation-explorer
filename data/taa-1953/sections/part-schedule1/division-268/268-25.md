# 268-25  Accuracy of estimate irrelevant to liability to pay

You are liable to pay the unpaid amount of the estimate even if:

<a id="s268-25-a"></a>
**(a)** the underlying liability never existed or has been discharged in full; or

<a id="s268-25-b"></a>
**(b)** the unpaid amount of the underlying liability is less than the unpaid amount of the estimate.

**Note:** Section 268-40 revokes the estimate if you give the Commissioner a statutory declaration, or file an affidavit, to the effect that the underlying liability never existed.

**Note:** Subdivision 268-D provides ways in which you can challenge the estimate or its amount.
