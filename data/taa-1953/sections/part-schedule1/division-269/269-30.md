# 269-30  Effect on penalty of directors' obligation ending before end

of notice period

<a id="s269-30-1"></a>
**(1)**  Subject to subsection (2), a penalty of yours under this Division is remitted if the directors of the company stop being under the relevant obligation under section 269-15:

> <a id="s269-30-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  before the Commissioner gives you notice of the penalty under section 269-25; or

> <a id="s269-30-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  within 21 days after the Commissioner gives you notice of the penalty under that section.

> **Note:** An administrator or a small business restructuring practitioner of the company being appointed, or the company beginning to be wound up, after the last day mentioned in column 2 will, to the extent mentioned in column 3, have no effect on the penalty.

> **Note:** The sum mentioned in paragraph 35(1)(e) of the Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992 is the sum of:

> <a id="s269-30-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the total of the company's individual superannuation guarantee shortfalls; and

> <a id="s269-30-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the company's nominal interest component; and

> <a id="s269-30-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the company's administration component; specified in the superannuation guarantee statement.

> **Note:** This subsection will not affect the operation of subsection (1) in respect of penalties that relate to GST instalments.

<a id="s269-30-3"></a>
**(3)**  If you become a director of the company during or after the 3 months mentioned in item 1, 2, 5 or 6, treat the reference in the item to the 3 months as being a reference to the 3 months after the day you become a director of the company.
