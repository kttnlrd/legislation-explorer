# 340-5  Release from particular liabilities in cases of serious hardship

Applying for release

<a id="s340-5-1"></a>
**(1)**  You may apply to the Commissioner to release you, in whole or in part, from a liability of yours if section 340-10 applies to the liability.

<a id="s340-5-2"></a>
**(2)**  The application must be in the *approved form. Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Generic assessment, collection and recovery rules Chapter 4 Release from particular liabilities Part 4-50 Section 340-5

<a id="s340-5-3"></a>
**(3)**  The Commissioner may release you, in whole or in part, from the liability if you are an entity specified in the column headed "Entity" of the following table and the condition specified in the column headed "Condition" of the table is satisfied. Entity and condition Item           Entity                        Condition 1              an individual                 you would suffer serious hardship if you were required to satisfy the liability 2              a trustee of the estate       the dependants of the deceased of a deceased                 individual would suffer serious individual                    hardship if you were required to satisfy the liability Effect of the Commissioner's decision

<a id="s340-5-4"></a>
**(4)**  If the Commissioner:

> <a id="s340-5-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  refuses to release you in whole from the liability; or

> <a id="s340-5-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  releases you in part from the liability; nothing in this section prevents you from making a further application or applications under subsection (1) in relation to the liability. Notification of the Commissioner's decision

<a id="s340-5-5"></a>
**(5)**  The Commissioner must notify you in writing of the Commissioner's decision within 28 days after making the decision.

<a id="s340-5-6"></a>
**(6)**  A failure to comply with subsection (5) does not affect the validity of the Commissioner's decision. Objections against the Commissioner's decision

<a id="s340-5-7"></a>
**(7)**  If you are dissatisfied with the Commissioner's decision, you may object against the decision in the manner set out in Part IVC.
