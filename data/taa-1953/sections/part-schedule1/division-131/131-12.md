# 131-12  Withdrawing or amending your request for a release authority relating to an FHSS determination


<a id="s131-12-1"></a>
**(1)** You may, by notifying the Commissioner in the *approved form, withdraw or amend your valid request made under section 131-5 if:

> <a id="s131-12-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** your request relates to a *first home super saver determination given to you; and

> <a id="s131-12-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** in the case of amending your request—you satisfy paragraphs 138-10(2)(a) and (b); and Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of other taxes Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-20 Section 131-15

> <a id="s131-12-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** the Commissioner has not already issued a release authority in relation to your request.

<a id="s131-12-2"></a>
**(2)** Your amended request is treated as a valid request under section 131-5 if it complies with subsection 131-5(2) and paragraphs 131-5(3)(a) and (c).

<a id="s131-12-3"></a>
**(3)** Withdrawing your request does not prevent you from making a later request under section 131-5 in relation to the *first home super saver determination. Issuing a release authority to superannuation provider
