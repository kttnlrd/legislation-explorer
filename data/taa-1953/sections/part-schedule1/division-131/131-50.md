# 131-50  Notifying Commissioner


<a id="s131-50-1"></a>
**(1)**  A *superannuation provider issued with a release authority under section 131-15 must notify the Commissioner of a payment made in accordance with this Subdivision.

<a id="s131-50-2"></a>
**(2)**  A *superannuation provider that: Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of other taxes Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-20 Section 131-55

> <a id="s131-50-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  has been issued with a release authority under section 131-15; and

> <a id="s131-50-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  is not required to pay an amount under section 131-35, or is required under that section to pay an amount less than the amount stated in the release authority; must notify the Commissioner that the provider is not required to comply with the release authority.

<a id="s131-50-3"></a>
**(3)**  A notice under this section must be given in the *approved form within the period applying under subsection 131-35(1) or 131-40(1) for the release authority.

> **Note:** Subsection 286-75(1) provides for an administrative penalty for failing to comply with this section.
