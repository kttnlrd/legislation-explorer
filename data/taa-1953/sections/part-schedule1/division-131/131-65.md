# 131-65  Entitlement to credits


<a id="s131-65-1"></a>
**(1)**  If a *superannuation provider pays an amount in relation to a release authority issued under section 131-15 in relation to you, you are entitled to a credit equal to that amount.

> **Note:** Division 3 of Part IIB provides for the treatment of credits that you are entitled to under a taxation law.

<a id="s131-65-2"></a>
**(2)**  The credit arises on the day the Commissioner receives the amount. Exception for voluntary payments of Division 293 tax debt account

<a id="s131-65-3"></a>
**(3)**  However, if the amount paid in relation to the release authority relates to an amount of *assessed Division 293 tax that is *deferred to a debt account for a *superannuation interest:

> <a id="s131-65-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  subsection (1) does not apply in relation to the payment; and

> <a id="s131-65-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  treat the payment as if it were a voluntary payment under section 133-70 in relation to that debt account. Exception for voluntary payments of Division 296 tax debt account

<a id="s131-65-4"></a>
**(4)**  Also, if the amount paid in relation to the release authority relates to an amount of *assessed Division 296 tax that is *deferred to a Division 296 debt account for a *superannuation interest:

> <a id="s131-65-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  subsection (1) does not apply in relation to the payment; and Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of other taxes Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-20 Section 131-70

> <a id="s131-65-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  treat the payment as if it were a voluntary payment under section 134-70 in relation to that *Division 296 debt account.
