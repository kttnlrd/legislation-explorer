# 398-5  Reporting to Agriculture Department

FMD provider must provide monthly information

<a id="s398-5-1"></a>
**(1)**  An *FMD provider must, before the 11th day after the end of a calendar month, give in writing to the *Agriculture Secretary the information specified in subsection (3) if the provider holds a *farm management deposit at the end of that month. Penalty: 10 penalty units.

<a id="s398-5-2"></a>
**(2)**  An offence under subsection (1) is an offence of strict liability.

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code. Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Administration Chapter 5 Record-keeping and other obligations relating to taxpayers Part 5-25 Section 398-5 Information required

<a id="s398-5-3"></a>
**(3)**  The information is:

> <a id="s398-5-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the number of *farm management deposits held at the end of that month; and

> <a id="s398-5-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the number of depositors in respect of such deposits at the end of that month; and

> <a id="s398-5-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the sum of the balances of such deposits at the end of that month; and

> <a id="s398-5-3-d"></a>
> **(d)**  any other information, in relation to farm management deposits held by the *FMD provider at any time in that month, that is required by the regulations for the purposes of this section. Regulations not to require identity of depositor

<a id="s398-5-4"></a>
**(4)**  Regulations made for the purposes of paragraph (3)(d) must not require information:

> <a id="s398-5-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  that discloses the identity of a depositor; or

> <a id="s398-5-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  from which the identity of a depositor could reasonably be inferred. Part 5-30—Payment, ABN and identification verification system
