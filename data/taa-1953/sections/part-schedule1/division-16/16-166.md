# 16-166  Payment summary for a departing Australia superannuation

payment Within 14 days after an entity (the payer) makes a *departing Australia superannuation payment, the payer must:

<a id="s16-166-a"></a>
**(a)** give a *payment summary that covers the payment to the recipient of the payment; and

<a id="s16-166-b"></a>
**(b)** give a copy of the summary to the Commissioner.
