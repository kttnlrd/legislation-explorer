# 16-25  Failure to withhold: offence


<a id="s16-25-1"></a>
**(1)** An entity must not fail to withhold an amount as required by Division 12. Penalty: 10 penalty units.

> **Note:** See section 4AA of the Crimes Act 1914 for the current value of a penalty unit.

> **Note:** See sections 16-30, 16-35, 16-40 and 16-43 for an alternative administrative penalty.

<a id="s16-25-2"></a>
**(2)** An entity must not fail to pay to the Commissioner an amount as required by Division 12A or 13 or Subdivision 14-A, 14-B, 14-C or 14-D. Penalty: 10 penalty units.

> **Note:** See section 4AA of the Crimes Act 1914 for the current value of a penalty unit. Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of income tax Chapter 2 Pay as you go (PAYG) withholding Part 2-5 Section 16-30

> **Note:** See sections 16-30, 16-35, 16-40 and 16-43 for an alternative administrative penalty.

<a id="s16-25-3"></a>
**(3)** An offence against subsection (1) or (2) is a strict liability offence.

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code.

<a id="s16-25-4"></a>
**(4)** If a person is convicted of an offence in relation to:

> <a id="s16-25-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** a failure by that person or someone else to withhold an amount as required by Division 12; or

> <a id="s16-25-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** a failure by that person or someone else to pay to the Commissioner an amount as required by Division 12A or 13 or Subdivision 14-A, 14-B, 14-C or 14-D; the court may order the convicted person to pay to the Commissioner an amount up to the *amount required to be withheld. The court may so order in addition to imposing a penalty on the convicted person.
