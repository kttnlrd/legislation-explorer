# 16-195  Payer's right to recover amounts of penalty: certain

withholding taxes

<a id="s16-195-1"></a>
**(1)**  An entity that has paid an amount of penalty under section 16-30, 16-35 or 16-40 for a *withholding payment covered by:

> <a id="s16-195-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  Subdivision 12-F (about a dividend, interest or royalty payment); or

> <a id="s16-195-1-aa"></a>
> **(aa)**  section 12-305 (about a departing Australia superannuation payment); or

> <a id="s16-195-1-ab"></a>
> **(ab)**  Subdivision 12-FC (about labour mobility programs); or

> <a id="s16-195-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  section 12-320 (about a mining payment); or

> <a id="s16-195-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  Subdivision 12-H (about distributions of withholding MIT income); may recover an amount equal to the amount of penalty from the person liable to pay the *withholding tax, or *mining withholding tax, for the withholding payment. Note        Sections 16-30, 16-35 and 16-40 provide for an administrative penalty for failing to comply with Division 12 or 14.

<a id="s16-195-2"></a>
**(2)**  Subsection (3) applies if an entity has paid an amount of penalty under section 12-415 to the Commissioner for a failure to give a notice, or to make details available on a website, as required by section 12-395 in relation to an amount (the relevant amount). <a id="s16-195-3"></a>
**(3)**  The entity may recover from another entity that is liable to pay *managed investment trust withholding tax in relation to an amount attributable to the relevant amount the lesser of:

> <a id="s16-195-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an amount equal to the amount of that tax that the other entity is liable to pay; and

> <a id="s16-195-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the amount of the penalty.
