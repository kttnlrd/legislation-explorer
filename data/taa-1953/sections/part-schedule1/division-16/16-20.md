# 16-20  Payer discharged from liability to recipient for amount

withheld

<a id="s16-20-1"></a>
**(1)** An entity that:

<a id="s16-20-1-a"></a>
**(a)** withholds an amount as required by Division 12; or

<a id="s16-20-1-b"></a>
**(b)** pays to the Commissioner an amount as required by Division 12A, 13 or 14; is discharged from all liability to pay or account for that amount to any entity except the Commissioner.

**Note:** The entity may be required to refund the amount in some circumstances. See Subdivision 18-B.
