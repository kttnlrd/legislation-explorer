# 290-135  Delay in making ruling

If:

> <a id="s290-135-a"></a>
> **(a)** an entity applied in writing to the Commissioner for a *public ruling, *private ruling or *oral ruling in relation to a *scheme; and

> <a id="s290-135-b"></a>
> **(b)** the Commissioner has neither made the ruling nor told the entity in writing that the Commissioner has declined to make the ruling; the Commissioner must not make an application under section 290-125 in relation to conduct or proposed conduct by an entity in relation to the scheme until the Commissioner makes the ruling or tells the entity in writing that the Commissioner has declined to make the ruling.
