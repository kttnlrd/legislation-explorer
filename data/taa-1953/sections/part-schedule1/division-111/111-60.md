# 111-60  Alteration of contracts if cost of complying with agreement is

affected by later alteration to wine tax or luxury car tax laws

<a id="s111-60-1"></a>
**(1)**  If, after a contract involving a *supply, or a *taxable dealing in relation to *wine, has been made, an alteration to the *wine tax law or the *luxury car tax law happens and the alteration directly causes an increase or decrease in the cost to a party to the agreement of complying with the agreement, then the contract is altered as follows: Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of other taxes Chapter 3 Indirect taxes Part 3-10 Section 111-60

> <a id="s111-60-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if the cost is increased—by allowing the party to add the increase to the contract price;

> <a id="s111-60-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if the cost is decreased—by allowing the other party to deduct the decrease from the contract price.

<a id="s111-60-2"></a>
**(2)**  The contract is not altered if:

> <a id="s111-60-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the contract has express written provision to the contrary; or

> <a id="s111-60-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  it is clear from the terms of the contract that the alteration of the *wine tax law or the *luxury car tax law has been taken into account in the agreed contract price.
