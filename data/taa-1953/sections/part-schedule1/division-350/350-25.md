# 350-25  Evidence by affidavit in proceedings to recover tax-related

liabilities In a proceeding to recover an amount of a *tax-related liability:

<a id="s350-25-a"></a>
**(a)** a person may give evidence by affidavit; and

<a id="s350-25-b"></a>
**(b)** the court may require the person to attend before it:

<a id="s350-25-i"></a>
**(i)** to be cross-examined on that evidence; or

<a id="s350-25-ii"></a>
**(ii)** to give other evidence relating to the proceedings. Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Administration Chapter 5 The Australian Taxation Office Part 5-1 Section 352-1
