# 426-200  Limitation on community charity corporations making

certain transfers A *community charity corporation must not provide money, property or benefits to:

> <a id="s426-200-a"></a>
> **(a)** another community charity corporation; or

> <a id="s426-200-b"></a>
> **(b)** an *ancillary fund; or

> <a id="s426-200-c"></a>
> **(c)** a *community charity trust; unless permitted to do so by the *community charity corporation guidelines.
