# 426-175  What this Subdivision is about

This Subdivision deals with philanthropic companies known as community charity corporations. The Minister must make guidelines determining when community charity corporations are entitled to be endorsed as deductible gift recipients. This Subdivision also provides for penalties for failures to comply with the guidelines.
