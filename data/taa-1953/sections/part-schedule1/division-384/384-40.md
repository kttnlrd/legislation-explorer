# 384-40  Taxation objection

If you are dissatisfied with:

> <a id="s384-40-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a decision of the Commissioner to give an *education direction, or to vary one otherwise than in accordance with a request under section 384-35; or

> <a id="s384-40-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a decision of the Commissioner under section 384-35 to refuse to vary an education direction; you may object against the decision in the manner set out in Part IVC.
