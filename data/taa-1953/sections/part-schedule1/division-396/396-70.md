# 396-70  Exemptions—other cases

Exemptions for particular entities

<a id="s396-70-1"></a>
**(1)** The Commissioner may, in writing, notify an entity that it:

> <a id="s396-70-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** is not required to prepare and give reports under section 396-55; or Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Administration Chapter 5 Record-keeping and other obligations relating to taxpayers Part 5-25 Section 396-75

> <a id="s396-70-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** is not required to do so for specified classes of transactions.

<a id="s396-70-2"></a>
**(2)** An entity dissatisfied with a decision to:

> <a id="s396-70-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** give it a notice under subsection (1); or

> <a id="s396-70-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** not give it a notice under subsection (1); may object against the decision in the manner set out in Part IVC.

<a id="s396-70-3"></a>
**(3)** A notice under subsection (1) is not a legislative instrument. General exemptions

<a id="s396-70-4"></a>
**(4)** The Commissioner may, by legislative instrument, determine that specified classes of entities:

> <a id="s396-70-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** are not required to prepare and give reports under section 396-55; or

> <a id="s396-70-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** are not required to do so for specified classes of transactions.
