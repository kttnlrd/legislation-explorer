# 396-120  Application of Common Reporting Standard

Scope of this section

<a id="s396-120-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies:

<a id="s396-120-1-a"></a>
**(a)** for the purposes of section 288-85 (Failure by Reporting Financial Institution to obtain self-certification); and

<a id="s396-120-1-b"></a>
**(b)** for the purposes of this Subdivision:

<a id="s396-120-1-b-i"></a>
**(i)** in determining whether the conditions in subsection 396-105(1) are satisfied; and

<a id="s396-120-1-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** in determining which information the *CRS states a Reporting Financial Institution must report. General reporting requirements

<a id="s396-120-2"></a>
**(2)** Paragraph F of Section I of the *CRS is to be disregarded. Reportable and Participating Jurisdictions

<a id="s396-120-3"></a>
**(3)** All jurisdictions (other than Australia) are to be treated as Reportable Jurisdictions.

<a id="s396-120-4"></a>
**(4)** Without limiting subparagraph D(5) of Section VIII of the *CRS, Australia is to be treated as a Participating Jurisdiction. Accounts

<a id="s396-120-5"></a>
**(5)** Without limiting subparagraph D(1) of Section VIII of the *CRS, an account maintained by a Reporting Financial Institution for an entity is treated as being a Reportable Account (within the meaning of the CRS) if: Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Administration Chapter 5 Record-keeping and other obligations relating to taxpayers Part 5-25 Section 396-125

<a id="s396-120-5-a"></a>
**(a)** the Reporting Financial Institution does not apply the due diligence procedures described in the CRS in relation to the account; and

<a id="s396-120-5-b"></a>
**(b)** the CRS does not state that the account is not required to be identified; and

<a id="s396-120-5-c"></a>
**(c)** the account would be such a Reportable Account if the Reporting Financial Institution applied those procedures.

<a id="s396-120-6"></a>
**(6)** The date provided for in subparagraph C(9) of Section VIII of the *CRS (about Preexisting Accounts) (as affected by paragraph 396-115(5)(b) in this Schedule) is taken to be 30 June 2017.

<a id="s396-120-7"></a>
**(7)** A reference in the *CRS to a New Account is treated as being a reference to a Financial Account maintained by a Reporting Financial Institution that is not a Preexisting Account. Dollar amounts

<a id="s396-120-8"></a>
**(8)** An entity may choose to treat all dollar amounts in the *CRS as being in Australian dollars.

**Note:** Otherwise, all dollar amounts are in United States dollars: see subparagraph C(4) of Section VII of the CRS.
