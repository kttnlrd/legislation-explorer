# 263-5  What this Subdivision is about

This Subdivision can be activated if there is in force an agreement between Australia and a foreign country or territory that contains an article relating to assistance in collection of foreign tax debts. The Commissioner can collect from an entity an amount in respect of a tax debt that the person owes to such a country or territory or take action to conserve assets of the entity. The Commissioner is required to remit amounts collected to the foreign country or territory concerned.
