# 263-30  When amount is due and payable


<a id="s263-30-1"></a>
**(1)** When particulars of a *foreign revenue claim are entered in the Register, the amount owed by the debtor becomes a pecuniary liability to the Commonwealth by the debtor.

> **Note:** The amount to be recovered from the debtor will be a primary tax debt for the purposes of Part IIB and the Commissioner may allocate the debt to a running balance account under that Part.

> **Note:** For provisions about collection and recovery of the debt, see Part 4-15.

<a id="s263-30-1A"></a>
**(1A)**  To avoid doubt, the amount owed by the debtor may not be the same as the amount (if any) entered in the Register.

<a id="s263-30-2"></a>
**(2)** The amount owed by the debtor becomes due and payable 30 days after notice of the particulars of the *foreign revenue claim is given to the debtor or on a later day specified in the notice.

<a id="s263-30-3"></a>
**(3)** If that amount remains unpaid after it is due and payable, the debtor is liable to pay *general interest charge on the unpaid amount for each day in the period that: Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Generic assessment, collection and recovery rules Chapter 4 Collection and recovery of tax-related liabilities and other amounts Part 4-15 Section 263-35

> <a id="s263-30-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** started at the beginning of the day by which the amount was due to be paid; and

> <a id="s263-30-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** finishes at the end of the last day at the end of which either of the following remains unpaid:

> > <a id="s263-30-3-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)** the amount;

> > <a id="s263-30-3-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** general interest charge on any of the amount.
