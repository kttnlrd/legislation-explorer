# 255-110  Offence

You commit an offence if:

<a id="s255-110-a"></a>
**(a)** the Commissioner requires you to give security under section 255-100; and

<a id="s255-110-b"></a>
**(b)** you fail to give that security as required. Penalty: 100 penalty units.
