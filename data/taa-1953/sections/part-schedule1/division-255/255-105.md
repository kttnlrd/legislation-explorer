# 255-105  Notice of requirement to give security

Commissioner must give notice of requirement to give security

<a id="s255-105-1"></a>
**(1)**  If the Commissioner requires you to give security under section 255-100, he or she must give you written notice of the requirement. Content of notice

<a id="s255-105-2"></a>
**(2)**  The notice must:

> <a id="s255-105-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  state that you are required to give the security to the Commissioner; and

> <a id="s255-105-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  explain why the Commissioner requires the security; and

> <a id="s255-105-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  set out the amount of the security; and

> <a id="s255-105-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  describe the means by which you are required to give the security under subsection 255-100(2); and Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Generic assessment, collection and recovery rules Chapter 4 Collection and recovery of tax-related liabilities and other amounts Part 4-15 Section 255-110

> <a id="s255-105-2-e"></a>
> **(e)**  specify the time by which you are required to give the security; and

> <a id="s255-105-2-f"></a>
> **(f)**  explain how you may have the Commissioner’s decision to require you to give the security reviewed.

<a id="s255-105-3"></a>
**(3)**  To avoid doubt, a single notice may relate to security for the payment of 2 or more existing or future *tax-related liabilities, but must comply with subsection (2) in relation to each of them. When notice is given

<a id="s255-105-4"></a>
**(4)**  Despite section 29 of the Acts Interpretation Act 1901, a notice under subsection (1) is taken to be given at the time the Commissioner leaves or posts it.

> **Note:** Section 28A of the Acts Interpretation Act 1901 may be relevant to giving a notice under subsection (1). Miscellaneous

<a id="s255-105-5"></a>
**(5)**  A failure to comply with this section does not affect the validity of the requirement to give the security under section 255-100.
