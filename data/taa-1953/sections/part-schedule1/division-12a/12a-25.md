# 12A-25  Meaning of AMIT DIR payment

An AMIT DIR payment means any of the following:

<a id="s12A-25-a"></a>
**(a)** an *AMIT dividend payment;

<a id="s12A-25-b"></a>
**(b)** an *AMIT interest payment;

<a id="s12A-25-c"></a>
**(c)** an *AMIT royalty payment.
