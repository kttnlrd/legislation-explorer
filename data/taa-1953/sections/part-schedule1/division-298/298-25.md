# 298-25  General interest charge on unpaid penalty

If any of the penalty remains unpaid after it is due, the entity is liable to pay the *general interest charge on the unpaid amount of the penalty for each day in the period that: Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Generic assessment, collection and recovery rules Chapter 4 Charges and penalties Part 4-25 Section 298-30

> <a id="s298-25-a"></a>
> **(a)** started at the beginning of the day by which the amount was due to be paid; and

> <a id="s298-25-b"></a>
> **(b)** finishes at the end of the last day, at the end of which, any of the following remains unpaid:

> > <a id="s298-25-i"></a>
> > **(i)** the amount;

> > <a id="s298-25-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** general interest charge on any of the amount.

> **Note:** The general interest charge is worked out under Part IIA.
