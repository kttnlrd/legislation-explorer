# 298-100  Criminal proceedings after civil proceedings

Criminal proceedings may be started against an entity for conduct that is substantially the same as conduct in relation to which a civil Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Generic assessment, collection and recovery rules Chapter 4 Charges and penalties Part 4-25 Section 298-105 penalty order under the civil penalty provisions could be made regardless of whether a civil penalty order has been made against the entity.
