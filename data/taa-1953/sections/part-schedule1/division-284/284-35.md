# 284-35  Application of Division to partnerships


<a id="s284-35-1"></a>
**(1)** If you are a partner in a partnership and:

<a id="s284-35-1-a"></a>
**(a)** a statement about the partnership net income or partnership loss is made by a partner or the partnership's agent to the Commissioner or to an entity who is exercising powers or performing functions under a *taxation law about the partnership; and

<a id="s284-35-1-b"></a>
**(b)** the statement:

<a id="s284-35-1-b-i"></a>
**(i)** is false or misleading in a material particular, whether because of things in it or omitted from it; or

<a id="s284-35-1-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** treated an *income tax law as applying to a matter or identical matters in a particular way that was not *reasonably arguable; this Division applies to you as if you had made the statement.

<a id="s284-35-2"></a>
**(2)** If you are a partner in a partnership and:

<a id="s284-35-2-a"></a>
**(a)** the partnership participated in a *scheme; and

<a id="s284-35-2-b"></a>
**(b)** the partnership net income would have been greater, or the partnership loss would have been smaller, apart from the scheme; this Division applies to you as if the proportion of the *scheme benefit that is the same as your share of the partnership net income or partnership loss were your scheme benefit.
