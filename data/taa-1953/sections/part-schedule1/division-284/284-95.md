# 284-95  Joint and several liability of directors of corporate trustee

that makes a false or misleading statement

<a id="s284-95-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies if a trustee of a *self managed superannuation fund, or of a fund that is treated as a self managed superannuation fund under subsection 10(4) of the Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act 1993:

<a id="s284-95-1-a"></a>
**(a)** is liable to an administrative penalty under subsection 284-75(1) or (4); and

<a id="s284-95-1-b"></a>
**(b)** is a body corporate. Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Generic assessment, collection and recovery rules Chapter 4 Charges and penalties Part 4-25 Section 284-140

<a id="s284-95-2"></a>
**(2)** The directors of the body corporate at the time it becomes liable to the penalty are jointly and severally liable to pay the amount of the *tax-related liability in respect of the penalty.

**Note:** See section 265-45 for rules on joint liability. Subdivision 284-C—Penalties relating to schemes Guide to Subdivision 284-C
