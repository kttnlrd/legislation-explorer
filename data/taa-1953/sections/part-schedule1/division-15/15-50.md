# 15-50  Declarations

Declarations about matters

<a id="s15-50-1"></a>
**(1)**  An individual who:

> <a id="s15-50-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  expects to receive a *withholding payment covered by Subdivision 12-B, 12-C or 12-D, or an *alienated personal services payment to which Division 13 applies, from an entity; and

> <a id="s15-50-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  wishes to have a matter relating to the individual’s income tax or other liability referred to in paragraph 11-1(b), (ca), (caa), (cb), (cc), (cd), (da) or (db) taken into account by the entity in working out the *amount required to be withheld from the payment; may give the entity a declaration about the matter in the *approved form. When declarations under subsection (1) can’t be given

<a id="s15-50-2"></a>
**(2)**  The individual cannot give a declaration under subsection (1) unless:

> <a id="s15-50-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a *TFN declaration is in effect between the individual and the entity, or a *voluntary agreement covers the payment; and

> <a id="s15-50-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if the individual has given another entity a declaration on any matter—that declaration is not in effect. Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of income tax Chapter 2 Pay as you go (PAYG) withholding Part 2-5 Section 15-50 Declarations changing information given in TFN declaration

<a id="s15-50-3"></a>
**(3)**  If:

> <a id="s15-50-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an individual has given a *TFN declaration to an entity; and

> <a id="s15-50-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the individual made a statement about a matter in the TFN declaration; and

> <a id="s15-50-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the individual’s circumstances change in relation to the matter; the individual may give the entity a declaration about the matter in the *approved form. Regulations

<a id="s15-50-4"></a>
**(4)**  The regulations may prescribe:

> <a id="s15-50-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  when a declaration under subsection (1) or (3) starts or ceases to be in effect; and

> <a id="s15-50-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  when a declaration under subsection (1) or (3) is taken to have been given.

<a id="s15-50-5"></a>
**(5)**  If:

> <a id="s15-50-5-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an individual gives an entity a declaration under subsection (1) or (3) about a matter; and

> <a id="s15-50-5-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the individual’s circumstances change in relation to the matter; the regulations may also prescribe when the individual must give the entity a new declaration about the matter.
