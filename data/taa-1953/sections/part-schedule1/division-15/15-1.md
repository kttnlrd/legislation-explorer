# 15-1  What this Division is about

This Division is mainly about how to work out how much an entity must withhold under Division 12. In most cases, the entity will need to use either the Commissioner’s withholding schedules or the regulations. The entity will also need to take into account a TFN declaration or declaration under section 15-50 it has been given because, under the schedules and regulations, the declaration may affect how to calculate the amount to withhold. This Division also deals with when an individual can make such a declaration (other than a TFN declaration) so as to change the amount that must be withheld from payments to the individual.
