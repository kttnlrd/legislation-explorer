# 12-330  Payer must ask Commissioner how much to withhold


<a id="s12-330-1"></a>
**(1)** An entity must not intentionally make a payment from which section 12-325 requires it to withhold an amount, unless:

> <a id="s12-330-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the entity has notified the Commissioner in writing of the amount of the proposed payment; and

> <a id="s12-330-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the Commissioner has later notified the entity in writing of the amount (if any) that the entity must withhold from the payment in respect of tax or *petroleum resource rent tax that is or may become payable by a foreign resident to whom the payment is made; or the payment is covered by a certificate in force under section 12-335. Penalty: 20 penalty units.

> **Note:** See section 4AA of the Crimes Act 1914 for the current value of a penalty unit. Failure to notify not an offence against section 8C

<a id="s12-330-2"></a>
**(2)** An entity that fails to notify the Commissioner as required by subsection (1) does not commit an offence against section 8C.
