# 12-390  Withholding by custodians and other entities

Withholding by custodians

<a id="s12-390-1"></a>
**(1)**  A *custodian must withhold an amount from a payment (the later payment) it makes if:

> <a id="s12-390-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  all or some of the later payment (the covered part) is reasonably attributable to the part of an earlier payment received by the custodian that was covered by a notice or information under section 12-395; and

> <a id="s12-390-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the later payment is made to an entity covered by section 12-410.

> **Note:** The covered part referred to in paragraph (1)(a) is attributable to a fund payment made by a withholding MIT, or 2 or more fund payments made by one or more withholding MITs. One or more of those withholding MITs may be AMITs.

> **Note:** An entity may be covered by section 12-410 if the entity has an address outside Australia or payment is authorised to be made to a place outside Australia.

> **Note:** If the payment is made to a recipient not covered by section 12-410, the custodian is required to give a notice to the recipient or publish Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of income tax Chapter 2 Pay as you go (PAYG) withholding Part 2-5 Section 12-390 information on a website setting out certain details about the payment: see section 12-395.

**(2)**  The amount the *custodian must withhold is: Covered part ´ Rate applicable under subsection (3)

<a id="s12-390-3"></a>
**(3)**  The rate is:

> <a id="s12-390-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if the address or place for payment of the recipient is in an *information exchange country:

> > <a id="s12-390-3-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  15% for *fund payments (except to the extent mentioned in subparagraph (ii) or (iii)); or

> > <a id="s12-390-3-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  10% for fund payments, to the extent that they are, or are attributable to, fund payments from a *clean building managed investment trust (except to the extent mentioned in subparagraph (iii)); or

> > <a id="s12-390-3-a-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  30% for fund payments, to the extent that they are attributable to *non-concessional MIT income (see section 12-435); or

> <a id="s12-390-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  otherwise—30%. Withholding by other entities

<a id="s12-390-4"></a>
**(4)**  An entity that is not a *withholding MIT or a *custodian must withhold an amount from a payment it receives if:

> <a id="s12-390-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the payment or part of it (the covered part) was covered by a notice or information under section 12-395; and

> <a id="s12-390-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  a foreign resident (the recipient) is or becomes entitled:

> > <a id="s12-390-4-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  to receive from the entity; or

> > <a id="s12-390-4-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  to have the entity credit to the recipient, or otherwise deal with on the recipient's behalf or as the recipient directs; an amount (the attributable amount) reasonably attributable to the covered part.

> **Note:** The covered part referred to in paragraph (4)(a) is attributable to a fund payment made by a withholding MIT, or 2 or more fund payments made by one or more withholding MITs. One or more of those withholding MITs may be AMITs.

> **Note:** If the recipient is not a foreign resident, the entity is required to give a notice to the recipient or publish information on a website setting out certain details about the payment: see section 12-395.

<a id="s12-390-5"></a>
**(5)**  The amount the entity must withhold is: Attributable amount ´ Rate applicable under subsection (6)

<a id="s12-390-6"></a>
**(6)**  The rate is:

> <a id="s12-390-6-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if the recipient is a resident of an *information exchange country:

> > <a id="s12-390-6-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  15% for *fund payments (except to the extent mentioned in subparagraph (ii) or (iii)); or

> > <a id="s12-390-6-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  10% for fund payments, to the extent that they are, or are attributable to, fund payments from a *clean building managed investment trust (except to the extent mentioned in subparagraph (iii)); or

> > <a id="s12-390-6-a-iii"></a>
> > **(iii)**  30% for fund payments, to the extent that they are attributable to *non-concessional MIT income (see section 12-435); or

> <a id="s12-390-6-b"></a>
> **(b)**  otherwise—30%.

<a id="s12-390-7"></a>
**(7)**  An entity is a resident of an *information exchange country if:

> <a id="s12-390-7-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity is a resident of that country for the purposes of the taxation laws of that country; or

> <a id="s12-390-7-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if there are no taxation laws of that country applicable to the entity or the entity's residency status cannot be determined under those laws:

> > <a id="s12-390-7-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  for an individual—the individual is ordinarily resident in that country; or

> > <a id="s12-390-7-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  for another entity—the entity is incorporated or formed in that country and is carrying on a business in that country.

<a id="s12-390-8"></a>
**(8)**  An amount required to be withheld under subsection (4) must be withheld:

> <a id="s12-390-8-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if the recipient is so entitled when the entity receives the payment—immediately after receipt; or Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of income tax Chapter 2 Pay as you go (PAYG) withholding Part 2-5 Section 12-395

> <a id="s12-390-8-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if the recipient becomes so entitled at a later time— immediately after the later time. Meaning of custodian

<a id="s12-390-9"></a>
**(9)**  An entity is a custodian if:

> <a id="s12-390-9-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity is *carrying on a *business that consists predominantly of providing a custodial or depository service (within the meaning of the Corporations Act 2001) pursuant to an *Australian financial services licence; or

> <a id="s12-390-9-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the entity is acting on behalf of an entity that is carrying on such a business pursuant to such a licence. Exceptions

<a id="s12-390-10"></a>
**(10)**  This section does not apply:

> <a id="s12-390-10-a"></a>
> **(a)**  to a company unless the company would, apart from section 12-420, be acting in the capacity as *agent for the recipient; or

> <a id="s12-390-10-b"></a>
> **(b)**  to an amount paid or received by an entity to the extent that no *managed investment trust withholding tax is payable in respect of the amount or an amount reasonably attributable to the amount.
