# 12-325  Natural resource payment


<a id="s12-325-1"></a>
**(1)** An entity must withhold an amount from a payment it makes to a foreign resident, or to 2 or more entities at least one of which is a foreign resident, if the payment is worked out wholly or partly by reference to the value or quantity of *natural resources produced or recovered in Australia.

<a id="s12-325-2"></a>
**(2)** The amount to be withheld is:

<a id="s12-325-2-a"></a>
**(a)** the amount notified by the Commissioner under section 12-330; or

<a id="s12-325-2-b"></a>
**(b)** the amount worked out under a certificate in force under section 12-335 that covers the payment; as appropriate. Exception

<a id="s12-325-3"></a>
**(3)** Subsection (1) does not apply if:

<a id="s12-325-3-a"></a>
**(a)** the Commissioner has notified the entity under section 12-330 that the entity does not need to withhold an amount from the payment; or Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of income tax Chapter 2 Pay as you go (PAYG) withholding Part 2-5 Section 12-330

<a id="s12-325-3-b"></a>
**(b)** a certificate in force under section 12-335 covers the payment and does not require the entity to withhold an amount from it.
