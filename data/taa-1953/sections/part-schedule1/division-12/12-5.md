# 12-5  What to do if more than one provision requires a withholding


<a id="s12-5-1"></a>
**(1)**  If more than one provision in this Division covers a payment, only one amount is to be withheld from the payment.

<a id="s12-5-2"></a>
**(2)**  The provision to apply is the one that is most specific to the circumstances of the payment. **(3)**  Apply a provision in this Division (apart from a provision in Subdivision 12-FB) that covers a payment in priority to a provision in Subdivision 12-FB that also covers the payment.

> **Note:** Some provisions of this Division clearly do not cover a payment covered by some other provisions. For example: •   Section 12-55 (about voluntary agreements) covers a payment only if no other provision requires the payer to withhold an amount from the payment.
