# 12-180  Beneficiary becomes presently entitled to income of closely

held trust Scope

<a id="s12-180-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies if:

> <a id="s12-180-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  at the end of an income year of a trust, a beneficiary of the trust is presently entitled, for the purposes of Division 6 of Part III of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936, to a share of the income of the trust of that year; and

> <a id="s12-180-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  paragraph 12-175(1)(c) in this Schedule applies to the trustee of the trust; and

> <a id="s12-180-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  paragraph 12-175(1)(d) applies to the beneficiary. Trustee must withhold

<a id="s12-180-2"></a>
**(2)**  The trustee must withhold an amount from that share of the *net income of the trust, if:

> <a id="s12-180-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the beneficiary did not *quote the beneficiary's *tax file number to the trustee before the end of the year; and Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of income tax Chapter 2 Pay as you go (PAYG) withholding Part 2-5 Section 12-185

> <a id="s12-180-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the trustee is not liable to pay tax in respect of that share under section 98 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936; and

> <a id="s12-180-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the trustee is not required to make a correct TB statement about that share under Division 6D of Part III of that Act (about trustee beneficiary non-disclosure tax); and

> <a id="s12-180-2-d"></a>
> **(d)**  family trust distribution tax is not payable on that share of the income of the trust under Schedule 2F to that Act.

> **Note:** If the trust is a unit trust, the trustee may be required to withhold under section 12-145 in priority to this section: see section 12-5.

> **Note:** The trustee commits an offence if the trustee fails to withhold an amount as required by this section: see section 16-25. Application of rest of Part

<a id="s12-180-3"></a>
**(3)**  This Part (other than section 12-175) applies as if the trustee had paid that share of the *net income of the trust to the beneficiary at the end of the income year. Entitlements already paid

<a id="s12-180-4"></a>
**(4)**  Subsections (2) and (3) do not apply to that share of the *net income of the trust to the extent that the trustee distributed any of that share to the beneficiary during the income year.

> **Note:** The trustee may have been required to withhold from that distribution under section 12-175. Trusts that end during the year

<a id="s12-180-5"></a>
**(5)**  This section applies as if each reference to the end of an income year were a reference to the time occurring just before the trust ends, if the trust ends during the income year.
