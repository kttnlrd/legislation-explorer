# 12-120  Compensation, sickness or accident payment

An entity must withhold an amount from a payment of compensation, or of sickness or accident pay, it makes to an individual if the payment: Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of income tax Chapter 2 Pay as you go (PAYG) withholding Part 2-5 Section 12-120

<a id="s12-120-a"></a>
**(a)** is made because of that or another individual's incapacity for work; and

<a id="s12-120-b"></a>
**(b)** is calculated at a periodical rate; and

<a id="s12-120-c"></a>
**(c)** is not a payment made under an insurance policy to the policy owner. For exceptions, see section 12-1.
