# 12-435  Meaning of non-concessional MIT income

Non-concessional MIT income means any of the following:

> <a id="s12-435-a"></a>
> **(a)** *MIT cross staple arrangement income;

> <a id="s12-435-b"></a>
> **(b)** *MIT trading trust income;

> <a id="s12-435-c"></a>
> **(c)** *MIT agricultural income;

> <a id="s12-435-d"></a>
