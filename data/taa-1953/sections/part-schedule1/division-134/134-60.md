# 134-60  Division 296 debt account to be kept for deferred

Division 296 tax Accounts to be kept

<a id="s134-60-1"></a>
**(1)**  The Commissioner is to keep a debt account (a Division 296 debt account) for *Division 296 tax for you for a *superannuation interest, if an amount of your *assessed Division 296 tax is *deferred to a Division 296 debt account for the superannuation interest. Account to be debited for Division 296 tax

<a id="s134-60-2"></a>
**(2)**  The Commissioner must debit the *Division 296 debt account for the amount of *assessed Division 296 tax that is *deferred to a Division 296 debt account for the *superannuation interest.
