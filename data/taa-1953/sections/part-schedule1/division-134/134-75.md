# 134-75  Commissioner must notify superannuation provider of Division 296 debt account

If the Commissioner starts to keep a *Division 296 debt account for you for a *superannuation interest, the Commissioner must give the *superannuation provider in relation to the superannuation interest a notice saying so. Subdivision 134-C—Compulsory payment Guide to Subdivision 134-C
