# 134-15  Attribution of Division 296 tax to defined benefit interest


<a id="s134-15-1"></a>
**(1)** For the purposes of section 134-10, the amount of your *assessed Division 296 tax for an income year attributable to a *superannuation interest that is or includes a *defined benefit interest is to be worked out using the following formula: Your *relevant superannuation earnings for the *Division 296 tax           year for the superannuation interest ´ for the year                Your *total superannuation earnings for the year

<a id="s134-15-2"></a>
**(2)** However, no part of the *assessed Division 296 tax for the year is attributable to the *superannuation interest if:

<a id="s134-15-2-a"></a>
**(a)** your *relevant superannuation earnings for the year for the superannuation interest is nil; or

<a id="s134-15-2-b"></a>
**(b)** the superannuation interest is in a *superannuation plan that is a *foreign superannuation fund for the income year; or

<a id="s134-15-2-c"></a>
**(c)** the superannuation interest is a *Division 296 excluded interest in relation to the year.
