# 133-75  Commissioner must notify superannuation provider of debt account

If the Commissioner starts to keep a debt account for *Division 293 tax for you for a *superannuation interest, the Commissioner must give the *superannuation provider in relation to the superannuation interest a notice saying so. Subdivision 133-C—Compulsory payment Guide to Subdivision 133-C
