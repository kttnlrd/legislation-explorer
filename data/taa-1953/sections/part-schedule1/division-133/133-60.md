# 133-60  Debt account to be kept for deferred tax

Accounts to be kept

<a id="s133-60-1"></a>
**(1)**  The Commissioner is to keep a debt account for *Division 293 tax for you for a *superannuation interest, if an amount of your *assessed Division 293 tax is *deferred to a debt account for the superannuation interest. Account to be debited for Division 293 tax

<a id="s133-60-2"></a>
**(2)**  The Commissioner must debit the debt account for the amount of *assessed Division 293 tax that is *deferred to a debt account for the *superannuation interest.
