# 135-95  Defined benefit interests—releasing amounts to pay debt

account discharge liability The exclusion of *defined benefit interests from subsection 135-75(4) and paragraph 135-85(c) is to be disregarded for a release authority issued under item 3 of the table in subsection 135-10(1) (about debt account discharge liability).
