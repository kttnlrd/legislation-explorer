# 135-70  What this Subdivision is about

This Subdivision sets out a general requirement for a superannuation provider to comply with a release authority. The Subdivision also includes provisions about how much must be paid, who it must be paid to, which interest it is to be paid from, and how the payments are treated by the Commissioner.
