# 135-10  Release authorities


<a id="s135-10-1"></a>
**(1)**  If the condition mentioned in column 1 of an item in the following table is satisfied:

> <a id="s135-10-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the Commissioner must issue you with a release authority under that item; and

> <a id="s135-10-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you have a release entitlement:

> > <a id="s135-10-1-b-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  equal to the amount mentioned in column 2 of that item; and

> > <a id="s135-10-1-b-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  arising at the time mentioned in column 3 of that item.

<a id="s135-10-2"></a>
**(2)**  A release authority must:

> <a id="s135-10-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  state the amount of the *release entitlement in respect of which it is given; and Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of other taxes Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-20 Section 135-35

> **(b)**  be dated; and

> <a id="s135-10-2-c"></a>
> **(c)**  contain any other information that the Commissioner considers relevant. Commissioner may issue a further release authority

<a id="s135-10-3"></a>
**(3)**  The Commissioner may at any time issue you with a further release authority in respect of a *release entitlement if:

> <a id="s135-10-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the Commissioner is satisfied that it is reasonable in the circumstances to do so; and

> <a id="s135-10-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the Commissioner has issued you with an earlier release authority in respect of that release entitlement. Despite paragraph (2)(a), the further release authority must state the amount the Commissioner considers reasonable in the circumstances, but not exceeding the amount of the release entitlement.
