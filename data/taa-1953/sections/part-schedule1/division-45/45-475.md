# 45-475  Working out your notional tax

Notional tax if no withholding income

<a id="s45-475-1"></a>
**(1)**  Your notional tax is your *adjusted tax (worked out under section 45-340) on your *adjusted taxable income (worked out under section 45-480) for the *base year. Notional tax if trust has withholding income

<a id="s45-475-2"></a>
**(2)**  However, your notional tax (as worked out under subsection (1)) is reduced if the trust’s assessable income for the *base assessment includes amounts in respect of *withholding payments (except *non-quotation withholding payments).

<a id="s45-475-3"></a>
**(3)**  It is reduced (but not below nil) by your *adjusted tax (worked out under section 45-340) on your *adjusted withholding income (worked out under section 45-485) for the *base year. Commissioner may take into account actual and proposed changes to the law

<a id="s45-475-4"></a>
**(4)**  Subsections 45-325(4) and (5) apply for the purposes of working out your *notional tax under this section.
