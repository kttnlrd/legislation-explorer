# 45-370  Working out your adjusted assessed taxable income for the variation year


<a id="s45-370-1"></a>
**(1)** Your adjusted assessed taxable income for the variation year is your taxable income for the year, reduced by any *net capital gain included in your assessable income for the year. Exception: superannuation entities and net capital gains

<a id="s45-370-2"></a>
**(2)** In working out the adjusted assessed taxable income, taxable income is not reduced by any *net capital gain in the case of:

> <a id="s45-370-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** a *complying approved deposit fund or a *non-complying approved deposit fund for the variation year; or

> <a id="s45-370-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** a *complying superannuation fund or a *non-complying superannuation fund for the variation year; or

> <a id="s45-370-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** a *pooled superannuation trust for the variation year. <a id="s45-370-3"></a>
**(3)** The adjusted assessed taxable income of a *life insurance company for the variation year is worked out as follows: Method statement Step 1.       Recalculate the *ordinary class of the taxable income for the variation year on the basis that the assessable income that relates to the class did not include any *net capital gain. Step 2.       Add to the step 1 result the *complying superannuation class of the taxable income for the variation year.
