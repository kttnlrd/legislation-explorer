# 45-233  Reduction in GIC liability under section 45-232 if shortfall is made up in later instalment


<a id="s45-233-1"></a>
**(1)** This section reduces the amount (the shortfall) on which you are liable to pay the *general interest charge under subsection 45-232(2) if, for a later *instalment quarter (the later quarter) that is in the same income year as the variation quarter, the amount worked out as follows is a negative amount: *Acceptable amount of your instalment for - Actual amount of your instalment the later quarter          for the later quarter That amount (expressed as a positive number) is called the top up.

<a id="s45-233-2"></a>
**(2)** For the purposes of the formula in subsection (1): actual amount of your instalment for the later quarter means:

> <a id="s45-233-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** the amount of your instalment for the later quarter, as worked out under section 45-112; or

> <a id="s45-233-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** if you claimed a credit under section 45-420 for the later quarter—the amount of the credit, expressed as a negative amount. Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of income tax Chapter 2 Pay as you go (PAYG) instalments Part 2-10 Section 45-235 Amount of the reduction

<a id="s45-233-3"></a>
**(3)** The shortfall is reduced by applying so much of the top up as does not exceed the shortfall.

<a id="s45-233-4"></a>
**(4)** However, if some of the top up has already been applied (under any other application or applications of this section) to reduce the amount on which you are liable to pay the *general interest charge under subsection 45-232(2) as it applies to a different *instalment quarter, the shortfall is reduced by applying so much of the top up as has not already been applied, and does not exceed the shortfall. Period for which reduction has effect

<a id="s45-233-5"></a>
**(5)** The reduction has effect for each day in the period that:

> <a id="s45-233-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** started at the beginning of the day by which the instalment for the later quarter was due to be paid; and

> <a id="s45-233-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** finishes at the end of the day on which your assessed tax for the income year is due to be paid.
