# 45-480  Working out your adjusted taxable income


<a id="s45-480-1"></a>
**(1)** Your adjusted taxable income for the *base year is worked out using the formula: Adjusted net income ´        Relevant share of the trust      Reduced net income of the trust

<a id="s45-480-2"></a>
**(2)** For the purposes of the formula in subsection (1): adjusted net income of the trust means the net income of the trust, as worked out for the purposes of the *base assessment and:

> <a id="s45-480-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** reduced by any *net capital gain included in the trust's assessable income as so worked out; and

> <a id="s45-480-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** increased by any deductions for *tax losses that were made in so working out that net income; and

> <a id="s45-480-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** reduced by the amount of any tax loss, to the extent that it is *unutilised at the end of the *base year. reduced net income of the trust means the net income of the trust, as worked out for the purposes of the *base assessment and reduced by any *net capital gain included in the trust's assessable income as so worked out. relevant share means the *reduced beneficiary's share, or the *reduced no beneficiary's share, as appropriate, of the net income of the trust, as worked out for the purposes of the *base assessment.
