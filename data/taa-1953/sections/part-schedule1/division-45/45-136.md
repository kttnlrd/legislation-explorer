# 45-136  Monthly payer


<a id="s45-136-1"></a>
**(1)** You are a monthly payer at a time if:

> <a id="s45-136-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** you were a monthly payer immediately before that time; or

> <a id="s45-136-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** if paragraph (a) does not apply—you satisfy the requirement in subsection 45-138(1) for the income year in which that time occurs.

> **Note:** If paragraph (b) applies, see subsection (3) for the time at which you become a monthly payer. Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of income tax Chapter 2 Pay as you go (PAYG) instalments Part 2-10 Section 45-138

<a id="s45-136-2"></a>
**(2)** The starting instalment month in an income year (the current year) is:

> <a id="s45-136-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** if the Commissioner gives you an instalment rate for the first time during an *instalment month in the current year—the next instalment month in the current year; or

> <a id="s45-136-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** if the Commissioner has given you an instalment rate during a previous income year and your instalment rate has not been withdrawn—the first instalment month in the current year. How and when you become such a payer

<a id="s45-136-3"></a>
**(3)** Despite subsection (1), if paragraph (1)(b) applies, you become a *monthly payer just before the end of the *starting instalment month in the income year. How and when you stop being such a payer

<a id="s45-136-4"></a>
**(4)** Despite subsection (1), you stop being a *monthly payer at the start of the first *instalment month in a later income year if:

> <a id="s45-136-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** you do not satisfy the requirement in subsection 45-138(1) for that later income year; and

> <a id="s45-136-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** you give the Commissioner a notice (the MP stop notice) in the *approved form for that later income year before the start of that later income year.
