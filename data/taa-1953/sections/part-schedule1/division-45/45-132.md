# 45-132  Quarterly payer who pays 4 instalments annually on the basis of GDP-adjusted notional tax


<a id="s45-132-1"></a>
**(1)** You are a quarterly payer who pays 4 instalments annually on the basis of GDP-adjusted notional tax if, at the end of the *starting instalment quarter in an income year:

<a id="s45-132-1-a"></a>
**(a)** you satisfy the conditions to be a *quarterly payer who pays on the basis of GDP-adjusted notional tax under section 45-130; and

<a id="s45-132-1-b"></a>
**(b)** you do not satisfy the conditions to be a *quarterly payer who pays 2 instalments annually on the basis of GDP-adjusted notional tax under section 45-134. How and when you become such a payer

<a id="s45-132-2"></a>
**(2)** You become such a payer just before the end of the *starting instalment quarter if paragraphs (1)(a) and (b) are satisfied. How and when you stop being such a payer

<a id="s45-132-3"></a>
**(3)** You stop being a *quarterly payer who pays 4 instalments annually on the basis of GDP-adjusted notional tax at the start of the first *instalment quarter in the next income year if you fail to satisfy paragraphs (1)(a) and (b) at the end of that quarter. Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of income tax Chapter 2 Pay as you go (PAYG) instalments Part 2-10 Section 45-134

<a id="s45-132-4"></a>
**(4)** In addition, you stop being such a payer at the start of the first *instalment quarter in the next income year if:

<a id="s45-132-4-a"></a>
**(a)** at the end of that quarter, you become:

<a id="s45-132-4-a-i"></a>
**(i)** a *quarterly payer who pays on the basis of instalment income; or

<a id="s45-132-4-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** an *annual payer; or

<a id="s45-132-4-b"></a>
**(b)** at the end of the first *instalment month of that quarter, you become a *monthly payer.
