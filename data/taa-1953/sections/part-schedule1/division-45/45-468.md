# 45-468  Multi-rate trustee may pay quarterly instalments

Subdivision 45-D (about quarterly payers) applies to you in the same way as it applies to an individual.

> **Note:** This means that a multi-rate trustee may pay instalments on the basis of GDP-adjusted notional tax if the trustee otherwise satisfies the relevant test that applies to an individual. How Commissioner works out instalment rate and notional tax for a multi-rate trustee
