# 45-15  Liability for instalments


<a id="s45-15-1"></a>
**(1)** The Commissioner may give you an instalment rate from time to time, by giving you written notice of the rate.

<a id="s45-15-2"></a>
**(2)** You are liable to pay instalments under this Division if the Commissioner has given you an instalment rate.

> **Note:** The instalment rate that the Commissioner gives you is worked out under section 45-320 or 45-775.

> **Note:** If your assessable income has always consisted wholly of withholding payments (other than non-quotation withholding payments), the Commissioner will not give you an instalment rate.

> **Note:** Work out the amount of your instalments under Subdivision 45-C.

> **Note:** If the Commissioner withdraws the rate under section 45-90, you are not liable to pay further instalments.

> **Note:** For provisions about collection and recovery of amounts you are liable to pay under this Part, see Part 4-15.
