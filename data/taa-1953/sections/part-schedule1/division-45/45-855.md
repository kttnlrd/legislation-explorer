# 45-855  Section 701-1 disregarded for certain purposes

If:

<a id="s45-855-a"></a>
**(a)** an amount is required to be worked out for the purpose of determining the *instalment income of an entity that is a *member of a *consolidated group for a period that is all or a part of a *consolidation transitional year for the entity; and

<a id="s45-855-b"></a>
**(b)** the period ends before Subdivision 45-Q starts to apply, because of subsection 45-705(2) or subparagraph 45-705(3)(c)(ii), (4)(d)(ii) or (iv), to the *head company of the group; that amount must be worked out without regard to any application of section 701-1 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 to the entity in relation to the period.
