# 357-85  Effect on ruling if relevant provision re-enacted

If:

> <a id="s357-85-a"></a>
> **(a)** the Commissioner makes a ruling about a relevant provision (the old provision); and

> <a id="s357-85-b"></a>
> **(b)** that provision is re-enacted or remade (with or without modifications, and whether or not the old provision is repealed); the ruling is taken also to be a ruling about that provision as re-enacted or remade (the new provision), but only so far as the new provision expresses the same ideas as the old provision.

> **Note:** Section 357-55 specifies the relevant provisions.

> **Note:** Ideas in taxation provisions are not necessarily different just because different forms of words are used: see section 15AC of the Acts Interpretation Act 1901 and section 1-3 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997.
