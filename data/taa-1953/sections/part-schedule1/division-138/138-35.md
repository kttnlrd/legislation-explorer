# 138-35  Eligible contributions

Limits on amount of eligible contributions

<a id="s138-35-1"></a>
**(1)** For the purposes of this Subdivision:

<a id="s138-35-1-a"></a>
**(a)** the maximum amount of contributions that may be eligible to be released is $50,000; and

<a id="s138-35-1-b"></a>
**(b)** the maximum amount of contributions made in a particular *financial year that may be eligible to be released is $15,000. Eligible contributions

<a id="s138-35-2"></a>
**(2)** A *concessional contribution, or *non-concessional contribution, for a *financial year is not eligible to be released unless it:

<a id="s138-35-2-a"></a>
**(a)** is made in respect of you in the financial year; and

<a id="s138-35-2-b"></a>
**(b)** is:

<a id="s138-35-2-b-i"></a>
**(i)** an employer contribution that is not a mandated employer contribution (within the meaning of Part 5 of the Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Regulations 1994); or

<a id="s138-35-2-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** a member contribution (within the meaning of that Part) that is made by you; other than a contribution to the extent it is required to be made because of a law of the Commonwealth or of a State or Territory, or the rules of the relevant *superannuation fund; and

<a id="s138-35-2-c"></a>
**(c)** is not a contribution made in respect of a *defined benefit interest; and

<a id="s138-35-2-d"></a>
**(d)** is not a contribution to a *constitutionally protected fund.

<a id="s138-35-3"></a>
**(3)** If:

<a id="s138-35-3-a"></a>
**(a)** you have *excess concessional contributions for a *financial year (your excess amount); and

<a id="s138-35-3-b"></a>
**(b)** your excess amount is greater than your *concessional contributions for the financial year that are not eligible to be released under subsection (2) (your non-eligible contributions); concessional contributions that are equal to the difference between your excess amount and your non-eligible contributions are not eligible to be released.

<a id="s138-35-4"></a>
**(4)** If:

<a id="s138-35-4-a"></a>
**(a)** you have *non-concessional contributions for the *financial year that exceed your non-concessional contributions cap for the financial year (your excess amount); and

<a id="s138-35-4-b"></a>
**(b)** your excess amount is greater than your non-concessional contributions for the financial year that are not eligible to be released under subsection (2) (your non-eligible contributions); non-concessional contributions that are equal to the difference between your excess amount and your non-eligible contributions are not eligible to be released.

<a id="s138-35-5"></a>
**(5)** For the purposes of this section, disregard paragraph 292-90(1)(b) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997.

**Note:** Under paragraph 292-90(1)(b) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997, your non-concessional contributions for a financial year would Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of other taxes Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-20 Section 138-40 include the amount of your excess concessional contributions (if any) for the financial year.
