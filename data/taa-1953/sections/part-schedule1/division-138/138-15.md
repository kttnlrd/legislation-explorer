# 138-15  Review

If you are dissatisfied with:

<a id="s138-15-a"></a>
**(a)** a *first home super saver determination in relation to you; or

<a id="s138-15-b"></a>
**(b)** a decision the Commissioner makes not to make a determination under subsection 138-10(2A); or

<a id="s138-15-c"></a>
**(c)** a decision the Commissioner makes under subsection 138-13(1):

<a id="s138-15-i"></a>
**(i)** to amend or revoke a determination; or

<a id="s138-15-ii"></a>
**(ii)** not to amend or revoke a determination; you may object against the determination, or the decision, as the case requires, in the manner set out in Part IVC. Subdivision 138-B—FHSS maximum release amount Guide to Subdivision 138-B
