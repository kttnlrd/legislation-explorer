# 138-30  FHSS releasable contributions amount


<a id="s138-30-1"></a>
**(1)** Your FHSS releasable contributions amount is the sum of the following amounts for each *financial year that starts on 1 July 2017 or a later 1 July:

<a id="s138-30-1-a"></a>
**(a)** your *FHSS eligible non-concessional contributions for the financial year;

<a id="s138-30-1-b"></a>
**(b)** 85% of your *FHSS eligible concessional contributions for the financial year. Order of counting contributions

<a id="s138-30-2"></a>
**(2)** In determining which contributions are to be counted towards your *FHSS releasable contributions amount, contributions are to be counted in the order in which they were made (from earliest to latest).

<a id="s138-30-3"></a>
**(3)** For subsection (2):

<a id="s138-30-3-a"></a>
**(a)** if an *FHSS eligible concessional contribution, and an *FHSS eligible non-concessional contribution, is made in respect of Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of other taxes Chapter 3 Superannuation Part 3-20 Section 138-35 you at the same time, the FHSS eligible non-concessional contribution is taken to have been made first; and

<a id="s138-30-3-b"></a>
**(b)** if, for a particular *financial year, you personally made both *FHSS eligible concessional contributions and *FHSS eligible non-concessional contributions, the FHSS eligible non-concessional contributions are taken to have been made first.

**Example:** For paragraph (b), in the 2018-2019 financial year, you made voluntary contributions of $1,000 each fortnight, up to a total of $25,000. At the end of the financial year, you claim a deduction for $15,000 (leaving $10,000 of the contributions as non-concessional contributions). If all of the non-concessional contributions are eligible to be released under section 138-35, the first 10 contributions made for the financial year are taken to have been the non-concessional contributions, and the later contributions are taken to be the concessional contributions.
