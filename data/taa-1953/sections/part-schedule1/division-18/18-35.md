# 18-35  Credit: penalty under section 12-415, 16-30, 16-35 or 16-40 or

related general interest charge

<a id="s18-35-1"></a>
**(1)** If an entity has paid:

<a id="s18-35-1-a"></a>
**(a)** an amount of penalty under section 16-30 or 16-40 to the Commissioner for a *withholding payment covered by Subdivision 12-F or 12-H; or

<a id="s18-35-1-b"></a>
**(b)** an amount of *general interest charge under section 298-25 for the penalty; the entity liable to pay the *withholding tax for the withholding payment is entitled to a credit equal to the amount of penalty, or general interest charge, as appropriate.

<a id="s18-35-1AA"></a>
**(1AA)**  If an entity has paid:

<a id="s18-35-1AA-a"></a>
**(a)** an amount of penalty under section 16-30 or 16-35 to the Commissioner for a *withholding payment covered by Subdivision 12-FC (labour mobility programs); or

<a id="s18-35-1AA-b"></a>
**(b)** an amount of *general interest charge under section 298-25 for the penalty; the entity liable to pay the *labour mobility program withholding tax for the withholding payment is entitled to a credit equal to the lesser of:

<a id="s18-35-1AA-c"></a>
**(c)** the amount of the penalty, or general interest charge, as appropriate; and

<a id="s18-35-1AA-d"></a>
**(d)** the amount of labour mobility program withholding tax (and any general interest charge under section 840-910 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997) in relation to the withholding payment, reduced by:

<a id="s18-35-1AA-d-i"></a>
**(i)** any credits from an application of section 18-33 in relation to the withholding payment; and

<a id="s18-35-1AA-d-ii"></a>
**(ii)** any credits from a previous application of this subsection in relation to the withholding payment.

<a id="s18-35-1A"></a>
**(1A)**  If an entity has paid:

<a id="s18-35-1A-a"></a>
**(a)** an amount of penalty under section 12-415 to the Commissioner for a failure to give a notice, or to make details available on a website, as required by section 12-395; or

<a id="s18-35-1A-b"></a>
**(b)** an amount of *general interest charge under section 298-25 for the penalty; the entity liable to pay the *managed investment trust withholding tax in relation to the amount (the relevant amount) giving rise to the penalty is entitled to a credit equal to the lesser of:

<a id="s18-35-1A-d"></a>
**(d)** the amount of penalty, or general interest charge, as appropriate; and Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of income tax Chapter 2 Pay as you go (PAYG) withholding Part 2-5 Section 18-40

<a id="s18-35-1A-e"></a>
**(e)** the amount of managed investment trust withholding tax (and any general interest charge under section 840-810 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997) in relation to the relevant amount. Remission

<a id="s18-35-2"></a>
**(2)** If:

<a id="s18-35-2-a"></a>
**(a)** an entity has paid to the Commissioner an amount of penalty mentioned in paragraph (1)(a), (1AA)(a) or (1A)(a); and

<a id="s18-35-2-b"></a>
**(b)** the Commissioner remits the whole or a part of the amount of the penalty under section 298-20; then:

<a id="s18-35-2-c"></a>
**(c)** any credit under subsection (1), (1AA) or (1A) relating to the amount paid to the Commissioner is reduced by the amount that is remitted; and

<a id="s18-35-2-d"></a>
**(d)** the Commissioner must pay to the entity an amount equal to the amount that is remitted.

<a id="s18-35-3"></a>
**(3)** If:

<a id="s18-35-3-a"></a>
**(a)** an entity has paid to the Commissioner an amount of *general interest charge mentioned in paragraph (1)(b), (1AA)(b) or (1A)(b); and

<a id="s18-35-3-b"></a>
**(b)** the Commissioner remits the whole or a part of the amount of the charge under section 8AAG; then:

<a id="s18-35-3-c"></a>
**(c)** any credit under subsection (1), (1AA) or (1A) relating to the amount is reduced by the amount that is remitted; and

<a id="s18-35-3-d"></a>
**(d)** the Commissioner must pay to the entity an amount equal to the amount that is remitted.
