# 18-45  Credit—mining payment

Credit—amount withheld

<a id="s18-45-1"></a>
**(1)** If there is an *amount withheld from a *withholding payment that is covered by section 12-320 (mining payment):

> <a id="s18-45-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** if paragraph (b) does not apply—the entity liable to pay *mining withholding tax under section 128V of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 on the payment is entitled to a credit of an amount equal to the amount withheld; or

> <a id="s18-45-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** if, under subsection 128U(4) of that Act, separate mining payments are taken to have been made to, or applied for the benefit of, 2 or more entities because of that payment—each of those entities is entitled to a credit equal to the amount worked out using the formula: The amount withheld ´ Number of those entities Credit—penalty amount

<a id="s18-45-2"></a>
**(2)** If an entity has paid to the Commissioner a penalty amount under section 16-30 or 16-35 in relation to an *amount required to be withheld under section 12-320 (mining payment):

> <a id="s18-45-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** if paragraph (1)(a) applies—the entity mentioned in that paragraph is entitled to a credit equal to the penalty amount; or

> <a id="s18-45-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** if paragraph (1)(b) applies—each of the entities mentioned in that paragraph is entitled to a credit of an amount worked out using the formula: The penalty amount ´ Number of those entities Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Collection, recovery and administration of income tax Chapter 2 Pay as you go (PAYG) withholding Part 2-5 Section 18-55 Remission

<a id="s18-45-3"></a>
**(3)** If the Commissioner remits the whole or a part of the amount of penalty under section 298-20 that has been paid to the Commissioner by the entity:

> <a id="s18-45-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** any credit that relates to the amount is reduced by the amount that is remitted; and

> <a id="s18-45-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** the Commissioner must pay to the entity an amount equal to the amount that is remitted. Entitlement to credit: Petroleum resource rent tax
