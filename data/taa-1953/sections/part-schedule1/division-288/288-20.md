# 288-20  Penalty for non-electronic payment

An entity that:

<a id="s288-20-a"></a>
**(a)** under subsection 33-10(2) of the *GST Act, is required to pay an *assessed net amount for a tax period electronically; or

<a id="s288-20-b"></a>
**(b)** under section 8AAZMA, or section 45-72 in this Schedule, is required to pay an amount electronically; but pays it another way, is liable to an administrative penalty of 5 penalty units for each payment of one or more such amounts.

**Note:** See section 4AA of the Crimes Act 1914 for the current value of a penalty unit.

**Note:** Division 298 contains machinery provisions for administrative and civil penalties.
