# 288-10  Penalty for non-electronic notification

An entity that:

> <a id="s288-10-a"></a>
> **(a)**  under subsection 31-25(2) of the *GST Act, is required to *lodge a *GST return electronically; or Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Generic assessment, collection and recovery rules Chapter 4 Charges and penalties Part 4-25 Section 288-20

> <a id="s288-10-aa"></a>
> **(aa)**  under subsection 45-20(2A) in this Schedule, is required to give a notification electronically; or

> <a id="s288-10-b"></a>
> **(b)**  under section 388-80 in this Schedule, is required to notify another *BAS amount electronically; but lodges, gives or notifies it in another way, is liable to an administrative penalty of 5 penalty units.

> **Note:** See section 4AA of the Crimes Act 1914 for the current value of a penalty unit.

> **Note:** Division 298 contains machinery provisions for administrative and civil penalties.
