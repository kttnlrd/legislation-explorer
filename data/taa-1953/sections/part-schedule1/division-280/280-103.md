# 280-103  Liability to shortfall interest charge—general


<a id="s280-103-1"></a>
**(1)**  Your liability to pay *shortfall interest charge exists whether or not you are liable to any penalty under this Act.

<a id="s280-103-2"></a>
**(2)**  Neither the Commonwealth nor an authority of the Commonwealth is liable to pay *shortfall interest charge. Collection and recovery of income tax and other liabilities Schedule 1 Generic assessment, collection and recovery rules Chapter 4 Charges and penalties Part 4-25 Section 280-105
