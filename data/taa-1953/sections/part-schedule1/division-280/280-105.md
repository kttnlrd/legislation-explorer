# 280-105  Amount of shortfall interest charge


<a id="s280-105-1"></a>
**(1)** The *shortfall interest charge for a day is worked out by multiplying the rate worked out under subsection (2) for that day by the sum of these amounts:

> <a id="s280-105-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the additional amount of income tax, *excess exploration credit tax, *petroleum resource rent tax, *excess non-concessional contributions tax, *Division 293 tax or *Division 296 tax or the amount that you are liable to pay shortfall interest charge on under subsection 280-102F(1); and

> <a id="s280-105-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the shortfall interest charge on that amount from previous days.
