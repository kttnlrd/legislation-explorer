# 444-50  Superannuation funds

If a superannuation fund does not have a trustee of the fund, this Schedule applies to the fund as if:

> <a id="s444-50-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the entity that manages the fund were the trustee of the fund; or

> <a id="s444-50-b"></a>
> **(b)**  each of the entities that manage the fund were a trustee of the fund.

> **Note:** The trustee of a superannuation fund is taken to be an entity: see subsection 960-100(2) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997. Subdivision 444-D—Incapacitated entities Table of sections 444-70       Representatives of incapacitated entities
