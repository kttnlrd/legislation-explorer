# 12-90  Unused leave payments

An entity must withhold an amount from any of the following payments it makes to an individual:

> <a id="s12-90-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an *unused annual leave payment;

> <a id="s12-90-b"></a>
> **(b)**  an *unused long service leave payment, to the extent that the payment is included in the individual's assessable income. For exceptions, see section 12-1.
