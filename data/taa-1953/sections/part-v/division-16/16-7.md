# 16-7  Treat entity obliged to pay under Subdivision 12A-C as having

withheld amount under Division 12 For the purposes of this Division:

> <a id="s16-7-a"></a>
> **(a)**  if an entity must pay an amount to the Commissioner under Subdivision 12A-C, treat the entity as being obliged to withhold the amount under Division 12; and

> <a id="s16-7-b"></a>
> **(b)**  if an entity has paid an amount to the Commissioner under Subdivision 12A-C, treat the entity as having withheld the amount under Division 12. Authorised Version C2026C00170 registered 28/04/2026 Part 2-5 Pay as you go (PAYG) withholding Section 16-20
