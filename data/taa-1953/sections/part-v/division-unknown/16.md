# 16  Payments out of Consolidated Revenue Fund


<a id="s16-1"></a>
**(1)** Where the Commissioner is required or permitted to pay an amount to a person by or under a provision of a taxation law other than:

> <a id="s16-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** a general administration provision; or

> <a id="s16-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** a provision prescribed for the purposes of this paragraph; the amount is payable out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, which is appropriated accordingly.

<a id="s16-2"></a>
**(2)** Where:

> <a id="s16-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** an amount is required or permitted to be paid to a person:

> > <a id="s16-2-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** by or under a provision of a taxation law other than: (A) a general administration provision; or (B) a provision prescribed for the purposes of this sub-subparagraph; or

> > <a id="s16-2-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** by way of the repayment, whether in whole or in part, to the person of an amount paid to the Commonwealth;

> <a id="s16-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** except as mentioned in paragraph (c), there is no provision of a taxation law by or under which the Commissioner is required or permitted to pay the amount; and

> <a id="s16-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** the Commissioner is required or permitted to pay the amount by or under a general administration provision; the amount shall be taken, for the purposes of subsection (1), to be an amount that the Commissioner is required or permitted to pay to the person by or under a provision of a taxation law of the kind referred to in that subsection.

<a id="s16-3"></a>
**(3)** In this section, general administration provision means a provision of a taxation law that provides that the Commissioner has the general administration of the taxation law.
