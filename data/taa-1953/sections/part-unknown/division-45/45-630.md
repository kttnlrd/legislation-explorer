# 45-630  When do you get a tax detriment from a scheme?


<a id="s45-630-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section describes how to work out whether you get a tax detriment from a *scheme and, if so, the amount of the tax detriment.

<a id="s45-630-2"></a>
**(2)**  First, determine your actual *tax position for an income year (apart from this Subdivision).

<a id="s45-630-3"></a>
**(3)**  Next, determine your *hypothetical tax position for the same income year (apart from this Subdivision).

<a id="s45-630-4"></a>
**(4)**  Then compare each *component of the 2 positions. If the amount of that component of the actual *tax position is higher than the Authorised Version C2026C00170 registered 28/04/2026 Part 2-10 Pay as you go (PAYG) instalments Section 45-635 amount of that component of the *hypothetical tax position, the difference between the 2 amounts is a tax detriment that you get from the *scheme.

> **Example:** In the fact situation in the example in section 45-610, X Pty Ltd gets a tax detriment from the scheme for the annual instalment component of its tax position for the income year.

> **Note:** The difference between the 2 amounts is not a tax detriment to the extent that it is attributable to certain things for which the income tax law expressly provides. See section 45-635.

> **Note:** An entity may get 2 or more tax detriments from the same scheme. One reason is that the scheme may affect 2 or more components of the entity's tax position for an income year. Another reason is that the scheme may affect the tax position for 2 or more income years.
