# 269-20  Penalty

Penalty for director on or before due day

<a id="s269-20-1"></a>
**(1)**  You are liable to pay to the Commissioner a penalty if:

> <a id="s269-20-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  at the end of the due day, the directors of the company are still under an obligation under section 269-15; and

> <a id="s269-20-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  you were under that obligation at or before that time (because you were a director).

> **Note:** Paragraph (1)(b) applies even if you stopped being a director before the end of the due day: see subsection 269-15(2).

<a id="s269-20-2"></a>
**(2)**  The penalty is due and payable at the end of the due day. > **Note:** The Commissioner must not commence proceedings to recover the penalty until the end of 21 days after the Commissioner gives you notice of the penalty under section 269-25. Penalty for new director

<a id="s269-20-3"></a>
**(3)**  You are also liable to pay to the Commissioner a penalty if:

> <a id="s269-20-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  after the due day, you became a director of the company and began to be under an obligation under section 269-15; and

> <a id="s269-20-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  30 days later, you are still under that obligation.

<a id="s269-20-4"></a>
**(4)**  The penalty is due and payable at the end of that 30th day.

> **Note:** The Commissioner must not commence proceedings to recover the penalty until the end of 21 days after the Commissioner gives you notice of the penalty under section 269-25. Amount of penalty

<a id="s269-20-5"></a>
**(5)**  The amount of a penalty under this section is equal to the unpaid amount of the company's liability under its obligation.

> **Note:** See section 269-40 for the effect on your penalty of the company discharging its obligation, or of another director paying his or her penalty.

> **Note:** See section 269-45 for your rights of indemnity and contribution.
