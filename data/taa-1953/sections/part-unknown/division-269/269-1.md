# 269-1  What this Division is about

This Division deals with obligations of a company:

> <a id="s269-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  under Subdivision 16-B in this Schedule (obligation to pay withheld amounts to the Commissioner); and

> <a id="s269-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  under Division 268 in this Schedule (obligation to pay estimates); and

> <a id="s269-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  under Part 3 of the Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992 (obligation to pay superannuation guarantee charge); and

> <a id="s269-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  under Divisions 33 and 35 of the GST Act in respect of assessed net amounts; and

> <a id="s269-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  under Division 162 of the GST Act in respect of GST instalments. The directors of a company have a duty to ensure that the company either:

> <a id="s269-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  meets those obligations; or

> <a id="s269-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  goes promptly into voluntary administration or restructuring under the Corporations Act 2001 or into liquidation. Authorised Version C2026C00170 registered 28/04/2026 Part 4-15 Collection and recovery of tax-related liabilities and other amounts Section 269-5 The directors' duties are enforced by penalties.

> **Note:** The duties this Division imposes on the directors of the company are in addition to the similar duties imposed on the public officer of the company.
