# 115-1  What this Division is about

An ADI that is liable to pay levy under the Major Bank Levy Act 2017 must give quarterly returns to the Commissioner. An amount of levy is due and payable when an ADI's last PAYG instalment within an instalment quarter is due. Table of sections 115-5      Returns 115-10     When major bank levy is due and payable
