# 426-100  What this Subdivision is about

This Subdivision deals with types of philanthropic trust funds known as public ancillary funds, private ancillary funds and community charity trusts. Authorised Version C2026C00170 registered 28/04/2026 Part 5-35 Registration and similar processes for various taxes Section 426-100 The Minister must make guidelines determining when such trust funds are entitled to be endorsed as deductible gift recipients. This Subdivision also provides for:

> <a id="s426-100-a"></a>
> **(a)**  penalties for trustees who fail to comply with the applicable trust fund guidelines, and the liability of directors of trustees to pay those penalties in certain circumstances; and

> <a id="s426-100-b"></a>
> **(b)**  powers for the Commissioner to suspend or remove trustees who breach their obligations.
