# 14Q  Interpretation


<a id="s14Q-1"></a>
**(1)** In this Part, unless the contrary intention appears: authorized officer means a person who is:

<a id="s14Q-1-a"></a>
**(a)** an officer for the purposes of the Customs Act 1901; or

<a id="s14Q-1-b"></a>
**(b)** a member of the Australian Federal Police. departure authorization certificate means a certificate under subsection 14U(1). departure prohibition order means an order under subsection 14S(1).

<a id="s14Q-2"></a>
**(2)** A reference in this Part to the departure of a person from Australia for a foreign country is a reference to the departure of the person from Australia for a foreign country, whether or not the person intends to return to Australia.
