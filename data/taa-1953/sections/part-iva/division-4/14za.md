# 14ZA  Certain tax debtors to produce authority to depart etc.


<a id="s14ZA-1"></a>
**(1)**  Where:

> <a id="s14ZA-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a person in respect of whom a departure prohibition order is in force is about to depart from Australia for a foreign country; and

> <a id="s14ZA-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the departure is authorized by a departure authorization certificate; the person shall, if required to do so pursuant to this subsection by an authorized officer, produce a copy of the departure authorization certificate for inspection by the authorized officer. Penalty: 5 penalty units.

<a id="s14ZA-1A"></a>
**(1A)**  An offence under subsection (1) is an offence of strict liability.

> **Note:** For strict liability, see section 6.1 of the Criminal Code.

<a id="s14ZA-2"></a>
**(2)**  Section 8C does not apply in relation to a requirement made pursuant to subsection (1) of this section.
