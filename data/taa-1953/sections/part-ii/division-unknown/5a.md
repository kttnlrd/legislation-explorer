# 5A  Remuneration and allowances of Commissioner and Second Commissioners


<a id="s5A-1"></a>
**(1)** The Commissioner of Taxation and the Second Commissioners of Taxation shall be paid such remuneration as is determined by the Remuneration Tribunal but, if no determination of that Commissioner of Taxation, Second Commissioner of Taxation and Deputy Commissioner of Taxation Part II Section 6 remuneration by the Tribunal is in operation, they shall be paid remuneration at the respective rates that were applicable immediately before the commencement of this section.

<a id="s5A-2"></a>
**(2)** The Commissioner of Taxation and the Second Commissioners of Taxation shall be paid such allowances as are prescribed.

<a id="s5A-3"></a>
**(3)** This section has effect subject to the Remuneration Tribunal Act 1973.
