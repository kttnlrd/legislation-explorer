# 8M  Penalties for offences against subsections 8K(1) and (1B) and 8L(1) and (1A)


<a id="s8M-1"></a>
**(1)**  Subject to subsection (2), an offence against subsection 8K(1) or

<a id="s8M-1B"></a>
**(1B)**  or 8L(1) or (1A) is punishable on conviction by a fine not exceeding 20 penalty units.

<a id="s8M-2"></a>
**(2)**  Where:

> <a id="s8M-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a person is convicted of an offence against subsection 8K(1) or (1B) or 8L(1) or (1A); and

> <a id="s8M-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the court before which the person is convicted is satisfied that the person has previously been convicted of a relevant offence; the penalty that the court may impose in respect of the first-mentioned offence is a fine not exceeding 40 penalty units.
