# 8V  Penalties for offences against sections 8T and 8U


<a id="s8V-1"></a>
**(1)** Subject to subsection (2), an offence against section 8T or 8U is punishable on conviction by a fine not exceeding 50 penalty units or imprisonment for a period not exceeding 12 months, or both.

<a id="s8V-2"></a>
**(2)** Where:

<a id="s8V-2-a"></a>
**(a)** a person is convicted of an offence against section 8T or 8U; and

<a id="s8V-2-b"></a>
**(b)** the court before which the person is convicted is satisfied that the person has previously been convicted of a relevant offence; the penalty that the court may impose in respect of the first-mentioned offence is a fine not exceeding 100 penalty units or imprisonment for a period not exceeding 2 years, or both.
