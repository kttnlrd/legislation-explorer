# 13D  Interpretation


<a id="s13D-1"></a>
**(1)** In this Part, unless the contrary intention appears: Australian Capital Territory includes the Jervis Bay Territory. officer means:

> <a id="s13D-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** a person appointed or engaged under the Public Service Act 1999; or

> <a id="s13D-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** a State taxation officer. State includes the Northern Territory. State Minister means:

> <a id="s13D-1-a-2"></a>
> **(a)** in relation to a State other than the Northern Territory—a Minister of the Crown of the State; or

> <a id="s13D-1-b-2"></a>
> **(b)** in relation to the Northern Territory—a Minister of the Northern Territory. State taxation officer means:

> <a id="s13D-1-a-3"></a>
> **(a)** a person or authority, not being a State Minister, who or which is for the time being authorised under a law of a State to perform the functions of a State taxation officer under this Part; or

> <a id="s13D-1-b-3"></a>
> **(b)** a person, not being a State Minister, authorised by writing signed by a person or authority referred to in paragraph (a) to act under this Part. State tax law means a law of a State relating to taxation. Territory means the Australian Capital Territory. Territory taxation officer means:

> <a id="s13D-1-a-4"></a>
> **(a)** a person or authority, not being a Minister of State of the Commonwealth, who or which is for the time being authorised under a law of the Territory to perform the functions of a Territory taxation officer under this Part; or

> <a id="s13D-1-b-4"></a>
> **(b)** a person, not being a Minister of State of the Commonwealth, authorised by writing signed by a person or authority referred to in paragraph (a) to act under this Part. Territory tax law means a law of the Territory relating to taxation.

<a id="s13D-2"></a>
**(2)** A reference in Division 3 or 4 to a State tax law includes a reference to a Territory tax law.

<a id="s13D-3"></a>
**(3)** A reference in Division 3 or 4 to a State taxation officer includes a reference to a Territory taxation officer.
