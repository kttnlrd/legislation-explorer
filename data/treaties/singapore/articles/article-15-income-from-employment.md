title: "Article 15 — Income from Employment"
---
A student or trainee who is, or was immediately before visiting one of the Contracting States, a resident of the other Contracting State and is present in the first-mentioned Contracting State solely for the purpose of his education or training shall not be taxed in that first-mentioned Contracting State on payments which he receives for the purpose of his maintenance, education or training provided that such payments are made to him from outside that first-mentioned Contracting State.
