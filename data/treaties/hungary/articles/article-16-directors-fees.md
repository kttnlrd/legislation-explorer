title: "Article 16 — Directors' Fees"

' Fees Directors ' fees and similar payments derived by a person who is a resident of one of the Contracting States in the person ' s capacity as a member of the board of directors or any similar organ of a company which is a resident of the other Contracting State may be taxed in that other State.
