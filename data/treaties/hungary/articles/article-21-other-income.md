title: "Article 21 — Other Income"
---
Where a student or trainee, who is a resident of a Contracting State or who was a resident of that State immediately before visiting the other Contracting State and who is temporarily present in that other State solely for the purpose of education or training, receives payments from sources outside that other State for the purpose of maintenance, education, or training, those payments shall be exempt from tax in that other State.
