title: "Article 23 — Mutual Agreement Procedure"
---
Income, profits or gains derived by a resident of a Contracting State which, under any one or more of Articles 6 to 8, 10 to 17, 19 and 22, may be taxed in the other Contracting State shall, for the purposes of Article 24 and of the income tax laws of the respective States be deemed to be income from sources in that other State.
