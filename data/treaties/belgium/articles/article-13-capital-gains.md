-
 TAXATION OF INCOME
Alienation of Property
(1)
Income from the alienation of real property may be taxed in the Contracting State in which that property is situated.
(2)
For the purposes of this Article
-
(a)
the term 
"
real property
"
 shall include
-
(i)
a lease of land or any other direct interest in or over land;
(ii)
rights to exploit, or to explore for, natural resources; and
(iii)
shares or comparable interests in a company, the assets of which consist wholly or principally of direct interests in or over land in one of the Contracting States or of rights to exploit, or to explore for, natural resources in one of the Contracting States;
(b)
real property shall be deemed to be situated
-
(i)
where it consists of direct interests in or over land 
-
 in the Contracting State in which the land is situated;
(ii)
where it consists of rights to exploit, or to explore for, natural resources 
-
 in the Contracting State in which the natural resources are situated or the exploration may take place; and
(iii)
where it consists of shares or comparable interests in a company, the assets of which consist wholly or principally of direct interests in or over land in one of the Contracting States or of rights to exploit, or to explore for, natural resources in one of the Contracting States 
-
 in the Contracting State in which the assets or the principal assets of the company are situated.
(3)
Subject to the provisions of paragraph (1), income from the alienation of capital assets of an enterprise of a Contracting State shall be taxable only in that Contracting State, but, where those assets form part of the business property of a permanent establishment situated in the other Contracting State, such income may be taxed in that other State.
