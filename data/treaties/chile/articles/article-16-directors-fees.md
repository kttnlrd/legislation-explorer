title: "Article 16 — Directors' Fees"
---
Directors ' Fees Directors ' fees and other similar payments derived by a resident of a Contracting State in that person ' s capacity as a member of the board of directors or a similar organ of a company which is a resident of the other Contracting State may be taxed in that other State.
