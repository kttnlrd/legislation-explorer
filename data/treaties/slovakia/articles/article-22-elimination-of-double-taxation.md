title: "Article 22 — Elimination of Double Taxation"
---
1.
Income, profits or gains derived by a resident of the Slovak Republic which, under the provisions of any one or more of Articles 6 to 8 and 10 to 19, may be taxed in Australia shall for the purposes of the law of Australia relating to Australian tax be deemed to be income from sources in Australia.
2.
Income, profits or gains derived by a resident of Australia which, under the provisions of any one or more of Articles 6 to 8 and 10 to 19, may be taxed in the Slovak Republic shall for the purposes of paragraph 1 of Article 23 and of the law of Australia relating to Australian tax be deemed to be income from sources in the Slovak Republic.
