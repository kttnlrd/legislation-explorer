title: "Article 2 — Taxes Covered"
---
The existing taxes to which this Agreement shall apply are:

- **(a)** in Australia: the income tax, and the resource rent tax in respect of offshore projects relating to exploration for or exploitation of petroleum resources, imposed under the federal law of Australia;

- **(b)** in Argentina: the income tax ( impuesto a las ganancias ). This Agreement shall also apply to any identical or substantially similar taxes which are imposed under the law of the Argentine Republic or the federal law of Australia after the date of signature of this Agreement in addition to, or in place of, the existing taxes. The competent authorities of the Contracting States shall notify each other of any substantial changes which have been made in the laws of their respective States relating to the taxes to which this Agreement applies within a reasonable period of time after those changes.
