title: "Article 6 — Income from Real Property"
---
**(1)** Income from real property may be taxed by the Contracting State in which such real property is situated.

**(2)** For the purposes of this Convention:

- **(i)** a leasehold interest in land, whether or not improved, shall be regarded as real property situated where the land to which the interest relates is situated; and (ii) rights to exploit or to explore for natural resources shall be regarded as real property situated where the natural resources are situated or sought.
