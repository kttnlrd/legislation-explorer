# Article 23 — Mutual Agreement Procedure
Swedish Agreement
AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF AUSTRALIA AND THE GOVERNMENT OF SWEDEN FOR THE AVOIDANCE OF DOUBLE TAXATION AND THE PREVENTION OF FISCAL EVASION WITH RESPECT TO TAXES ON INCOME
Source of Income
(1)
Income derived by a resident of Sweden which, under any one or more of Articles 6 to 8, Articles 10 to 18 and Article 22 may be taxed in Australia, shall for the purposes of the income tax law of Australia be deemed to be income from sources in Australia.
(2)
Income derived by a resident of Australia which, under any one or more of Articles 6 to 8, Articles 10 to 18 and Article 22 may be taxed in Sweden, shall for the purposes of paragraph (1) of Article 24 and of the income tax law of Australia be deemed to be income from sources in Sweden.
