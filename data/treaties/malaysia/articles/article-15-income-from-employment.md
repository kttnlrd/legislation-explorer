title: "Article 15 — Income from Employment"
---
' Fees Notwithstanding the provisions of Article 14, directors ' fees and similar payments derived by a resident of one of the Contracting States in his capacity as a member of the board of directors of a company which is a resident of the other Contracting State may be taxed in that other State.
