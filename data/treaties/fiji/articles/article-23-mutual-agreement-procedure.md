# Article 23 — Mutual Agreement Procedure
Fijian Agreement
AGREEMENT BETWEEN AUSTRALIA AND FIJI FOR THE AVOIDANCE OF DOUBLE TAXATION AND THE PREVENTION OF FISCAL EVASION WITH RESPECT TO TAXES ON INCOME
Income Not Expressly Mentioned
(1)
Items of income of a resident of one of the Contracting States which are not expressly mentioned in the foregoing Articles of this Agreement shall be taxable only in that State.
(2)
However, any such income derived by a resident of one of the Contracting States from sources in the other Contracting State may also be taxed in that other State.
(3)
The provisions of paragraph (1) shall not apply to income derived by a resident of one of the Contracting States where that income is effectively connected with a permanent establishment or fixed base situated in the other Contracting State. In such a case, the provisions of Article 7 or 14, as the case may be, shall apply.
