title: "Article 21 — Other Income"
---
1. Income, profits or gains derived by a resident of a Contracting State which, under Articles to , to and may be taxed in the other Contracting State, shall be deemed to be income from sources in that other State. 2. Profits included in the profits of an enterprise of a Contracting State under paragraph 1 of Article shall for purposes of the taxation of that enterprise be deemed to be income of that enterprise derived from sources in that Contracting State. 3. Income, profits or gains derived by a resident of a Contracting State which, under any one or more of Articles to , to and , may be taxed in the other Contracting State shall for the purposes of Article and of the law of the first-mentioned Contracting State relating to its tax be deemed to arise from sources in the other Contracting State.
