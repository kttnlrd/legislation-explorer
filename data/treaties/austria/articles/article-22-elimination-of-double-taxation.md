title: "Article 22 — Elimination of Double Taxation"
---
Source of Income

**(1)** Income derived by a resident of Austria which, under any one or more of Articles 6 to 8, Articles 10 to 19 and Article 21, may be taxed in Australia shall for the purposes of the law of Australia relating to Australian tax be deemed to be income from sources in Australia.

**(2)** Income derived by a resident of Australia which, under any one or more of Articles 6 to 8, Articles 10 to 19 and Article 21, may be taxed in Austria shall for the purposes of paragraph (1) of Article 23 and of the law of Australia relating to Australian tax be deemed to be income from sources in Austria.
