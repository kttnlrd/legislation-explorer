-
 TAXATION OF INCOME
Income of Dual Resident
Where a person, who by reason of the provisions of paragraph (1) of Article 
4
 is a resident of both States but by reason of the provisions of paragraph (3) or (4) of that Article is deemed for the purposes of this Agreement to be a resident solely of one of the States, derives income from sources in that State or from sources outside both States, that income shall be taxable only in that State.
