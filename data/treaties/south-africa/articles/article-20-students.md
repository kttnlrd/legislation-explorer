title: "Article 20 — Students"
---
A student who is temporarily present in a Contracting State solely for the purpose of the student
'
s education and who is, or immediately before being so present was, a resident of the other Contracting State, shall be exempt from tax in the firstmentioned State on payments received from sources outside that firstmentioned State for the purposes of the student
'
s maintenance or education.
