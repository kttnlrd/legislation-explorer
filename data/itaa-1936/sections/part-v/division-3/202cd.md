# 202CD Sending of TFN declaration to Commissioner

<a id="s202CD-1"></a>
**(1)** Where a recipient gives a payer a TFN declaration, the payer shall:

> <a id="s202CD-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** countersign the original of the declaration;

> <a id="s202CD-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** within 14 days after the declaration is made, send the original to the office of a Deputy Commissioner; and

> <a id="s202CD-1-c"></a>
> **(c)** retain the copy of the declaration in accordance with subsection (6). Penalty: 10 penalty units.

<a id="s202CD-4"></a>
**(4)** If:

> <a id="s202CD-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** a TFN declaration, when given to a payer, does not quote the recipient's tax file number; and

> <a id="s202CD-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** before the payer sends the declaration to the Deputy Commissioner, the recipient informs the payer of the recipient's tax file number; the payer shall write the number on the declaration and on the copy. Penalty: 10 penalty units.

<a id="s202CD-5"></a>
**(5)** Where a tax file number has been written on a declaration under subsection (4), the declaration shall be regarded as stating that number as the tax file number of the recipient who made the declaration. <a id="s202CD-5A"></a>
**(5A)** A payer who fails to comply with subsection (1) or (4) is liable to pay to the Commissioner a penalty of 10 penalty units.

> **Note:** See section 4AA of the Crimes Act 1914 for the current value of a penalty unit.

> **Note:** Division 298 in Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953 contains machinery provisions relating to civil penalties.

<a id="s202CD-6"></a>
**(6)** The payer shall retain the copy of a TFN declaration until the second 1 July after the day on which the declaration ceases to have effect.
