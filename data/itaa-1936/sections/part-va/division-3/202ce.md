# 202CE  Effect of incorrect quotation of tax file number


<a id="s202CE-1"></a>
**(1)** If the Commissioner is satisfied:

> <a id="s202CE-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** that the tax file number stated in a TFN declaration:

> > <a id="s202CE-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** has been cancelled or withdrawn since the declaration was given; or

> > <a id="s202CE-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** is otherwise wrong; and

> <a id="s202CE-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** that the recipient has a tax file number; the Commissioner may give to the payer concerned written notice of the incorrect statement and the recipient's tax file number.

<a id="s202CE-2"></a>
**(2)** If a notice is given under subsection (1), the TFN declaration shall be regarded, for the purposes of this Part, as having always stated the recipient's tax file number.

<a id="s202CE-3"></a>
**(3)** If:

> <a id="s202CE-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** the Commissioner is satisfied that the tax file number stated in a TFN declaration:

> > <a id="s202CE-3-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)** has been cancelled since the declaration was given; or

> > <a id="s202CE-3-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** is for any other reason not the recipient's tax file number; and

> <a id="s202CE-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** the Commissioner is not satisfied that the recipient has a tax file number; the Commissioner may, by written notice given to the payer, inform the payer accordingly.

<a id="s202CE-4"></a>
**(4)** A notice under subsection (3) takes effect on the day specified in the notice, being a day not earlier than the day on which a copy of the notice is given to the recipient under subsection (5).

<a id="s202CE-5"></a>
**(5)** The Commissioner shall give a copy of any notice under subsection (3) to the recipient concerned, together with a written statement of the reasons for the decision to give the notice.

<a id="s202CE-6"></a>
**(6)** On and from the day on which a notice under subsection (3) takes effect, the TFN declaration concerned shall be taken not to state the tax file number of the recipient concerned.

<a id="s202CE-7"></a>
**(7)** Subsection (6) does not apply to a TFN declaration given to the Employment Secretary or to the Chief Executive Centrelink:

> <a id="s202CE-7-a"></a>
> **(a)** by a person who is an applicant for an austudy payment, a jobseeker payment or a youth allowance under the Social Security Act 1991; or

> <a id="s202CE-7-aaa"></a>
> **(aaa)** by a person who is not a member of a couple and is an applicant for a parenting payment under the Social Security Act 1991; or

> <a id="s202CE-7-b"></a>
> **(b)** by a person who is a recipient for the purposes of this Part because the person receives, or expects to receive, a payment referred to in paragraph (a). Persons receiving benefits under Veterans' Entitlements Act

<a id="s202CE-8"></a>
**(8)** Subsection (6) does not apply to a TFN declaration given to the Veterans' Affairs Secretary:

> <a id="s202CE-8-a"></a>
> **(a)** by a person who is an applicant for a pension or allowance under the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986; or

> <a id="s202CE-8-b"></a>
> **(b)** by a person who is a recipient for the purposes of this Part because the person receives, or expects to receive, a pension, veteran payment (within the meaning of that Act) or allowance under that Act.

<a id="s202CE-9"></a>
**(9)** Subsection (6) does not apply to a TFN declaration given to the Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Commission: Tax file numbers Part VA Quotation of tax file numbers by recipients of eligible PAYG payments Division 3 Section 202CEA

> <a id="s202CE-9-a"></a>
> **(a)** by a person who is an applicant for compensation or an allowance under the Military Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2004; or

> <a id="s202CE-9-b"></a>
> **(b)** by a person who is a recipient for the purposes of this Part because the person receives, or expects to receive, such compensation or allowance.
