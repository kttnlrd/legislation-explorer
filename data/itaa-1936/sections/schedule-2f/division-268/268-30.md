# 268-30  Calculate the notional loss or net income for each period


<a id="s268-30-1"></a>
**(1)**  A notional loss or notional net income of the trust must be worked out for each period into which the income year has been divided in accordance with Subdivision 268-B.

<a id="s268-30-2"></a>
**(2)**  The trust has a notional loss for a period if the deductions attributed to the period under section 268-35 exceed the assessable income attributed to the period under section 268-40. The notional loss is the amount of the excess. For a period during which the trust was in partnership, the notional loss is worked out under Subdivision 268-D.

<a id="s268-30-3"></a>
**(3)**  On the other hand, if that assessable income exceeds those deductions, the trust has a notional net income for the period, equal to the excess. For a period during which the trust was in partnership, the notional net income is worked out under Subdivision 268-D.

<a id="s268-30-4"></a>
**(4)**  If the trust has a notional loss for none of the periods in the income year, this Subdivision has no further application, and the trust’s net income for the income year is calculated in the usual way. The usual way of working out net income is set out in section 95.
