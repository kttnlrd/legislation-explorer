# 266-5  What this Division is about

This Division is about the income tax consequences, for various kinds of fixed trusts, of certain events: •      for an ordinary fixed trust, the event is a change in ownership (subject to a non-fixed trust exception); •      for an unlisted widely held trust, the event is an abnormal trading in its units, or the end of an income year, together with a change in ownership; •      for a listed widely held trust, the event is an abnormal trading in its units, together with a change in ownership and business; •      for an unlisted very widely held trust or a wholesale widely held trust, the event is an abnormal trading in its units, together with a change in ownership. in ownership Section 266-10
