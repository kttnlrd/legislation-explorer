# 272-105  Widely held unit trust

Basic meaning

<a id="s272-105-1"></a>
**(1)**  A trust is a widely held unit trust if:

> <a id="s272-105-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  it is a fixed trust that is a unit trust; and

> <a id="s272-105-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  it is not closely held (see subsection (2)). A trust is also a widely held unit trust if it is an AMIT (including an AMIT arising from the operation of section 276-20 (Trust with classes of interests—each class treated as separate AMIT)). Closely held—income test

<a id="s272-105-2"></a>
**(2)**  A trust is closely held if:

> <a id="s272-105-2-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an individual has, or up to 20 individuals have between them; or

> <a id="s272-105-2-b"></a>
> **(b)**  no individual has, or no individuals have between them; directly or indirectly and for their own benefit, fixed entitlements to a 75% or greater share of the income of the trust. Closely held—capital test

<a id="s272-105-2A"></a>
**(2A)**  A trust is also closely held if:

> <a id="s272-105-2A-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an individual has, or up to 20 individuals have between them; or

> <a id="s272-105-2A-b"></a>
> **(b)**  no individual has, or no individuals have between them; directly or indirectly and for their own benefit, fixed entitlements to a 75% or greater share of the capital of the trust. Single individual

<a id="s272-105-3"></a>
**(3)**  For the purposes of subsection (2) or (2A), all of the following are taken to be a single individual:

> <a id="s272-105-3-a"></a>
> **(a)**  an individual, whether or not the individual holds units in the unit trust; and

> <a id="s272-105-3-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the individual's relatives; and

> <a id="s272-105-3-c"></a>
> **(c)**  in relation to any units in respect of which other individuals are nominees of the individual or of the individual's relatives—those other individuals. Exception

<a id="s272-105-4"></a>
**(4)**  A unit trust is not a widely held unit trust if, because of:

> <a id="s272-105-4-a"></a>
> **(a)**  any provision in the trust instrument, or in any contract, agreement or instrument:

> > <a id="s272-105-4-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  authorising the variation or abrogation of rights attaching to any of the units; or

> > <a id="s272-105-4-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  relating to the conversion, cancellation, extinguishment or redemption of any of the units; or

> <a id="s272-105-4-b"></a>
> **(b)**  any contract, agreement, arrangement, option or instrument under which a person has power to acquire any of the units; or

> <a id="s272-105-4-c"></a>
> **(c)**  any power, authority or discretion in a person in relation to the rights attaching to any of the units; it is reasonable to conclude that the rights attaching to any of the units are capable of being varied or abrogated in such a way (even if they are not in fact varied or abrogated in that way) that directly or indirectly the requirement in paragraph (1)(b) would not be satisfied.
