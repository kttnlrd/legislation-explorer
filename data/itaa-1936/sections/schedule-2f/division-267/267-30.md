# 267-30  If certain distributions are made, the trust must pass the

pattern of distributions test When trust must meet the condition

<a id="s267-30-1"></a>
**(1)** If either or both of the following happened, the trust must meet the condition in subsection (2):

<a id="s267-30-1-a"></a>
**(a)** the trust distributed income:

<a id="s267-30-1-a-i"></a>
**(i)** in the income year or within 2 months after its end; and

<a id="s267-30-1-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** in at least one of the 6 earlier income years; or

<a id="s267-30-1-b"></a>
**(b)** the trust distributed capital:

<a id="s267-30-1-b-i"></a>
**(i)** in the income year or within 2 months after its end; and

<a id="s267-30-1-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** in at least one of the 6 earlier income years. The condition

<a id="s267-30-2"></a>
**(2)** The condition is that the trust must pass the pattern of distributions test for the income year. To find out whether the trust passes the pattern of distributions test for the income year: see Subdivision 269-D.
