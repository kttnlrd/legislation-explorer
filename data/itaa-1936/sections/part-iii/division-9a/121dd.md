# 121DD  Meaning of OB leasing activity


<a id="s121DD-1"></a>
**(1)** An OB leasing activity is leasing activity with an offshore person involving offshore property.

<a id="s121DD-2"></a>
**(2)** Without limiting subsection (1), OB leasing activity includes entering into:

> <a id="s121DD-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** any arrangement (within the meaning of section 51AD) under which a right to use offshore property is granted by the owner to another person; or

> <a id="s121DD-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** any arrangement (within the meaning of that section) under which a right to use offshore property, being a right derived directly or indirectly from a right mentioned in paragraph (a) in relation to the property, is granted by a person to another person; with an offshore person.
