# 102UG  Correct TB statement

Share of net income case

<a id="s102UG-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies if a share of the net income of a closely held trust for a year of income is included in the assessable income of a trustee beneficiary of the trust under section 97 and the share comprises or includes an untaxed part. Tax-preferred amount case

<a id="s102UG-2"></a>
**(2)** This section also applies if a trustee beneficiary of a closely held trust is presently entitled at the end of a year of income to a share of a tax-preferred amount of the trust. Correct TB statement

<a id="s102UG-3"></a>
**(3)** If this section applies, the trustee of the closely held trust makes a correct TB statement about the share if the trustee correctly states, in the approved form:

<a id="s102UG-3-a"></a>
**(a)** if the trustee beneficiary is a resident at the end of the year of income:

<a id="s102UG-3-a-i"></a>
**(i)** the name and tax file number of the trustee beneficiary; and

<a id="s102UG-3-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** the amount of the untaxed part of the share or the amount of the share of the tax-preferred amount; and

<a id="s102UG-3-b"></a>
**(b)** if the trustee beneficiary is a non-resident at the end of the year of income:

<a id="s102UG-3-b-i"></a>
**(i)** the name and address of the trustee beneficiary; and

<a id="s102UG-3-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** the amount of the untaxed part of the share or the amount of the share of the tax-preferred amount.

**Note:** If a closely held trust has multiple trustee beneficiaries, the requirements in subsection (3) will have to be met for each of them for the trustee of the closely held trust to avoid paying any trustee beneficiary non-disclosure tax.
