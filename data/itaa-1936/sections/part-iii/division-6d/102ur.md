# 102UR  Notice of liability


<a id="s102UR-1"></a>
**(1)** The Commissioner may give a person or persons, by post or otherwise, a notice specifying:

> <a id="s102UR-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the amount of any trustee beneficiary non-disclosure tax that the Commissioner has ascertained is payable by the person or persons; and

> <a id="s102UR-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the day on which that tax became or will become due and payable. Effect of notice on liability etc.

<a id="s102UR-2"></a>
**(2)** The amount of the liability of a person or persons to trustee beneficiary non-disclosure tax, and the due date for payment of the tax, are not dependent on, or in any way affected by, the giving of a notice. Amendment of notice

<a id="s102UR-3"></a>
**(3)** The Commissioner may at any time amend a notice. An amended notice is a notice for the purposes of this section. Inconsistency between notices

<a id="s102UR-4"></a>
**(4)** If there is an inconsistency between notices that relate to the same subject matter, the later notice prevails to the extent of the inconsistency. Objections

<a id="s102UR-5"></a>
**(5)** A person who is or persons who are dissatisfied with a notice made in relation to the person or persons may object against it in the manner set out in Part IVC of the Taxation Administration Act 1953.
