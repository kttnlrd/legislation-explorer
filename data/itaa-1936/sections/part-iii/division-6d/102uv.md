# 102UV  Trustee of closely held trust may record etc. tax file number


<a id="s102UV-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies if a trustee beneficiary in respect of:

> <a id="s102UV-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** a share of the net income of a closely held trust for a year of income that is included in the assessable income of the trustee beneficiary of the trust under section 97; or

> <a id="s102UV-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** a share of a tax-preferred amount of a closely held trust to which the trustee beneficiary of the trust is presently entitled at the end of a year of income; quotes his or her tax file number to the trustee of the closely held trust in connection with that trustee making a correct TB statement about that share.

<a id="s102UV-2"></a>
**(2)** Section 8WB of the Taxation Administration Act 1953 does not prohibit the trustee of the closely held trust from:

> <a id="s102UV-2-a"></a>
> **(a)** recording the tax file number or maintaining such a record; or

> <a id="s102UV-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** using the tax file number in a manner connecting it with the identity of the trustee beneficiary; or

> <a id="s102UV-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** divulging or communicating the tax file number to a third person; in connection with that trustee making a correct TB statement about that share. Adjustment of Division 6 assessable amount in relation to capital gains, franked distributions and franking credits Division 6E Section 102UW
