# 128GB  Division not to apply to interest payments on offshore borrowings by offshore banking units


<a id="s128GB-1"></a>
**(1)** This section applies to:

<a id="s128GB-1-a"></a>
**(a)** interest paid by a person in respect of an offshore borrowing of the person; or Dividends, interest and royalties paid to non-residents and to certain other persons Division 11A Section 128NA

<a id="s128GB-1-b"></a>
**(b)** interest consisting of gold paid by a person in respect of an offshore gold borrowing of the person; if, when the borrowing took place, the person was an offshore banking unit (whether or not the person is still an offshore banking unit when the interest is paid).

<a id="s128GB-1A"></a>
**(1A)**  However, this section does not apply to:

<a id="s128GB-1A-a"></a>
**(a)** interest paid on or after 1 January 2024; and

<a id="s128GB-1A-b"></a>
**(b)** interest consisting of gold paid on or after 1 January 2024.

<a id="s128GB-2"></a>
**(2)** Tax is not payable in accordance with this Division in respect of interest to which this section applies.
