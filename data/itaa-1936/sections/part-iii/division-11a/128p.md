# 128P  Objections

If an applicant for a certificate under this Division is dissatisfied with a decision of the Commissioner:

<a id="s128P-a"></a>
**(a)** in any case—to refuse to issue the certificate; or

<a id="s128P-b"></a>
**(b)** in the case of a certificate under section 128AB—to specify a particular amount in the certificate; the applicant may object against the decision in the manner set out in Part IVC of the Taxation Administration Act 1953.
