# 124ZS  Definitions

In this Subdivision: non-CGT assessable income means an amount included in assessable income otherwise than under Part 3-1 or 3-3 (about CGT) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 or Subdivision C of this Division. SME investment means an investment other than an unregulated investment.

**Note:** SME stands for small and medium enterprises. unregulated investment has the same meaning as in the Pooled Development Funds Act 1992.
