# 102AC  Persons to whom Division applies


<a id="s102AC-1"></a>
**(1)** For the purposes of this Division, a person is a prescribed person in relation to a year of income if:

> <a id="s102AC-1-a"></a>
> **(a)** the person is less than 18 years of age on the last day of the year of income; and

> <a id="s102AC-1-b"></a>
> **(b)** the person is not an excepted person in relation to the year of income.

<a id="s102AC-2"></a>
**(2)** Subject to this section, a person (in this subsection referred to as the minor) is an excepted person in relation to a year of income for the purposes of this Division if, and only if:

> <a id="s102AC-2-b"></a>
> **(b)** the minor was engaged in a full-time occupation on the last day of the year of income;

> <a id="s102AC-2-c"></a>
> **(c)** the minor is a person:

> > <a id="s102AC-2-c-i"></a>
> > **(i)** in respect of whom a carer allowance under the Social Security Act 1991 was payable in respect of a period that included the last day of the year of income; or

> > <a id="s102AC-2-c-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** to whom a disability support pension under that Act was payable in respect of a period that included the last day of the year of income; or

> <a id="s102AC-2-d"></a>
> **(d)** the Commissioner:

> > <a id="s102AC-2-d-i"></a>
> > **(i)** has received a certificate issued by a legally qualified medical practitioner certifying that the minor is: (A) a disabled child, or a disabled adult, within the meaning of Part 2.19 of the Social Security Act 1991; or (B) a person who has a continuing inability to work within the meaning of Part 2.3 of the Social Security Act 1991 or is permanently blind; and

> > <a id="s102AC-2-d-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** is satisfied that, on the last day of the year of income, the minor was a person of the kind mentioned in sub-subparagraph (i)(A) or (B);

> <a id="s102AC-2-da"></a>
> **(da)** the minor is the principal beneficiary of a special disability trust;

> <a id="s102AC-2-e"></a>
> **(e)** a double orphan pension was payable in respect of the minor under the Social Security Act 1991 in respect of a period that included the last day of the year of income;

> <a id="s102AC-2-f"></a>
> **(f)** but for section 1003 of the Social Security Act 1991, a double orphan pension would have been payable in respect of the minor under that Act in respect of a period that included the last day of the year of income; or

> <a id="s102AC-2-g"></a>
> **(g)** the Commissioner:

> > <a id="s102AC-2-g-i"></a>
> > **(i)** has received a certificate issued by a legally qualified medical practitioner certifying that the minor is a person who, by reason of a permanent disability, is unlikely to be able to engage in a full-time occupation; and

> > <a id="s102AC-2-g-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)** is satisfied that, on the last day of the year of income, the minor was such a person.

<a id="s102AC-3"></a>
**(3)** Where:

> <a id="s102AC-3-a"></a>
> **(a)** a double orphan pension was payable, or would, but for section 1003 of the Social Security Act 1991, have been payable, in respect of a person under that Act in respect of a period during a year of income, being a period that included the last day of the year of income; and

> <a id="s102AC-3-b"></a>
> **(b)** during the whole of the period referred to in paragraph (a), the person was wholly or substantially dependent for support on a relative or relatives of the person; that person shall not be taken by virtue of paragraph (2)(e) or (f) to be an excepted person in relation to the year of income.

<a id="s102AC-4"></a>
**(4)** Where:

> <a id="s102AC-4-a"></a>
> **(a)** the Commissioner is of the opinion that, during a period during a year of income, being a period that included the last day of the year of income, a person was a person who, by reason of a permanent disability, was unlikely to be able to engage in a full-time occupation; and

> <a id="s102AC-4-b"></a>
> **(b)** during the whole of the period referred to in paragraph (a), the person was wholly or substantially dependent for support on a relative or relatives of the person; that person shall not be taken, by virtue of paragraph (2)(g), to be an excepted person in relation to the year of income.

<a id="s102AC-5"></a>
**(5)** For the purposes of subsections (3) and (4), a person shall be taken to have been wholly or substantially dependent for support on a relative or relatives of the person during any period during which that person resided with a relative or relatives of the person unless the contrary is established to the satisfaction of the Commissioner.

<a id="s102AC-6"></a>
**(6)** Subject to this section, a person shall be taken, for the purposes of subsection (2), to have been engaged in a full-time occupation on the last day of a year of income if, and only if:

> <a id="s102AC-6-a"></a>
> **(a)** the person was, on the last day of the year of income, a person engaged in a full-time occupation; or

> <a id="s102AC-6-b"></a>
> **(b)** in a case to which paragraph (a) does not apply—the person was engaged in a full-time occupation during the year of income for a period of not less than 3 months or for periods the aggregate of which is not less than 3 months.

<a id="s102AC-7"></a>
**(7)** Where:

> <a id="s102AC-7-a"></a>
> **(a)** during a period during a year of income, a person was engaged in a full-time occupation; and

> <a id="s102AC-7-b"></a>
> **(b)** during the year of income and after the expiration of that period, the person was engaged in a course of full-time education at a school, college, university or similar institution; no regard shall be had to that period in determining whether the person is to be taken, by virtue of paragraph (6)(b), to have been engaged in a full-time occupation on the last day of the year of income.

<a id="s102AC-8"></a>
**(8)** A person shall not be taken to have been engaged in a full-time occupation on the last day of a year of income unless the Commissioner is satisfied that, on that day:

> <a id="s102AC-8-a"></a>
> **(a)** the person had the intention of engaging in a full-time occupation or full-time occupations during the whole or a substantial part of the next succeeding year of income; and

> <a id="s102AC-8-b"></a>
> **(b)** the person did not have the intention of engaging in a course of full-time education at a school, college, university or similar institution at any time during the next succeeding year of income.
