# 94U  Incorporation

For the purposes of the income tax law, the partnership is taken to have been incorporated:

> <a id="s94U-a"></a>
> **(a)** in the place where it was formed; and

> <a id="s94U-b"></a>
> **(b)** under a law in force in that place.
