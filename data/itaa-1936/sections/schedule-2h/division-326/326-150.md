# 326-150  Disposal by post-CGT member of a demutualisation share

(other than a demutualisation original share) or an interest in such a share

<a id="s326-150-1"></a>
**(1)** If:

<a id="s326-150-1-a"></a>
**(a)** the disposal is a disposal of a demutualisation share (other than a demutualisation original share) or an interest in such a share; and

<a id="s326-150-1-b"></a>
**(b)** the disposer is a post-CGT member; then, for the purpose of working out whether the disposer made a capital gain or capital loss from the disposal, the disposer is taken to have done the following:

<a id="s326-150-1-c"></a>
**(c)** to have paid for the acquisition of the share or interest both of the following amounts:

<a id="s326-150-1-c-i"></a>
**(i)** the amount worked out by using the formula: Share or interest Undeducted membership ´          disposed of costs                     Number of disposer's shares

<a id="s326-150-1-c-ii"></a>
**(ii)** any amount actually paid for the acquisition;

<a id="s326-150-1-d"></a>
**(d)** to have paid the amount referred to in subparagraph (c)(i) on the demutualisation resolution day;

<a id="s326-150-1-e"></a>
**(e)** to have paid any amount referred to in subparagraph (c)(ii) when it was actually paid;

<a id="s326-150-1-f"></a>
**(f)** to have acquired the share or interest on the demutualisation resolution day.

<a id="s326-150-2"></a>
**(2)** In the formula in subparagraph (1)(c)(i): Demutualisation of mutual entities other than insurance companies and health insurers Schedule 2H Demutualisation Division 326 Section 326-155 number of disposer's shares means the number of demutualisation shares issued to the disposer or in which the disposer had an interest.
