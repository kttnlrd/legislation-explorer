# 326-100  Disposal by pre-CGT member of a demutualisation original

share or a non-demutualisation bonus share, or an interest in such a share, before demutualisation listing day where member did not acquire membership rights by disposing of membership rights in another mutual entity

<a id="s326-100-1"></a>
**(1)**  If:

> <a id="s326-100-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the disposal is a disposal of either:

> > <a id="s326-100-1-a-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  a demutualisation original share or an interest in such a share; or

> > <a id="s326-100-1-a-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  a non-demutualisation bonus share or an interest in such a share; and

> <a id="s326-100-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the disposer did not acquire membership rights in the demutualising entity by disposing of membership rights in another mutual entity; and

> <a id="s326-100-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the disposer is a pre-CGT member; and

> <a id="s326-100-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the disposal occurs before the demutualisation listing day;

> <a id="s326-100-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  for the purpose of applying Subdivision 130-A of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997, the amount paid for the acquisition of all the demutualisation original shares that is to be taken into account under that Division or Subdivision, as the case may be, is taken to be the amount worked out by using the formula: Adjusted market ´ Number of disposer's shares value         Total number of shares

> <a id="s326-100-1-f"></a>
> **(f)**  if the disposal is a disposal of a demutualisation original share or an interest in such a share, the disposer is taken:

> > <a id="s326-100-1-f-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  to have paid the amount referred to in paragraph (e) on the demutualisation resolution day; and

> > <a id="s326-100-1-f-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  to have acquired the share or interest on the demutualisation resolution day.

<a id="s326-100-2"></a>
**(2)**  In the formula in paragraph (1)(e): number of disposer's shares means the number of demutualisation original shares issued to the disposer or in which the disposer had an interest. total number of shares means the total number of demutualisation shares issued.
