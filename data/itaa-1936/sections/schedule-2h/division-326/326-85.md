# 326-85  Disposal by pre-CGT member of a demutualisation share

(other than a demutualisation original share) or an interest in such a share on or after demutualisation listing day where member did not acquire membership rights by disposing of membership rights in another mutual entity

<a id="s326-85-1"></a>
**(1)**  If:

> <a id="s326-85-1-a"></a>
> **(a)**  the disposal is a disposal of a demutualisation share (other than a demutualisation original share) or an interest in such a share; and

> <a id="s326-85-1-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the disposer did not acquire membership rights in the demutualising entity by disposing of membership rights in another mutual entity; and

> <a id="s326-85-1-c"></a>
> **(c)**  the disposer is a pre-CGT member; and

> <a id="s326-85-1-d"></a>
> **(d)**  the disposal occurs on or after the demutualisation listing day; then, for the purpose of working out whether the disposer made a capital gain or capital loss from the disposal, the disposer is taken to have done the following:

> <a id="s326-85-1-e"></a>
> **(e)**  to have paid for the acquisition of the share or interest the lesser of the following amounts:

> > <a id="s326-85-1-e-i"></a>
> > **(i)**  the amount worked out by using the formula: Share or interest disposed of Adjusted market value ´ Total number of shares

> > <a id="s326-85-1-e-ii"></a>
> > **(ii)**  the amount worked out by using the formula: health insurers
