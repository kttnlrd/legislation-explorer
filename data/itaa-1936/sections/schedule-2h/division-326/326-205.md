# 326-205  Transfer of share or distribution of proceeds of sale of share

not to have any CGT consequences If a trustee transfers an ordinary share or distributes the proceeds of the sale of an ordinary share as mentioned in subparagraph 326-55(1)(h)(ii), Parts 3-1 and 3-3 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 do not apply in respect of any CGT event constituted by or arising from the transfer or distribution.
