# 326-225  Payment out of assets of demutualised entity that is not

included in assessable income is taken not to be a dividend If:

> <a id="s326-225-a"></a>
> **(a)**  a payment out of the assets of a demutualised entity is made to a taxpayer who holds shares or an interest in shares in the entity; and

> <a id="s326-225-b"></a>
> **(b)**  the amount paid is a dividend that is not included in the taxpayer's assessable income; the payment is taken, for the purposes of section 104-135 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997, not to be the payment of a dividend.
