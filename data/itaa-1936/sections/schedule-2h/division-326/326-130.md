# 326-130  Adjusted first day trading price of demutualisation shares


<a id="s326-130-1"></a>
**(1)** For the purposes of this Subdivision, the adjusted first day trading price of demutualisation shares is the amount worked out using the formula: First day trading price - Value of franking surplus of demutualisation shares      Total number of demutualisation shares issued

<a id="s326-130-2"></a>
**(2)** In this section: first day trading price of demutualisation shares means the price per share, as published by ASX Limited, at which the demutualisation shares were last traded, on the stock market operated by ASX Limited, on the demutualisation listing day. value of franking surplus means the value, as determined by a qualified valuer, of the franking surplus of the demutualised entity on the demutualisation listing day.
