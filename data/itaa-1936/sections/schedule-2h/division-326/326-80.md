# 326-80  Disposal by pre-CGT member of a demutualisation share

(other than a demutualisation original share) or an interest in such a share before demutualisation listing day where member did not acquire membership rights by disposing of membership rights in another mutual entity

<a id="s326-80-1"></a>
**(1)** If:

<a id="s326-80-1-a"></a>
**(a)** the disposal is a disposal of a demutualisation share (other than a demutualisation original share) or an interest in such a share; and

<a id="s326-80-1-b"></a>
**(b)** the disposer did not acquire membership rights in the demutualising entity by disposing of membership rights in another mutual entity; and

<a id="s326-80-1-c"></a>
**(c)** the disposer is a pre-CGT member; and

<a id="s326-80-1-d"></a>
**(d)** the disposal occurs before the demutualisation listing day; then, for the purpose of working out whether the disposer made a capital gain or capital loss from the disposal, the disposer is taken to have done the following:

<a id="s326-80-1-e"></a>
**(e)** to have paid for the acquisition of the share or interest the amount worked out by using the formula: Share or interest disposed of Adjusted market value ´ Total number of shares Demutualisation of mutual entities other than insurance companies and health insurers Schedule 2H Demutualisation Division 326 Section 326-85

<a id="s326-80-1-f"></a>
**(f)** to have paid that amount on the demutualisation resolution day;

<a id="s326-80-1-g"></a>
**(g)** to have acquired the share or interest on the demutualisation resolution day.

<a id="s326-80-2"></a>
**(2)** In the formula in paragraph (1)(e): total number of shares means the total number of demutualisation shares issued.
