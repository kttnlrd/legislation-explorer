# 326-65  Extinguishment of membership rights

Application

<a id="s326-65-1"></a>
**(1)**  This section applies where membership rights are extinguished as mentioned in paragraph 326-45(1)(a), 326-50(1)(a), 326-52(1)(a) or 326-55(1)(a). Modification

<a id="s326-65-2"></a>
**(2)**  A capital gain or capital loss arising from the extinguishment of the membership rights of a member is to be disregarded.
