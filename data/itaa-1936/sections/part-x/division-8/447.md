# 447  Tainted sales income


<a id="s447-1"></a>
**(1)** Subject to this Division, for the purposes of this Part, the following amounts are tainted sales income of a company of a statutory accounting period:

<a id="s447-1-a"></a>
**(a)** income from the sale of goods by the company where all of the following conditions are satisfied:

<a id="s447-1-a-i"></a>
**(i)** the goods were sold to the company by another entity;

<a id="s447-1-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** either of the following sub-subparagraphs applies at the time of the sale of the goods to the company: (A) the seller of the goods to the company was an associate of the company and a Part X Australian resident; (B) the goods were sold to the company by an associate of the company who was not a Part X Australian resident, in the course of a business carried on by the associate at or through a permanent establishment of the associate in Australia;

<a id="s447-1-a-iii"></a>
**(iii)** if the goods were altered by the company—the income does not pass the substantial alteration test set out in subsection (4);

<a id="s447-1-b"></a>
**(b)** income from the sale of goods by the company where all of the following conditions are satisfied:

<a id="s447-1-b-i"></a>
**(i)** the goods were sold to the company by another entity;

<a id="s447-1-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** either of the following sub-subparagraphs applies at the time of the purchase of the goods from the company: (A) the purchaser of the goods from the company was an associate of the company and a Part X Australian resident; (B) the purchaser of the goods from the company was an associate of the company who was not a Part X Australian resident and the purchase was made in the course of a business carried on by the purchaser at or through a permanent establishment of the purchaser in Australia;

<a id="s447-1-b-iii"></a>
**(iii)** if the goods were altered by the company—the income does not pass the substantial alteration test set out in subsection (4);

<a id="s447-1-c"></a>
**(c)** income from the sale of goods (in this paragraph called the manufactured goods) by the company where all of the following conditions are satisfied:

<a id="s447-1-c-i"></a>
**(i)** the manufactured goods were manufactured by the company;

<a id="s447-1-c-ii"></a>
**(ii)** any of the raw materials or goods from which the manufactured goods were manufactured were sold to the company by another entity;

<a id="s447-1-c-iii"></a>
**(iii)** either of the following sub-subparagraphs applies at the time of the sale to the company of the raw materials or goods from which the manufactured goods were manufactured: (A) the entity who sold to the company the raw materials or goods from which the manufactured goods were manufactured was an associate of the company and a Part X Australian resident; (B) the raw materials or goods from which the manufactured goods were manufactured were sold to the company by an associate of the company who was not a Part X Australian resident, in the course of a business carried on by the associate at or through a permanent establishment of the associate in Australia;

<a id="s447-1-c-iv"></a>
**(iv)** the income does not pass the substantial manufacture test set out in subsection (4A);

<a id="s447-1-d"></a>
**(d)** income from the sale of goods (in this paragraph called the manufactured goods) by the company where all of the following conditions are satisfied:

<a id="s447-1-d-i"></a>
**(i)** the manufactured goods were manufactured by the company;

<a id="s447-1-d-ii"></a>
**(ii)** any of the raw materials or goods from which the manufactured goods were manufactured were sold to the company by another entity;

<a id="s447-1-d-iii"></a>
**(iii)** either of the following sub-subparagraphs applies at the time of the purchase of the manufactured goods from the company: (A) the purchaser of the manufactured goods from the company was an associate of the company and a Part X Australian resident; (B) the purchaser of the manufactured goods from the company was an associate of the company who was not a Part X Australian resident and the purchase was made in the course of a business carried on by the purchaser at or through a permanent establishment of the purchaser in Australia;

<a id="s447-1-d-iv"></a>
**(iv)** the income does not pass the substantial manufacture test set out in subsection (4A);

<a id="s447-1-e"></a>
**(e)** income from the sale of goods (in this paragraph called the primary production goods) by the company where all of the following conditions are satisfied:

<a id="s447-1-e-i"></a>
**(i)** the primary production goods were: (A) primary products produced, raised or grown by the company; or (B) goods manufactured by the company, in whole or in part, from primary products produced, raised or grown by the company;

<a id="s447-1-e-ii"></a>
**(ii)** any of the propagative material from which the primary products were produced, raised or grown was sold to the company by another entity;

<a id="s447-1-e-iii"></a>
**(iii)** either of the following sub-subparagraphs applies at the time of the sale to the company of the propagative material: (A) the entity who sold the propagative material to the company was an associate of the company and a Part X Australian resident; (B) the propagative material was sold to the company by an associate of the company who was not a Part X Australian resident, in the course of a business carried on by the associate at or through a permanent establishment of the associate in Australia;

<a id="s447-1-e-iv"></a>
**(iv)** the income does not pass the substantial production test set out in subsection (4B);

<a id="s447-1-f"></a>
**(f)** income from the sale of goods (in this paragraph called the primary production goods) by the company where all of the following conditions are satisfied:

<a id="s447-1-f-i"></a>
**(i)** the primary production goods were: (A) primary products produced, raised or grown by the company; or (B) goods manufactured by the company, in whole or in part, from primary products produced, raised or grown by the company;

<a id="s447-1-f-ii"></a>
**(ii)** any of the propagative material from which the primary products were produced, raised or grown was sold to the company by another entity;

<a id="s447-1-f-iii"></a>
**(iii)** either of the following sub-subparagraphs applies at the time of the purchase of the primary production goods from the company: (A) the purchaser of the primary production goods from the company was an associate of the company and a Part X Australian resident; (B) the purchaser of the primary production goods from the company was an associate of the company who was not a Part X Australian resident and the purchase was made in the course of a business carried on by the purchaser at or through a permanent establishment of the purchaser in Australia;

<a id="s447-1-f-iv"></a>
**(iv)** the income does not pass the substantial production test set out in subsection (4B).

<a id="s447-2"></a>
**(2)** Where:

<a id="s447-2-a"></a>
**(a)** a company provides any of the following services:

<a id="s447-2-a-i"></a>
**(i)** drinks and meals;

<a id="s447-2-a-ii"></a>
**(ii)** accommodation in a hotel, motel, guest-house or similar place;

<a id="s447-2-a-iii"></a>
**(iii)** the provision of, or the use of facilities for, entertainment, recreation or instruction; and

<a id="s447-2-b"></a>
**(b)** if subparagraph (a)(ii) or (iii) applies—the transaction for the provision of the services includes the sale of goods of a kind that are commonly supplied in connection with the services concerned; the tainted sales income of the company does not include income from the sale of:

<a id="s447-2-c"></a>
**(c)** if subparagraph (a)(i) applies—the drink or food concerned; or

<a id="s447-2-d"></a>
**(d)** if subparagraph (a)(ii) or (iii) applies—the goods referred to in paragraph (b).

<a id="s447-3"></a>
**(3)** The tainted sales income of a company of a statutory accounting period does not include passive income of the company of the statutory accounting period.

<a id="s447-4"></a>
**(4)** For the purposes of this section, income from the sale of goods by a company passes the substantial alteration test if:

<a id="s447-4-a"></a>
**(a)** the company substantially altered the goods; and

<a id="s447-4-b"></a>
**(b)** a substantial part of that alteration was carried out by the directors or employees of the company.

<a id="s447-4A"></a>
**(4A)**  For the purposes of this section, income from the sale of goods by a company passes the substantial manufacture test if a substantial part of the manufacture of the goods was carried out by the directors or employees of the company.

<a id="s447-4B"></a>
**(4B)**  For the purposes of this section, income from the sale of goods by a company passes the substantial production test if:

<a id="s447-4B-a"></a>
**(a)** if the goods are primary products—a substantial part of the production, raising or growing of the goods was carried out by the directors or employees of the company; or

<a id="s447-4B-b"></a>
**(b)** if the goods are manufactured by the company, in whole or in part, from primary products produced, raised or grown by the company—a substantial part of:

<a id="s447-4B-b-i"></a>
**(i)** the manufacture of the goods; and

<a id="s447-4B-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** those production, raising or growing activities; was carried out by the directors or employees of the company.

<a id="s447-4C"></a>
**(4C)**  For the purposes of subsections (4), (4A) and (4B), the effect of an activity on the market value of the goods concerned is to be ignored.

<a id="s447-5"></a>
**(5)** If, apart from this subsection, goods are purchased or sold by 2 or more entities acting jointly, subsection (1) is to be applied successively as if each such entity were the sole purchaser or seller, as the case may be.

<a id="s447-6"></a>
**(6)** In this section: animals includes fish. primary products means:

<a id="s447-6-a"></a>
**(a)** agricultural or horticultural produce; or

<a id="s447-6-b"></a>
**(b)** trees or crops, whether on or attached to land or not; or

<a id="s447-6-c"></a>
**(c)** timber; or

<a id="s447-6-d"></a>
**(d)** animals (whether dead or alive); or

<a id="s447-6-e"></a>
**(e)** the bodily produce (including natural increase) of animals.
