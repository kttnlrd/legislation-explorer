# 359  Attribution tracing interest in a CFP

An entity that is a partner in a CFP holds an attribution tracing interest in the CFP at a particular time equal to the percentage that the entity holds, or is entitled to acquire, at that time of: percentages Section 360

> <a id="s359-a"></a>
> **(a)** the total interests in the profits of the CFP; or

> <a id="s359-b"></a>
> **(b)** the total interests in the CFP property; or, if those percentages differ, the greater of those percentages.
