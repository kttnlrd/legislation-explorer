# 333  Companies that are residents of unlisted countries


<a id="s333-1"></a>
**(1)** For the purposes of this Part, a company is a resident of an unlisted country at a particular time if, and only if:

<a id="s333-1-a"></a>
**(a)** the company is, in accordance with subsection (2), a resident of a particular unlisted country at that time; or

<a id="s333-1-b"></a>
**(b)** paragraph (a) does not apply and the company is at that time neither:

<a id="s333-1-b-i"></a>
**(i)** a Part X Australian resident; nor

<a id="s333-1-b-ii"></a>
**(ii)** a resident of a particular listed country.

<a id="s333-2"></a>
**(2)** For the purposes of this Part, a company is a resident of a particular unlisted country (in this section called the unlisted country of residence) at a particular time if, and only if:

<a id="s333-2-a"></a>
**(a)** the company is not a Part X Australian resident at that time; and

<a id="s333-2-b"></a>
**(b)** the company is not treated as a resident of a listed country at that time for the purposes of the tax law of the listed country; and

<a id="s333-2-c"></a>
**(c)** any of the following subparagraphs applies:

<a id="s333-2-c-i"></a>
**(i)** both of the following conditions are satisfied at that time: (A) the company is treated as a resident of the unlisted country of residence for the purposes of the tax law of the unlisted country of residence; (B) the company is not treated as a resident of any other unlisted country for the purposes of the tax law of the unlisted country;

<a id="s333-2-c-ii"></a>
**(ii)** both of the following conditions are satisfied at that time: (A) the company is treated as a resident of the unlisted country of residence and at least one other unlisted country for the purposes of the tax laws of each of those unlisted countries; (B) the company is incorporated in the unlisted country of residence;

<a id="s333-2-c-iii"></a>
**(iii)** both of the following conditions are satisfied at that time: (A) the company is not treated as a resident of any unlisted country for the purposes of the tax law of the unlisted country; (B) the company's management and control is solely or principally located in the unlisted country of residence.

<a id="s333-2-c-iv"></a>
**(iv)** all of the following conditions are satisfied at that time: (A) the company is not treated as a resident of any unlisted country for the purposes of the tax law of the unlisted country; (B) the company's management and control is not solely or principally located in the unlisted country of residence; (C) the company is incorporated in the unlisted country of residence.
