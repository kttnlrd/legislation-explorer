# 57-105  Special rules for mining and quarrying

Exploration and prospecting—assume no expenditure

<a id="s57-105-1"></a>
**(1)** In working out the transition taxpayer's allowable deductions under the former Subdivision 330-A or 330-C or Division 40 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997, assume that the transition taxpayer incurred no expenditure on exploration and prospecting before the transition time. Assume that no excess deductions available

<a id="s57-105-2"></a>
**(2)** In working out the transition taxpayer's allowable deductions under the former Subdivision 330-A or 330-C of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997, assume that, for each year of income before the transition year, the transition taxpayer's assessable income would have exceeded the total of the transition taxpayer's deductions for the year.

> **Note:** This means that the transition taxpayer can have no excess deductions remaining from years of income before the transition year. Subdivision 57-K—Balancing adjustments
