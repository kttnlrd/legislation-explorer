# 57-5  Entities to which this Division applies

If:

> <a id="s57-5-a"></a>
> **(a)** at a particular time, all of the income of a taxpayer is wholly exempt from income tax; and

> <a id="s57-5-b"></a>
> **(b)** immediately after that time, the taxpayer's income becomes to any extent assessable income; then:

> <a id="s57-5-c"></a>
> **(c)** the taxpayer is a transition taxpayer; and

> <a id="s57-5-d"></a>
> **(d)** the time when the taxpayer's income becomes to that extent assessable is the transition time; and

> <a id="s57-5-e"></a>
> **(e)** the year of income in which the transition time occurs is the transition year for the taxpayer. Subdivision 57-B—Predecessors of the transition taxpayer
