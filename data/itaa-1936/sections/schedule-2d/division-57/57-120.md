# 57-120  Cancellation of franking surplus, credit or debit

Cancellation of surplus

<a id="s57-120-1"></a>
**(1)** Subject to subsections (3) and (4), if, immediately before the transition time, the transition taxpayer or a subsidiary (see section 57-125) of the transition taxpayer has a franking surplus, then the surplus is reduced to nil at the transition time. Cancellation of credit/debit

<a id="s57-120-2"></a>
**(2)** Subject to subsections (3) and (4), if:

<a id="s57-120-2-a"></a>
**(a)** at any time after the transition time, there arises a franking credit or a franking debit of the transition taxpayer or of a subsidiary of the transition taxpayer; and

<a id="s57-120-2-b"></a>
**(b)** the franking credit or franking debit is to any extent attributable to a period, or to an event taking place, before the transition time; the franking credit or franking debit is to that extent taken not to have arisen. Cases where subsections (1) and (2) do not apply to the transition taxpayer

<a id="s57-120-3"></a>
**(3)** If:

<a id="s57-120-3-a"></a>
**(a)** one or more franking debits of the transition taxpayer arise after the transition time; and

<a id="s57-120-3-b"></a>
**(b)** any of the debits is to an extent (the amount of which is the pre-transition time component of the debit) attributable to the period, or to an event taking place, before the transition time; and

<a id="s57-120-3-c"></a>
**(c)** immediately before the transition time:

<a id="s57-120-3-c-i"></a>
**(i)** there was a franking surplus of the transition taxpayer that was less than the total of the pre-transition time components of all of the debits; or

<a id="s57-120-3-c-ii"></a>
**(ii)** there was no franking surplus of the transition taxpayer; then:

<a id="s57-120-3-d"></a>
**(d)** in a case covered by subparagraph (c)(i)—subsection (1) does not apply to the surplus; and

<a id="s57-120-3-e"></a>
**(e)** in any case—subsection (2) does not apply to the debits. Cases where subsections (1) and (2) do not apply to a subsidiary

<a id="s57-120-4"></a>
**(4)** If:

<a id="s57-120-4-a"></a>
**(a)** one or more franking debits of a subsidiary of the transition taxpayer arise after the transition time; and

<a id="s57-120-4-b"></a>
**(b)** any of the debits is to an extent (the amount of which is the pre-transition time component of the debit) attributable to the period, or to an event taking place, before the transition time; and

<a id="s57-120-4-c"></a>
**(c)** immediately before the transition time:

<a id="s57-120-4-c-i"></a>
**(i)** there was a franking surplus of the subsidiary that was less than the total of the pre-transition time components of all of the debits; or

<a id="s57-120-4-c-ii"></a>
**(ii)** there was no franking surplus of the subsidiary; then:

<a id="s57-120-4-d"></a>
**(d)** in a case covered by subparagraph (c)(i)—subsection (1) does not apply to the surplus; and

<a id="s57-120-4-e"></a>
**(e)** in any case—subsection (2) does not apply to the debits.
