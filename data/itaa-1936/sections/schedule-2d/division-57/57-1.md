# 57-1  What this Division is about

This Division is about the income tax treatment of a taxpayer whose income ceases to be wholly exempt. Broadly, income, outgoings, gains and losses are attributed to the periods before and after the loss of full exemption. Subdivision 57-A—Key concepts
