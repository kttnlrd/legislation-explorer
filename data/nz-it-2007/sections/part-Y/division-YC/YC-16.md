# YC 16  Disregarding market value changes

**When this section applies**

**(1)** This section applies if—

- (a) for a company at a time, the requirements of a continuity provision are not met; and

- (b) the requirements would have been met but for a change in the market value interest of 1 or more persons; and

- (c) the change is solely attributable to—

  - (i) a change in the market value of the tangible and intangible assets of the company:

  - (ii) a change in the market value of any 1 or more shares in the company that is not attributable to any change in the terms of those shares:

  - (iii) a combination of those factors.

**Requirements of continuity provision treated as met**

**(2)** The requirements of the continuity provision are treated as met at the time.

*Defined in this Act: company, continuity provisions, market value, market value interest, share*
