# DV 3  Formula for calculating maximum deduction

**Formula**

**(1)**  The formula referred to in section DV 2(8) is— taxable income − non-resident passive income.

**Definition of items in formula**

**(2)**  The items in the formula are defined in subsections (3) and (4).

**Taxable income**

**(3)**  Taxable income is the amount that would be the master superannuation fund's taxable income in the tax year in which the expenditure is incurred in the absence of sections DV 2 to DV 4.

**Non-resident passive income**

**(4)**  Non-resident passive income is the total of any amounts of non-resident passive income of any of the kinds to which section RF 2(5) (Non-resident passive income) applies derived by the master superannuation fund in the corresponding income year in which the expenditure is incurred.

*Defined in this Act: amount, corresponding income year, deduction, non-resident passive income, superannuation fund, tax year, taxable income*
