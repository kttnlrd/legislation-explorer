# EH 72  Income when refund given on application

**Year of income**

**(1)** A refund under section EH 71 is income, under section CB 27 (Income equalisation schemes), derived by the person in the income year in which the Commissioner receives the application for the refund.

**When year of income may be different**

**(2)** However, subsection (3) applies instead of subsection (1) if—

- (a) the Commissioner receives the application for a refund in the specified period for an accounting year or, if the Commissioner allows in a case or class of cases, within a longer period; and

- (b) the person chooses in the application that the refund is to be income in the accounting year to which the specified period or the longer period relates.

**Different year of income**

**(3)** The refund is income under section CB 27 in the corresponding income year to which the specified period or the longer period relates.

*Defined in this Act: accounting year, apply, Commissioner, corresponding income year, income, income year, person, specified period*
