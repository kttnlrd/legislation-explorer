# OP 25  Consolidated ICA non-resident withholding tax withheld

**Credit**

**(1)** A consolidated imputation group that includes an Australian ICA company has an imputation credit for an amount of non-resident passive income derived by the company from which non-resident withholding tax (NRWT) is withheld.

**Table reference**

**(2)** The imputation credit in subsection (1) is referred to in table O19: imputation credits of consolidated imputation groups, row 20 (NRWT paid).

**Credit date**

**(3)** The credit date is the day the amount of tax is withheld.

*Defined in this Act: amount, Australian ICA company, consolidated imputation group, imputation credit, non-resident passive income, NRWT, pay*
