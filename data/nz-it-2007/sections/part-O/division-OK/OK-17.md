# OK 17  MACA tax advantage arrangement

**Debit**

**(1)** A Maori authority has a Maori authority debit for the amount of a further debit for a tax advantage arrangement referred to in section GB 43(2) and (3) (Reconstruction of Maori authority credit arrangements to obtain tax advantage).

**Table reference**

**(2)** The Maori authority debit in subsection (1) is referred to in table O18: Maori authority debits, row 9 (tax advantage arrangement).

**Debit date**

**(3)** The debit date is the last day of the tax year in which the tax advantage arrangement began.

*Defined in this Act: amount, arrangement, Maori authority, Maori authority credit account, Maori authority debit, tax advantage, tax year*
