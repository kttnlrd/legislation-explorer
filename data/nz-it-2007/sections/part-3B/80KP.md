# 80KP  When entitlement to income-tested benefit ends

**(1)**  This section applies for the purposes of section 80KN when—

- (a) a person's entitlement to an income-tested benefit ends; and

- (b) they apply to the chief executive to have the payment of their family support continued.

**(2)**  The chief executive must continue to pay the family support to the person for a period determined by the chief executive in consultation with the Commissioner. The amount is determined as if the person were still being paid an income-tested benefit during this period.
