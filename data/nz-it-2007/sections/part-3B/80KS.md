# 80KS  Request by person to stop or re-start payment of tax credit

**(1)** Subsection (2) applies, despite section 80KN or 80KP, when a person notifies the chief executive not to pay them the tax credit.

**(2)** The chief executive must, as soon as practicable, stop paying the person the tax credit.

**(3)** Subsection (4) applies when a person cancels their notification under subsection (1).

**(4)** The chief executive must, as soon as practicable, start paying the person the tax credit again.
