# CW 21  Amounts derived by visiting crew of pleasure craft

**Exempt income**

**(1)** Income that a non-resident crew member derives from performing services in New Zealand relating to a pleasure craft while it is in New Zealand is exempt income if—

- (a) the services are performed for a person who is not resident in New Zealand; and

- (b) the pleasure craft is the subject of a security given under section 136 of the Customs and Excise Act 2018; and

- (c) the pleasure craft is not owned, wholly or partly or directly or indirectly, by—

  - (i) a resident of New Zealand; or

  - (ii) a controlled foreign company.

**Some definitions**

**(2)** In this section,— non-resident crew member means a person who— (a) is a crew member of a pleasure craft; and (b) is a non-resident, a matter determined without applying section YD 1(3) (Residence of natural persons); and (c) is not present in New Zealand on more than 365 days in any 2-year period that starts on or after 28 May 2002; and (d) is not in New Zealand unlawfully under the Immigration Act 2009 pleasure craft is defined in section 2 of the Maritime Transport Act 1994.
