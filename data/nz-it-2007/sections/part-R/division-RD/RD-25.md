# RD 25  FBT rules and their application

**Meaning**

**(1)** The FBT rules means—

- (a) sections CX 2 to CX 38 (which relate to fringe benefits); and

- (b) sections GA 2, GB 31, and GB 32 (which relate to FBT); and

- (c) sections RD 26 to RD 63; and

- (d) schedules 1, part C (Basic tax rates: income tax, ESCT, RWT, and attributed fringe benefits) and 5 (Fringe benefit values for motor vehicles); and

- (e) sections 46B to 46E, 93, Part 7, and section 139B of the Tax Administration Act 1994.

**Application**

**(2)** The FBT rules apply to an employer who provides a fringe benefit to their employee in connection with their employment.

*Defined in this Act: employee, employer, employment, FBT, FBT rules, fringe benefit*
