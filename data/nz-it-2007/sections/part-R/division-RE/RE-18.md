# RE 18  Payments made by RWT proxies

**Calculation of amount of tax**

**(1)** A person who is an RWT proxy for a person paying resident passive income that consists of a dividend must withhold an amount of tax for the payment calculated using the formula— tax rate × amount paid ÷ (1 − tax rate).

**Definition of items in formula**

**(2)** In the formula,—

- (a) tax rate is the basic rate set out in schedule 1, part D, clause 3 (Basic tax rates: income tax, ESCT, RSCT, RWT, and attributed fringe benefits):

- (b) amount paid is the amount of the dividend paid.

*Defined in this Act: amount, amount of tax, dividend, pay, resident passive income, RWT proxy*
