# LO 4  When income tax unpaid

**When this section applies**

**(1)**  This section applies when—

- (a) a person has a tax credit under section LO 1 and a Maori authority credit is attached to a dividend; and

- (b) the dividend is paid by a Maori authority that has a debit balance in their Maori authority credit account at the end of the tax year; and

- (c) the authority has not paid further income tax by the due date referred to in section OK 21 (Further income tax for closing debit balance).

**Amount of reduction**

**(2)**  The Commissioner may reduce the person's credit by an amount equal to their proportion of the unpaid amount under subsection (1)﻿(c).

**When failure to pay remedied**

**(3)**  To the extent to which a Maori authority remedies the failure after the due date, this section does not apply.

*Defined in this Act: amount, Commissioner, dividend, further income tax, Maori authority, Maori authority credit, Maori authority credit account, pay, tax credit, tax year*
