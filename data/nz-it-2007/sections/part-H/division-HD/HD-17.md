# HD 17  Agent paying premiums to residents of Switzerland

**When this section applies**

**(1)** This section applies when—

- (a) an insurer derives income under section CR 3 (Income of non-resident general insurer); and

- (b) an agent of the insurer under section HD 16 pays the premium to an insurer or to some other person not carrying on a business in New Zealand through a fixed establishment in New Zealand; and

- (c) the insurer or other person is treated as being resident in Switzerland for the purposes of a double tax agreement between the government of New Zealand and the government of Switzerland.

**Disclosure**

**(2)** The agent must disclose details of the payment of the premium to the Commissioner in the manner, if any, required by the Commissioner.

*Defined in this Act: agent, business, Commissioner, double tax agreement, fixed establishment, income, insurer, New Zealand, pay, premium*
