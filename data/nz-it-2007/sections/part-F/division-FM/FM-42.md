# FM 42  When company liquidated

If a company is no longer part of a consolidated group because it has been liquidated,—

- (a) the company is not treated as leaving the consolidated group from the start of the income year of the liquidation under sections FM 39 and FM 40:

- (b) part-year financial statements are not required under section FM 40(4)(b).

*Defined in this Act: company, consolidated group, income year, liquidation*
