# FE 38  Measuring ownership interests in companies

For the purposes of this subpart, a person's ownership interest in a company is the total of the following percentages:

- (a) any direct ownership interests they hold in the company; and

- (b) any direct ownership interests held in the company by an associated person; and

- (c) any indirect ownership interests they hold in the company; and

- (d) any indirect ownership interests held in the company by an associated person.

*Defined in this Act: associated person, company, ownership interest*
